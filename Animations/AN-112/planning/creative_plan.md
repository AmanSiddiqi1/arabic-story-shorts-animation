# AN-112 Abu Hassan al-Ziyadi and the Khorasan Man: creative plan

Film length 171.90 s of narration + 3.0 s THE END = 174.9 s. 19 scenes, 199 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3 and every cue word is in `creative_plan.json` with its start time from `inputs/timeline.md`. The JSON is the storyboard; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A penniless Baghdad scholar spends a pilgrim's deposit, is caught out the very next morning, and is rescued by a Caliph who dreamed he must help him. The pilgrim then forgives the debt, and the scholar ends up a judge.

**Conflict.** Abu Hassan against his own promise. Nobody in the story is a villain: the shopkeepers are owed, the pilgrim is grieving, the guards are messengers. The tension is all in one object, the purse.

**Spine (16 beats in the JSON).** Poor scholar (2.12) and his debts (13.34); the deposit of ten thousand (24.36) and its terms (38.98); he spends it (46.24); the surprise return (58.40); "my money, now" (69.38); the excuse (77.60); the sleepless night (81.70); the guards (91.12); the dream reveal (107.64); fifty thousand in three gifts (123.74); the tears (139.26); the refusal (148.52); the judge's turban (160.26); the story spreads (168.66).

**Surprises.** 1. He is back the next morning (58.40). 2. The guards are not arresting him (reveal completed at 114.62). 3. The Khorasani refuses the money he came for (148.52). 4. The appointment as judge (160.26).

**Emotional arc.** Shame, temptation, short false comfort, panic, dread, wonder, relief, shared tears, honour.

**Ending.** The judge on his bench as years wheel past the window; the townspeople, including everyone we met, pass the story along; THE END at 171.9 held 3 s.

**Moral.** Trust is heavy, and honesty about hardship is met with mercy; help can come from where no one expects it.

## 3. Character acting

- **Abu Hassan** (`costume:'physician'`, age 0.55). His turban is his barometer: crooked by default, jumps off at 60.84 (`hop` on a headwear offset), slips in the fluster at 70.14, straight when he is at peace (S16, S18), and replaced by the oversized judge's turban that drops over his eyes (160.26, again at 172.6). Eyes do most of the work: gaze into the empty purse (5.1), boxed-in flicks left and right (9.5), locked on the bow (39.2), three guilty targets before opening the purse (45.1), darting in S8, circling the ceiling without a blink in S9 (79.62). Body: `sq` 0.88 to 0.94 when crushed, stretch 1.12 when he bolts upright, `tremble` in front of bills and guards. He hides the purse on the side away from the visitor with `handLW`.
- **The Khorasani** (`costume:'merchant'`, green and yellow palette). First visit: open, warm, bundle bouncing after each stop. Second visit: heavy steps, `wet` eyes, `lid` 0.4, and a patient open palm (`PERSON_HANDS.reach`). In S17 `teardrops`, a sleeve wipe with cloak follow-through, a slow look round the room, then closing his own palm at 145.20: that small gesture is the heart of the film. He ends mock-stern with folded arms and one eye opening (`wink`).
- **Caliph al-Maamun** (`costume:'caliph'`, seated with `sit:1, seat:'bench'` at `throne.sitY`). Never stern: leans in, beckons, strokes his beard (`PERSON_HANDS.chin`) while his gaze reads the three story boxes, hides a smile behind his hand at 155.88. In the dream he lies (`lie:1, lid:1`), bolts up with `anticipate` + `landSquash` three times, each time more tired.
- **Shopkeepers, guards, crowd.** Always offset by 0.15 to 0.3 s and different in height. Guard two is half a beat late on every move and checks guard one. The stern lead guard finally grins at 114.62.
- **Mule** (`donkey` pal 1, s 1.12). Ears alternate while walking, go flat at the guards (90.5), `headTurn: 1` to look back at its lost rider (88.26), leans back before it lets itself be led (94.64).
- **Listeners' reactions.** Whoever is not speaking is listening: Abu cups an ear (21.9), the visitor's brow lifts at the excuse (77.4), the kneeling Abu blinks up confused when the Caliph starts his own story (103.84).
- **Secondary motion.** Purse cord and tassels, turban tails, the travel bundle, the bills that keep unrolling after the hand stops, the blanket lagging in S9.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground y 900; faces and props stay in the panel, nothing important below y 1000.
- S1 opens as an establishing wide and pushes in; the shopkeepers squeeze Abu from both edges.
- The home scenes use one two-shot grammar: visitor centre-left, Abu right, purse between them on the desk. Who has the purse is always readable from which side of the desk it is on. S7 and S16 use the identical framing and palm pose so the audience feels the difference.
- Push-ins on decisions and shocks: the purse landing (24.3), Abu alone with it (46.2), his face at 60.84, the turban at 160.3. A whip pan to the door at 58.9. A slight shake only in S8.
- S10 and S11 track along the 2400-wide dawn street with the mule at 40 percent from the left; guards enter from both lanes to frame him.
- S12 starts wide so the kneeling scholar is small under the arches, then closes to a two-shot.
- S13 is the one formal change: the thought cloud becomes the frame. Moon top left, voice balloon from the top right, bed across the lower middle.
- S18 cranes down from the lamps; S19 pulls out so the crowd forms a row at the panel bottom under the judge.
- Lettering sits above or between heads, never on a face; arrows always start at the purse or end on it.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour.** The moth from the empty purse; the second bill that rolls past his feet; the purse that flops like a sock behind his back; the sun doing a lap for TOMORROW!; the wobbling excuse cloud with a question-mark friend; the mule asking its rider for directions; the late guard; the boy dragging the big purse backwards; the purse pushed back and forth; the turban over the eyes.
- **Suspense.** The bow on the purse and Abu's swallow (39.2); three looks before he opens it (45.1); the counter running down under his contented face; the shadow in the doorway (59.0); the spear tip entering first (89.74); the sealed order (92.48).
- **Surprise.** The knock on the very next morning arrives straight after PLENTY OF TIME. The guards' portrait pays off in the dream. The refusal.
- **Emotion.** The father fading from the cloud; the sleepless eyes; relief at 115.18; forehead to the carpet (126.58); the closing palm (145.20); the hand on the shoulder (149.74).
- **Clarity devices.** Two arrows for the two terms of the deposit; one purse counter (10,000 to 0) and one gift counter (10,000, 20,000, 50,000); three story boxes for "he told him everything"; a NIGHT 1, 2, 3 counter for "repeated".
