## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Payoff (0-12.6):** the film opens on the last frame of episode 1 (hand a finger-width from the hidden lever, sunset). He turns it; the horse sinks through the cloud deck; PHEW!
- **Strange country (12.6-25.7):** deserts, rivers, cities with ??? over them; one blue dome glints at him. He picks the flat roof of the tallest palace.
- **Trespasser (25.7-40.2):** touchdown at dusk, a cat as the only witness, the horse tied to a post "in case it wanders", tiptoe for water. A loose tile: CLACK!
- **Inciting meeting (40.2-60.8):** the princess hears it, the door opens, maid and lamp behind her. He is caught holding her water cup. WHO ARE YOU? HOW DID YOU GET HERE? He can only point up.
- **Disbelief (60.8-79.5):** a bow, an explanation in pictures (father, toy palace, the arc across the sky, a question mark). She knocks on the wood: YOU ARE JOKING!
- **Proof and climax (79.5-98.8):** UP and DOWN, a hover 150 units above the tiles, her eyes (the film's only face close-up), HA HA!, THE STRANGEST HORSE IN THE WORLD!
- **Tenderness (98.8-114.7):** LATER, IN THE GARDEN... two countries side by side in picture balloons; his worried father; "I go back, then I come back to you".
- **Scare and promise (114.7-132.8):** guards behind the hedge; the password, and the moon's reflection pulls into a gold path on the river; from the roof she points out TWO TOWERS and the BLUE DOME.
- **Open ending (132.8-151.5):** the charcoal map, the vault, the flight across the moon, guards a moment too late, her hand on her heart: TO BE CONTINUED...
- **Conflict:** not a villain but trust: a stranger on a roof at night must be believed. **Arc:** relief, curiosity, embarrassment, wonder, warmth, hurry, wistfulness. **Moral:** courtesy and honesty turn a stranger into a friend; mark the way back before you leave.

## 3. Character acting
- **Firuz:** an episode older. In the air: held breath, then the whole body lets go (`sq` 0.94, head arriving after the shoulders with `lagged`). On the roof he is a polite burglar: crouch landing (`hop`, `landSquash`), tiptoe `walk` at amp 1.3 with `footfalls`, `PERSON_HANDS.hush` to a cat, a deep drink with the throat bobbing. Caught: a `sq` 1.05 pop, a crooked smile, the cup put back without looking. He explains with his hands (`flow` arcs, `palmsUp` shrug). In the garden his face carries the feeling: `lid` 0.4, `wet` eyes at 108.9, then the jaw sets. His second vault (81.86) is clean where episode 1's was a scramble. Gaze by `lookAt` / `gazePlan`, a blink on every head turn; the cape is always late.
- **Princess:** upright, economical, quick eyes. Wary means `lid` 0.25, `brow` 0.3, weight on the back foot, a raised palm; she never turns her back on him. Her arc is in three hands: palm out (54.32), hand on hip (75.66), hands to cheeks (90.04). The laugh is a full-body bounce on twos with the veil ribbon a beat behind. In the garden a reach that is started and taken back (109.2). At the end a wave that stops halfway and comes to rest on her heart.
- **Maid:** the comic barometer, always 0.2 s behind the princess and twice as big: hides, peeks over a shoulder, `tremble` in the lamp arm (so the light pool wobbles), crouches under the lamp during the hover, dares one finger on a brass hoof, pretends not to listen in the garden, then becomes the brisk one when guards come.
- **Guards:** two shapes (tall with torch, short with spear), never in step (`walk` phase 0.5 apart); mostly helmets and spear tips above the hedge; on the roof they look at each other and shrug.
- **The ebony horse:** lifeless, as the bible says: no blink, breath, leg, ear or tail motion. People test it (a knock, a hand waved at the eye, the cat rubbing its leg) and it never answers. Its acting is `powerOn`, glints, light bands, the rig's 6 px bob and the shadow.
- **Cat:** one eye at the landing, an ear at the hush, `arch` and `puff` at the hover, tail up against the wooden leg after the laugh, watching the take-off.
- Moving holds on twos everywhere: `idleSway` on `tip`, head and hands, `breathe` on `sq`, veil and cape ribbons, lamp flame, carpets, fountain, fireflies. Groups never in sync (`stagger`, own seeds).

## 4. Camera and composition
- **Wide where the place matters:** S3 (pan over the land), S4 (the whole river city, zoom 0.8), S5 (terrace, dome, city and sky for the landing), S13 (whole garden with the horse on the roof, zoom 0.72), S16 (skyline with towers and dome, zoom 0.75), S18 (zoom 0.62, the roof in the lower third under a huge moonlit sky).
- **Pans where it is detailed:** S6 tracks 500 units of roof with the tiptoe; S7 pans from the pavilion to the full terrace; S10 tracks with the princess to the horse; S15 whips to the gate and pans with the run. Floors stay near panel y 900 to 960; no push-in that empties the lower picture.
- **Close-ups only at peaks:** two control inserts (1.9-5.6 lever, 82.84 knob) and one face close-up (her eyes, 88.3-90.6). The map is a callout card over the scene, not a cut-away.
- **Two-shots** keep the lamp between the two leads until she lowers her hand; in the garden nothing stands between them.
- **Flight framing** as in the bible: horse in the upper third, descending = nose down with clouds scrolling up; the take-off leaves up-left across the moon so the towers, river and dome (the things he must find again) stay in the final frame.
- Balloons and tags live in the sky band (panel y 110..340) with tails to heads; the moon is never covered during the password. The bottom third of the film is the caption strip.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** tying up a wooden horse and glancing back to see whether it wanders; the hush to a cat; being caught with the cup; the maid behind the lamp; pointing straight up as the only answer; knocking on the "statue"; guards who arrive to find a princess, a maid and a cat.
- **Suspense:** the held breath before the horse obeys; the tile; the line of light under the door; spear tips sliding along the hedge; torch shadows climbing the stair wall while he vaults.
- **Surprise:** the hover (a statue leaves the floor in silence); the moon's scattered reflection snapping into a path on the word "moon".
- **Emotion:** the king still reaching at an empty sky inside a small cloud; her started reach; two arcs, one going home and one coming back; the wave that ends on her heart; the last brass twinkle.
- **Series threads:** the map and the three landmarks are drawn clearly so later episodes can call them back; the guards' royal blue is this court's colour.
