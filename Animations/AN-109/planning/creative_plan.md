# AN-109 Ibrahim bin al-Mahdi and the Merchant's Sister: creative plan

Film length 173.5 s (audio 170.56 s + THE END hold). 14 scenes, 170 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is in `creative_plan.json` (the machine-readable storyboard, which is the authority for timing). This file explains the intent.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A prince out for a stroll follows his nose to a stranger's door and his heart to a hand at the window, bluffs his way in, wins the house with a lute, and leaves with a bride. The proof is standing in the caliph's court.

**Shape.** A frame story: Ibrahim tells the caliph al-Ma'mun (0-6 s and 156-170 s); the tale itself plays inside his thought cloud.

**Conflict.** A closed door (no strangers) against a man with two appetites and no invitation. **Emotional arc:** appetite, wonder, cheek, embarrassment, redemption through skill, honesty, suspense, joy. **Moral:** honesty and open-handedness turn a gatecrasher into family.

Three running objects carry the story without words: the **aroma ribbon** (appetite), the **hand with the gold bangle** (love), and the **four-note row with a gap** (his tactless remark, then its proof).

## 3. Character acting

- **Ibrahim** (green robe, yellow turban, no crown). Signature: the *too-casual face* (serene half lids, small smile) whenever he is bluffing: 26.44, 32.36, 93.88, and once more to camera in the last frame. Signature move: the *sniff* (chest lift `sq` 1.05, `lid` 1) at 9.94, 40.4 and 107.08. As a musician he nods the beat *before* the singer plays it (54.92). Regret is played down and slow (66.0: head sinks, hand to neck, slow blink). The confession (96.12-99) uses no gestures at all; stillness is his dignity. At 112.1 he braces with one eye peeking (`wink`).
- **Merchant** (Library merchant). Big, warm, physical: arms wide (37.08), knee-slap laugh (107.76), the full comic fall with the hopping turban (101.24: `anticipate`, stretch, `tip` -0.35, `impactSquash`). His key acting moment is 110.76-113.28: laugh dies, three gaze stops (balloon, curtain, guest), then a slow smile. No anger.
- **Singer** (maiden + oud). Proud professional: chin up, precise. Anger = standing tall (`sq` 1.08), `flush`, head turned away, lute hugged. Her change (77.38-79.5) is one continuous pose transition: arms unfold, flush drains, eyes widen, `wet`, sits without looking, then bows.
- **Sister** (lady). First only a hand, twice, with the same finger curl (14.78 and 134.28). In person: eyes down, one quick look up, a smile she tries to hide, soft flush on the nod (140.9), an open smile only in her new home (149.1).
- **Friends.** friendB reacts first and biggest (cheeks, jumps up to clap); friendA late and small (covers mouth, slow clap). **Women 1-3:** brisk, shy, amused; never in step. **Witnesses:** one brisk, one puffing two steps behind. **Tailor:** finishes two stitches before he bothers to look up.
- **Caliph:** chin on hand, leaning in at 2.14 and in the identical pose at 156.0 (now grinning); double take boy / Ibrahim at 164.22. **Son:** saccades between father and caliph at 2.6; bow then an irrepressible wave at 163.22.
- Everyone: `LIFE.t` blinks, explicit `lookAt` targets, `breathe` on holds, `settleTo` on every pose change, `stagger(i)` in groups.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, floor/ground y 900; faces kept between y 560 and 760. Balloons, clouds and insets live in the upper wall or sky (y 250-550), on the side away from the speaker's face.
- **Court (S1, S14):** wide; throne right, Ibrahim centre, boy left; the cloud above Ibrahim. `taleZoom` in and out are the only two "special" moves and bracket the tale.
- **Street (S2-S4):** house facade in the right half, tailor's cloth stall at the left. S3 tilts along Ibrahim's eye line to a tight window shot (zoom 2.3) with his turban tip at the bottom edge; back to wide for the popped cloud; `twoShot` with the tailor. S4 tracks the walkers, then a door group shot wide enough for two thought clouds.
- **Interiors (S5-S12):** guests in the left half, performer and curtain on the right; the curtain alcove is the women's doorway, the left door is for guests and witnesses, so entrances always tell us who is coming. S9 is a held `twoShot` with one push-in for the confession and a snap back for the fall. S10 is a deliberately static wide (queue + pinned memory inset). S11 starts close on the curtain edge and pulls out with her.
- **S13:** static wide, then a slow push-in so the frame tightens as the room fills.
- Transitions: zoom (taleZoom) 6.0 and 156.0; wipe into the house 38.64; whip pan 45.4; **one LATER... card at 84.0**; match cut purse stack to empty room 147.0. Panel-only shake (2 px) on the merchant's fall and on gift landings.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the double mistake as two symmetrical thought clouds with Ibrahim's eyes going left, right, camera (35.38); the tailor watching the "stranger" walk straight in (27.98); the hopping turban (101.24); the door that cannot close on the gifts (155.08); the final look to camera.
- **Suspense:** stillness before the confession (95.36: only pupils move); three wrong hands with a 0 / 3 counter; the silent pause 133.4-134.3 with only the curtain edge trembling.
- **Surprise:** the hand cancels the food (cloud pops, ribbons fall limp, 16.82); the gap in the note row; the boy at court who was there all along.
- **Emotion:** the song of longing whose hearts fade before they arrive (54.38); the gold note filling the gap (76.06); the host choosing generosity over offence (113.28); the merchant seated beside the throne (169.66).
- **Set-up and payoff pairs:** sniff 9.94 / 40.4 / 107.08; finger curl on shutter 14.78 / curtain 134.28; note gap 62.22 / 76.06; window glance 37.9 / ceiling glance 88.7; caliph's pose 2.14 / 156.0; boy 0.0 / 162.56.
