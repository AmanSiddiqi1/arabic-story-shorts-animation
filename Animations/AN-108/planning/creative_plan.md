# AN-108 The Chief of the Kus Police and the Sharper: creative plan

Film 0.00-181.46 s (MP3 177.96 s + 3.5 s THE END). 20 scenes, 139 actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is in `creative_plan.json`, which is the authority for implementation.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
A con told as dry comedy. The chief of police is proud (of his badge) and a little greedy; the sharper sells him a feeling, not a chest.
- **Setup (0-8):** evening, the chief at home, polishing his badge under his own WANTED poster.
- **Inciting (8.6):** a finely dressed stranger; a servant staggers in under a heavy chest; a "secret matter" (20.9).
- **Rising:** confession of highway robbery (33.1, thought cloud), repentance on his knees (40.0), the chest "worth 40,000" (53.8), the lid opens on a blaze (56.9), the offer: all of it for 1,000 lawful dinars (82.6).
- **Turn / climax:** the chief is pleased twice, by piety and by profit (99.6); he pays over the treasurer's doubt (105.8) and hugs the chest.
- **Suspense:** the pair vanish into the night (118.5); the chief almost bites a ring to test it, shrugs, sleeps on the chest (124.9). NEXT DAY... card at 132.5.
- **Reveal (142-155):** the goldsmith tests piece by piece: not gold, not silver; BRASS, TIN, GLASS. The glow dies in three steps.
- **Resolution / moral (155-178):** rage, guards, no trace; he understands that the man bought his trust cheaply. The expert on thieves sits under his WANTED poster, which slides into his lap. Shame, not cruelty.

Conflict: the chief's self-image against what the audience can see. Surprises: the confession; the lid; the verdict. Arc for the viewer: amused suspicion, dazzle, dread at the near test, satisfaction, a little pity.

## 3. Character acting
- **Chief** (`vizier` preset, red): proud posture (chest out, `tip` -0.04, `lid` 0.35), always touching his badge. He reads everything late. Key faces: flattered (19.4), anger at the confession (`brow` 1, hand to badge, glance at the poster 35.3), melting (brow 1 to -0.3 at 40.8), dazzle (hands to cheeks, coin eyes 64.6), greedy side glance (96.1), bliss hugging the chest (108.1), the yawn instead of the test (126.2), frozen smile as the tags land (148.9-154.3), flush and stamp with `anticipate` (155-157.6), slow realisation and the shame ending (165.4-177.7: look up, one lens stare, eyes break away, head drops, turban slides).
- **Sharper** (`merchant` preset, green and yellow): gliding walk, slow gestures, eyes that stay on the chief even when he bows. **Mask on / mask off:** his face changes only when the chief is not looking (wink 43.6, crossed fingers 94.0, glance 111.2) and fully only in the street (120.2). The purse disappears into his robe in 3 frames (107.5), the fastest move in the film.
- **Servant:** sells the weight (`sq` 0.92, `tip` -0.08, `tremble`, 12 px dips per step), then is bored by the show (eye roll 42, shading his eyes 67.9), and skips once free of it (119.2).
- **Treasurer** (age 0.8): the only doubter. Squints at the stranger, clutches the purse, tries to whisper (105.1), is waved quiet, leaves shaking his head.
- **Goldsmith** (age 0.85): the opposite of the sharper: small, plain, no flourish, never dazzled. One eye shut, the other huge behind the loupe; he does not watch the glass fall.
- **Guards:** A eager, B half a second late (`stagger`); they end on the film's one shared look to camera (164.6).
- Everyone has a gaze target and blinks (`LIFE.t`); pose changes use `settleTo`; listeners react (the servant and the treasurer are the audience's eyes).

## 4. Camera and composition
- One warm room for most of the film, so the camera does the variety: establishing wide (S1), two-shots for talk (S3, S4, S6, S9, S13), an **insert on the chest** for the lid (S8, zoom 2.3-2.6) answered by the **same insert in cold morning light** for the test (S17), close on the chief for his two motives (S11) and his rage (S18), and a final pull-out that stacks poster, cloud and slumped chief (S20).
- Two exteriors open the film out: a cool night street for the escape (S14, track right) and a bright 2400-wide street pan for the search (S19).
- Left to right order in the room never changes: door, servant, chest, sharper, chief, curtain. Money moves right to left (treasurer, chief, sharper); the chest moves left to right. The window between them is the clock (dusk, night, dawn, morning).
- Thought clouds and price tags live in the upper third; faces and hands stay between y 560 and 900; nothing important below the floor line. Panel-only shake at 16.95, 157.04, 178.05.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Running irony:** the WANTED poster of a masked robber hangs above the chief from the first shot; the robber in the confession cloud wears the same mask; the poster lands in the chief's lap on "expert on thieves".
- **Dramatic irony:** the audience gets three tiny mask-off moments; the treasurer's ignored warning; the bite test that is started and abandoned (the worst moment to yawn).
- **The glow is the lie:** `goldGlow` lights the chief's face yellow while he believes; it drops by a third on each verdict and the room goes flat when GLASS lands.
- **Comic numbers:** 40,000 counter, struck for ONLY 1,000!, then the chief's private sum with PROFIT!; at the end the tag 1,000 points at a heart wearing his badge: the real price of his trust.
- **Humour by contrast:** the servant's heavy stagger in, feather-light skip out; the sharper's silk manners against the goldsmith's plain thumbnail (TINK!).
