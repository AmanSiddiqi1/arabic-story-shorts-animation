# AN-108 The Chief of the Kus Police and the Sharper: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/AN-108_v1.mp4` (180.00 s), all 30 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/`, plus full-size frames and fine-step montages in `review/v1_pack/extra/` (`m1..m5.jpg`, `n1..n4.jpg`). Times are seconds on the film. Positions use the plan's marks (floor y 900, chest x 520 after the slide, chief stool x 690).

## Summary
No blockers. The story is told in order, every event is present, the subtitles (Arabic and English) are readable, correctly placed and in sync, and the film ends on THE END at 178.2-180.0. Several things work well and must be kept: the HEAVY! carry, the confession cloud with STAND AND DELIVER!, the 40,000 counter struck for ONLY 1,000!, the lid insert with the ring and the red stone held up on their words (60.5-64.5), the chief's coin eyes (64.7), the PROFIT! sum cloud (98.2-100.5), the goldsmith insert with loupe, TINK! and the GOLD / SILVER tags struck out on their words, the turban blown off by rage (157.0-157.5), and the guards' GONE! look to camera (164.7).

REVISE because four key beats are staged so that the viewer cannot see them: the ending (the poster gag and the shame are played in a distant wide), the abandoned test at night (hidden behind the open lid), the hug (the chief is a head lying on the chest for 9 s), and the verdict (GLASS is on screen for half a second, and a tag contradicts the subtitle). All are staging and camera fixes; nothing needs a new asset.

## Notes

1. **174.7-180.0, MAJOR. The ending is played in a far wide shot.**
   At 174.7 the cloud pops and the camera pulls out to the full room. The poster slides off the wall (176.0-176.5) and lands in the chief's lap while he is about 110 px tall in a 972 px panel, bottom centre, with two-thirds of the frame empty floor and wall. His face is about 45 px wide, so the look up, the one stare at the lens and the head drop (plan 165.4-177.7) cannot be read, and the poster covers his torso. This is the film's signature irony, timed on "خبيرًا باللصوص" (176.00-177.68), and it lands as a small detail. See `extra/m5.jpg`, frame 4.
   Fix: do not pull out to the wide. From 174.7 hold a medium shot (zoom about 1.7-1.9) that contains only the poster on the wall (700, 470), the chief on the floor and the right half of the chest; the poster and the chief are in one vertical line, so the fall stays in frame without a camera move. Poster: nail pops at 175.36 ("نفسه"), `fallY` with a slight swing from 176.00 ("خبيرًا"), lands at 176.78 ("باللصوص") leaning against his knees, NOT over his chest, so his face and badge stay visible above it and the masked face on the poster sits just under his own. Acting: eyes follow the poster down (gaze up, then down), one beat to the lens at 177.0, `lid` 0.6 and head `tip` forward at 177.7, turban slides 10 px. Lid drop with `impactSquash` and panel shake at 178.05 as now. Only then, if wanted, a slow 10 % ease-out during the THE END hold. Keep THE END at the top, clear of the poster nail hole.
