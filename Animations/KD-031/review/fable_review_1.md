# KD-031 The Pair of Hoopoes and the Treasure: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/KD-031_v1.mp4` (174.3 s) through all 30 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` plus full-size frames pulled into `review/v1_pack/extra/` (montages `m1.jpg` to `m15.jpg`, `sub1.jpg`, `glitch.jpg`).

## Summary

No blockers. The story is told in order and is faithful and family friendly: every event of the narration is on screen, the ending is held, and the two-coins / two-birds motif carries through. Subtitles are verbatim, well timed and readable in both languages, including the longest chunk (106.87-116.56, three Arabic lines plus two English lines, fits with margin). Strong moments already: the cage cut-ins (24.7-27.5, 49.7-52.0), the payment with the counter 2, 1, 0 and the coin bite (57.0-59.0), the fanned crests and the heart drifting down to the man (92.0-95.7), the frozen one-eyed overhearing (99.7-101.7), the ground window, the jump with the rolling cage (109.0-110.5), CLINK and the two fingers (134.5-140.5), KEEP IT (162.2), the king at the window and the birds landing on the awning at the end.

Why REVISE and not PASS WITH NOTES: three of the story's key beats do not read at phone size (the "separate them" thought, the gentle opening of the cage, the birds caught out by the man's question), and most of the film is staged as a small figure in a wide shot above a large empty band of ground. These are staging and camera changes, not new assets, and they lift the film noticeably. Nothing below touches narration, subtitle text or timing.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 40.5-54.5 s, MAJOR: the "buy one, leave the other" thought is too small to read
What: the thought cloud holds a cage about 90 px wide on the 1080 px frame. The hand lifting one bird out (43.5-46.0) reads as a pale diagonal bar and a pink dot; the bird left behind stretching through the bars cannot be seen; the heart cracking at 52.4-53.5 is a few pixels. The cloud also sits on the stall awning.
Why: this is the turn of the whole story (the reason he gives up both coins). If the viewer cannot see one bird taken and the other left reaching, the sacrifice at 57.7 has no cause.
Fix: enlarge the cloud and its contents by about 1.7x (cloud roughly 520 units wide, cage about 170 wide, the same size as the real cage in the wide shot) and move the camera so the cloud sits in clear sky above the roofline, not over the awning. Inside the cloud: at واحدًا (44.42) the hand lifts bird A clearly out and up to the right, wings drooping; at الآخر (46.16) bird B presses to the bars, neck stretched, beak open toward A, `tremble`. Hold that picture to 48.3. At يفرقهما (52.40) make the heart at least 60 units wide and split it with a visible gap, halves drifting toward each bird. Keep the man's face clear below the cloud.

### 2. 116.9-121.2 s, MAJOR: the comic turn is lost, the birds' embarrassment is invisible
What: during the man's question the shot stays wide; the hoopoes are about 40 px tall on the branch and do not visibly change. The planned gag at بكما (120.48), one scratching his head with a foot and the other studying her toes, cannot be seen. The camera only pushes in at 121.2, after the line is over.
Why: this is the one planned laugh of the film and the set-up for the moral. Without the birds' reaction the question has no punchline and the answer at 121.22 arrives cold.
Fix: at أمسكت (119.68) cut or push to the branch framing already used at 121.5 (birds and cloud in the top half, man's head at the bottom). On بكما (120.48): crests drop to 0 in two frames, bird A lifts a foot and scratches his head, bird B looks down at her toes and shuffles sideways, both with `sq` about 0.92, staggered by 4-6 frames; a small sweat drop is fine. Hold to 121.2, then bird B raises her head and steps forward for أجاب (121.22). Keep the man's `?` and arms-out pose but let it start at 120.2 so it does not compete.

### 3. 79.5-83.0 s, MAJOR: "gently opened the cage" is not acted
What: the man kneels facing the camera, smiles at the viewer and holds both arms out to the sides like a goalkeeper (81.0-82.7). His hand touches the door only briefly at 80.5-80.7; the door swing is hidden behind his forearm; his eyes are never on the birds. The first bird pops out at 82.5 with no beat of hesitation.
Why: برفق is the emotional word of the paragraph. The tenderness is what earns the reward later.
Fix: three-quarter pose toward the cage, gaze on the birds throughout (`lookAt` the cage, then follow the first bird). وفتح (79.96): one hand steadies the cage top, the other takes the door with finger and thumb. القفص (81.02) to برفق (81.62): the door swings slowly over about 0.7 s, clearly visible on the side away from his arm; `tip` about -0.08, soft brow, one slow blink. Then he withdraws both hands low and leans back (not arms flung wide). The birds look at the open door, at him, at each other (0.4 s) before the first hop at 82.5. Tighten the camera for this beat so man and cage fill about 60 % of the panel height.

### 4. Whole film, worst at 0-24, 28-40, 54-62, 66-68, 71-79, 105-121, 151-160, 164.7-174.3 s, MAJOR: figures small above a large empty ground band
What: in the default wide framing the people are about 150-200 px tall on a 1200 px panel and the bottom 30-40 % of the panel is empty paving, soil or floor tiles. Examples: 30.0 (haggling), 67.2 (man and cage a small knot in the middle), 76.5 (road), 108.0-116.0 (the whole question played wide), 153.6 (hall, half the panel is floor), 169.6 (ending).
Why: on a phone the faces, where all the acting is, are about 40 px. The close shots in this film (25.5, 69.5, 92.3, 134.7) are far stronger than the wides; the wides are simply too loose.
Fix: keep the sets, change the camera. For dialogue two-shots (28.2-40.3, 54.7-62.5, 151.2-164.5) use a `twoShot` zoom of about 1.5-1.7x with the feet near the bottom edge of the panel, so the ground band is at most 12-15 % of the height. In the grove before 101.5 and during 105.6-121 frame from the grass line upward (the plan itself says to show the soil only from 101.5, at 114.0 and in S13). For the street, road and palace-front wides keep one establishing second, then push in. Check every label and balloon still clears the faces after the zoom.

### 5. 142.5-146.7 s, MAJOR: the prayer reads as a cheer
What: on شكر / ودعا the man stands with both fists raised beside his head, mouth open (144.5-146.2). It looks like flexing or celebrating over the gold. The plan called for a bow and palms up.
Why: the line is thanks and a prayer for the birds' safety. A victory pose makes him look greedy at the exact moment the story says he is grateful.
Fix: شكر (142.50): turn to the birds, hand on chest, a clear bow of head and torso (`tip` about 0.15), eyes up to the branch. ودعا (144.42): both forearms forward at chest height, palms open and up (`PERSON_HANDS` open), head tilted up, lids softly closed for about 0.6 s, small smile. The birds answer with crests at 1 and a nod before their loop at 145.7. Close the gold rays down to a soft glow from 142.5 so the jar does not upstage the thanks.

### 6. 160.5-162.1 s, MAJOR: the king walks into the gold rays and the man
What: when the king rises and steps down (161.0-162.0) his body overlaps the jar and the opaque gold rays cover his face; king, jar, rays and servant become one clump (see 161.5). The climax gesture is only clear from 162.2 when the rays vanish.
Why: احتفظ به is the last surprise. The viewer should see the king's kind face and his hand arriving on the jar, not a tangle.
Fix: fade the rays to a low glow by لكن (159.72), when the king raises his palm. The king stops one body width to the right of the jar, never behind it; servant left, jar centre, king right, with clear gaps. On احتفظ (162.04) he bends and slides the jar about 60 units toward the servant with one hand (visible travel, the servant's hands come up in surprise and he leans back), then an open palm toward him on لك (164.28). Also give the king the planned listening pose at 153.4 (chin on hand, eyes going to the jar at 154.5); at present he sits still for five seconds.

### 7. 164.7-174.3 s, MAJOR: the ending picture is weak for the last image of the film
What: the man stands exactly in front of the awning post, which runs behind him from cap to shoes like a pole through his body (169.2 onward). He is small, the memory cloud's birds are about 25 px, the real hoopoes cross behind the THE END card at 171.5-171.7, and from 172.5 the man's wave has stopped and he looks at the camera, not at the birds on the awning.
Why: "remembering the two birds" and the reunion of the two real birds with the man is the emotional close.
Fix: stop the man about 80 units left of the post, in clear wall. From يتذكر (169.02) push in to about 1.5x on the man and the upper right sky; make the cloud's birds twice as large. Route the real hoopoes below or beside the THE END card (or place the card top left, over the palace tower). Keep the wave going with a slower, smaller follow-through to the last frame, his gaze on the two birds, both birds with crests at 1 looking down at him. Bring THE END in at 171.6 as now.

### 8. 62.7-74.5 s, MINOR: the three reasons for not releasing them here are soft
What: (a) the planned finger stopping on the latch at 64.2-65.0 is not readable; he just holds the cage in front of his face. (b) The "middle of the market" wide at 66.5-68.0 is an empty street with one cart. (c) In the hunger close-up (68.2-70.7) the cage covers the man's mouth and the birds do not visibly sag. (d) The second hunter (72.5-74.0) is a small figure at the far left edge and the man never looks toward him.
Why: these four pictures are the argument of the sentence; each should land in under a second.
Fix: (a) lower the cage to chest height, raise one finger to the latch on يطلق (64.94), freeze, then pull the finger back with a small head shake. (b) Add two or three passers-by silhouettes or legs crossing in the foreground during 66.5-68.0 (library `person` with costume recolours), or drop the wide and stay closer. (c) Hold the cage beside his face, not over it; on جائعين (69.32) both birds droop (`sag` -0.5, `lid` 0.7, crest 0), one beak opens and closes. (d) On صياد (72.98) the man's eyes snap left first, then his head; bring the peeking hunter and net further into frame and about 30 % larger; then the hug and stride away.

### 9. 102.2-105.5 and 114.0-116.2 s, MINOR: the gold in the ground window looks like a honeycomb
What: from مليئة (103.88) the jar's cutaway fills with a regular yellow cell pattern that reads as a beehive or a corn cob, and the only label is JAR.
Why: this is the promise the whole second half pays off; it should say gold at a glance.
Fix: draw the fill as stacked round coins (overlapping discs with a darker rim, a few tilted) with two or three staggered glints from `goldGlow` at low strength, and change or add the label to GOLD! on بالذهب (104.72). Keep the man fully in frame at the left during the window (at 102.2-105.2 he is cut in half by the panel edge) or leave him out completely.

### 10. 126.3-126.5 s, MINOR (glitch): blue dotted rectangle on the fig trunk
What: for about four frames a hard-edged blue Ben-Day rectangle sits over the trunk at the right edge of the panel (see `extra/glitch.jpg`, frames 15-18). It is probably the planned passing cloud shadow, but it appears as a rectangular patch that pops on and off.
Why: it reads as a rendering error in the middle of the moral.
Fix: remove it, or draw the shadow as a soft, wide, low-alpha band that travels across the whole panel over about 1.5 s starting at القدر (125.20), clipped to the picture panel, with eased fade in and out.

### 11. 121.2-129.3 s, MINOR: the speaking hoopoe's own acting is small
What: the cloud does the work (the net fading at 126.5-127.5 and snapping back at 127.7 is good), but the bird that speaks is about 60 px and her "covers her eyes" on العيون (127.18) cannot be seen. The cloud is also still attached to the man by its tail while a bird is talking.
Why: the moral should feel spoken by the bird, with one clear gesture.
Fix: frame slightly tighter on the branch from 124.0 (birds about 1.4x their present size, man's head still at the bottom). On العيون (127.18) she lifts one wing fully across her face for 0.5 s, then peeks over it at واضحة (128.58); the other bird nods. Switch the cloud's tail bubbles toward the speaking bird from 121.2, or drop the tail for this passage.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- `groundWindow` with an inside-draw callback (the round see-through window on the buried jar) works very well and is reusable for any buried or hidden object.
- The coin counter that ticks 2, 1, 0 in the panel corner, tied to coins physically changing hands.
- The "overhear" pose: frozen mid-bend, one eye open (`wink`), with the speaker in a separate cut-in and lettering PSST...
- A heart that travels from one character to another as a thank-you (94.5-95.7).
- Weak flight against strong flight (sagging arcs at 83-89, a clean loop at 145.7-149) as a visible before and after.
- Rule to record in the guide: thought-cloud contents must be at least as large as the same object in the wide shot (about 150 units), and the default dialogue framing should keep the ground band under 15 % of the panel height.
