Fix: use the spacing already used from 121.5 (about 70 units apart, as at 125.9). ### B. 81.0-82.4 s: make the cage door a solid shape
The door is a thin yellow frame drawn over the bars, so its swing on القفص / برفق is nearly invisible (`extra/b_open.jpg`, `b_open2.jpg`). Fix: draw the door as a filled panel (paper or yellow fill, 3 bars, black outline at the same weight as the cage base) and show a dark opening where it was. The first bird should leave through that opening, to the right, not across the front of the bars toward his hand.
