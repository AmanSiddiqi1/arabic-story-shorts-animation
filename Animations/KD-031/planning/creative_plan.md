## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A poor servant spends his whole wages, two dinars, to keep a pair of caged hoopoes together and set them free. The birds repay him with a buried jar of gold, and even the king lets him keep it.

**Conflict.** It is inside the man: two coins, one meant for charity and one for food, against two birds that cannot be bought separately. The hunter is only a price, not a villain.

**Emotional arc.** Dull routine, a hopeful decision, a small careful plan, pity, a real sacrifice (the counter reaches DINARS: 0 at 61.02 and he smiles anyway), care on the long walk, the fragile flight, then wonder, a gentle laugh at the birds' expense, a quiet piece of wisdom, joy, and a second gift from the king. The film's running picture is **two**: two coins, two birds, two fingers held up at the open jar (140.5), two birds in the last sky.

**Surprises.** The birds talk; the ground opens like a window on the buried jar; the man's question turns the wise birds into two embarrassed ones; the king gives the gold back.

**Ending and moral.** Kindness given without counting comes back multiplied; and when destiny arrives, even obvious things can be hidden from the sharpest eyes. The last picture: the man with his jar, waving at two free hoopoes.

## 3. Character acting

- **The servant (man).** Open, modest, quick to feel. Default brow -0.2 and a small smile. His thinking is always shown by the eyes first (saccades with `gazePlan(…, 0.07)`), then the head. Big shapes use arm **angles** so they read on a phone: the coin held up (33.8), the point up and the point down (112.68, 114.0), the two fingers (140.5). Emotional keys: the frozen chin hand at 46.98; fear at 48.32 (brow -0.8, `sq` 0.94, `tremble`); compassion at 54.66 (`wet`, long blink, `breathe`); gentleness at 81.62 (slow hands, `tip` -0.08, no sudden move); astonishment at 109.36 (`anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash`, no blink for 1 s); the single look to camera at 134.58.
- **The hoopoes.** The crest is their face: 0 = weak or caught out, 0.3-0.5 = interest, 1 = thanks, joy, the moral. In the cage: `drop`, `lid` 0.7, `sag` -0.5, `tremble`, one propping the other. They never move in unison: hoopoeB follows hoopoeA by 3-6 frames (`stagger`), except in S12 where she leads. Talking is a beak flutter on the speaker only; the listener tilts `headRot`. Embarrassment at 120.48: one scratches his head with a foot, the other studies her toes.
- **Weak flight against strong flight.** S9: frantic flaps, a drop to 20 units above the grass, three sagging arcs, an overshoot on landing. S14 and S16: one clean loop, level flight. The difference is the rescue made visible.
- **The hunter.** Sly, not cruel: half lids, brow -0.4, looks the customer up and down (29.92), holds still before refusing (37.3), bites a coin (58.68).
- **The rich master.** Three poses only: arms folded, surprised palm, back turned.
- **The king.** Slow and kind. Chin on hand while listening (153.38), a calm stop palm (159.72), rises with anticipation (160.54), bends to the man (161.16). He never frowns.
- **Listeners react.** The birds watch the haggling (38.66); the man overhears with one eye (`wink`, 100.22); the birds lean over the dig (132.3); the king's eyes go to the real jar when its icon appears (154.54).

- **Humour.** The hunter's coin bite; the birds' heads following the haggling like a tennis match; the wise birds caught out by a simple question; the man frozen mid-bend with one eye open; two fingers held up beside a jar of hundreds.

- **Surprise.** The ground window (102.24); CLINK! with the look to camera (134.58); the rays from the jar (138.4); the king sliding the jar back (162.04).
- **Emotion.** The heart between the caged pair and its cracked twin in the cloud (50.68, 52.4); DINARS: 0 followed by a smile (61.02-61.98); the slow door (81.02); the crests fanned in thanks (91.98); the last wave.
- **The moral as a picture.** In the cloud the bold net fades to a faint outline while the tiny birds walk on, and the hoopoe covers her own eyes (126.46-127.86). No extra words are needed.
