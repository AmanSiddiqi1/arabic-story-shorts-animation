# AN-151 The Illiterate Who Set Up for a Schoolmaster: creative plan

Film length 173.53 s (MP3 169.53 s + 4.0 s THE END). 17 scenes, 191 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is in `creative_plan.json`.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A mosque servant who cannot read puts on an enormous turban, opens a school, and bluffs his way through a letter he holds upside down.

**Conflict.** Appearance against knowledge. The turban does all the work; the man under it knows nothing and must never be found out.

**Spine (beats).**
1. 0-9 s, setup: the servant cannot read (3.38) but is good at fooling people (7.42, wink to camera).
2. 9-21 s, inciting: he opens a school (11.12), hangs tablets, one upside down, and crowns himself with the big turban (19.12).
3. 21-43 s, rising: parents judge by the turban (24.32) and send their children; he only says WRITE and READ while the pupils teach one another (38.04).
4. 43-65 s, turn and suspense: a woman brings a letter (47.00); he tries to flee and cannot (55.24); he holds the page upside down (62.58).
5. 65-86 s, climax of the lie: his worried head-shaking makes her think her husband is dead (72.48); he nods yes to mourning (80.44) without knowing one word.
6. 86-118 s, the truth: she goes home weeping; the neighbour reads the letter properly: well, back in ten days, a quilt and a candle snuffer (111.76 to 117.38).
7. 118-149 s, confrontation: furious, she marches back (118.08); he will not confess and invents the quilt and snuffer story (133.12 to 143.42).
8. 149-169 s, surprise and resolution: she accepts it (151.20) and leaves; he stays under his turban while the children teach themselves (165.82).

**Surprises.** The turban jam (52.92), the upside-down page (62.58), the absurd told picture (138.30), her acceptance (151.20).

**Emotional arc.** Amusement, dread on the woman's behalf, real grief (short), relief, anger, disbelief, and a dry shrug at the end.

**Ending and moral.** He is not punished. The film says it with pictures: the counter LESSONS TAUGHT: 0, a pupil doing the teaching, a child quietly turning the upside-down tablet the right way round, and the turban sliding over the sleeper's eyes. A big turban is not knowledge.

## 3. Character acting

- **Impostor.** Two faces. Mask on: lid 0.4 to 0.5, chin up, slow gestures (`PERSON_HANDS.chin`, `pointUp`, `chest`), the wise nod. Mask off, only when no adult looks: darting saccades (`gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`), gulp, sweat drop, the look to camera (`gaze [0, 0.05]`) at 5.94, 84.00, 134.04, 165.82. The peek (`wink`) at 42.60, 68.10, 146.38. Anticipation before every big move (`anticipate`), `landSquash` under the turban at 19.12, `fallY` + `impactSquash` + `rebound` at 55.24, deflation `sq 0.93` at 154.90.
- **The turban** is a second actor: it lags every head move by 2 to 4 frames (`wob`), slides over an eye when he is careless (`slump`), hops at the door bang (`hopY`), and ends over both eyes.
- **Woman.** Clear readable states, each with a settle: hopeful (brow -0.3, smile), frightened (eyes wide, `cheeks`, `tremble`), grief (`wet`, tear arcs, head back, `tip -0.1`), stunned relief (lid opening, hiccup), fury (brow +1, `hipsHands`, fast `walk` with `footfalls` dust), then the easy "of course" nod. Her gaze always has a target: the letter, his face, the cloud.
- **Pupils.** The audience inside the film. Four personalities (A eager, B clever, C nervous, D slow and kind), four blink seeds, reactions with `stagger`. Heads turn like a tennis crowd in S14; arms folded at 127.46; jaws drop one by one at 152.10.
- **Neighbour.** Calm, kind, unhurried; `cupEar`, `palmsUp`, `reach`. His reading (finger sliding right to left, page upright) is the visual opposite of the impostor's.
- **Listeners always react.** In every two-shot the one who is not speaking has a gaze target, blinks and a small response (lean, nod, brow change).

## 4. Camera and composition

- Establishing wides for each new place (S1, S3, S10), two-shots built with `twoShot` for every exchange (S7 to S9, S14), push-ins on decisions (9.28 idea, 19.94 turban, 81.84 "not one word", 132.0 the invention), one insert (the upside-down page, 62.58) and one pull-out for the ending.
- The street is a 2400 strip tracked with `camX`: the woman's sad walk goes left (S10), her angry march goes right (S13), so direction carries the mood.
- Faces and key props stay above world y 1000. Clouds and the told picture sit in the upper wall space (y 250 to 520) with the camera tilted to include them; balloons never cover a face or the turban.
- Schoolroom staging is constant so the audience learns it: door left, pupils centre-left, lesson stand, teacher right on his cushion, window far right, tablets across the wall with No. 4 upside down.
- Panel-only shake for the turban jam and the door slam.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour.** The upside-down motif in three sizes: a wall tablet (16.88), the letter (62.58, 147.42), and a pupil turning his own tablet over to "read like the teacher" (81.84). The turban as a liability. The label CAN'T READ! swatted away at 131.08. Counters LESSONS TAUGHT: 0 and WORDS READ: 0.
- **Suspense.** The tiptoe to the window while the pupils' eyes follow; the held, eyes-closed beat before the nod (79.48); the still room at 128.14 where only his pupils move.
- **Dramatic irony.** We see the page is upside down while she studies his face for the news.
- **Emotion.** Her grief is played straight but short: one tear at 72.48, the wail after YES!, the children crying out of step. Relief arrives through the children first (they stop crying one by one at 97.42).
- **Surprise.** OH! I SEE. and the pupils' look to camera.
- **Contrast.** The neighbour's ordinary small turban and correct reading against the giant turban and the inverted page.

- Start from `Library/template/`; cue with `w(p, n)`; set `LIFE.t = t` and add `blink(t, seed)` wherever a `lid` is set by hand.

- Seated figures: `sit: 1, seat: 'floor'` for teacher and pupils; the doorstep uses `seat: 'bench'`.
- Walks: position functions through `walk` and `footfalls` (child strides shorter); the march in S13 covers x 420 to 1450 between 121.0 and 124.1 with the camera leading by 150.

- Window jam: draw the impostor clipped at the opening (even-odd clip), turban top outside, legs with `swing(t, 52.92, 0.5)`.
