## Polish notes (optional, do not trigger v3)

### 1. 124.5 to 128.5 s. The confrontation is crowded; his face is behind her letter
What: when the woman storms in she stops shoulder to shoulder with the seated teacher. Her head is tucked under the rim of his turban, and the letter she thrusts out covers the left half of his face (`extra/m7.jpg` right, `m8.jpg` left). His reaction to WHY?! (125.66) is hard to see until he stands at 129.0. This staging predates v2.
Fix: stop her about 70 world px further left (her outstretched hand ends at his shoulder, not his face), and hold the letter at her chest height with the top edge below his chin. Then his gulp and darting eyes at 126.7 to 128.1 read.

### 2. 152.4 to 154.2 s. An unexplained fifth child at the door; the exit is very fast
What: one of the woman's own children (white cap, yellow dress) stands in the doorway from about 152.4. In the pupils' close shot it is sliced by the left panel edge (152.5 to 152.9), and in the wide it overlaps pupil A (`extra/m9.jpg` middle and right). The woman then crosses from the stand to the door, about half the panel, in roughly 0.4 s (153.6 to 154.0, `extra/m12.jpg`).
Fix: either remove the child from the room, or stand it fully inside the doorway (clear of pupil A, about 60 px further left) and keep it out of the 151.95 to 152.9 close shot. Start her walk at 153.22 ("وانصرفت") rather than about 153.6 so the same distance takes 0.8 s, with `walk` and `footfalls`.

### 3. 138.3 to 144.5 s. Told picture: the husband's head nearly disappears behind the quilt roll
What: once the quilt is rolled, only a sliver of white turban shows behind the blue end cap (`extra/m8.jpg` middle). "Dead, wrapped in the quilt" reads as a striped roll and nothing else.
Fix: let the head and turban stick out 35 to 40 px past the left end of the roll (closed eyes visible), and put the snuffer and candle 20 px further left so they do not sit on the head.

### 4. 65.0 to 73.2 s. The letter at chest height is as big as his body
What: the lowered sheet runs from his collar to the floor and hides arms, torso and legs (`extra/m4.jpg`). The shake reads, but he looks like a head behind a board.
Fix: scale the held sheet to about 70 percent in this shot (top edge still 30 to 50 px under the chin) so the shoulders and the hand going to the forehead are visible from the start.

### 5. 7.9 to 8.3 s. The passer-by is pushed out by the push-in
What: the camera pushes in on the servant as the passer-by nods, so the passer-by is cut by the right edge at 8.0 and gone at 8.2 (`extra/m1.jpg`). The "fooled" reaction is brief.
Fix: delay the push-in to 8.15, after he has turned away, or keep his whole figure inside the frame until then.

### 6. 33.7 to 36.2 s. The stick is a long pole from off screen
What: after the pan the teacher is out of frame, so the stick enters from the right edge and crosses pupil B's chest and tablet; the READ! tail points off the panel (`extra/m1.jpg` right).
Fix: stop the pan about 60 px earlier so the teacher's hand and the turban rim stay in frame, and end the stick tip 20 px short of B's tablet.

### 7. 106.8 to 118.0 s. Letter picture: empty lower third
What: after the 100 px raise the action sits on a horizon line in the middle and the bottom 30 percent of the page is blank sand. Cosmetic.
Fix: none needed; if touched, scale the letter world up about 1.15 around the gifts from 113.2.
