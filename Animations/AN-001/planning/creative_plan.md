# AN-001 The Bull and the Ass: creative plan

Film length 179.25 s (narration 176.25 s + 3.0 s THE END). 19 scenes, 143 actions, all times in seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard with every action, cue word, pose field and camera key is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A kind donkey teaches an overworked bull to play sick. The merchant, who understands animals, sends the donkey to plough instead. The donkey has to scare the bull back to work with a lie.

**Conflict.** Comfort against labour, and cleverness that lands on its owner. Nobody is a villain: the bull is simple and sincere, the donkey kind but sly, the merchant amused, the worker baffled.

## 3. Character acting

- **Bull**: slow, heavy, honest. Eyes lead, head follows 4 frames later, ears and tail arrive last. Tired = `sag` (head below the shoulders, swayed back). Sick act is always too big (`tongue`, `legUp`). Happy = springy `sq` 1.05 steps, `tailUp`. Fear = `tremble` growing 2 to 5 px, `eyes:'wide'`, `noBlink`, nose ring rattling late on `swing`.
- **Donkey**: all in the ears (`earL`, `earR` independent, 3-frame lag) and the lids. Comfort = `lid` 0.35-0.5, sideways `chew`. Thinking = saccades with `gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`, one ear up and one down. **Mask on / mask off**: worried `brow -0.7` while the bull looks, `mouth:'smirk'` the moment he looks away (147.1, 148.2). Exhausted = `sag` 1, two-stage collapse, `breathe` amplitude 5-6.
- **Merchant**: calm and amused; never hurries. Gestures from `PERSON_HANDS`: `hipsHands`, `cupEar`, `moustache`, `pointUp`, `pointFwd`. The film's first look to the lens and a `wink` at 88.6.
- **Worker**: the baffled straight man. Double take (71.6), heave with slipping feet, `palmsUp` defeat, `shield` with the yoke at 161.7, empty hands left in the air at 162.8.
- **Listeners always react**: the donkey's guilty glance at his trough (30.4), the real bull's eyes following the food in the cloud (58.5), the bull's grin behind the donkey (96.9), the donkey's one-eye check (159.0).
- Anticipation and settle on every big move: `anticipate` before the pull (6.2), the spring-up (155.5) and the yawn; `settleTo`, `impactSquash`, `landSquash` on stops and flops. Blinks through `LIFE.t`; two looks to camera only (88.6 merchant, 151.9 donkey).

## 4. Camera and composition

- Panel shows 1000 x 1230 world units at zoom 1; minimum zoom 1.0. Faces stay between y 480 and 820; speech and thought pictures live in the band y 300-560 above the post; nothing important below y 950.
- **Mirrors.** S4 and S13 share one two-shot `{x:500, y:700, zoom:1.3}` and the lantern, roles swapped. S2 and S10 share the field path, plough and worker, animals swapped. S7 and S18 share the doorway with opposite outcomes. The audience should feel the swap without a caption.
- Push-ins on decisions: the bull's "I" (32.0), the donkey's idea (37-45), the merchant (82.8), the donkey's second idea (123-132).
- One whip pan in the film (95.8), along the merchant's pointing arm. One hard punch-in (143.12). One long static (S7) so the physical comedy reads.
- Reveal by pull-out at 172.4: the window with the far bull appears above the donkey.
- Time: one card (NEXT MORNING... at 67.2); the other time changes are sky sweeps with `dayCycle` (11.1, 22.6, 102.3, 110.2, 152.9).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Dramatic irony, planted early.** The merchant's turban slides into the window at 56.7 and he cups his ear at 61.3 while the animals plot beneath him. The narration reveals it only at 82.8-89.9, where an inset circle replays the moment. The audience is ahead of the animals for 25 seconds.
- **The plan is the picture.** In S6 a little bull acts each instruction inside the cloud on its word, so the plan is clear without reading.
- **Comic contrast of troughs**: the donkey's heaped yellow one against the bull's thin straw, side by side from the first shot; the bull licking his empty at 159.96 closes the gag.
- **The peek** (73.9) and **suspense by stillness** (119.0: only the donkey's pupils move while the bull thanks him).
- **The punch line beat** at 96.2-97.4: freeze, ears up, barley falls, ears drop one by one, hoof to chest.
- **Softened threat**: the butcher is only a shop sign with a cleaver inside a speech picture.
- **Warm ending**: respect in the ear salute, no gloating, no caption.

- Both leads are new characters; test their key expressions at phone size before staging (sick act, smug, shocked, exhausted, smirk).

- S6 runs 22 s in one set-up; it works only if the cloud changes on every listed word and the merchant's arrival is clearly visible.
- The time-lapse has 2.5 s: three crossings, the sun arc and the counter, nothing more.
- Owner questions (six, in `owner_questions`): the early window eavesdrop, the butcher as an icon only, no hitting of the bull, the invented lettering list, the ending without a moral caption, one LATER card.
