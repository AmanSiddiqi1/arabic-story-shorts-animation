## Fixes for v3 (one final pass, five items, nothing else)

### 1. 99.2 to 101.5 (S11 wide, outline wiped). MAJOR. The X is on the caliph
- What: in the far view the red dashed rectangle sits at the pyramid's lower-left corner exactly where the caliph and the camel stand, so it frames them, and the red X at 100.62 is centred on the caliph's head.
- Why: this is the sentence "he gave up the idea of tearing down the pyramids". The picture must say the plan is cancelled, not the man.
- Fix (keep the peel-off chips, they are good):
  - For the wide shot only (99.2 to 101.6), no figure may overlap the rectangle. Keep the two horses left of its left edge and place the caliph and the camel right of its right edge, with at least 30 world units of clear wall on both sides; the caliph stands just right of it, facing it. If moving them is awkward against the close shot before it, the alternative is to draw the rectangle one block-course higher on the pyramid face so its bottom edge is above every head.
  - Draw the outline at full opacity on ترك 99.60 and hold it untouched for at least 0.6 s so it is recognised as the plan from 26.9.
  - On هدم 100.62 the caliph makes one clear arm sweep toward it (arm starts raised, sweeps across over 0.35 s); the chips peel in the direction of the sweep; the red X pops at the centre of the rectangle on bare stone, 1.3 times its present size, and holds 0.5 s before fading.
  - Check stills at 100.0 and 100.9: no red line crosses a face.

### 2. 72.7 to 74.2 (S9 opening). The worker is cut in half by the red cloth
- What: the worker who brings the chest walks behind the red slab under the scale; his head and chest show above it and his feet below it (`m7.jpg`, frames 72.8, 73.4, 74.1). The same fault was in v1 and was missed in round 1.
- Why: it is a visible drawing-order fault at the start of the climax scene.
- Fix: draw the worker after the red cloth so he walks in front of it, carrying the chest at waist height; he sets it down in front of the cloth (contact on بحساب 73.3), one step back, exits right. Alternatively give the slab visible table legs and keep him fully behind it with no feet showing. The small dark green chest is hard to see on sand; draw it in the same red and yellow as the hoard chest in S8 so it is recognised as the find.

### 3. 96.1 to 97.2 (S10 end, the empty chest). The chest hides the vizier's face
- What: the chest is held upright in front of his head; one eye peeks out beside it (`m4.jpg` 96.6). The upturned chest, the shakes and the apologetic smile cannot be seen; only the falling coin survives.
- Fix: hold the chest above the turban at full arm's length (its bottom edge at least 20 px above the turban top), rotated 180 degrees with the lid hanging open; two shakes of about 8 degrees on المكلفة 96.12 and 96.45; one coin drops from the open mouth past his face and spins flat on the sand; his eyes follow the coin down, then the apologetic smile toward the caliph (screen left). Face fully visible throughout.

### 4. 36.5 to 41.0 (S5, the bent pick). Two workers stacked on each other
- What: w2 (red cap, white tunic) stands directly in front of w3 (brown apron) from 33.0, so their bodies, arms and both picks overlap; the hooked pick lies across w3's face (`m2.jpg` 38.7).
- Fix: separate them by at least 70 world units (for example w3 one step left toward the X, w2 where he is, still whole inside the right edge; shift the camera right by up to 20 units if needed). The hooked pick is held at w2's eye level beside his own head on clear wall, not over anyone's face, for the look at it (37.4) and the look to the lens (38.6 to 38.93).

### 5. Edge and overlap clean-up (three places, camera or position only)
- 19.5 to 21.6: move the "?" onto bare block face right of the green villager (no overlap with any figure, at least 60 px inside the right edge); alternatively shift the camera about 40 units right to make the room. The caliph's elbow must not touch the left border.
- 50.2 to 53.5: the push to zoom 1.1 cuts the caliph in half at the left edge. Centre the push about 40 world units further left, or keep zoom 1.0, so he is whole (he is the one paying).
- 24.7 to 26.2: the white X on the dig block is half outside the right edge. Shift the camera right or end the arrow and X about 60 px inside the panel.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Interior insert set for a cutaway:** wide section view for scale, then a hard cut to a separate interior set with the actors at 1.4 scale. Far better than pushing the camera into a set drawn for zoom 1.0. Worth a guide entry and a generic `tunnelInterior` set.
- **Equal piles plus a bar equals sign** (`m3.jpg` 83.5): ready for the Library as a comparison pattern.
- **Head-down sand wipe with seeded grains** (S16): ready as `sandBlow(ctx, drawFigure, p, {grains, wind})`.
- **Rule for plan outlines, X marks and question marks:** a symbol that comments on an object must sit on clear background, never across a character, because a red X or "?" over a face is read as a statement about that person. Add to the guide beside the label rules.
- **Layer check for carried props:** anyone who walks past furniture must be wholly in front of it or wholly behind it; add "feet under a table top" to the implcheck still list.
