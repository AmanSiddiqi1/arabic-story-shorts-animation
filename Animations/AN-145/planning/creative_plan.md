# AN-145 Al-Maamun and the Pyramids of Egypt: creative plan

Film length 177.86 s (MP3 173.86 s + THE END held 4.0 s). 17 scenes, 165 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (authoritative for times, coordinates and asset dimensions).

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A caliph digs into a pyramid for treasure, spends a fortune on a tiny tunnel, and finds gold worth exactly what the digging cost.

**Conflict.** One proud man and his pay chest against a mountain of fitted stone. The stone never fights back; it simply does not move, and the bill grows.

**Beats.**
1. Setup (2.62, 6.96): he rides into Egypt; a camera tilt reveals the pyramids; he measures himself against one block.
2. Inciting (16.24): locals whisper of treasure inside; curiosity takes hold (push-in at 18.82).
3. Rising (21.80 to 43.20): OPEN IT!, then the red dashed outline of "a large part"; workers gather; picks bounce and bend.
4. Cost (43.20 to 53.68): days wheel overhead in a locked shot while the pay chest empties; SPENT counts to 100.
5. Low point (53.68 to 65.06): cutaway shows how tiny the tunnel is in the mass; nothing there. LATER...
6. Turn (66.00 to 72.30): the wall breaks in the one real pause of the narration; gold light.
7. Surprise (72.30 to 87.56): the weighing. Two counters stop on the same number; the beam settles dead level on 82.32; EXACTLY! on 84.26.
8. Climax (87.56 to 97.28): he understands. PROFIT 0. One look at the lens.
9. Resolution (97.28 to 107.66): he takes the find, wipes the demolition outline away, stops the work, rides off.
10. The tale's descriptions (107.66 to 152.62): wonder of the world, exact stones, iron and lead, unknown chambers, the storytellers' jewels, weapons, books and unseen guardians, then the honest stamp: LEGEND.
11. Moral (152.62 to end): kings and builders blow away as sand; the pyramids stay; a small rider bows and leaves.

**Emotional arc.** Awe, appetite, impatience, worry, brief joy, comic deflation, acceptance, then a calm widening of time. **Ending:** dusk, the tunnel scar as a pinprick, THE END at 173.66 held to 177.86. **Moral:** monuments outlast those who covet them; wisdom is knowing when to stop.

## 3. Character acting

- **Caliph** (person `costume: 'caliph'`, age 0.35). Default: chest out, `PERSON_HANDS.hipsHands`. Awe = `eyes: 'wide'`, `brow -0.6`, `tip -0.06` leaning back, one hand holding his turban. Appetite = `PERSON_HANDS.chin`, `lid 0.3`, slow smile. Command = `anticipate` dip then `pointFwd`; the big arm sweep uses arm angles. Impatience = `folded` arms with a foot tap. At the scale his head follows the beam with `lookAt`; `noBlink` 80.16 to 83.7 and six frames of total stillness at 82.32 (suspense by stillness). Deflation at 91.74: gaze down, `lid 0.4`, `sq 0.96`, then the one lens stare of the film at 94.34 (`gaze [0, 0.05]`). Ending: `PERSON_HANDS.chest`, head drop, eyes closed 8 frames.
- **Vizier** (`costume: 'vizier'`, age 0.6): the worried conscience and the ledger. Eyes always travel caliph, object, ledger (`gazePlan`). Raps the stone and winces (S2), drops his pen at the outline (S4), writes faster and faster (S6), lifts his hands off the ledger at EXACTLY!, upturns the empty chest with an apologetic smile (S10).
- **Workers**: w1 eager (leads, hops, last to drop his tool), w3 foreman (steady, carries the torch, finds the hoard), w2 tired (`lid 0.3`, late by 6 to 8 frames, first to sit, first to smile at STOP!). Use `stagger(i)` everywhere. Strike = crouch (`sq 0.92`), lift 0.3 s, whip, contact, `rebound`, `tremble` up the arms. Fatigue = doubled cycle time, `breathe` at twice the amplitude, forearm across the brow.
- **Gossip and w1** (S3): `cupMouth`, nod, thumb over the shoulder, `hush`, eyes sweeping left and right.
- **Storyteller and children**: he performs with his hands (`pointUp`, one-hand `cupMouth`, `armsOut` with `tremble`), then sits back and laughs. kid1 believes everything (wide eyes, hides, relief hop with `landSquash`); kid2 is the sceptic (one brow up, leans in only for the books, nods at NOT PROVEN FACT).
- **Ancient king and builders**: no fear when they turn to sand; they keep looking up at the pyramid. The circlet's damped roll is the follow-through.
- **Animals**: horse ears forward on halts, tail swish, `lurch` on the rider; camel chews with `lid 0.4`, rises rear first (`kneel 1..0`).
- **Secondary motion**: dust on footfalls, coins on arcs, the torch disc flicker, the pen, the beam's damped `swing`, the tag zero rolling flat.

## 4. Camera and composition

- Three views of one place: **far** (scale and time), **face** (people and work, world zoom 1.0 fits the panel exactly) and **section** (the truth inside). Cuts between them are motivated: face to section is a match cut on the dig block at 53.68; section to face at 72.30 follows the chest out.
- Reveal by tilt at 5.78 to 7.9; a second tilt up the courses at 11.6. Push-ins on decisions: 18.8 (curiosity), 79.1 to 82.3 (the beam), 87.56 to 93.4 (the realisation). Pull-outs for humility: 41.0 (the tiny dent), 99.6 (the whole pyramid over the scar), the final slow 4 percent ease.
- S6 is locked on purpose so the time-lapse reads. S16 has only a slow drift.
- Faces stay above y 900; all tags, counters and the progress gauge are in panel space above y 420 and never over a face. The story cloud (620 x 400 at (560, 400)) leaves the storyteller and children clear below it.
- Small figures: in the section view keep zoom at 2.4 or more when the workers act. In the far view figures are for scale; the final bow is supported by the arrow and the coin balloon.
- Panel-only shake on the three strike contacts in S5 only.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour**: the block as tall as the caliph; the vizier losing count of the blocks; the pick that curls into a hook and the worker's look to the lens; a progress gauge at 3 percent; the tennis-match head at the scale; each man pressing a pan in hope; PROFIT 0 rolling flat like a coin; the last coin from the upturned chest; the blade that springs out of the joint.
- **Suspense**: the rocking beam narrowing over four seconds with a slow push-in, then stillness; the torch sweep over bare stone; pale eyes opening in the cloud.
- **Surprise**: the pick going through the wall in the only pause of the narration (66.68 to 67.20), with the single mid-film sound; the two counters landing on the same number.
- **Emotion**: the last look up before leaving (respect replaces greed); the builders watching their work as they blow away; the bow at the scar.
- **Honesty beat**: the stamp LEGEND and the cloud turning to outlines make the script's disclaimer a picture, with the solid real pyramid left in frame.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus

- Start from `Library/template/`; word cues with `w(p, n)` or the literal times in the JSON. `LIFE.t = t` every frame.
- Build and still-check `pyramidSite` first, one still per view with a person for scale. Bake static layers with `bake()`; keep `dig`, `outline`, `len`, `breach`, `legend`, `scar` live.
- Walks: `walk(xf, t)` with `footfalls` and `dustBurst` for every entrance; riders with `rideOn(ctx, 'horse', {...walk(xf, t, {stride: GAIT.horse * s}), y, s}, {costume})`; halts with `lurch`.
- Strikes: one `strikeCycle(t, hits)` giving `aL`, `aR` and tool rotation; contacts at 34.76, 35.44, 36.50; the tool is drawn at `P.hands`. Contact effects: spark lines, chips, `tremble`, panel shake. The block itself never reacts.
- Time-lapse: `skyCycle(ctx, kfn(t, [[43.2, 0.2], [46.4, 3.2]]) % 1)` and a shadow polygon from the same u. The accelerating version in S16 runs u from 0.02 to about 6 between 160.22 and 163.84, then eases to 0.45.
- Scale: `tilt` keys are in the JSON notes; use `swing` for the decay and `settleTo` for the stop. Counters with `S.counter` in panel space landing at 81.52 to 82.3.

- Sand: draw the figure offscreen, tint, wipe from the head down, seeded dots from `K.rng` by index so each frame is a pure function of t.
- Sound: three effects only (66.94 chime, 173.66 chime, 175.0 whoosh), `master: 0.6`.
- Subtitles: build the chunk files from the `subtitles` array; verify against the script by program.
