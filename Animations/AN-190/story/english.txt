Hasib Karim al-Din inherited five pages from his learned father Daniel but struggled with study and work. His mother equipped him to become a woodcutter.

A storm drove them into a cave. Hasib discovered a hidden cistern of honey and guarded it while his companions sold the honey.

Greedy for the money, the woodcutters abandoned Hasib in the empty cistern. He widened a crack and followed a hidden passage into a luminous chamber.

Hasib saw a golden throne and countless serpents. Their queen reassured him, offered fruit, and told far-ranging tales, including Bulukiya’s adventures.

The Queen allowed him home after he promised never to enter a public bath. He reunited with his family and reconciled with the frightened woodcutters.

A bathhouse keeper pressed Hasib inside against his wishes. Vizier Shamhur seized him and, through violence, forced him to reveal the Queen’s hiding place to heal King Karazdan.

The Queen appeared and taught Hasib how to survive the vizier’s trick. The vizier killed her; he drank a poisonous preparation and died, while Hasib drank another and gained wondrous knowledge.

Following the Queen’s instructions, Hasib healed the king and became chief vizier. His success remained shadowed by the death of the creature who had saved him.
