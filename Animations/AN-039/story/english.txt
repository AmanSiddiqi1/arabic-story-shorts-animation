A poor farmer and his wife had a sick friend who needed treatment. A physician told them sesame could help him, so the farmer bought some and brought it home.

The wife hulled the sesame and spread it out to dry. While she was busy, a female mongoose carried some into her burrow. She returned repeatedly until much of the sesame was gone.

The wife noticed the loss and sat watching with a stick. She saw the mongoose approach, but it acted cleverly: it brought some seeds from its burrow and returned them in full view of the woman.

The wife thought the mongoose was returning food rather than stealing it. She waited for the real thief. Meanwhile the mongoose looked for another animal it could send into danger in its place.

It found a mouse living nearby and said, “There is plenty of delicious sesame, more than anyone needs. Come take your share. My family has eaten its fill.”

The mouse desired the food. It did not ask why the mongoose offered it so freely or notice the woman sitting beside the sesame. It hurried toward the seeds, thinking only of the meal ahead.

As soon as the mouse reached the food, the woman raised her stick and struck its head. The mouse died immediately. The wife believed she had caught the animal stealing the sesame all along.

The mongoose watched from a distance. It had stolen most of the seeds but escaped by making the mouse appear guilty. The mouse was dead, while the mongoose returned safely to its burrow.
