# AN-039 The Mouse and the Ichneumon: creative plan

Film length 170.59 s of narration plus a 3.0 s THE END hold (173.59 s). All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time comes from `inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard (16 scenes, 170 actions, subtitle chunks) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction behind it.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A mongoose who has been stealing a farmer's sesame fools the wife by returning a few seeds, then talks a greedy mouse into walking under the stick in her place.

**Conflict.** The wife wants the thief. The mongoose is the thief and needs someone else to be caught. The mouse wants a free meal and asks no questions.

**Emotional arc.** Warm and a little funny (the poor couple), playful (the thefts), tense (the stick appears), delight at the trick (seeds returned), unease (the sweet talk), dread (the audience sees the woman, the mouse does not), a hard stop (the blow), bitter irony (the wife's pride), cold calm (the mongoose leaves).

**Ending and moral.** The script states no moral, so none is written on screen. The last pictures say it: the small still mouse on the mat, then the mongoose sliding into her burrow. A gift offered too easily hides a trap.

## 3. Character acting

- **Mongoose (the lead).** Three clear gaits so her intent always reads: the low *sneak* when stealing (sq 0.85, tail low, eyes flicking to the window), the upright *honest march* when returning seeds (rear 0.6, chin up, tail high, eyes on the woman to be sure she watches), and the unhurried trot of the ending. Her sentinel pose (`rear` 1) is her thinking and checking pose. **Mask on, mask off:** innocent wide eyes whenever she is observed; a 6 to 10 frame smirk only when nobody in the scene can see her face (62.82, 74.94, 103.30). At the end she does not gloat: one long unsmiling look at the mouse (160.88), one look back (168.82).
- **Wife.** A decisive body: double take (42.26), a hard sit with `landSquash` (45.56), then long stillness in which only the eyes work (`gazePlan` with 0.07 s snaps). Her brow tells her belief: angry V while guarding, raised when the seeds come back (61.66), warm half-lid when she waves (70.46), proud after the hit (142.82). The strike uses arm angles (about [2.45, 2.85]) so the raised stick reads at phone size.
- **Mouse.** All appetite: nose and ears lead every move, tail curls tight with desire (107.64), gaze locked on food and never on a face. Her only doubt is the question mark she shakes off (110.82). The look to camera at 132.24 (hold, realise, lens stare, gulp) is the one moment she understands. After 136.06 she never moves again.
- **Farmer.** Poverty through one gag (the empty pockets) and one beat of feeling (looking at his last coin with lid 0.4 before handing it over, 15.56).
- **Listeners react.** In S2 all pupils jump to the balloon; in S10 the mouse nods on the stressed words and steps closer three times (96.76, 98.46, 99.4), so the pull of the lie is visible as distance closing.
- **Secondary motion.** Mongoose tail follows through after every stop (`swing`); the sack bobs a frame late on each step; the bib flips on the skid; seeds hop on the hit (`kick`).

## 4. Camera and composition

- Faces and key action stay in the upper part of the panel; small animals are shot at zoom 1.4 or more when alone (they are about 95 and 55 units high).
- **Eye-line pans.** At 50.50 the camera follows the wife's glance to find the mongoose. At 115.54 it pulls out from the mouse to find the woman: the audience sees what the mouse does not.
- **Three geography shots** make the trap readable: 87.20 (the hole is close to the yard), 116.96 (the woman by the sesame), 151.22 (the mongoose far from the scene).
- **Push-ins on decisions:** the mongoose's idea (55.16 to 56.26), the mouse's look to camera (132.24), the last push on the mouse (164.52).
- **Two-shots** via `twoShot` for wife and mongoose across the mat (53.1 to 64.7) and for the con (S10).
- The strike is wide (zoom 1.3) so the whole arc of the stick is in frame; shake is panel-only. The dead mouse is then held in a still frame.
- One LATER... card (19.7 to 20.8) covers the walk home and the cut to the yard. Sky goes to the dusk preset for the ending.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the empty pockets; the thefts getting faster and more careless with a counter 1, 2, 3; the freeze when the silhouette passes the window; the tortoise that will not be recruited; the balloon heap swelling until it presses the balloon's edge; the bib tied on the run.
- **Suspense:** eyes opening in the bush (29.88); the wife's stillness with a stick; the shadow sliding over the eating mouse; the stick held at the top from 132.24 to 134.95.
- **Surprise:** the mongoose walking openly to the mat and giving seeds back.
- **Dramatic irony:** the wife scanning every direction except the mongoose (73.28); the mouse's dream cloud placed exactly over the woman (118.82); the wife's replay cloud with the wrong animal (145.44) answered by the true replay in the inset (155.70) and the hoard under the burrow (157.06).
- **Emotion:** the single seed rolling from the mouse's paw; dusk; no music, no lettering over the body.
- **Lettering (16):** SESAME, DOCTOR, MONGOOSE, MOUSE, STICK, SNIFF!, HMM?, LESS!, HELPER!, PSST!, YUM!, DANGER, THWACK!, GOT YOU!, REAL THIEF, GUILTY?, plus THE END. No dashes.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- **Violence.** The hit is hidden by a flash and a star burst; the mouse is shown whole on her back with X eyes and no blood. After 136.06 nothing about her is played for laughs.
- **Hit timing.** The narrator says "struck" at 133.02 but the contact is at 135.20, in the pause, so that the thud never covers a word. The stick is visibly winding up through those 2 s. If the owner prefers the hit on the word, it must be silent.
- **Dependence on planned assets.** Most supporting assets are planned elsewhere but not yet in `Library/`. If this film is animated first it has to build them.
- **Three sets in the first 20 s.** If time is short, stage S3 in the yard with the stall in an inset circle.
- **Small leads.** Mongoose and mouse need close framing; check stills at phone size.
- Owner questions (6) are listed in `creative_plan.json` under `owner_questions` with the default chosen for each: softened strike, hit timing, the two added gags, the added lettering, no moral caption, eyelashes on the mongoose.
