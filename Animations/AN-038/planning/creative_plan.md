# AN-038 The Falcon and the Partridge: creative plan

Film length 166.5 s (MP3 163.53 s + 3.0 s THE END). All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard (15 scenes, 185 timed actions, subtitle chunks) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A falcon who cannot reach a partridge in its thorny nest talks it out with sweet promises, seizes and eats it, and is destroyed by his own meal, while a grape-eating fox watches the whole hunt from a distance.

**Conflict.** Force fails (the thorns), so the hunter switches to words. The partridge's defence is doubt; the story is the slow removal of that doubt. The audience always knows more than the partridge: we see the talons flex, the crossed claws, the bare ground.

**Emotional arc.** Appetite and ease (fox, grapes), fright (chase), comedy of the liar (mask on and off), dread (the walk out), shock (the grab), anger and dignity (the curse), slapstick comeuppance (feathers), stillness, a thoughtful exit.

**Ending and moral.** No moral is spoken. The fox's thought cloud (155.26 to 158.9) shows the end of the hunt and then its beginning (the smiling face, the grain), so the viewer joins them. Sweet words from a known hunter are bait, and a meal won by deceit can poison the eater.

## 3. Character acting

**Falcon (the mask).** Two faces, used by rule. Sweet face: brow raised, small smile, `lid` 0.4 to 0.5, head tilts (`headRot` 0.25), feet hidden (`sit`), soft `talk` 0.35. Real face: `brow` 1, smirk or shut beak, feet out, `talons` flexing. He wears the sweet face only while the partridge can see him; it slips for 4 frames at 44.4 (the snub), at 58.6 (caught out) and at 80.6 (the smirk at the foot on the rim). Into the mask he eases with `settleTo`; at 88.86 it drops in 3 frames with no settle. After 98 he never tilts his head again. Suspense by stillness at 84.9 to 88.8: only his pupils (`lookAt`) follow the partridge. Decline (132 to 143): `sag`, `drop`, `lid` 0.6, sway with `tip`, `tremble`, `wet` eyes, a worried raised brow; the one look to camera at 142.46 before the fall.

**Partridge.** Starts as two eyes over the nest rim (`drop`, `tremble`), and rises in the frame as its trust grows: eyes (18.4), beak (37.18), head (51.8), chest and a foot on the rim (80.24). Doubt is brow V and leaning away (`tip` -0.1); the fading is the brow levelling, slower blinks and nods. On the ground: stretched neck, tiny `hop` steps with holds. After the grab: `sq` 0.85, pinpoint pupils, `wet`. In the curse the trembling stops at 120.28: the steady stare is its strongest moment.

**Fox.** The audience's stand-in. Chewing is `talk` 0.3 at 4 Hz with `sit` and `plant`. Ears lead every reaction (ears first, head 3 frames later). His chewing stops at each turn of the plot; he loses his appetite at 123.44; he never interferes. Final walk with `walk`/`footfalls`, one sideways glance at the body, pace unchanged.

**Anticipation and follow-through.** Crouch before the rear-up (7.36), the landing dip of the branch with damped `swing` (21.4), the long wing-opening and crouch (89.72 to 91.4) before the pounce, `landSquash` on every landing, feathers and dust as secondary motion after every impact, the partridge's legs swinging during the lift.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1600 wide; the panel shows 1000 x 1230 at zoom 1. Cam x 500 holds fox and snag together; cam x 1100 holds snag and nest tree.
- S1 establishing with a pan; S2 tracks the chase left to right in the upper half of the panel.
- Dialogue (S4, S6) in a locked `twoShot(falconHead, partridgeHead, {margin: 150})`: falcon left, nook right, thorn bars as a wall between them. S6 mirrors S4 with the partridge higher.
- Inserts: the claws at 46.5 (zoom 3.0) and the grip at 94.02 (zoom 2.8).
- S9 is vertical: falcon high, partridge small below with its back turned, his shadow around it. Push in on the mask drop, pull back for the pounce.
- Crane up with the lift (95.92 to 98.2). Keys on fall start and impact at 143.96.
- The final wide (152.62): fox far left, falcon far right, empty ground between: "from afar" as a picture.
- All faces and key action stay in the top two-thirds; icons and clouds go into empty sky, never over a face. Shake only inside the panel clip.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the practised smile that first comes out as a leer (30.08); whistling while covering his claws (48.5); the unanswerable question as a looping chase icon (58.12); crossed claws during the oath and the fox's eye-roll; trying to stick a feather back on (136.16); the burp (128.8).
- **Dramatic irony:** the bare ground under the golden grain balloon (69.28); the talon flexing below the branch line while he says "not to harm you" (36.26).
- **Suspense:** the ? marks disappearing one by one; the motionless falcon while the partridge climbs out; the shadow spreading before the grab; the hold on the partridge beginning to look up (92.0 to 93.0).
- **Surprise:** the 3-frame mask drop; the shout that stops the strike (107.2); the first feather letting go (134.72).
- **Emotion:** the look back at the empty nest (100.08); the heart called back and cracked (112.44 to 115.52); the last feather landing on his chest (147.28); the feather on the thorn in the end frame.
- **Setups and payoffs:** heart (37.88, 112.44); grain balloon (38.86, 67.34, 101.62, 158.12); "easy" belly pat (127.88) against the green belly (132.12); the fox's grapes (eaten with joy, pushed away, left behind).
