# AN-125 Harun al-Rashid and Zubaydah in the Bath: creative plan

Film length 175.55 s of narration + 3.5 s THE END (ends 179.05). 16 scenes, 148 actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (authoritative for times).

**Discretion rule (applies to every scene):** Zubaydah is never drawn undressed. She bathes behind an opaque folding screen (`bathScreen`); only her veiled head, and at most a sleeved hand with the silver vessel, is seen. The lady figure is drawn fully clothed first and the screen after it. The camera never takes Harun's point of view. The "frank verses" are shown only by hearts, icons and blushing.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (P1, 0-23.4):** Harun loves Zubaydah and builds her a garden: pool, water from four directions, trees that close round it. The promise is privacy (stamp PRIVATE!).
- **Rising (P2, 23.4-45.2):** a blazing day; she enjoys the water and shade, goes behind the screen and pours water over herself from a silver vessel.
- **Inciting act and suspense (P3, 45.2-67.6):** Harun hears, tiptoes through the trees and watches from a bush, against the purpose of his own garden. A sparrow is the garden's conscience.
- **Surprise and comedy (P4, 67.6-90.2):** she notices, blushes, ducks, grabs her mantle, loses it; the veil tip still shows. Harun, caught, is ashamed and retreats.
- **Emotion and problem (P5, 90.2-110.5):** LATER, in the palace, the picture stays in his mind. He begins a verse (ribbon) and gets stuck after half a line.
- **Turn (P6, 110.5-132.9):** Abu Nuwas is called and understands at once.
- **Climax (P7, 132.9-153.9):** the poem: pool, shade, silver vessel, then the bold part, which joins up with the caliph's half line.
- **Resolution (P8, 153.9-175.3):** Harun laughs, rewards him; the poet leaves delighted. THE END.
- **Conflict:** small and comic: curiosity against privacy, then a thought that cannot be finished. **Arc:** affection, heat and relief, mischief, shared embarrassment, longing, frustration, delight.
- **Moral:** a quick, understanding mind finishes what another only began, and is rewarded.

## 3. Character acting
- **Harun** (`person` costume 'caliph', age 0.25): proud and tender in S1-S2 (gaze on her, hand to chest). Sneaking: tiptoe gait, `PERSON_HANDS.hush`, saccades left and right, body flat behind the trunk. Watching: only eyes, a slow blink, `noBlink` when caught. Caught: look to camera, gulp (drop pulse), soft flush, hand over his own eyes, tilt away, a bump with a 2-frame squash. Stuck poet: orator pose with anticipation, then mouth held open, temple scratch, ceiling saccades, sag (`sq` 0.96, `lid` 0.5). With Abu Nuwas: leans in (`tip` 0.08), gaze follows the ribbon tip, nods, blush and fanning in S14, bouncing laugh, claps.
- **Zubaydah** (`person` costume 'lady'): heat (fanning hand, `lid` 0.4, frown, one sweat drop), delight (eyes wide, raised brow, smile), relief sigh with closed eyes. Behind the screen she acts with the head and eyes only: sink with a bob, happy shiver under the water, head shake. Noticing: freeze, saccades, eyes lock, head whip with veil lag, flush, small "o", duck with anticipation and squash, tremble, groping hands, the veil tip that will not stay down (overshoot spring).
- **Abu Nuwas** (`person` costume 'poet'): relaxed swagger, turban tail and quill as secondary motion, flourish bow, one raised brow while reading, instant finger-up with overshoot, wink, palm out to refuse thinking time, quill twirl, knuckle crack as anticipation for the recital, sly lean and cupped mouth for the bold verses, modest shrug, eyes popping at the purse weight, skip on exit.
- **Sparrow** (`bird` kind 'sparrow'): pants in the heat, turns its back and covers its eye when she goes behind the screen, glares at Harun (brow), hops down and pecks his crown, chirps the warning that makes her look, scolds him as he leaves. Always has a gaze target.
- Every character blinks (`LIFE.t`), listeners watch speakers, and no two figures move in unison.

## 4. Camera and composition
- Garden strip is 1600 wide; the camera tracks along it. Pool and screen sit at x 1000..1200, the hiding bush at x 580, the palace door at x 70..230, so the peeper and the bath can be cut apart or linked in one wide two-shot (70.36).
- S1 push-in two-shot; S2 establishing pull-out and a push as the foliage closes; S3 track with her walk; S4 medium on the screen with one pull-back for "alone"; S5 track with the tiptoe; S6 close on his eyes (never his view); S7 medium on the screen with one linking wide; S8 close then track.
- Hall: `twoShot(poet, harun)` for dialogue; push-ins on the stuck moment (S10), the "AHA" (S12) and Harun's blush (S14); crane up for the ribbon arch (S13); track to the door for the exit.
- Faces and key action stay above panel y 1000; labels and lettering never cover a face; thought cloud and ribbon live in the upper half.
- One transition card: LATER... at 90.24 (garden to palace), with the camera cut hidden at its midpoint.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Irony made visible:** the PRIVATE! stamp from the building scene returns exactly when the narrator says the garden was designed for privacy, and its arrow bends to point at the peeping eyes.
- **Humour:** the sparrow's manners versus the caliph's; the peck; the mantle that slips; the veil tip that springs back; BONK! on the retreat; the hourglass flicked away; the purse weight.
- **Suspense by stillness:** 56.4-60 only leaves and pupils move.
- **Surprise:** her eyes finding his; the poet's instant answer.
- **Emotion:** affection in S1, shared embarrassment in S7-S8 (both blush), longing in the thought cloud, the pleasure of a completed thought when the two ribbon ends join.
- **Poetry as a picture:** `verseRibbon` carries icons, stops in three dots, and is completed into a loop; the same three dots are recalled at "عجز" 151.40.
