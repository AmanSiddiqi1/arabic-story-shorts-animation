## Remaining notes (minor; none of these triggers v3)

### A. 111.5-120.2: the spirit reads as an owl sitting on the well
- Wrong: the ghost is a compact round shape with two yellow eyes, about the size of the crow, and for most of its time on screen it sits level with the well crossbeam directly behind the princess. With the well's beam under it, the first reading is "a dark bird on the well". Its orbit is hard to notice at this size, and the wavy tail does not show. The exit at 120.2-120.5 (stretched upward, POOF!) does work.
- Why: the viewer should see that something is on her, not near the well.
- Fix, if a pass happens: stand the sultan and princess about 120 px further left so her head has clear sky behind it and the well crossbeam is not behind her; draw the ghost about 1.6 times larger with the tail trailing down towards her veil, and make the orbit a visible ellipse round her head (radius about one head width, in front of her on the near half and behind on the far half). Keep spiral eyes and sway as they are.

### B. 55.5-57.5: the envier is half hidden behind the kind man at the well
- The envier's body is covered from the shoulder down by the kind man for 2 s under the OLD WELL label (`extra/m4.jpg` 56.5). Faces are both visible, so the beat reads; separating them by about 40 px would show his posture before the shove.

### C. 74.5-94.5: the lower quarter of the cavern shot is two flat dark slabs
- The closer framing left a plain brown block and a plain olive block under the actors for 20 s. A few of the grey stones already used in the shaft walls, or a darker dot shade, would stop it looking unfinished. Cosmetic only.

### D. 121.5-125.0: the kind man is cut by the left border during the cure
- While the camera sits on the sultan and princess, the healer is half out of frame at the left edge. He is not the subject of the shot, so it is acceptable; shifting the camera about 50 px left would keep his face whole for the CURED! beat.

### E. 143.5: the cat pops in at the right edge
- The cat appears cropped by the right border on the dais at 143.5 and is on the throne arm by 144.0. A two-step walk-in from 143.2 would be smoother. Cosmetic.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Hold, then open** (161.5-163.5): a threat gesture frozen with no blink while only the other character trembles, released on the turning word. Works for any mercy or reprieve beat.
- **Counted transfer** (114.5-116.5): the counter ticks while the counted objects visibly move from source to hand to destination, so the number and the picture agree.
- **Exit through a door, then hold on the empty doorway** (168.5-169.5) before cutting to the closing image; gentler than a fade for a character leaving the story.
- Guide rule confirmed by this film: one identifying costume colour per principal, and no extra may wear it.
- Guide rule suggested: a floating creature should never rest in line with a perch-like prop (beam, branch, wall top), or it will read as sitting on it.
