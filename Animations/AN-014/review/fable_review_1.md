# AN-014 The Envious Man and the Envied: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/AN-014_v1.mp4` (173.0 s) through all 29 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` plus 42 full-size frames pulled into `review/v1_pack/extra/` (montages `m1.jpg` to `m7.jpg`).

## Summary

The story is complete and in order, every narrated event is on screen, the ending and THE END hold are present, and the subtitles are readable and accurate. No blockers. Good work that should be kept as it is: the shove (60.3-61.2), the tilt down the shaft and the one-eye wake (64.0-67.0), the yellow eyes in the earth (70.5-73.5), the 1-7 counter, the smoke arc and POOF (119.0-121.0), the empty throne with the crown on the seat (139.7-141.2), and the shove replayed in the thought cloud (157.2-160.0).

It needs a v2 because several staging faults blur the story at exactly the points that carry it: who the envier is in the court scene, the raised hand that opens, the "private" talk, and the evil spirit. Two of them are plain picture errors (the envier standing inside the well at 49 s, feet showing under a solid wall at 10 s). All fixes are staging, costume colour, draw order and camera; no new assets are required.

Blockers: none.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 149.5-155.0 and 164.7-166.9, MAJOR: three red-capped men, and the arrow points at the wrong one
- Wrong: the envier's identifier through the whole film is the red cap and moustache. The second follower at the rescue (99.7-106.5, white robe, red cap, moustache), the same figure at the far left of the court crowd (149.5-154.5), and the servant who brings the gifts (164.7-166.9, white robe, red cap, moustache) all wear the same thing. In the crowd close-up (152.0-153.2) the envier is almost fully hidden behind the blue-robed man (only the rim of his cap shows), the arrow on جاره 151.94 lands on the blue man, and the clearly visible red cap belongs to the look-alike at the far left. At 165.2-166.2 the envier and the servant stand side by side like twins.
- Why: this is the recognition beat. A viewer who picks the wrong man loses the ending. At 99.7 it also reads for a moment as the envier coming back to help with the rescue.
- Fix: red cap for the envier only. Give the second follower and the far-left petitioner a white or green turban, and give the servant palace livery (blue turban, blue sash, no moustache). In 150.98-153.4 put the envier in the back row with his whole cap and both eyes visible between two shoulders, ducking (the plan's peek motif: cap, then eyes); land the arrow tip on his cap at 151.94; the crowd steps aside at 153.4 as now.

### 2. 161.38-163.2, MAJOR: the raised hand that opens is too small to be the climax
- Wrong: the plan's key image (hand raised on العقاب 161.38, held, opens on لكن 162.18) plays in the wide shot with the king about 200 px tall at the right edge. The raised hand is a small dot beside his turban (161.6-162.4) and its opening cannot be seen; at 163.2 the arm is simply already pointing. The envier's peek through his fingers is not readable either.
- Why: this is the moral turn of the film and its one suspense hold. At present it passes as a generic gesture.
- Fix: at 161.38 cut (or 0.25 s push) to a tight two-shot: king from the waist up on the right, the kneeling envier's head and shielding hands lower left (`twoShot` with a small margin; king's head at least 300 px). Raise the arm with a closed fist on العقاب 161.38, hold it dead still to 162.18 with only the envier trembling (`tremble` 3) and the king's pupils on him. On لكن 162.18 the fingers open into a flat, palm-up hand and the brow lifts (`brow` to 0, small smile at 162.6). Envier: one eye opens between the fingers at 162.7 (`wink`), then both. Pull back to the present wide on أمر 163.9 so the servant's entrance has room.

### 4. 54.7-57.2, MAJOR: the "private" talk has a third person standing with them

### 5. 73.8-94.6, MAJOR: 21 seconds with the three actors tiny in the bottom third of the panel
- Wrong: in the cavern cutaway the jinn are about 110 px tall and the listening man about 150 px, all standing on the bottom edge of the panel; the upper 60 percent is plain brown earth with a balloon in it. From 85.7 to 94.6 the man is cut off at the right edge (only a sleeve shows), so the dramatic irony the plan asks for (jinn B checking nobody is listening while the man is one wall away) is not on screen.
- Why: this is the longest scene in the film and holds the secret that drives the rest; faces and gestures are not legible on a phone, and the plan's rule "faces in the upper two-thirds" is not met.
- Fix: frame it about 1.6x closer and lower the horizon of interest: camera centred so the cavern floor sits at about 70 percent of panel height, jinn heads near mid-panel, the balloon above them, and the man's whole head and ear-hand inside the right edge for the entire scene (jinn left two-thirds, man right third, as planned). When jinn B looks over his shoulder (about 87.5-88.5, the yellow arc), his gaze should sweep to the wall and the man should freeze with wide eyes and a hand over his mouth.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Tilt down the cutaway instead of showing the fall** (64.0-65.0): shove, hold on the empty rim, then the camera travels down the shaft to the heap with stars. Reusable for any fall into a pit or well.
- **Eyes in the earth before the reveal** (70.5-73.7): pairs of eyes blinking in solid ground at the exact positions where the characters stand after the iris opens.
- **Icon balloon chain for reported speech** (77.5-94.5): one balloon whose content changes on each cue word (person, crown, ill, question, tick, count, object), with a dark-field balloon for white objects (91.5-93.0).
- **Memory icons into and out of the turban** (96.2-97.3, 112.5-113.5) for "he remembered the words".
- **Succession by empty seat** (133.7, 139.7): the figure fades and leaves the headgear on the seat; gentle way to show a death.
- New guide rule suggested: **one identifying costume colour per principal** (no extra may wear the villain's cap colour), and **no extra shares a principal's close-up for more than 1 s**.
