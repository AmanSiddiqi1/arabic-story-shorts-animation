Two men lived in one neighborhood. One of them was loved by the people for his good character. His neighbor envied him because people loved him, and wished he would lose everything he had.

The kind man tried to avoid trouble, but his neighbor’s envy did not stop. Eventually he left town and settled far away, There he opened a house of worship, and people began visiting him and listening to his advice.

Hearing that his neighbor had become famous, the envious man visited pretending friendship. He asked to speak privately near an old well. As the man approached it, the envier pushed him inside and fled.

The man did not die in the well. At night he heard strange voices beneath the ground. They were the voices of jinn who knew the sultan’s daughter was ill because of an evil spirit, and who also knew how she could be cured.

One jinn said the treatment required seven white hairs from the tail of a black cat. The man remembered the words. In the morning his followers searched for him, heard his voice, and pulled him out.

Later the sultan arrived seeking help for his daughter. The man took the hairs from the cat and burned them as he had heard in the well. The spirit left the princess, and she recovered before her father’s eyes.

The delighted sultan married his daughter to the man. Then the vizier died, and the man became vizier in his place. After the sultan’s death he took power, and became king of the country he had come to as a stranger.

One day the king saw his old neighbor among the people. The envier realized he faced the man he had pushed into the well and feared punishment. But the king ordered gifts and money for him and let him leave safely.
