# AN-014 The Envious Man and the Envied: creative plan

Film length 173.0 s (MP3 170.00 s + 3.0 s THE END). All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard with every action, cue word and pose note is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** An envious neighbour pushes a good man into a well. Down there the man overhears two jinn, learns how to cure the sultan's daughter, and rises to be king. When the envier later stands before him, he gives him gifts and lets him go.

**Conflict.** One man is loved, the other cannot bear it. The envier acts; the kind man never fights back. The story's question is what the kind man will do when he finally has all the power.

**Emotional arc.** Warm (S1), sour and comic (S2-S3), sad and brave (S4-S5), peaceful (S6), tense (S7-S9), spooky-comic and curious (S10-S12), relief (S13), wonder and joy (S14-S16), solemn rise (S17-S18), suspense (S19), grace (S20).

**Ending and moral.** The film's last image is a raised hand that opens. Envy hurt only the envier; goodness and forgiveness lifted the other man higher than revenge could. The envier's red flush, present in every earlier scene, is gone for the first time as he leaves.

## 3. Character acting

- **Kind man.** Open, unhurried, always looks at the person in front of him (`look()` gaze plans). Never angry: trouble gets a raised brow, a slow blink and a breath. Signature gesture: hand on chest (`PERSON_HANDS.chest`) at 22.16, 109.6, 125.2, 137.9, 147.9. Heavy decisions use the grief-to-resolve pattern (head drop, long blink, breath, level head, no smile) at 28.1 and 139.8. In the well: wake with `wink` then both eyes, pat-check, failed climb with `landSquash`, then stillness with only the pupils moving (84.4).
- **Envier.** Thin, tight, late. Default `brow` 0.6, `lid` 0.3. The planned `flush` rises on 11.9, 26.0, 46.52 and is absent only in S20. The peek (fingers, cap, eyes) is his motif. **Mask on / mask off** in S8-S9: the pulled-on smile is on only while the kind man can see his face; it drops for 0.35 s over the shoulder in the hug and falls off before the shove. Shove: `gazePlan(…, 0.07)` saccades left and right, 0.5 s lean-back `anticipate`, lunge, follow-through held 0.4 s. S19: gaze climbs shoes, robe, face; double take with `settleTo`; `tremble` 3, `PERSON_HANDS.shield`, kneel. S20: peek between fingers (`wink`), arms sag under the purse, shame ending (look up, eyes break away, head drops).
- **Jinn.** A is the know-it-all (chest out, big hands, temple tap, smirk, `lid` 0.4). B is the eager, timid listener (chin on fist, shiver with `tremble`, checks over his shoulder). They never move in unison and always look at each other or at the balloon.
- **Sultan.** Heavy walk with `footfalls`, crown lagging 2 frames. A father first: `wet` eyes and low shoulders when he pleads; held disbelief, then joy; `anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash` at 125.88.
- **Princess.** Ill: sway (`tip` ±0.06, 1.6 s cycle), limp arms, `spiralEyes`. Recovery: spin slows, two blinks, breath, posture rises with overshoot. Later she is the one who offers the crown.
- **Cat.** Calm and self-possessed: tail up, arches into the stroking hand, ear flick per hair, licks a paw. Ends on the throne arm with the tail tip flicking.
- **Groups.** Villagers, followers and petitioners use `stagger`; listeners nod at different moments; the three heaves on the rope use `countedBeats` with the second follower half a beat late.
- Everyone blinks (`LIFE.t = t`); blinks are suppressed during held shock (60.3-61.0, 155.9-157.2).

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground y 900; faces and key action stay in the upper two-thirds of the panel. Balloons, clouds and labels sit above head height and never cover a face.
- Establishing wides at 0.0, 33.98, 98.14 and 149.96. Two-shots (`twoShot`) for the hug, the well, the cavern talk and the final confrontation: the victim's face is always in frame.
- Push-ins on decisions and realisations: the envy (9.72-11.9), the listener in the well (84.4), the king's look (150.98).
- The fall is told by a **tilt down the cutaway shaft** at 64.02 instead of showing the body fall.
- The cavern is a cutaway two-shot: jinn on the left two-thirds, the listening man on the right third, with the wall between them visible. This makes the dramatic irony readable without words.
- Pull-out at 144.7 to make room for the round inset (planned `inset circle`) of the dusty traveller.
- Transitions: whip 9.72 and 30.3; iris 73.78; dissolve 98.14; the single LATER... card at 106.36 (camera and cast change under its covered midpoint); wipe 129.7; dissolve 138.68. Panel-only shake at 60.26 and 120.2.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour.** The staggered doors; the swatted heart; the envier pretending to dust the wall; the smile pulled on with two fingers; jinn B checking that nobody is listening while the man is one wall away; a follower looking under a bucket; the sultan's crown hanging in the air; the gift pile up to the envier's chin.
- **Suspense.** The old well is in frame from 33.98, long before it matters. The stone that falls in and makes no sound (56.44). The look left, look right before the shove. Stillness in the well with only pupils moving. The king's raised hand held from 161.38 to 162.18.
- **Surprise.** One eye opening on "did not die"; eyes in the dark earth; the cat popping onto the end of the tail in the balloon; the hand that opens.
- **Emotion.** The hand on his own door before leaving; the father's wet eyes; the empty seats with a lamp going out; the inset of the stranger he once was; the envier unable to hold the king's gaze.
- **Setup and payoff.** The sandal (60.9, 101.9, 106.0). The three memory icons into the turban (96.3) and out again (112.5). The SHOVE pose (60.26) replayed exactly in the thought cloud (158.74). The cat's white tail hairs (93.5, 112.44, 171.6). The lamp that goes out twice and is relit.
