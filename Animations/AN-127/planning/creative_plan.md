# AN-127 Mus'ab bin al-Zubayr and Ayishah His Wife: creative plan

Film length 179.81 s of narration + 3.5 s THE END (to 183.3 s). 15 scenes, 157 timed actions, 6 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is the start of the named Arabic word in `inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the intent.

**The one rule of this film:** the script is frank about a married couple's intimacy, and the picture is not. Everything private happens behind a curtain, a door or closed shutters. The audience sees only hearts, a moon crossing the sky, and the faces of whoever is outside. The comedy is in those faces.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (0-23 s):** in Medina the young noble Musab meets clever Izzah and sends her to look at Ayishah bint Talhah before he proposes.
- **Rising comedy (23-61 s):** Izzah's report is absurdly long. Face, eyes, nose, smile (icons, ticks), then a whispered part that makes him blush. Her mission was beauty, not wealth or lineage. One more note: big feet. And a joke: he may not keep up with her.
- **Turn (61-68 s):** he takes it seriously, thinks, and still chooses her. This is the one sincere close-up of Musab.
- **Reveal (68-81 s):** the wedding. We finally see Ayishah (tall, bright, red dress, big yellow slippers). The curtain closes on the couple.
- **LATER (81 s):** Izzah's gathering for the bride and the women of Quraysh.
- **Emotion (92-113 s):** Ayishah recites love verses; the verse ribbon drifts across the room and curls round Musab at the door. Everybody sees who the poem is for.
- **Comic flashback (113-134 s):** the wedding night from the street (hearts, moon, a deadpan cat), and the neighbour's praise next morning.
- **Suspense and climax (134-157 s):** a proper lady visitor overhears from the front room, and asks Ayishah if she is not ashamed.
- **Resolution and moral (157-183 s):** Ayishah answers without apology: a wife shows her husband her love, by day and by night. No quarrel, no punishment; the two women end up laughing over tea.
- **Conflict:** propriety (Izzah's teasing, the visitor's shock) against Ayishah's open, confident love. She never blushes; everyone else does.
- **Emotional arc:** curiosity, embarrassment, resolve, delight, shock, calm pride, shared laughter.

## 3. Character acting
- **Musab** (noble, s 1.25): open and readable. Eager hops and leaning in during the report; mirrors each icon (touches his own nose, copies the smile). Blush sequence at 36.64: eyes wide, mouth "o", soft `flush`, `tremble` 1.5, then hand over mouth and one look to camera. The decision (61-68) is played slowly: `chin` hand, saccades (`gazePlan` with 0.07 ease), one long blink, `anticipate` breath, then a pointing arm with `settleTo` overshoot. After the wedding he is simply happy: springy `hop` + `landSquash`, whistling.
- **Izzah** (s 1.2): resting face is one raised brow (`brow -0.4`) and `lid 0.3`. She plays to him and to us: temple tap, `wink`, an elbow nudge that actually moves him 10 px, a laugh with bouncing shoulders. At 63.48 the mask drops (worried `clasp`) so her relief at 67.6 means something. In S9 she is the told-you-so in the corner.
- **Ayishah** (s 1.3, taller than everyone): never hurried, never blushing. Chin level, steady gaze, blinks slow. She leads every approach (she closes the gap at 78.10, she tugs him to the alcove). In S9 her eyes do the storytelling: 2-frame saccades to the door while her body faces the guests. In S14 her gestures open up step by step (`palmsUp`, `pointUp`, `chest`, `armsOut`) and the visitor leans back from it.
- **Visitor** (s 1.2, upright): the suspense-by-stillness beat at 137.76 (`noBlink`, only pupils move), then escalating reactions: `cupEar`, tipped cup, `cheeks`, lens stare, slump. Indignation at 152.72 with `sit` 1 to 0 and overshoot. Ending: mouth opens, nothing comes, shrug, laugh.
- **Guests** (four, s 1.1): never in unison. Use `stagger(i)` for entering, sitting, head turns and giggles; each sways on her own `K.noise` phase. Youngest gasps, oldest grins.
- **Neighbour:** teasing mime (running arms, flexed arm), wink.
- **Roof cat (S10):** the falling-reaction rule: watches, ears prick, deadpan lens stare with slow blink, paw over eyes, curls up asleep (`zzz`).
- Everyone always has a gaze target; set `LIFE.t = t` for blinks; add hand blinks where `lid` is set.

## 4. Camera and composition
- World 1000 x 1230, floor/ground y 900, panel shows the top two-thirds; faces sit at y 560..640 at s 1.2-1.3. Balloons, clouds and icon cards live at y 300..420 and never cover a face.
- S1 opens on an establishing wide and eases to a two-shot (`twoShot`, margin 150). S2 pans with Izzah to the door, keeping Musab at the frame edge (the one who waits).
- S5 tilts down for the feet comparison and back up for the cloud. S6 is the film's only solo push-in (zoom 1.5 to 2.0), then a snap wide on the pointing arm.
- S7: full room for the reveal, two-shot for the vows, pull back so the curtain is in frame for its close.
- S9 is long, so the camera carries it: medium (rise), reveal pan to the door (96.26), wide (ribbon), close on her eyes (105.22), wide (guests' tennis-match looks).
- S10 is an exterior with a slow upward drift; no people. S12 pushes slowly toward the visitor while the curtain stays in the right edge of frame.
- S14 pushes in on Ayishah for her answer and returns to the two-shot for sun and moon. S15 pulls out to the whole room for THE END.
- Transitions: wipes inside the first act, one **LATER...** card at 81.20 (camera cut hidden at its midpoint), iris into the flashback at 113.48, dissolve night to morning, wipe to ANOTHER DAY.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** the scroll that keeps rolling; Musab copying every icon; the slippers; tiny Musab puffing in the joke cloud; the cat; the neighbour's flex; the visitor's cup.
- **Running motifs:** (1) the heart is the only sign of intimacy; (2) the curtain/shutters closing; (3) the big yellow slippers, set up at 49.48 and paid off when Ayishah first walks in at 70.80 and when she steps from the curtain at 152.00; (4) the grey ring that turns gold on his decision and appears between their hands at the wedding.
- **Suspense:** the visitor alone with the curtain (137-150): stillness first, then escalation.
- **Surprise:** the verse ribbon leaving the circle of women and wrapping round Musab; Ayishah's total lack of embarrassment when the visitor expects shame.
- **Emotion:** the sincere decision in S6; the look between husband and wife across the room in S9 and again at THE END.
