A wealthy pious Israelite warned his son never to swear an oath by God, even truthfully. The son inherited his fortune and kept this promise despite false claims.

Fraudsters demanded money and challenged him to swear he owed nothing. He refused to take the oath, paying them until his wealth was gone.

He sailed with his wife and two sons to seek a new life, but a storm wrecked their vessel. He washed ashore alone on an island.

The tale describes wondrous sea creatures and a buried treasure he found through mysterious guidance. His generosity attracted ships and settlers, and he eventually became the island’s king.

The sons grew up separately and reached the island by different routes. Without recognizing each other or their father, one became a secretary and the other a palace steward.

Their mother arrived aboard a merchant’s ship. While guarding it, the two young men exchanged shipwreck stories and recognized each other as brothers; their mother overheard.

To bring the family before the ruler, the mother falsely accused the young men of improper advances. During the hearing they recounted their past, and the king recognized his lost sons.

She revealed that she was the young men’s mother and the king’s long-lost wife. All four were reunited after years of separation and hardship.
