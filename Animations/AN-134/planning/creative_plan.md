# AN-134 The Caliph al-Hakim and the Merchant: creative plan

Film length 173.28 s of narration plus a 3.0 s THE END hold (176.28 s). 17 scenes, 178 timed actions, 12 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every action in `creative_plan.json` carries its Arabic cue word and that word's start time. This file gives the direction; the JSON gives the frame-level list.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A caliph stops at a garden for a drink of water, is given an instant feast for his whole procession, learns that it is only the owner's ordinary day, and repays the welcome with the entire year's mint of dirhams.

**Spine (beats).**
1. Setup, 0-10.7: the great procession on the road; the garden appears.
2. Inciting, 14.2-21.8: "a drink of water"; the owner runs, pours, hands the cup up. **The cup is planted here and pays off in the last shot.**
3. Rising, 21.8-30.6: invitation, agreement, the caliph enters.
4. Surprise, 30.6 (وفجأة): servants pop out of nowhere; rugs and cushions.
5. Comic escalation, 42.8-64.2: dishes arrive, then multiply to 100 of each kind (56.32 مئة); the caliph's jaw drops.
6. Suspense, 64.2-74.9: the caliph's suspicion ("did you know I was coming?") as a thought cloud.
7. Turn, 77.0 (لم): no; this is the daily routine. The cloud is crossed out and pops.
8. Reveal, 85.9-108.6: the hundred windows of the house; everything that arrives every day; the servants only gathered it.
9. Emotion, 108.6-131.2: "an ordinary day"; the caliph works out the wealth and his wonder grows.
10. Gratitude, 131.2-137.7: he thanks God.
11. Climax, 137.7-151.0: the order; the treasury; the counter to 3,700,000.
12. Resolution, 151.9-161.7: all of it to the merchant, "and it deserves more".
13. Moral, 161.7-176.3: he rides home; cup of water and chests joined by one arrow.

**Conflict.** There is no antagonist. The tension is the caliph's puzzle: how can this be real? It rises in three steps (speed, quantity, cost) and each answer makes the wonder bigger, not smaller.

**Emotional arc.** Idle pride on the road, simple thirst, pleased surprise, open astonishment, a moment of suspicion, disbelief, admiration, gratitude, decisive generosity. The owner's arc is flat on purpose (calm, modest, faintly amused) until 153.6, when for the first time he is the astonished one. That reversal is the emotional payoff.

**Moral.** Open-handed hospitality brings rewards beyond anything expected.

## 3. Character acting

**Caliph** (`person` costume `caliph`). Regal and half-lidded on the road (lid 0.25, chin up). His face opens in steps and never goes back: thirst (warm brow, drinking mime), pleasure (AHH!, eye glint), surprise (sq 0.94 startle at 31.92), astonishment (mouth `o`, no blink 59.0-60.2, crown hop), suspicion (brow -0.4, lid 0.35, hand on chin at 63.44), then stillness at 121.3 where only one slow blink moves. The decision is physical: he puts the cup down (130.3) and stands. Thanking God: arms by angles (`aL/aR` about ±2.45..2.85, since hand targets are too short overhead), gaze `[0, -1]`, lid to 1 for half a second. The order at 138.28 is the first sharp movement he makes in the film (`turnFlip`, cape lag, `pointFwd`).

**Owner / merchant** (`person` costume `merchant`, green robe, yellow turban). The opposite energy: he runs exactly once (16.82, `walk` at a short fast stride, tip 0.12, turban lagging) and after that never hurries. Folded hands (`PERSON_HANDS.folded`), lid 0.3, small rock on the heels while miracles happen around him. His gestures are small and domestic: `palmsUp` shrug, thumb over the shoulder at his house, plumping a cushion while the caliph computes his fortune. Comic peek at 26.6 (`wink: 'R'` during the "rest" mime). At 153.6 he finally breaks: saccades from chest to chest, "me?" with a finger on his own chest, a look behind him, `cheeks`, `tremble`, `kneel`. Ending: the single look to camera of the film (`gaze [0, 0.05]`) with a `palmsUp` shrug holding the cup.

**Vizier** (costume `vizier`). Silent second banana and audience stand-in: yawns on the road, eyes the sweets (hand creeping, caught by a guard), listens with `cupEar` and full cheeks, shrugs at the caliph at 84.44, has to gulp before saluting at 138.3, mops his brow under the counter.

**Servants** (costume `servant`, four pal variants) **and the child** (`child: true`). Drilled but individual: each has a different walk phase and patting rhythm; `stagger(i)` on every group reaction. The child gets three moments: the cushion tower (only legs visible, `swing`), the caught orange, the peep into the chest with a coin glint in each eye.

**Guards** (planned `guard` preset, spears). Background rhythm and two gags: the spear that will not go under the arch (29.4), the cushion that is too soft (39.0), the shiver at the cold jug (49.5).

**Horses.** Head nod, ear flicks, tail swish while walking; the white horse nods agreement three frames after its rider (27.2) and half-rears on the turn home (`rear` 0.3).

General: `LIFE.t = t` for blinks; every figure has a gaze target at all times (speaker and listener look at each other; everyone tracks the orange, the landing trays, the opening lids). Anticipation before every rise, sit, run and point; `settleTo` on every pose change; cushions and rugs squash and rebound under whoever lands on them.

## 4. Camera and composition

- The panel shows the top two-thirds; faces and hands stay between world y 480 and 880, props on the rug at y 900..1010, nothing important below.
- **Road scenes** (S1-S3, S17): side-on strip. The procession travels left to right going out and right to left going home, so the return reads at once. The garden always lies to the right.
- **Garden scenes**: the owner on screen left, the caliph on the right, facing each other; this never flips (S16 keeps owner left, caliph beside him, chests further right). Two-shots are framed low (camera y about 640-660, zoom 1.5-1.6) to leave the upper half of the panel for thought clouds, balloons and day cards, so nothing covers a face.
- **Scale moves carry the comedy**: the crane to the high wide for the 10 x 10 blocks (53.3), and the cut straight back to a medium of the caliph's face on حتى (58.22).
- **Push-ins on decisions**: the hand-over of the cup (18.4-19.4, zoom 1.9), the caliph's eyes (115.06, zoom 2.6), his dawning wonder (121.9-123.1), the goblet at the end (166.4, zoom 2.4).
- **Diagram scene** S9 is flat and static on purpose (a change of visual register); liveliness comes from the sweep, the inset circle and the three waves.
- **Treasury** S15: wide, push to the vault, an insert layer for coin and stamp, then wide again with the counter across the top band.
- Last image: pull-out to the wall, gate, chests and tiny owner in golden light; THE END in the sky band.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour by contrast**: the more the caliph is amazed, the calmer the owner is. He steadies a tower of cushions with one finger without looking.
- **Humour by scale**: one rug becomes three, one tray becomes a hundred, one window becomes a hundred, one coin becomes 3,700,000; each uses a counter so the audience feels the number.
- **Suspense**: the caliph's thought cloud makes the owner look like a schemer for six seconds; the red X and the pop clear him. The listeners' held, unblinking wait at 74.56 is the only stillness in the first half.
- **Surprise**: وفجأة gets a real empty beat first (30.62-31.9) so the pop-out lands.
- **Setup and payoff**: the goblet (S2) returns in the caliph's balloon (157.78, with a heart) and in the owner's hand (166.38); the dismount is replayed in an inset at 99.1; the owner's thumb at the roofline (83.1) leads to the house.
- **Reversal**: the calm man kneels with his hands on his cheeks; the astonished man is now the calm, smiling one.
- **Emotion**: the thanks to God is played simply and quietly (no lettering, light through leaves), then one wet-eyed beat for each man (135.7, 160.88).
- **Lettering diet**: nine comic words in 173 s (AHH!, FLUMP!, BRRR!, 100!, DID YOU KNOW?, NO IDEA!, CLINK!, ALL OF IT!, AND MORE!) plus title, THE END and short labels. No en or em dashes.
