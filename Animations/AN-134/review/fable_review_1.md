# AN-134 The Caliph al-Hakim and the Merchant: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/AN-134_v1.mp4` (176.28 s) through all 30 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` plus full-size frames in `review/v1_pack/extra/`. The plan, the narration timings in `v1_pack/narration.txt` and the creative plan were read. `Library/Animation_Capabilities_Guide.md` was not consulted for this round, so the fixes below name effects and staging rather than guaranteed library fields; check field names against the guide.

## Summary

The story is told in order, every beat of the plan is present, the subtitles are verbatim, in sync and readable, and the ending holds. No blockers. The film is worth a v2 because four of its most important images are spoiled by things drawn on top of them: the climax number, the owner's face in the final shot, the first hand-over of the cup, and the caliph's astonished mouth. Two set-piece scenes (the "hundred of each kind" high view and the hundred-window house) are also much weaker than the scenes around them. All fixes are staging, layering and framing; no new assets are needed.

What already works and must not change: the title and road opening (0-10.7), the drinking mime and thought bubble (14.5-16.5), the bow and "rest" mime at the gate (22-27), the empty beat before the servants pop out (30.7-31.9), the rugs unrolling (34-36), the suspicion cloud with the red X (69-79), the banquet balloon that sags and drifts away (110-113), the three day cards (120.5-121.7), the coin stamp insert (142.2-144), the LATER card, the caliph's balloon with goblet and heart (157.7-160.7), the ride home (162-165.5) and THE END.

## Notes

### 1. 150.38-151.0, MAJOR: the WOW! burst covers the climax number
On درهم (150.38) the counter lands on 3,700,000 and a red WOW! burst pops over its top right corner, hiding the last two or three digits. The frame at 150.6 and 150.9 reads "3,700,0" then a red star. This is the number the whole film builds to and it is fully legible for only about 0.2 s (150.2-150.4).
Fix: remove the WOW! burst, or move it clear of the counter (for example below right, over the blue wall near the window at about panel x 800, y 330, no overlap with any digit or with the DIRHAMS tag). Keep the counter's own slam. Hold the complete "3,700,000 DIRHAMS" unobstructed from 150.38 until the LATER card starts at 151.0. Also check the italic 7 against the 1: at this size it is close to a 1; if the style allows, add 4-6 % letter spacing or a slightly larger size so "3,700,000" cannot be read as "3,100,000".

### 2. 167.2-172.7, MAJOR: the moral arrow is drawn through the owner's face in the last shot
The curved arrow from the ONE CUP OF WATER label runs straight down through the owner's turban, eyes and beard before curving to the chests. His face is covered for the whole final beat, including the single look to camera and shrug planned on يتوقعها (170.54). That look is the last acting moment of the film and it cannot be seen. The arrow's second half also crosses the child's face.
Fix: start the arrow at the goblet itself, which he holds out at his left (screen left of his body), not at the label. Route it as an arc that goes up and over his turban, clear of both heads by at least 40 px, and comes down on the first chest; or route it low, under his hands and in front of the wall base. Give the ONE CUP OF WATER label a short pointer to the goblet only. Keep 3,700,000 DIRHAMS above the chests where it is. Then make the shrug readable: on يتوقعها 170.54 raise both palms, eyebrows up, eyes to camera, hold to 172.4 without a blink.

### 3. 18.5-21.6, MAJOR: the cup hand-over is hidden behind the horse
The planted prop of the film is the cup, and the plan gives the hand-over a push-in (وقدّم 18.52, contact 19.3). In the render the owner arrives behind the wall directly behind the white horse's neck and head: from 19.0 to 21.6 only the top of his yellow turban shows between the caliph and the horse's head. We never see him hand anything up; the goblet simply appears in the caliph's hand.
Fix: bring the owner out to where he can be seen. Simplest and consistent with the next shot (at 21.7 he is already standing on the road by the gate): on فأسرع 17.20 he runs along the inside of the wall to the right, comes out through the gate and by وقدّم 18.52 stands on the road in front of the horse's head (screen right of the muzzle, nothing overlapping him), reaching the cup up at full arm stretch on tiptoe. The caliph leans down and to screen right and takes it at 19.3 (له 19.38). Keep the drink and AHH! as they are. If he must stay inside the wall, stop him at least one horse-head width to the right of the muzzle and have the caliph lean over to him.

### 4. 58.2-63.4 and 114.9-117.8, MAJOR: the goblet sits over the caliph's mouth during both astonishment close-ups
From حتى 58.22 the caliph holds the goblet up in front of his lower face. The planned jaw drop on تعجب (59.02) and the double take on سرعة (60.72) happen behind the cup: what shows is the cup with a small black shape beside it. The same happens in the zoom 2.6 close-up on رآها (115.06-117.8), where the cup covers his mouth for nearly three seconds. These are the two places where his face is supposed to carry the film.
Fix: in both ranges the cup hand rests on his knee (as it does at 123.4, which reads well). At 58.2 lower it over 0.25 s before the jaw drop, or play it as a gag: the cup tips forward in his slack hand at 59.0 and he rights it at 60.4. Mouth `o`, brows up, no blink 59.0-60.2 as planned. In the 115-117.8 close-up also shift the camera or the caliph about 60 px sideways: the tree trunk currently rises exactly out of the centre of his crown.

### 5. 53.3-58.2, MAJOR: the "hundred of each kind" high view is the weakest image in the film
The crane lands on a flat red slab that fills three quarters of the panel, with tray icons about 45 px wide in a block that uses under half the width, three dark red blotches on the rug, and the owner, caliph and vizier cut off at the chest by the bottom edge of the panel. At 53.5 the frame is two flat colour bands with nothing in them. This is the comic escalation of the first half (مئة 56.32) and it should be the most abundant picture so far; at the moment it is the emptiest. The counter already shows 100 and a second red "100!" burst is stamped beside it, so the number is said twice.
Fix: (a) make the 10 x 10 block fill the panel: about 80 px per tray, block roughly 820 px wide, centred, bottom row near panel y 1000; (b) remove the dark blotches; (c) put the three watchers fully inside the frame, small, along the bottom edge on the lawn with heads tilted up and eyes following the rows as they fill, or remove them from this shot entirely (the cut to the caliph on حتى gives the reaction anyway); (d) keep the counter, drop the extra "100!" burst, and let the counter slam with a 1-frame scale pop on مئة 56.32; (e) on كل 57.36 the two extra blocks should arrive with different dishes that can be told apart (sweets, jugs) at the same tray size, sliding in from the sides while the first block shrinks to make room. Start the first trays at 53.9, not 54.2, so the frame is never empty.

### 6. 86.0-98.8, MAJOR: the hundred-window house is small, static and bottom-heavy
For 13 s the facade sits in the upper two thirds with a blank green lawn across the whole lower third. The owner as presenter is about 150 px tall in the bottom left corner. The three waving busts planned for جارية (89.92) are not readable (a few tiny heads in the bottom row). The inset on كل (91.72) is good. The three waves (طعامًا 94.72, وشرابًا 95.68, وأشياء 96.76) are swarms of very small marks, the DRINK wave especially; nothing visibly collects at the door, and the FOOD / DRINK / COMFORTS tags sit on the empty lawn, away from the action.
Fix: reframe so the house base is near panel y 1100 and the facade is about 15 % larger; lawn strip no more than 150 px. Owner 1.6 x larger, standing in front of the facade's left edge, pointer tip actually touching what he names. On جارية light three windows first as large busts (same drawing as the inset, about 90 px) that wave, then the rest fill. For each wave send about 25 icons at twice the present size and stagger them, and let a visible heap grow at the door: fruit and trays, then jugs, then cushions; place each tag beside its part of the heap as it lands. Add the slow 3 % push the plan allows from 86.6 to 98.8. The heap at the door is what the servants "gather" in the next scene, so it should look like the same heap at the gate at 101.4.

### 7. 152.9-161.5, MAJOR: the reversal (the calm owner finally astonished) is underplayed and the gold rays ghost the cast
This is named in the plan as the emotional payoff. In the render the camera centres on the chests; the owner is at the far left, overlapped by the caliph's arm, and his "me?" (التاجر 155.22) and hands to cheeks (157.0) are small gestures in a corner. The pale gold ray wedges from the open chests are drawn over the vizier and guards, who appear as washed-out ghosts for eight seconds. ALL OF IT! (كله 154.12) is clipped by the right edge of the panel ("ALL O").
Fix: draw the rays behind all figures (or clip them to the area above the chest lids and keep them off faces), at about half the present opacity. Move ALL OF IT! fully inside the panel, above the chests and left of the rays. On التاجر 155.22 cut or push to a two-shot of owner and caliph at about zoom 1.9 with the nearest chest in the right third: owner separated from the caliph by at least a body width, finger to his own chest, a look over his shoulder at 155.7, then on 157.0 `cheeks`, `tremble`, eyes wide, no blink, and the kneel. Hold the caliph calm and smiling beside him. Pull back out for the balloon at 157.78 and AND MORE! at 160.0 as now.

### 8. 131.2-137.6, MINOR: the light shaft washes out the caliph while he thanks God
The plan asks for a quiet, simple beat. The shaft is a hard-edged milky wedge drawn over the caliph, the vizier and a guard, so the caliph is a pale orange figure for six seconds and his raised palms and closed eyes on الله (132.58) lose contrast. From 135.0 a second fan of rays over the food adds clutter.
Fix: draw the shaft behind the figures (after the wall and trunks, before the characters), soften it to roughly 15 % opacity with two or three narrower beams, and drop or reduce the ray fan on the food. Keep ONE OF HIS PEOPLE and the owner's bow.

### 9. 39.0-138.0, MINOR: a guard's spear lies diagonally across the vizier and into the caliph
After the guards sit (39.0-39.8) one spear is held slanting down to the left. In every garden two-shot for the next 100 s a brown pole crosses the vizier's body and ends at the caliph's waist, looking as if it is attached to him.
Fix: after the "cushion too soft" gag settles (about 40.0) the guard plants the spear upright beside him like the other guard, or lays it flat on the grass behind the cushions. Check stills at 59.3, 105.8 and 126.5.

### 10. 40.2-42.6 and 85.6-85.8, MINOR: a blank dark green band at the top of the panel
On the pull-out for ROOM FOR EVERYONE the camera shows above the top of the garden world: a flat dark green strip about 90 px high sits above the canopy for 2.4 s. It shows again for a frame or two in the whip at 85.7.
Fix: clamp the camera so the canopy or sky fills the top edge, or extend the canopy and sky drawing upward by 150 px in the `inside` view.

### 11. 68.7-79.3 and 124.7-129.7, MINOR: thought-cloud contents are small or covered
Suspicion cloud: the spying owner is about 75 px tall on screen (the plan asks for 110 or more) and the lower half of the cloud is empty sand. At 73.8 the "?" lands exactly on the owner figure, and the DID YOU KNOW? tag covers the cloud's tail bubbles. NO IDEA! (77.5) is small and sits on the cloud's edge.
Fix: scale the inner scene about 1.5 x and move its ground line down so the figures use the cloud; place the "?" in the right half of the cloud beside the owner; put DID YOU KNOW? left of the tail; NO IDEA! larger, over the owner's own head outside the cloud.
Wealth cloud: the "tray = coin" equation is tiny (the "=" reads as an 8) and the growing pile is cut by the panel top from 126.0. Enlarge the equation to about twice its size across the top of the cloud, and keep the pile inside the panel until المال 125.82, when it bursts the cloud outline, not the panel edge.

### 12. 99.1-105.4, MINOR: the gathering chain is crowded and its arrows cross faces
Four servants stand shoulder to shoulder directly behind the seated owner and the THE USUAL arrow runs across two servants' faces and the owner's turban. The pass-along (جمع 101.44) is hard to follow, and the kneeling presentation on وقدموه (105.44) is lost in the group.
Fix: space the servants about 30 % wider and half a step behind and to the left of the owner so each pair of hands is visible; lift the arrow to arc above the heads; let one item (the fruit tray) travel hand to hand in clear silhouette. On وقدموه one servant steps forward alone, kneels in front of the caliph with the tray in clear space and the caliph takes the pomegranate.

## Subtitles
Arabic is verbatim, large and in sync; English is accurate to the Arabic and correctly placed. Two three-line Arabic chunks (44.2-53.2 and 122.0-131.2) push the English to the full width of the caption panel with almost no side margin ("...VESSELS OF COOL", "...WHEN HE GRASPED HOW"). It stays readable; if the caption code allows, reduce the English size by about 8 % for chunks whose Arabic takes three lines, or allow the English a third line. Do not change text or timing.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- Empty beat before a group pop-in from behind tree trunks (30.7-31.9): a clean recipe for "suddenly".
- Suspicion thought cloud that is crossed out with a two-stroke red X and then pops in a narration gap.
- Speech balloon that sags and blows away for "it was not X".
- Coin-stamp insert panel (lift, strike, CLINK!, coin flips out) as a layer over the scene.
- Rule worth writing into the guide: overlays (bursts, arrows, rays, light shafts, held props) must be checked against every face and every key number at the beat they land on; arrows start at the object, not the label, and are routed around heads.
