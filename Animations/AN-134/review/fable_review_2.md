## Remaining notes (polish, no v3 required)

### A. 167.2-168.2 and 136.2-137.4: pointer arrows still land on the owner's head
In the final close-up the ONE CUP OF WATER pointer comes straight down from the label, crosses the left edge of the turban and ends on his brow, beside the goblet rather than on it. The same fault exists on ONE OF HIS PEOPLE (136.2-137.4): the arrow runs down through the turban and its tip sits on his cheek. THIS MERCHANT (128.5-129.0) stops on top of the turban, which is fine.
Fix if a later pass happens: in 167.2-168.2 place the label lower left (about panel x 150, y 700 in the close-up framing) with a short horizontal pointer that ends on the goblet bowl, never above the head. For ONE OF HIS PEOPLE end the arrow 20 px above the turban tip, as THIS MERCHANT does.

### B. 53.5-54.7: the high view opens on an empty red rug
53.5 is flat red with a yellow strip, 53.7 shows a row of canopy crowns sliding across the rug, and only one to nine trays are down by 54.5. For about a second the "abundance" picture is the emptiest in the film.
Fix: start the first row at 53.5 as the crane lands (rows arriving every 0.25 s instead of filling tray by tray), or hold the crane on the three watchers until 53.9 and cut to the rug with the first two rows already down. Keep the canopy out of the high view, or let it pass before the cut.

### C. 148.5-151.0: the cross-bar on the 7 reads as a strike-through
The bar extends left past the stem to the comma, so the number reads "3,-700,000", and during the count-up every 7 looks struck out ("3,057,537" at 149.0). It does prevent the 7/1 confusion and the number is legible, so this is cosmetic.
Fix: shorten the bar to about 40 % of the digit width, centred on the stem, 60 % of its present thickness; or drop the bar and rely on the wider letter spacing.

### D. 168.5-172.7: the final wide shot is small and its two labels touch
The cast and chests occupy a band about a quarter of the panel high; the lower 40 % is empty road. The 3,700,000 DIRHAMS tag sits on top of the ONE CUP OF WATER pointer, and the moral arrow passes over the blue-capped servant's face behind the owner. The shrug on يتوقعها (170.54) is a small movement of one arm.
Fix: zoom about 1.25 with the ground line near panel y 1050; move ONE CUP OF WATER to the left of the owner's head with a short pointer to the goblet so the two tags are side by side, cup tag left and dirhams tag right; move that servant one step left. For the shrug, raise the free arm to shoulder height with the palm up and tilt the head (`tip`) about 8 degrees, held 170.54-172.4.

### E. 115.0-117.7: the close-up face could do more
The face is now unobstructed, but the caliph's expression in the zoom is close to neutral for nearly three seconds. On رآها (115.06) a slow widening of the eyes with brows up, and on عادي a single look toward the owner (`gaze` left) and back, would use the shot.

### F. 99.1-108.4: presentation still crowded
Optional: remove the servant standing between owner and caliph from 105.0 so the kneeling servant with the pomegranate tray is alone in that gap.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- Diagram scene with a presenter and a telescoping pointer, a counter tied to window fill, and three labelled heaps growing at the door (86-99): a good pattern for "N people each send X".
- Prop gag for astonishment: the cup tips in a slack hand on the jaw drop and is righted a second later, which keeps the mouth visible.
- Rule for the guide: a pointer arrow ends above a head or on the named prop, never on the face; when a label and its target are both near a head, put the label beside the prop and use a short horizontal pointer.
- Rule for the guide: a crane or cut to a "counting" surface should land with the first rows already present, so no frame is a flat colour field.
