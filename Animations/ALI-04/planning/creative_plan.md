## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (0-16.3):** the film opens on the last picture of episode 3. The merchant asks for a safe yard; Ali Baba, all kindness, opens the gate himself. Six jars thud down, too heavy for oil. Morgiana has seen the ring.
- **Reveal for the audience (16.3-33.3):** a band of moonlight shows the leader inside the merchant (17.32). Ring insert with the split stone (20.84-24.5). The jars in cutaway: five men, one jar of oil (26.42-31.4). WAIT FOR MY SIGNAL. Tape transition LATER THAT NIGHT...
- **Inciting (33.3-45.5):** Morgiana's lamp dies; she goes to the nearest jar for oil and a jar whispers IS IT TIME? (42.9).
- **Turn (45.5-52.3):** she stands like a statue (45.5), an eye close-up, and she reads the whole row: not ordinary jars.
- **Comic courage (52.3-65):** she answers as the leader, NOT YET. (55.52), tests a second jar (62.56), and runs.
- **Decision (65-70.2):** in the kitchen: not alone against five; the sleepers upstairs; the neighbours and the guard.
- **Rising action in whispers (70.2-105.6):** two doors tapped, the boy sent running, the gates shut without a sound, a creak, a sneeze stopped, guards on tiptoe, two gates sealed, the long watch at the window while the moon moves, and the signal (104.0).
- **Climax (105.6-117.8):** pebbles tick on the lids; nobody answers because every lid is being sat on. He opens the door: impact frame CAUGHT! (112.46) and a pan across everyone waiting.
- **Surprise and resolution (117.8-131.9):** he goes over the back wall leaving cloak and turban in the captain's hands. Five heads rise into a ring of spears: UH OH. They are helped out, stiff and sheepish, and walked to the judge. Morgiana finally fills her lamp.
- **Cliffhanger (131.96-140):** on the roof above the arch the leader watches his men pass, looks at his ring and plans. TO BE CONTINUED...
- **Emotional arc:** warm and unsuspecting (Ali Baba); smug (leader); frightened for one breath, then exact and brave (Morgiana); conspiratorial fun (neighbours); cold resolve at the end.
- **Moral:** a calm, clever head and good neighbours beat a hidden enemy.

## 3. Character acting
All characters on twos (`celStep(t, 12)`), blinks, lip flaps and jar eyes on threes (`celStep(t, 8)`).
- **Morgiana** thinks with her eyes first: eyes snap, the body stops one drawing later, the scarf tail swings on and settles (`swing`, `lagged`). Fear is one beat (hand to mouth, 50.58), then she is exact: counting on fingers, the comic leader stance for NOT YET., knees that wobble only after the bluff (`tremble`), flat palms for "stay", one drumming finger at the window that she stops herself. Arms folded and one eyebrow at the reveal. A straight pointing arm at the wall.
- **Oil merchant / leader:** heavy footfalls and brows under a merchant's smile (lid 0.3); the smile drops off in three held drawings when he is alone (15.2). Showman with the jars. At the shutter only his hand acts: drumming, flicking, clenching. The take at 112.46 is the one big stretch (sq 1.12, turban hop). Undisguised he is the cold leader of episode 3, cape three drawings late.
- **Ali Baba:** whole-hearted welcome (arms wide, sq 1.05), sympathy sweat while the jars are unloaded, and a sleepy double take (eyes, head, step) at 116.36.
- **Thieves in jars:** five poses and five rhythms (`stagger(i)`): thumbs up, yawn, fan, sleeper, nail biter. Under the lids they are only eyes. Out of the jars they stay folded for a moment, legs asleep.
- **Neighbours:** old woman fierce and tiny with her broom; baker big, sleepy and gentle (the sneeze in three rising gasps, the bounce on the lid with `landSquash`); the boy eager (wrong-hand salute, slippers off, gone in six drawings).
- **Guards:** `sneakChain` single file with the accordion bump (89.96); tall thin, short stout, young; the captain all moustache and eyebrows, a reflex pebble catch at 107.56.
- **Cat:** the witness from episode 3; echoes Morgiana's tiptoe half a beat late.
- **Moving holds:** `idleSway`, `breathe`, scarf, cape, plume, neck cloths, leaves, lantern flicker in every pause; the two deliberate freezes keep eyes and light alive. `groundShadow {flat: true}` under everyone.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **The audience knows everything before Morgiana does** (cutaway at 26.42), so her walk to the jar is suspense, and her deduction (ghost outlines, 48.8) is a pleasure to watch.
- **Whispering as a visual rule:** small thin balloons, tiptoe gaits, slow gates, wrapped spear butts; the single loud moment is the lantern reveal.
- **Humour without harm:** the beard-scarf bluff, three lids dropping like piano keys, the cat's echo, the helmet over the eyes, the sneeze, the baker bouncing on a lid, the thief who pulls his lid shut again.
- **Payoffs:** the errand for lamp oil ends with the lamp lit from the one honest jar (128.84); the ring seen at the handclasp (9.98) returns in the inset (49.8), at the shutter (102.86) and on the roof (133.36).
- **Colour as story:** apricot evening, teal and violet-rose night with amber lantern pools, the warm kitchen, blue and brass guards against indigo thieves; vermilion and turquoise remain the brightest notes.
- **Series seeds:** the leader now knows who beat him (turquoise scarf in his thought cloud); the neighbours and the courtyard return in episodes 5 and 6.
