The man told Ali Baba, “I am an oil merchant, and I need a safe courtyard for my jars tonight.” Ali Baba welcomed him and allowed him to place the jars in the yard.

The man was the disguised thieves’ leader. He wore a silver ring with a split blue stone and had hidden men in most jars, leaving oil in just one.

Morgiana went into the yard seeking lamp oil and heard a whisper from one jar: “Is it time?” She stopped moving and realized that the jars were not ordinary.

Morgiana whispered back, “Not yet.” She moved carefully to another jar and heard the same question. She hurried to the kitchen to think of a way to protect the household.

Morgiana woke trusted neighbors and asked one to fetch the town guards. They quietly closed the courtyard gates and stood near the jars without attracting the men’s attention.

The guards arrived quietly and took positions at both gates to prevent the thieves from escaping. Morgiana watched the jars from the kitchen window until the leader gave his signal.

When the leader gave his signal, nobody answered. He opened the gate and saw guards and neighbors waiting around the courtyard.

The leader escaped over a back wall, but his men remained trapped in the jars. Guards carefully let them out and took them to the judge. The leader kept planning his return.
