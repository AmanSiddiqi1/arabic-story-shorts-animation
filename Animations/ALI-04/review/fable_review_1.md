# ALI-04 "The Merchant of Oil" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 1 (v1)

Film reviewed: `Animations/ALI-04/renders/ALI-04_v1.mp4` (140.0 s). Sources: all 24 contact strips in `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second), 38 panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited below, 9 native-resolution crops, and a frame-difference scan of the panel (10 fps) for frozen stretches. Times are seconds on the film. World x values are the courtyard strip's (jars 890..1465, street gate 730, back gate 1590, guest door 1930, guest window 2040..2140).

Not checked: sound was not listened to; stepped cel timing (twos and threes), landing squash and follow-through fall between sampled frames and cannot be judged from stills.

## Verdict: REVISE

No BLOCKER. Every narrated event is on screen and in order, subtitles are sharp and complete in both languages, TO BE CONTINUED is present, there are no broken frames, and nothing harmful is shown (the capture is spears held level, raised hands and a walk to the judge).

It is REVISE and not PASS WITH NOTES for three reasons, all confined to single shots:
- the resolution ("the guards carefully let them out", 126.4-129.5) cannot be read: eleven figures stand in one overlapped block in front of the jars (note 1);
- the cliffhanger is held without the villain: the leader walks off the roof at 135.5 and the last 4.5 s are the procession (note 2);
- the signal and the "nobody answered" gag have no visible pebble and lettering over two faces (note 3).
The series faults found in ALI-03 (jars that read as baskets, door marks over the cast) are also here, and in this episode the jars are the story (note 5).

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 122.2-129.5 s: the capture and the release are one block of bodies
- **What is wrong.** From the cut at 122.2 seven people stand shoulder to shoulder in front of the jar row and hide it; the thieves' heads appear as a second row floating above them; the spears lie level across the old neighbour's and the baker's faces (stills 123.6, 125.0). From 126.4 the freed thieves, the captain, Morgiana and Ali Baba push into the same block: eleven overlapped figures (stills 127.6, 128.8). No thief is seen leaving a jar. The counter shows only 3, 4, 5 (128.2-128.7) over nothing that can be counted. The plum thief pulling his lid shut again and Morgiana filling her lamp from the oil jar (the payoff of her errand, planned at 128.84) cannot be found; she is wedged between the captain and Ali Baba.
- **Why it matters.** "His men remained trapped in the jars" and "the guards carefully let them out" are the resolution of the episode. The picture has to be jars with men in them, then men out of them.
- **Fix.**
  - Clear the jars. Frame the whole row (camera x about 1180). Nobody stands in front of jars 1 to 5. Neighbours (old woman, boy, baker) in a loose group at x 700..840, left of jar 1; Morgiana and Ali Baba at x 1520..1620, right of jar 6. Two guards at each end of the row, half a step behind the jar line, spears upright or tipped about 25 degrees inward above the jar tops, never across a face.
  - "رجاله" 122.96: the five heads rise one after another (`stagger(i)`, as now), hands up beside the ears so the arms read. "بقوا" 123.84: eyes go left, right, to the spear points. "محاصرين" 124.48: UH OH. as now; the plum man sinks and pulls his lid over himself, the baker lifts it off again with two fingers on "الجرار" 125.64.
  - Release, one man per beat, starting at 126.4 as planned: a guard offers a hand, the man climbs out with a small hop and `landSquash`, and stays folded and stiff in front of his own jar (y 925). Landings at 126.4, 127.0, 127.5 ("أخرجهم" 127.35), 128.0, 128.5; the counter ticks 1 to 5 on each landing and holds 5 to 129.3.
  - "بحذر" 128.84: Morgiana, alone at jar 6 (x 1465, the stained oil jar), tips it and her lamp lights (`abGlow`, a small ring so the eye finds it); Ali Baba beside her finishes his sleepy double take.

### 2. 131.96-140.0 s: the cliffhanger is held without the leader
- **What is wrong.** The leader rises behind the parapet at 132.0, says I WILL RETURN. at 134.2-135.0, then walks off the roof to the left and is gone by 135.6. TO BE CONTINUED... plays for 4.4 s over the procession and the cat. The ring is never seen in this shot (planned at 133.36). His thought cloud holds a 20 px brown door and a 25 px turquoise chip (crop at 134.6): nobody can tell that he now knows who beat him. Morgiana, planned in the last frame, is a sliver behind the old neighbour at the right edge (139.8).
- **Why it matters.** This is the frame held longest and the seed of episodes 5 and 6: the villain above, the house below, the woman he has noticed.
- **Fix.**
  - Keep the leader on the roof to the last frame (or let him sink behind the parapet at 139.3, eyes last). Arms on the parapet, cape three drawings late, `breathe` and a slow `idleSway` throughout.
  - "القائد" 133.36: he lifts the ringed hand in front of his chest, the ring drawn about twice its normal size for this shot, one `abGlint` on it. "يخطط" 134.04: the cloud shows ONE large picture, Morgiana's head and shoulders with the turquoise scarf (about 120 px tall), nothing else. "للعودة" 134.88: I WILL RETURN. as now.
  - Hold beats after the last word: 136.2 the fist closes over the ring; 137.2 his eyes follow the procession to the left (`gaze` -1, `lid` 0.35); 138.2 the eyes come back to Morgiana's door and stay. The cat looks up at him at 136.8.
  - Morgiana stands alone in her lit doorway (x 960), lamp burning, clear of the old neighbour, who sweeps a full body width behind the last guard. Slow the procession a little so the captain is still in frame at 139.5.

### 3. 105.6-110.3 s: the signal has no pebble, and "nobody answered" is hidden
- **What is wrong.**
  - 105.6-106.3: the two TIK! boxes sit on the baker's and the boy's faces (crop at 106.0). No pebble can be seen at any size; the throwing hand (106.5-107.2) is in a separate shot, so cause and effect never share a frame.
  - 105.5-109.5: the boy lying on jar 1 and the baker sitting on jar 2 are stacked into one shape with the frying pan and rolling pin, and at 107.5-108.7 they are cut by the left edge (still 108.2). The old neighbour and three guards stand in front of the jars, so "every lid is being held down" reads as "people standing about".
  - 99.7-105.2 (window view): the hidden watchers' feet and spear butts stick out below the jars (still 100.6), so the jars seem to have legs.
- **Why it matters.** This is the climax's joke and the proof that Morgiana's plan works: he signals, the lids try to answer, and cannot.
- **Fix.**
  - One frame holding both ends: world x about 1200..2200, jars 4 to 6 on the left, the guest window on the right. "حين" 106.08: the ringed hand flicks; a cream pebble (radius 8) flies on a dotted arc and hits lid 5; the lid hops 6 px; a small italic TIK! rises above the lid, no box, nothing over a face. "القائد" 107.00: second flick; "إشارته" 107.56: the captain's hand snaps up and catches it (a ring flash on the catch).
  - "لم" 108.87: pan left along the keepers, one per jar, each beside or on top of his own jar and never in front of another: the boy lying across jar 1, the baker sitting on jar 2 with hanging feet, the broom on jar 3, a spear butt each on jars 4 and 5 with the guard standing in the gap. Lead the pan so nobody is cut by the panel edge. "يجبه" 109.16: lid 2 bumps up 8 px under the baker, he bounces (`landSquash`) and looks down; "أحد" 109.78: his finger goes to his lips, the boy grins.
  - Window view: put the hiding watchers on y 880 or less (or clip them at the jar base) so only helmet points, broom tip and nightcap show.

### 4. 38.4-45.5 s: Morgiana hears the whisper while doing nothing
- **What is wrong.** From 38.5 to 42.5 she stands beside jar 1 with one hand at her head, facing the camera (stills 41.6; quiet stretch 38.8-40.3). The planned reach for oil and the frozen hand are not there, so the eyes under the lid at 41.5 and IS IT TIME? at 42.9 come with no action for them to interrupt. The lamp is a small yellow shape at her waist.
- **Why it matters.** The audience already knows what is in the jar; the suspense is her hand going toward the lid.
- **Fix.** Turn her to the jar (`dir` 1, a small `tip` in). "للمصباح" 37.46: she shakes the lamp, upside down, one drop (make the lamp about 1.4 times larger for this scene). "فسمعت" 39.03: her free hand reaches for the lid of jar 1; "خافتًا" 40.30: the lid lifts 10 px by itself before she touches it and her hand stops a finger's width away, eyes wide, scarf tail swinging on late; "الجرار" 41.72: the eyes in the gap; 42.9 IS IT TIME? as now. The hand stays frozen in the air through the statue beat at 45.5 and only comes back to her chest after the eye close-up (47.5). The cat arches as now.

### 5. Series note (inherited from episode 3, apply the ALI-03 fixes here)
- **Jars read as wicker baskets, all film.** The cream net covers every jar edge to edge, and the six stand touching, so the row is one orange lattice wall (stills 27.4, 41.6, 93.5). Here they must read as glazed jars big enough for a man: terracotta with a hard highlight band, dark neck, flat lid, two rope hoops and a carrying loop instead of the net; 15 to 20 units of wall showing between neighbours. The oil jar keeps its stain. Same for the mule panniers at 0-12 (about 30 px, the planned lid lifting at 6.6 is invisible; let one near jar be large enough for the lid and two eye-whites, with a glint ring).
- **Door marks over the cast.** A white cross is drawn on a thief's shoulder through the final hold (crop at 136.3; 135.7-140), and the red dot floats in the open, lit doorway of door 2 at 133.0-135.5 and beside the captain at 129.5-131. Marks belong to the wall or the door leaf: draw them before the cast and hide a leaf's mark when the door is open.
- **Near-black arch.** 7.0-7.8: the street arch is a black ball at the left edge beside the mules. Lighten it to the violet it has at 132.
- **Balloon tails** are wide dark wedges (WELCOME! 9.0-10.0, WAIT FOR MY SIGNAL. 28-29); use the thin tail.

### 6. 33.3-64.2, 78.4-96.0, 105.5-129.5 s: the courtyard is one picture
- **What is wrong.** Every courtyard shot is the same three bands at the same size: sky in the top 45%, the wall with jars and figures as a strip in the middle (figures about 200 px tall in a 1196 px panel), bare violet paving with a few tufts in the bottom 35%. Wall, paving and sky are all violet. Only the camera x and the moon change (compare stills 41.6, 55.8, 63.3, 86.3, 93.5, 123.6).
- **Why it matters.** The plan's own warning, and the owner's "full scenes" point: the lower third is empty for most of the film.
- **Fix (dressing and paint, not push-ins).**
  - A foreground pass on the paving, different in each zone and sliding a little faster than the yard: by the kitchen wing a well or a water trough with a bucket and a washing basket; in front of the jar row a low border of potted herbs and the cat's bowl; by the guest wing a bench with a rug and a fallen lemon or two. Keep it below the figures' feet.
  - Paint the night paving cooler than the wall (blue-teal against the rose wall, as the plan's "teal and violet-rose"), and give each lantern a warm amber pool on the ground. From 88.4, when guards and neighbours bring lanterns, the pools multiply; the reveal at 112.5 already does this well.
  - Vary the height once or twice: for the two-person scenes 38.4-58.4 a modest zoom of about 1.2 with the ground line near 72% of the panel, so Morgiana is a third taller and the row still runs out of frame; keep the wide for 48.5-50.5, 92.5-96 and 113-117.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Cutaway prop with an occupant** (`oilJar` `cut` / `peek`): works well at 26.4-29 and 95.0; worth moving to the Library once approved.
- **View through a window frame** (dark frame as a front pass, 99.7-105.2): a cheap second framing for any long one-set scene.
- **Ghost-outline deduction** (48.7-50.5): outline and tick per object as a character's eyes pass it.
- **Group spacing helper:** place n figures with a minimum gap and two depth rows, culled to the camera, so crowds never stack (needed by notes 1, 3, 7; asked for in ALI-03 note 10).
- **Counter tied to landings** (`countAs`): the number ticks when its object arrives (note 1; same request as ALI-03 note 7).
- **Marks parented to a surface** and the thin balloon tail, as in the ALI-03 review.
