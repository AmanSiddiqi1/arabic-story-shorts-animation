# AN-090 The Sweep and the Noble Lady: creative plan

Film length 175.1 s (MP3 172.10 s, last word ends 171.96 s, THE END held to 175.1). 16 scenes, 159 timed actions in `creative_plan.json` (the storyboard of record; this file explains the direction). All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`.

**Content rule for this film.** The story turns on an affair and a revenge. The staging is family-appropriate and discreet throughout: symbols instead of events (a table conversation and a window sky for "the night", a rose and a cracked heart for the betrayal, a folding screen for the bath). The two leads never touch except when the purse is handed over. No beds, embraces, kisses or undress. No blow is ever shown. Every such choice is listed in `owner_questions` (11 items).

## 3. Character acting

- **The sweep.** Three looks on one face (rags, clean, pilgrim). In rags: head low, short quick steps, gaze on the ground. Clean: stands taller (sq 1.04) but keeps darting eyes; he never quite believes it. Signature gestures: sniffing his own sleeve (55.04 recoil, 68.12 bliss, 105.22 shrug) and pointing at his own chest (48.04, 144.9). Prayer pose written once: dip (`anticipate`), `aL/aR` raised with `PERSON_HANDS.palmsUp`, gaze up, then a one-eye peek (`wink`) at 9.5, 166.2 and 172.6. Grief at 157.0 follows the guide's recipe: slow head drop, `wet`, one tear, long blink, a breath.
- **The lady.** Composed: lid 0.35, chin high, slow blinks. Three masks: bored (37.0), the one-sided smile of a decision (46.3), steel when she tells the secret (128.3). Mask off twice when he cannot see: 89.6 and after the door shuts at 125. Anger only at 137.4-140.6 (fist, planned `flush`, stamp). The pointing arm at 47.3 and 145.8 is one shared pose object.
- **The husband.** Enters chest out with cape lag, shrinks as he sees her back; apology escalates in three offers (gift, flowers, turban to the floor); relief breath on 123.1.
- **Pilgrims A, B, C.** Leader, eager second, nervous old third: never in unison (`stagger`), each with his own gesture (flush, hands on hips, head hold; point, open palms, cupped shout). At the end they sit like children and laugh in turn.
- **The emir.** Authority by stillness, then the stifled laugh: `tremble` 2 units, lips shut, burst at 169.2.
- **The donkey.** The audience's stand-in: ears flatten at the smell (32.4), eyes in the dark (43.0), sits outside the shut gate (52.0), knees sag under gifts (100.0), deadpan look to camera (101.5), ear shrug (104.4), nuzzle (158.8).
- **Listeners always react:** guards lean from the smell, hidden eyes follow the apology like a tennis match, the real maid freezes at "الخادمات" (136.6).
- Everyone blinks (`LIFE.t = t`); every pose change has anticipation and `settleTo`.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the smell as a character (`stinkLines` that beckon the lady, make guards cover noses, and finally turn into a rose); pedalling feet while carried; the donkey's rump left outside the alley; SORRY x3 with the candle burning down; the search ring rejecting clean men; the emir failing to keep a straight face; the last gag (he prays again).
- **Suspense:** eyes in the alley as spears pass; the knocks on the eighth night; trembling eyes in the lattice.
- **Surprise:** HIM!; the red X on the punishment cloud; FILTHIEST!.
- **Emotion:** her fading smile hints at the secret long before it is told; the eight moons going dark; the window light going out; the reach that falls.
- **Setup and payoff:** opening prayer and cloud repeated at 163-166 with new meaning; the pointing arm; the sleeve sniff; moons lit, then dark.
- **Lettering diet:** 12 words plus two cards (listed in owner question 9).
