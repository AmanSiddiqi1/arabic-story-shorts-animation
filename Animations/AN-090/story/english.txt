During the pilgrimage, a man at the Kaaba prayed that a woman would quarrel with her husband again. Pilgrims were scandalized and brought him before their emir.

The emir ordered his punishment, but the man asked to tell his story. He used to collect slaughterhouse refuse on a donkey when he saw a wealthy lady's procession and guards.

The sweeper hid in an alley to let the procession pass, but the lady pointed him out. Guards took him to her palace, where he feared punishment for the smell of his work.

Instead, servants bathed him, dressed him richly, and led him into a splendid hall. The lady fed him delicacies, leaving him convinced he was dreaming.

He spent the night with her and received fifty gold pieces. She summoned him repeatedly for eight nights, rewarding him each morning, without explaining why.

On the eighth night he hid as the lady’s husband returned. The husband begged forgiveness, and she reconciled with him. Afterwards, she explained her secret.

She had caught her husband with a servant and, in anger, vowed revenge by seeking the filthiest man in Baghdad. She chose the sweeper and now ended their affair.

Sad at losing her, the sweeper went on pilgrimage and prayed that she would quarrel with her husband again. After hearing his story, the emir released him.
