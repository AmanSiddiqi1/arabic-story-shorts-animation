# AN-116 Wardan the Butcher's Adventure with the Lady and the Bear: creative plan

Film length 173.61 s of narration + 3.0 s end hold (ends 176.6 s). 14 scenes, 179 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is the start of the named Arabic script word. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (same folder); this file explains the direction.

**Content note (read first).** The script contains an intimate act between the woman and the bear and two killings. This plan keeps all three **off-panel**: the film shows Wardan's reaction, a wall shadow and a dark panel, never the acts. No blood, no wounds, a clean cleaver throughout. The verbatim subtitles and the narration still say the words; that decision belongs to the owner (see section 8).

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Spine:** curiosity (0-20 s), enquiry (20-41), pursuit (41-64), discovery (64-85), violence and refusal (85-108), treasure (108-130), the twist at the gate (130-151), reward (151-173).
- **Conflict:** Wardan's curiosity against a secret that is guarded by a blindfold, a mountain and a stone door; then Wardan against the woman's refusal; finally Wardan (who thinks he is caught) against the caliph (who already knows everything).
- **Surprises:** the bear revealed by the widening lamp (72.66); the caliph asking "about the woman and the bear" at the gate (136.8-139.0); the hoard that was waiting for Wardan alone (142.4-150.5); the caliph pushing the basket back (163.5).
- **Emotional arc:** amused curiosity, tension, shock, grim resolve, awe at the gold, false relief ("THE END?"), fear, wonder, gratitude, pride.
- **Ending:** match cut from the basket of gold to a fruit basket in his own market; banner WARDAN'S MARKET; THE END held 3 s.
- **Moral:** what is destined for a man reaches him; a just ruler leaves a man what he earned. Curiosity is dangerous, so the middle of the film is played seriously, not for laughs.

## 3. Character acting
- **Wardan** (`person`, costume `beanSeller`, brown tunic, white apron, red cap, cleaver in the sash). His face carries the film. Curiosity = raised brow (`brow -0.4`), moustache stroke (`PERSON_HANDS.moustache`), thinking saccades (`gazePlan` ease 0.07). Sneaking = planned `sneak` gait, `tip 0.12`, freezes of 3-4 frames with `noBlink`. Shock in S6 = `eyes 'wide'`, `sq 0.88` then `settleTo`, hands over the eyes, planned `flush`, then the one long stare to camera (`gaze [0, 0.05]`). After 84.3 his brow sets (`+0.5`): he stops being a spectator. In S11-S12 he is small again: `tremble`, `kneel`, `clasp`. In S13 the joy is a `hop` + `landSquash` with wet eyes.
- **The lady** (`person`, costume `lady`, dark blue and black, planned `faceCover` outdoors). Outdoors she never looks at anyone: eyes ahead, `lid 0.3`, exact gestures. Underground, unveiled, she is warm and at ease (humming sway while cooking). Grief at 95.24: head back, `talk 1`, `tremble 2`, then `kneel` with `wet` eyes and one tear; rage at 103.86: wet eyes dry to a glare (`lid 0.3`, `brow +1`).
- **The porter** (`servant`, bare feet, basket on a head strap): tired bob, `impactSquash` when the parcel lands, counts the shopping on his fingers, mimes the blindfold, gropes (`reachBoth`, grope-tap steps), shrugs.
- **The bear** (new `bear`, s 1.1): slow and heavy. Reveal = sniff and a slow blink. Eats with both paws, eyes closing with pleasure, ear twitches; sleeps with a big `breathe`. It never threatens anyone: its size is the threat.
- **The caliph** (`caliph`, age 0.3, on the white `horse` with `rideOn`): stillness is his power. Half lids, chin up, one finger, one straight point, one calm palm. The first smile comes only at 140.42; the warmest at 163.5.
- **Supporting:** the shop boy (`child: true`) is a 5 s comic act (apron over the head, salute, crate); two soldiers (planned `soldier` preset) always move 4 frames apart (`stagger`) and bump helmets once.
- **Secondary motion:** hanging joints, shop sign, lamp and banner on `swing()`; the basket bounces on each `footfalls` hit; cap hops on big reactions; dust on doors.

## 4. Camera and composition
- World 1000 x 1230, ground and floors at y 900; faces and action stay above y 1000 so nothing sits near the caption panel.
- **Market:** establishing wide, push to the stall, `twoShot` for the porter dialogue with room above for the cloud.
- **Road (S4):** a 3000-wide `countryRoad` strip tracked with `camX`; pursuer screen-left behind cover, pursued screen-right, the mountain sliding in as the goal.
- **Cave (S5):** cutaway. Wardan is always at the left edge on the stair, in shadow; the lamp circle is the stage. The bear is revealed by widening the light, not by a cut.
- **S6:** close on Wardan at zoom 2.0 to 2.3 with the pillar filling the right third, so the room is physically out of frame.
- **S7 and S9:** the camera leaves the action at the decisive moment (wall shadow at 91.2; the lamp at 108.4) and returns to the result.
- **Gate:** low wide with the horse broadside across the road, then a two-shot that keeps the rider's and Wardan's faces and leaves the top of frame for the balloons and the cloud.
- **Ending:** pull-out from the basket to the whole market; THE END at y 520 between the banner and the heads.
- Transitions: cuts inside sequences; one `later` card (NEXT MORNING..., 41.34) with the camera reset hidden under it; a dissolve into sleep (85.2); a whip into the gate (129.96); a match cut (167.22).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** the growing coin stack and Wardan leaning further each day; the boy in the huge apron; the porter walking right past the hidden Wardan; THE END? in his own daydream one second before the caliph appears; soldiers bouncing off the door; BONK!
- **Suspense:** the blindfold explained before it is seen; freezes with only the pupils moving; the stair descent step by step; the tiptoe past the sleeping lady.
- **Surprise:** light-reveal of the bear; the caliph's balloon with the two silhouettes (he knows!); the hand mark that fits only Wardan.
- **Emotion:** her grief is played straight (kneel, tear, forehead on the bear's shoulder); Wardan bows his head, cap in hand, after the dark; the returned basket is the warm peak.
- **Setup and payoff:** the cleaver in the sash from the first shot; the WARDAN label at 0.36 and again at 169.5; the basket travels through the whole film (lamb, treasure, YOURS!, fruit).
