# The Thief and the Moonbeam: creative director review 2 (original vs v1)

Reviewed: `renders/The Thief and the Moonbeam - v1.mp4` (166.0 s) against the original and against `review/fable_review_1.md`. I watched all of v1 at 4 fps (my own v1-only strips of the animation panel, cut from the v1 MP4 so the figures are large enough to judge), plus full-size stills and 6 fps pulls of the key beats: waking, pretence, flashback, shock, whisper, wait, chant, grip, leap, crash, owner's entry, confrontation and ending. Times are film times.

Not checked by me: audio. I relied on the stated QC for narration sync, loudness and subtitles. Blinks and 2-4 frame settles cannot be confirmed at 4-6 fps; I saw eyelids at different heights in many frames, which is consistent with blinking.

**Verdict: v1 is clearly and meaningfully better than the original. It is not quite final: the film's central gag (gripping the moonbeam) still does not read, and there are four visible staging defects. Eight corrections are listed in section 5.**

## 3. Are the expressions, movements and interactions convincing?

**Convincing:**
- The owner waking (one eye, then the other, pupils tracking the feet) is the best-acted beat in the film.
- The wink at 49.3 and the wife's growing smile make the couple a team.
- The staggered shock at 93.3 gives the three thieves separate characters for the first time.
- The waiting beat (103.6-108.5), with the owner's one-eye peek under the Zs, is funny and clear.
- The chant builds properly to the seventh word.
- The crash has weight: stretch, a two-frame pancake squash, rebound, stillness, twitch.
- The ending plays as a small scene: look up, wave, exchange, head down.

**Not yet convincing:**
- The grip and hang (see correction 1). The leader appears to salute, then floats upright with straight legs in front of the moon.
- The flashback descents, which leave the cloud instead of travelling down the beam.
- The leader's return after "LATER..." (109.5-116.3), which is as flat as the original.
- The thief's cower from 143 to 150 is one held pose; the wife holds her hands at her collar from 137 to the end with only her face changing. Both are acceptable but stiff.

## 4. New defects in v1

1. **"PSST!" covers the leader's face**, 97.2-99.5. The balloon sits on his head while he is also tucked behind No. 2, so the speaker is invisible during "قال كبيرهم بصوت منخفض". Worse than the original, where it was only clipped by the frame.
2. **Flashback figure cropped by the panel edge**: 72.75-74.0 and again 89.75-90.6 he slides to the right edge and is cut in half; 76.5-78 a sliver of him sits at the edge during the camera pull-out; the cloud is empty 90.75-92.6.
3. **The enlarged cloud covers the hole and the roof edge** from 62 to 93, so the thieves appear to sit on the cloud and listen to it rather than at the hole. Readable, but the geometry is muddier than before.
4. **The owner's leap passes in front of his wife**, 141.6-142.0: he enters cut by the left frame edge, then his body fully hides her for about three frames.
5. **Headless companions**, 146.0-157.3: the camera tilts up far enough to show the two thieves' legs and torsos but cuts their heads off at the top (carried over from the original; more noticeable now that everything else is framed with care).
6. **At 152.0 the lowering stick crosses the thief's torso**, which for a moment looks like a prod or a blow.
7. **The wife's hand-over-mouth disc** (62-92) reads as a white ball or bubble at her mouth for 30 s (carried over).
8. 51.5: the leader's cap is clipped by the top of the frame while the three stand grinning at the lens.

No sync errors, popping props or disappearing elements seen. Nothing in v1 is worse than the original except items 1 and 4.

## 5. Corrections for the final version (in priority order)

1. **Make the moonbeam grip readable, in both places (P1).**
   - Leader, 128.40-129.60: both arms extended forward and up into the beam at about 45 degrees (use arm angles, as in the chant), fists stacked one above the other, clear of the head and silhouetted against the beam. No hand at the forehead.
   - Hang, 130.0-133.2: keep that two-fist pose above and ahead of him so the body hangs *from* the fists: whole-body `tip` of about 0.08-0.10, legs apart and swinging to rest over the first second, a slow 12 px sink. Break the 3.2 s hold at about 131.9: eyes open, look down, look at fists.
   - Flashback, 88.22-89.64: the young owner takes the identical two-fist pose on the cloud's beam and holds it 0.5 s. It must differ visibly from his "look" pose at 80-82.5 (one hand shading the eyes).
   - Owner in bed at 88.2: the same two fists.
2. **Keep the flashback descents inside the cloud and on the beam (P1).** 72.12-74.12 and 89.64-91.42: he travels along the cloud's drawn beam (re-angle or extend that beam inside the cloud if needed so there is a visible path down), never past the cloud outline and never touching the panel edge. 91.42-93.0: he is visible inside the cloud, landing on tiptoe with a finger to his lips. Remove the stray sliver at 76.5-78.
3. **Uncover the leader during the whisper (P1).** 96.8-99.5: move "PSST!" up and to the right so its tail points at his mouth and no part covers a face, show it for about 1.2 s only, and bring the leader out from behind No. 2 (draw him in front, or shift him 30-40 units right) so his cupped hand and face are seen.
4. **Stage the leader at the hole (P2; item 11 from review 1).** 108.9-116.3: he alone takes two steps to the lip (108.9-110.7); looks down the drop and wobbles back half a step (111.1-112.3); on "ونظر" at 112.34 his head tips up and his gaze climbs the beam to the moon by 114.5; 115.6 on "قال" a throat-clear and squared shoulders. Mates stay two steps behind him, fully inside the frame (No. 3 is cut by the left edge from 113.5 to 131: shift the camera about 40 units left or move the mates right).
5. **Clean up the owner's leap (P2).** 141.1-142.3: start him fully inside the frame on his own side of the bed, and take the arc clearly above the wife's head (or over the foot of the bed) so she is never hidden; she ducks and watches him go over. Landing time (about 142.2) stays.
6. **Fix the confrontation framing (P2).** 146.0-157.3: do not tilt up to the roof. Hold a tighter shot on the wife, owner and thief so their faces are larger. The pull-back at 157.5 that reveals the companions then lands harder. (If the roof must stay in shot, include the companions' whole heads.) While there, vary the thief's cower once at about 147 (peek between his arms, then cover again) and lower the stick to the owner's far side at 151.5-152.5 so it never crosses the thief.
7. **Let the thieves hear the word "secret" (P3).** 50.4-54.0: heads turn toward the hole in the order leader, No. 2, No. 3; a lean; No. 3 elbows No. 2. Keep the leader's cap inside the frame at 51.5.
8. **Limit the wife's hand-over-mouth (P3).** Show it only at 79.0-80.5 as the stifled giggle after her question, with shaking shoulders; from 62 to 78 and 80.5 to 92 she listens with visible reactions (smile, glances at her husband and at the ceiling).

Items 1-3 are needed before I would call the film final. Items 4-6 are worth doing in the same pass. Items 7-8 are optional.
