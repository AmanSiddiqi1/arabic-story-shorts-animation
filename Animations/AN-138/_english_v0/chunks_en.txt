Yahya ibn Khalid al-Barmaki returned home|from the caliph’s palace|and found a poor man waiting at his gate,|hoping for help.
The man explained|his desperate need.|Yahya asked neither about his family nor his occupation,|but ordered a comfortable room prepared for him inside.
Yahya instructed his treasurer|to give the guest one thousand dirhams daily|and serve him the same fine food enjoyed by the household,|without setting a deadline.
The man stayed for days and then weeks,|eating well and receiving the regular payment.|Each morning brought|another thousand dirhams.
After thirty days he held thirty thousand dirhams.|He began to fear|that Yahya would notice how much he had received|and demand some back.
Rather than risk the imagined demand,|he packed quietly|and slipped away with all the money,|saying no farewell.
When servants reported|his secret departure,|Yahya neither grew angry nor sent anyone after him.|He was surprised that the man had feared his generosity would end.
Yahya declared that even if the guest had stayed for life,|he would never have stopped the gifts or meals.|The man was gone,|though the welcome had never ended.
