# KD-007 The Three Fish: creative plan

Film length 168.40 s of narration + 3.5 s end hold = 171.9 s. All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard (9 scenes, 150 word-cued actions) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction behind it.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** Three fish hear that fishermen will return with nets. One leaves at once, one waits and escapes only by playing dead, one shrugs and is caught.

**Conflict.** Not fish against fishermen (the men are just doing their job) but each fish against her own habit: decision, hesitation, carelessness. The stream is open for exactly one day.

**Spine (22 beats in the JSON).**
- Setup, 0-42 s: a hidden pond, one narrow way out (planted at 10.56), three temperaments shown as three small gags.
- Inciting, 42-63 s: footfalls, two fishermen, "tomorrow, with nets" (56.04). Fear in three flavours (61.56).
- First answer, 63-81 s: Fish 1 goes now (69.18) and is safe (78.42).
- Second and third answers, 81-103 s: Fish 2 turns back from the open stream to think (87.82) while the sun sinks; Fish 3 says relax (94.92) and plays.
- LATER card at 103.58: NEXT MORNING...
- Turn, 104-125 s: nets, the stream blocked (110.02), Fish 2 bounces off the barrier (119.62), then the idea (123.84).
- Surprise, 125-147 s: the dead-fish float (131.66), carried to the river bank, the flip (142.90).
- Climax and loss, 147-158 s: Fish 3 finds no hiding place (150.96) and is netted (154.84).
- Resolution, 158-172 s: two fish free in the wide river, a look back, THE END.

**Planted surprises.** (a) Fish 3 floats belly-up for fun at 18.68 and Fish 2 watches; at 122.74 Fish 2 remembers it and at 129.12 copies the exact pose. The careless sister unknowingly gives the clever one her trick. (b) Fish 2's dash at 113.72 repeats Fish 1's dash at 69.18 shot for shot, and ends in mesh. (c) The rock Fish 3 bonked for a laugh at 34.38 is the rock that cannot hide her at 151.66.

**Emotional arc.** Ease, alarm, admiration, unease (the sinking sun), dread, glee at the trick, sorrow, bittersweet freedom.

**Ending and moral.** The last picture is two fish, not three, in open water. Moral: act while the way is open; wit may save you late, carelessness will not. One optional line under THE END: ACT IN TIME.

## 3. Character acting

**Fish 1 (wise).** Economy. Small tail beats, level gaze, lid about 0.2. She never looks twice: one saccade, then the move. Her anticipation is short (0.12-0.25 s). Her only soft moment is the look back at 79.14 (brow raised, slow blink) and the long blink at 166.6.

**Fish 2 (clever, hesitant).** The most acting. Big eyes that ping-pong (`gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)` saccades), half-lunges that settle back (`settleTo`) at 28.94 and 82.72, a fin raised like a reasonable hand at 84.12. In S6 the same body finally commits: full `anticipate`, stretch 1.15. At the barrier: impact squash, two weaker tries, wet eyes, fins on the mesh. The idea: saccades, lock on the cloud, brow from raised to sly, smirk, cheeks `puff`, one look to camera (`gaze [0, 0.05]`), the only one in the film. The float: `roll` 1, `limp` 1, and the peek (lid 1 to 0.8 and back) at 132.8, 136.3 and 140.8. In S9 she swims level with Fish 1 without glancing at her for permission: she has changed.

**Fish 3 (carefree).** Eyes shut and smiling for two thirds of the film, always late to react (`stagger`), loops and bubbles as idle business. In S8 the smile is gone for the first time: eyes wide, no anticipation before any move (panic), `turnFlip` with dur 0.25, wet eyes, dorsal flat, fins pulled in.

**Fishermen.** Leader and copycat (the Thief pattern). A: long strides, lean, `PERSON_HANDS.pointDown`, thumb over the shoulder, `headHold`, `palmsUp`. B: short quick steps, bumps into A, counts with a bobbing finger (`countedBeats`), `armsOut` to `reachBoth` to `grip` for the net mime, pinches his nose. Listener always looks at the speaker. They are never monsters: no gloating close-up at the catch.

**Rules for all.** Every character has a gaze target every frame; `LIFE.t = t` for blinks; groups never move in unison (0.2-0.3 s offsets); pose changes overshoot and settle; secondary motion on reeds (`swing`), lily pads, bubbles, the hanging net and the cap.

## 4. Camera and composition

- **Split level as the grammar.** People are in the top third looking down, fish in the lower part looking up, the water surface between them. Suspense comes from showing both levels at once (49.6, 132.4).
- **Right is safety.** The river is always to the right. Fish 1 and Fish 2 escape left to right; Fish 3's last bolt to the right ends at the barrier.
- **Tight, then wide.** The stream is the tightest framing (zoom 2.4 at 71.7-72.9) so that the pull-out to zoom 1.0 at 73.0-75.6 makes the river feel huge. S8 closes the frame with two walls of mesh; S9 opens it again.
- **Repetition.** The 113.72 dash reuses the camera keys of 69.18.
- **Push-ins on decisions:** Fish 1 at 63.4-65, Fish 2 at 121.0-124.4.
- **Transitions:** cuts inside the pond; whip pans between pond and river (81.1, 146.7); one LATER card (NEXT MORNING..., midpoint 103.58, the camera and sky change hidden under it); a dissolve into the last scene.
- Faces and key action stay between world y 250 and 1000; nothing important near the panel bottom. Shake is panel-only.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** B walking into A (46.62); the crumb theft (30.96); BONK (34.38); the nose pinch over a "dead" fish whose eye peeks behind his back (136.3); A throwing his cap down (145.28).
- **Suspense:** rings on the surface before the men are seen, used twice (42.54 and 104.66) so the second time needs no explanation; the frozen fish under the shadow; the float where only one eyelid moves.
- **Dramatic irony:** Fish 2 says "until tomorrow" pointing at a sun that is setting (86.14); the men carry her over their own barrier (138.62).
- **Surprise:** the BOING where the audience expects the stream; the flip.
- **Emotion:** Fish 2's face on the mesh (118.94); the empty pond and the one bubble (158.0); the fading pink shape in the last thought cloud (166.6).

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus

- Start from `Library/template/`; set `WW = 2200`, keep `WH = 1230`, clamp the camera to the wider world, never zoom below 1.0. Camera keys per scene are in each scene's `camera.notes` as `{x, y, zoom}`.
- World anchors: far-bank ground y 470 (feet 462), surface y 500, pond x 90..910 (bed y 1090), stream x 910..1330 (bed y 585), river from x 1330 (bed y 1160); ROCK (300, 1060), WEEDS (640, 1080), barrier x 915, NOOK (1950, 1110), BANK_SPOT (1450, 455).
- Draw order: back layer, people, water, fish and nets, front earth, surface line and lily pads, lettering, `finish` last.
- Fish: `x, y` is the body centre. Build `roll` like `turnFlip` (never thinner than 0.7). Define `FLOAT = {roll: 1, limp: 1, eyes: 'shut', mouth: 'open'}` once and use it at 18.68, in the cloud at 122.74 and at 129.12.
- All fish positions through `swimPath` so tails follow distance; idle `amp` 0.25.
- Fishermen ripples: `footfalls(X, t0, t1)` gives the ring times; draw `ripple()` at the surface point below each foot and `kick()` the lily pads.
- Mesh: lines interpolated across a deformable quad; `bulge` with `settleTo` for drag and the BOING; 35 % paper fill so fish read through it.
- Cue each action with `w(p, n)` from `timing.json`; the JSON gives the word and its time for every cue.
- SFX (`SND`, `master: 0.6`) only at: 5.95, 12.5, 21.4, 27.08, 42.15, 47.55, 66.7, 99.72, 103.45, 125.42, 146.7, 168.5. The splash (143.90) and BOING (118.2) fall on spoken words and stay silent.
- Lettering: no dashes; labels at least 28 px and headlines 44 px at 1280x720 scale; nothing over a face. If a frame feels busy, drop HMM..., FWUMP! and FREE first.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

1. The `fish` figure carries the film: approve three clearly different silhouettes on a still before staging.
2. "Placed her near the river" is staged as the far bank directly above the river at x 1450. Check on a still that it reads as beside the river.
3. Fish 3 is caught, not shown harmed; the film stops at the net leaving the water and holds on the empty pond. Confirm this restraint suits the audience.
4. `villager`, `dayCycle` and `thoughtCloud` are planned by KD-005 but not built; whichever story is animated first builds them.
5. ACT IN TIME under THE END is added moral lettering; remove it if only THE END is wanted.
6. Two effects that would want sound (splash, BOING) are silent because the narrator is speaking; the picture has to carry them.
