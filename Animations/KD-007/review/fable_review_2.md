## Notes for polish (optional; none triggers v3)

### 1. 70.0-70.6 s. NEW in v2. Fish 1's launch passes behind Fish 2
- What is wrong: Fish 2 was moved to (590, 640) for note 5, which is on Fish 1's dash line. At 70.2-70.5 Fish 2 is drawn on top of Fish 1, so for about 0.5 s the two read as one red and yellow shape (`extra/A.jpg`, bottom right). At 70.7 Fish 2 is still stuck to Fish 1's tail at the left edge.
- Why it matters: "سبحت فورًا" (70.06) is Fish 1's defining action, and its start is the one moment it is hidden.
- Fix: from 69.4 let Fish 2 sink about 90 world units (to about y 730) with a small backward recoil, as if making way, so that Fish 1's path is clear water. Alternatively raise Fish 1's dash line by 80 units for the first 200 units of travel. Keep Fish 2's gaze following Fish 1 out of frame.

### 2. 32.2-33.2 and 35.0-36.2 s. Fish 3's face is behind the weeds in her own portrait
- What is wrong: she starts inside the weed clump and rebounds back into it, so the clean view lasts only 33.5-34.9. CAREFREE (35.2-36.0) plays over a face striped by weed stems.
- Fix: start her path in front of or just left of the weeds (or draw her in front of the weed layer for this shot only), and end the rebound about 60-80 world units left of the clump so the smile at 35.1 is in clear water. Nothing else in the shot needs to change.

### 3. 100.7-101.2 s. PLAN? cloud over Fish 3's loop
- Fix: bring the cloud back only after the loop passes (from about 101.4), or offset it 80 units to the right of Fish 2 so the loop's top arc is clear.

### 4. 165.5-171.9 s. Last image: fish small and Fish 2 in the weeds
- What is wrong: the final hold is the widest river shot; the fish are about 55 px long, and Fish 2 sits behind the nook weeds (`extra/C.jpg`, bottom left). At 164.0-165.7 the two bodies also overlap heavily instead of swimming side by side.
- Fix: place the pair about 120 world units higher and 100 further left (open water, clear of the weeds and rocks), with a vertical gap of at least one body height between them, and hold the end shot at zoom about 1.25 centred on them, as long as THE END and ACT IN TIME stay clear.

### 5. 111.2-111.8 s. Dawn-to-day blend still flashes grey
- Fix: make the blend linear over the full 110.0-112.6, or blend yellow to blue through a pale warm tone rather than through grey (interpolate the two sky presets in one step, not via a mid preset).

### 6. 39.5-40.5 s. Second count is crowded
- Fix: spread the three fish in a loose line at least one body length apart in open water (not on the rock and weeds), and put each burst above its own fish at full size from its first frame.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- `k7tag`: a label that follows its character. Promote to a Library helper (character-anchored tag).
- `k7glint`: three tick marks beside an eye to sell a small eye action at phone size. Useful for any peek, wink or glance.
- `k7wrapBal`: balanced line breaking for both subtitle languages, with no orphan words. Worth moving into `Library/core/subtitle.js` as an opt-in so other films get it without a scene copy.
- The "one arrow beside the path, one X on impact, fade in 0.4 s" pattern (`K7BOLTS`) for failed-attempt sequences.
- Staging rule learned twice in this film: when a character is moved to fix one beat, re-check every dash or path that crosses the new position (note 1 above).
