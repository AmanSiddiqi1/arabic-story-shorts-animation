Three fish lived in a quiet pond among the trees. A nearby river was connected to the pond by a narrow stream. People rarely came to that place, so the fish swam peacefully every day.

The first fish was wise and quick to decide. The second was clever but sometimes hesitated. The third rarely thought about danger. Despite their differences, the three fish lived together in the pond.

One day, two fishermen passed by the water. One looked into the pond and said, “Look at those fish! We will come back tomorrow with nets and catch them.” The fish heard the men and became afraid.

“I will not wait for them to return,” said the first fish. She immediately swam through the narrow stream into the wide river. There she found a safe place far from the nets.

The second fish said, “We have until tomorrow.” She stayed in the pond, looking for a plan. The third said, “Do not worry! Perhaps the fishermen will not come back at all.” Then she carried on swimming and playing.

But the fishermen returned in the morning with large nets. They blocked the stream leading to the river. The second fish tried to escape but found the way closed. Then she quickly thought of one last trick.

The second fish rolled onto her back and let her body float without moving. The fishermen thought she was dead, took her out, and placed her near the river. Suddenly, she jumped into the water and swam away.

The third fish darted in every direction, unable to find a hiding place. At last, she was caught in the fishermen’s net. In the wide river, the other two fish swam freely, far from their old pond.
