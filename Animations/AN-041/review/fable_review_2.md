## Notes (polish only, most important first)

### 1. 46.0-47.0, 50.5-53.5, 93.2-94.5, 119.2-120.2, 138.2-139.9: the pointing wing is a stiff slab
What is wrong: the story-drawn wing is a straight parallel-sided black bar with a thick paper-white rim and a square shoulder end (frames 46.3, 93.5, 139.0). It reads as a wing only because of the feather tips; at phone size it can look like a board or a lid laid across her chest, and it covers the eggs and most of her breast. During 50.5-53.5 the same pointing pose is used for "shielding the nest", so the shield reads as a third accusation.
Why it matters: this is the crow's one big gesture, used five times.
Fix: taper it (shoulder about 1.4x the tip width, leading edge slightly curved), root it at her shoulder so the top end disappears into the body instead of ending square, thin the rim to about 2 px and keep the rim only on the edge that overlaps her body, and add 3-4 separated primary feathers at the tip. For 50.34-53.6 use a different pose: wing spread low and wide over the eggs (rotated towards horizontal, tip past the nest's far edge) so "cover" and "point" are two shapes.

### 2. 162.3-163.1: the look back on «وحده» is not visible
What is wrong: in the wide dusk shot the fox stands at the cave mouth facing left for about 0.8 s with no readable head turn (`m9_lookback.jpg`); the push-in then starts and he walks in. The ending is clean, but the plan's last emotional beat (he looks back up at the tiny crow) is lost.
Why it matters: it is the difference between "he went home" and "he went home alone".
Fix: on 162.78 flip only the head to face up and right towards the summit (the body keeps facing the cave), ears first, and hold it through the first half of the push (162.8-163.3) so it is seen at the larger size; add a thin dotted sight line from his eye to the crow for those 0.5 s if the head alone is too small. Then head forward and in on «كهفه» 163.82.

### 3. 116.0-122.2: the medallion falcon does not smile
What is wrong: the falcon in the medallion has a hooked, downturned beak and a heavy lid (frames 121.0, 121.9); the fox beside it has a small smile. The joke reads as "falcon = fox" (good enough), not "same sweet smile". Also at 116.2-117.0 the medallion slides across the sun, and the `=` is small and tilted.
Fix: give the medallion falcon an obvious upturned mouth line behind the beak and a raised brow matching the fox's performing face; widen the fox's grin for 120.68-121.76. Route the medallion's path below the sun. Draw the `=` upright, about 1.4x larger.

### 4. 147.0-148.5: the last look, with the crow half out of frame
What is wrong: during the push-in on the collapse the crow slides to the right border and is cut in half (frame 147.9), and the fox's "one look up at her" at 147.76 is not visible: his pupils stay forward and down and his brow reads sullen (`m10.jpg`).
Fix: centre the push about 40 world units further right so her head and beak stay inside; on 147.76 raise the pupils fully up-right with `brow` lifted at the inner end (sad, not angry) and `tip` back to 0 for 0.5 s, then drop again before the turn.

### 5. 161.5-163.0: stray yellow strokes at the right border
In the wide dusk shot a few yellow strokes (sun rays, the sun itself being outside the panel) poke in at the right edge beside the mountain slope (`m8.jpg`, frames 161.6 and 162.7). Either bring the low sun fully into the picture as a half disc on the horizon right of the mountain, or hide it for this shot.

### 6. 48.5-48.8: bird silhouettes cross the shadow's face
The three fleeing bird shapes start on top of the shadow's muzzle and for two or three frames look like a moustache on it (frame 48.6). Start them 60 units to the right of the jaws, in front of the open mouth, so the picture is "jaws snapping at birds", then let them flee up and out.

### 7. 46.0-47.0 and 139.2-139.9: the fox's head is cut by the left border in the crow push-ins
Acceptable as her shot, but at 139.7 only half his face is in frame. Shift these two framings about 30 world units left, or let him be fully out; a half face is the weakest option.

## Reusable techniques for the Library

- "Trick in four beats" inside a cloud: prize in view, `LOOK!` with arrow, unison head turn, snatch, staggered turn back.
