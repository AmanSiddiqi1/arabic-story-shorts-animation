A little louse lived in a wealthy man’s bed. | She hid along the edges of the bedding all day | and came out only when the man was asleep. | She moved quietly so that no one noticed her.
One night, a flea came looking for a place to sleep. | He saw the soft bed and told the louse, | “What a lovely place! Let me stay with you tonight.” | The louse welcomed him, but she was worried.
She told him, “You may stay, but be very careful. | This man wakes quickly if he feels a strong bite. | Wait until he is fast asleep, | move very gently, and do not hurry.”
“Do not worry!” said the flea. “I know what to do.” | But he could not wait. | He was hungry, and whenever he heard the man move, he wanted to feed even more. | The louse stayed in her hiding place.
Before the man was fully asleep, | the flea jumped onto his arm and bit him hard. | The man felt a sudden pain, opened his eyes, and rose from bed in anger. | “There is an insect in my bed!” he shouted.
The servants heard him and came carrying lamps. | They searched between the pillows and the blanket | and lifted the edges of the bedding. | The flea sensed danger, jumped quickly to the floor, and hid in a small crack.
The louse was still in her usual place. | She tried to hide deeper, but one of the servants saw her. | He caught her and got rid of her. | The servants did not find the flea, and after a while the man returned to sleep.
When everything became quiet, the flea came out of hiding. | He looked at the bed, where the louse no longer lived, | and jumped away. | His night in what had seemed a comfortable, safe place was over.
