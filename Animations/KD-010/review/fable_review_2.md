## Reusable techniques for the Library

- Closing lens for a tiny protagonist's exit: lit character against a bright disc (moon) inside the lens, exit by hopping out of the lens, hold the empty lens 0.4 s, then close. Worth a line in the guide next to the macro lens entry.
- "Threat without contact": giant fingertips wider than the victim, a flat shadow sliding over her, and a cut one body height before contact. Family-safe and stronger than showing the grab.
- Short proof lens on a hand (1 s) to say who is being carried in a wide shot.
- Actor layer drawn after the light discs with its own lighter night tint (ease the tint in; see note 5).
- Ironic lens: the searchers' wide shot with a lens showing the hidden character's eyes plus a NO FLEA label.
