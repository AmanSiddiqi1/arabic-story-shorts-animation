# AN-153 Abd al-Rahman the Maghribi's Story of the Rukh: creative plan

Film length 172.77 s narration + 4.0 s THE END = 176.77 s. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is in `creative_plan.json` (15 scenes, 171 actions). This file gives the direction; the JSON gives the frame-by-frame list.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

A tall tale inside a frame. Abd al-Rahman, a traveller from the Maghrib, tells two listeners what happened on a voyage near China.

- **Setup (0-22 s):** the teller in his room; a closed curtain behind him hides something tall. His thought cloud becomes the sea. The ship stops at an unknown island for water and wood; three men wander inland.
- **Inciting find (22-48 s):** a white dome four men tall. They do not know what it is, knock, strike it with the wood axe, it cracks: an egg of the legendary Roc. Inside, a gigantic chick.
- **The careless act (48-64 s):** they take meat and feathers (shown discreetly) and leave. The camera stays with the broken egg; a vast wing shadow slides over it. Suspense: we know before the crew does.
- **Pursuit (64-98 s):** at sea a speck becomes a bird wider than the ship, its wings dim the light, a boulder hangs in its claws. The crew understand why. Fear, then speed.
- **Climax (97-107 s):** the Roc drops the rock; the ship has moved just far enough; the stone hits the sea a hand behind the stern.
- **Relief and tall claims (107-150 s):** the crew retell it at once. Then the two claims, each bigger than the last: white beards turned black for ever, and a feather whose quill holds a goatskin water vessel. The curtain opens on the feather (payoff of the first shot).
- **Ending and moral (150-177 s):** he carried the feather home as proof. A last cloud replays the legend in small and dissolves into sparkles: this is a story of wonder, not natural history. A real sparrow lands beside the giant feather and shrugs; the teller winks. THE END.

Conflict: careless men against the consequence of what they did. Emotional arc: curiosity, wonder, unease, terror, relief, laughter, affectionate doubt. Surprises: the dome is an egg (37.0), the chick (46.3), the shadow (61.1), the near miss (104.2), the beards (122.3), the feather (137.6), the waterskin (145.9).

## 3. Character acting

- **Abd al-Rahman:** a showman. Big readable arm shapes (arm angles `aL/aR`, not hand targets near the head), `brow` -0.3 to -0.6, pauses before reveals (the look back at 136.96). On the ship he is the same man in the same green robe: proud of his feather (66.58), rigid with guilt (81.98, saccades with `gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`), one look to camera (87.3). `wink: 'R'` at 116.6 and 171.64.
- **Doubter:** arms `PERSON_HANDS.folded`, `lid` 0.3-0.4, `brow` 0.4. Mask slips at 133.96 (strokes his own beard, snaps back when watched). Breaks at 139.78: mouth `o`, turban tilt as follow-through. Smiles only at the very end.
- **Boy:** always a few frames ahead of the adults; hops (`hop`, `landSquash`), leans (`tip` 0.08), peers into the quill (143.54).
- **Captain:** fear first (`tremble`, `wet`, `sq` 0.93), then resolve (anticipation dip, strong point, `talk` 1).
- **The two elders:** stoop from `age` 0.9, slow lids, beards with 2-frame lag. Chewing = `talk` 0.3 at 3 Hz. The beard change is played as dramatic irony: each sees the other's beard before his own; mirrored points with a 2-frame offset; cross-eyed look at their own beards (125.56).
- **Young sailors:** sailorA is the comic (axe wind-ups with `anticipate`, whistle of false innocence at 86.14, blowing into the sail, the sneaked bite). SailorB notices the bird first (70.58).
- **Groups:** never in unison: `stagger(i)` on every shared reaction; falling fear on the second axe blow (smaller flinch).
- **Roc:** slow heavy `flap` (0.7 Hz), `brow` 0.8-1, head turned down with `gaze` on the ship, `grip` 0.7 to 1 at 79.0 and 1 to 0 in 3 frames at 96.98 with legs springing up; rock `swing` a beat behind each stroke. It is never a monster: it leaves once the rock is gone.
- **Sparrow:** 2-frame head tilts (`headRot`), wing lifted with `sag` -0.8, shrug as `sq` 1.06 then 0.95.
- Everyone: `LIFE.t = t` auto-blinks; `noBlink` during held shock (76.0, 102.88); `breathe()` on holds.

## 4. Camera and composition

- Faces and action stay in the top two-thirds panel (world 1000 x 1230, ground y 900); lettering never covers a face.
- Scale is the story: the dome reveal is a tilt and pull-out from faces (24.56); the bird reveal is a pull-out to two-thirds sky (67.72); the feather reveal is a tilt up to the ceiling (138.52) with the listeners' heads following.
- Suspense by stillness at 54.86-64: locked wide shot of the empty clearing, slow drift only, then the shadow.
- The climax is side-on and wide so the geometry reads: dotted fall line, red ring, ship advancing 180 units; one push to the stern for the near miss (102.34), back out for the splash. Panel-only shake (3 units for axe blows, 6 for the splash).
- Dialogue-style beats use `twoShot` (elders 115-128, teller and doubter 146.3-149).
- Transitions: zoom into the cloud (9.42), one LATER card (island to sea, 63.6-65.0), a match cut medallion to teller (128.04), page turns around the camel vignette.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Setup and payoff:** the curtain bulge in the first shot pays off at 137.62; the elders' meal at 65.2 pays off at 122.34; Abd's own feather on the mast (66.58) is the one in the room.
- **Suspense:** the audience sees the shadow at the egg before the crew sees the bird; the rock's round shadow on the deck (96.16) is a target; the fall is slowed only for the rock (102.34-104.16).
- **Humour:** the accordion bump (21.4), the innocent whistle with a feather behind the back (86.14), blowing into the sail (93.32), topping each other's "this big" (112.24-113.5), the mirrored beard discovery, the hopeful moustache (127.12), the camel's deadpan look (155.6), the sparrow's shrug.
- **Emotion:** wonder at the dome, relief after the splash (long exhale, a hug), the doubter's grudging nod and final smile.
- **The last line as a picture:** the legend becomes an ink drawing and dissolves; a real, small bird sits beside the impossible feather.

- Shadows are navy alpha 0.35 overlays after the cast, inside the panel clip. Call `finish` last.
- LATER card: covered midpoint about 64.3; change set and camera there with a key pair 0.01 s apart.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- The climax has no narration pause, so the splash is silent: it must land through the slowed fall, the flash, the column, SPLASH! and the shake.
- The person figure is front-facing only: the low-angle shot at 74.8 shows faces looking up, not backs of heads; rowing is body `tip` plus oars drawn by the dhow.
- Six crew on a 640-wide deck is tight: keep 85-100 units between figures; drop sailorB from a shot if needed.
- Room coordinates (curtain, window, ceiling) are approximate, measure on the set anchors; the feather may need `curl` or s 0.9 to fit.
- Verify that `pal.beard` overrides the age-driven beard colour.
- Owner questions (7, in the JSON): the abridged English (SCRIPT), the discreet handling of the chick, the invented listeners, four added gags, the elephant on the legend card, the camel ride as "his homeland", and the lettering list.
