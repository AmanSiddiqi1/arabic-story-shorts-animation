Abd al-Rahman the Maghribi told a marvelous sea story from waters near China. His ship stopped at an island to collect water and wood.

The sailors found an enormous white dome. They struck it until it cracked and discovered, according to the legend, that it was an egg of the giant bird called the Roc.

Inside lay a gigantic chick. The men took some meat and feathers back to the ship, without considering what might happen when the parent returned.

After they sailed, an immense bird appeared overhead, carrying a huge rock in its claws. The crew realized it was pursuing them because of the broken egg.

The captain urged the ship onward. The bird dropped its rock, but the vessel had moved just far enough for the stone to crash into the sea.

The passengers survived and marveled at their escape. The narrator adds the fantastic claim that eating the chick’s meat turned elderly men’s white beards black.

Abd al-Rahman further claimed their beards never went gray again. He kept a feather whose hollow shaft was said to hold a goatskin water vessel.

He brought the feather home as proof of his tale. The Roc story is a legend of marvels, not a scientific account of an actual bird.
