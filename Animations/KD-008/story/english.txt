A tortoise lived in a beautiful pond among the trees. She had two duck friends who visited her every morning. The tortoise loved hearing their stories of rivers, mountains, and distant places.

Many days passed without rain, and the pond began to lose water. The mud around its edges dried, and only a little water remained. The tortoise was frightened because she could not travel far quickly.

One duck said, “We know a large lake beyond the hills, and it has plenty of water.” The tortoise asked, “But how can I get there? You can fly, while I walk very slowly.”

The ducks thought for a moment, then found a strong stick. “We will hold the two ends with our beaks,” they said, “and you hold the middle in your mouth. Then we can carry you through the air to the lake.”

The tortoise was delighted, but the ducks warned her, “There is one very important thing. Do not open your mouth while we are flying, no matter what you hear. If you speak, you will fall!” The tortoise nodded in agreement.

The tortoise gripped the stick in her mouth, and the ducks spread their wings. The ducks and tortoise rose above the trees and houses. The tortoise looked down at the distant ground, amazed and happy, but she stayed silent.

They passed above a village, and some people saw them. “Look!” someone shouted. “A tortoise is flying between two ducks!” Others laughed and looked up. The tortoise grew angry and wanted to tell them she was not some strange bird.

The tortoise forgot her friends’ warning and opened her mouth to answer the people. At that very moment, she let go of the stick and fell to the ground. The tortoise did not survive the fall, and the two ducks reached the lake alone.
