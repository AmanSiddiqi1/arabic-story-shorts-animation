## Notes (polish only, most useful first; none triggers v3)

### 1. 120.5-124.0, 141.2-153.5 | MINOR | Duck A is still half out of the left edge in the aerial close-ups

What is wrong: the rig sits left of centre, so duck A is a head and bill at the panel edge (122.3, 143.9, 152.8) while there is about 60-100 px of empty sky to the right of duck B's bill. In the kept push-in at 148.7-150.3 she is reduced to an eye on the end of the stick (149.5), which looks like a detached head.

Why it matters: the two friends should read as a pair framing her; one of them is currently a sliver.

Fix: in both close-ups shift the camera centre about 70-90 world px to the left (the rig moves right on screen) so duck A's head, neck and the front of her body are inside the panel; keep duck B's bill tip about 30 px inside the right edge. For 148.7-150.3 cap the push-in at about 1.45 instead of 1.6, or aim it at the tortoise's head and the drifting warning icon with the same leftward offset.

### 2. 129.0-133.5 | MINOR | Only the tortoise is visible at the top edge while the child points

What is wrong: after the push-in at 129.0 the ducks leave the frame and the tortoise is cut by the top edge (130.0). The child points and the man shades his eyes at something that is mostly off screen for four and a half seconds.

Fix: either hold the 126-128.7 framing about one second longer and tilt the camera up roughly 60-80 px during the push-in so the whole rig stays under the top edge until the cut at 133.7, or let the rig fly out of frame completely by 129.7 so the look up is at an off-screen subject on purpose. The cut to the low angle at 133.7 already pays it off, so either is fine.

### 3. 162.0-162.4 | MINOR | The dust in the wide shot reads as a dome on a roof

What is wrong: in the wide (162.1) the dust is an outline in the same sand colour as the walls, sitting on the roofline. It reads as part of the building.

Fix: fill it with the darker tan used in the village shot (162.5 onward), make it about 1.5 times larger and let it rise 30-40 px over these frames so it reads as dust before the cut.

### 4. 111.5-113.7 | MINOR | Duck B's head sits on a green tree crown during lift-off

What is wrong: the lone tall tree with the red bird stands directly behind duck B, so her green head merges with the green crown and the bird (113.2).

Fix: move that tree about 150 world px to the right, or lift the rig's path about 40 px here so duck B's head is against the yellow sky.

### 5. 103.0-105.4 | MINOR | Bite insert: the stick is at eye level behind her head

What is wrong: before the clamp the stick crosses behind the top of her head (103.3, 104.5) and her open mouth is below it, so for two seconds she seems to be standing behind a rail.

Fix: draw the stick after the head for the whole insert and lower it about 35-45 px to snout height, so at 104.0 the mouth opens just under it and at 105.44 the jaw closes over it.

### 6. 31.5-33.2 | MINOR | A LITTLE WATER label is clipped by the right panel edge

What is wrong: the final R is cut by the panel border (32.2). This is the same in v1 and was missed in round 1.

Fix: move the label about 30 px left, keeping the arrow tip on the puddle.

### 7. 84.0-94.5 | MINOR | Duck B is still at the right edge for the first half of the rule scene

What is wrong: before the pull-out at 95.0, duck B is only a head at the right edge while duck A delivers the rule.

Fix: optional. Shift the camera about 60 px right for 84.2-94.7 so duck B's head and chest are inside; the tortoise has room on the left.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library

- "Unload spring": on release, ducks and stick jump up a fixed minimum distance in 4-6 frames with a back ease while the rider sinks slowly. It guarantees a visible gap and reads at phone size.
- Demonstration plant: a character steps into the foreground, presents a prop, then performs the rule on it (here the pebble), with every other character's `gaze` following it.
- Mirrored set copy to cover a camera crane (used for the forest at 112-114); worth a set option such as `extendRight`.
- Still wanted from round 1: a camera helper that keeps a list of named points inside the panel with a margin (it would have caught notes 1, 2 and 7 here).
