# KD-008 The Tortoise and the Two Ducks: creative plan

Film length 174.45 s (MP3 170.45 s + 4.0 s held ending). 11 scenes, 116 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue below is the listed start of the Arabic word in `inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard with every action, pose field and helper is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** When her pond dries up, a tortoise is carried through the sky by two duck friends on a stick she must hold in her mouth. Stung by the laughter of villagers below, she opens it to answer, and falls.

**Conflict.** Outside: drought, distance, a slow body. Inside: she must keep one promise (a shut mouth) against two feelings, first delight, then wounded pride. Delight she masters (124.74 صامتة). Pride she does not (154.26 فمها).

**Spine (16 beats in the JSON).** Home and friendship (0.60, 6.32) → the pond shrinks (25.50) → fear (33.80) → the lake beyond the hills, and "how?" (43.54) → the stick and the plan (64.88) → the one rule (90.92) and its proof with a pebble (96.42) → lift-off (109.98) → first test passed (124.74) → the shout (133.02) → anger (141.30) → the mouth opens (154.26) → the fall (160.82) → she does not survive (163.86) → the ducks arrive alone (169.10).

**Surprises and payoffs.**
- The pebble (96.42): duck B opens her bill to speak and a pebble drops. Same picture, same place on screen, at 154.26 with the tortoise.
- The near miss (122.12): joy almost opens her mouth. The audience learns how thin the margin is, and relaxes just before the village.
- The ducks cannot warn her: their bills hold the stick. They can only stare and shake their heads (143.72, 152.10).
- The lake at the end is the exact picture promised in the thought cloud at 43.54, fish and all, now with nobody smiling.

**Emotional arc.** Content → wistful → frightened → hopeful → doubtful → delighted → solemn → amazed → proud and angry → one second of understanding → silence. The first 100 s are warm and funny so that the last 20 s land.

**Ending and moral.** The script states no moral, so none is written on screen. The picture says it: two ducks on a wide lake, a stick floating between them with an empty middle. THE END at 170.6, held to 174.45.

## 3. Character acting

**Tortoise** (heroine; Library `tortoise` at about 1.9 scale with new optional fields). Slow, heavy, honest face. Her neck is her main instrument: long when curious or reaching, pulled in when afraid (`hide` 0.4-0.5). Use `gaze` plans on every beat (pond edge, ducks, cloud, hills, crowd, stick), `lid` for sleepiness, effort and the half-lidded snail look, `wet` for 38.68 and as an eye shine at 18.02 and 121.16, `sq` for sighs, clamps and the breath before the shout, `tip` for effort and shrugs. Every pose change overshoots and settles (`settleTo`). Hops are small and heavy (`anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash`, dust). In the air her legs hang on `swing`; mood shows in the legs too (paddling in fear, one kick of delight, rigid in anger, trailing upward in the fall). Anger: brow slams to a V, `lid` 0.3, red `flush` climbs the face, `tremble`, steam. Realisation at 157.96: brow flips to raised, flush drains, pupils to the stick, then one look to the lens. No gulp gag.

**Duck A** (white): leader, speaks first, economical gestures (wing on chest, one raised feather, bill pressed shut). **Duck B** (mallard): eager, physical, reacts 4-6 frames after A and bigger (the flap and hop, standing on the stick, the pebble). They never move in unison: flap phases a third of a cycle apart, nods on different beats, heads tilted opposite ways when listening. In the air they act only with eyes, brows, head shakes and wing rhythm, because their bills are full: that helplessness is the suspense. At the lake: low heads, slow blinks, duck B one tear.

**Villagers** (person variants). Child notices first (small, quick, hop); the man is the big shape (lean back, `cupMouth`, arm thrown up by angle `aR`, not a hand target, so it reads on a phone); the woman laughs behind `mouthCover`. Reactions always staggered. After the fall: stillness, eyes break away, head drops, cap off (the Thief "shame ending" pattern).

**Listeners.** Whoever is not speaking watches the speaker (`lookAt`) and answers with a nod, a tilt or a blink a few frames late.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground y 900. Faces and key action stay in the upper two-thirds of the panel; clouds, balloons and labels never cover a face.
- S1 establishing wide with a slow push; S2 pan along the cracked shore, push to her fear, pull back for the three useless steps; S3 `twoShot` for the dialogue, pulled out when the hills are named; S4 tracks the ducks to the branch, then gives the upper half to the plan board; S5 pushes in for the rule and tilts with the pebble.
- S6 insert on the bite, then pull out until both wingspans fit. S7 cranes up with the rig; the rig itself is the composition (two ducks high, stick, tortoise hanging centre). One tilt down past her feet (119.30), one close-up (121.16) that keeps both duck heads in the corners so their alarm at 122.12 is in shot.
- S8 intercuts ground and air: wide ground with the small rig and its shadow; low angle up at 133.86; air at 136.06; ground laugh at 137.04; a tilt up along the pointing arms (140.46) lands on her face for the anger; slow push to the jaw until 148.66.
- S9: mouth and stick on the panel centre line. Hold the ducks 0.4 s after the release, then whip-tilt down after her. Panel-only shake at 162.06.
- S10 static, then a slow tilt up. S11 wide that matches the S3 cloud picture, slow pull-out under THE END.
- One transition card only: **DAYS LATER...** at 19.4, with the camera cut hidden at its covered midpoint.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the three-step sprint with a counter (37.40); the snail overtaking her (58.58); duck B bouncing on the stick (65.68); "me?" (72.34); jaw practice (73.96); the frozen dance (84.24); MM HM! through a shut mouth (101.64); the heavy lift-off dip (111.74).
- **Suspense:** the rule is taught three ways (gesture, icon, pebble). The near miss at 122.12 proves it is real. From 141.30 the camera closes on her jaw while the ducks plead silently; a hairline of daylight at the lip by 148.
- **Surprise:** the Coyote-style hang at 155-159 is played straight: the ducks, relieved of her weight, bob upward and the stick springs straight, so physics tells her before she understands.
- **Emotion:** her wet eyes at 38.68; the pat on the shell at 101.64; wonder at 121.16; the villagers' laughter turning to bowed heads; the empty middle of the stick.
- **Restraint:** no impact, no body, no impact sound. Dust behind the roofs, then silence.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- **Asset load** is above the usual four major items. The villagers and village can be shared with KD-005. If time is short, drop the two extra onlookers and the ball first, then `dayCycle` (use a simple sun arc).
- **Tortoise base:** if extending the small Library `tortoise` is awkward, use the `turtle` head (talk, brow, mouth exist) on the tortoise shell and legs as a new optional variant. Never change the default look.
- **The death** is staged off screen for a young audience. Please confirm this restraint, and that no written moral should be added (the script has none).
- **No impact sound:** there is no pause between 160.8 and 162.6. If a real silence is measured inside the long word at 162.58, a very soft thud could be added.
- **"I AM NOT A"** is invented lettering for the narrated wish to answer; remove it if only true sound words are wanted.
- Long word starts at 60.06, 109.98, 148.66 and 162.58 probably include leading silence; nudge those actions later if the audio shows it.
