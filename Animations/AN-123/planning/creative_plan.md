# AN-123 The Loves of the Boy and Girl at School: creative plan

Film length 174.0 s (narration 170.80 s + 3.2 s THE END). 18 scenes, 157 timed actions. Every time below is seconds on the final MP3; the exact word cues are in `creative_plan.json`.

**The idea in one line:** one small writing tablet fills up band by band, in four inks from four hands (blue boy, red girl, black teacher, green master), and each new hand is a danger that turns into a kindness.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

- **Setup (0-20.7):** a schoolroom. A boy loves the girl on the next mat, cannot say so, and fears the row behind him.
- **Inciting act (20.7-41.9):** everyone is distracted; he takes her tablet, wipes the lesson, writes two verses about a heart tired of hiding.
- **Answer (41.9-63.6):** she reads, understands, her eyes fill, and she writes two verses under his.
- **Suspense 1 and surprise (63.6-85.0):** the teacher walks in behind them, takes the tablet, frowns... and is not angry. He remembers his own youth and adds verses urging her to be kind.
- **Suspense 2 (85.0-108.9), after the one LATER card:** the girl's master arrives; the tablet lands in his hands. He reads three messages by three authors, understands the looks and tears, lets them wait, and does not forbid it.
- **Climax and joke (108.9-126.5):** he writes his own blessing in the last free band, then teases the teacher: never seen a schoolmaster work as such a bold matchmaker.
- **Resolution (126.5-148.5):** judge and witnesses, married on the spot, no years of waiting, a feast and gifts.
- **Moral and coda (148.5-174.0):** verses on a small tablet became a shared life; a long happy life until death; nobody forgot the teacher.

**Conflict:** shyness and fear of being seen, then fear of the two adults who hold power over them. **Surprises:** each authority adds a verse instead of a punishment. **Emotional arc:** longing, fear, joy, dread, relief, laughter, tenderness. **Ending:** the tablet on the classroom wall, the old teacher winks. **Moral:** a sincere word kindly received can change a life.

## 3. Character acting

- **Boy:** all nerves. Every big move has anticipation (`anticipate`, sq 0.94 then 1.05) and a collapse after (`settleTo`). Eyes lead: `gazePlan` saccades (ease 0.07) when checking who is watching. His tablet is his shield: he hides behind it at 16.5 and from 42.6 to 62.0, lowering it by degrees (eyes, nose, grin). `tremble` at 16.5, 71.0, 106.4. Soft `flush` when she looks at him.
- **Girl:** quiet and exact. Reading is played with eye saccades right to left with holds (45.48-48.0), then one slow head turn. Tears: `wet: true` from 52.64, one `teardrops` drop, a long blink at 53.3, then resolve (head level) before she writes. When her master enters she stands with eyes down and `PERSON_HANDS.clasp`: her smile switches off, the only reminder of her position.
- **Teacher:** the comic heart. Entrance by stealth (`sneak`), false anger (brow 0.9, stick rising an inch), a dead still hold, then brow to -0.4 and a moustache smile. Wink (`wink: 'R'`) at 80.24 and at 169.54. Under the master's teasing he shrinks (sq 0.92), turban slips, `palmsUp` shrug, then laughs a beat after the master.
- **Master:** big, slow, readable. He is the largest figure (s 1.08) and moves least. His long unreadable hold 105.46-107.46 uses pupils only. Smile arrives exactly on الزواج. Pantomime in the joke: finger wag, hand shading the eyes at "never seen".
- **Back row (three classmates):** always staggered (`stagger(i)`), three personalities: mate1 suspicious, mate2 sleepy, mate3 eager. They are the chorus: they notice the teacher first (65.24), follow the flying tablet, hide laughter at MATCHMAKER!.
- **Judge and witnesses:** judge with half shut eyes and tiny quick steps; tall witness and short witness out of step, the short one bumps (`lurch`) at 130.4.
- **Secondary motion:** hair buns and turbans settle after head turns; the hearts drift and bump; the tablet on its nail swings once (`swing`). `LIFE.t = t` gives everyone blinks; `breathe` on every hold.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, floor y 900. Front row mats at x 330 (girl) and x 600 (boy); back row at y 850, scale 0.72; teacher's cushion x 830; door in the left wall; window centre back (read the real positions from `palaceHallAnchors`).
- **One fixed insert framing** for the tablet (506 x 690 centred 500, 640): S4, S6, S9, S11, S16. The audience watches the same board fill, band by band.
- Two-shots (`twoShot`) for every exchange of looks; the eye line between the mats is the film's main diagonal.
- Push-ins on decisions: the boy before he sneaks (24.2), the girl's tear (50.2-52.6), the teacher's change of heart (73.1-74.2), the master's verdict (105.4-107.4).
- Reveals: the door opening between the two dreaming heads (66.0); the tablet shrinking and becoming a door (152.1-153.8).
- One LATER card (84.9-85.3). One match-cut (153.84). One slow sky scene with no cuts (S17). Shake is not used anywhere.
- Faces and hands stay inside panel y 54..1250; lettering in the upper band, never over a face.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the heart he has to hide behind his back; the innocent whistle; GULP! to camera; the teacher's tablet squirting out of his hands into the very hands he feared; the teacher medallion sweating in band 3; MATCHMAKER! and the heart escaping from his turban; mismatched witnesses.
- **Suspense:** three holds built the same way so each resolves faster in the viewer's mind but not on screen: classmates (15.6), teacher (73.1), master (105.46). Stillness, pupils only.
- **Surprise:** each adult reaches for the pen instead of the stick.
- **Emotion:** her tear landing on the tablet edge; her hand on his sleeve at مواساة; the master's gifts; the old couple on the bench and the two stars.
- **Running picture:** four inks. By the end the colours alone tell the story, so the tablet on the wall in S18 needs no caption.
