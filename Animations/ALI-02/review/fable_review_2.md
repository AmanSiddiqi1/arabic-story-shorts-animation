## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 87.3-90.0 and 97.6-98.6 s: the two men who are sent away are never seen going
- **What is wrong.** "وأرسلوا رجلين للتحقق" and "حتى ابتعد الرجلان" are the hinge of the escape, but the scree is a sliver of boulders on the right panel edge and the band is a clump cut by that edge (stills 88.6, 89.0, 98.3). The chrome "2" (88.2-89.2) hangs in the sky above nobody. No one is seen climbing at 88.3 and no one is seen disappearing at 98.2. The viewer has to take both events from the subtitle.
- **Why it matters.** WAIT! only makes sense if we can see what Cassim is waiting for: two men still close enough to look back. When they go over the top, the picture should release him. At present the freeze ends for no visible reason.
- **Fix.**
  - Shift the camera about 150 world units right for 87.3-90.0 (pan there over 0.5 s on "وأرسلوا" 87.28), so the door sits in the left third and the lower half of the slope is in the right third with clear sky above it. The mule may leave the frame; Cassim's turban in the doorway stays.
  - On "رجلين" 88.34 the leader's arm points up the slope and the two scouts (the ones logged at x 2150 and 2236) step out of the clump and climb, staggered by 0.25 s, with `walk` and `footfalls` on the boulder tops, capes lagging. Put the "2" directly above them with a short arrow, not in the open sky.
  - In the wide after the whip pan (95.2-100.4) keep the same extra 100-150 units on the right so both scouts are visible high on the slope as small back views. On "ابتعد" 97.64 they reach the crest; on "الرجلان" 98.22 they drop behind it one after the other (heads last). Keep the thief's look back at 97.7-98.0 exactly as it is; it works.
  - Then the beckon at 99.2 answers a visible change.

### 2. 102.6-106.5 s: the abandoned gold is a small lump, and both reactions are on the panel edges
- **What is wrong.** For 3.5 s the middle of the frame is bare rock and the door glow. The sacks are one low overlapping heap about 8 % of the panel height that reads as three (still 104.6), under a counter that says 5. The cart leaves at the left edge half out of frame; the pointing thief on "وراءهما" is cut in half by the right edge (still 106.2).
- **Why it matters.** "Leaving most of the gold behind" is the price of Cassim's greed and the last picture before the lesson at home. It should be the fattest, clearest object in the shot.
- **Fix.**
  - Lay the five sacks in a loose row with gaps (x about 1560..1760, alternating s 0.9 and 1.05, one fallen on its side with the coin spill), so five can be counted at phone size. Counter and arrow as now on "أكياس" 104.26, the arrow tip on the middle sack.
  - Frame it lower and a little wider (zoom about 1.0, floor line at 70 % of the panel) so the sacks sit in the middle third.
  - Bring the mule cart fully into frame for 102.6-104.9 (start it at x about 1420), mule's head turned to the sacks on "أكياس", then the trot off left on "الذهب" 104.9 with wheel dust.
  - On "وراءهما" 105.48 move the turning thief at least 120 units into the frame (or pan right 100 units over 0.3 s) so his whole body, the pointing arm and the open mouth are inside the panel before the tape transition at 106.45.

### 3. 28.8-52.7 s: the cold cave reads as dim, not cold, and the sitting beats are too small to read
- **What is wrong.** The cold pass turns the cave grey-brown and dark (stills 30.8, 42.5): the walls lose their colour but do not become blue. In the full-room wide (40.7-43.9) the lower 45 % of the panel is near-black floor, and Cassim's head is about 50 px high, so the head sinking, the coin pushed away with one finger and the chin on the fist cannot be seen. The sesame seed (a bright fleck near his turban, 41.0-42.7) reads as a spark.
- **Why it matters.** This is the owner's "varied bright colour" point for 24 seconds of the film, and the emotional low of Cassim's half.
- **Fix.**
  - In `a2CaveWorld` `o.cool`: lighten the multiply (alpha about 0.5 instead of 0.78) and shift it bluer (`#7f93e6`), raise the screen pass to about 0.25, and keep a clear warm rim on the mound and the chest. Aim for a moonlit blue-violet room in which the coins on the floor are still visible as pale discs, clearly different from the gold cave at 12.6 but as light as the sunset rock at 126.
  - Keep the pull-out to zoom 1.0 for the sit (40.1-41.4), then push slowly to about 1.4 on Cassim and the nearest three sacks over 41.6-43.0, so the coin push on "الأكياس" 42.32 and the chin on fist on "يتذكر" 43.02 are readable. The floor line stays in the lower third.
  - Make the seed a recognisable pale teardrop with a dark outline, about 28 px in the panel after the push, crossing in front of his face at 42.6-43.4 while his eyes look the other way.

### 4. 69.5-71.4 s: the leader's shadow reads as a pole with a ball on it
- **What is wrong.** The shadow is a straight diagonal bar from the leader's feet to a disc on the copper slab (still 69.9). It crosses Cassim's turban and face but is not shaped like a man.
- **Why it matters.** This is the leader's silent answer to the plea; the plan's "shadow, not a word". It should be recognisably his.
- **Fix.** Build it from the leader's own outline: head cloth with the cord bump, shoulders, the cape flare. Skew it along the floor from his feet toward Cassim and fold it upright where it meets Cassim and the sacks behind him (floor part flattened to about 35 % height, wall part upright). Slide it in over 0.5 s on "بالخروج" 69.52 as now, flat multiply at about 0.35, hard edge. Cassim shrinks under it: `sq` 0.94, feather flat, eyes up at the leader. Keep the slow blink.

### 5. 131.2-137.0 s: the leader gives his order from inside a huddle
- **What is wrong.** After the men close in (130.5-131.2) six figures overlap in one block in front of the rock (still 133.0); three faces are hidden and the leader's arms merge with the men behind him. The arm throw toward the town on "سنبحث" 135.54, the payoff of the film's running arm gesture, is hard to pick out.
- **Why it matters.** The same arm that opened the rock now points at the town. It needs clear air around it.
- **Fix.** Keep the men in the planned ragged half circle with about 70 units between them and a gap of at least 110 units on each side of the leader, the two nearest half a head lower (y +10) so the leader is the tallest shape. On 135.54 the leader's pointing arm goes to the right into empty sky, held through 136.9, cape following three drawings late; the men's heads turn to follow the arm in a wave (`stagger(i)`).

### 6. 64.8-71.4 s: the kneel is shallow
- **What is wrong.** After the drop at 64.8 Cassim is only about a quarter of a head lower than when standing (stills 65.3, 68.6), so from 67.7 to 71.4 he looks as if he has stood up again.
- **Fix.** Lower him by a clear half body (hips to the floor line, robe hem spread on the ground, toes behind), head level with the leader's chest, and keep that height to 71.4. The turban gag and the snatch stay as they are; they are the best gag in the film.

### 7. 86.2-86.8 s: a black thief outside the door
- **What is wrong.** A man steps out of the doorway almost black and stays black while already standing in front of the rock beside coloured men (still 86.6).
- **Fix.** Same rule as round 1 note 2: silhouette amount 0 once the figure has crossed the door line; fade it out over the two drawings in which he crosses.

### 8. 138.5-141.9 s: the riders fade out in the middle of the road
- **What is wrong.** The six riders are correct and coloured but small (about 40 px high), enter only at 138.5, and dissolve at 141.1-141.9 before they reach anything.
- **Fix.** Start them on "البلدة" 137.74 at the right edge about 1.4 times their present size, shrinking as they go, and let them ride behind the first house or through a gate gap under the dome at about 141.5 instead of fading, the dust trail settling after them. One lit window going dark as they pass would be a small extra threat.

### 9. 92.7-94.7 s: Ali Baba's signal competes with the horse
- **What is wrong.** The chestnut horse's head is a hand's width from Ali Baba's raised palm and looks straight at him for two seconds; the olive sleeve is close in value to the bush (still 93.8).
- **Fix.** Turn the horse's head away (grazing, to the right) from 92.7, and lift the palm so it is against the sky, clear of the bush and the horse. Palm on "أشار" 93.00, one small downward pat on "ينتظر" in the inset.
