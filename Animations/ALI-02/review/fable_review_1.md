# ALI-02 "The Forgotten Password" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 1 (v1)

Film reviewed: `Animations/ALI-02/renders/ALI-02_v1.mp4` (142.7 s). Sources: all 24 contact strips in `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second), 42 full-resolution panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited below, 6 stills of ALI-01-ANIME v4 for continuity, and a frame-difference scan of the panel (10 fps) for frozen stretches. Times are seconds on the film.

Not checked: sound was not listened to; stepped cel timing (twos and threes) cannot be judged from stills; landing squash on the hop at 1.4 and the sit at 40.1 falls between sampled frames.

## Verdict: REVISE

No BLOCKER. Every narrated event is on screen in order, subtitles are sharp and complete in both languages, THE END is present, no broken frames.

It is REVISE because three things clearly hurt the story or the owner's points, and all three are confined to single shots:
1. The rescue (90.9-100.4) is staged so that the thieves face Cassim from one body-width away, and the leader's eyes are on him. "While the thieves were distracted" is contradicted by the picture.
2. Cassim is tinted near-black, face included, for his whole "Open Sesame!" call (4.2-7.0). That is the owner's "no tint over the characters" point, on the first line of the film.
3. The last picture (137.2-142.7) is a tree trunk, a leftover horse and a swarm of about thirty ant-sized black horses. The town the leader points at is hidden, so the cliffhanger has no target.

The animator's own flag is confirmed: 8.2-71.4 is one picture (door left, Cassim, gold right) with small variations (note 4).

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 90.9-100.4 s: the escape happens in plain sight of the thieves
- **What is wrong.** From 87.0 the four remaining men and the leader stand in a tight clump just right of the door, all facing the camera. Cassim walks out at 91.2-91.8 and stands in the open one body-width from the leader until 100.4. At 96.5 and 99.6 the leader's pupils are turned left, straight at Cassim, with angry brows. Nobody looks at the scree. The plan's "row of turned backs that might turn" is not on screen.
- **Why it matters.** The narration says the thieves were distracted and Ali Baba signalled Cassim to wait. On screen there is nothing to wait for and no reason he is not caught, so the cleverness of the pebbles and the suspense of WAIT! are both lost. This is the centre of Ali Baba's half of the film.
- **Fix.**
  - On "فظنوا" 84.10 the heads snap right (as planned); on "حراسًا" 85.40 the whole group walks right with `walk` and `footfalls` to x 2100..2260 at the foot of the scree, staggered, leaving at least 250 units of empty ground between the door edge and the nearest thief. The leader goes with them and stops nearest the scree.
  - From 88.3 to 100.4 every thief has `gaze` hard right and up at the slope (never left), a small `tip` toward the scree, one pointing arm, one shading his eyes. The Library person has no back view, so sell "turned away" with a cover cel: after drawing each man, draw a keffiyeh-coloured back-of-head shape over the face (same outline as the head cloth, no features) and the cape on the camera side. One story helper, used for the five figures.
  - Cassim does not come out at 91.2. On "انشغل" 91.66 only his turban, feather and one eye lean out of the left edge of the doorway (clipped by the door edge, as the leader was at 53.4). He stays there through the pan to Ali Baba (92.5-94.7).
  - On "قاسم" 95.10 he takes two tiptoe steps out and on "ينتظر" 96.36 freezes on one foot at the palm signal (the pose at 96.5 is good; keep it, but with the gap and the backs behind him).
  - The suspense beat that is missing: at 97.64 "ابتعد" one thief (man 0) starts to turn his head left (remove the cover cel for 6 drawings, eyes sliding left), Cassim's feather buzzes, the thief yawns and turns back as the two scouts vanish on "الرجلان" 98.22. Then the beckon and the run as now.
  - Keep the inset of Ali Baba and the WAIT! tag; they read well.

### 2. 4.2-7.0 s: Cassim is a dark silhouette while he says the words correctly
- **What is wrong.** From 4.2 (before the rock opens at 6.2) Cassim's robe goes near-black and his face dark brown (stills 5.5, 6.9). The doorway silhouette tint is being applied while he stands in the open in front of the closed rock. In ALI-01 v4 the same call (leader 44.0, Ali Baba 84.0) is in full colour.
- **Why it matters.** This is the setup of the whole gag: the confident, correct call that he will fail to repeat at 18.3, 22.4 and 24.4. The viewer has to see his smug face and the arm throw clearly. It is also the owner's colour point broken in the first ten seconds.
- **Fix.** Silhouette amount 0 until he actually crosses the door line. Full colour for 3.5-6.9; from 6.9 to 7.3, while he walks into the gold, ramp to the planned 25-50% only, with a warm rim from the door instead of darkness. Keep skin in its own colour throughout. Use the freed clarity for acting on "فنادى" 3.50: chest out, `lid` 0.3, a hand rub, then the arm throw landing on the 4.90 onset.

### 3. 137.2-142.7 s: the cliffhanger has no target and the riders read as ants
- **What is wrong.** After the whip pan the frame is centred on the hiding tree, whose trunk covers the middle of the far town; the town is a flat band of lilac blocks. The neon arrow (138.2-139.3) points at the thicket and the chestnut horse, which is still standing there after its rider has left. From 138.5 two stacked rows of about thirty tiny black horse silhouettes cross the foreground below the tree: smaller than the flowers beside them, nearer than the tree, and far more than six riders. The SEARCH THE TOWN! balloon is cut by the right edge from 137.2 to 138.6. A tape tear smears the last riders at 141.8.
- **Why it matters.** "We will search for them in town" must end on the town with the riders heading for it; that is the hook for episode 3 and the frame held longest (4.7 s).
- **Fix.**
  - End the whip pan with the tree in the left third (or drop the tree from this view) so the town sits centre and right, unobstructed, at about twice its present height: roofs, the dome and two palms recognisable from the yard at 107, in warm sunset colours with a few lit windows, not a single lilac band.
  - Arrow tip on the town's dome on "البلدة" 137.74, fading by 139.2.
  - Riders: one row only, six riders, on the road line at the foot of the town (far = small is then correct), entering from the right already moving and shrinking toward the town gate with a dust trail. Coloured, not black (leader's crimson cape first). If `abSilRow` is kept, mask it to six figures and tint it dusk violet with a peach rim.
  - Remove the chestnut horse from this shot (it rode off with the others at 136.9).
  - Keep the balloon fully inside the panel for its whole life: it belongs to the leader, so let it fade out on the pan (gone by 137.3) rather than travel with the camera.
  - No tape tear inside the last second; let the hold be clean with only the dust settling, window lights twinkling and the sun sinking a few pixels.

### 4. 8.2-71.4 s: the cave is one picture for 63 seconds
- **What is wrong.** Every cave shot has the door cropped at the left edge, Cassim left of centre, the gold mound and the copper slab right of centre, at nearly the same zoom (stills 12.6, 19.0, 27.3, 30.8, 38.0, 43.5, 51.7 are interchangeable). The planned tilt up the slab (25.74) is a small reframe that does not make the door big. The three jump cuts (33.5-36.4) do not change the picture. The planned cooling of the light is not visible. The silver and cloth zones are a sliver at the right edge.
- **Why it matters.** Cassim carries this stretch alone; his slide from greedy to hopeless needs the room to change around him. It is also the owner's first point: show the place wide, then pan across it.
- **Fix (five different pictures, same set, no new assets).**
  - **8.2-13.4 gold wide.** After the match cut, start at zoom 1.0 on the right half of the cave (mound, chest, silver, cloth rolls, crystals) with Cassim entering small from the left, and pan left with him as he fills sacks, ending where the shot ends now. The counter stays.
  - **16.7-20.0 the door.** Keep as is (this is the one framing the others should not repeat).
  - **25.74-28.8 "لكن الصخرة بقيت مغلقة": a real low angle.** Camera drops so the floor line is in the bottom tenth and the door slab fills two thirds of the panel height; Cassim's head and raised fists are at the bottom, small. Hold on "مغلقة" 28.16 with only his feather drooping.
  - **28.84-32.3 heart.** Cool the plate here and keep it cool: swap to a repainted cave plate (dusty blue-violet walls, gold only as a rim on the mound), not a tint over Cassim. Skin and emerald stay as they are. The cool plate stays until the daylight at 52.69.
  - **33.46-36.4 jump cuts.** Three clearly different set-ups, one per phrase: "حاول" 33.46 at the door from the right side of it (Cassim on the right of the frame facing left, door centre); "مرة" 34.74 close two-thirds figure pounding the slab with both fists; "أخرى" 35.72 from behind the sacks with the door small in the distance and Cassim tiny in front of it. The balloon follows him in each.
  - **40.1-43.9 "جلس بجانب الأكياس": the widest shot of the cave.** Pull out to zoom 1.0 over the sit so the whole room is visible, door closed at the far left, and Cassim a small figure beside five fat sacks he no longer looks at. This is the lonely picture the plan asks for; at present it is the same medium shot with him sitting.
  - **63.06-70.4 two-shot.** Push to the planned zoom 1.5 on leader and Cassim once Cassim has stepped clear (note 5); the men stay as a row of heads at the left edge.

### 5. 60.3-67.6 s: Cassim pops up inside the leader, and never kneels
- **What is wrong.** At 60.7-61.3 Cassim springs up directly behind the leader's shoulder; the two heads overlap and the WHO ARE YOU? tail crosses Cassim's turban (still 61.0). From 63.1 to 67.6 he stands with raised or clasped hands; the planned kneel on "ووعد" 64.82 is not there. The turban falling off at 67.0-67.5 happens from a standing tilt, so it reads as a glitch rather than a bow gone wrong. NEVER AGAIN! I PROMISE! is cut by the right edge (note 9).
- **Why it matters.** This is the climax of Cassim's fear. "Trembled and promised" needs the abject pose, and the turban gag needs the bow to cause it.
- **Fix.**
  - Leader stops at x 300 for the shout; Cassim rises from behind the sacks at x 560 or more, so there is a clear gap of a head-width between them at 60.7. Impact frame as now.
  - "ارتجف" 63.06: knees together, `wet`, 2 unit `tremble`, feather buzzing (visible at this size only if the camera is at zoom 1.5).
  - "ووعد" 64.82: drop to `kneel: 1` over four drawings with `landSquash`, `PERSON_HANDS.clasp` raised toward the leader. "يعود" 66.26: forehead goes down (`tip` forward 0.5 or more); the turban keeps going one drawing later, rolls a short arc toward the leader's feet and wobbles to rest. "مرة أخرى" 66.86-67.3: bald head comes up, one hand pats the bare scalp, eyes flick to the turban.
  - "بالخروج" 69.52: the leader's answer. Make the shadow a clear hard-edged shape that slides across Cassim and the sacks over 0.5 s (now it is a faint triangle, 70.2), and give the leader the planned single slow blink. Cassim stays on his knees through 71.4; do not stand him up again at 68.0.

### 6. 80.3-83.4 s: the throw and the pebbles do not read
- **What is wrong.** Ali Baba is a head above the bush with no visible throwing arm (80.2-81.0). The pebbles are two grey dots about 10 px wide against the sky (81.5). On the scree they are specks at the right edge of the frame (82.6), and the CLATTER! tag touches the edge. The chestnut horse a hand's width from Ali Baba never reacts.
- **Why it matters.** The trick is the turning point; the viewer must see what was thrown, by whom, and where it landed.
- **Fix.**
  - "فرمى" 80.30: Ali Baba rises to chest height above the thicket, arm far back (`tip` -0.15), eyes on the target first; release on 80.9 with the sash tail following through.
  - Pebbles: three stones, 3 times the present size (about 30 px in the panel), light grey with a dark outline and a short white arc trail each, staggered 0.12 s apart. The whip pan follows the first one.
  - "تتدحرج" 82.02: end the pan with the scree in the right half of the frame, not the right tenth; each bounce gets a dust puff and a 2-frame squash; one dislodged larger stone rolls down after them. CLATTER! sits over the slope, fully inside the panel.
  - The horse: at 79.7 ears forward and head swinging toward the thicket, a snort; Ali Baba's eyes snap to it and he sinks an inch; the horse loses interest. One second, and the plan's suspense beat is back.

### 7. 102.5-106.4 s: five sacks you cannot see, and a cart that teleports
- **What is wrong.** The counter 5 and the arrow (103.6-104.8) point at a low heap of sacks mostly hidden behind the thieves' legs in the doorway. At 104.9 the mule flips to face left and the cart jumps from its left side to its right (stills 104.2 and 105.3), then trots off a metre from five thieves who do not react until a small double take at 106.0.
- **Why it matters.** "Leaving most of the gold behind" is the cost of Cassim's greed, the moral's evidence. The mule gag only works if its turn is believable and somebody notices.
- **Fix.** Put the five full sacks on open ground left of the door (x about 1560..1740), large, coins spilling from one, with the thieves still over at the scree from note 1. Counter and arrow on "أكياس" 104.26. For the mule: show the turn as a short walked arc (cart swinging round behind it over 8-10 drawings, dust at the wheels) starting on "تاركين" 102.58 so it is already facing left by 104.9, or start the shot with the cart already turned. On "وراءهما" 105.48 one thief turns, points, mouth open; the tape transition cuts him off.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Back-of-head cover cel** for the front-facing `person` (head-cloth or hair shape over the face, cape on the camera side): gives "turned away" crowds without a new rig. Needed by note 1; useful in every thief and guard scene.
- **Balloon clamp** in the speech helper: keep the box inside the panel margin while the tail stays on the speaker (note 9).
- **`doorPeek`**: a figure clipped by a door or rock edge showing only turban, feather and one eye, with a snap-back; the person counterpart of the fox's `peekFrom`.
- **Thrown-object arc with trail and bounce** (`pebbleThrow`/`rollDown` once enlarged): size floor of about 28 px in the panel so small props survive phone size.
- **Repainted "cool" variant of a plate** for a mood change in one location (cave gold to cave cold) instead of any tint: the indoor equivalent of the time-of-day models.
