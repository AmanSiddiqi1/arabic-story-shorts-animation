Cassim reached the rock before sunrise, called “Open Sesame!” and entered. He filled his sacks with gold until he could barely lift them.

When he wanted to leave, he stood by the door but forgot the magic words. He shouted, “Open Wheat! Open Barley!” The rock stayed shut, and his heart began beating with fear.

Cassim tried again and again, confusing every grain he could name. He sat beside his sacks trying to remember, until he suddenly heard horses outside and knew the thieves had returned.

The leader opened the door and found a stranger among the treasure. He shouted angrily, “Who are you? How did you get in?” Cassim trembled and promised never to return if they let him leave.

Worried about his brother, Ali Baba followed the cart tracks. He saw the thieves at the entrance and rolled pebbles down the rocks. Thinking guards were coming, they sent two men to check.

While the thieves were distracted, Ali Baba signaled Cassim to wait until the two men moved away. Then they ran through the trees together, leaving most of the gold behind.

At home Ali Baba said, “Your desire for money nearly put us both in great danger.” Cassim felt ashamed, thanked his brother for saving him, and promised to avoid the cave and its secrets.

The leader stood by the rock thinking of the escapees. “Two people know our secret now. We’ll search for them in town,” he told his men.
