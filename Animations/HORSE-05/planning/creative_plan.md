Style `cel-anime-80s`, episode 5 of 5 of the Ebony Horse series: the finale, ending on THE END. Narration 144.51 s, last word ends 143.91 s, end hold 4.0 s, film 148.51 s. 19 scenes, 204 actions, 19 lettering items, 9 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. Every action is in `creative_plan.json`; this file gives the direction. Follow the owner direction in `Library/styles/cel-anime-80s/look.md` (reference film ALI-01-ANIME v4), the series bible in `Animations/HORSE-01/planning/creative_plan.md` section 9, and the drawings of HORSE-02 (princessPalace, princessGarden, moonPath), HORSE-03 (twoRiders, tailTorn, turbanScrap, queen, foreignKing) and HORSE-04 (farPalace, farKing, farGuards, craftsmanDisguise, toolbox, gateBar, memoryCard).

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **The offer (0-10.2, P1):** the film opens on episode 4's last frame. The craftsman takes his hand off the knob, bows badly and says he can test the horse, but the princess knows its story. The inventor, who has just seen the crimson sash, is silenced by a spear butt.
- **Joy kept in, greed kept out (10.2-24.9, P2):** she walks to the horse with a straight face and a ribbon that gives her away. The inventor tiptoes for the knob; two spears cross. WAIT!
- **The condition (24.9-37.3, P3a):** bent over a hoof he whispers the plan in two pictures (her palace, then his). She answers: yes, and tell the king the whole truth.
- **The reveal (37.3-46.5, P3b), first peak:** cap and cloak fly, the crown comes out of the toolbox, I AM PRINCE FIRUZ!, and the saffron scrap fits the torn turban. Everyone waits for the king.
- **Judgment (46.5-67.4, P4):** her testimony in three picture cards; the king has the inventor held for the judge, lets the pair go, and the inventor ends behind the grille he once peered through. CLANK!
- **The rescue in the sky (67.4-86.9, P5), second peak:** two pilots run a checklist, the knob turns, the horse rises before the amazed court and circles the great dome, leaving a gold ring.
- **The way home (86.9-104.9, P6):** fields, rivers, her mountains. She lowers the horse herself while Firuz rides with his hands in the air; they follow her river between the two towers.
- **Home (104.9-125.2, P7):** landing, the embrace, his thanks, his promise to her father, the maid with her trunks, the story told on the garden bench as the light turns gold.
- **Two pilots (125.2-148.5, P8):** DAYS LATER... the escort by land to his city with the wooden horse on a cart; her wobbling first solo flight in the garden of episode 1; the sunset sky of episode 1 with two riders. THE END.
- **Conflict:** the lie of episode 4 against the whole truth, before a fair judge. **Arc:** suspense, comic relief, nerve, release, wonder, warmth, laughter. **Surprises:** she, not he, sets the condition; she, not he, lands the horse. **Moral:** the whole truth, told calmly, opens the gate that tricks could not; a kept promise brings everyone home.

## 3. Character acting
- **Firuz as craftsman (to 37.32):** the bad actor of episode 4: rounded shoulders (`tip` 0.07), squint (`lid` 0.3), cloak a drawing late (`lagged`). The prince leaks at 4.1 (chin too proud), 14.3 (the twitch of a smile) and 30.22 (the tap on his chest). **As prince (from 37.9):** `tip` 0, open eyes, cape on `swing`; the bow he could not finish in episode 4 done whole with `flow` (59.02); the episode 1 vault twice (`anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash`); in the air generous: hands off, hands up.
- **Princess:** the still centre for the first minute (`lid` 0.2, `breathe`, complete gestures: the level arm at 49.5 arrives in three drawings and holds). Her feelings live in the rose ribbon until 60.62, when she finally hops. `wet` three times for half a second (28.42, 61.96, 98.26). From the saddle on she is the keener pilot: checklist finger, the light lever hand, the laugh.
- **Inventor:** mask on for the king (`lid` 0.15, smile), off when unwatched (`lid` 0.45, `brow` 0.4), gone for good at 40.32 (`eyes: 'wide'`, `tremble`, `sq` 0.92). Old body: shallow bows, tiptoe with shoulders up, a `hop` to see over spears. The torn tail is always late and he chews it. His last acting is two eyes in a grille following a gold ring.
- **Far king:** eyes lead the head (`gazePlan`); belly-first walk with `footfalls`; the scale hands and one slow palm for authority; a child again at 59.84 (the sneaked knuckle rap) and 79.4 (`hop`, both hands on the turban).
- **Guards:** six individuals on their own seeds (`stagger`): the ripple of helmets, the sleepy one always last and at 79.94 flat on the paving (`impactSquash`); the captain's palm and tidy key.
- **Family:** the queen runs first; the father's sternness melts in two steps (110.04, 119.36); the maid acts with petals and trunks; the two blue guards are never together; the cat trots, rubs, watches the story cloud and finally rides the wooden horse.
- **The ebony horse:** lifeless wood. It acts through light bands, glints, `powerOn`, the rigid `flyRig` (turns of 4 drawings), its shadow and its gold ribbons.
- Moving holds on twos everywhere (`idleSway`, `breathe`, ribbons, pennants, fountain, glints); a blink on every head turn; left and right never mirror.

- Balloons, cards and counters sit in the sky or wall band with tails to heads, never over a face.

- **Suspense:** will the inventor speak (1.3); the hand toward the knob; the long wait for the king (43.8-46.5).
- **Surprise:** the reveal; she lands the horse; the grille from the inside.
- **Emotion:** hands on hearts (the gesture from her roof); the gold path between the towers by daylight; the embrace; the father's hand on Firuz's shoulder; the wave back at 135.12.
- **Series threads closed:** the crimson corner and the scrap (episodes 3-4) become evidence; the hidden lever becomes her skill; the password's path leads home; the knocking child and the vault of episode 1 return; the two-seat counters of episode 3 become two pilots.
