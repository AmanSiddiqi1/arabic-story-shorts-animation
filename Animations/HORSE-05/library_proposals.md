# HORSE-05 library proposals (move after approval; one at a time)

1. **riverRun** (set, `src/h05_sky.js`: `h5RiverRun`, `h5RRSky`, `h5RRFar`): side-view river strip 2800 at panel scale (far bank of flat roofs with washing lines and minarets on two parallax layers, palms, river with drifting glints, boats, the two towers with the lamp bridge, rose palace beyond, reed front strip); callers add the reflection and the gold path. Generic river journey.
2. **kingdomBelow 'fields'** (set, `h5FieldsTile` + `h5Land` + `h5Range`): patchwork with poppy, wheat and pasture fields, three braided rivers, windmills, villages, in the perspective bands of `ehLand`; `h5Range` is the rising mountain band.
3. **disguiseOff** (motion, `h5ThrownCloak`, `h5CloakOnHead`, `h5Cap`, `h5HairCrown`): the cloak thrown as a flat panel along an arc and draped over a helmet, the flying cap, and a crown drawn separately from the head (out of a box, slipping, pushed back).
4. **twoRiders options** (`h5Pair`, `h5RiderHeads`): princess in front, Firuz behind (riding costume), solo front rider with `rear: null`; rider scales follow the horse.
5. **saddleRope**, **scalesIcon**, **memoryCardRound** (props, `src/h05_common.js`): crimson cord round two riders (taut with a vibration), balance scales icon (tilt), round picture card.
6. **farPalace dome front pass and ribbon ring** (`h5DomeCircle` in `src/h05_square.js`): the great dome redrawn over a rig behind it and a gold ribbon ring in two halves round the dome.
