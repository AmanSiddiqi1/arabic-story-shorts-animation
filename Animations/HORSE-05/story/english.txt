Firuz stood beside the horse and told the king, “I can test it, but the princess knows its story.”

The princess agreed and approached, hiding her delight. The inventor tried to move closer, but the guards told him to wait until the inspection was over.

Firuz whispered, “Let us return to your family first, then visit my palace as planned.” She said, “Yes, but tell the king the whole truth.” He revealed his name and pointed to the inventor; the princess looked to the king for permission.

The princess explained what had happened since her abduction. The king ordered the inventor detained for the judge and allowed the pair to test the horse and return to her family, keeping it secured until the hearing.

Firuz and the princess mounted together and checked the controls and ropes. He turned the ascent knob, and the horse rose before the amazed king and guards, circling once above the palace.

They flew over fields and rivers until the mountains of the princess’s homeland appeared. She lowered the horse when her family’s palace came into view, following a river she knew well.

They landed safely, and her family welcomed them joyfully. Firuz thanked her for her courage and resourcefulness and promised to take her to his country with her attendants. They sat together and told what had happened.

Days later, the princess traveled with Firuz and her escort to his palace, as promised, after a joyful farewell to her family. She learned to fly the horse, and they laughed, “Now our kingdoms have two pilots!”
