# AN-132 The Simpleton and the Sharper: creative plan

Film length 178.85 s (MP3 175.85 s + 3.0 s THE END). 17 scenes, three sets (road, hut, market). All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is in `creative_plan.json`.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Spine.** A trusting man leads his donkey on a neck rope and never looks back (0-9). Two tricksters watch; one bets he can take the donkey unnoticed (9-22). He unties the walking donkey, hands it to his partner (22.5-32.8), loops the rope round his own neck and walks on as the donkey (32.8-42.8). After a long walk he stops; the owner pulls, nothing moves (42.8-56), turns and finds a man (56-67). The lie: a drunk son struck his mother, her curse made him a donkey for years (67-88.7), today her blessing turned him back (88.7-99.6). The owner believes, apologises, frees him (99.6-111). At home his wife believes too and gives charity (111-132). Days later he goes to buy a donkey (132-147.5), finds his own (147.5-155), scolds it in the ear for sinning again, refuses to buy it (155-169) and leaves, never understanding (169-175.6).
- **Conflict.** Cunning against trust. Nobody is hurt; the loss is a donkey and common sense.
- **Surprises.** The loop going over the thief's own head (36.14); the reveal on the turn (58.94); the transformation (81.78); the donkey for sale (148.2); the owner's conclusion (162.68), which is the real punchline.
- **Emotional arc.** Sunny contentment, mischief, astonishment, pity and guilt (played sincerely), domestic piety, then righteous indignation aimed at the wrong target. The donkey alone feels real joy (148.2) and real sadness (167.86).
- **Ending and moral.** He walks away proud. The thieves peek out; the donkey looks at us. A man who believes everything can be robbed twice.

## 3. Character acting
- **Simpleton** (`person` costume villager, s 0.9): open face, raised brows (-0.3), gaze strictly forward [1, 0] from 0 to 55.9 with one near turn at 46.18. Bouncy walk. Big reactions are sincere: jump with `anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash` and a lagging turban (61.18); `wet` eyes, trembling lip and one tear (99.64); bow with `tip` 0.3 (102.16). Anger is small and prim: `flush`, brow 1, finger wag, nose up with `lid` 1 (168.1).
- **Sharper** (`person` costume servant, blue tunic, red cap, s 0.92): two faces. SLY (lid 0.3, smile, wink) whenever the owner cannot see; MASK (wet, brow -0.6, frown, slow head drop) whenever he can. Slips at 78.88 (eye between the fingers), 101.2 (grin while the owner wipes his eyes), 110.14 (heel click). Donkey imitation: `tip` 0.22, `sq` 0.95, hands as ears flicking alternately. Looks to camera only at 36.86, 47.8, 101.3 and 174.12.
- **Partner** (`person` costume bedouin, s 0.82): the nervous copycat, always 3-4 frames late (`stagger`), saccade eyes, hands clasped.
- **Donkey** (`donkey` pal 0, red bridle, s 0.72): the only one who notices everything. Ear acting leads (earL/earR), then eyes, then head. Looks back at his owner when led away (`headTurn` 1, `sag` 0.5 at 31.48); joy at 148.2 (ears 1, `tailSwish`); `bray` 1 at 152.0; bewildered ears and a deadpan look to camera at 164.08; `sag` 0.8 and `wet` at 167.86; last slow blink at 176.6.
- **Wife** (villageWoman): mirrors her husband's trust: `cheeks` at 120.4, tremble and a guilty look at the peg (122.8), brisk and practical for the purse and in S13 (`hipsHands`).
- **Mother** (oldWoman with stick, cloud only): gentle advice, hurt, arms raised for the curse, kneeling `reachBoth` for the blessing. **Beggar** (ascetic): stooped, hopeful, bows.
- Everyone has a gaze target and blinks (`LIFE.t = t`; `noBlink` during the freezes at 13.2, 58.26, 147.56). Listeners react: the owner outside the tale cloud has an action on every sentence.
- Secondary motion: rope sag one beat behind the walk, ear spring after the loop passes, turban lag, shawl ends, tail swish, sack bounce.

## 4. Camera and composition
- Road section: one side-scrolling strip driven by SX(t) = 80 + 45 t. Wide establishing (S1), medium on the bush with the walkers crossing in the foreground (S2), track on the donkey for the theft with the owner's back always in frame at the right edge (S3), `twoShot` for the rope gag (S4), wide for the passing of time and a symmetric tug of war (S5), slow push on the turn then a whip pan along the rope for the reveal (S6).
- Tale (S7-S9): fixed two-shot at zoom 1.0, men at the bottom, the 720 x 420 cloud in the upper half, slow push into the cloud for the curse and back out for the cure.
- Close on the believing face (S10, zoom 1.7), full room for the hut (S11, S13), pan to the door for the charity (S12), tracking pan along the market row (S14), push in on the recognition (S15), the tight ear two-shot (S16, zoom 1.8-1.95), final pull out to the tableau (S17).
- Faces and key action stay between world y 250 and 900; lettering and balloons go in the sky or upper corners, never over a face. Panel-only shake (2-4 px) at 51.92, 54.6, 61.18, 168.1.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Suspense:** the owner passes within arm's reach of the frozen thieves (13.24); the untying is done while walking with the rope held taut (25.8-28.6); the near turn for a fly (46.18).
- **Humour:** the loop as a crown (34.42); hand ears (40.22); the tug of war against a serenely leaning man (51.92-55.3); the cloud donkey still wearing the red cap (84.42); TA-DA (98.0); the owner doing the donkey's work himself (133.68); the donkey's helpful flicks during the checklist (151.2) and its look to camera (164.08).
- **Surprise:** whip-pan reveal (58.94); POOF (81.78); the thieves as the sellers (147.1, 171.4).
- **Emotion:** the owner's sincere tear and careful removal of the rope (99.64, 107.48); the couple's worry cloud (124.94); the donkey reaching after his owner (167.86, 171.0).
- **Dramatic irony:** the audience always sees the mask slip; the owner never does. The rope loop returns in the thief's hand at 173.3 to close the film.

## 8. Warnings and open questions
- Only four narration pauses: almost no room for sound effects; timing and faces carry the comedy.
- Profile walking with a front-facing figure and a rope behind is the main staging risk.
- Cloud figures are about 115 px tall: verify legibility at phone size.
- The English text is a condensed summary of the Arabic (see the `SCRIPT:` entry); P6 chunks cannot match clause for clause.
- Owner questions (7, in the JSON): the softened blow, the depiction of drinking, the thieves shown as market sellers, the beggar, the invented lettering, the red bridle, and the script note.
