# AN-088 The City of Many-Columned Iram and Abdullah Son of Abi Kalabah: creative plan

Film length 173.94 s (MP3 170.94 s + 3.0 s THE END). All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard (18 scenes, 163 actions, every cue word and time) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction. Paragraph 2 is absent by design: the script has P1, P3-P8.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
A story inside a story. **Frame:** Abdullah, a plain desert man looking for a lost she-camel, walks into a magnificent city with nobody in it (P1), carries off pearls and perfume (P3), is doubted by the Caliph, proves it, and the scholar Ka'b names the place (P4). **Legend:** King Shaddad reads about Paradise, wants a copy on earth, strips his people of their treasure (P5), builds for centuries (P6), and is destroyed by a great cry one day before he arrives (P7). **Frame again:** the road vanished; Abdullah saw the remains of a city its owner never reached; a warning against pride and power (P8).

- Conflict: in the frame, belief (will anyone believe him?); in the legend, a king against his own limits.
- Surprises: the wall rising over the dune (11.68), PEOPLE: 0 (24.50), the pearl as proof (52.28), the name IRAM! (61.48), the cry (138.24).
- Emotional arc: tired, then wonder, then unease, then comic nerves at court, then pomp and greed, then a pang (the robbed couple, 92.78), then the long build, then joy (119.64), then dread (131.6-138), then silence, then quiet reflection.
- Ending: the crown sinks into the sand in front of the night city; THE END at 170.6, held to 173.94.
- Two threads tie the halves together: the **pearls** (taken from the people 90.3, found in the street 25.1, shown as proof 50.7, the window into the past 157.9) and the **crown** (too big in S9, all that is left at 141.0, buried at 169.7).

## 3. Character acting
- **Abdullah** (person, costume bedouin): always the smallest status in the frame. Tired walk (tip 0.05, lid 0.3), double takes with a small `hop`, rubs his eyes with both fists (14.0), cupped-mouth call and cupped-ear wait (19.4, 20.6), tiptoe search, armsOut shrug. At court his gaze darts up at the arches and he trembles slightly (`tremble`); his hands droop when doubted (47.3); the pearl lift is his one proud stretch (sq 1.06). In S16 the chest gesture ("me?") and a shy smile.
- **The Caliph** (costume caliph, seated): economy of movement. Chin stroke, one pointing snap with `anticipate`, lid 0.5 with one raised brow for doubt, folded arms and two head shakes (gaze -1/1/-1). Payoff: at 52.3 the arms fly open and he half rises; in S16 he sits on the throne edge with hands on knees.
- **Ka'b** (costume physician, age 1, stick): half-speed walk, calm lids, then bright eyes when he recognises the pearl. Blows dust off the book before opening it; his finger follows the line as he reads.
- **Shaddad** (caliph preset, yellow and red palette, crown 1.3x): lounging, lid 0.5, drumming fingers; greedy V brow (brow 1) when the idea lands; hipsHands; rubs his hands at each heap and traps a rolling pearl under his shoe. Aged 0.9 and asleep at 114.3, one eye opens first (`wink`), then the biggest jump in the film (`anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash`). At 136.9 he is the last to look up, still smiling at the city.
- **Groups** never move together: governors bow 0.2 s apart and collide at the door; builders hammer on offset cycles; courtiers enter one by one with different walk phases; heads turn up in `stagger`.
- **The robbed couple** (inset, 91.9-93.2): the woman's hands follow the necklace a beat too long (reachBoth), `wet` eyes, then both look at open empty palms. Four seconds, played straight.
- Everyone blinks through `LIFE.t`, and every figure has a `lookAt` target in every shot.

## 4. Camera and composition
- World 1000 x 1230 (desert strip 2400 wide), ground y 900; heads kept between y 560 and y 760 in dialogue shots. Labels above heads, never over a face.
- Reveals by camera: the crane up the wall (11.5-14.0), the push through the dark gate arch (20.6), the push into the book page (68.4) and back out (144.8), the push into the pearl and the iris out (157.9-160.8).
- Rank at court is shown by the gap between Abdullah (x 380) and the throne (x 730); the pearl is held in that gap so both eye lines meet on it.
- The build (S11-S12) is one locked front view so the growing city reads like a time-lapse; one foreground builder is the clock.
- S14 tracks sideways with the procession; S15 stops the camera with them. Panel-only shake for the cry (138.8-139.5). The ending pulls out slowly and stops.
- Transitions: LATER... at 36.1 (the news travels), page turn with LONG AGO... at 70.9, page turn back at 144.78, iris at 160.8; otherwise cuts and two wipes.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** the camel thought cloud with "?", HELLO? and its shrinking echo, PEOPLE: 0, the messenger's cap, governors stuck in the doorway, the engineer reading while walking into the spring, the king asleep under a cobweb.
- **Suspense:** the empty street (stillness, no birds), and 131.6-138.2: banners flip, the counter drops to 1, a step held in the air, the sky darkens, heads lift one by one, the king last.
- **Surprise:** the cry is white and fast: rings, 2 white frames, shake, then sand. There is no sound effect on it (no pause in the narration), so the picture must do it.
- **Emotion:** the robbed couple; the generations of builders; the open gate with nobody in it; the SO CLOSE arrow that stops short; the crown going under.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus
- Copy `Library/template`; cues with `w(p, n)`; `LIFE.t = t`; bake static sets with `bake()` and draw build fields, sand, sky and glints live.
- All walks through `walk(X, t)` + `footfalls` + `dustBurst`; the procession is one position function with per-figure x offsets of 90 to 110 and different phases; the far row is the same figures flat black at s 0.45.
- iramCity build fields rise inside a clip at the wall top with `K.beat` overshoot; columns are a loop of 9 with `clamp(columns - i)`.
- sandBlow: draw the figure offscreen, tint it sand colour, clip below a wipe line moving head to feet, and emit seeded dots above it; everything is a function of p.
- Sound: pop 7.3, chime 113.95, chime 170.7 only. `SND` master 0.6.
