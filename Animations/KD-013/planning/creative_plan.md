# KD-013 The Ascetic and the Thieves: creative plan

Film length 166.0 s (narration 162.54 s + 3.5 s THE END). 19 scenes, 158 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. The full action list, with acting and Library fields for each action, is in `creative_plan.json`; this file gives the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** Three thieves take a holy man's goat without touching it: one after another they call it a dog until he doubts his own eyes and leaves it by the road.

**Conflict.** One man's own eyes against three confident voices. The goat never changes; only his certainty does.

**Spine (17 beats in the JSON).**
- Setup: he buys a fat goat (5.26), ties the rope (9.90), walks home happy; eyes open in the tree behind him (18.40).
- Inciting: three thieves want the goat for nothing (21.54). Turn: no force, "we will make the man abandon it himself" (31.86-40.28).
- Rising, in three steps with a visible counter: thief 1 (50.88) and he laughs (52.50); thief 2 (73.60) and he stops (77.12); thief 3 (91.46) and he worries (100.18).
- Suspense: he weighs it up, 1 against 3 (111.52). Surprise: he blames the seller's magic (126.46).
- Climax: he unties the rope himself (127.82-131.30).
- Reveal and comic release: the thieves stroll out and pick the rope up (144.98), laughing (149.42).
- Moral image: he hunts the seller while the goat is still a goat (160.22).

**Emotional arc of the ascetic.** Content, amused certainty, first crack, worry, paralysis, false certainty (anger). The audience is ahead of him from 18.4 s on, so the pleasure is dramatic irony.

**Moral.** A lie does not become true because three people repeat it. The narration does not state it, so the film shows it and adds no moral card.

## 3. Character acting

- **Ascetic** (person, owner base, `ascetic` costume). His state is readable from three things: the **smile** (full until 73.6, half until 77.1, gone after), the **rope** (slack and swung fondly, then held short, then loose in a limp hand, then dropped) and his **walk** (spring with `kick` on each footfall, then flat, then a trudge with `tip 0.05`). Brows: -0.3 content, -0.5 first worry (79.9), -0.9 fear (100.2), +1 anger (134.1 on). Eyes: he looks at people until 77 s, then mostly at the goat; `wet` at 117.8. Suspense by stillness at 78.6 (pupils only). `spiralEyes` only at 127.06-127.6.
- **Thief 1, leader.** Economical and sly: `lid 0.4`, `brow 0.6`, one-finger gestures, temple tap, a `wink` to the lens at 40.3. His lie is an over-acted double take (`PERSON_HANDS.cheeks`, saccades, `sq 1.06`). Mask on / mask off at 61.52.
- **Thief 2, eager.** Short and round, always a beat late (`stagger`), wants to use the sack, deflates (`sq 0.92`). His lie is close inspection and pity (`tip 0.3`, eye rub, tut); the false beard swings (`swing`) and slips.
- **Thief 3, confident.** Tall, slow, half lids (`lid 0.35`), `hipsHands`, `folded`, a heavy hand on the shoulder. He does not act surprised at all, which is why he is believed.
- **Goat.** The straight man. Constant sideways chew, slow blinks, ears that bounce late, a look to the lens at 91.9, 139.1 and 161.0. It bleats exactly when the truth is spoken (55.94, 160.22). It never resists anyone: it walks off with the thieves as happily as with the ascetic.
- **Seller.** Friendly and plain in S1-S2. The shifty and sorcerer versions exist only inside thought clouds.
- Everywhere: a gaze target and blinks for every character (`LIFE.t = t`), anticipation before pose changes (`anticipate`), `settleTo` after, listeners react a few frames late, no two thieves move in unison.

## 4. Camera and composition

- Two worlds: `marketStreet` (1000 wide) and `countryRoad` (3000 wide, home to the right). The ascetic always travels left to right going home and right to left going back, so direction itself tells the story.
- Establishing wide and push-in (S1), insert for the knot (S2 tying, S15 untying: the same framing, zoom about 2.0 and 2.6), tracking walks (S3, S18), `twoShot` for each encounter with the goat kept in the bottom of frame, slow push-in on the first doubt (S9), tilt up to make sky room for the big cloud (S12), static wide for the abandonment (S16) so the ambush has a calm frame to break.
- Each encounter has its own landmark and entrance: milestone and skid-in, bush and rise, wall and stride-in from ahead.
- Counter DOG? top-right of the picture panel; thief labels with arrows on the ordinal words; balloons on the far side of the speaker; nothing below y 1250. Faces above the subtitle panel at all times.
- One `later` card at 62.6-63.9 ("A LITTLE LATER...", matching `بعد قليل`) hides the camera jump to the bush. Wipes at 12.0 and 153.8 for the changes of place. An inset circle at 157.6 shows the goat and the ascetic at the same time.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** thieves in masks whose disguises fool only the ascetic; the moth in the pocket; the goat bumping into his legs; the leader lifting the rope with two fingers after all that effort; the goat's deadpan look to camera.
- **Suspense:** eyes in the tree at 18.4; the counter climbing 1, 2, 3; the scale tipping; the long quiet wide before the ambush.
- **Surprise:** the plan is not to steal (NO FORCE); the ascetic's conclusion is magic, not a trick.
- **Emotion:** the goat looking up at him as the loop comes off (130.5); his chin sinking at 119.8.
- **Setup and payoff:** rope tied / rope untied in the same framing; the leader's rope-drop mime (39.46) / the real drop (131.3); temple tap (38.10 / 150.8); the unused sack.
- **Clarity device:** the truth is always visible in the objective shot. The goat becomes dog-like only under the blue dream wash, which marks his view.
