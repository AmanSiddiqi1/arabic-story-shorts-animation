## Reusable techniques for the Library

- **Animal in front, head clear of the person** in every held two-shot: confirmed as a rule worth writing into the guide; it is what turned the middle of this film around.
- **Flicker on a visible face** (`asDog` about 0.55 for 10 frames) for a first doubt, with the full transformation reserved for the dream wash: a clean two-step way to show a character's perception slipping without lying in the objective shot.
- **Inset circle for "meanwhile"** in clear sky, subject only, label outside with the arrow stopping at the rim, placed so the end plate can sit beside it.
- **Cross the object, not the negative label** (NO FORCE with the X on the sack).
- **Balanced caption line breaks** to avoid one-word orphan lines in Arabic.
