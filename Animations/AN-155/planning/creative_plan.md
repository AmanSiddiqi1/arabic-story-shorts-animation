# AN-155 Di'ibil al-Khuza'i with the Lady and Muslim bin al-Walid: creative plan

Film length 179.87 s (MP3 175.87 s + 4.0 s THE END hold; limit 180 s). 18 scenes, 186 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is in `creative_plan.json` (this file explains the intent).

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Spine.** A poet who lives by his wit meets a woman who is quicker than he is, then a friend who is slyer than both of his verses. Three movements:

1. **The duel of charm (0 to 58 s).** Diibil, idle by a door in Baghdad, sees a beautiful woman pass (5.92). He throws her a verse of sleeplessness and tears (12.92). She answers at once in the same metre (25.28). He tries a question about love; she tops it (38.3). He changes metre to trap her; she changes with him without looking (47.14). Three for three. He asks her to come with him, and she chooses to (58.06).
2. **The errand (58 to 109 s).** His own room is not fit for a guest (62.56), so he takes her to his friend Muslim, a poet with a fine house (75.96). Muslim has no money for food (82.62) and hands Diibil a precious kerchief to sell (90.88). Diibil runs, sells, buys, and trots back picturing their thanks (108.44).
3. **The door (109 to 176 s).** The room is empty and the private door slams (117.12). An arm takes the food; Muslim thanks him sweetly and shuts him out (122.56). Diibil understands (127.6). Verses fly through the door (131.82), he kicks it (139.12), and Muslim ends the argument: my house, my kerchief, my money (144.24 to 145.88). Diibil admits defeat (163.74), walks off at dusk and never hears of her again (171.68).

**Conflict.** Wit against wit, twice: first playful (Diibil and the lady), then costly (Diibil and Muslim). **Surprises:** her instant answer; the empty purse; the slammed door; the hand that comes back for the jug. **Emotional arc:** boredom, infatuation, swagger, embarrassment, eager helpfulness, bliss, shock, rage, deflation, rueful acceptance. **Ending:** quiet and a little sad after the farce: a small figure in a darkening street. **Moral (shown, not lettered):** the clever talker lost to the man who owned the house, the kerchief and the money; mind whose errand you run.

**Tone.** Comedy of manners. The lady is never a prize being handed over on screen: she is the sharpest person in the film and always composed. The private room is only a closed door.

## 3. Character acting

- **Diibil** (hammy, transparent, lovable). Every feeling reaches his whole body: `sq` stretch on delight, `tip` forward when declaiming, turban tail as secondary motion (lags the head 3 frames, streams in the run, hangs dead at the end). Gestures from `PERSON_HANDS`: `chin`, `chest`, `cupEar`, `pointUp`, `pointFwd`, `palmsUp`, `clasp`, `hipsHands`, `reach`. Signature moves: the one-eyed peek while posing (17.8), look to camera (31.36, 127.6), the conductor arm that sinks (49.2), the finger that sinks (150.82). In the last act his brow travels 0.9 (rage) to -0.6 (sorrow) by `kfn`, never by a snap.
- **The lady** (still, exact, amused). Minimal body motion; eyes lead and the head follows 0.2 s late. Half lids (`lid` 0.2 to 0.5), small late smiles. Her confidence is shown by looking elsewhere while winning: at her veil (31.9), at her nails (48.6). Her agreement is a real decision: a held thinking beat at 56.64, then a nod at 58.06 while he waits without moving.
- **Muslim** (warm host on top, operator beneath). Mask on / mask off: hearty smile, `armsOut`, bows when watched; half lids, raised brow and one-sided smile when Diibil's eyes are elsewhere (77.28, 81.6, 87.74, 96.36). The plan is told by three saccades with `gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)` at 87.74. Behind the door he acts with eyes, brows and one hand through the peephole: slow patient blink (140.78), counting fingers, palm-up shrug (151.66).
- **Listeners always react.** Whoever is not speaking has a gaze target and a response a few frames late (`stagger`): the lady reads each ribbon with saccades; Diibil goes cross-eyed at the ribbon tip (25.5); the guests mirror glances (86.94).
- **Weight and motion.** Walks by `walk(xf, t, {stride})` with `footfalls` (lady stride 70, Diibil 80, run 110, loaded trot 60, defeated walk 50). Stand-up at 11.0 uses `anticipate` + `hop` + `landSquash`; pose changes use `settleTo`; the carried stack and lamps use `swing`; the jug hops on each footfall with `kick`. `tremble` for rage at 138.14; `breathe` on holds. `LIFE.t = t` for blinks, suppressed in the three held stares.

## 4. Camera and composition

- **Street act:** establishing wide with a push-in (S1), then a steady two-shot via `twoShot(diibilHead, ladyHead, {margin: 150})` so the ribbons own the air between the heads. Close-ups only on decisions and reactions: her blink (19.3), his jaw (29.92), the request (54.2).
- **Travel:** the street is one 2400 strip; tracking by `camX` (S6, S7, S11, S12, S18). The market is passed on the way to Muslim's house, so it is familiar when Diibil runs back to it.
- **House act:** wide interior for geography (S9, S13), pan with Diibil's eyes to the private door (113.0), tight two-shot of man and door for the farce (S14, S15), pull-out for the three ownership tags so the room dwarfs him (S16), slow push-in for the admission (S17).
- **Ending:** crane back to a wide dusk street; the figure small in the left third, title in the sky.
- **Layout rules:** faces between world y 680 and 740 at s 0.9; ribbons in y 540 to 680; clouds and tags in the upper third; nothing important below y 960. Labels and bursts never cover a face. Panel-only shake on 117.12 and 139.12.
- **Transitions:** cuts; one match cut through the house door (80.2); one card MEANWHILE... at 109.24 for the time that passed while he shopped (camera and set change under the card's midpoint).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour.** Verse as a physical object: it can stop at a nose, steal a heart, loop a head, stab a door, and flick a face. The hand that comes back for the jug (pat, pat, got it). Arms that keep cradling a stack that has gone. The kick that hurts only the kicker. The finger raised to argue that slowly sinks.
- **Suspense and dramatic irony.** The audience sees Muslim's look (77.28), the plan forming (87.74) and the wink (96.36); Diibil sees none of it. His happy thought cloud (105.8) is laid out exactly like the room he is about to find empty (111.48).
- **Surprise.** Her fake-out before the instant answer (21.42 to 25.28). The moth from the purse. The slam.
- **Emotion.** The drain from rage to sadness in one slow push-in; the repeated `cupEar` (19.1 hopeful, 173.28 hopeless); the dashed outline of the lady fading beside him.
- **Counters and labels on key beats:** VERSES MATCHED 1, 2, 3; coins 5 down to 0; MUSLIM 3, DIIBIL 0.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- **Subject matter.** The private room stays implicit: a door, a sleeve, a hem. No suggestive pictures or lettering. See `owner_questions` in the JSON (7 items, one of them SCRIPT).
- **English is a summary** of the Arabic in P1, P6, P7 and P8; both are kept verbatim.
- **Dense narration:** only four effects; the slam and the kick are silent and carried by shake and lettering.
- **Lettering at the limit** (19 items): drop QUICK WIT!, VALUABLE!, SOLD! first if a reviewer finds it busy.
- **Readability risks:** the peephole face (keep zoom at 1.45 or more in S15), the metre marks (flash them yellow when they match), small Diibil in the S16 pull-out.
