# AN-087 Ibrahim bin al-Mahdi and the Barber-Surgeon: creative plan

Film length 169.38 s (MP3 166.38 s + 3.0 s THE END). 12 scenes, 154 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; the machine-readable storyboard with every cue word is `creative_plan.json` (the source of truth for times, coordinates and Library calls).

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A prince with a price on his head is sheltered by a poor barber who refuses the reward and by a soldier's wife who hides him from her own husband, and is sold by a woman who owed him her freedom. The caliph pardons him and pays each of them back in kind.

**Conflict.** Outside: every stranger could sell him. Inside: can he trust anyone? The story answers three times (yes, yes, no) and the answers do not follow rank or debt: the stranger is loyal, the one who owes him betrays.

**Beats.** Throne lost (7.32) → reward posted (13.28) → hides against his own poster (21.76, comic) → fed but afraid (31.18) → the "weapon" is a lute (37.2, surprise) → "I know who you are" (44.52, reveal) → the notice torn up, the song (49.46 to 53.5, the heart of the film) → purse refused (58.94) → LATER → disguise, soldier, shove at the bridge (70.16, action peak) → the rescuer is the soldier's wife (83.98, surprise) → three days → the freedwoman's greed (96.96, turn) → confession, the court splits (108.78 to 116) → pardon (118.82) → the retelling → four judgments (141 to 160) → song and THE END.

**Emotional arc.** Smug → hunted and jumpy → suspicious → relieved → trusting (the song) → cocky in disguise → panicked → helpless behind the curtain → betrayed → humble → forgiven → generous. Ibrahim's turban is the gauge: knocked crooked at 7.3, lost under the veil, restored at 120.3, straight at last at 163.

**Surprises to protect.** The lute (do not show its shape before 37.2). The barber already knowing (he must look harmless until 41.3). The soldier walking into the hiding place (no hint in the room before 80.2). The old woman's smile dropping only after the door shuts.

**Ending and moral.** Mercy ends the quarrel; loyalty is repaid and betrayal for money is punished. The last picture repeats the barber-room song in the palace: the same sway, now with the caliph beside them.

## 3. Character acting

- **Ibrahim.** A singer and a prince, not a fighter: soft, expressive, easily startled. Smug lids (0.45) on the throne; after 7.3 his eyes never rest (saccade gaze plans, `tremble`) until the song, where the first natural blinks and a `lid` 0.35 smile arrive. In disguise he overacts (tiny `walk` amplitude, `tip` sway, finger wave). In court he is plain and still: `kneel`, `clasp`, `wet`, one slow blink. Anticipation before the shove (`anticipate` 69.9), follow-through as stretch `sq` 1.06.
- **Barber.** Always slow and open. His movements have no hidden beat, which is why the audience (with Ibrahim) suspects him: back turned at the shelf, both arms in the chest. Smile, raised brows, `cupMouth` for the secret, then the one fast move of his part: the tear at 49.46. Refusal: `shield` palm with overshoot, then `chest`.
- **Ma'mun.** Economy. Pupils only when he weighs the two viziers; a hidden laugh (`mouthCover`) at the disguise; 1.6 s with eyes shut and two drumming fingers before the pardon; after that warmer and quicker (cupped ear, chuckle at the splash in the inset).
- **Soldier.** The comic body: bored lean on the spear, double take with helmet lag, windmilling teeter, look to camera before the fall, asymmetric wet limp, sneeze, proud salute that turns into the baffled apron pose.
- **Wife.** Two messages at once: bright face to her husband, flat hush hand behind her back to the curtain (86.34). In court one temple tap and a `wink`.
- **Freedwoman.** Mask on / mask off: the smile drops in 3 frames when the door shuts; pupils travel to the notice; `coinEyes`; in court the shame pattern (look up, eyes break away, head drops).
- **Listeners and groups.** Guards and viziers never move in unison (`stagger`); everyone has a gaze target every frame; `LIFE.t` blinks throughout.
- **Secondary motion.** Crooked turban after the fall, helmet lag, drips and puddle rings on the soldier's footfalls, curtain tremble, lamp flames, torn poster halves rocking down, the veil's flutter in S12.

## 4. Camera and composition

- Panel world 1000 x 1230, ground y 900; faces stay above y 700 in every shot and nothing important sits in the bottom 15 % of the panel.
- S1 wide with a push to the throne on the fall; S2 starts close on the poster and pulls out for the street; S3 to S5 are one room: wide, then a two-shot that tightens to Ibrahim's frozen face at 44.5, then the widest warm frame for the song (53.5).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour.** His own poster (21.76); TA-DA! after dread; TWANG!; the beard tip under the veil; the finger wave to the soldier; the long teeter and the late SPLASH!; the empty bowl sliding back from under the curtain; the court's HA HA!; NEW JOB: BARBER; the veil landing on the soldier at the end.
- **Suspense.** Stillness with moving pupils (29.4, 108.8, 116.1); the wrapped object from the chest; the soldier turning toward the curtain to hang his cloak (85.8); the sword and the heart hanging over the kneeling man.
- **Surprise.** The lute; "I know who you are"; the husband; the smile that drops.
- **Emotion.** The torn notice and the song; the refused purse (wet eyes); the wife's hush hand; "UNCLE!" as he is lifted from his knees; Ibrahim running to fetch the barber by the hand.
- **Setup and payoff.** Reward poster: posted (S2), torn (S5), checked (S6), obeyed (S8). Lute: S4, S5, S11, S12. Veil: on (61.7), lost (68.5), lifted (104.5), laid down (106.8), thrown away (162). Barber's kit: his razor at 25.6 becomes the soldier's at 149.
