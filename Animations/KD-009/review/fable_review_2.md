The narration says "small piece" at exactly this moment (صغيرة 36.88). If touched: `holdS` about 1.5, and ease the scale down to 1.0 over 0.3 s (37.7 to 38.0) as the hand moves to the gap.
- **B. 43.2 to 43.7: the lower "holds it open" arrow points at bare ground**, about one log height below the log, and the upper arrow sits on the label box. If touched: put the lower arrow's tip on the underside of the bottom half and drop the upper arrow 40 units so it clears the box.
- **C. 60.4 to 60.8: two monkeys on screen.** The camera reveals the real monkey on the perch while the inset is still up, so for about 0.4 s he is shown twice. If touched: fade the inset out by 60.3, before the pan reaches the perch.
- **D. 139.5 to 142.2: the "calm down" wide is the emptiest frame in the film** (about 30 % plain ground, two small figures, 3 s). It follows the best acting in the rescue, so the drop in energy is felt. If touched: use the same y bias as the opening wide here.
- **E. 77 to 102: the tail reads as a third arm** (see note 5). A slightly thinner stroke for the tail than for the arms, or a curl at the tuft, would be enough.
