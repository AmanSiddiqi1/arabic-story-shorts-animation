## Remaining notes (polish, do not trigger v3)

### 1. 135.7-138.3 and 155.6-159.7 s. Hero stands with both hands on hips and a heavy brow
What: while the poor man approaches and begs (135.7-138.3) the hero faces him with fists on hips and brows pulled down (`extra/sheetC.jpg`, 137.5). After the gift he goes back to the same pose and holds it through the stoop leaving the poor man and through the push-back (155.6-159.7, `extra/sheetE.jpg`, 157.0 and 159.4); the change log says "open palms" at 158.2 but the hands stay on the hips.
Why: hands on hips reads as a householder challenging a beggar, then as a man waiting to be thanked. The remembering shot that follows is right, so this is a 2-3 s wrong first impression, not a broken beat.
Fix: 135.7-138.3: arms relaxed at the sides or one hand resting on the nearest sack, brow about -0.15, `gaze` on the poor man's face, the smile fading on "صدقة" 138.70 as planned. 155.6-158.2: hands loose and slightly forward (the offering hand lowering slowly), small smile. 158.2-159.7 on "أعاد": both palms open and out at waist height, brows up, head `tip` about 0.08, eyes following the sacks as they slide back.

### 2. 176.9-178.9 s. The walk into the house is a pile-up
What: after the "after you" gesture the friend and the hero occupy the same spot at 177.0-177.6, so one head sits behind the other (`extra/sheetF.jpg` 177.2, `extra/sheetG.jpg` 177.5), and the hero turns to face the camera with hands on hips in the doorway at 177.8. Wife and child then follow as darkened figures in the frame until 178.9.
Why: it is the last picture, under THE END. It reads, but it is the least tidy 2 s of the film.
Fix: do less. Friend walks in alone 176.9-177.7 while the hero holds the "after you" arm and stays left of the door frame, clear of him by at least 60 px. Hero follows 177.7-178.5. Wife and child stay outside to the right of the door, waving, and the door stays open on the lit room; heart and THE END as now. That removes four entries in 2 s and keeps the family in the last frame.

### 3. 61.6-62.4 s. The lady's exit stacks heads

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- Drawn-path digit substitution for a Bangers numeral that misreads at size (`a111Txt7` / `a111With7`): worth moving to `Library/` as a general "safe numerals" helper for counters.
- Memory cloud rule: one large subject on a plain dream wash, at least 45% of the cloud height, never a miniature scene.
- "Transfer across a drawn line": a ground line plus a carried prop gives a division, a gift and a return with the same three objects.
- Guide note: a character's idle pose must not be hands on hips in scenes of asking, giving or receiving; set a neutral arms-down idle for those ranges.
- Guide note: when several characters enter one doorway, stagger them by at least a body width or leave some outside; never route two through the same x in the same second.
