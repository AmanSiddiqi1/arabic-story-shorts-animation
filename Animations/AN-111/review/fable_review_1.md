Verdict: REVISE

# AN-111 The Devout Israelite: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/AN-111_v1.mp4` (180.00 s), all 30 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/`, plus full-size frames in `review/v1_pack/extra/` (sheets `sheet_0..3.jpg`, `sheet_crops.jpg`).

## Summary

The story is told in order, nothing is missing, the ending is there, and both subtitle languages are readable and in sync. The first half is good: the 3-2-1-0 coin routine, the first gift, the bare room, the cracked platter and the whole fish stall sequence (swelling fish, stink lines, PHEW!) work well. The pearl insert (97.0-102.3) is the best shot in the film.

It needs a v2 because of one on-screen factual error (the fortune reads as 10,000) and because the second half, which carries the moral, is staged weaker than the first: a repeated walk, a hero who looks stern while he remembers, a gift that never leaves his hands, and a family reaction too small to read. None of this needs new assets; it is staging, camera and pose work.

No BLOCKER. 7 MAJOR, 5 MINOR. Narration, subtitle text and timing must stay as they are.

## Notes

### 1. 124.2-127.6 s. MAJOR. The fortune reads "10,000", not 70,000
What: in both the counter burst and the yellow lettering, the Bangers "7" has no visible top bar at this size and slant and reads as a "1": the screen says "10,000 DIRHAMS" and "10,000!" while the narrator says "سبعين ألف درهم" and the English line says seventy thousand (see `sheet_crops.jpg`, top left; compare the clear "40,000" beside it).
Why: it is the one number in the story and it is wrong by a factor of seven for anyone reading the picture.
Fix: make the 7 unmistakable. Either draw the leading digit as a path (wide flat top bar, diagonal stem, optional crossbar) at the same size, or set these two strings in a face whose 7 is clear, keeping the burst. Check the intermediate counter values that start with 7 as well. Confirm with a full-size still at 125.8. While there, move the counter burst about 120 px right or the shelf down: it sits on top of the shelf jars and looks cluttered.

### 2. 127.7-134.6 s. MAJOR. The walk home is played twice and the poor man is already there
What: in S19 the hero walks in with the yoke, sets three sacks down beside a seated old man by the door (129.5), and has his thought cloud. At 133.2 he is suddenly back at the left with the sacks on the yoke, walks in again and sets them down again at 134.5. The old man has been sitting in shot since 127.7, eight seconds before "قابله فقير" (135.74).
Why: the repeat reads as a glitch, and the early beggar wastes the meeting. The hero daydreams about his fortune next to a beggar he ignores, which is the opposite of his character.
Fix: one continuous action. S19 (127.84-133.0): hero walks in from the left, stops about x 380 well short of the door, lowers the yoke on "يحمل" 129.74, cloud on "ثروة" 130.64, hand on heart 131.84. The poor man is NOT in S19. S20 starts from the exact end pose of S19 (sacks already on the ground, no second walk); the closer framing is just a cut. The poor man shuffles in from the right edge or round the house corner during 134.6-135.7 (`walk` amp 0.6, `tip` 0.08), arriving on "قابله" 135.74, cupped hand on "وطلب" 137.38.

### 3. 139.7-143.8 s (also 64.7-65.7, 162.0-167.5). MAJOR. The hero looks stern when he should look moved
What: in the remembering close shot his brows are pulled hard down, mouth flat, both hands on his hips, facing the beggar. On a phone it reads as annoyance or refusal. The same hard brow appears on the "sniff" at 64.7-65.7 and during the reveal (kneeling with an angry brow at 163-167).
Why: this is the hinge of the film. The audience must see sympathy and a decision, not a man deciding whether to send a beggar away.
Fix: 139.84 "تذكر": drop the hands from the hips (one hand to the chest or chin), brow soft and raised at the inner ends (about -0.2 to -0.3, never positive), `gaze` drifting up to the cloud, mouth slightly open; at 143.12 the slow single blink, then gaze back down to the old man's face with a small smile before the kneel at 144.02. At 64.7: curious sniff (brows up, head `tip` toward the right, nostril lines), not a scowl. At 162.8-167.5: awe (brows up, `lid` wide, `noBlink` 162.8-163.6 as planned).

### 4. 146.5-159.9 s. MAJOR. The half is never actually given, so it cannot be given back
What: after the dust line and HALF | HALF (good), the hero picks up two sacks and holds them against his own chest from 146.7 to 159.9. The poor man never receives or even reaches for them. "أعاد المال" at 158.20 is a small arm movement toward sacks that are still in the hero's hands. The third use of the giving gesture is therefore missing.
Why: this is the climax. "Gave half without hesitation" and "gave the money back" are the two story events of the scene and neither is visible as a transfer.
Fix: on "وقدم" 146.52 use the shared `GIVE` pose: he carries the two sacks across the dust line and sets them at the poor man's feet by 148.0, then opens his hand toward the man's cupped palm, eyes on the man's face (no glance back at the other two sacks). Keep the dust line and the two remaining sacks visible on his side. On "تردد" 150.06 the half step closer. The poor man looks down at the sacks, then up. At 155.64 the stoop leaves him; raised palm at 157.24; on "أعاد" 158.20 he pushes the two sacks back across the line with staff or hand so they rejoin the other two (four together by 159.2), and the hero's face is confused (brows up, head tilt), not knowing. Hold the push-in planned for 147.6 on the hands and the sacks.

### 5. 150.9-155.6 s. MAJOR. The family's reaction is too small and the shot is dead for 4.7 s
What: S23 pulls out to a wide of the whole house. Four figures stand in a row about 110 px tall; the wife's hand to her mouth, the child's stomach rub on "الجوع" and the wife's nod are not readable, and nothing else moves. The English subtitle does not change during this stretch, so the picture has to carry it.
Why: the plan calls the wife's nod the thing that lets the audience accept the second gift. It is invisible.
Fix: replace the wide with a medium shot of the doorway (wife and child filling about half the panel height, the hero's shoulder and the two given sacks at the left edge for geography). Door opens 151.0; wife's hand to mouth and worried brow at 151.84; child looks at the sacks, then rubs her stomach on "الجوع" 153.32 (4-6 frames after the wife, `stagger`); wife looks from the sacks to her husband, lowers the hand and gives one clear nod at 154.48 (head `tip` down and up, small smile). Cut back to the two-shot at 155.6.

### 6. 103.3-120.3 s. MAJOR. Jewel shop: 17 s in a wide where the pearl and the honesty beat cannot be seen
What: both figures are small in the lower middle of a tall, mostly empty room. The pearl rolling out (107.80) is a few pixels; the expert pushing his own coins forward and pulling them back on "لكنه" 114.82 is a tiny hand move; the hero stands half behind the desk edge with hands on hips for most of it.
Why: "it is valuable, but I cannot afford it, go to a richer man" is an honesty beat that sets up the 70,000. At present it reads as two men standing at a desk.
Fix: after the entrance (104.22), go to a `twoShot` over the desk by 105.4 at roughly 1.7-1.9x the current scale, desk top around y 820. Draw the pearl on a dark cloth on the desk at radius 14 or more with its glint. On 109.12 the expert bolts upright (`sq` 1.06, `settleTo`), lens to eye 109.96 with a short push-in on lens and pearl. "ثمينة" 113.78: spread hands. "لكنه" 114.82: he slides his small coin stack next to the pearl (hold 0.4 s so the comparison reads), shakes his head (two `tip` swings), slides it back. Returns the pearl into the hero's open hands 116.20. Balloon and pointing arm on "تاجر" 117.88 stay as they are. The hero should react each time: hopeful, puzzled, then nodding.

### 7. 87.0-96.9 s. MAJOR. The pearl discovery hides the wife's face
What: the wife has a white band across her lower face from 87.0 (a smell cloth, presumably) and at 94.5-96.9 the lifted pearl and its starburst sit exactly on top of her head. Of the planned "three held stares" only the hero's and the child's exist. The pearl itself is small at 94.2.
Why: the wife is the one who finds it; her face is the reaction shot of the film's big surprise.
Fix: keep the glow-before-pearl idea at 92.98 (it works). At "لؤلؤة" 94.34 she lifts the pearl to the side of her head, toward the hero (about 90 px left of her face, at eye height), so that all three faces and the pearl are clear in one row. Pearl radius at least 18 px at this framing. Shrink the starburst to about 60% and draw it behind the heads. Have her pull the cloth down at 92.6 as she leans in, so her open-mouthed stare is visible; `noBlink` 95.0-95.9 on all three as planned.

### 8. 26.5-36.6 s and 59.6-63.0 s. MINOR. Overlaps in the market
What: (a) during the whole meeting and gift the thread-stall keeper's head sits directly behind the hero's head, giving him a second face at his ear (see `sheet_0.jpg`, frames 3 and 4). (b) The lady (60.2-61.7) and the servant (61.5-62.7) walk straight through the hero's position, overlapping him and each other; each refusal lasts under a second and there is no look at the goods.
Why: (a) spoils the cleanest emotional two-shot of the first act; (b) "no one wanted them" lands only because of the NO THANKS! lettering.
Fix: (a) shift the hero and friend about 110 px right, or have the stall keeper duck out of sight after the sale at 24.76. (b) Each passer-by stops 70-80 px clear of him, looks at the platter (`gaze`), shakes the head or waves (60.80 lady, 62.20 servant) and walks on behind him on a farther ground line, never crossing his body. His arms sink in two steps at 63.46 as planned (this part works).

### 9. 167.8-169.9 s. MINOR. "Thanked God" plays as a victory cheer
What: he kneels with both fists pumped above his head, eyes shut (see `sheet_crops.jpg`, bottom left).
Why: fists read as "I won". The line is gratitude, and the plan asks for open raised palms.
Fix: kneeling, forearms raised to shoulder height with open palms upward (`PERSON_HANDS` open), eyes up to the sky with a soft brow, a slow breath; wife and child arrive at 169.46 and the wife copies the open palms.

### 10. 173.3-179.9 s. MINOR. The ending has a pop-in, a ghost frame and a child left outside
What: the hero is not in the shot until he fades in over his wife at 175.0, already holding the tray. At 177.5 the two men cross-fade through each other on the way in. At 178.0-178.7 the door closes with the child still outside; she then vanishes.
Why: the last picture is the moral ("still giving"); it should be the cleanest staging in the film, and it should rhyme with 32.86 and 146.52.
Fix: the hero walks out of the doorway with the bread tray during 173.3-174.6 (wife and child step aside to the right of the door); friend enters from the left 174.30; on "للعطاء" 175.18 the same `GIVE` pose (hand to the friend's open palm, eyes on his face), tray passed by 176.0. Then all walk in with real `walk` cycles in order (friend, hero, wife, child last, in by 178.6), the door closes at about 179.0, heart and THE END as now. No opacity fades on characters.

### 11. 0.0-3.1 s, 20.5-24.0 s, 37.0-41.5 s. MINOR. Wide shots leave the cast tiny over an empty ground band
What: the opening holds the full hut cutaway for 3 s with the three figures about 70 px tall, and the bottom quarter of the panel is plain brown floor. The yard shots have the same proportions.
Why: first impression on a phone; "رجل صالح" is said over a man who cannot be seen, and his hand-to-chest at 0.80 is lost.
Fix: start the film already pushed in to about the 4.5 s framing (or start the push at 0.3 and finish by 1.5), then use the remaining time for the spindle beat. In the yard shots frame with ground at about 80% of panel height so the figures gain 25-30% in size. Keep the S27 = S1 rhyme by using the same tighter framing for both.

### 12. 78.2-84.2 s and 141.2-143.8 s. MINOR. Two cluttered moments
What: (a) at the swap, the two-way arrow, the stink lines, the platter and the fish all pile onto one spot (78.2-78.8), and at 83.2-84.2 the seller holds the platter in front of his own face while the jar and the fan float beside it. (b) The memory cloud shows a miniature market wide in which the hero is about 25 px tall; the child's empty tray in the second cloud is equally small.
Why: (a) the handshake and the seller's relieved breath (83.22) are the pay-off of the comedy; (b) the memory is the reason for the second gift.
Fix: (a) fade the stink lines to 30% during 77.7-79.1, put the arrow above the heads, and have the seller hug the platter at chest height with the jar on the counter, face clear for the deep free breath. (b) Fill each cloud with one large subject on a plain dream wash: first himself from the waist up with the two unsold things and sinking arms (142.42), then the child holding the empty tray (143.12), each at least 45% of the cloud height.

## Subtitles
Arabic is verbatim, large and well placed. The English reads correctly against the Arabic in every chunk I checked and is legible at full size, including the three-line chunk at 144.46-155.64 (smallest text in the film, still readable). No change requested.

## What to keep
The coin counter routine (12.5-17.5), the gift at 32.9-35.5, the empty-tray and neck-rub scene (42-49), the platter crack drawing on "متشقق", the fish stall build (66-73), the pearl insert with the crossed-out drill hole (97-102.3), the HALF | HALF dust line, and the stoop leaving the poor man before the light.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- Insert pattern "object + one squinting eye + ghost defect crossed out" (S15) is a clean way to show a negative fact such as "never drilled".
- Ground-strip lettering under a drawn dust line (HALF | HALF) for any division or sharing beat.
- Add to the guide: check Bangers numerals at size on a still. The 7 can read as 1; use a drawn digit when a number matters.
- Add to the guide: when a prop is lifted for a reveal, place it beside the holder's head, never on it, and draw bursts behind faces.
