Style `cel-anime-80s`, episode 3 of 5 of the Ebony Horse series. ## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Homecoming (0-17.7):** Firuz lands in his father's garden; a slumped king springs up, the household rushes in. He tells the story with two picture clouds (the lever, the far city with a blue dome). **Plant:** behind a cypress the inventor listens, and his are the only eyes that stay on the blue dome when everyone else looks up.
- **The promise (18.9-33.3):** DAYS LATER... on the rose palace roof at golden dusk. The password, her hand on her heart, her parents. She chooses to come later, by land, with an escort; the horse has two seats and a trunk slides off to prove it.
- **The opening for the villain (34.4-51.6):** Firuz hides the horse under a striped cloth in the rose bower and leaves by the gate. On the desert road a hooded rider in a caravan taps his ear. A saffron turban rises behind the hedge, directly behind the princess.
- **The plan (51.6-82.5, P4 and P5):** the script tells the trick twice. The first telling is staged as the inventor's **rehearsal in a large thought cloud**: everything works at once, he leaps like an acrobat, the "prince nearby" is a cardboard cut-out on a stick. The real inventor mouths his lines behind the hedge and laughs without a sound.
- **The event (82.5-119.6, P6 and P7):** the second telling is the real thing, and harder: the cat hisses, the princess backs away, the cloth snags. What wins her is true knowledge: the UP knob and the DOWN lever, exactly what Firuz showed her in episode 2. She sits to learn; he scrambles up behind and gives the knob a hard half turn. Impact frame. The cat tears a scrap from his turban tail. In the sky: TAKE ME BACK! NO!
- **Discovery and vow (120.6-144.4):** golden afternoon. Firuz returns with a rose to an empty bench and an empty bower. The cat shows him the saffron scrap; in a thought cloud it fits the notch in the inventor's turban. He ties it into his crimson sash, looks up at the fading ribbon trail and promises to bring them back. TO BE CONTINUED...
- **Conflict:** trust again, but turned over: in episode 2 honesty made a stranger a friend, here a liar uses true facts. **Arc:** joy, warmth, playfulness, unease, comic menace, alarm, loss, resolve. **Moral:** a liar who knows true things is the most dangerous kind; a promise is kept by going after what was taken.

## 3. Character acting
- **Firuz:** light and quick at home (`hop`, `landSquash`, `PERSON_HANDS.armsOut`, talking with whole-arm `flow` arcs); formal and a little clumsy before her parents (the crown slips again). Hiding the horse he is the polite burglar of episode 2 (saccades left and right, chin in hand, a toe nudging the cloth). In S18 the walk dies in two drawings and the cape swings past him (`swing`); the smile fades over six drawings; the rose droops after the hand (`lagged`). In S19-S20: eyes first, then head, then body; kneel with `flow`; `eyes: 'wide'`, then `brow` 0.6; the jaw sets with no frown; fist to chest; cape lifting. Blink on every head turn.
- **Inventor:** two faces. Mask on: `lid` 0.15, `brow` -0.2 to -0.3, smile, slow bows on `flow`. Mask off (only when she is not looking: 87.6, 99.3, and for good at 107.18): `lid` 0.4-0.5, `brow` 0.4, mouth closed, eyes in saccades (`gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`). Behind the hedge he acts with turban, eyes and hands only: beard stroke, temple tap, finger up, hand rub, silent laugh (`tremble`, `mouthCover`). His body is old: the real bow is shallower than the imagined one, the real mount is a two-stage scramble with `tremble`, the turban always a drawing late (`lagged`). One look to camera (99.3).
- **Plan figures:** deliberately too smooth. Plan inventor never stumbles and leaps with a turn; plan princess smiles at once. They are on twos like everyone, but with no anticipation and no overshoot, which reads as daydream.
- **Princess:** upright and economical as in episode 2. Wary set: weight on the back foot, `lid` 0.25-0.3, `brow` 0.3, raised palm, `folded` arms, the look up and down, two glances at the empty gate. Her arms unfold on the two controls (96.9): she is convinced by evidence. On the horse she is curious (she likes machines), then `eyes: 'wide'`, then angry rather than helpless: she shouts, points, and her eyes find the DOWN lever (118.8), which sets up later episodes.
- **King:** slumped (`sq` 0.96, `lid` 0.5), then a pop (`sq` 1.06), the belly-bouncing run with `footfalls`, a hug that squashes both, one tear (`wet`).
- **Foreign king and queen:** he stern and slow, lowering a spear with one finger; she amused behind her hand. Guards a beat apart, helmet slipping.
- **Maid:** a walking stack of trunks with eyes; the trunk slides off with `fallY` and `impactSquash`.
- **Cat:** the film's conscience. Ear to the hedge first (51.0), the full stare (66.9), `arch` + `puff` + hiss (83.58), sits by the princess as a second doubter, arches again (107.3), springs at the turban tail (109.62), hangs, drops with the scrap, and in the end shows it to Firuz and leans on his boot.
- **The ebony horse:** lifeless as always; under the cloth it is a lump with one brass hoof showing. Acting by `powerOn`, glints, the rig and its shadow.
- Moving holds on twos everywhere: `idleSway` on `tip`, head and hands, `breathe` on `sq`, veil, cape and turban-tail ribbons, fountain, butterflies, banners, the cloud edge. Groups never in sync (`stagger`, own seeds).

## 4. Camera and composition
- **Wide where the place matters:** S2 (whole home palace, zoom 0.62), S4 (terrace, dome, towers, river, 0.75), S6 (whole garden with roof and dome, 0.72), S7 (desert road tracking), S16 (pull out to 0.62 as the horse leaves), S20 (0.62: a small prince under a huge golden sky).
- **Pans where it is detailed:** S3 pans from the listeners to the cypress; S5 pans from the family to the horse; S7 tracks 1200 units of road; S13 and S18 follow the walks in from the gate. Ground stays near panel y 930 to 955.
- **One held frame for the plan (S8-S12):** zoom 0.9, centre (1000, 520): the cloud in the upper half, the real garden alive in the lower half. The viewer watches the liar and his lie at once.
- **Close-ups only at peaks:** two inserts (the hand on the knob 107.94, the scrap 129.12). No face close-ups; S14 goes no closer than zoom 1.0 with full figures.
- **Flight framing** as in the bible: horse in the upper third, shadow on the lawn at lift-off, climbing = nose up; in S17 level with a 6 px bob, the river city sliding away below.
- Balloons and tags in the sky band with tails to heads, never over a face; the caption strip is the bottom third of the film.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** the trunk on the polished rump; the hoof that will not stay hidden; the hooded cloak pushed into a point by the turban; the imagined acrobat and the real twinge in the back; the cardboard prince; the cloth that snags; a finger in the ear for "refused to listen".
- **Suspense:** the eyes that do not look up (17.0); the turban rising right behind her (49.94); the plan shown in full before it starts, so every real step is watched for whether it will work; mask off behind her back; the held beat at 109.62.
- **Surprise:** the plan does not go as imagined (the cat, her retreat), and then it works anyway; the impact frame after a quiet lesson; the cat coming down with the clue.
- **Emotion:** the king's run; her hand on her heart again; the rose sinking; the dashed outlines where horse and princess were; saffron tied into crimson; the fading gold trail.
- **Series threads:** the password and the moon path pay off; the foreign king is introduced for episode 4; the princess knows where DOWN is; the scrap stays in Firuz's sash.
