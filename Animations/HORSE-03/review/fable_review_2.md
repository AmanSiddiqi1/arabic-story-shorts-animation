## Notes (polish, most useful first)

### 1. 10.7-17.6 s and 42.8-46.0 s: thought-cloud pictures still show their rectangle
- **What is wrong.** At 16.9 the flying palace sits in a pale-blue box with straight left, right and bottom edges inside the white cloud. `revision_1.md` reports the 42.8-46 cloud unchanged too.
- **Why it matters.** It is the first cloud of the film and it looks pasted; the 24.5-30 cloud, already repainted, shows the fix works.
- **Fix.** Same treatment as the 24.5-30 cloud: clip the picture to the cloud outline and paint its sky edge to edge. In the 42.8 cloud slide or cross-fade one full picture to the next, and keep the THE INVENTOR tag clear of the cloud.

### 2. 51.6-82.5 s: the real princess under the plan cloud
- **What is wrong.** Below 31 s of lively daydream she reads in one pose (strips 52-54 and 78-82 show the same silhouette).
- **Why it matters.** She is the target of the plan; a little life makes the irony play.
- **Fix (three small beats, no camera change).** 57.54: she turns a page and smiles as the plan princess claps. 66.0-67.5: the cat stares hard at the hedge, the turban dips to the brow and comes back. 70.46: a hand comes up to the real inventor's back above the hedge on the imagined leap.

### 3. 86.2-87.3, 98.5-99.6, 137.7-140.0 s: balloon tails
- **What is wrong.** The tail is a wide black wedge that lands on the inventor's turban (86.5, 99.0) and on Firuz's crown (138.6), the darkest shape next to the face at the vow.
- **Fix.** The narrow tail used in the revised HORSE-01 and HORSE-02, ending 20 units above the headgear.

### 4. 101.0-105.6 s: the mount is half hidden
- **What is wrong.** At 102.0-102.3 the princess climbs on the far side and is seen only between the horse's legs, while the inventor stands in front of the hindquarters and the stool. At 105.5-105.7 he climbs on the far side and only his turban shows.
- **Fix.** Keep him at x 1060 or further left (clear of the rump) until فركب 105.36. Let her step onto the stool on the near side at 101.4-102.3 (stool drawn in front of the horse's legs), then swing up. For his mount, bring the near arm and shoulder over the rump for 0.3 s before he is seated at 106.0.

### 5. 134.0-139.5 s: props at Firuz's feet during the vow

- **Fix.** Fallen stool 60 units further right (about x 1290, the cat 40 units beyond it), or Firuz 40 units left for the vow. Check the still at 138.6: both boots and a ground shadow visible, no leaf on the arm.

### 6. 19.7-34.0 and 126.5-139.5 s: plain upper sky in the closer framings

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- `h3Push` / `h2Push` (zoom about the ground line so the sky is cropped and the ground stays): one series helper, and a line in the Capabilities Guide.
- `h3BranchBack`: a movable foreground prop joins the depth-sorted rows once it no longer hides anything. Add as a guide rule.
- Front cloud layers are drawn before the riders, never over them (guide rule, from note 3 of round 1).
- A launch draught that flips a dropped cloth once and leaves it as a heap: a cheap way to show "it was here" without leaving a sign that says "it is here".
