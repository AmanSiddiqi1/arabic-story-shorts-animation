## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A mouse turned into a girl asks for the strongest husband in the world. Sun, cloud, wind and mountain each point to someone stronger, until the chain ends at a mouse, and she chooses to be a mouse again.

**Shape.** A cumulative tale with a circular ending. The fun is the chain (each giant is humbled by the next, on screen, in a physical gag); the feeling is the father and daughter (he saves her, raises her, and lets her go).

## 3. Character acting

- **Ascetic / father.** Stillness first (S1: only beads and breathing), so his sudden rise for the falling mouse reads. Gentle hands: every touch has a 0.25 s hover before contact. After the card: white beard, `tip 0.05` stoop, stick, slower `walk()` (amp 0.7). Thinking at 51.3 uses `gazePlan` saccades (0.07) before the glint finds him. The farewell (154.28) follows the guide's grief-to-resolve pattern, kept light: long blink, breath, one tear, firm smiling nod; `wet` eyes from 151.30 to the end.
- **Daughter.** One habit carries her identity through three bodies: a double **nose twitch** (mouse 19.8, child 26.0, maiden 37.7 and 143.3, she-mouse 157.5) and the **two round hair buns** that read as ears. As a child she takes two steps to his one and peeks from behind his robe. As a maiden she is confident: `hipsHands`, strong-arm flex by arm angles, `hop` plus `landSquash` for joy (100.32). Her decision is acted before it is spoken: at 143.26 she watches the she-mouse in the thought cloud, brows lift, a slow blink, hand to a bun.
- **Mouse of the mountain.** Polite and house-proud: sweeping, straightening whiskers, a bow. Looks up so far that he tips over (`tip -0.3`, `rebound`). Refuses with palms out and an apologetic brow, never rude. At ME?! ears shoot up, cheeks dot red, tail curls into a heart.
- **Giants.** Each has one attitude that the next one breaks: sun serene then nervous (eyes slide to the edge before the cloud appears); cloud smug (`lid 0.5`) then alarmed; wind a show-off with big `anticipate` wind-ups, then a pancake; mountain slow, heavy lids, tickled rather than hurt. Their faces use the same eye helper as the cast, so `gaze`, `lid` and `blink()` work; they never blink in unison.
- **Listeners always react.** The pair on the hill follow every giant with `lookAt`; clothes and scarf stream in the wind; mother eavesdrops with `cupEar` and drops her cloth.
- **Secondary motion.** Tail and held mouse on `swing()`, beads, scarf and robe streamers, the drooping sun ray, leaves and chips from seeded `K.rng`.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground y 900; faces and key action stay above the caption panel. Sky scenes use a locked wide (zoom 1.0) so the dimming and the shoves read; people are small (s 0.6) under giants 400 units wide.
- Eye-line moves: tilt up his pointing arm to the sun (54.6), follow the mountain's pupils down to the hole (108.0-110.3), tilt up the maiden from the mouse (132.6). These three tilts are the film's main camera ideas.
- S12 is a low mouse-level two-shot (zoom 2.6): the mouse is about 150 px tall, the father kneels into frame, the maiden is feet and hem until the tilt.
- Crane-outs for scale at 104.5 (the great mountain) and 162.3 (ending), both to zoom 0.82.
- Push-ins on decisions: the child reveal (26.0), her stand-up (45.4), the two-shot at 147.5 with `twoShot` margin 70.
- Hidden cut under the YEARS LATER card at 35.9. Panel-only shake for the rumble (106.3), the shoves and the bursts.
- Thought clouds and balloons sit beside or above heads, never over a face; the rank strip hides in close shots.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the rank strip's STRONGEST? tag hopping from icon to icon; the cloud ping-ponged across the sky; the wind's grand wind-up ending in BONK on a mountain that stays asleep; the boulder plug answered by a second hole; a sandal three times the width of the front door; a fingertip knocking over the tiny bed; mother at the window.
- **Suspense:** the hawk's shadow before the hawk; the stars gathering through the 22.0-23.2 pause; two eye glints in the hole held for 1.4 s before the mouse appears; the fifth strip slot showing only "?".
- **Surprise:** both POOFs use the identical burst so the second is recognised instantly; the mouse's entrance; her shadow on the rock.
- **Emotion:** mirrored images tie the ends together: the mouse on his cupped palms (13.4) returns in the memory cloud (150.0); his raised hands and the light cone (16.3) return at 155.3; the child looking at her hands (25.3) is repeated by the she-mouse (157.5). The folded yellow scarf is what he carries home.
