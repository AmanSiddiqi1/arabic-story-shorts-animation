Verdict: REVISE

# KD-014 The Mouse Marries Only a Mouse: Fable review, round 1 (v1 cloud render, 168.0 s)

Reviewed: all 28 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second, 0-168 s), 36 full-size frames pulled into `review/v1_pack/extra/` (`sheet_0..5.jpg`, `cap_95.png`, `strip_79.png`), the plan, the word timings and the capabilities guide.

## Summary

The first 100 seconds work. The fall through the fig leaves, the prayer and light cone, the POOF with the bun-eared child, the YEARS counter, BLOCKED!, the cloud being ping-ponged and the wind's BONK on the sleeping mountain all read at phone size and land on their words. Subtitles are verbatim, large, well timed and readable in both languages; the English is accurate. THE END is present and the film is 168.0 s. No blockers.

The reason for REVISE is the last 40 seconds (126.7-168), which carry the whole point of the story and are the weakest part of the film: the mouse is drawn almost half as tall as the people, so "she is human, my home is very small" has no visual force; the daughter is out of frame while the mouse says it, and the HUMAN label points at the father; her entry into the mouse's home is a 0.5 s tangle followed by a light coming on in a different hole; and the father reacts to the transformation he just prayed for with alarm. Notes 1-4 fix that. Notes 5-8 are smaller clarity problems that recur through the film.

Do not change narration, subtitle text or subtitle timing. All times are seconds on the final MP3.

## Notes

### 1. 134.0-137.2, MAJOR: "she is human" is said with the daughter out of frame, and HUMAN labels the father
- **Wrong:** from 127.7 to 137.2 the daughter is a sliver at the left edge (half a face, one sleeve). At 136.12 (إنسانة) the HUMAN label appears with its arrow ending on the father's beard (see `extra/sheet_3.jpg`, frame 136.5). The only person the viewer can see being called "human" is the father.
- **Why:** this line is the turn of the story. The mouse's objection is about her, and her reaction to it is what sets up her decision at 145.27.
- **Fix:** on لكنها (135.39) the mouse looks up and past the father and raises one paw toward her; cut or ease out to the three-shot framing already used at 133.0-133.7 (daughter, father, mouse all whole) and hold it from 135.4 to 137.2. HUMAN at 136.12 with the arrow tip on her head, label beside her, not over a face. She reacts: glance down at her own hands (repeat of the child's look at 25.3), `brow` raised, small step back. Father's gaze goes from mouse to her. Then continue to MY HOME at 137.20 as now.

### 2. 126.7-161.0, MAJOR: mouse and humans are nearly the same size, so the scale joke and the refusal do not read
- **Wrong:** in the mouse-level shots the mouse is as big as the father's torso (128.5: his hand rests on a mouse head the size of his own). In the three-shots the people are only about 1.6-2.2 times the mouse's height (133.2, 145.5, 150.5). After the POOF the she-mouse comes up to the father's hip (158.0, 159.2). The plan asked for a low mouse-level shot with the father kneeling into frame and the maiden as feet and hem; that was not built. The sandal-beside-the-door gag is also missing.
- **Why:** "my home is very small" and "not a human" are size arguments. If she could nearly fit, there is no problem to solve, and the fingertip-and-bed lens at 138.5 has to do all the work alone.
- **Fix:** hold a human to mouse height ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 in every frame where both are on screen, from 126.7 to the end.
  - Three-shots (133.0-133.7, 137.5-156.2, 157.5-162.9): scale the people up about 1.5x and the mice down to about 0.75x, and move the camera in and down so the ground band is no more than 15 % of the panel (it is 35-40 % now and empty). The mouse stays at 150 px or more on the 1080 frame, so it still acts.
  - Mouse-level shots (126.7-132.7, 134.0-135.4): keep the mouse at its present screen size and draw the father at true relative scale: first his feet and hem arrive at 127.28 (فأرًا) with `footfalls` puffs and a `kick` hop on the mouse, the mouse looks up and tips over at 128.52 (الجبل, `tip -0.3`, `rebound`), then the father kneels into frame at 129.92 (وسأله) so only his head, beard, shoulders and one hand fit. Remove the hand-on-the-mouse's-head pat at 128.0-129.7; it makes the mouse look like a dog.
  - Add the planned sandal beside the door at 137.78 (صغير): her foot steps in next to the hole, about three times the hole's width, one beat before the lens BONK at 138.40 (جدًا).
  - The she-mouse after 157.46 is the same size as the he-mouse (as now), so against the larger father she is properly small.

### 3. 159.2-162.5, MAJOR: her entry into the mouse's home is unreadable, and the light comes on in the wrong hole
- **Wrong:** at 160.2-160.7 the two mice are a pile at the hole (she is standing on him, he is on his back, `extra/sheet_5.jpg` frame 160.5); her wave lasts about 0.25 s; by 161.0 both are gone. Then a new lit yellow dome appears to the right of the dark hole they were next to (161.2, 162.5). Two holes, one dark and one lit, and nobody was seen going into the lit one.
- **Why:** دخلت بيت الفأر is the resolution. The viewer must see her go in, see the father let her go, and see that the lit door is her home now.
- **Fix:** one doorway only for this beat (keep the other hole hidden behind the fallen boulder, or do not draw it).
  - 159.22 (دخلت): the he-mouse steps to one side of the door and bows her in (`bow`, one paw sweeping to the door). She walks to the threshold with `walk()` (4 short steps, no slide), tails apart, no overlap between the two bodies.
  - 160.34 (بيت): on the threshold she turns back, stands, and waves; hold the wave to about 161.1. Father, kneeling, waves back with a small hand (gaze on her, `wet` eyes, smile).
  - 161.1-161.6: she goes in, tail last; he follows; a 2-frame tail flick at the door.
  - 161.7: the light comes on in that same hole (warm fill plus a soft glow on the ground in front), one quiet chime is acceptable only if it sits after 164.68 or in a gap; otherwise silent.
  - 161.9-162.9 (وانتهت رحلتها): father picks up and folds the yellow scarf, one long blink. Crane out on الطويلة (162.98) as now.

### 4. 157.5-159.5, MAJOR: the father reacts with alarm to the change he just agreed to and prayed for
- **Wrong:** as the smoke clears the father has both hands on his head and an open "o" mouth (157.7-158.2, `extra/sheet_4.jpg` frame 158.0), then a neutral face. The planned farewell (long blink, breath, one tear, firm smiling nod, `wet` eyes from 151.30) is not visible anywhere from 151 to 162.
- **Why:** the plan's ending is "not a loss": he lets her go. Shock makes it look like an accident and removes the only tender beat of the finale.
- **Fix:** 154.28 (وافق): slow nod with `settleTo`, `lid` closing to a long blink, then a smile; `wet 0.6` from 151.30 (كنت) onward. 155.34 (الأب): the raised hands and light cone as now (mirror of 16.3). 157.5-158.5: hands come down from prayer to his chest (`chest` gesture), gaze follows the smoke down to the she-mouse, smile, one tear on 158.50 (جديد) as the noses touch. Keep the heart. No `headHold`, no open mouth.

### 5. 73.2-80.3, 119.7-122.5, 163.0-163.5, MAJOR: the rank strip sits on the giants' faces
- **Wrong:** the strip and its STRONGEST? tag lie across the peeking sun's eyes for seven seconds (`extra/strip_79.png`), and exactly across the mountain's eyes while it says لا أقدر على منعه (120.5, 121.5 in `extra/sheet_3.jpg`). The plan says lettering never covers a face and the strip hides in close shots.
- **Why:** the annoyed sun peeking over the cloud and the mountain's tickled, helpless eyes are the jokes of those shots; both are hidden behind UI.
- **Fix:** keep the strip at the top but keep faces out of its band (roughly the top 110 px of the panel). S8: lower the sun-and-cloud group by about 90 world units from 73.2 so the sun's eyes clear the strip. 119.7-122.5: frame the mountain so its eyes sit below the strip (tilt down slightly or zoom out to about 0.9) and bring the father fully into frame (he is cut in half at the left edge); or fade the strip out for this shot and pop it back at 122.5. Check 163.0-163.5 the same way during the crane.

### 6. 67.1-74.2, 76.6-79.9, 88.9-92.2, MAJOR: the giants "speak" on the word "said", then go silent for their own line
- **Wrong:** the sun's mouth opens 67.7-68.7 (over قالت) and is a fixed frown for «السحاب أقوى مني» (69.07-70.98). The cloud talks 76.5-77.2 (فقال) and holds a fixed small "o" for «الريح أقوى مني» (78.01-79.86). The wind talks 88.7-89.5 (فقالت) and is a closed smile for «الجبل أقوى مني» (90.15-92.16).
- **Why:** the film gives the four giants faces so that they can say these lines. With the mouth moving before the quote and still during it, the lines read as narration over a picture.
- **Fix:** drive each `talk` from the quoted words: sun 69.07-70.98 and 71.27-72.9 (stops as the cloud covers it); cloud 78.01-79.86 and 80.23-80.7, then the strain faces as now; wind 90.15-92.16, then 92.68-95.6 between the puffs; mountain 108.57-109.6 before the camera leaves it. During the "said" verb give a breath-in (`anticipate`, brow lift) instead of talk. The expression can stay sad or alarmed; the mouth only needs to open and close on the syllables. Keep the listeners' `lookAt` on the speaker.

### 7. 42.2-50.4, MAJOR: the groom thought cloud is cut off by the top of the panel, and her gesture crosses her father's face
- **Wrong:** the cloud is cropped so only the silhouette's legs show (44.0), then a cropped wide blob under THE STRONGEST! (50.2); it never reads as a man, strong or otherwise. At 48.0-48.5 her raised hand sits on her father's hat and cheek, and at 49.98 (الجميع) her spread arm goes straight across his face (`extra/sheet_1.jpg` frames 48.9 and 50.2). The flex on أقوى (48.86) is not readable.
- **Why:** this is the wish that launches the quest; the whole chain is a search for the figure in that cloud.
- **Fix:** bring the cloud fully inside the panel (centre about 150 world units lower, or scale to 0.8) and put it over the empty sky left of the roof, tail to the speaker. 42.20 (الرجل): whole plain groom silhouette with a "?" on it. 48.86 (أقوى): the silhouette swells to a broad-shouldered strongman; she does a clear two-arm flex with `aL/aR` angles, standing half a body width farther from her father (she already stands at 45.4; step her right). 49.98 (الجميع): arms wide, both hands clear of his head; he rocks back with the "?" as now. Take the cloud off by 50.7.

### 8. 163.0-168.0, MAJOR: the final tableau is crowded at the top, and the mountain shuts its eyes
- **Wrong:** THE END covers the cloud's body and half its face and touches the snow cap; the rank strip, cloud, card and sun are all packed into the top fifth while the bottom third of the panel is empty ground (`extra/sheet_5.jpg` frame 166.5). The mountain's lids close to blank grey slabs at 164.5 and stay that way for the whole hold. The wind is about 40 px wide and easy to miss.
- **Why:** the plan's last picture is the whole sky watching over a tiny lit door. One watcher is hidden, one is too small to see, and the biggest one has stopped looking.
- **Fix:** put THE END in the empty ground band under the mountain (or across the lower slope, clear of the father and the door). Cloud left, sun right, wind at 2x size between cloud and mountain, all with `gaze` aimed at the lit door and staggered blinks. Mountain: half lids (`lid` about 0.45), pupils down toward the door, the smile from 164.12 held; no full close. Give the lit door a soft pulse (2 s cycle) so the eye goes to it. The STRONGEST? tag can leave at 164.7 so only the five icons and the heart remain.

### 9. 77.5-100.0, MINOR: the wind is the smallest thing in its own scenes
- **Wrong:** in S8 the wind is a pale half-hidden ball at the left edge, the same value as the dimmed sky (79.0); in S9 its face is smaller than one tree crown (91.0).
- **Why:** it has to beat the cloud and then lose to the mountain; it should feel like a force until the BONK.
- **Fix:** scale `windSpirit` to about 1.8x with longer streamers; in S8 bring it fully into frame by 78.01 (الريح) with the label, and give it a visible cheek-puff `anticipate` before each shove at 80.70 and 82.66. In S9 route it above the pair rather than across their faces (86.7-87.2). Keep the BONK pose as it is; it is the best gag in the film.

### 10. 122.5-126.5, MINOR: "she looked at her father in surprise", but both stare at the camera

### 11. 143.2-144.1, MINOR: the crossed-out icon in the mouse's thought cloud is unreadable

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- `rankStrip` with a hopping STRONGEST? tag: a good device for any cumulative tale. Add a rule to the guide: the strip reserves the top band, and the scene keeps faces out of it (or the strip auto-hides when a face rectangle intersects it).
- `poofTransform`, `elementFace` and the `windSpirit` flat/limp states are worth promoting once the talk timing is fixed.
- Guide entry to add: "Quoted speech by a non-human speaker: breath-in on the 'said' verb, `talk` only across the quoted words."
- Guide entry to add: "Scale stories: fix the size ratio first (here at least 4.5 to 1) and choose framings that keep the small character at 150 px or more, instead of enlarging the small character."
