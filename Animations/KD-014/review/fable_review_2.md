## Notes (polish only, none triggers a v3)

### 1. 137.6-139.6, MINOR: the lens in the sandal insert is small, dark, and sits on two faces
- **Wrong:** in the mouse-level insert the lens with the tiny bed and fingertip is about 280 px across on the 1080 frame and is placed over the father's cheek and hand and the daughter's chin (`extra/sheet_b.jpg` 138.6, `sheet_c.jpg` 139.3). Its interior is near black, so the bed and the fingertip are hard to read at phone size. In v1 the lens stood in clear space and was larger (`extra/v1_compare.jpg`, 138.6). The upper right of the frame, above the mouse, is empty.
- **Why:** جدًا (138.40) is the punchline of "my home is very small"; the sandal now sets it up well, and the lens should pay it off.
- **Fix:** move the lens to the empty upper right (centre near panel x 760, y 330 on the 1080 frame), scale it about 1.5x, and lighten its interior to the mid grey used in the DIG DIG DIG lens at 116.5. Put the dashed leader from the lens to the door, as at 114.2-118.5. Move BONK! to sit under the lens. Keep both faces clear.

### 2. 159.7-161.5, MINOR: the he-mouse host is half cut by the right edge of the panel
- **Wrong:** after the bow he stands on the far side of the door at the frame edge, so only his head and shoulders show while she waves and enters (`sheet_e.jpg` 160.4, `sheet_f.jpg` 160.9 and 161.2).
- **Why:** he is the groom welcoming her in; half a mouse at the edge reads as a framing mistake.
- **Fix:** either pan the camera about 40 world units right from 159.5 to 161.8 (the father stays whole), or stand him at about x 676 instead of 700. Keep the gap between the two mice.

### 3. 122.7-126.5, 144.5-146.7, 155.5-156.2, MINOR: a black zigzag at the top edge

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- Scale stories: fix the ratio first (here 4.5 to 1), then alternate a small-character-level shot (the big character enters as feet and hem, then kneels in) with a three-shot at zoom 3 or more where the ground band is under 15 %. Worth a guide entry with these timings as the example.
- Door entry pattern: host steps aside and bows, guest walks to the threshold, turn and wave held about 0.8 s, in head first, tail flick, light in the same door. Reusable for any burrow, nest or house ending.
- Rule for face rigs on sets (`elementFace` on the mountain): hide the features when the camera is zoomed past the face, so parts of it do not poke into close shots.
- `rankStrip`: the "hide when a face is in the top band" behaviour used here should become the default.
