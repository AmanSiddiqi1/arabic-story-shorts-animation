Style `cel-anime-80s`, episode 4 of 5 of the Ebony Horse series. Narration 141.52 s, last word ends 140.95 s, end hold 4.0 s, film 145.52 s. 17 scenes, 210 actions, 19 lettering items, 8 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. Every action is in `creative_plan.json`; this file gives the direction. Follow the owner direction in `Library/styles/cel-anime-80s/look.md` (reference film ALI-01-ANIME v4), the series bible in `Animations/HORSE-01/planning/creative_plan.md` section 9, and the drawings planned in HORSE-02 (moonPath, palaceRoof, guard settings) and HORSE-03 (twoRiders, tailTorn, turbanScrap).

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **The lie in the garden (0-20.3, P1):** continuing from episode 3, the inventor flies on over snow mountains and comes down in the broad garden of a white palace with tangerine domes. Neither rider knows the place. He bows: a gift for the king, and a princess who chose to come. She shakes her head once.
- **The truth, said calmly (20.3-39.1, P2):** she walks up to the king and says it in two short sentences. The king, a lover of marvels, cannot keep his eyes (or his finger) off the horse; then he stops his guards in mid step: first the whole truth.
- **Thinking one move ahead (39.1-57.5, P3):** she catches the inventor edging toward the knob, and has the horse wheeled into a closed courtyard. CLANK. Inside she shows the guards the hidden DOWN lever and puts her hands behind her back: not while he is near. His turban is at the gate grille.
- **Meanwhile (57.5-75.6, P4):** Firuz on a bay horse, caravan to caravan. A brass rosette from the saddle cloth in the sand. An old man at a well: it flew that way.
- **The disguise (75.6-94.8, P5):** crown into the toolbox, behind a palm trunk a prince, out the other side a craftsman, with a corner of crimson sash that will not stay hidden. He talks his way past two guards with a knuckle on wood and a quarter turn of a brass knocker.
- **Under guard (94.8-112.8, P6):** he sees the horse and for one beat forgets his part. Both controls are sound. He asks for the open square, tomorrow morning. Evening comes; lanterns light.
- **The password (112.8-128.5, P7):** the peak. Above the court a lattice bay glows. He speaks to the moon: THE MOON KNOWS THE RIVER'S PATH, and the moon's glints on the water rill line up into one gold path. One close-up: her eyes in two star openings. A memory card of her own roof. She knows.
- **Signal and morning (128.5-145.5, P8):** two taps on the knob, two flicks of a rose ribbon. NEXT MORNING... the whole square gathers; his hand stops a finger's width from the knob; the inventor sees crimson and saffron at the craftsman's hip. TO BE CONTINUED...
- **Conflict:** a liar with a marvel against a girl with only the truth, before a judge who must decide whom to believe. **Arc:** unease, composure, comic relief, caution, pursuit, nerve, hush, joy held in, suspense. **Moral:** calm truth and patience undo a liar; a kept promise finds its way.

## 3. Character acting
- **Princess:** the still centre. Upright, slow breath (`breathe` on `sq`), `lid` 0.15-0.25, few gestures and each one complete: a level pointing arm that arrives in three drawings and holds, a hand flat on the chest, hands behind the back for "I will not". Her eyes work ahead of everyone (`gazePlan`): the lever in S1, the inventor's tiptoe in S5. Emotion leaks only twice: `wet` for 0.6 s on "my family" (27.98) and the tear at 126.72. Behind the lattice she acts with eyes, fingers and the rose ribbon only.
- **Inventor:** two faces as in episode 3. Mask on for the king (`lid` 0.15, smile, bows on `flow`); mask off for two drawings when contradicted (18.18), and whenever he thinks nobody looks (`lid` 0.45, `brow` 0.4). The torn turban tail is always a drawing late (`lagged`) and he dabs his forehead with it. From S6 he is a pair of eyes and a turban at the gate grille: arriving late, ducking, squinting, being shuttered. Last frame: `eyes: 'wide'`, hand to the torn tail.
- **Far king:** curious child in a big body. Eyes lead the head by three drawings; belly-first walk with `footfalls`; knuckle raps; `hop` back with `landSquash`; then real authority in one slow raised palm. Weighs with scale hands (`flow` on `aL`/`aR`). In S17 chin on fist, leaning further forward through the hold.
- **Firuz as prince (S7-S10):** forward lean in the saddle, `lurch` on stops, cape `swing`; kneel on `flow`; jaw set, `brow` 0.4, no frown.
- **Firuz as craftsman:** a prince acting badly on purpose. Rounded shoulders (`tip` 0.07), squint (`lid` 0.3), short steps, the box a drawing behind the arm. The prince slips out three times: the graceful bow he has to spoil (84.32), the soft face at the horse (97.32), the straight look up at the screen (125.68). Running gag: tugging the cloak over the crimson corner, which springs back (`rebound`).
- **Guards:** four individuals (tall, short, moustache, sleepy), never in step (`stagger`, own seeds): one-foot freeze with wobble, feet down in a ripple, helmets knocking, heads following hammer taps with lag, dozing and yawning in S14.
- **Caravan people:** the driver thinks before pointing; the merchant points before being asked; the old man tells it with whole arms while two children fly in circles.
- **Animals:** the bay horse is everything the ebony horse is not (ears, snort, nudge); camels chew; peacocks fan, tilt and stroll; doves cross.
- **The ebony horse:** lifeless. It acts through light bands, glints, `reveal` of the lever, one soft `powerOn` step in the insert, and by being wheeled about on a cart.
- Moving holds on twos everywhere (`idleSway` on `tip`, head and hands, veil, cape and pennant ribbons, lantern flames, the rill glints). A blink on every head turn.

## 4. Camera and composition
- **Wide where the place matters:** S2 opens at zoom 0.62 on the whole far palace; S10 the same from outside in day; S17 pulls out to 0.66, the widest and last view. S7 and S9 are desert wides.
- **Pans across detailed sets:** S2 pans the garden (pool, trees, peacocks, guards arriving); S4 pans with the king; S5 tracks the cart across the square; S7 tracks 650 units of road; S12 pans from gate to horse. Ground between panel y 930 and 955.
- **One held frame for S14 to S16** (zoom 0.9, centre (1040, 470)): horse and craftsman lower left, moon in the middle, lattice bay upper right, the gold path on the rill joining them. The viewer sees speaker and listener at once.
- **Close shots only at peaks:** the rosette in his hand (65.88), the hand on knob and lever (100.78), and the one face close-up of the film, her eyes in the lattice (120.44-124.5). The lever in S6 is shown by a round callout inset so the wide shot stays.
- **Flight framing** (S1) as in the bible: horse in the upper third, level, then nose down 12 degrees with clouds scrolling up.
- Balloons and tags sit in the sky or wall band with tails to heads, never over a face; the caption strip is the bottom third of the film.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** watering can on the gardener's foot; king and peacock tilting heads together; the finger near the knob that makes two enemies jump as one; guards frozen on one foot; knocking helmets; the merchant who points before the question; the toolbox that shows the crown; the bow that is too good; the shutter on the inventor's face.
- **Suspense:** his hand creeping to the knob; the eyes at the grille; the craftsman who goes straight to the hidden lever while the inventor watches; guard D turning during the signal; the hovering hand and the inventor's stare in the last frame.
- **Surprise:** she, not the king, proposes locking the horse away; the swap behind the palm trunk; the moon glints lining up.
- **Emotion:** "my family" with shining eyes and a raised chin; Firuz nearly greeting the horse; the eyes in the lattice; the hand on the heart from her roof in episode 2.
- **Series threads:** DOWN lever (episodes 1 to 3) stays her secret; saffron scrap and torn tail from episode 3; password and moon path from episode 2; the square, king and guards are ready for episode 5.
