## The hand-reach fix

Checked at 31.4-32.7, 51.9, 87.8-88.6, 99.6, 100.4, 111.9, 128.65-128.95 and 140.3-143.5. **No broken, stretched or popping arms anywhere.** The reaches that go out sideways look natural: the craftsman's rise to the knob at the end (the arm travels over about 0.7 s and settles), the two taps at 128.6-128.95, the captain's arm to the shutter at 111.9.

One pattern is left, from the length clamp the change log describes: when the target is beyond arm's length and above the shoulder, the hand ends beside the character's own head, so the pose reads as touching his cheek or ear instead of touching the object. Seen at 87.8-88.6 (craftsman, hand at his temple, gate behind him), 99.6-100.4 (hammer and hand at his ear), 51.9 (princess, hand at her cheek). It is not wrong enough to hold the film; the fix is in note 4.

## Notes (most important first; all polish)

### 1. 28.5-32.5 s (S4): the king's curiosity stays offstage
- **What is wrong.** On 29.94-31.46 (الملك إلى الحصان بدهشة) the king stays at the right edge behind the princess; at 31.6 his arm goes out sideways from there and the two riders throw their hands up. The peacock fan covers him from the waist down the whole time.
- **Why it matters.** The king's wonder at the horse is what makes him keep it. It is the one story beat still told only by the narration.
- **Fix (smallest version).** 28.56 (نظر): king's `gaze` to the horse, then head. 29.94 (الملك): he takes three steps with `walk` + `footfalls` past the princess (she shifts half a pace right) to stand at the horse's chest, at least 60 units to the side of the knob. 30.78 (الحصان): two knuckle raps with a ring each. 31.46 (بدهشة): hop back with `landSquash`. From 20.3 the peacock stands on the lawn at the lower left with its fan folded. No camera change needed.

### 2. 138.6-145.5 s (S17): the two watchers are half covered
- **What is wrong.** In the last frame the king's face is split by a guard's spear and his body is behind a guard; the inventor's lower face is crossed by the same guard's sleeve and two spears. The inventor's wide eyes do read, and the callout carries the meaning.
- **Why it matters.** The last picture is the inventor's recognition; his whole face should be clear.
- **Fix.** Move guard C 60 units left and guard D 40 units right of the inventor, spears held on their outer sides, so his head, both hands and the turban tail are clear. Raise the king's bench 25 px or shift him 50 units left so no spear crosses his face.

### 3. 55.1-56.4 and 111.5-112.3 s: the inventor at the grille (round 1 note 8)
- **What is wrong.** The shutter closes on nobody. "Not while he is near" (52.9) is spoken with him out of sight.
- **Fix.** As in round 1: two eyes, turban front and fingertips at the bars, at least 70 px wide, for a full second before 55.68 (قريبًا) and for 0.4 s before the shutter at 111.52 (التالي).

### 4. 87.6-89 and 99.3-100.7 s: hands end at his own head
- **What is wrong.** See "The hand-reach fix" above. The rap on the wood and the knocker turn look like a hand at the temple; the gate is behind him and the balloon's wedge covers it.
- **Fix.** Bring the body to the object, not the hand: at 87.5 he takes one step so the gate leaf is 50 to 70 units in front of his shoulder at chest height, `dir` toward it, and the hand target is at chest height (never above the shoulder line unless the object is 60 units or more to the side). Two raps with ring marks at 87.76, a quarter turn of the knocker with a glint at 88.44. Same rule at 99.3-100.7: he stands at the horse's shoulder and works at the knob from the side, as he does at 143.

### 5. Balloon tails (87.8, 104.8, 118.6 s) and two overlaps (26.5-28.4, 72-73.7 s)
- **What is wrong.** The tails are still dark wedges about 90 px wide that cover the gate, the horse's neck and the horse's head. At 27 the picture balloon's tail still crosses I WISH TO GO HOME.
- **Fix.** Narrow every tail to 20 to 30 px, or use the thin white tail already used for the plan picture at 131.0. Move the home picture left of the text balloon; show the route picture 71.0-72.3 and the text after it.

### 6. Court and desert floors (46.6-57, 57.5-75.6, 94.8-132 s)
- **What is wrong.** The lower third of the court is plain paving (99.5, 114.0, 127.0) and the desert band is plain salmon.
- **Fix.** As in round 1 note 11: two potted orange trees and a bench in the court's foreground corners, lantern light pools at dusk; rocks, dry bushes and tracks on a nearer desert layer. Nothing above knee height where figures stand.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **`h4Bay` listening window:** silhouette, one whole opening with the eyes, dimmed empty cells, name tag once. Use for any figure behind a screen or grille (it is the fix for note 3 too).
- **`h4EyesClose`:** eye close-up through a lattice with a dark interior, lid acting, pupil shrink, shine and held tear.
- **Signal pair by callouts:** two round insets in sequence (action, then answer) with a dotted lead to the source, so a held wide shot can carry a small exchange.
- **Seen-object callout:** dotted eye line, then a round inset of the object beside the watcher's head with an arrow (141.2). A clear way to show recognition without a cut.
- **`h4W` world-to-local hand targets:** move into the Library so `handRW`/`handLW` work when a default hand is set, with the rule "bring the body within arm's length; no target above the shoulder unless 60 units to the side". HORSE-05 needs the same fix.
