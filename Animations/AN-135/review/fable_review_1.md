Verdict: REVISE

# AN-135 King Kisra Anushirwan and the Village Damsel: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/AN-135_v1.mp4` (174.0 s), all 29 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/`, plus 19 full-size frames in `review/v1_pack/extra/` (montages `m1.jpg` to `m4.jpg`).

## Summary
The story is told in order, is easy to follow, and the best ideas of the plan are on screen and work: the cane field as a meter (lush, yellowing 96.5-98, bowed at dusk, springing back 151.7-153), the picture balloons (thirsty face / upended bowl / bellyache; the crown wringing the little house at 134.2), the freeze and sweat drop at 133.7-135.7, the torn scroll, the crown going on, the small crown for the girl. Subtitles are verbatim, readable and correctly timed in both languages; the English reads accurately against the Arabic. No broken frames, THE END holds 3.3 s. No blockers.

It is REVISE and not PASS WITH NOTES for three substantive reasons: the story's key evidence (the evening bowl holding less) is not visible on the real prop; the closing moral tableau is badly composed (villagers cut by the panel edge, no pull-out); and there is a visible layering glitch in the first eight seconds. The rest is staging polish that should be done in the same pass.

Never change narration, subtitle text or subtitle timing.

## Notes

1. **108.0-125.5 s, MAJOR. The evening bowl looks as full as the morning bowl.** At 109.2 the bowl she hands over shows a pale green surface right up to the rim; in the morning two-shot (37-76) the bowl is seen side-on with no level at all. The whole story turns on "three stalks gave less than one", and at the moment only the balloon says so. Fix: draw `smallBowl` with its `cut` cross-section in both hand-overs. Morning (37.1-47): `fill` 1.0, brim visible, one drop spilling on the hand-over at 39.0. Evening (107.94 "فسألها" onward): `fill` about 0.4, a clear dark inner wall above the level. On "عصيرًا" 114.46 have the king tilt the bowl toward camera and look down into it (gaze down, brows up), then look at her. On "الأول" 124.42 he looks from the bowl to the bowed cane, as planned.

2. **163.6-174.0 s, MAJOR. The moral tableau is cramped and cut off.** The villagers enter at 164.2 and stop half outside the left panel edge, on top of the horse; the planned pull-out to the full yard never happens, so the final image is the same two-shot with a pile of clipped figures at the edge. The ghost TAX sack (167.0-170.2) is pale green over green trees and cane and is hard to read; its pop at 170.2 is barely seen. The king's bow to her at "ملك" 165.86 does not read, and the horse's stolen bite under THE END is hidden behind the villagers. Fix: from 163.0 ease the camera out to the full yard (zoom about 1.0, centred so that x 60..940 is in frame) by 164.9 "قرار". Stand three villagers fully inside the frame, left of the hitching post (x about 90, 150, 215), in front of the horse's hindquarters, not over its head. Draw the sack in the open sky above their heads (white fill at 0.85 alpha, black dashed outline, black TAX), sagging toward them on "يستطيع" 166.98 and bursting into stars on "أهلها" 169.60 with the villagers raising their arms. Give the king a real bow at 165.86 (`tip` 0.12, hand on chest, 0.6 s, crown stays on) and the girl a small nod back. Keep the horse's head clear so the cane bite at about 171.5 is visible.

3. **6.2-7.8 s, MAJOR (glitch). The gazelle is drawn on top of the king and horse.** From 6.5 to 7.5 the gazelle overlaps the rider and at 7.2-7.5 it sits in his lap (see `extra/f_7.2.png`). It also looks as though he has caught it, which contradicts "strayed while chasing". Fix: keep the gazelle at least 250 units ahead of the horse for the whole chase, drawn before the horse (behind it) if they ever share x, and let it gain ground from "وهو" 6.10 and leap into the bush or off the right edge by "غزالًا" 7.50. The king then reins in (`lurch`) and looks left and right for his men, who are gone, before the thirst beat at 8.72.

4. **101.5-141.2 s and 150-156 s, MAJOR. The horse is a headless rump for forty seconds.** In the evening two-shot the frame cuts the horse off in front of the saddle, so only hindquarters, tail and the saddlebag hang at the left edge. The planned horse acting is all lost: the yawn during the wait (about 106), and above all the accusing stare at "بسوء" 137.98, which is the laugh that releases the climax. Fix: in the evening, park the horse so its head is inside the two-shot (for example facing right with the head at about x 330, saddlebag still visible behind the king's shoulder), or shift the two-shot centre about 90 units left. Then stage the yawn at 106.0 and, at 137.98, a slow head turn toward the king with half lids, held to 139.0; the king's pupils flick to the horse and away.

5. **25.5-37.0 s, MAJOR. The pressing scene is too far away and the girl hides behind her own bowl.** The plan asked for a close shot on hands and bowl; the render is a wide of an empty wall with the action in the middle quarter of the panel, and the girl is visible only from the nose up. This is the surprise that sets up everything (one stalk, a full bowl) and the choice to add the powder. Fix: frame at zoom about 1.7 centred on the bowl rim so bowl, hands and her whole face fill the panel width; stand her behind and slightly to the right of the bowl so her chest and arms are visible. Wring with a visible twist on "وعصرت" 25.74 (two twists, `sq` on each), let the level climb in three steps to the brim by 29.0 with her brows rising on the last step (surprise even for her is fine, pleased smile). Before the powder at 33.72, hold the planned glance at the door (32.7-33.2) with a clear head turn, not only pupils. Keep the `1 STALK` counter but move it clear of the window.

6. **72.0-76.0 s, MAJOR. The punchline "only one" is muddled.** The king's question balloon shows four canes, not the planned three, and stays up while she answers; ONLY ONE! is small yellow lettering laid over green cane and over the corner of his balloon; the limp stalk she holds up is the same green as the field behind it and disappears; a second "1" badge sits over the roof. Fix: close the king's balloon by 72.0 "قالت". On "عودًا" 74.12 she lifts the spent stalk high in the open sky gap between the cane tops and the roof (arm fully raised, stalk drawn pale yellow and drooping so it reads as squeezed), the king's and horse's eyes follow it up. Put ONLY ONE! as a white boxed label with black text in the sky beside it and drop the extra badge. On "فقط" 75.38 keep his jolt (it works). Use three canes in the question balloon so it rhymes with the evening's three.

7. **41.0-42.0 s, MINOR. The crown insert shows no crown.** The cut-in on the saddlebag (41.2-41.8) shows a closed brown bag and a tiny star; a viewer cannot tell there is a crown inside, so "not knowing he was the king" has no picture and the crown at 156.7 comes from nowhere. Fix: in the insert let the bag flap lift for 0.5 s on "الملك" 41.46 so three gold crown points with a red jewel show, glint, flap drops; the horse looks at camera as planned. Also show the points peeking for a beat at 2.2 in the opening ride.

8. **44.2-46.2 s, MINOR. SIP... lettering sits on the girl's face.** Both SIP words are drawn over her eyes and forehead while she is the one watching him drink. Fix: place them in the sky to the upper left of the king's turban (above the horse's ears), stepping up and left, same timing ("على مهل" 44.26). Give her a small satisfied nod at 45.2 "وأعجبه".

9. **104.0-107.8 s, MINOR. The wait does not read.** The window silhouette is a small black T-shaped figure with round ears that looks like a toy bear, not a girl wringing cane; the three waiting dots and the foot tap are not visible. Fix: enlarge the window as the plan warned (about 110 x 100) or push the camera in toward door and window from 104.9 "تأخرت"; animate the silhouette's arms twisting a stalk, three times, each a new stalk passed up from below (this pre-tells "three"). Add the three dots over the king's head one by one (105.2, 105.9, 106.6), a visible toe tap with `footfalls`, and the horse yawn from note 4.

10. **141.7-148.5 s, MINOR. The remembering beat is long and nearly still, and the red line points at the wrong thing.** The greyed cloud returning is good. The dashed red line (144.5-145.2) runs from the cloud to the king's turban and is gone in 0.7 s; the plan was coin tower to the bowed cane, which is the actual realisation. From 145.5 to 148.5 almost nothing moves. Fix: on "عزم" 144.26 draw the dashed line from the coin tower down to the nearest bowed cane top, hold it to 146.5, and droop that stalk a little further when the line lands. Then the shame pattern: eyes up to the girl (145.2), away, head down with `tip` and lid 0.6 by 146.2; she strokes the leaf at 146.68 looking at the cane, not at him. Push in slowly through the beat so it does not sit as a wide.

11. **13.3-15.2 s and 94.9-97.2 s, MINOR. Riding through the field and the vanishing girl.** On arrival the horse and rider are drawn behind the cane stalks (14.0-15.0), as if riding through the crop, then pop in front at 15.2. On departure the girl in the doorway disappears in one frame at 96.2 while he is still passing. Fix: bring him in along the front of the field (drawn after the cane) to the hitching post. On leaving, she watches him go for a beat with a small wave or a puzzled head tilt, steps back and the door swings shut over 0.4 s by 96.6, before the stalks start to yellow.

12. **Throughout the yard scenes, MINOR. Dead ground band.** In every yard shot the bottom fifth of the picture panel is empty brown ground and the cast's feet sit at about 78 % of the panel height, while balloons are pressed against the top edge (the three-frame balloon at 112.0 is clipped on the left as it opens; the crown balloon at 133-136 touches the top border). Fix: lower the two-shot camera centre by about 60 units so there is less bare ground and more sky for balloons, and open the wide balloon from its tail outward so it never crosses the panel edge. Do this only if it does not disturb the fixes above.

## Subtitles and faithfulness
Arabic and English are present for every chunk, appear with the speech, and stay inside the caption panel (the longest, 160.0-170.5, takes three Arabic and three English lines and still fits). The re-translated English is accurate and natural; no change requested. The content is faithful and family friendly; the wedding is light (crown and one heart), which suits the text "as the tale tells". The dissolve at 159.2 from orange dusk to a blue daytime sky reads as an epilogue on a later day and is acceptable.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- **Prop as meter:** one keyframed `LUSH(t)` driving a whole field with per-stalk stagger and overshoot; worth documenting in the guide as the pattern for "the land reacts".
- **Progressive strip balloon:** frames revealed one by one on the spoken words, with a red X and a highlighted final frame (112-125).
- **Returning thought cloud, desaturated,** in the same screen position to signal memory (141.7).
- Guide rule to add: any quantity the story compares (full vs half bowl) must be visible on the real prop in both scenes, not only in a balloon; and tableau extras must stand fully inside the panel.
