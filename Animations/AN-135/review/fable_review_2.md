## Reusable techniques for the Library
- **Tilt-to-camera look-in:** a character tilts a cross-section prop toward the viewer and looks down into it, then at the other character; the cheapest way to make a quantity the subject of a shot.
- **Witness animal:** a parked animal whose head stays inside the two-shot and delivers one slow stare on the accusing word; note in the guide that the head must be inside the frame for every camera move of the scene (check push-ins).
- Guide rules to add: a bow needs body `tip` of at least 0.25 to read below zoom 1.3; UI marks such as waiting dots must be placed on sky or wall with a light fill, never over foliage; when two scenes share a prop across a cut (the bush at 8.72), keep its draw order the same on both sides.
