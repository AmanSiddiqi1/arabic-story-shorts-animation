Everything else below is small, and v3 should be one short, targeted pass: fix 1 is required, fixes 2 to 5 are cheap and ride along.

## Fixes for v3 (one pass, most important first)

### 1. 85.5 to 90.2 and 100.2 to 144.1, MAJOR (new in v2): the sheaves hide the faces of the girls who are speaking
What is wrong: in the huddle (87.0, 88.9, 89.9) girl1's sheaf and girl2's sheaf are raised in front of girl1's head. At 88.9 her nose, mouth and one eye are behind yellow arrowheads while she delivers "only Ma'an bin Zaidah would do this". In the wide that follows (103.0) all three have arrowheads over mouth and chin, and it continues through the recitals: girl1 at 119.5, girl2 at 127.0 and girl3 at 143.2 recite with the mouth covered. At 147.5 and 161.5 the same sheaves sit at chest height and the faces are clear, so the correct height already exists in the film.
Why it matters: the recognition is the second surprise of the story and the recitals are a fifth of the running time; in both the speaker is the subject of the shot and has no visible mouth.
Fix:
- Rule for every frame from 76.7 to the end: the highest arrowhead tip of a held sheaf stays at least 10 px (screen) below the holder's chin, and no sheaf is drawn in front of another girl's head. Use the 147.5 hold height as the default everywhere.
- Huddle 85.44 to 90.2: girls lean heads together as now, but sheaves drop to waist level (grip about 35 to 45 world units lower than at 87.0), girl2's sheaf carried on her right side, away from girl1. Girl1 faces clear, `talk` on from 88.57 ("لا يفعل") to 92.06, the existing point at Ma'an on 89.84 kept.
- 100.2 to 107.9 (agreement) and each recital (girl1 108.9 to 121.3, girl2 122.7 to 135.3, girl3 135.9 to 144.1): the reciter holds her sheaf in one hand at hip height (the free hand keeps the existing gesture), mouth fully visible with `talk`; the two listeners keep theirs at chest height below the chin.
- Deliberate lifts stay as they are: girl2's single arrow at 81.6 to 84.0 and the raised arrow at 95.2 to 100.2.
Check with stills at 87.0, 88.9, 103.0, 119.5, 127.0, 143.2.

### 2. 65.7 to 69.9, 80.2, 121.7 to 124.2, 135.2 to 137.0, 144.5, 158.5, MINOR: the sun pops and greys
What is wrong: (a) between the verses the sun fades back in for 1.5 to 2.5 s and out again (121.7 to 124.2, 135.2 to 137.0), a blink in the corner three times in 36 s; (b) each fade passes through a flat grey disc (80.2, 110.0, 121.7, 135.2, 137.0, 154.0, 158.5) that reads as an eclipse; (c) the sun is drawn on the paper background of the quiver insert and sits behind the arm at 69.3.
Fix: keep the sun hidden continuously from 110.0 to 144.5. Wherever it must hide or return, scale it (1 to 0 over 0.25 s with its rays) or cut on the shot change, rather than fading alpha. Do not draw it at all in the insert 65.7 to 69.9.

### 3. 55.5 to 57.3, MINOR: the purse still reads as a clay pot
What is wrong: at 56.2 it is a rigid red-brown bulb about the size of man1's head with a small yellow tie.
Fix: draw it at about half that size as a limp pouch: tan leather (not red), sagging sides that flap when shaken, long yellow drawstrings dangling. Enlarge the moth to about 2x and give it a visible flutter path of 0.6 s after the second shake.

### 4. 164.5 to 169.9, MINOR: ending polish
What is wrong: the men stop walking at about 164.5 and stand; man2 is sliced by the left frame edge from 168.5; THE END's lower-left corner sits on the sun's rays; the sun is mid-sky.
Fix: keep all three men walking (walk cycle, slow) up the dune to 169.9 while staying fully inside the frame (stop their x at least 40 px inside the left edge, continue the scale-down to about 0.45 instead of sliding out). Lower the sun so its disc sits just above the far dune line on the left third, clear of THE END by 40 px, or move THE END to panel centre-right above the girls.

### 5. 124.5 to 128.2, MINOR: verse 2 opens on a lone still figure

## Reusable techniques for the Library

- Held-prop face clearance: any prop held at the chest (sheaf, bouquet, bundle) needs a clamp so its top stays below the holder's chin and never overlaps a neighbour's head, with an explicit `lift` state for deliberate raises. Worth building into a shared `arrowBundle` / held-bundle helper.
- Tail-first draw for a reveal insert (object leaves its container with the plain end first, the valuable end clears the rim on the cue word): the 65.7 to 69.9 insert is a good template.
- Panel-space inset with the sun hidden for the full run of consecutive insets, not per inset.
- "Come to the giver" handover: giver fixed, receivers step to one mark in turn and retire upstage; reads much better than a giver walking down a line.
