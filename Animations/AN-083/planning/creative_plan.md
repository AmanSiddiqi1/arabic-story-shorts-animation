# AN-083 Ma'an the Son of Zaidah and the Three Girls: creative plan

Film length 169.9 s (MP3 165.41 s + THE END hold 4.5 s). 16 scenes, 130 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time comes from `inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (same folder); this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A thirsty prince out hunting is given water by three girls who never ask who he is. With no coins to thank them he gives away his thirty gold-tipped arrows, and the gift itself tells them his name.

**Conflict.** Small and human: first thirst (no water), then a debt of thanks he cannot pay (no money). Each lack is solved by generosity: theirs, then his.

**Surprises.** (1) The gold inside an ordinary quiver. (2) The reversal of who is unmasked: the girls did not ask his name, and his own gift gives him away.

**Emotional arc.** Heat and comic misery, relief, embarrassment, delight, recognition, lyrical praise, a quiet tender beat (the shroud), warm farewell.

**Ending.** A wide late-afternoon desert: girls on the right dune with golden bundles, the men small and leaving on the left. THE END at 165.2, held to 169.9.

**Moral.** Kindness given without asking who it is for is answered by generosity; a person is known by what he gives.

**One staging idea drives the film:** Ma'an is dressed plainly (blue bedouin robe, no crown). The audience sees a traveller, exactly as the girls do, so "not from his clothing or attendants" at 155.5-157.5 is something the picture has shown all along.

## 3. Character acting

All six are the Library `person` with costume presets; `LIFE.t = t` for blinks, a seed each.

- **Ma'an** (`bedouin`, blue robe, yellow sash, white headcloth, age 0.3, s 1.0). Courteous and unshowy. Walks with `tip` 0.04 forward when fresh, 0 then -0.03 when spent. Signature gesture: right hand on chest (`PERSON_HANDS.chest`) with a head bow (`tip` 0.08, `lid` 0.6 for 6 frames), used at 30.9, 45.1, 119.6 and 146.2 so it becomes his. Thinking (60.7): `chin` gesture with saccades (`gazePlan` 0.07); the idea arrives eyes first, then brows, then smile. Caught at 90.56: the only look to camera in the film, with a `hush` finger. In the verses he is a listener: `lid` 0.3, small nods, gaze moving between reciter and inset, embarrassed by praise (gaze down, hand to neck).
- **man1** (sand `bedouin`, age 0.45). Loyal, slightly smug: folded arms and a knowing nod when the gold appears. Offers his empty skin to the prince with lowered eyes at 17.66.
- **man2** (brown robe, red headcloth, age 0.15). The clown: reacts biggest and 3 to 5 frames late (`stagger`). Fans himself, stumbles (22.0), splashes his face, shrugs with his bow, slaps his forehead when the quiver is emptied, trips on the bow at 163.6.
- **girl1** (red dress, yellow scarf, s 0.9). Acts before she speaks: she is unslinging the skin while girl2 is still forming a question. She is the one who works out the name (85.4-90.5): breath in (`sq` 1.03), point snaps out and settles (`settleTo`).
- **girl2** (blue, white scarf, s 0.86). Lively: `talk` 0.8, `armsOut`, `mouthCover` laugh, finger wag. Nearly drops her arrows and catches them on her chest.
- **girl3** (green, red scarf, s 0.8). Shy: half hidden behind girl2, `lid` 0.4, peeps over her bundle, on tiptoe in the huddle. Her verse begins with eyes down and a small voice and steadies as she looks up at her picture.

Rules for groups: the men never share a walk phase, the girls never share one (offset 0.35 of a cycle); every shared reaction is staggered; every pose change has a dip before (`anticipate`) and an overshoot after (`settleTo`). Secondary motion: waterskins slosh and sway 2 frames after each step, the quiver bounces 3 px on footfalls, headcloth tails flick in the wind at 6.2, bundles dip the hands that receive them.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 2000 x 1230; the picture panel is 972 x 1196, faces kept at y 680-730 world, nothing important below y 930.
- S1 establishing track (zoom 1.0 to 1.45) so the sun and the emptiness read first; S2 pushes in on the search to 2.1 on Ma'an.
- S3 pans right so the girls enter from the unseen side of the frame; men look up-right, girls down-left.
- S4 `twoShot(Ma'an, girl1, {margin: 150})`, pushing in on the handover, which crosses the exact centre.
- S7 is the only close-up and the only insert: face, tilt to the quiver, cut to the arrowheads against sky.
- S10 uses the film's one whip: from the huddle to Ma'an on his name.
- S12-S14 share one framing ({x 1400-1430, y 600, zoom 1.15}): group in the lower half, verse inset (centre world 1380,330, radius 230) in the upper half. Rim colour changes per reciter (yellow, red, blue).
- S15 pulls out to hold the three sky icons; S16 is a long pull-out to zoom 1.0 with `skyCycle` warming.
- Counters and icon rows are drawn in panel space so camera moves cannot crop them. Lettering never covers a face.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the single drop that misses the open mouth (11.6); the skin shaken by the ear; the purse turned upside down and inspected with one eye; "we came to hunt" shrug with the bow; the prince proudly showing his emptied quiver; the huntsmen mock offended at being crossed out (157.5).
- **Suspense:** 60.7-69.2 is played slowly: eyes stop on the strap, the flap opens, the arrow rises shaft first with the head hidden by the rim, then 6 frames of stillness before the glint.
- **Surprise:** GOLD! at 69.22, and the whip to the caught prince at 90.56.
- **Kindness without questions (the heart of P2):** girl2's "?" balloon starts and pops unfinished while girl1 is already giving.
- **Emotion:** the third verse is staged in stillness, with no smiles and no lettering beyond the small THIRD tag; then the film breathes out with Ma'an's wet eyes at 144-146.
- **Setup and payoff:** the plain robe (setup from frame one, payoff 156.2); the hand-on-chest bow; the waterskin handover replayed as a small memory inset at 148.6.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

**Warnings**
- The three girls share one figure: keep robe colour, height and temperament distinct in every shot.
- S12-S14 are 36 s of described verse; the insets must change every 1-2 s.
- The person figure is front-facing only; check that the walk-away in S16 reads as leaving.
- `desertRoad`, `inset circle`, `goldGlow`, `purse`, `coinPile` are planned elsewhere but not yet built.
- Word starts can lead the audible onset by up to 0.4 s; check 69.22 (gold) and 90.56 (the name) by ear.
- Lettering count is 14, above the usual 8-12; the FIRST / SECOND / THIRD tags are the first to cut.

**Owner questions (8, defaults in the JSON `owner_questions`)**: party on foot with no horses; plain costume for Ma'an; two huntsmen; verses as picture insets with no written verse; softened battle, wound and shroud imagery; added comic business; added English lettering; placement of the LATER... card.
