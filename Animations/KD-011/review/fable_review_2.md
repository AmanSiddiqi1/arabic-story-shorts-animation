## Notes (optional polish, most useful first; none triggers a v3)

### 1. 76.2-76.9 s. MINOR. The boy's failed wink reads as a yawn, then as surprise
What is wrong: at 76.3 the boy has both eyes shut with a small round open mouth, and at 76.5-76.9 his eyes are open with a round "O" mouth (`extra/C_wink.jpg`). The scene therefore ends on a face that looks startled, just before the merchant leaves him.
Why it matters: this is the last picture of the boy before the father's fear; it should be the happiest one.
Fix: for 76.2-76.6 use both lids shut with a wide closed-curve grin (no open round mouth) and a small `tip` of the head; from 76.6 to the cut at 77.0 open the eyes and keep the wide smile.

### 2. 139.0-140.4 s. MINOR. The boy's face is covered in the first embrace, under the SAFE! arrow
What is wrong: when the boy reaches his standing father, the father's forearm crosses the boy's head for about 1.4 s, exactly while the SAFE! label points at him (`extra/F_run.jpg`).
Fix: stop the boy about 40 world units further left, or keep the father's near arm raised above the boy's head until he kneels at 140.5, so the boy's face is visible under the label.

### 3. 27.4-28.0 s. MINOR. The padlock glance is too small to notice
What is wrong: the body holds still as asked, but the pupils barely leave centre (`extra/G_misc.jpg`, 27.6), so the suspense beat passes unseen at phone size.
Fix: push `gaze` fully down-left for the 0.6 s, with a slight `lid` narrowing, and keep the small sparkle on the padlock that is already there.

### 4. 143.5-143.9 s. MINOR. The boy slips behind the standing father
What is wrong: during the swap of places the boy's head is half hidden behind the father's shoulder for about 0.4 s (`extra/B_hug2.jpg`, 143.75).
Fix: let the boy walk round in front of the father, or start his move 0.2 s earlier so that he is clear by 143.7.

### 5. 30.3-31.2 s. MINOR, unchanged from v1. SOLD! is partly behind the friend's head
What is wrong: the stamp reads "OLD!" at 31.0 because the friend is drawn over it (`extra/I_sold.jpg`; identical in v1, `extra/J_sold_v1.jpg`, so this is not a regression).
Fix: draw the stamp last (above both men) or shift it about 60 px left onto the stack.
