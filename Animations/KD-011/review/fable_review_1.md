Verdict: REVISE

# KD-011 The Rats and the Iron: Fable review, round 1 (film v1)

Reviewed: `renders/KD-011_v1.mp4` (161.0 s) through all 27 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` plus 16 full-size montages pulled from the MP4 into `review/v1_pack/extra/` (A-P, sub). Compared with `planning/creative_plan.md`, the word timings and the Capabilities Guide.

## Summary
The story is told in order, is faithful and family-friendly, and the sync to the narration is good throughout (labels, counters, SOLD!, CRUNCH!, IMPOSSIBLE!, the equals sign, HE KNOWS!, SAFE!, PAID IN FULL! all land on their words). The mirror structure works: both picture clouds, then side by side with the equals sign, is clear and funny. The friend's acting (sweat, hand to cheek, flush at 121.5) and the close-up silence at 117-122 are the best things in the film. Subtitles are correct, readable and well timed; the English matches the Arabic chunk for chunk and I found no translation error.

It needs a v2 because of one visible staging defect on the central prop (the iron sits in front of the locked doors for 11 s), a muddled emotional payoff (the hug), a closing gag that happens off the edge of the frame, and several smaller staging faults listed below. No BLOCKER: nothing is a wrong story event and no subtitle is unreadable.

Do not change narration, subtitle text or subtitle timing for any of these.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 17.0-28.5 s. MAJOR. The iron cart is drawn in front of the closed, padlocked storeroom doors
What is wrong: after CLUNK! at 17.0 the doors are shut and barred, but the cart and the iron stack are still drawn on top of them (see `extra/B.jpg` at 17.5 and 20.0, `extra/C.jpg` at 25.0 and 27.5). It stays that way through the promise, the parting and the whole "needed money" beat, until the doors open at 28.7. The cart also hides the padlock.
Why it matters: the script says the iron is kept in the friend's house. On screen it looks as if it was left in the street, and the suspense beat at 27.38 (only the friend's pupils creep to the padlock) cannot read because the padlock is covered.
Fix: draw the cart and stack before the door leaves (behind them) whenever the doors are closed, so from 17.0 to 28.5 we see only shut doors, bar and padlock. Keep the reveal at 28.7-29.5 as it is (doors open, iron inside, cobweb). If a reminder of the contents is wanted during 24.2-28.5, use a small `thoughtCloud` over the friend with the stack inside at 27.38 (المال), not an overlay on the doors. At 27.38 hold the body still and move only `gaze` down-left to the padlock for about 0.6 s before the coin and "?" icon.

### 2. 141.3-143.2 s. MAJOR. The hug turns into a two-headed shape
What is wrong: the run-in is good (father kneels with arms open at 140.5-141.2, `extra/P.jpg` middle). Then he lifts the boy and the two heads are stacked almost on the same spot: at 141.8 the father's face is half hidden behind the boy's head, at 142.5 the boy's head covers the father's cheek and eye (`extra/J.jpg` right, `extra/K.jpg` left). Same costume colours make it one yellow blob.
Why it matters: this is the emotional payoff of the film (وفرح أبوه عندما رآه, 140.7-143.3). Neither face reads at the moment of joy.
Fix: keep the father kneeling (`kneel: 1`) for the whole hug as the plan intended and do not lift. Put the boy beside him, heads cheek to cheek with their centres at least 0.7 of a head width apart (boy's head on the father's left shoulder side, both faces fully visible), father's arms wrapped with `handLW/handRW` on the boy's back, boy's arms around his neck. Both smiling, father `wet: 0.5`, eyes closing on رآه (142.70). One squeeze with `sq` at 141.6, heart as now. Then the bread offer at 142.70 from this pose. Stand the father up during 143.3-143.8 for the payment.

### 3. 157.2-159.0 s. MAJOR. The closing gag is unreadable and clipped
What is wrong: the rat trots in nicely during the last words, but the bite itself is missing. At 157.5 the rat sits by the nail, at 157.7 it is already facing away to the right, at 158.0 TINK! appears cut by the right panel edge with only the rat's tail still in frame, and OW! pops at the edge at 158.2 with no rat on screen (`extra/N.jpg`, `extra/L.jpg`). The shot is the widest of the film, so rat and nail are tiny. THE END arrives at 158.7.
Why it matters: this is the planned last laugh (a real rat cannot eat iron). As rendered a viewer sees a rat leave and two cut-off words.
Fix: the 3.5 s hold has room. Stage it inside the frame, at about panel x 700-850, with the nail there too.
- 157.3: rat arrives at the nail, sniffs (head down, whiskers).
- 157.6: bites the nail, `sq` squash on the head; star and TINK! directly above it, fully inside x 54..1026, held to 158.2.
- 158.2: rat recoils with `hop`, a paw to its mouth; OW! above it, held to 158.8.
- 158.8-159.4: rat scurries off right.
- 159.3: THE END (still at least 1.7 s of hold).
During 157.2-159.0 push in a little and shift the camera centre right so the three friends sit left of centre and the rat is at least 90 px long on screen; the friend's `gaze` follows the rat and the men exchange one look. The effects sit after 157.24, which the plan allows.

### 4. 67.0-69.8 s. MAJOR. "He took him" has no walk, and a ball floats in the doorway
What is wrong: from 67.0 the merchant and the boy stand facing the camera, overlapping, with the merchant's hand on the boy's shoulder, and do not move while the camera drifts. At 69.5 they fade out where they stand. At 69.8 the street is empty, the door is open and the ball hangs in mid air in the dark doorway (`extra/M.jpg`).
Why it matters: أخذه إلى مكان آمن (69.34) is the counter-move of the story and must look plainly kind. A fade reads as a disappearance, the opposite of the reassurance the plan asks for.
Fix: 66.6-67.4 the merchant returns the ball and offers his hand; the boy takes it (boy smiling). 67.8-69.6 they walk right, hand in hand and side by side with no overlap (boy on the door side), using `walk` and `footfalls`, the boy taking two steps per adult step and swinging the joined hands, ball under his other arm. They go through the open door at about 69.6 and the cut to the interior follows at 69.9. Remove the fade and the floating ball.

### 5. 73.6-75.5 s. MAJOR. The boy looks unhappy in the safe place
What is wrong: after YUM! the boy has pink lowered lids and a down-turned mouth for about 2 s (`extra/F.jpg` left, 74.2), exactly while the narrator says the merchant asked him to wait. He smiles again at 75.8. The planned shared wink at 75.8 is not visible (both eyes open on both figures).
Why it matters: the plan's own warning: the boy must be seen safe, fed and content before the father worries. A sad child alone with the merchant sends the wrong signal for a children's film.
Fix: keep a smile through 72.5-77.0. Chewing: alternate cheek puffs with eyes open or happy closed arcs, never a frown. On ينتظر (about 74.8) the merchant raises a flat "wait here" palm, the boy nods (`tip`) twice. At 75.8 the merchant's `wink` held 0.4 s, then at 76.2 the boy's failed wink (both lids shut, big grin) held 0.4 s.

### 6. 0.7-3.3 s. MAJOR. A look-alike appears before the merchant, then the crowd hides him
What is wrong: the first person on screen (0.7-2.6) is a bystander at the storeroom door in a blue robe and white turban, the merchant's colours (`extra/A.jpg`). The real merchant enters at 1.5 with THE MERCHANT pointing at him while the double is still beside him. At 2.5-3.3 a woman in red and a porter pass in front of the merchant and cover him and the cart (`extra/M.jpg` left).
Why it matters: the opening must establish one hero. Two near-identical men confuse the label, and the hero is hidden in his own introduction.
Fix: give the bystander a different costume and palette (for example the `villager` preset in green or brown, no turban) or bring him in only after 3.5. Draw the passers-by behind the merchant and the cart (z-order) or route them along the far side of the street, so the merchant is never covered between 1.5 and 4.4.

### 7. 42.7-44.1 s. MINOR. The speaker is cut by the frame during his own line
What is wrong: the push-in on the friend spotting the rat slices the merchant at the left edge (half a head at 43.2, `extra/D.jpg` left) while the subtitle and voice give his line سأله التاجر: «ماذا حدث؟» (43.01-45.82). The camera only returns at 44.2.
Fix: do the push-in on the friend and the rat inside the pause 41.6-42.9, and be back in the doorstep two-shot by 43.0 with the merchant leaning in, brows up, one open palm. Alternatively limit the zoom so that both whole heads stay in frame.

### 8. 54.7-56.5 s and 107.2-109.6 s. MINOR. Push-ins on the merchant slice the friend's face at the right edge
What is wrong: in both merchant singles the friend's head is cut vertically through the face by the panel border.
Fix: choose one of two clean framings: a true single (camera centre moved left until the friend is fully out), or a looser frame with the friend's whole head inside. For 54.72 the single is better (it is the merchant's look to camera at 55.44). For 107-109.6 keep the friend whole so that his dread is visible while the merchant answers calmly.

### 9. 53.2-54.4 s. MINOR. The fed rats collapse into one tangle
What is wrong: after CRUNCH! all five rats converge on one spot and read as a knot of bodies and tails (`extra/D.jpg` right, 53.6). The BURP! at 54.2 has no visible owner. The planned bibs are absent, which is acceptable, but the rats then need to read some other way.
Why it matters: this picture is the lie itself and it returns in S14 and S16.
Fix: when the last bar is gone, spread the rats along the cloud floor at five distinct x positions (`stagger(i)`), sitting up with round bellies, tails curled; one pats its belly, the straggler licks a paw. Put BURP! over one specific rat with a small `sq` on it at 54.2. In the reduced clouds at 110-117 and 124.7-127 use the same spread, mid-meal, rather than a clump on the stack.

### 10. 133.0-136.5 s. MINOR. The "iron OR coins" graphic is weak and gets cropped
What is wrong: the grey stack sits on the dark blue sky with little contrast, the items are small and unframed, and the push-in for the handshake at 135.7 cuts the graphic at the top edge (`extra/J.jpg` left and middle).
Fix: put stack, OR and coins inside one style `box` on paper colour, larger (stack and coin pile about 150 px wide each), popping on الحديد (about 133.0), أو and ثمنه, with the coin counter filling on كاملًا. Fade the whole box out by 135.6, before the push-in.

### 11. 148.2-148.4 s. MINOR. PAID IN FULL! survives the cut into the evening scene
What is wrong: the stamp is still on screen for the first frames of the sunset shot (`extra/P.jpg` left).
Fix: end the stamp at the cut (fade complete by 148.2) or start the cut a few frames later.

### 12. 14.6-16.4 s and 60.6-61.3 s. MINOR. The two men overlap on the doorstep
What is wrong: while the cart rolls in (15.0-16.2) the friend stands in front of the merchant and covers half his body (`extra/B.jpg` left). At 60.7-61.2 the merchant steps across to pat the friend's shoulder and again overlaps him.
Fix: keep at least 150 px between body centres. At 14.6-16.4 put the merchant left of the doorway and the friend right of it, both looking at the cart going in. At 60.6 let the merchant reach the shoulder with `handRW` from his own mark (x 300, one small step at most) and turn away with the private smile at 60.4 as planned.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- "Mirror clouds": two `thoughtCloud`s with the same box, a label under each and a popped equals sign between them, for stories where one claim is answered by another.
- A z-order rule for the guide: anything stored behind doors is drawn before the door leaves; passers-by in a busy street are drawn behind the labelled hero.
- A hug rule for the guide: the adult kneels, heads are offset by at least 0.7 of a head width, and both faces stay visible; do not lift a child across the adult's face.
- Edge rule for end gags: lettering and its actor must both be inside panel x 54..1026 for the full hold.
