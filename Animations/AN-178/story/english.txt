A man near the Kaaba heard a woman praying gratefully at night. Beside her slept a child, and she began telling him an extraordinary sea story.

She had sailed for pilgrimage while pregnant. A storm wrecked the ship, and she gave birth on a floating plank amid the waves.

A surviving sailor climbed aboard the plank and tried to coerce her, threatening to throw her into the sea when she refused.

The man seized her baby and threw him into the water. She prayed for rescue, and the tale says a great sea creature carried the assailant away.

She remained alone, grieving for her baby. After a day and a night, she saw a ship and was finally taken aboard.

To her amazement, the baby was on board. The sailors said a huge creature had stopped their ship with the child resting on its back.

She embraced her son and vowed to remain near the sacred house in thanksgiving. That was how the man encountered them beside the Kaaba.

The listener offered her money, which she refused, affirming her trust in God. The story presents their rescue as a miracle, not a verifiable event.
