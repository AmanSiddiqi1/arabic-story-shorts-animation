Ali Baba lived with his family in a small town and worked as a poor woodcutter. | Each morning he took his donkey to the forest | and gathered wood to sell at market.
One day he heard horses approaching quickly. | Afraid that the riders were thieves, he climbed a tall tree, | hid among its leaves, and watched the path below.
Forty men carrying heavy sacks reached a large rock. | Their leader called, “Open Sesame!” | The rock split open, revealing a secret door.
The men entered the cave carrying their sacks, and the door closed behind them. | Much later they came out and mounted their horses. | The leader called, “Close Sesame!” and the rock returned to normal.
Ali Baba waited until the horses disappeared, then carefully descended the tree. | He approached the rock and repeated the words he had heard: “Open Sesame!” | The door opened before his eyes.
Inside, he found gold, silver, and costly cloth. | Though amazed by the treasure, he took only a small sack of coins, | closed the door, and hurried home.
Ali Baba told his wife what he had seen and asked her not to tell anyone. | But his brother Cassim noticed the new coins | and kept asking questions until he discovered the cave’s secret.
Cassim could think only of gold. | He learned the rock’s location and the magic words from Ali Baba, then set off with his cart and empty sacks. | Would he remember how to get out?
