# ALI-01-ANIME "Open Sesame" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 3 (v4, owner-directed restage)

Film reviewed: `Animations/ALI-01-ANIME/renders/ALI-01-ANIME_v4.mp4` (142.93 s). Sources: all 25 contact strips in `review/v4_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second), 60 full-resolution panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited below, and a frame-difference scan of the whole film to find frozen stretches. Times are seconds on the film. The owner's six points are the yardstick; reviews 1 and 2 are overridden where they conflict.

## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

No BLOCKER. The story is told in order, every narrated event is on screen, subtitles are sharp and verbatim in both languages, the ending and THE END are present, no broken frames.

v4 does what the owner asked for on framing, density, colour, balloons and limbs. The one owner point that is only partly met is movement (point 6): the acting on the beats is good, but between beats several shots stop dead. Notes 1 to 4 are worth one short pass before the owner sees the film if a render is available; each is confined to one shot or one helper. Notes 5 to 11 are polish. A stills check of the changed places is enough afterwards; a fourth full review is not needed.

Not checked: sound was not listened to; stepped cel timing (twos and threes) cannot be judged from stills; landing squash at 76.2 falls between sampled frames.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 137.9-142.9 s: the last picture shows a small grey boulder, not the rock
- **What is wrong.** In the closing wide the rock is a dark blue-grey lump about the height of the mule (140.0), a different shape and colour from the tall tan dome seen eight times before. The impact rays from 138.2 stay on screen, static, for the whole hold to 142.9 and cross the cart and the mule. The bottom fifth of the panel is near-black grass. It is the darkest frame of a film the owner asked to be lighter.
- **Why it matters.** "Would he remember how to get out?" only lands if the viewer recognises the place Cassim has reached. A boulder the size of his mule cannot hold a cave, so the "?" hangs over nothing. This is also the frame held longest.
- **Fix.**
  - Use the same dome silhouette as the 38.2 wide (same outline and strata lines), in the pre-dawn palette: dusty violet body, a warm peach rim on the top and the side facing the dawn. Height at least 55% of the panel, base on the road line; the right edge may crop it.
  - Stop the mule about one mule-length short of the rock so there is air between nose and stone.
  - On "يخرج" 137.93: Cassim's head tips back to look up the rock face (gaze up, one blink on the tilt), whip hand drops three drawings later, the mule's ears go back. Hold that pose with a small sway.
  - Rays: let the impact frame play (138.2-138.6), then fade every ray out by 139.2 when THE END arrives. Nothing static may lie over the cart during the hold.
  - Lift the foreground grass band from near-black to a mid indigo and keep the pale flowers; let a thin band of dawn orange sit on the horizon behind the cart. The "?" and THE END stay as they are.

### 2. Holds where everything stops (owner point 6)
- **What is wrong.** The frame scan and paired stills show the cast identical for 0.8 to 2 s at a time while only the camera drifts:
  - 54.7-56.2 and 56.7-58.6: the riderless horses are one frozen drawing for almost 4 s across the MUCH LATER transition (stills 57.2 and 58.3 are the same).
  - 73.0-74.1: tree, leaves and Ali Baba's eyes do not move at all for a full second.
  - 106.5-108.5: the wife holds hands-on-head and Ali Baba holds his point without a change (stills 107.0 and 107.75 match); again 109.6-110.4 and 120.4-121.4.
  - 127.0-128.0: Cassim's pointing arm and Ali Baba's crossed arms are locked.
  - 41.0-42.5: the leader stands with both arms straight down like a toy soldier before the call.
- **Why it matters.** These are the quiet seconds between the big beats, so a frozen picture is noticed. It is the first item of the "alive" checklist (nothing frozen while on screen) and the owner named believable movement as a requirement.
- **Fix** (all on twos, each channel with its own seed, no new cast):
  - Horses 54.7-58.6: a tail swish every 1.5 to 2 s, one ear flick each at different times, the black horse lowers and lifts its head once (about 57.6 on "وقت"), a hoof shifts. After the transition start them in a different pose from before it, so time visibly passed (one grazing, one turned).
  - 73.0-74.1: canopy blobs breathe a few pixels on two periods; eyes dart left (73.2), right (73.6), a blink, then settle before "ثم" 74.12.
  - Hut 106.5-110.4: add `idleSway` to `tip` and `nod` and `breathe` to `sq` on both. Wife: hands rise to her head with the fingers arriving late, then come down to her mouth by "رأى" 108.37, and she leans toward the coins. Ali Baba: the pointing arm bobs on each stressed word (107.11, 107.83), and his head turns to her with a blink at 108.95 "وطلب".
  - 127.0-128.0: Cassim's point jabs twice (126.99, 127.6) with his turban feather following late; Ali Baba shifts his weight back away from him.
  - 41.0-42.5: put the leader's weight on one leg, near hand on the reins, far hand on his hip, and a slow head turn across the rock with a blink before the breath in.

### 3. 130.8-131.6 s: the whispered words overlap and cannot be read
- **What is wrong.** OPEN and SESAME fly from Ali Baba to Cassim as chrome letters and cross each other, so the screen reads "O SESAME" and then "OF SESAME" (stills 131.0 and 131.2). The only speech in the film that is not a balloon is also the only one that cannot be read.
- **Why it matters.** This is the moment the secret changes hands, which sets up the ending. The owner asked for speech as English balloons.
- **Fix.** From "والكلمات" 130.77 show a small balloon with its tail on Ali Baba's mouth, placed in the clear wall between the two heads: `open...` at 130.77, `open sesame...` at 131.1, in the same wobbling small style as the timid call at 83.2, held to 131.75. Ali Baba keeps his hand cupped at his mouth. Cassim's ear side tips toward him and his grin widens three drawings after the second word. If the flying letters are kept as well, release SESAME only after OPEN has landed so they never share space.

### 4. 102.7-105.5 s: the sack rides on Ali Baba's head
- **What is wrong.** On the ride home the pale sack with its coin sits on top of his turban like a hat (stills 103.0 and 103.5). One shot earlier (102.0) he holds it against his belly.
- **Why it matters.** The small sack is the moral of the story and this is the last time it is seen before the hut. On his head it reads as a joke or a drawing mistake.
- **Fix.** Hug it to his chest with the near arm (hand over the knot), far hand on the reins, body leaning forward. On "مسرعًا" 103.64 the donkey's ears go back, his turban tail streams, and he glances back over his shoulder once (104.2) with a blink.

### 5. 79.7-85.5 s: six seconds against a blank mauve wall
- **What is wrong.** Ali Baba's approach and timid call play in front of the rock filling the whole panel: one flat mauve field, a strip of ground, nothing else (stills 80.5, 83.5). The rock here is also darker and more violet than the tan-pink rock in the wides on either side (78.6, 86.6).
- **Why it matters.** It is the hero's biggest acting beat and the one shot that goes against owner points 1 to 3 together: tight, empty and single-hued.
- **Fix.**
  - Open at about 60% of the present zoom with Ali Baba on the left third: rock edge, dusk sky with a cloud, the bush line and the hiding tree visible in the left 30%, the donkey's ears showing over the thicket and watching him.
  - Push in to the present framing only from "افتح" 82.84 for the call, and hold it until the impact frame at 85.0.
  - Dress the foot of the rock: stones, two tufts, a flower, a moss patch, and the sparrow from 68.7 hopping on a ledge; it flies off on the impact frame.
  - Match the rock colour to the 86.6 wide (warm tan-pink with violet only in the shade side).
  - The memory inset at 81.7-82.4 is good; keep it, clear of his head.

### 6. Whole film: nobody casts a shadow (polish)
No character, horse, donkey or cart has a contact shadow, so figures sit on the ground plates like stickers, most visibly in the mediums (16.5-22.4, 40.5-45.2, 60.0-62.4, 79.7-85.5) and on the hut floor. Add a flat `groundShadow(..., { flat: true })` in the plate's shade colour under every standing figure and under each horse, shrinking on the hop at 25.7, the climb, the mount at 60.2-61.0 and the landing at 76.2 (check that landing has a squash and a one-drawing settle).

### 7. 96.4-97.2 s: the sleeve blocks in the bust (carry-over, polish)
Two flat green rectangles rise in front of Ali Baba's chest with hands hanging below the frame edge (still 96.7). They read as pillars. Drop them: let the shoulders rise and the chin lift, then cut to the hands insert at 97.2, which carries the reach.

### 8. 35.6-36.3 and 104.2-105.3 s: foreground trunk on the subject (carry-over, polish)
The dark trunk covers the middle riders while the counter runs 26 to 33, and it covers Ali Baba in the last second before the cut to the hut. Shift the first into the cut to the sack insert (36.3-36.5) and phase the second so it has passed by 104.6.

### 9. Mirrored and cloned poses (polish)
- 76.5-77.7 (after the landing) and 87.0-89.2 (door open, walking in): both arms held out at the same angle. Offset them: one hand to his chest, the other half raised, arriving two drawings apart.
- 34.5-38.0, 60.7-62.2, 70.5-72.7: every rider has the same crossed-forearms pose. Give three variants by seed: both hands on the reins, one hand on the sack behind, one arm raised urging the horse.

### 10. Colour continuity and the dark close-up (polish)
- The forest is blue-green at 7.7-16.2 but lilac with peach light at 34.5-38.0, the same day, between two midday-blue glade shots. Use the morning forest palette for 34.5-38.0. Keep the dusk version for 102.7-105.3 but add green to the plants and a warm orange path so it is not one violet wash.
- 48.2-50.0: the eye close-up sits in near-black with dull leaves. Make the surround deep leaf green with two or three sunlit blobs; the gold reflection in the irises will still carry.

### 11. Small things (polish)
- 30.7-30.9: the donkey's closed eye is still a grey rectangle. Draw a curved lid in donkey grey with a lash line.
- 56.7-58.7: the two pale dots drifting over the dusk rock still read as dirt. Make them a moth with wings, or remove them.
- 9.5-10.3: Ali Baba's axe head lies across the donkey's face while he leads it. Put him a head's width ahead of the muzzle, lead rope visible between them.
- 70.5-72.0: the dust hides every horse's legs. Keep it below the knees so the gallop reads.
- 113.2-116.9: the wall around Cassim's window is bare. Add the shelf edge, a hanging garlic string or a crack with a lizard.
- 105.5-113.0: the lower third of the hut wide is empty floor under a heavy vignette. Lower the group to about 70% of the panel height or put sandals, the broom and a basket in the foreground, and ease the vignette.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **`abSay` speech balloon with a build** (first word and ellipsis, then the full line on the stressed word, tail on the speaker's head): generalise as `say(ctx, S, t, { to, steps: [[t0, text], ...], timid })` for any style.
- **Coat and costume tables on an existing rig** (six horse coats, five robes chosen by seed without touching Library defaults): a `variant(seed, table)` helper plus a documented palette-override argument on `horse` and `rider`.
- **Scatter dressing baked into a plate** (`abDress`, `abStone`, `abTuft`, `abFlower`, `abFlock`, `abSun`): move to `Library/sets` as `dress(plate, seed, { stones, tufts, flowers, keepClear })` with a keep-clear rectangle for the acting area.
- **Memory inset** (81.7-82.4: a small framed replay of what the character saw, with tape tracking lines) and the **picture balloon** (108.5-109.4: a balloon holding a drawing instead of words): both tell "he remembers" and "he tells" without text.
- **Words as objects left behind on the road** (135.9-137.7): a `dropBehind(t, cue, { arc, squash, dim })` helper for anything falling off a moving vehicle and staying in the world.
- **Idle horses**: a `standIdle(t, seed)` bundle (tail swish, ear flick, head dip, hoof shift) would have prevented note 2's frozen horses and is needed by every film with waiting animals.
