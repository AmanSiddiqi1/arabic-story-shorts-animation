# AN-142 The Devout Woman and the Two Wicked Elders: creative plan

Film length 173.18 s (MP3 170.18 s + 3.0 s end hold). 18 scenes, 127 actions, two worlds. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard with every action is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

- **Logline.** Two old garden keepers accuse a devout woman with a story they made up; a twelve-year-old boy saves her by asking each of them, alone, one simple question.
- **Conflict.** One honest voice against two agreed liars and a crowd that does not check.
- **Arc.** Calm (P1) > menace behind friendly masks (P2) > the lie set loose (P3) > cold public shame (P4) > a small figure steps forward (P5) > the same question twice (P6, P7) > the lie collapses (P7) > legend and moral (P8).
- **Spine beats.** B1 2.52 the woman; B2 14.44 ablution at the fountain; B3 25.72 the keepers stare; B4 30.36 she refuses; B5 36.34 the threat of false testimony; B6 51.42 they shout; B7 60.76 the invented young man; B8 68.64 nobody checks; B9 73.74 three days; B10 81.92 she raises her hands; B11 90.10 Daniel; B12 101.76 one at a time; B13 115.80 EAST, pear; B14 132.26 WEST, apple; B15 137.40 the contradiction (climax); B16 153.54 fire from the sky; B17 162.96 the simple question.
- **Planted early, paid off late.** The apple tree (west, left, red) and the pear tree (east, right, yellow) are in the first frame and stay in every garden shot, so the two answers at 115.80 and 132.26 are checked by the audience against a picture it already knows. The low sun on the right fixes east before any lettering.
- **Surprises.** A yellow cap hopping behind the grown-ups (85.12); the boy's stop palm (95.02); the second cloud lighting the opposite half (132.26); the dotted young man popping like a bubble (142.14).
- **Ending.** The garden of the opening, the woman walking to prayer again, Daniel waving from the gate, THE END at 170.4 held to 173.18.
- **Moral.** Do not believe an accusation until the witnesses have been tested.

## 3. Character acting

- **The woman** (`costume: 'lady'`): calm default `lid 0.2..0.4`, small smile, `breathe()`. Her strength is stillness: at 32.36 only one slow blink while the camera pushes in; she never trembles and never runs. Fear is brows only (`brow -0.5`), `wet` for a moment at 38.76 and again at 83.22 (one tear) and 148.20 (relief). Prayer arms at 81.92 use `aL/aR` angles about [2.45, 2.85], not hand targets. Nobody ever touches her; keep 60 units of air.
- **Elder1** (tall, green, EAST/pear, `age 0.95`, `hold: 'stick'`, key ring): the leader. Friendly mask (smile with `lid 0.45`), then V brows at 33.26. Makes up his answer with saccades up and aside (`gazePlan` 0.07) at 110.58, then puffs up (`sq 1.04`). One deliberate look into the lens with a gulp (drop pulse) at 103.66.
- **Elder2** (short, red-brown, WEST/apple, `s 0.88`): the copycat, always a beat late (`stagger`). Wakes with one eye (`wink`) at 23.90, peeks at the crowd during his mock piety at 57.66, wrings his hands (`PERSON_HANDS.clasp`) at 123.24.
- **Daniel** (`costume: 'servant', child: true`, yellow tunic, blue cap): anticipation crouch and `hop` with `landSquash` at 87.98; `sq 0.88` squeezing through at 88.64; 2-frame eye saccades while thinking at 93.36; innocent face toward the elder, a tiny smile only while the elder looks away (119.16); eyebrows shoot up for four frames at 132.26; shy head rub (`headHold`, `tip 0.04`) at 161.42.
- **Crowd and headman**: five fixed individuals, never in unison (`stagger(i)` 3-5 frames), accordion stops with `lurch`, eyes move before heads. The young ochre villager doubts at 68.64, frowns at the two cards at 134.48 and nods to himself at 146.60. The headman's shame at 144.98: look up at her, eyes break away, head drops.
- **Secondary motion**: key ring `swing`, beards and veil tail lagging 2 frames, bobbing fruit, fountain jet, the dove as a barometer (leaves at 29.02, returns at 158.28 and 170.4).
- Set `LIFE.t = t` so everyone blinks; `noBlink` during the shock hold 138.48-139.5.

## 4. Camera and composition

- **East is always screen right, west screen left**, in the garden, in both clouds and on both cards.
- Garden world 1600 wide: establishing at zoom 0.78 (both trees and the gate), push to 1.25 on the gate, ease right to show the dome. Two-shot of the elders on the bench (zoom 1.6) pulling out to include her. Push-in to 1.8 on her refusal. The wide at 52.30 leaves her small beside the fountain.
- Street world (marketStreet strip, w 2400): tracking shots for the criers and the procession; the square is the right end with the platform at x 2060..2260 (top y 850).
- S12 and S14 are staged identically (Daniel x 1890, elder x 2010, cloud 560 x 400 at (1990, 380), tilt up and out to zoom 1.15) so only the lit half, the colour and the tree differ.
- Panel-only shakes at 52.30, 137.40 and 153.54; a storybook frame encloses the legend 150.10-158.3; S17 dims the surroundings to 45 percent.
- Faces and hands stay above the caption panel; balloons, clouds and cards sit above y 580 and never cover a face; title and THE END in the sky band (y 120..330).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Suspense**: the audience knows she is innocent; the crowd's eyes sliding away from hers (62.92) and the hushed villager (69.12) show how close the truth was. Three sky cycles in 1.5 s make the three days feel long without showing a parade.
- **Surprise and the mechanism**: the proof is visual. Two clouds with the same little garden, opposite halves lit, different fruit: the contradiction needs no words when the cards meet at 136.62.
- **Humour (light)**: elder2 dozing and waking one eye at a time; the boy waist-high to the headman; elder1's gulp to the lens; the smug stroll off behind the cloth stall just before his friend undoes him; the card flipping face down so elder2 cannot peek.
- **Emotion**: her raised arms in the light cone at dusk; her arms coming down at 148.20 and the first smile since the garden; the return to the first picture at the end.
- **Restraint**: the demand is an unreadable scribble; the invented man is a dashed empty outline; the punishment is a bare platform; the fire is a bolt, a flash and then objects on a scorch mark.

- The English is shorter than the Arabic in P1, P5, P6, P7 and paraphrased in P8; both are kept verbatim. In P6 the last English chunk stays up during the third Arabic chunk.
- Up to 9 figures share the frame in the crowd shots: two depth rows, check phone legibility on a contact sheet; drop to 4 townspeople if crowded.
