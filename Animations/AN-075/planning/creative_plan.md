# AN-075 The Hermits: creative plan

Film length 174.59 s of narration + 4.0 s end hold (ends 178.59). 16 scenes, 188 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue names the Arabic script word and its start time. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (same folder); this file explains the intent.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

Two short tales about men who live alone with God on a mountain, told back to back.

**Tale one (0-34.9 s, P1-P2).** An old hermit splits his one loaf each day: half for himself, half for a pair of pigeons. He blesses them, they multiply until the mountain is full of birds, and the birds gather round him to praise God. One evening he dies; the flocks scatter over villages and mountains.

**Tale two (34.9-174.4 s, P2 end to P8).** On another mountain a shepherd lives from his flock's milk and wool; even the wild beasts leave his sheep alone. He falls gravely ill and keeps to a cave. A beautiful woman offers to stay and nurse him: she is an angel testing his resolve. He refuses, she tries again, he covers his face with his robe and prays; the test ends and the angel ascends. In a nearby village another devout man dreams he must visit the shepherd. On the hot road he rests at a spring, frightens the animals that come to drink, grieves that he kept them from water and leaves the place to them. He reaches the cave, is welcomed with astonishment and joy, and the two live together in worship until both die.

- **Conflict:** quiet, internal. The only antagonist is the temptation in the cave, and she is secretly on his side.
- **Surprises:** the mountain filling with birds (17.18); the wolf who lies down (53.68); the shadow with wings (80.42, the audience learns the secret before the shepherd); the cover-up (99.24); the ascent (105.58); the animals returning the moment the visitor gives way (142.85).
- **Emotional arc:** warmth, a first loss (29.26), a fresh start, illness and loneliness, tension, relief, a second lonely man, regret, joy of meeting, companionship, a gentle double ending.
- **Ending:** the two grow old side by side and their eyes close; the camera rises to the sky; the pair of doves from tale one lands on the cave rock. THE END at 174.6, held 4 s.
- **Moral:** quiet goodness is never alone. Kindness to small creatures and steadiness under temptation bring company, peace and a good end.
- **Visual link between the tales:** the doves and one white feather (falls at 34.1, crosses the cut at 36.3, is left by the angel at 108.0, falls at the spring at 131.5, and closes the film at 173.5).

## 3. Character acting

- **Hermit** (`person` costume `ascetic`, age 0.9, `sit` on the rock): everything slow. Half lids, `breathe()`, gaze plans between bread, slab and birds. Anticipation dip before the loaf breaks. His death is acted by subtraction: `lid` to 1, `noBlink`, breathing amplitude to 0, the beads sliding out of an opening hand. No fall.
- **Doves** (`bird`, planned dove kind): never in unison. Bird mime for pecking (`drop`, `headRot`), praise (head back, `beak` open in a travelling wave), grief (`sag`, `wet`). The first dove trusts last on arrival and leaves last at the death.
- **Shepherd** (`person` costume `bedouin`, age 0.3, `hold: 'stick'`): open and sunny at first (stick taps on footfalls, hand on chest). Illness: `tremble`, `headHold`, knees to `kneel` with `impactSquash`, tiny shuffling `walk` with `tip` 0.18, then `lie`. In the cave his refusals are gentle: flat palm (`shield`), head shakes, `pointUp`. The cover-up is the comic and moral peak: 0.3 s anticipation with eyes squeezed shut, the cloth whipped across with overshoot (`faceCover`), then a breathing puff. One-eye peek (`wink`) after the ascent.
- **Woman / angel** (`person` costume `lady`, white and yellow): poised, small smooth steps, hands folded. Persuasion escalates through gestures (`chest`, `palmsUp`, room sweep, `kneel` + bowl, `clasp`, `pointDown`). At 102.52 the "mask" comes off: the coaxing brow relaxes into a plain kind smile before any light appears. Her true form never has a face.
- **Visitor** (`person` costume `ascetic`, brown palette, age 0.45): eager walker; heat droop (`tip` 0.1, shorter steps); `sit` with `landSquash`; suspense by stillness at the spring (pupils only); the awkward frozen wave; regret (`drop`, `wet`, long blink); the tiptoe exit (`sneak`, `hush`) and open-hand offer.
- **Sheep, lamb, wolf, deer, hare**: reactions carry the comedy and the meaning. Sheep react in a `stagger`; the shorn sheep looks at its flank, then at the camera; the lamb is never afraid (sniffs the wolf, trots to the stranger). The wolf's brow goes from 1 to -0.4 as he watches the prayer. At the spring the order of flight (birds, hare, deer) makes it read; at the cave the sheep do NOT flee, which is the contrast.
- **Listeners always look at the speaker** (`lookAt`), and every pose change has anticipation and `settleTo`.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230 per view, ground y 900; faces kept between y 640 and 780 so nothing important nears the caption panel.
- **Scale tells distance:** the opening crane-out (1.5 s) makes the hermit a dot on a 2000-high cliff with clouds below him.
- **Two-shots for the temptation** via `twoShot()`, with the back wall kept bare between the heads so the shadow reveal (S8) has a clean screen. One push-in only, on the cover-up (99.2).
- **Tilt up** follows the ascent and returns to the man left behind.
- **Tracking walks** (S12, S14) stop when the walker stops; the pan to the spring is motivated by his rest.
- **Final crane** leaves the two men at the bottom of frame and ends on sky, doves and title.
- Transitions: cuts, one `later` card at 34.9 (ANOTHER MOUNTAIN...), one foreground trunk wipe at 143.18.
- Lettering and counters sit top-left or top-right; labels never cover a face.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the dove on the turban; the bird counter running away; the shorn sheep's double take; the wolf's yawn; the woman blinking at a face that has turned into a cloth; the one-eye peek; "ME?".
- **Suspense:** the wolf's ears over the bush while everyone sleeps; the shadow on the floor before the woman appears; the visitor frozen while the deer lowers its head.
- **Dramatic irony:** the audience sees the wings on the wall; the shepherd is looking the other way.
- **Emotion:** the nudge that gets no answer (29.94); the ewe checking on the sick man (64.06); the visitor's regret; the shoulder clasp; two old men at sunset.
- **Payoffs:** he gives up the spring and the deer drinks at once; the dream cloud returns pixel-identical in front of the real cave; the doves close the film.
