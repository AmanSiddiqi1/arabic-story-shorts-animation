A hermit lived on a distant mountain. Each day he ate half his bread and gave the other half to a pair of pigeons. Their young multiplied.

The birds gathered near him and praised God, as the tale relates. When the hermit died, they scattered through villages and mountains. Another story began on another mountain.

A shepherd lived from his sheep’s milk and wool, praying in peace while wild beasts left the flock alone. Then he fell gravely ill and stayed in a cave.

One day a beautiful woman entered and offered to keep him company and help him recover. She was really an angel in a woman’s form, testing his resolve.

The shepherd refused, saying he had chosen devotion. She tried again, but he covered his face and prayed. The test ended, and the angel ascended to heaven.

In a nearby village another devotee dreamed he should visit the shepherd. Traveling in the heat, he rested by a spring and saw birds and animals coming to drink.

The animals fled at his presence. Regretting that he had kept them from water, he moved on and reached the shepherd’s cave, where he was warmly welcomed.

The visitor explained his dream. The two men lived together in worship, caring for the flock and eating its milk and meat, until both eventually died.
