# KD-015 The Ascetic, the Ghee, and the Honey: creative plan

Film length 171.14 s (narration 168.14 s + 3.0 s THE END). One set (the hut), one real character, one prop that matters (the jar above his head), and a thought cloud that grows until it is bigger than the room. All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard with every action, cue word and library call is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Core idea in one picture:** a small man sits under a full jar; a dream balloon swells above him; the jar hangs between him and the dream. Everything he imagines is paid for by the one real thing in the room, and that thing is directly over his head.

**Conflict:** the man against his own imagination. There is no villain; the jar is innocent and the son does not exist.
**Emotional arc:** contentment (P1), appetite (P2), giddy pride (P3-P5), anger (P6), slapstick shock (P7), quiet regret (P8).
**Surprises:** the first swing does nothing (it is only a picture); the jar is hit by the *upswing* of the second blow; the dream pops at the exact frame the jar breaks.
**Ending:** a held wide shot of the bare room, the last drop on his nose, THE END where the dream used to be.
**Moral (shown, never written):** do not spend what you only imagine; a daydream can cost you the real thing you hold.

## 3. Character acting

- **The ascetic** is the whole cast of the real world. Three registers: *modest* (small steps, one pat of the stomach, half smile, lid 0.4), *dreaming* (eyes up-right or shut, rocking with `breathe`, hands clasped, mouth moving silently), *angry* (brow 1, `flush`, `tremble`, tip 0.08, turban tail lagging 2 frames).
- **Eye lines tell the joke.** In P1 he looks at real things (bowls, jar mouth). From 33.6 his eyes leave the jar for the cloud and never return until 136.9. Use `look()`/`gazePlan` with named targets; saccade ease 0.07 while counting. The blind grope for the stick (116.26) works only if the eyes stay locked on the cloud.
- **He mirrors his dream.** Arms wide on "wide" (57.58), hands on hips when the dream self does (79.28), teaching finger (94.48), stomach pats twice at "food" (67.74) against once in P1.
- **Anticipation and follow-through:** tiptoe stretch before the pour; 1.8 s raise of the stick; crouch `anticipate` before the fatal upswing; after SPLAT `impactSquash` and `rebound`; in S8 everything is slow `settleTo`.
- **Comedy beat (138.1-139.3):** hold, realise, look to camera `gaze [0, 0.05]`, gulp, no blink. Then flinch `sq 0.88`.
- **The jar acts too:** always swaying; heavier and slower when full; a drip that beads, stretches and falls unnoticed; rocks at the near miss; jumps at the crack; the rope stub swings damped afterwards.
- **Dream figures** act simply and clearly: goats never in unison (`stagger`), cows lazy (lid 0.5), neighbours each with a different reaction, the boy cheeky (`turnFlip`, folded arms, tongue, wink).
- **Ending:** wet eyes at the ghosts, head sinking a little on each X, cross-eyed at the drop, one long exhale. No smile.

- S1 establishing wide then push-in; S2 tilt up and down the rope; S3-S5 wide on the cloud with his head and the jar in the lower left; S6 push-in on the face, then down to the groping hand, then out; S7 slow tilt with the stick, whip to the jar, shake, cut wide for the burst, follow the blob down; S8 close, tilt to shards, pull out to the final wide.
- The final wide repeats the S2 framing with the upper right empty: the audience remembers what filled it.
- Shakes are panel-only. Lettering sits right of x 520 or below y 780, never on a face or the jar.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Suspense:** the dotted plumb line (26.2), the unnoticed drip (60.5), kneeling brings his head closer (118.5), the silent near miss (123.3), the quivering tip (127.6).
- **Humour:** the dream outgrowing the room and rattling the lamp; the stomach pat count; the dream self's costume change; the boy's tongue; the swing that only ripples a picture; the look to camera; the cross-eyed last drop.
- **Surprise:** it is the upswing that breaks the jar, and the dream pops with it.
- **Emotion:** the proud tear at 103.6 makes the loss land; the three ghosts crossed out on "no money, no goats, no field" replay the dream in six seconds.
- **Irony label pair:** IMAGINARY SON (131.98) against REAL JAR (136.94), same style, five seconds apart.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus

- Start from `Library/template/`; one scene file is enough (one set). Cues with `w(p, n)`; the JSON gives every cue as the script word and its start time.
- Draw order: sky, `hutRoom` back, lamp glow, `thoughtCloud` and its clipped contents, `hangingJar`, ascetic, stick, goo, shards, `hutRoom` front, labels, `finish` last.
- Key the cloud as one object (box, tint, dashed, lobe, pop); draw its contents in box-local coordinates so growth costs nothing.
