A little sparrow visited a peacock from morning until evening. One day birds gathered on a mountain to choose a king who would settle their disagreements.

The sparrow proposed the peacock, and the birds agreed. The new king made his friend a close minister, who brought him food and news daily.

One day the sparrow was late. Asked why, he explained that a hunter had set a grain-baited net near his nest and captured two large birds.

The sparrow wanted to move away, but the peacock advised him to stay, saying no one can escape fate. The sparrow obeyed.

The sparrow remained watchful, avoiding the net and finding food elsewhere. He never forgot the birds taken by the hunter.

Another day he saw two sparrows fighting on the ground. As minister, he felt he should intervene before they hurt each other.

He flew down between them, and at that moment the hunter cast his net over all three. The minister was trapped, caught and handed to the hunter’s companion.

The sparrow recalled the peacock’s advice and the danger he had feared. Neither his office nor his caution saved him as the hunter carried off the former minister.
