# AN-136 The Water-Carrier and the Goldsmith's Wife: creative plan

Film length 170.07 s of narration + 3.5 s THE END hold. 20 scenes, 157 timed actions (all in `creative_plan.json`, each tied to an Arabic word and its start time). Times are seconds on the final MP3.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

On the one day a Bukhara goldsmith squeezes a customer's hand in his shop, the water-carrier who has served his house blamelessly for thirty years does exactly the same to his wife, and she is the one who works out why.

- **Conflict:** not wife against husband, but a puzzle: why would a man who was blameless for thirty years do this today? The wife is the detective, and her question to her husband (51.7) is the first clue that she suspects a cause inside her own house.
- **Surprises:** the grab itself (33.24, after 20 s of calm routine); the confession turning out to be the same act (78.62, staged as the mirror image of the first hand close-up); her reaction being astonishment instead of anger (87.62); the carrier on his knees next morning (133.16).
- **Emotional arc:** calm order (0-30) → shock (33-44) → interrogation with comic squirming (44-65) → confession and the click of recognition (65-105) → shame, prayer, a sleepless night (105-126) → pardon asked and the truth told plainly (126-147) → the saying, and the wife standing upright between two bowed men (147-170).
- **Ending:** no punishment and no gloating. Her last look is to the audience at "exposed the truth" (167.94); THE END at 169.9.
- **Moral:** What you do to others comes back to your own door: the repayment matches the deed.

## 3. Character acting

- **Goldsmith (green):** starts the proudest person in the film (chest out, craftsman's wink at 2.5, purse toss at 46.5). His denial at 57.3-60 is all hands and no eye contact (`gazePlan` saccades to ceiling, lamp, floor; smile too wide). Each stage of the truth takes something off him: the smile (61.9), the height (`sit` at 104.8, `kneel` at 108.2), the turban (107.3). From then on he is bare-headed with the turban in his hands, including the last shot.
- **Wife (blue):** the stillest figure. Lowered lids and beads in S2-S3 (she never looks at the carrier in thirty years: that is the point of S3). Shock is played as a freeze, hand left in the air (37.6), then the hand turned over and studied (39.0), a move she repeats at 86.7 when she makes the connection. In the interrogation she barely moves (folded arms, `lid` 0.3, no blink) so his fidgeting reads. Astonishment at 87.6, never rage. Final pose: upright, hands together, chin up, small closed smile.
- **Water-carrier (brown):** bent under the skin (`tip` 0.12-0.18), eyes always on the ground, the hand-on-chest bow at the door (19.8) that returns in her memory (42.2) and in the last picture (167.0). The lapse is played with the eyes first (28.4: pupils leave the water while the body keeps pouring, the cistern overflows unnoticed), then a straight walk at her. Next day: heavier, stops inside the door, sees her, the skin slides off, knees (133.16), clasped hands, forehead to the ground (140.0).
- **Customer (red):** only in the cloud; polite, then a sharp withdrawal (83.3).
- **Everyone:** `LIFE.t` blinks, a gaze target at all times (`lookAt`), anticipation before each big move (`anticipate`), overshoot and settle on pose changes (`settleTo`), `walk` + `footfalls` for every step, `breathe` on holds, listeners react 3-4 frames late (`stagger`). Secondary motion: the waterskin sloshes a beat behind his steps and flaps when empty; the purse swings on its string; the bracelet bounces on the wrist.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground y 900; faces and hands stay in the upper two-thirds panel, lettering never over a face.
- **Three kinds of picture:** (1) wide and two-shot staging in the courtyard and the room; (2) the **hand insert** (S5, S10), the same framing twice, mirrored and recoloured; (3) flat graphic cards (the scale, S19).
- **Geography repeats on purpose:** S4 and S16 use the same courtyard blocking (he enters left, she is at x 700). On day one he closes the gap; on day two he stops 200 units short and kneels.
- **Eye-line reversal:** S1 he looks down at his gold; S14 he looks up at her from his knees; S18 three heads in a rising line (kneeling carrier, wife, husband peeking from the door).
- Push-ins on decisions (31.0 his approach, 60.2 her demand, 98.0 her question); pull-outs to make room for clouds and insets (66.8, 89.4); `twoShot` for all dialogue. Panel-only shake (3 px) on the two squeezes and the pan landings.
- One `later` card: NEXT DAY... at 125.9, hiding the cut from room to courtyard. Dusk, night and dawn are told by the room's window (`u`).
- Final composition is symmetrical: wife centre in a light cone, a bowed man on each side, THE END in the sky above the wall.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **The match cut is the story.** Because S10 repeats S5 exactly (flipped, green and red instead of brown and blue), the audience solves the puzzle about nine seconds before the wife says "the same way" (92.14). S12 then pays it off by hanging the two panels side by side with an equals sign.
- **Suspense:** 28.4-32.3, pupils-only stare, overflowing cistern, the gap closing. 56.2-56.8, the silent beat before the denial.
- **Humour:** thirty years as a sped-up loop with a greying beard and a year counter; the purse toss dying at the sight of her face; the denial played with too many hands; 30 YEARS, 0 TIMES; the husband peeking round the door with his turban in his hands while his wife reports him.
- **Emotion:** the turban coming off (107.3); the shared prayer with staggered palms; the sleepless night where one small cloud with two tails hangs over both heads until the window goes yellow.
- **Restraint:** the wrong itself is two stylised hands for about three seconds each time. No leering faces, no struggle.
- **Lettering diet:** about 20 short words, mostly labels and counters on key beats; sound words only SQUEEZE! (twice, matching).
