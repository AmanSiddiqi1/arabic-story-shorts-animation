Ali of Cairo inherited great wealth from his jeweler father, who warned him against reckless company and spending. Ali ignored the warning and squandered his fortune.

He sold his jewels, property, and finally his home. His former companions refused help. A kind woman fed his family while Ali left Cairo seeking relief.

Ali traveled through Damietta and Damascus toward Baghdad. Bandits attacked his caravan, but he reached the city and found hospitality from guards and a merchant.

The merchant offered him lodging, but Ali insisted on a splendid house rumored to kill anyone who slept there. In despair, he ignored the warning.

At night a mysterious voice called Ali by name and asked whether to send down gold. He answered boldly, and coins rained into the room.

The voice said a jinn guarded treasure intended for Ali. He requested another hoard from Yemen and safe transport for his wife and children.

Three days later a wondrous caravan brought treasure and his family. Reunited in Baghdad, Ali heard his wife remind him not to repeat his mistakes.
