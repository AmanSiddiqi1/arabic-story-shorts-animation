## Remaining notes (polish only; none of these triggers v3)

### 1. 11.5 to 14.6: the moonlight screen covers the soldier's face
What is wrong: the moonlight wedge is drawn as a pale dot screen on top of the characters. While he sleeps (11.5 to 13.5) his head is a grey dotted ball and the Zzz is half erased (`f_11.9`, `f_12.4`); when he sits up at 13.7 to 14.5 the screen still lies over his face (`f_14.2`) and only clears with the cut-in at 14.7.
Why it matters: this is the inciting incident, and the wake-up at 13.16 (استيقظ) is his first reaction shot of the film.
Fix: draw the wedge before the soldier and the Zzz (it should light the wall, the mat and the bag only), or clip it so it ends at the bag's right edge. Keep the bag bright as now. Check stills at 11.9, 12.4 and 14.2.

### 2. 111.0 to 113.0: the bag in the lit theft is hard to find
What is wrong: in the kneeling shot the bag is a dark brown box against the dark brown skirting, partly behind the sharper's forearm (`f_112.0`). The blade is visible, but the slit and what it is cutting are not; the beat only becomes clear when the red purse comes out at 113.0.
Why it matters: this is the answer to the night silhouette and the first showing of THE MOVE.
Fix: give the bag the same lit treatment it has in S2 (the lighter orange-brown of `f_11.9`), move it 20 to 30 px right so it clears his arm, and open the pale paper-coloured slit at 111.36 (الجندي) as in S2 at 15.58.

### 3. 104.9 to 108.0: watching shot framed too far right
What is wrong: the soldier sits at the left edge with his arm cropped by the panel border and the bag out of frame (`f_105.6`), so the sharper's open eye has no visible target and "arm round the bag, arm slides off" at 107.9 cannot be seen.
Why it matters: the narration's verb is "watched"; the object of the watching should be in the picture.
Fix: move the camera centre about 100 px left (or drop the zoom from 1.75 to about 1.55) so the order on screen is bag, soldier, stool, sharper's head, with at least 40 px of margin left of the bag. Nothing else changes.

### 4. 98.9 to 99.6: small glitches at the wall
What is wrong: the reaching forearm is a straight vertical green post, and its lower end shows as a green blob on the ground in front of the wall (`f_99.3`; also strip frames 99.0, 99.2, 99.5).
Fix: clip the arm at the wall's top edge (`clipY` at the wall top) and tilt it about 20 degrees toward the bag with a bent wrist so it reads as reaching, not waving.

### 5. 123.5 to 133: stray and leaning canes
What is wrong: at 123.5 to 123.7 a cane stands on its own on the carpet to the right of guard B (`f_123.6`); from 124.7 to 133 guard A's staff leans diagonally across the bodies of the bedouin and the old merchant (`f_128.5`). Faces are clear, so the round-1 rule is met, but the diagonal is a distracting line through the chorus during the SAFE build-up.
Fix: remove the free-standing cane (guard B has no cane in this scene), and keep guard A's staff vertical at his outer side while he leans in, as in 62.4 to 123.

### 6. 61.5 to 62.2: the soldier's rush hides the sharper
What is wrong: when the soldier lunges for the purse he stops directly in front of the sharper, whose face disappears for about 0.7 s (`f_62.0`).
Fix: stop the soldier 60 px further left (his reaching hand still arrives at the purse), or draw him behind the sharper for this beat, so the sharper's amused side glance is visible while the chief's palm stops the soldier.

### 7. 167.2 to 168.0: the 0 counter sits on two guests' faces
What is wrong: the yellow 0 burst covers the bedouin and the old merchant (`f_167.4`) while the moth flies out.
Fix: move the counter to the upper third above the door arch or over the empty wall at the right, clear of all heads.

### 8. 122.7 to 123.2: LIKE THIS... is still too short
What is wrong: the label scales up from a tiny size at 122.7 and has faded by 123.2.
Fix: pop it at full size at 122.84 (نفسها) and hold it to 124.0, in the same upper-left slot.

### 9. 170.0 to 170.5: the guests turn into ghosts at the door
What is wrong: the four guests fade to half transparency as they pass the doorway, then reappear small outside.
Fix: keep them opaque and let the door jamb hide them (`behind(ctx)`), then show the leap for joy outside as now.
