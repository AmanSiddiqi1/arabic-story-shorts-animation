# AN-105 The Sharper of Alexandria and the Chief of Police: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/AN-105_v1.mp4` through all 30 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 fps, 0 to 180 s) plus 26 full-size frames in `review/v1_pack/extra/`. Sound was not auditioned (pictures only), so effect levels are not judged here.

## Summary
The story is told in the right order, nothing is missing, the ending is present, and both subtitle languages are readable and correctly timed (the English matches the Arabic chunk by chunk). No blockers. The best passages are the confession conjuring (55.5 to 58.7), the "may I?" close shot (116.7 to 118.7), the SAFE thought cloud before the snatch (127.5 to 131.5), the tick, tick, hourglass ruling (156.7 to 160.7) and the halos on the guests (162.5).

A v2 is worth it because four key beats are weakened by staging, not by missing ideas: (a) one guard's cane lies across faces for about 80 seconds, (b) the hall crowd is a single overlapping clump with the soldier standing on the cane bundle, (c) the flashback stalking shows the thief in plain view beside his victim, and (d) most of the hall is played in a wide shot whose lower 40 percent is empty floor, so faces are small on a phone. Two visible glitches (a headless sleeper, guards drawn through the pool) and the last image (turban reads as a white box) should also be fixed.

Do not change narration, subtitle text or subtitle timing. All times below are seconds on the final MP3.

## Notes

### 1. 45.2 to 123.5, MAJOR: the cane lies across faces for more than a minute
What is wrong: at 45.2 guard A swings the cane to a horizontal hold. From then on it is a bar across the eyes of two guests (45.2 to 55.2, see `extra/f_45.6.png`, `f_51.0.png`), then, after a short diagonal hold in the close shot (55.5 to 62), it returns as a horizontal bar across the soldier's face and neck from about 62.5 until about 123.5 (`f_67.0.png`, `f_84.9.png`, `f_122.9.png`).
Why it matters: (1) it looks as if the cane is pressed on the guests' heads, which contradicts the plan's rule that no cane touches anyone and weakens the family-friendly reading; (2) it hides the faces whose fear and relief are the emotion of this passage; (3) a threat that never goes away stops being a threat, so the STOP! at 50.78 has nothing to release.
Fix:
- 44.90 (قبل) to 46.16 (الضرب): raise the cane high and steep (about 60 to 70 degrees from horizontal), tip above all heads, held at the top with only the tip wobbling (`swing`). Nothing of the cane may cross a face.
- 50.78 (دعوا): the cane freezes at the top; guard A's head snaps to the stranger.
- 53.14 (فأنا) to 55.0: the cane droops slowly as the guard stares.
- 62.36 (فتنفس): guard A plants the cane upright, butt on the floor at his side, like a staff, and keeps it there (or lays it on the bundle). It stays vertical and clear of every face until the collapse at 134.1.
- From 62.36 to 130.8 no cane segment may overlap any head. Check with stills at 51.0, 67.0, 85.0, 122.9.

### 4. 95.5 to 101.5, MAJOR: the stalking flashback does not read as stalking

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- The shared thought cloud with a stamp (purse + chain + padlock, then SAFE stamped on at the cue word, then burst on the reversal) is a compact way to show a group's false belief.
- The verdict as a three-frame picture balloon (tick, tick, hourglass tipping over) explains a legal argument without words; worth a helper (`iconStrip` with per-frame ticks).
- Halos popping on over "innocent" bystanders.
- Rule for the Capabilities Guide: a held prop (cane, staff, sword) must have a rest pose that clears all faces, and any held threat needs an explicit release beat.
- Rule: for hiding behind cover, clip the character at the cover's edge and show only headgear and eyes; never the torso.
