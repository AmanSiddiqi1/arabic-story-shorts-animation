A soldier arrived in Alexandria and stayed at an inn for travelers,|with a purse of a thousand gold pieces.|He slept part of the night, then woke to find his bag slit open and the money stolen.|He hurried to the police chief, Husam al-Din, to report what had happened.
The police chief ordered everyone at the inn arrested,|though there was no clear evidence against them.|In the morning he brought out sticks to beat them until one of them confessed,|while the soldier stood waiting to get his money back.
Before the beating began, a man pushed his way through the crowd|and shouted: Let these innocent people go, I am the one who took the money!|He took the purse of gold from his sleeve and set it before the police chief and the soldier,|The detained travelers were relieved.
People praised the confession,|but he said his real skill was not in returning the money,|but in stealing it a second time.|Amazed, the police chief asked how he had done it, and the trickster began explaining from the start.
The trickster said he had seen the soldier receive the gold at the money changers’ market in Cairo.|He followed him from city to city without finding a chance to steal it,|until he stayed at the same inn in Alexandria and watched him until he fell asleep.
He explained how he had slit the soldier’s bag at night and quietly taken the money.|Then he reached for the purse on the table, saying he would show them the very same move.|Everyone drew closer to watch the show,|thinking the money was now safe.
Suddenly he grabbed the purse and dashed out of the hall,|plunged into a nearby pool,|and escaped through Alexandria’s interconnected lanes.|The police chief shouted for a chase, but his men were too slow and came back without the trickster or the money.
The police chief told the soldier he had found the thief and got his money back for a moment,|so he could no longer claim anything from the people of the inn.|The soldier left empty-handed, and the travelers escaped the beating.|As for the trickster, he escaped, leaving the police chief disgraced.
