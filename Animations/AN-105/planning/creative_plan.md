# AN-105 The Sharper of Alexandria and the Chief of Police: creative plan

Film length 178.26 s of narration + 3.5 s THE END hold (ends 181.76). 22 scenes, 205 timed actions. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (every action with its cue word and Library call); this file gives the direction behind it. All times are seconds on the final MP3.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
**Spine.** A proud soldier brings 1000 gold pieces to Alexandria (7.36). In the night his bag is slit (15.58). The police chief, Husam al-Din, arrests every guest of the inn without proof (25.9) and next morning has canes brought to beat out a confession (37.08). A stranger stops it (50.78), confesses, and conjures the purse from his sleeve onto the table (57.9). Praised, he boasts that returning it is nothing: his real art is stealing it twice (77.62). Asked how, he tells his tale (flashback 88.26 to 115.0): Cairo, the road, the inn, the two-finger lift (113.08). "Like this", he says, lifting the purse from the table (121.1); everyone leans in, sure it is safe (130.22); he snatches it (130.82), jumps into a pool (137.14) and is lost in the lanes. The guards come back with nothing (149.14). The chief rules that the money was recovered "for a moment", so the guests owe nothing (159.22). Soldier out empty-handed (167.08), guests free, chief left with the scandal (175.72).

**Conflict.** Three wants collide: the soldier wants his gold, the chief wants a quick confession, the sharper wants an audience. The innocents are caught between.

**Surprises.** (1) The confession. (2) The boast. (3) The second theft, which the audience can see coming two seconds before the characters (his eyes on the door): dramatic irony, then release.

**Emotional arc.** Pride, shock, dread (the raised cane), relief (PHEW!), delight in the storyteller, held breath, slapstick release, deflation, a wry last image.

**Ending and moral.** A hasty, heavy-handed judge is easily made a fool of; the innocent are saved by a rogue's vanity, not by justice. Last image: the chief alone, turban pulled over his eyes, one eye peeking at us.

## 3. Character acting
- **Soldier** (person, planned `soldier` preset, red): proud walk (chest out, gaze up), a habit of patting the bag flap twice (S1, S10) that pays off when the bag is empty. Shock take at 17.52 (`anticipate`, `sq` 1.12, `settleTo`, no blink). Waiting: `PERSON_HANDS.folded`, toe tap. After the snatch he is spun (`turnFlip`) and sits. Exit: `tip` 0.12, head down, `wet` eyes, low-amplitude `walk`.
- **Chief** (vizier preset, navy and red): pompous `lid` 0.4, chin up, `PERSON_HANDS.chin` beard stroking. His turban is his dignity meter: straight when in control, slipping at 80.12 and 143.02, pulled over his eyes at 177.34. Double take with 2-frame saccades at 79.06. Planned `flush` at 143.7 and 175.72.
- **Sharper** (merchant preset, green robe, yellow turban): a conjuror. Light steps, `brow` -0.4, one-sided smile, hands always presenting (`palmsUp`, `chest`, `pointUp`, `hush`). Mask on / mask off: innocent raised brows (-0.5) when watched, pupils flicking to the door when not. The two-finger lift is built from arm angles and shown twice (113.08, 121.1). Before the snatch: shoe turns, knees sink 6 px (`anticipate`). Two winks to the lens only (78.12, 141.88) plus the hush at 114.16.
- **Guards** (soldier preset, blue): A tall, stern, slow; B short, eager, always 3 frames late, helmet too big. Cane handling is a strike cycle that never completes.
- **Four guests** (villager with nightcap and pillow, bedouin, old merchant, young servant): a chorus that never moves in unison (`stagger` 3 to 4 frames). Fear: `lid` 1, `PERSON_HANDS.shield`, `tremble`, one peek (`wink`). Relief: `sq` 1.06 then 0.93. Listening: they sit cross-legged like children. Freed: `sneak`, then `hop` + `landSquash`.
- **Everyone, always:** a gaze target (`lookAt`, `gazePlan`), blinks (`LIFE.t`), overshoot and settle on pose changes, secondary motion on turban tails, cape, bag and sign (`swing`).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Suspense:** the cane held at the top with nothing moving but its tip (44.9 to 46.6); the stillness before the snatch (130.22 to 130.82) where only his pupils move.
- **Dramatic irony:** all eyes on his face while his hand travels to the purse (115.8 to 118.6); the shared SAFE thought cloud while his shoe turns to the door.
- **Setup and payoff:** silhouette arm (12.2) answered by the lit theft (111.36); the two-finger pose; the double pat on the bag; the yellow turban planted three times before he speaks; the soldier's 1000 counter that falls to 0, returns, and ends as GOLD 0 in a guard's helmet.
- **Humour:** each guest pointing at his neighbour (39.32); listeners sitting down for story time (84.92); hiding behind a palm trunk and a wall (S11); the purse crossing the pool high and dry like a periscope (138.04); guards jamming in the door and swapping lanes (146 to 150.9); the ruling drawn as tick, tick, hourglass (156.6 to 159.8); the moth (167.08).
- **Emotion:** the long sigh at 62.36 and the soldier's empty hands at 167.9 are played straight.
- Violence is only threatened: no cane touches anyone.
