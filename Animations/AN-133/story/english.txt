One day Harun al-Rashid found a fresh stain on his bed that he believed was semen. Shocked, he summoned his wife Zubaydah for an explanation.

He demanded to know about the stain. She agreed it resembled semen but did not know its source. Harun suspected infidelity and threatened her unless she confessed.

Zubaydah swore she was innocent. Harun called Judge Abu Yusuf, who heard their accounts without immediately deciding who was at fault.

The judge looked up and noticed a crack above the bed. In the tale he suggested a bat hiding there might have left a similar-looking fluid.

He probed the crack with a spear, and a bat fell out. Those present accepted this as the explanation, clearing Zubaydah of suspicion.

Relieved, Zubaydah promised a splendid reward. She showed him rare fruit in the room and asked whether he preferred it to another kind still in the garden.

Abu Yusuf joked that one cannot judge an absent party. When both fruits arrived, he sampled each and said that whenever he praised one, the other argued its case.

Harun laughed at the judge’s courtroom comparison and gave him an expensive gift. Zubaydah fulfilled her promise, and Abu Yusuf left the palace delighted.
