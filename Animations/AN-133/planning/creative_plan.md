# AN-133 The Kazi Abu Yusuf with Harun al-Rashid and Queen Zubaydah: creative plan

Film length 178.26 s (MP3 175.26 s + 3.0 s end hold). 20 scenes, 198 actions, 16 quiet effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (same folder); this file explains the intent.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A mark on the caliph's bed almost wrecks his marriage, until a patient judge looks up, finds a crack in the ceiling and knocks a bat out of it; then he wins the room again with a joke about two fruits.

**Conflict.** Harun's haste against the truth. Zubaydah has only her word; Abu Yusuf has patience and a pair of eyes.

**Arc in three movements.**
1. *Suspicion (0 to 57.9, P1 to P3).* Rest, discovery (8.30), anger (22.96), her honest "I do not know" (31.28), accusation and threat (37.82, 39.88), her oath (46.08), his doubt, the summons (54.72). Emotion: comfort, shock, fear, hurt dignity.
2. *Investigation (57.9 to 109.2, P3 to P5).* The judge listens to both evenly (61.00, 61.78), refuses to hurry (63.48), looks up (67.82), finds the crack (70.92), proposes the bat (76.96), tests it with a spear (90.26), the bat falls (91.78 to 93.36), suspicion dissolves (100.78), Zubaydah is cleared (104.82). Emotion: suspense, surprise, relief.
3. *Comedy and reward (109.2 to 178.26, P6 to P8).* Promise of a reward (113.76), the fruit question (123.60 to 128.54), "no judging the absent" (134.72), tasting (140.70, 141.60), "the other argues its case" (149.84), the caliph's laugh (153.70), gifts (164.70, 167.34), the happy exit (171.64 to 174.12).

**Surprises.** The eyes blinking in the crack (71.50, a plant), the bat's fall, the bat's shrug to camera (98.44), the yellow fruit shouting OBJECTION! (149.84) and then jiggling in the judge's real hand (151.24).

**Ending.** Outside the palace Abu Yusuf skips off with casket and purse, the reconciled couple wave from the balcony, the bat crosses the sky. THE END from 175.4, held to 178.26.

**Moral.** Do not judge in haste or on appearances: look for the evidence, and hear the absent party before you rule. The fruit joke is the same lesson told lightly, so the second half is a mirror of the first, not an appendix.

## 3. Character acting

- **Harun** (`person` costume `caliph`, age 0.3). A man of quick weather. Tired and soft in S1 (lid 0.5, yawn). The discovery is played by stillness: only the pupils move (6.74). Anger uses `brow` 0.8 to 1, the planned `flush`, jabs on counted words (`countedBeats`), a foot stamp with crown wobble (`swing`). Doubt arrives as a physical drop: the raised hand sinks (49.88), eyes to the floor, a long blink. Relief is one deep `breathe` (102.44). Shame: one hand behind the head, eyes down then up (102.98). The laugh (153.70) is the planned person laugh: head back, `tip` -0.12, `lid` 0.9, shoulders shaking, crown hopping. The crooked crown is a running gag: crooked at 11.6, straight for the sitting room, crooked again at 154.84.
- **Zubaydah** (`person` costume `lady`, red palette). Never a victim pose: no kneeling. Her honesty reads because she walks *to* the mark and studies it (27.06) before answering. Fear is real (mouth cover 37.82, shield 40.64, `wet`), then a visible turn at 43.0: long blink, head level, hand on heart. The oath uses an arm angle so the open palm reads. One tear only (47.00). After the bat: tall, chin up, no gloating; the smile comes a beat later (105.58). In the sitting room she is playful: skip (`hop`, `landSquash`), wink (122.10), weighing two hands like a scale (125.68).
- **Abu Yusuf** (`person` costume `physician` as the planned `judge` preset, age 0.7). Economy. Default `lid` 0.4, slow even `walk`, hands behind his back. He listens with identical nods to both parties (61.00, 61.78). His eyes lead his head by 6 frames on the look up (67.82). The spear moment has anticipation (`anticipate`, sq 0.94) and a strike cycle with damped return. In the comedy he plays mock gravity: squares the turban (131.18), winks, speaks to a finger. Tasting: eyes shut on the first bite, pop open on the second.
- **Servant** (`person` costume `servant`). The fast, nervous contrast: peek and flee, planned `runInPlace` exit, the too-long spear at the door, a proud run with the garden fruit.
- **Bat** (new). Two eye dots in the crack (71.50, 78.66); a tumbling fall (`fallY`, rotation), `impactSquash` and `rebound`, dizzy spirals, a wing shrug with the film's one look to camera (98.44), hops and flight out of the window.
- **Groups.** Every shared look is staggered (`stagger(i)`, 4 to 6 frames); breathing phases differ; listeners always have a gaze target (`lookAt`, `gazePlan`). `LIFE.t = t` for natural blinks, suppressed in the three held stares.
- **Secondary motion.** The hanging lamp sways on footfalls; the crown and the judge's turban settle late; the spear tip sways (`swing`); the purse bulge wobbles on arrival.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Tact as style.** The mark is an abstract splotch with a question mark; the suspicion is a cracked heart and a dashed faceless man. The pictures tell the emotion and leave the word to the narrator.
- **Suspense:** the unmarked crack from frame one; the blink in the dark; the judge's flat palm that makes the caliph wait; two careful pokes before the third.
- **Surprise and comedy:** the spear that will not fit through the door; the bat's shrug; the gag that jumps from the thought cloud into the judge's hand (151.24); the crown.
- **Emotion:** "he looks, she does not" at 57.28; her single tear; his apology with open palms (107.26); the joined hands at 170.46 that close the quarrel without a word.
- **Echoes:** the balance scale appears three times (the summons balloon 54.72, level over the judge 64.32, with an empty pan 133.86), tying the serious case to the joke. The purse balloon of 113.76 returns with a tick at 168.10. Zubaydah weighing two hands is a human scale.
