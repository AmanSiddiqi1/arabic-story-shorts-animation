A water bird lived near a wide river and enjoyed searching for food among the rocks. One day he saw a man’s body on the riverbank and watched cautiously from a distance.

The bird wondered what had happened, then saw birds of prey approaching the body. Fearing danger if he stayed, he flew far from home until he reached another river.

He settled beside a tree at the water. A tortoise saw him and asked why he was sad. He explained that he had left his familiar home and feared enemies who had suddenly appeared nearby.

The tortoise said exile was easier with a true friend. She invited him to stay nearby and talk each day. Her words comforted him, and he began to feel safe in his new home.

Yet the bird kept thinking of his old home. After a while he said he wanted to return briefly to see whether the predators had gone. The tortoise agreed to wait and urged him to be careful.

He flew to the old bank and found no predators. Only scattered bones remained. Thinking the danger was over, he returned to the tortoise and told her what he saw. They went back together.

The friends spent peaceful days there. The bird searched for food beside the water, and the tortoise stayed near him. His old fear seemed like a distant memory.

One day a hungry falcon flew over the river. It spotted the water bird and swooped before he could escape. The falcon killed and carried him away, leaving the tortoise alone in the place they had chosen together.
