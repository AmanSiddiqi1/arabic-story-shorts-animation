## Fixes for v3 (one final correction pass, at most these four)

### 1. 111.5-134.2 s, MAJOR: the crab appears to stand on the far ridge with the bird perched on him
What is wrong: through the approach, the eye close-up, the bones pan and the whole realisation scene, the dark red ridge of the far hills runs exactly under the crab's pincer tips and legs (`f_113.0`, `f_118.0`, `f_123.0`, `f_130.5`; strips 108-134). With the bird's legs now short, the picture reads as a crab standing on a hilltop with a bird sitting on his head, for about 22 s. At 133.94 the ground then drops away, which looks like a take-off.
Why it matters: the line at 133.94 is "before the bird could land". If they already look landed from 112 s, the line is contradicted and the pull-up reads backwards. It is the same horizon-at-foot-level error as round-1 note 1, moved earlier by the tighter framing.
Fix: keep clear sky under the crab for the whole of 110.94-133.94. Raise the hover point (`K19HOV`, bird and crab together) about 90 world units for S12 and S13 and raise the y of the camera keys for 110.94-115.23 and 120.77-133.94 by the same amount, so the pair stays framed as now and the ridge drops to the bottom of the frame: at least one shell-height (about 100 units) of sky between the pincer tips and the ridge. Reduce `K19LIFT` by the same 90 so the climax from 133.94 lands exactly where it is now; do not change anything after 135.4. In the eye close-up (115.24-116.71, zoom 3.0) no ridge should be in frame at all. In the bones pan (116.72-120.77) the crab may be cropped to the lower shell and hanging pincers at the top edge, but must not touch the ridge. Keep the ledge below frame until 116.72 as now. Give the legs and pincers the small `swing` they have in the flight scene (102-110), not a still planted pose.

### 2. 6.0-14.0, 20.2-21.5 and 49.2-51.2 s, MAJOR: fish swim over the dry bank
What is wrong: several fish are drawn fully or half on the brown earth, left of the shoreline: the red fish and then the yellow fish that the bird hunts at 6.0-14.0 (`f_7.5`, `f_12.2`), the yellow fish after the feeble peck at 20.5 (`f_20.5`), and the fleeing fish at 49.2-51.2 (`f_49.5`).
Why it matters: it is in the first seconds of the film and on the hunting beat itself; fish lying on dirt read as a drawing error and blur where the water is, on which every later pond shot depends.
Fix: keep every swimming fish inside the water polygon (`K19WATERPTS`). Either clamp each fish's x so the whole body (half a body length of margin) stays right of the shoreline at its depth, or clip the swimming-fish layer to `K19WATERPTS` and move the hunt targets about 60-80 units right so the jab on يصطاد 8.06 and the miss on وضعف 11.90 still land on a fish in open water just off the bank. Fish that are deliberately out of the water (leaps, the hoppers at 72.5, fish in the beak) are exempt. At 49.2-51.2 let the panicking fish scatter right and up, not through the bank.

### 3. 44.5-49.0 s, MINOR: the school around the crab is a knotted ring
What is wrong: when the crab reaches the fish, the twelve fish form one tight ring, each overlapping the next, with the crab's thought-cloud tail rising through it (`f_45.5`, `f_47.0`). It reads as a wreath, not as listeners, and individual faces are lost.
Why it matters: this is the beat where the crab himself delivers the lie; the fish should be an audience facing him, so that EEK! at 48.78 is a visible change from listening to panic.
Fix: in `k19school6`, replace the ring with a loose two-row arc to the right of and above the crab (x about 620-940, y about 760-960), spacing at least one body length, all facing left toward him with `lookAt` on the crab, small independent bob. Keep the yellow and the pink in the front row. No fish between the crab and the cloud.
