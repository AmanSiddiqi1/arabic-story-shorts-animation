# ALI-05 "The Unexpected Guest" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 2 (v2)

Film reviewed: `Animations/ALI-05/renders/ALI-05_v2.mp4` (137.8 s). Sources: 64 panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments of the round 1 notes and at spot checks through the rest of the film, and a 10 fps frame-difference scan of the panel. Times are seconds on the film.

Not checked: sound was not listened to. The 23 contact strips of the v2 pack were not gone through one by one this round (economy); stretches between the stills cited below (mainly 14-31, 51-60, 66-78, 84-91, 99-104, 119-123) were sampled at one or two frames each, not continuously. Stepped cel timing, blinks, squash and follow-through cannot be judged from stills. The scan measures picture change including the CRT noise, so it shows that no stretch is as quiet as v1's frozen insert but cannot prove every character is moving.

## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

No BLOCKER. Ready for the owner. The four things that made round 1 a REVISE are fixed on screen: the cliffhanger composition, the ring insert, the signal to Ali Baba and the switch-on smile. The notes below are polish; none needs a further review round.

## Notes (polish, most useful first)

### 1. 96.9-98.0 s: the struck-out lie is covered by its own correction
- **What is wrong.** At 97.4 the ...BY OLD WELL LANE, OF COURSE! balloon sits on top of MY FIRST TIME IN THIS TOWN!, hiding its lower half and most of the red strike. The lane-map picture bubble still has the old dark wedge tail (also the dish bubble at 34.0).
- **Why it matters.** The gag is that we read the lie, see it struck, then read the slip. Two overlapped lines of text read as a glitch.
- **Fix.** Put the second balloon about 70 px lower so the struck line and the whole strike stay readable above it, and keep both inside the panel. Give the picture bubbles the same thin tail as `abTail` (24 px root, bubble fill, cyan rule).

### 2. 38.5-42.2 s and 131.8-133.3 s: two eye-lines that would tie cause to effect
- **What is wrong.** At 39.4, 40.9 and 41.6 Morgiana in the window smiles straight ahead; she is not looking at the guest's hand below, so "spotted him" is still carried by the framing and the following insert only. In the ending her eyes are nearly closed in a smile throughout the turn (131.5-133.2), which is fine, but his glance up to the helmets (134.6-135.0) is too small to read at this size.
- **Fix.** Window: from "لمحته" 38.54 `gaze` down-left toward him ([-0.8, 0.7]), head tipped the same way one drawing later, mouth closed from the smile to neutral on "فعرفت" 41.14, with the existing lid snap. Ending: on the glance add a head tip up of one drawing and a single sweat drop, then back to the grin.

### 3. 46.3-47.1 s: the crack is a thin pale line on a bright blue stone
- **What is wrong.** At 46.6 the crack is a short white stroke; in the memory panel at 47.8 the same crack is dark and reads much better.
- **Fix.** Draw the crack as a dark blue line (the stone's outline colour) with the white as a 2-frame flash only, running the full width of the stone, as in the memory panel.

### 4. 110.4-111.5 s: the signal is small in the wide shot
- **What is wrong.** It reads, but her raised hand and the light ring are about 30 px at zoom 1.1.
- **Fix.** Optional: make the two light rings larger (radius about 40 world units) and add a small label-free light streak earlier, from 110.9, so the eye is already travelling toward Ali Baba before the pan.

### 5. 134.0-137.8 s: the top of the last picture is crowded
- **What is wrong.** The helmets, the washing line flags, the cat and the captain's plume share one strip at the right, and TO BE CONTINUED runs over the moon.
- **Fix.** Optional: drop the washing line (or its three flags) for this shot only, and move the title about 40 px left so the moon sits clear. Do not move the helmets or the cat; ALI-06 continuity is already written from these positions.

### 6. 4.6-13.8 s: the market ground is still about a quarter of the panel
- **What is wrong.** Brick paving fills roughly the lower 26% (5.0, 13.4). It is acceptable now that the face reads, and customers stand on it.
- **Fix.** Optional: one more ground prop in the empty lower right (a sack and a basket) or raise the camera centre about 40 world units.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- A `'grin'` mouth for the Library `person` (as a new optional mouth value, so existing drawings do not change): the revision found that `mouth: 'grin'` fell back to a flat line across the series. `a5Grin` is a working model.
- `abTail`: the thin balloon tail in the balloon's own fill; make it the default for this style's speech and picture bubbles.
- The clue insert pattern proven here: object at 15% of panel width, the observer's eyes in a strip above, one change per narrated word.
- The frame-difference scan as a `quiet stretches` list in `tools/review/make_pack.py` (still worth adding; it confirmed the insert fix in seconds).
