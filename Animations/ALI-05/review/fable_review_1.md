# ALI-05 "The Unexpected Guest" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 1 (v1)

Film reviewed: `Animations/ALI-05/renders/ALI-05_v1.mp4` (137.8 s). Sources: all 23 contact strips in `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second), 51 panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited below, a frame-difference scan of the panel (10 fps) for frozen stretches, and the scene sources for positions and camera values. Times are seconds on the film.

Not checked: sound was not listened to. Stepped cel timing (twos and threes), blinks, landing squash and follow-through fall between sampled frames and cannot be judged from stills.

## Verdict: REVISE

No BLOCKER. Every narrated event is on screen and in order, subtitles are complete and sharp in both languages, TO BE CONTINUED is present, no broken frames.

It is REVISE and not PASS WITH NOTES because three story beats and one owner point fail in ways the owner will see at once:
- the cliffhanger (note 1): Morgiana is cut in half by the left edge of the panel for the last 13 seconds, her only spoken line included, and the guest stops in the middle of the yard instead of at the barred gate;
- the ring clue (note 2): the insert is a frozen picture for 3.0 of its 4.9 seconds (owner: no frozen holds) with the lower half of the panel empty;
- "signaled Ali Baba" (note 3): the signal is a faint ring of light on her hands in a shot Ali Baba is not in;
- the two smiles (note 4): his switch-on smile, the idea the whole film is built on, cannot be told from his scowl.

The dinner room itself is the strongest part of the film and needs little.

Lessons from episodes 2-3, checked: near-black tints (none); balloons inside the panel (yes); door marks under the cast (no marks in this film); small story objects on a phone (misses: helmets at the window, the lentil bowl, the ring in the memory panel; notes 7, 8, 2); varied framings in one room (yes: seven framings from the wide pan to zoom 1.75); frozen holds (the ring insert).

Does the dinner keep changing? Yes. 51-69 and 80.6-122.6 never repeat a picture for more than about 4 s, the counter, balloons, voice ribbons and picture bubbles land on their words, and Morgiana's eyes act throughout. The one weakness is that 80.6-111.2 (30 s) never leaves the right end of the room (note 10).

Does the ring clue read? Half. The stone and its split are visible at 45-47, but the ring is about 60 px across in a 972 px panel during its own close-up, the picture does not move, and nothing says it is Morgiana who sees it.

### 2. 42.2-47.1 s: the ring insert is a frozen, half-empty picture

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- `clueCounter`: a labelled chrome counter that ticks on a cue word; good for any detective or riddle story.
- `disguiseWipe` with a light band: showing the audience who a disguised character is without telling the cast.
- The hanging balloon struck out by the speaker's own next line (once its tail is fixed).
- `voiceWave` ribbons that match or break: a way to show a recognised or changed voice in a film whose narration cannot change.
- Review tooling: a 10 fps frame-difference scan of the panel (percent of pixels changed) finds frozen inserts that 4 fps strips can hide; worth adding to `tools/review/make_pack.py` as a `quiet stretches` list.
- Rule for the guide: a clue insert gets (a) the object at 15% of the panel width or more, (b) the observer's eyes in the same picture, (c) one change per narrated word.
