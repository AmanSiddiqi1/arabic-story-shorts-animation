The thieves’ leader opened a small shop in town and introduced himself as a new merchant. | He smiled often at customers, but waited for a chance to approach Ali Baba’s home.
The merchant got to know Ali Baba’s nephew, who worked near the market. | He helped carry boxes | and offered a small gift, until the young man invited him to dinner with the family.
The guest arrived in fine clothes. | Morgiana spotted his distinctive silver ring with the split blue stone, | the same as the escaped oil merchant’s.
Near the dinner table Morgiana heard his voice again, deepening her suspicion. | The guest asked to sit close to the door and kept glancing toward the courtyard as though looking for an escape route.
Morgiana visited the neighbors who had helped her before and asked them to alert the guards quietly. | She returned smiling, served the guest simple food, and began asking about his travels and friends.
He claimed he had never visited town, but Morgiana knew he was lying when he named the old alleys. | She asked about an oil merchant; his voice changed, and he nearly dropped his cup.
Morgiana recognized the leader and signaled Ali Baba. | Guards were on their way, but the guest spotted them at the window and excused himself.
The man rushed toward the courtyard. | “One moment, sir; you forgot your gift!” Morgiana called. | He stopped and turned.
