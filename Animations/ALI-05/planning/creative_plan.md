## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (0-17.7):** the leader is back in a second disguise. A band of light shows the audience who he is at once (0.5), so the suspense is never "who is he?" but "when will they see?". His smile switches on for customers and off behind their backs; a thought cloud shows Ali Baba's house.
- **Inciting (17.7-35):** he finds his way in: the nephew. A caught crate, a small gift, an invitation. Only we see the real grin under the bow (33.7). Tape transition THAT EVENING...
- **Reveal for Morgiana (35-51):** fine clothes, a grand salute, the sleeve slips: the ring (insert 42.2), and a memory panel of the oil merchant's knocking fist. Clue 1.
- **Rising suspense (51-69):** the full dinner room. His voice matches a remembered one (clue 2). He asks for the cushion by the door and measures the way out with his eyes (dotted route, clue 3).
- **Turn (69-80.6):** Morgiana acts: the neighbours, the boy sent for the guards, the gate barred, and her own switch-on smile at the door, the mirror of his.
- **The duel at the table (80.6-106.6):** plain food, sweet questions. "My first time in this town" is struck out by his own talk of Old Well Lane (clue 4). "Ever met an oil merchant?": the voice breaks, the cup jumps (clue 5).
- **Climax (106.6-113):** eye close-up, the one impact frame (109.54, THE LEADER!), the signal to Ali Baba behind the guest's back.
- **Reversal (113-122.6):** helmets at the window; he sees them, excuses himself and bolts.
- **Cliffhanger (122.6-137.8):** the courtyard. ONE MOMENT, SIR! YOU FORGOT YOUR GIFT! He stops a palm from the barred gate and turns, smile rebuilt. Helmets rise behind the wall. TO BE CONTINUED...
- **Emotional arc:** oily charm, cold patience (him); innocence (nephew, Ali Baba); alert, certain, playful, steel under a smile (Morgiana); panic under manners (him).
- **Moral:** a watchful, calm mind sees through a friendly mask.

## 3. Character acting
All characters on twos (`celStep(t, 12)`), blinks and lip flaps on threes (`celStep(t, 8)`).
- **The merchant** is two men. The mask: one `SMILE` flow key list replayed identically for every customer (9.40, 10.14, 10.76, 17.0, 18.9), stately walk, hands hidden in long cuffs. The leader underneath: the smile drops in one drawing (11.90), `brow 0.7`, `lid 0.35`, drumming fingers, heavy `footfalls`, too much strength with the crates (25.0), eyes that go to the door while the head stays with the host (`gazePlan`, saccades held 0.4 s). Under pressure the mask stays on the mouth while the eyes give him away (102.14, 116.98). Cloak and feather always three drawings late (`lagged`). The last turn (131.82) is eyes, blink, `tip` 0.12 to -0.04, weight to the back foot, and the smile rebuilt at half speed.
- **Morgiana** thinks with her eyes first; the body stops one drawing later and the scarf tail settles (`swing`). She never frowns at him: her weapon is the serving smile she puts on at 79.92 (neutral to smile over three drawings, shoulders dropping), kneeling grace, level pouring hands. Her tells are tiny: the tray rattle she masters (56.26), the pouring hand that stops (93.46), one slow blink in the close-up (108.06). The signal to Ali Baba is small and exact: two taps on her ring finger, a head tilt, a two-drawing hush.
- **The nephew:** open and quick, short bouncy steps, `hop` with `landSquash` and `blush` at the gift, a trusting nod, a puzzled head tilt at 98.08.
- **Ali Baba:** the warm host (pats the cushion, raises his cup), then the series double take (eyes, head, body) at 111.88 and the worry gesture, hand on chest, arm round the child.
- **Family and listeners:** five different idle periods at the table; the child is the only one who notices Morgiana's look (57.4) and giggles twice; the wife goes still at 112.3.
- **Neighbours and guards:** rolling pin, broom and pan raised on staggered beats; hushes 0.15 s apart; helmets on `sneakChain` with the accordion bump (114.7) and a ragged duck (117.8), the captain's plume one beat late.
- **Animals:** the cat hisses at him in the first scene and watches the barred gate in the last; the donkey's ears go flat as he arrives.
- **Moving holds:** `idleSway` on tip and head, `breathe` on sq, steam, lamp flicker, bead curtain, cloak hem, feather, scarf tail in every pause; `groundShadow {flat: true}` under every standing figure.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Dramatic irony:** we know from 0.5 s. Every smile, bow and long cuff is funny and uneasy because of it.
- **Two smiles:** his is mechanical and repeated; hers is chosen once, at the door, and beats his.
- **Five clues, counted:** ring, voice, seat, lanes, cup. The `CLUES` counter lets a child follow her reasoning.
- **The liar's balloon:** MY FIRST TIME IN THIS TOWN! stays hanging over him and is struck out by his own chatter.
- **Humour:** fake strain with the crates; the thought bubble of five little thieves swatted like a fly; plain lentils beside a feast; the cup juggle; the captain's plume above the sill.
- **Suspense:** his toe pointing at the door under the cloak; the hush in the street; the barred gate that only we and Morgiana know about; the hand a palm from the bar.
- **Series threads:** the ring pays off episodes 3 and 4; the barred gate, the cuff back over the ring and the helmets set up episode 6; the shop booth returns as Morgiana's shop.
- **Colour as story:** bright blue-sky market, apricot evening street, amber and teal dinner room with six cushion colours, lilac moonlit courtyard. Vermilion (Ali Baba), turquoise (Morgiana) and coral (the guest) are the three strongest notes at the table.
