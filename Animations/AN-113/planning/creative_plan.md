# AN-113 The Poor Man and His Friend in Need: creative plan

Film length 176.39 s of narration plus a 3.5 s THE END hold (179.89 s). 17 scenes, 168 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is taken from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (schema `creative_plan/1`); this file explains the direction behind it.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
**Logline.** A ruined man borrows 500 dinars from an old friend, opens a jewel stall, and is showered with sudden luck, until a sealed note reveals that every stroke of luck was his friend in disguise.

**Moral.** The finest help is given so that the one helped never has to bow his head: true loyalty protects a friend's dignity as well as his purse.

**Tone.** Warm, gently comic mystery. The audience should enjoy the windfalls with the hero, sense that something is slightly off (the blue clue, the slipped smiles), and feel the reveal as a quiet lump in the throat rather than a punchline.

**Conflict.** It is inside the hero: need against pride. He can hardly bring himself to ask (S2, S3), and the whole film is his friend's answer to that shame. There is no villain.

**Spine (beats).**
- B1 2.18 `خسر` (setup): A rich man loses everything; the room empties around him.
- B2 8.98 `نصحته` (inciting): His wife urges him to turn to an old trusted friend.
- B3 19.52 `فقر` (emotion): He confesses his poverty, ashamed.
- B4 24.34 `وأعطاه` (turn): The friend listens, then gives 500 dinars to start again.
- B5 33.70 `فاستأجر` (rising): He opens a stall in the jewel market and waits.
- B6 44.60 `ثلاثة` (suspense): Three strangers arrive and ask about his father.
- B7 58.78 `فشهدوا` (rising): Market neighbours vouch that he is the dead merchant's son.
- B8 69.02 `ثلاثون` (surprise): Two bags: thirty thousand dinars and jewels, his "inheritance".
- B9 97.14 `دفعت` (surprise): A lady pays 3,000 for 500 dinars of jewels.
- B10 104.68 `أبواب` (comic): The doors of fortune literally swing open behind him.
- B11 119.50 `رفض` (turn): He tries to repay; the friend refuses: it was a gift.
- B12 126.32 `ورقة` (suspense): A sealed note, to be opened only at home.
- B13 142.96 `أقارب` (reveal): The three men were the friend's relatives.
- B14 148.50 `أم` (reveal): The lady was the friend's mother.
- B15 152.40 `أرسلها` (climax): All the money and jewels came secretly from the friend.
- B16 161.96 `يحرجه` (emotion): He did it so his friend would never have to ask.
- B17 172.80 `وفاء` (moral): Not an inheritance but the fruit of a loyal friendship that kept his dignity.

**Surprises.** Three windfalls that look like luck (the bags at 66.1, the lady's 3,000 at 97.1, the doors at 104.7), one refusal (119.5) and one reveal in three steps (142.2, 147.7, 152.4). The film plays fair: every helper carries the friend's blue and lets one smile slip.

**Emotional arc.** Pride (0-2 s), shame (3-20 s), relief (24-29 s), hope (33-42 s), bewildered joy (64-108 s), pride restored as he comes to repay (109-117 s), puzzlement (118-131 s), shock and laughter (139-154 s), and a still, grateful understanding (165 s to the end).

**Ending.** After the last word (176.06) the man stands straight with the note pressed to his heart, his wife's hand on his arm, both looking toward the dusk window; the friend medallion and the heart stay upper left. THE END pops at 176.5 and holds until 179.89 with breathing, blinks and one lamp flicker.

## 2. Scene-by-scene storyboard
**S1 0.00-8.98 s** (P1, `home`, wide / push-in, 10 actions). Rich to poor in one breath: the full room empties around him in three pops, his yellow turban turns plain, the purse hangs limp. His wife comes in with an empty bowl. Sample cues: 0.00 `كان` full merchant room: shelves loaded, coin stacks on the desk, treasure glowing in the alcove; 3.82 `كله` slumps: shoulders drop, hands fall. Lettering: POOF!.

**S2 8.98-15.84 s** (P1, `home`, two-shot / static, 8 actions). She advises him: go to your old friend. A portrait of the friend pops between them; hope returns to his face. Sample cues: 8.98 `نصحته` sets the bowl on the stool and touches his sleeve; 12.74 `قديم` looks up at the portrait. Lettering: none.

**S3 15.84-29.42 s** (P1/2, `friendHouse`, two-shot / push-in, 14 actions). At the friend's house he tells his trouble with his head low. The friend listens to the end without interrupting, then gives him a purse of 500 dinars. Sample cues: 15.84 `فذهب` enters through the opening door and walks in, slowing; 22.98 `الصديق` finishes, lets his hand drop, dares one look up. Lettering: 500 DINARS.

**S4 29.42-33.70 s** (P2, `jewelMarket`, medium / track, 4 actions). He walks through the market weighing the purse; a round memory inset shows him as a young jeweller at a bench. Sample cues: 29.42 `وكان` walks along the street, tossing the purse lightly in one hand, thinking; 31.78 `صائغًا` in the inset the young man taps a ring with a tiny hammer, holds the gem to his eye. Lettering: TINK!.

**S5 33.70-42.86 s** (P2, `jewelMarket`, wide / push-in, 8 actions). He opens a stall in the jewel market, lays out a few gems, sits down and waits. Only a cat comes by. Sample cues: 33.70 `فاستأجر` hands two coins from the purse to a hand at the frame edge and gets a key; 38.56 `البضاعة` nudges one gem straight with a fingertip, steps back to admire. Lettering: JEWELS.

**S6 42.86-52.86 s** (P3, `jewelMarket`, wide / pan, 10 actions). Three strangers arrive, each with one blue item. They ask after his father; he tells them his father is dead. Sample cues: 42.86 `جاء` the three walk in from the left in a line, the spokesman first; 48.18 `أبيه` speech balloon pops with the father medallion and a question mark. Lettering: 1 2 3.

**S7 52.86-64.84 s** (P3, `jewelMarket`, wide / pull-out, 12 actions). Are you truly his son? He calls two market neighbours, who vouch for him. While he is looking at them, the strangers trade one quick satisfied glance. Sample cues: 52.86 `فسألوه` points at him, then at the portrait; 58.78 `فشهدوا` points at the man with a big open hand and nods hard. Lettering: HIS SON!.

**S8 64.84-74.44 s** (P4, `jewelMarket`, medium / push-in, 10 actions). Two heavy bags land on his counter: thirty thousand dinars, ornaments and precious stones. He is thunderstruck. Sample cues: 64.84 `عندئذ` steps aside with a small bow and an open hand toward the counter; 69.02 `ثلاثون` gold light bursts from the open bag, coins brim over; a counter spins up. Lettering: THUD!, 30,000 DINARS.

**S9 74.44-85.50 s** (P4, `jewelMarket`, two-shot / pull-out, 10 actions). The spokesman explains: your father left these with us in trust, and we return them to his lawful heir. They bow and go; the last one cannot resist a look back. Sample cues: 74.44 `قالوا` speaks with one hand raised, the other on his chest; 81.02 `وإنهم` turns his open hand from himself to the man. Lettering: IN TRUST, THE HEIR. Transition: SOON AFTER...

**S10 85.50-100.10 s** (P5, `jewelMarket`, two-shot / push-in, 12 actions). Soon after, an old lady in blue chooses jewels priced at 500 dinars and pays 3,000, waving away his protest. Sample cues: 85.50 `وبعد` transition card SOON AFTER... crosses the panel; 95.22 `دينار` nods and sets a fat purse on the counter. Lettering: 500, 3,000!.

**S11 100.10-109.34 s** (P5, `jewelMarket`, medium / pull-out, 9 actions). Alone, he stares at the pile, looks straight at us, and feels the doors of fortune opening: behind him the street doors swing open one after another, pouring gold light. Sample cues: 100.10 `تعجب` looks at the purses, at the bags under the counter, back at the purses; 104.68 `أبواب` door one of the street swings open: gold light inside. Lettering: none.

**S12 109.34-117.92 s** (P6, `friendHouse`, wide / push-in, 7 actions). He carries 500 dinars to his friend and holds the purse out with both hands: I have come to repay the loan. Sample cues: 109.34 `حمل` strides in through the door with the fat purse held in front of him in both hands; 112.82 `وقال` stops, takes a breath, and holds the purse out at arm's length. Lettering: 500, REPAID?.

**S13 117.92-131.92 s** (P6, `friendHouse`, two-shot / push-in, 13 actions). The friend refuses: it was a gift from the start. Then he gives him a sealed note: do not open it until you are home. Sample cues: 117.92 `لكن` raises a flat palm: stop; 125.02 `ثم` draws a small rolled note with a red seal from his sleeve. Lettering: A GIFT!.

**S14 131.92-139.20 s** (P7, `home`, medium / push-in, 6 actions). At home, with his wife beside him, he breaks the seal and finds verses that tell the truth. Sample cues: 131.92 `فتح` sits, takes the note from his sash and thumbs the seal; 136.44 `أبياتًا` four lines of verse write themselves on, each as two half-lines. Lettering: none.

**S15 139.20-154.34 s** (P7, `home`, wide / tilt, 14 actions). The reveal, played in a big thought cloud above the note: the three strangers were the friend's relatives, the lady was his mother, and the money and jewels were sent by him in secret. Each time the friend steps into the picture in his blue. Sample cues: 139.20 `الثلاثة` the cloud pops open; inside, the three strangers stand in a row at the stall exactly as in S6; 147.68 `كانت` the friend steps in beside her, bends and kisses her hand. Lettering: HIS RELATIVES!, HIS MOTHER!, HIS GIFT!.

**S16 154.34-165.34 s** (P8, `home`, wide / static, 10 actions). Why he did it: in the cloud the friend writes the note, then we see the whole plan as a map with him at the centre, and last the thing he spared his friend: asking for money face to face. Sample cues: 154.34 `كتب` cloud: the friend sits at his desk writing the note, tongue between his teeth in concentration; 160.00 `يساعده` a fourth spoke runs from the friend to a small figure of the man at his stall, standing tall. Lettering: THE PLAN.

**S17 165.34-179.89 s** (P8, `home`, close / pull-out, 11 actions). He understands: not an unknown inheritance, but the fruit of a friend's loyalty, which kept his dignity whole. He holds the note to his heart and stands straight. THE END. Sample cues: 165.34 `فهم` lifts his head; the surprise has gone, something quieter arrives; 172.80 `وفاء` a red heart pops beside the friend medallion and beats twice. Lettering: THE END.

## 3. Character acting
- **The man.** His body tells the arc: proud chest (S1 start), slump with `sq 0.95`, `tip 0.05`, heavy `lid` (S1 to S3), short slowing steps into the friend's house; then the bright shopkeeper (`sit 0.5`, clasped hands, looking left and right with `gazePlan`), thunderstruck (`eyes: 'wide'`, `PERSON_HANDS.cheeks`, `tremble`), the one look to camera at 101.6, and the long even stride of S12. At the end no big gesture: wet eyes, a closed small smile, the note at his chest (`PERSON_HANDS.clasp`), `sq 1.04` settling to 1.0 as he stands.
- **The friend.** Stillness is his character. While the man talks (17.2 to 23.6) he does not move except blinks and one slow nod, hand on chin: listening is shown as an action. He gives quickly and without ceremony (24.3), refuses with a flat palm and kind eyes (117.9), and is playful only once, wagging a finger over the seal (128.7). His secret smile and wink come only after the man has gone (131.3).
- **The three strangers.** Never in unison: `stagger(i)` on walks, bows and reactions. Spokesman (kin1): grave, controls the others with a look. kin2: eager, nearly gives the game away (61.3 to 62.8). kin3: young, watches kin1 for cues, looks back with a grin as they leave (85.2). In the cloud at 142.9 all three drop the act at once and become a cheerful family.
- **The lady.** Short quick steps, three decisive jabs of the finger, purses set down without looking up; she pats his hand like a mother and smiles only after she has turned away (99.4). In the cloud she pats her son's cheek (148.5).
- **The wife.** Practical warmth in S2 (hand on his sleeve, a firm nod); in S14 to S17 she is the audience's stand-in: she reads over his shoulder, gasps with `mouthCover`, and lays a hand on his arm at the last word.
- **Listeners always look.** In every two-shot both gazes are set with `lookAt` or `look()`; listeners blink and nod on stresses. Every pose change has `anticipate` before and `settleTo` after; objects have weight (purse dip, `impactSquash` on the bags, necklace `swing`).

## 4. Camera and composition
- World 1000 x 1230, ground and floors at y 900; action and faces stay in the upper part of the panel, lettering never covers a face.
- The two visits to the friend (S3 and S12) share marks and framing so the change in the man is the only difference.
- The market scenes keep one geography: strangers and the lady always come from the left, neighbours from the right, the man behind his counter at x 560. The street doors used at 104.7 are visible in the wide shots from S5 on, so they are not a surprise prop.
- Push-ins mark decisions and discoveries: the listening friend (21.0), the open bag (69.0), the purses (97.1), the note changing hands (125.7), his face over the verses (136.4), the final close-up (165.3). Pull-outs admit new people (56.6) or release a scene (82.5, 103.4, 171.7).
- The reveal is composed in two storeys: the thought cloud (800 x 520) above, the reading couple below, so the picture of the truth and the man's reaction are on screen together.
- One `later` card, SOON AFTER..., at 85.5 to 87.2, with the camera cut hidden at its midpoint.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Suspense and fair play:** the blue clue and four mask-off smiles (62, 85.2, 99.4, 131.3) let an attentive viewer suspect the truth; the cloud at 139 to 154 confirms it by flashing each blue item.
- **Humour:** riches going POOF in three pops; the only "customer" is a cat (41.1); the lady's purses keep landing after he has stopped smiling (95.9); the doors of fortune open literally and one makes him jump (107.3); the friend slaps his hand over the seal (128.7); the forehead slap at HIS MOTHER! (148.8).
- **Surprise:** the bag's gold light on his face (69.0); the counter landing on 3,000 (98.8).
- **Emotion:** the friend's unmoving attention; the purse pushed back to the man's chest (119.5); the grey struck-out picture of the begging that never had to happen (161.9); the father's picture gently replaced by the friend's with a heart (171.7); the man standing straight on the last word, "his dignity".
