Reviewed: `Originals/The Lark and the Elephant - Halftone Pop 9x16 - Noto Naskh Arabic.mp4` (195 s). I read 27 of the 33 strips at 4 fps in order: 0-24 s and 42-195 s. The six strips inside 20.2-46.5 that I skipped (24-42 s) belong to the approved section. I also pulled twelve larger stills (50.0, 61.8, 76.0, 99.5, 111.5, 118.0, 135.0, 153.5, 163.0, 165.5, 179.5, 188.0) and a 1 fps sheet of the approved v2 clip for style matching. The section 20.2-46.5 is not reviewed. Everything below must leave it untouched and must meet it cleanly: at 20.2 the lark stands at (430, 830) with the camera at {370, 722, 1.8}; at 46.5 the lark is crouched at (385, 878), the elephant is at x 520 facing right, and the camera is at {474, 720, 1.7}.

All times are film seconds. Positions are world units of `scene_d.js` (1000 x 1230; ground `GR` 900; pond centre x 215, reeds to x 316; nest ruin x 420; pit x 580..880, floor at elephant y `GR + 276`; left branch perch y 604, x 90..310; right branch perch y 604, x 730..930). ## 1. Diagnosis

### What works
- **One continuous set.** Pond left, nest centre, pit right, a tree at each side. The geography is learnable and the approved section already uses it well.
- **Broad sync of the big beats.** Nest on "صنعت عُشًّا" (7.2), eggs on "ووضعت" (10.1), the lark at his face by "الفيل" (48.0), crows arrive on "الفيل" (97.5), blind on "صار أعمى" (101.2), frogs leave on "ذهبت الضفادع" (146.0).
- **The style reads on a phone** in every shot at zoom 1.5 or more.
- **The approved section sets a clear standard:** eye lines, blinks, anticipation, weight, footfall dust, distinct poses, two words in 26 s. The rest of the film now looks like a different, older film beside it. That gap is the main job.

### Main weaknesses
1. **Outside the approved section nobody acts.** Pupils are fixed, nobody blinks, every listener is a statue. The elephant is a frozen cut-out with a faint smile for the whole 11 s plea (48.4-59.6). The three birds do not move while the lark tells them her chicks are dead (73.4-81.0). The crows listen for 8.5 s without a reaction (86.0-94.5). The frogs stare straight ahead for 15 s (131-146). In the last 28 s the trapped elephant never moves at all (167-195).
2. **Icons and lettering do the storytelling.** I count about 45 pieces of lettering or icons outside the approved section: three "!", four "CHEEP!", egg, dead-chick and small-bird bubbles, "YES!", "GRR!" with steam puffs, "SMALL TOO!", the bulb, the eye bubble with a red cross, "CAW!", about 14 "PECK!", "ROAR!", a clock, "?", a pit bubble, an arrow, "LOUD!", a drop bubble, about 10 "RIBBIT!", a red "!", a thought cloud, "WHOA!", "CRASH!", "STUCK!", "SMALL?!", "FRIENDS!", a "9 friends" counter, "TEAM!". Many name a feeling the actor should show, and several overlap (STUCK over CRASH at 165.2-167.2; PECK over ROAR at 101.5-102.5).
3. **The trap's logic is invisible (145.9-167.3).** The frogs croak on the near rim, under the elephant's belly (150.5-161). He is already standing beside them, so there is nothing to be lured to. On "سار نحو الصوت" (157.6-161) he walks over and past the sound. The fall is a rigid sprite that rotates and eases down for 2.9 s (161.8-164.7); "سقط في الحُفرة" finishes at 164.2 while he is still in the air. He lands standing. "ولم يستطع الخروج" is the word "STUCK!": he never tries to climb.
4. **The blind wandering has no blindness, no walking and no time (102.8-123.9).** He slides 170 units in 9 s (105-114), flips direction through a one-frame sliver four times (110.5, 115.2, 119.2, 122.2), and parks on top of the pond and the frogs with his tongue out (111-115 and 119.5-122): the thirsty elephant who "cannot find water" is standing in it, and the frogs under him do not react. A clock stands in for "وقتًا طويلًا". The tongue appears at 110.0, 5 s before "بعطش" (114.98). The ears never turn to a sound; a "?" does it.
5. **The appeal has no drama (46.5-70.8).** The lark hovers on a metronome bob for 22 s, on top of his mouth and tusk rather than at his eye, behind three icon bubbles. His mouth opens at 59.5, two seconds before "نعم!" (61.56), and the "YES!" balloon sits on the lark with its tail at her head, so it reads as her line. "غضبت" is "GRR!" and steam. "عرفت أنها لا تستطيع مواجهة الفيل وحدها" (64.4-70.8), the turn the whole plot hangs on, is six seconds of the same hover.
6. **The attack is a black blob (94.5-102.8).** Three crows travel as one clump, then sit overlapped on his forehead for 5 s (97.5-103.5). He does not react at all until the X eyes pop at 101.2. The pecks are told by a flickering "PECK!".
7. **The ending gloats and hides the loser (167.3-195).** His face is at the bottom-right edge, mostly out of frame. The lark hovers for 25 s. Three birds and three frogs land in one unreadable pile on the left rim (179.5 on), then everyone bounces in a loop for 10 s under "TEAM!" over a trapped, blinded animal. "وأنت الآن تعرف نتيجة غرورك" gets no reaction from him.
8. **Staging faults.**
   - His front foot hangs over the pit corner from 46.5 to 102 (he stands at x 520; the pit starts at 580).
   - His rump is the backdrop for the birds (72.5-83.5) and for the frogs (126-144).
   - The lark lands in the middle of the three birds (73.2) and on top of the frogs (126.2); in both scenes speaker and listeners are one overlapping lump.

### 1. The trap, the fall and "could not get out" (P1)
**Time and words:** 145.90-167.30. "ذهبت الضفادع إلى الحُفرة، وبدأت تصدر أصواتًا عالية. سمع الفيل أصواتها، وظن أن الغدير قريب. سار نحو الصوت لأنه كان عطشانًا، ثم سقط في الحُفرة العميقة، ولم يستطع الخروج."

**Problem:** weakness 3.

**Direction.** The elephant comes from off screen left, so the whole width of the set lies between him and the sound, and he walks **past the real pond, now silent, toward the croaking pit**. That single picture is the plan.

- **Frogs go, 145.90-150.4.** Each frog crouches before each hop (`anticipate`, `sq` 0.85) and squashes on landing; they leave 0.25 s apart and do not hop in step. They stop at the very lip, x 515, 545, 575, facing left (toward where he will come from). At 149.2 the lead frog peers over the edge into the pit (`duck` 10, `gaze` down), then looks back at the others. Camera: wide, {470, 760, 1.2}, so the empty pond is seen at the left as they leave it.
- **"تصدر أصواتًا عالية", 149.92-152.26.** Lead frog inhales (`sq` 1.1) and croaks on 150.46; the second joins at 150.9, the third at 151.3, never in unison. Sound arcs leave the frogs and travel **left** along the ground. Two "RIBBIT!" at most in the whole scene (150.5 and 153.4), size 44, in the sky above the frogs. Up on the left branch the lark and the three birds turn their heads to watch the left edge of frame.
- **"سمع الفيل أصواتها", 152.48-154.74.** His trunk tip and head enter at frame left on 152.5 (x −200 to 60 by 154.6): slow, groping steps, head low (`drop` 14), ears sagging, tongue out. On "سمع" (152.48) the arcs reach him: the near ear snaps up and pivots forward (`earUp` 0 to 0.8 with `settleTo`), he stops with one foot in the air, head lifts. Hold 0.4 s, completely still. Remove the red "!".
- **"وظن أن الغدير قريب", 154.74-157.58.** Trunk stretches toward the sound and sniffs (two short `reach` pulses). The tongue curls, ears flap twice with pleasure, he swallows. Keep the one pond thought cloud (it is the only place where a mistaken belief is the story), 154.9-157.2, small, above his head and clear of the branch birds.
- **"سار نحو الصوت لأنه كان عطشانًا", 157.58-161.98.** He strides from 60 to 470: no more groping. Head up, trunk straight out ahead, steps lengthening (`amp` 0.5 to 1), footfall dust and thuds from `footfalls`. This is his arrogance coming back, and it is what kills him. At about 158.8-159.6 his feet pass directly behind the real pond; he does not slow. Its surface gives one ripple from his footfall. Camera eases from the wide to {520, 790, 1.5} by 160.5, keeping the frogs and the dark pit ahead of him at frame right.
  - 160.9: the frogs see him bearing down: eyes wide, croaks get faster. At 161.2 they spring aside to the left, between and behind his front legs, and go silent mid-croak.
  - 161.14-161.9 ("عطشانًا"): he stops at the lip (front foot at about 572), lowers the trunk to drink where the sound was. The trunk tip goes down past ground level into the dark and finds nothing; it feels left and right in empty air. Ears forward, puzzled. Hold.
- **Teeter, 161.98-162.9 ("ثم").** He steps forward to reach further. The front foot (`lift` 0.6, then down) comes down on nothing and keeps going. Body tips forward (`rot` 0 to 0.22). The rear legs scrabble backward, two quick steps with dust. Ears fly up, the trunk whips up and back, the tail goes rigid. Two clods break off the lip. He hangs on the brink for 0.4 s. Remove "WHOA!".
- **Fall, 162.92-163.6 ("سقط").** Release on the word. `fallY` with real acceleration: 276 units in about 0.6 s. `rot` 0.22 to 0.5 and back toward 0.1 at the bottom; `sq` 1.07 stretched along the fall; ears and trunk trail upward. The camera follows with a key on the release and a key on the impact: {715, 1010, 1.5} at 163.6.
- **Impact, 163.6 (inside "في الحُفرة").** `impactSquash` for 2 frames (`sq` 0.8), `rebound`, settled by 164.3. He lands on his haunches and the front legs buckle (`drop` 16): not standing to attention. A column of dust puffs up out of the pit mouth. At the rim the frogs are bounced 12 units by the shock, 2 frames apart. Move the shake spring and the stamp/thud sounds from 164.7 to 163.6. Keep one "CRASH!" starburst, in the sky above the pit and clear of everyone, 163.6-164.5. Remove "STUCK!".
- **"ولم يستطع الخروج", 165.02-167.30.**
  - Try 1, 165.0-165.9: he rears (`rot` −0.3), front feet on the wall, trunk stretched up toward the rim. The tip stops about 60 units short of the edge. The feet slide down leaving two scrape lines and a few crumbs; he drops back with a small squash and a thud.
  - Try 2, 165.9-166.6: lower and weaker; he slides sooner.
  - 166.6-167.3: he sits back, head hangs (`drop` 18), ears droop (`earUp` −0.3), the trunk lies limp on the floor, one long breath out. The frogs lean over the edge and look down, then at each other.
  - Camera {715, 960, 1.45} from 165.0 so the rim, the trunk's reach and the gap are all in frame.

**Note on a rejected option.** The earlier review asked for the frogs on the far rim so that the pit lies between him and the sound. There is no way for frogs to cross a 300-unit pit in this side view without changing the set in approved frames. With the elephant now arriving from the far left, the near lip works: the sound is at the edge and the "water" he reaches for is one step beyond it.

### 2. Blind and lost: groping, the near misses, the long walk, the sounds (P1)
**Time and words:** 102.80-123.90. "صار الفيل لا يرى أمامه، ولا يعرف الطريق إلى الطعام والماء. مشى وقتًا طويلًا حتى شعر بعطش شديد. كان يسمع الأصوات حوله، لكنه لم يستطع أن يرى شيئًا."

**Problem:** weakness 4.

**Direction.** Play it as dramatic irony: food and water are within a trunk's length and he misses both. He loses his swagger in three visible stages: alarm, groping, exhaustion.

- **A blind gait (new, see section 3).** Every step is led by the trunk: tip taps the ground ahead, then the foot follows. Stride half the normal length (`amp` 0.4), a pause between steps, head low (`drop` 10 to 16), head sweeping slowly left and right, ears held out and still (listening). No `gaze`.
- **Real turns.** Replace all four one-frame sliver flips with `turnFlip` (0.6 s, squeeze no thinner than 0.7, head leads, trunk swings through last).
- **102.80-105.50 "صار الفيل لا يرى أمامه".** At x 430. The crows are gone. He swings the trunk at the air in front of him and meets nothing; turns his head left, right, left with the ears wide. One front foot feels forward and pulls back. From the right branch the lark watches without moving; one slow blink.
- **105.50-110.32 "ولا يعرف الطريق إلى الطعام والماء".** He turns left (105.5-106.1) and gropes left to x 250 by 110.3.
  - On "الطعام" (108.94) the trunk reaches up to the leaves and branch of the left tree and closes on air about 30 units below the branch. The three birds sitting there lean away, one after the other, and freeze.
  - On "والماء" (109.60) the trunk tip sweeps down and passes just above the reeds of the pond without touching the water. The frogs sink until only their eyes show (`duck`, as in the approved section at 24.5-26.5) and watch the trunk go by (`gaze` tracking the tip). Nobody makes a sound.
  - Camera {330, 720, 1.6}, held: his head, the branch birds and the pond in one frame.
- **110.32-113.22 "مشى وقتًا طويلًا".** He trudges on to the left, half out of frame by 112.3. On "طويلًا" (112.32-113.22) use the style's one `transition` wipe as the time cut (caption "LATER..." in the style's own lettering, or no caption). Remove the clock and its ticking. This is the film's single transition and it replaces an icon; it is not an added decoration.
  - After the wipe the path strip carries a tangle of his footprints crossing and looping back on themselves (drawn once, kept until 128): he has gone round in circles.
- **113.30-116.70 "حتى شعر بعطش شديد".** He is now at x 440 facing left, visibly worse: head lower (`drop` 16), ears sagging (`earUp` −0.3), trunk dragging a line in the dust, each step slower than the last, to x 400. The tongue comes out on "بعطش" (114.98), not before. On "شديد" (115.66) he stops, sways (`tip` ±0.03, slow), and his knees give a little (`sq` 0.96). Remove the falling sweat drops.
- **116.70-119.76 "كان يسمع الأصوات حوله".** Sounds come from sources we can see, and his ears answer before his head does.
  - 117.2: a crow on the right branch caws (beak opens; two small arcs at the crow). His near ear pivots back toward it (`earOff`), then the head half-turns, late. He takes one step backward toward it.
  - 118.4: a bird on the left branch chirps, ahead of him and above. Both ears swing forward, the head comes up, the trunk lifts to sniff.
  - 119.2: the lark shifts on her perch (one wing flick). His ears twitch back again. He stands pulled both ways, ears working separately.
  - Remove the "?" and the two free-floating arc sets.
- **119.76-123.90 "لكنه لم يستطع أن يرى شيئًا".** He chooses the chirp and walks left to x 370 (head under the branch). 120.6-121.8: the trunk reaches straight up and pats along the underside of the branch. The birds have shuffled to the far end; the trunk tip stops a hand from the red bird, who leans back wide-eyed and holds its breath. On "يرى" (122.80) the trunk gives up and drops with a `swing`. From 122.5 he moves on to the left and out of frame (x −140 by 128.6, about 84 units/s, behind the pond; frogs stay ducked while he passes).
- **The lark, 116.7-123.8.** Her `gaze` follows him. At 122.9 her eyes go from him down to the pond and the three pairs of eyes in it, and hold there. That look is the cut to the next scene.

### 3. The appeal, "Yes!", her anger and what she understands (P1)
**Time and words:** 46.46-70.82. "طارت إلى الفيل وسألته: «لماذا كسرت بيضي وقتلت فِراخي؟ هل فعلت هذا لأنني صغيرة وضعيفة؟» أجاب الفيل بغرور: «نعم!» غضبت القُبَّرَة، لكنها عرفت أنها لا تستطيع مواجهة الفيل وحدها."

**Problem:** weakness 5, and the foot over the pit.

**Direction.** Two faces, both readable, for the whole exchange. She acts the question; he acts not caring. No icon bubbles.

- **Flight in, 46.5-48.6 ("طارت إلى الفيل").** Angry flight: a straight, fast line with rapid flaps, up over his back and round to the front of his face. At 47.3 his ear flicks as she passes it. At 47.6 his eye turns to her (`gaze`). She pulls up with an air-brake a few units from his eye (`rot` −0.3, two back-flaps): he draws his head back and takes one step backward (x 520 to 470, a real footfall with dust), then recovers himself: `lid` to 0.5. He was startled for a moment and now hides it.
- **Her mark:** about (770, 712), facing left: 120 units in front of his eye and slightly above it. Never on his trunk root or tusk. Hover with a slow irregular drift (two `K.noise` terms, about 6 units), not `sin(t*3)`.
- **Camera:** {690, 700, 2.0} held from 48.6 to 56.8. Both heads fill the frame; the three crows on the branch above are in the top right corner as witnesses.
- **"لماذا" 49.98.** She leans in (`rot` 0.15), `brow` down, `crest` up, beak on the syllables (`talk`), a hard forward bob on the word.
- **"كسرت بيضي" 51.32-52.46.** She twists and throws one wing back toward the nest (new `point`, see section 3), looks back at it herself (`gaze` to (420, 890), `headRot`), and snaps back to him on "بيضي". His eye follows her wing toward the ruin for half a second, then returns to her, unbothered. Remove the egg bubble.
- **"وقتلت فِراخي" 52.64-54.12.** Her voice breaks in the body: `wet` 1, the wingbeat falters and she drops 15 units, catches herself, `crest` half down. Remove the dead-chick bubble.
- **"هل فعلت هذا" 54.12-56.16.** She steadies, chest out, holds his eye.
- **"لأنني صغيرة وضعيفة؟" 56.16-59.60.** On "صغيرة" (57.22) the camera eases out to {560, 700, 1.45} over 0.7 s: the whole elephant and the speck in front of his face. The size contrast is the question. She stays still in the air and waits. Remove the small-bird bubble.
- **The elephant while she speaks (48.6-59.6).** He is never frozen, and everything he does says "this does not matter": breathing; a slow blink at 50.5; the glance at the ruin at 52.0; an ear flap at 53.4, as at a fly, exactly when she falters; the trunk tip idly curling at 54.5; at 56.0 his eye leaves her and looks over her head (`gaze` up-right), chin lifting (`drop` −8); the tail swishing throughout. Mouth shut.
- **59.2-59.6:** nothing moves except her wings. Her question hangs.
- **"أجاب الفيل بغرور" 59.60-61.56.** His eye comes back down to her, slowly (60.0-60.5). On "بغرور" (60.82) the anticipation: head rears up and back (`drop` −14, `sq` 1.04), eyes to the `smug` line, trunk curling up.
- **"نعم!" 61.56-62.24.** The mouth opens **on the word**, the head thrusts at her and the trunk flicks out: a snort. Three short air lines. She is blown back about 80 units to the right, tumbling once (`rot` through a full turn), feathers ruffled (`crest` splayed), and recovers her hover at about (850, 700) by 62.2. Keep "YES!": it is the one spoken word in the scene. Put the balloon above his forehead at about (560, 520) with the tail to his mouth, never over her.
- **62.24-63.0.** He turns his face away from her, eyes shut, one ear flap. Done with her.
- **"غضبت القُبَّرَة" 63.00-64.36.** She darts back at him and stops 40 units from his eye: `brow` fully down, `crest` spiked, body puffed (`sq` 1.08 wide), wings beating hard, a `tremble` of 2 units. He does not open his eyes. Remove "GRR!" and the steam.
- **"لكنها عرفت أنها لا تستطيع مواجهة الفيل وحدها" 64.36-70.82.**
  - 64.4-66.6: the fury stalls. Her eyes travel down him: his head, his shoulder, his leg, his foot (a `gazePlan` of four holds). The camera pulls back with the look to {540, 720, 1.3} by 67.0.

  - 69.6-70.8 ("الفيل وحدها"): she looks around the empty air on both sides of her, then her eyes go left and stop on the far tree (hold from 70.2). That look starts the next scene.
  - From 66.0 he lowers his trunk to a tuft of grass and starts to eat (item 10).

### 4. The crows' attack (P1)
**Time and words:** 94.48-102.80. "وافقت الغِربان، وطارت إلى الفيل، ونقرت عينيه حتى صار أعمى."

**Problem:** weakness 6.

**Direction.** One crow at a time, clear silhouettes, and an elephant who goes from contempt to panic. The contact itself is always covered by a 2-frame flash, in the same spirit as the dust that hides the nest in the approved section.

- **"وافقت الغِربان" 94.48-96.56.** The crows look at each other (lead to No. 2, No. 2 to No. 3, 3 frames apart). ## 4. Success criteria

1. **Mute test for the trap (145.9-167.3).** With sound and subtitles off, a new viewer can say: the frogs left their pond and croaked at the pit; the elephant heard them from far away, walked past the real pond toward the sound, stepped into the pit, tried to climb out twice and failed.
