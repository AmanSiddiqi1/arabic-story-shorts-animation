# ALI-03 "The Marked Door" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 2 (v2)

Film reviewed: `Animations/ALI-03/renders/ALI-03_v2.mp4` (147.5 s). Sources: 15 of the 25 contact strips in `review/v2_pack/strips/`, 84 panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited in round 1 and in `revision_1.md`, a frame-by-frame tile of the tape transition (v1 and v2), and an ffmpeg freeze scan of the story panel. Times are seconds on the film.

Not checked: sound was not listened to. Stepped cel timing (twos and threes), blinks, landing squash and follow-through fall between sampled frames and cannot be judged from stills. Strips 48-54, 66-84, 114-126 and 132-144 were covered by stills only, not read frame by frame. The freeze scan (blurred panel, threshold 0.08%, 0.8 s minimum) found no frozen stretch; it is a coarse test, so a very small moving hold would pass it.

No blockers. The round 1 blocker is gone: no red dot on any face or in any open doorway (56.3, 108.3, 135.3, 142.6-147.3 checked). The notes below are polish; notes 1 to 3 are worth doing if another pass is made, the rest are optional.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 60.5-61.6 s: the first copied cross is drawn with Morgiana and the neighbour merged
- **What is wrong.** At door 2 Morgiana stands on top of the old neighbour (stills 60.9 and 61.6: two heads on one body), facing the camera. The counter ticks to 2 and 3 while the hand that draws cannot be seen. Round 1 note 11, not done.
- **Why it matters.** This is Morgiana's trick, the idea the whole episode turns on. The viewer should see her make the mark.
- **Fix.** Keep the neighbour a clear step left of door 2 (x about 575) from 59.6 to 62.0, broom still, hand to her mouth, head following Morgiana. Morgiana stops right of the door (x about 720), turned to the wall (`dir` 1, small `tip` in), arm reaching to the cross position, one fast double stroke on "ورسمت" 60.78, scarf tail arriving late, eyes already on the next door. Same pose at doors 1, 4 and 5.

### 2. 75.2-84.2 s: cross 1 is still hidden
- **What is wrong.** The cross beside door 1 is a sliver between the saffron man's pointing sleeve and the leader's cape (native still 77.0). Only cross 2 reads, so the leader appears to look at one mark and the three men.
- **Why it matters.** WHICH DOOR?! needs two identical marks in view; one mark looks like the right answer.
- **Fix.** Move the three men about 80 units left (x 170..315) and drop the saffron man's raised arm until 80.9, or move the leader to x about 560, so a hand's width of bare wall shows around cross 1 (x 460). Keep its pulse.

### 3. 86.1-86.5 s: NEXT DAY... is on screen for about five frames
- **What is wrong.** The caption fades in and out inside the white flash and is fully readable for roughly 0.15 s (frame tile 86.1-86.5). It was the same in v1; round 1 missed it.
- **Why it matters.** It is the film's only time-passing card. The narration says "the next day", so the story survives, but the card is wasted.
- **Fix.** Keep the tape transition as it is and add the same words as a held label or small chrome title over the glade from 86.4 to about 87.6 (top of the panel, clear of the heads), fading before the leader's arm throw at 87.92.

### 4. 119.6-121.8 s: the balloon sits on the window gag
- **What is wrong.** ...IS CLEVERER THAN US! covers the window box and the lower half of the window (stills 120.2, 121.8), right under the turquoise slit, which is about 10 px wide. The slit closing as he looks up is easy to miss.
- **Why it matters.** It is the scene's irony: the cleverer person is directly above his head.
- **Fix.** Move the balloon left and lower, over the bare wall above the men (centre x about 760, below the lamp line), tail to the leader. Widen the slit to about 24 px with a small glint on the scarf at 120.0, and let the cat's head turn down on 121.64.

### 5. 116.2-127.5 s: the men still overlap and are cut by the left edge
- **What is wrong.** Four men stand in about 40% of the frame width, overlapping by a third of a body; the leftmost is half out of frame (stills 116.5, 123.0, 126.2). The silhouettes do differ now.
- **Fix.** Either drop to three men in this framing or widen the row to x 470..800 with a gap of bare wall between the nearest man and door 3. Low priority.

### 6. 21.9-23.2 and 25-34 s: the spy draws in the air and holds a white ball
- **What is wrong.** He faces the camera in the doorway and strokes at chest height (still 22.0); the chalk reads as a glowing white ball at his chest through 26.5-33 (stills 26.5, 31.5).
- **Fix.** Stand him right of the door, turned to the wall, arm at the cross position for the two strokes on 21.90 and 22.60. Draw the chalk as a short stick and pocket it on "ابتسم" 26.10.

### 7. 104.3-105.2 s: HEE HEE letters pile up
- The two words overlap into "HEHEE" over the shutter (stills 104.7, 104.9). Space them: the first HEE rising left of her head, the second 0.3 s later to the right and higher, each about 30 px, fading as it rises.

### 8. 138.2-141.4 s: one mule
- Still one whole mule and the nose of a second (still 139.5). "Animals laden with jars" would read better with two whole mules in frame on "دواب" 138.86: hold the camera about 120 units further left until 140.0, then follow the merchant.

### 9. 7.0-16.9 s: market floor
- The lower 40% of the panel is plain brick. Two or three foreground items (baskets, a sack of oranges, pigeons that hop aside as the spy passes) would fill it.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Marks parented to a door leaf** (`tsMarks` with `open`, `doorMark` `sx`): proven in this revision; move it to the Library with the series town.
- **Balloon clamp and thin tail** (`abSay` clamp, `thin`): now used in ALI-02 and ALI-03; it belongs in the shared speech helper.
- **Counter tied to object flares** (70.2-74.4): the counting pan can no longer run out of time.
- **Second-night plate** (`TS_MODEL.night2` with `a3AmberMoon`): a clean example of telling two nights apart by repainting, not tinting.
- **Held caption after a tape transition:** the transition's own caption is too brief to read at this length; a standard held label for the first second of the new scene would fix it for every film (note 3).
