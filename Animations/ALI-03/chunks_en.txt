The thieves’ leader sent a man to town to find Ali Baba’s house. | The man wandered through the market asking questions until a clue led him into a narrow street.
The spy stopped outside Ali Baba’s house and drew a little white chalk mark beside the door. | He smiled to himself: “I’ll return tonight, and the leader will recognize this place from the mark.”
But Morgiana, the clever woman who helped the household, noticed the mark when she returned from market. | She looked around and thought, “Why would someone choose only our door? | There must be a secret behind it.”
Morgiana brought chalk and copied the mark onto neighboring doors. | When the spy returned with the thieves after dark, | he found the whole street bearing the same white mark.
The leader angrily asked the spy, “Which door do we want?” | The man could not answer. | They withdrew, and the next day the leader sent another spy with a different mark.
The second spy placed a red dot on Ali Baba’s door and walked away. | Morgiana saw it from the window, | smiled quietly, and painted matching red dots on all the other doors.
The thieves returned, but still could not identify the house. | “Someone on this street is cleverer than we are,” said the leader. | He decided to come himself instead of sending a third spy.
Morgiana warned Ali Baba to beware of strangers. | That evening, a man arrived leading animals laden with oil jars and asked to stay the night.
