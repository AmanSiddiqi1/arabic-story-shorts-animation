## Notes (all optional polish, most useful first)

### 1. 134.5-137.5 s (and 62.5-63.0, and the same set at about 26.5-37.0): flat cobalt sea block beside a ruled beach edge
- **What is wrong.** At 136.6 and 137.2 the sea left of the beach is one flat cobalt rectangle covering about 40% of the width and the bottom 35% of the panel, and the sand ends in a straight vertical edge like a quay. This is the SIND-01 "no empty flat water low in the picture" lesson, and it sits under the reunion.
- **Fix.** One change to the cove set serves all three places: a wet sand band and a turquoise shallow strip along the edge (curved, not ruled), a foam line running up and back on a 3 s cycle, three or four swell lines with foam ticks in the cobalt, and two plank ends or a barrel rocking in the shallows. No new acting needed.

### 2. 60.5-62.5 s: the builders are still one knot
- **What is wrong.** At 61.0 the captain, Sinbad, teal and mustard overlap on about 250 units; Sinbad's leaning body covers mustard's arm and teal's body, and the raft is hidden behind their feet until 61.7.
- **Fix.** Move the captain 120 units left and mustard 100 units right of Sinbad for 60.5-62.5, so Sinbad's plank drag is in the clear; start the first plank on the water 40 units right of the group so each counter step shows a plank arriving.

### 3. 104.7-107.0 s: the lying pose reads as stacked blocks
- **What is wrong.** In the close-up the head rests past the stern with the turban hanging over the water, and the two forearms are parallel white capsules above and below the chest, so the body reads as a box rather than a tired man. The beat still works (closed eyes, glowing pebble).
- **Fix.** Shift the lie pose another 40 units toward the cargo so the turban rests on the deck or sack; drop the far arm to the deck along his side and keep only the near hand on the pebble; brows lifted in the middle on "وهو" 105.86 should be visible at this size (thicker brow stroke).

### 4. 147.2-151.4 s: the end push crops the captain and crowds the sailors
- **What is wrong.** At 149.8 the captain is cut by the right frame edge and the four sailors lean in a pile against Sinbad; the lower 40% of the panel is quay wall.
- **Fix.** Aim the push 80 units further right and 60 units lower (or stop at zoom 1.15) so the captain is whole and the ground sits lower; stand the sailors upright 90 units apart. If there is time, Sinbad kneels to his sister's height on "جميعًا" 146.32.

### 5. 112.7-122.7 s: finding and ride still knotted (R1 note 7)
- At 116.0 Sinbad, the child, three farmers, the basket, the waterwheel and the sheep overlap behind reeds; at 121.5 the donkey is hidden and only Sinbad's head shows above the farmers. Fix as in round 1: farmers 120 units apart, waterwheel 250 units right; on the ride put the donkey with Sinbad in clear profile ahead of the farmers, nobody overlapping it.

### 6. 75.7-80.7 s: the five waves (R1 note 9)

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Underwater band for cutaway water** (`sbTunUnder`): mirrored squashed wall reflection at 30%, a hard-edged deep band, submerged rocks with gem tips, streaks and foam fixed to the strip so they carry its speed, and one living thing per chapter. It turned the flat slab into the fullest part of the frame; worth a Library function and a Capabilities Guide entry.
- **Sound made visible at its source** (`SB6_WALLHIT`): a lip runs in, a burst on the spoken verb, a smaller echo on the next noun, lettering beside the hit, eyes then head to each.
- **Stepped, rimless lamp disc** with a broken gold reflection column of hard dashes on threes: the cel way to light a dark place without a tint.
