# SIND-06 library proposals (for the coordinator; Library/ is not edited from this story)

All in this story's `src/`, one function per drawing with header comments. Proposed after approval:

1. **mountainCove** (`src/sb_cove.js`: `sbCoveBack`, `sbCoveFront`, `sbCoveSea`, `sbCoveStream`, `sbWaterfall`, `sbCoveRiver`, `SB_COVE`):
   shipwreck shore ringed by cliffs, waterfall, stream to a cave mouth, live pebble glints. For any hidden cove or cave entrance.
2. **riverTunnel** (`src/sb_tunnel.js`: `sbTunnel`, `sbTunWater`, `sbRoofY`, `sbTunWY`, `sbChapterPlate`): underground river in cutaway,
   six chapters, dim/lit plates shown through a lantern circle (colour without tinting the cast). Plus **bat** (`sbBat`).
3. **riverVillage** (`src/sb_village.js`: `sbVillage`, `sbVillageFront`, `SB_VIL`): river's end, reed pool, canals, waterwheel, fields,
   mud brick village, far palace.
4. **raft** (`src/sb_raft.js`: `sbRaft`, `sbRaftPt`, `sbRaftPole`, `sbRaftFloat`) with build/pole/flag/lamp/cargo/rider and anchors.
5. **duck pose** (`src/sb_raft.js`: `sbDuck`): a person folded flat on the knees (fold 0..1).
6. **gemPebbles** and **oilLamp** (`src/sb_gems.js`: `sbPebble`, `sbPebbleList`, `sbPebbles`, `sbPebbleHeap`, `sbLantern`, `sbLampGlow`, `sbLampR`).
7. **currentStreaks**, **rockCoast** (+ `sbRockTeeth`, `sbLedgeBirds`), **dhow wreck states** (`sbDhowWreck`, `sbDebris`) and the
   **eyelid point of view** (`sbLidPOV`) in `src/sb_wreck.js`.
8. Small props (`src/sb6_common.js`): crab, leaf, tally marks, signal oar flag, camp shelter, hoe, basket, bowl, robe of honour.
