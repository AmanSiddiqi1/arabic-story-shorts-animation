# SIND-06 The River Beneath the Mountain: creative plan (80s Cel Anime)

Style `cel-anime-80s`, staged to the owner direction in `Library/styles/cel-anime-80s/look.md` (reference film ALI-01-ANIME v4) and to the series bible in `Animations/SIND-01/planning/creative_plan.md` section 6. Narration 147.37 s, last word 146.83 s, end hold 4.0 s, film 151.37 s. 20 scenes, 171 actions, 20 lettering items (title and THE END included), 8 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. The full storyboard is in `creative_plan.json`; this file gives the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (0-11.5):** the series opening on the Baghdad quay, then a happy crew on a glass calm sea among far islands. Sinbad's open armed breath at the bow.
- **Inciting (11.5-19.2):** the fishing line swings round by itself; pale streaks run through the water; the ship is carried, not sailed, and rose, ochre and slate mountains lift out of the horizon.
- **Wreck (19.2-26.5):** the captain's heave on the tiller comes too late. Impact frame CRASH! on the word (23.52), the mast falls, the hull opens, six figures jump clear.
- **Trapped (26.5-37.2):** everybody reaches a ribbon of sand (head count, ALL SAFE). The pull-out shows cliffs on three sides. A failed climb. NO WAY OUT.
- **Discovery (37.2-55.3):** Sinbad alone gets up and searches. One drop in the waterskin, a crab under the barrel, then the sound of water: a stream that runs away from the sea. A leaf shows where it goes: a low arch under the whole mountain. Then the banks begin to glitter.
- **Decision (55.3-75.8):** the thought picture (river through the mountain, houses beyond), the raft built from the wreck, the captain's lantern, the heap of pebbles. The crew look into the hole and stay; a signal flag, a fire, the first tally mark. "SEND HELP, SINBAD."
- **The tunnel (75.8-108.7):** the current takes the raft. Daylight shrinks to a coin; the roof comes down; he folds flat under a rock lip and rides a rapid on the rope loops. Then stillness: echoes, bats, no light either way. Hours pass in one shot (crystal gallery, a sky of glow worms) while the lantern burns down. He lies back, closes his eyes and hopes; a point of daylight he does not see grows until the frame is white.
- **Sunlight (108.7-123.0):** eyelids open on the sun and three faces. Farmers, the river's end, water, bread, a donkey ride to the village.
- **Loyalty (123.0-137.7):** the king honours him; Sinbad does not look at the robe and asks for a ship. The royal dhow returns to the cove, where the men who waited see the sail.
- **Home (137.7-151.4):** DAYS LATER..., the Baghdad quay in gold light. As he tells his family, a story balloon shows the thanks and the farewell to the farmers. The last pebble goes to his sister. THE END.
- **Conflict:** one man against rock, dark and time. **Moral:** choose the unknown way with care, and do not count yourself saved until your friends are.
- **Emotional arc:** content, alarmed, shaken, shut in, curious, resolved, parted, tense, awed, worn out, hoping, dazzled, grateful, loyal, home.

## 3. Character acting
All pose channels on twos (`celStep(t, 12)`); lids, glints, foam and flame on threes (`celStep(t, 8)`). Nobody is frozen: `idleSway`, `breathe`, ribbons with `lagged`, per member seeds.
- **Sinbad:** as the bible: chest and arms lead, eyes before head before body (`gazePlan`, a blink on each turn), turban tail and sash arrive late. Resolve tell: he tightens his sash (37.21). Concentration tell: tongue out (63.4). Fear is stillness: one foot held in the air when he hears the water (40.26). Tiredness in three steps in S14: upright, hugging knees, chin on knees, then two nods (`flow` NOD, the second deeper) before he lies down in two stages (elbow, shoulder). Hope at 105.86: brows lifted in the middle, wet lashes, the fist closing on the pebble. In the hall he never looks at the robe (127.64).
- **The captain:** weight and economy, the same man as SIND-01. `anticipate` before the heave on the tiller; counts heads with one finger; sits down weight first. His gift of the lantern (65.9) and the forearm grip (74.9) are his whole speech. He laughs once in the film, at 136.7, shoulders first, beard after.
- **The four sailors:** fixed tempers. Mustard: big, helpful, overdoes every push. Teal: oldest, practical, arms crossed. Rose: youngest, climbs, slips, hides, spots the sail, kisses the quay. Leaf: quiet lookout, lights the fire. Always `stagger(i)`; their four refusals and five waves are never in time.
- **The farmers:** the old one slow and warm (hand on chest, beard wags after the words); the tall one talks with his arms (the mime at 116.9); the round one offers food before anyone asks. The child is curiosity: upside down in the waking view, a poke at the glowing pebble, skipping ahead of the donkey.
- **King and vizier:** the king round and generous (leans in, crown slips, comes down the dais himself); the vizier one beat late in everything, then first to the door.
- **Animals:** crab (one wave of a claw), frog (croaks as the leaf passes, later stares into the cave), bats (one eye, one wing, a shuffle, each on its own seed), donkey (ears before head, a short bray), sheep, doves, the SIND-02 cat.

## 4. Camera and composition
Owner direction first: wide where the place matters, pans across detailed sets, the lower picture never empty, close views only at peaks.
- **Wides at zoom 0.72 to 1.15:** quay, sea, the cove pull-out to 0.72 (30.76) that shows the ring of cliffs, the tall gallery at 0.9, the river end at 0.9, the whole hall.
- **Pans carry the discovery:** the long pan right with Sinbad across the cove (37.2 to 44.4), then with the leaf to the cave mouth (to 46.3): the ALI-01 cave pan is the model. The pan with the donkey to the village (119.8 to 122.2).
- **Vertical moves, twice only:** up the sea cliffs (17.14) and up the mountain over the little arch (47.24), both for scale.
- **Tunnel:** a side cutaway tracking shot with the raft fixed at screen x 430 and the strip sliding (260, then 90, then 700 units per second). Never closer than 1.2. The lantern's lit circle is the composition.
- **Close views, three only:** the pebble insert (51.84, 0.9 s), the push to 1.2 on his face as the lids close (104.3), the eyelid point of view (108.68 to 112.8).
- **Impact frame:** CRASH! (23.52) only.
- **Screen direction:** the outward journey always runs screen right (ship, current, stream, raft, donkey); only the rescue ship travels right to left.
- **Echo framings:** S1 is the series opening; S19 and S20 repeat it in gold light.
- Faces and key action stay between world y 250 and y 900; balloons sit in the sky or wall band and point at the speaker.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Suspense:** the fishing line that turns by itself; the swing of the bow that is plainly too slow; the rock lip coming at head height; the ember that might go out.
- **Dramatic irony:** the point of daylight he does not see (107.14).
- **Surprise:** the stream that runs the wrong way; the glints starting at his own foot; the white bloom that turns out to be the sun.
- **Humour:** one drop from the waterskin; the crab; rose's three grabs at a rolling pebble; measuring the raft by lying on it; mustard's push; the vizier; the kiss on the quay; the cat and the light spot.
- **Emotion:** the captain's hand on the lantern; five different waves from the bank; the fist round the glowing pebble; the captain's laugh; the pebble in the sister's hands.
- **Setup and payoff:** the lantern (gift, light, ember); the pebble held to the sun by Sinbad (52.6), by the farm child in the balloon (143.7) and by his sister (149.6); SEND HELP, SINBAD. to SEND MY SHIP!; the first tally mark (73.0) to the wall of them (136.16); the star flag.
- **Colour as story:** fresh morning, blue sea, rose and ochre cliffs at midday, a tunnel of plum, teal and rose rock with five gem colours and gold lamp light, the full green of the fields, magenta and gold at court, gold homecoming. Plates change; the cast never takes a tint.

- Six people and a raft being built share S9 and S10: keep helpers simple and staggered.
- The farewell to the farmers plays inside a story balloon at half scale; keep it to five figures, a donkey and the ship.
- Seven short pauses only: 8 brief effects, none on the impact frame, a silent transition.
