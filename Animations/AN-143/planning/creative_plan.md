# AN-143 Ja'afar the Barmecide and the Old Badawi: creative plan

Film length 165.53 s narration + 3.5 s THE END = 169.03 s. One location (the Library `desertRoad` strip), 11 scenes, 149 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue word and time is in `creative_plan.json`, which is the authority when the two files differ.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

- **Setup (0-19 s).** The caliph rides out of Baghdad with Jaafar, Abu Nuwas and a companion. Ahead: an old Bedouin leaning on his donkey. They stop.
- **The victim (19-38 s).** From Basra, going to Baghdad for eye medicine; he can hardly see. We must like him here: tired, polite, one sore eye, a donkey that looks after him. A 1.5 s double-vision POV shows the riders as he sees them (he never knows one of the blobs is the caliph).
- **The order (38-57 s).** Harun wants a laugh and orders Jaafar to tease him. Jaafar foresees a harsh answer (thought cloud: the plant for the payoff). The caliph insists. Jaafar goes over on foot.
- **The bait (57-76 s).** "What will you give me for a cure?" The old man offers a sincere prayer and shows an empty purse. Jaafar's smile slips for one beat: he knows this is unkind.
- **The mock prescription (76-117 s).** Wind, sunlight, moonlight, lamplight; mix; three months in the air; a bottomless mortar; a cracked dish; nine months of nothing; "and your eye is cured!" The comedy is double: the absurd recipe drawn straight-faced in a picture balloon, and underneath it the old man's face going from eager, to doubtful, to a flat look at the camera, to a fist.
- **The turn (117-122 s).** He understands. Anger (flush), then a sly smile: he has a better answer.
- **Climax (122-133 s).** He stretches out on the donkey, feet toward Jaafar, a long held silence, then the blast (128.38): "take this as payment for your prescription now."
- **Second instalment (133-142 s).** If cured, he will send a servant to blacken Jaafar's face after his death: told in the old man's own picture balloon, mirroring Jaafar's.
- **Release (142-153 s).** FOOL!, curses, and the caliph, who has been holding it in since 96 s, explodes.
- **Resolution and moral (153-169 s).** The caliph admires the wit and gives 3000 silver dirhams. The old man lifts his arms to the sky exactly as in his prayer at 68.44 (the prayer was answered through the mocker's own master) and rides on to Baghdad. The joke ended in his favour.

Conflict: rank and cleverness against poverty and plain wit. Emotional arc: sympathy, discomfort, delighted revenge, warmth. Surprises: the fart, the balloon turned against Jaafar, the reward.

## 3. Character acting

- **Jaafar: a mask.** Courtier smile on (55.84), off only when the victim cannot see (smirk back at the caliph 64.96, mouth twitch 79.08). Hand on chest and chin up for the false physician. After the blast he is all reaction: `tip -0.2`, `PERSON_HANDS.shield`, turban hop with `settleTo`, nose pinched, `wet`, shrinking `sq` with each curse, backing with real footfalls. His one honest beat is 70.02 (gaze drops).
- **The Bedouin: slow, then precise.** Stoop (`age 0.9`), `wink: 'R'` as the bad eye with `soreEye` marks, peering by leaning (`tip 0.08`) and shading the good eye. Listening arc in S6-S7: cupEar and nods; nod stops halfway (85.44); beard scratch, `lid 0.35` (88.64); counts on fingers (91.72); mouth `o` (95.28); look to camera with one slow blink (106.48, the only lens stare he gets); fist (110.18). The turn at 121.26-121.98 is flush, then a one-sided smile. The climb has `anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash`; the strain is `sq 0.93` with `tremble`; release `sq 1.06` and bliss. At 161.02 he repeats the prayer gesture of 68.44.
- **The caliph: a fuse.** Grin (39.68), hidden shaking (96.76), held breath (132.92), three rising hitches (149.66), explosion (150.62: `tip -0.18`, mouth open, `lid 1`, `wet`, armsOut, a `lurch` that nearly unseats him). He must never laugh openly before 150.62.
- **The donkey: the witness.** Ears find sounds before the head (15.4); `headTurn` between speakers (28.64); stare to camera at the lamp (88.64) and before the blast (126.44); flat ears at 9 months; hop and `bray` on the blast; `smirk` at 141.28; sinks under each sack and grins; the lifted tail at 164.58.
- **Abu Nuwas and the companion.** Never in unison (`stagger`): the poet smirks first and collapses first; the companion honestly tries to follow the recipe and laughs last.
- **Secondary motion.** Keffiyeh tail and robe hems lag head moves and stir in wind; crown jiggles a beat late; sacks lag the donkey's bob by 2 frames; horse ears and tails idle on separate seeds. `LIFE.t = t` for blinks; held shocks suppress them.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour by contrast:** the grander Jaafar's gravity, the emptier the bowl. Every recipe object visibly holds nothing (cup, bowl, mortar, dish, spoon).
- **Running witness:** the donkey says what the old man is too polite to say, until he says it himself.
- **Suspense:** 125.54-128.38 is total stillness except a tremble and the donkey's eyes sliding to camera; Jaafar leans in at exactly the wrong time.
- **Plant and payoff:** the thought cloud at 50.58 (a burst flattening tiny Jaafar) pays off at 128.38 and 146.48; the prayer at 68.44 pays off at 161.02; Jaafar's CURED! flourish is mocked by the Bedouin at 134.92; Jaafar's balloon is answered by the Bedouin's balloon.
- **Emotion:** the empty purse and the sincere prayer make the teasing uncomfortable, so the reversal is earned. The ending is warm: the caliph admires him; nobody is hurt but Jaafar's nose and pride.
- **Last gag:** the lifted tail and the flinch at nothing.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- The narration is continuous after 30 s: effects are few, and the raspberry sits on a word boundary.
- Faces are small in the 1.0 wides; acting is carried by the closer shots listed above.
- `reclineOnMount` is untested on a mount; fallback: sit astride and lean far back (`tip -0.5`).
- Riders are front-facing figures; "looking back" is gaze plus a small head offset.
- The `poet` preset, `iconBalloon`, `stinkLines`, `flush`, `person laugh`, `templeTap`, `purse`, `coinPile` are planned assets, not yet in `Library/`.
- No LATER transition by design (plancheck warns).
- Owner questions (8) are in the JSON: the English omits three Arabic clauses (SCRIPT), the staging of the fart, the icon-only threat, wordless curses, the reclining pose, additions beyond the script, the lettering list, and the absence of a LATER card.
