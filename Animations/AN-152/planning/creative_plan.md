## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A king in disguise is tempted at a poor woman's door. She answers him with a book instead of a scream. Later a parable about a field and a lion clears her name and mends her marriage.

**Conflict.** Power against conscience (the king and the wife), then fear against trust (the husband and the wife). Nobody is a villain: the king is ashamed, the husband is afraid of a king, not angry at his wife.

## 3. Character acting

- **Wife** (`villageWoman`, blue dress). Her power is stillness. Level brows, closed mouth, steady `gaze`; she smiles only at بلطف 15.36 and from 154.6. Key moments: the arm-length offer of the bowl (body stays in the door); the flat palm (`PERSON_HANDS.shield`, arm angle so it reads); recognition by saccades (`gazePlan` ease 0.07), eyes wide for 4 frames only; the swallowed scream (mouth `o` 5 frames, long blink, breath `sq` 1.03); `tremble` 2 to 0; anticipation dip before the book rises. Sadness at 84.78: `wet`, `lid` 0.4, frown, head drop 8, one tear. The reconciliation beat at 154.6 is 0.7 s of nothing but a blink.
- **King** (`caliph`, `crown:false` + cloakWorn when disguised). Arc in his lids and spine: tired `tip` 0.06 and `lid` 0.35 in the heat; frozen bowl with only the pupils moving at 16.5; lean-in `tip` 0.07 and half-lidded smile while hinting; reading saccades; shame = head back, gaze to the ground, soft flush, `lid` 0.6, `sq` 0.97, hands vanish into the cloak; hand on chest with `lid` 1; bow `tip` 0.14; he leaves without looking back. In the palace: beard stroke (`chin`), raised brow at the brother, flush again at 112.4, rigid shock at 126.32 (brow -0.8, mouth o, `sq` 0.95, hand to crown, gaze [0, 0.05] at the lens for 12 frames, gulp), then the honest answer with level brows and eye contact, empty palms and a small bow of the head.
- **Husband** (`villager`). Springy walk home, smile fading in three steps (smile, closed, brow -0.7). Listening nod, then eyes drifting to the door. Fear, not anger: brows raised (negative `brow`), `folded` arms, `tremble` 1.5. Hesitant low-amplitude walk into the hall, hands clasped. Mirrors the tiny tenant at 127.38. Release: breath in (`sq` 1.05, `lid` 1), burst, shoulders drop 6, wet bright eyes. Runs home (`walk` + `footfalls` + `dustBurst`), `lurch` on the stop, bowed head and `reachBoth`.
- **Father and brother.** Never in unison (`stagger`): the father understands first every time, the brother 4 to 6 frames later and bigger. Father: stoop, stick, `templeTap`, a `wink` to his son, eyes lowered respectfully when the king understands. Brother: looks up at the lamps, bows too fast (overshoot), blurts, stares at the king with his mouth open until the stick taps his foot, hops with `landSquash` at the pop.
- **Lion in the balloon.** Proud entry (tail up, `lid` 0.4), `lurch` at the book, tail and ears down, reverse walk with `crouch` 0.4 and `lid` 0.5, exits mouth closed.
- **Secondary motion.** Cloak hem and scarf edge lag 2 frames; the bowl has an arrival dip; the bindle slumps; the lamp dips at 82.98; the gate swings with `swing()`; the songbird leaves when the king steps closer (22.3) and returns when he has gone (57.94).
