# AN-114 The Ruined Man Who Became Rich Again Through a Dream: creative plan

Film length 181.45 s (MP3 178.45 s, last word ends 178.26 s, THE END held 3.0 s). All times are seconds on the final MP3 and come from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard (21 scenes, 176 timed actions, subtitle chunks) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Spine.** A rich man in Baghdad loses everything. A dream tells him his fortune waits in Cairo. He walks there, sleeps in a mosque, is taken for a thief, beaten and jailed. The police chief laughs at his dream and, to prove dreams are worthless, tells his own: a house in Baghdad with a courtyard, a garden and a fountain with money buried under it. The man recognises his own house, says nothing, hurries home, digs and finds the treasure.

**The one idea that carries the film: the fountain.** The treasure is on screen from the first second. The film opens in the man's courtyard with the fountain dead centre (0.0), he sleeps on a mat right beside it on the night of the dream (14.56), and he walks away from it without a glance (28.16). The chief's dream cloud later builds *the same picture* piece by piece (118.74 to 126.66), a memory inset of the opening frame slides over it and matches exactly (141.20), and the last scenes return to the same framing to dig (159.98). The audience should get there half a second before the man does.

**Beats (cue word, time).** Setup بغداد 0.54; loss خسره 5.42; the dream القاهرة 22.02; decision قرر 27.50; sleep in the mosque ونام 41.86; thieves لصوص 48.44; near miss هرب 60.74; beating (softened) وضربوه 75.16; the laugh ضحك 101.36; the chief's dream مال 126.12; recognition بيته 142.92; the secret يكشف 144.80; treasure كنزًا 163.98; moral علمته 174.56.

**Conflict and arc.** Pride (0 to 4), loss and humiliation (4.75 to 13), hope (16.7 to 28.8), exhaustion (28.8 to 44.9), injustice (67 to 81.5), mockery (101 to 111.7), a slow dawning (123.0 to 143.6), hidden glee (144.3 to 156), joy and a quiet smile (164 to the end). Lowest point: day 3 in the cell (80.82). Highest: the chest opens (163.98).

**Surprises.** The thieves vanish and the innocent sleeper is taken (63.68). The mocking chief hands over the secret without knowing it (126.12). **Moral:** the journey did not lead to a treasure far away; it taught him where to look at home.

## 3. Character acting

- **The man.** One face through the film (black moustache and short beard, yellow headwear) so the costume change at 6.22 reads as the same person. Posture tells the arc: chest out and rocking on his heels when rich (`sq` 1.04, `brow` -0.3); `tip` 0.08 to 0.2 and `lid` 0.4 to 0.55 through poverty and the road; flat stillness while the chief talks (no blink from 126.12 to 136.5, only pupils on a `gazePlan`); **mask on / mask off** from 144.80 (bland when the chief looks, a grin for half a second when he does not); a sprint with `tip` 0.22; a calm seated smile at the end. Setup and payoff pose: the coin flip at 2.68 returns at 166.82.
- **The chief.** Slow, wide, certain. He never hurries and rarely looks at the man while describing the house (eyes on his own fingers at 127.46), which is why he misses the reaction. The laugh is built: moustache tremble and puffed cheeks from 100.6 (`tremble`, `anticipate`), release on ضحك 101.36 with a 4 per second body bounce, a wiped tear, a wagging finger. He ends folded and smug (`lid` 0.5).
- **Thieves.** Leader decides and hushes; the second is four frames late and clumsy (the near step at 48.44, a held one-leg wobble with a look to the lens). Sneak with high knees and a short `walk` stride; panic on the way back (long stride, no hush).
- **Guards.** Tall bored one with the torch, short puffing one with the spear, always half a beat late (`stagger`). They nod at each other before acting (68.86), which makes the mistake theirs.
- **Sleeper business.** Through S6 to S8 he only breathes (`breathe`), snorts and rolls once (48.9), scratches his nose at HELP! (56.6) and smiles in his sleep (64). His lids flutter under the torch (66.28) but do not open until hands are on him (73.04).
- Everyone always has a gaze target and blinks (`LIFE.t`), pose changes use `settleTo`, big moves get `anticipate`, landings `landSquash`, carried things (bundle, sack) lag by 2 frames.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground or floor at y 900; faces between y 620 and 700; clouds, balloons, the map card and THE END live in the upper half and never cover a face.
- **Matching frames.** S1 at 0.5 s, the chief's cloud and S19 use the same courtyard layout: door left, three arches, fountain x 480, garden right. Do not flip or restage it.
- Establishing push in S1; locked shots for counters (S2, S10); tracking strip left to right in S5 and the mirror, right to left and three times faster, in S18; a tilt up to dome and minaret (41.14); a pan with the thieves (49.88) and a slow pan back to the sleeper (63.68) for the irony; `twoShot` for all the dialogue with the frame favouring whoever is reacting; the only close-up of the film is the recognition (zoom 2.1 to 2.4, 134.58 to 144.26); pull out to the full courtyard for the moral.
- Panel-only shake on WHACK! and CLANG!. The cell-to-hall cut hides under the LATER card midpoint.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Dramatic irony:** he sleeps beside the treasure, dreams of gold far away, and leaves it dripping behind him. Keep the fountain in every courtyard frame.
- **Suspense by stillness:** 44.92 to 46.9 (only breathing) before the thieves' heads appear; 126.12 to 136.5 (the frozen listener).
- **Comedy:** the near step and look to camera (48.44); police and thieves missing each other by one heel (61.94); the sleeper who sleeps through everything; the chief popping a toy dream (106.16) seconds before describing a real one; three small coins handed with pity to a man who has just become rich (148.16).
- **Gentle handling of the hard part:** the beating is a scuffle cloud with one WHACK!, a bump and stars; the cell is three quick days with a tally.
- **Emotion:** one loaf for a day's work (11.72, wet eyes); day 3 at the window (80.82); the quiet pat on the fountain on the last word بيته (177.60).
- **The moral as a picture:** the map card draws the road out, strikes the Cairo coin, and brings the arrow home to a glowing coin under the house (169.20 to 177.5).
