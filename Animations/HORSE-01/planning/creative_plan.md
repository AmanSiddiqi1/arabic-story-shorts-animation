# HORSE-01 The Flying Wooden Horse: creative plan (80s Cel Anime)

Style `cel-anime-80s`, episode 1 of 5 of the Ebony Horse series. Narration 134.15 s, last word ends 133.62 s, end hold 4.0 s, film 138.15 s. 15 scenes, 154 actions, 20 lettering items, 8 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. Every action is in `creative_plan.json`; this file gives the direction. Reference film for the style: ALI-01-ANIME v4 (owner direction in `Library/styles/cel-anime-80s/look.md`).

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (0-18):** a sunny, crowded festival of inventors. A stranger wheels in a black wooden horse with brass fittings. The horse is the one thing on screen that is not alive.
- **Comic and claim (18-36):** the guests laugh (a dove perches on the "statue"); the stranger says it flies faster than the wind; the king says prove it. Firuz alone is not laughing: he is studying the knob.
- **Surprise (36-47):** one quarter turn of the neck knob, the brass lights in order, the wood lifts and circles the garden.
- **Inciting mistake (47-71.65):** Firuz asks to try. The inventor warns him and points to both controls, knob and ear. **The prince is not looking at the second point** (63.68). He vaults on and gives the knob a half turn. This plant pays off at 111.18.
- **Turn (71.65-90):** impact frame, straight up like an arrow, the palace shrinks to a toy. He calls; only a hawk passes. Conflict: boy against height, with no villain yet.
- **Rising suspense (90-108.7):** the knob the other way makes it climb more; clouds under his boots, cold, a search that finds nothing. Tape transition HOURS LATER... into golden light.
- **Reveal and climax (108.7-122.8):** he remembers the pointing finger, feels along the carved mane and finds the small lever.
- **Ending (122.8-138.15):** earth, lever, "perhaps this is my way to safety", the hand goes out, the sun sets, and the film holds a finger-width from the lever: TO BE CONTINUED...
- **Emotional arc:** boredom, curiosity, delight, bravado, terror, loneliness, cold stubbornness, hope. **Moral:** learn how to come down before you go up.

## 3. Character acting
- **Firuz:** always the one whose eyes go where the others' do not (the knob during the laughter, the stirrup during the warning). On the ground: on his toes, quick `walk` + `footfalls`, bounces (`sq`), cape arriving late (`lagged`, `swing` on stops). Vault: `anticipate`, `hop` 110, `landSquash`. In the sky his body tells the height: flat to the neck with `lid: 1` and `tremble`, one eye first (`wink`), wide eyes, then cupMouth, cupEar, shoulders dropping (`sq` 0.96), folded arms and chattering in the cold, a slow slump, and finally a slow `flow` reach with unsteady fingers. Gaze by `lookAt` / `gazePlan`; a blink on every head turn.
- **Inventor:** slow and sure: `flow` on `tip` for bows, half lids (`lid` 0.35), `brow` 0.3, a half smile; beard, sleeve and turban tail follow by delay. His alarm at 58.94 and 70.04 is real (palms out, headHold): he is honest in this episode, and the slyness only hints at episode 3.
- **King:** jolly weight: belly bounce, beard and crown lagging, chin stroke, a firm point; half rises in worry at 64.44 and reaches after his son at 74.36.
- **Guests (7) and child:** every one on its own seed and phase (`stagger`), laughing, whispering, clapping at different rates, heads turning one after another; reactions always track the horse with `lookAt`.
- **The ebony horse:** no blink, no breath, no leg, ear or tail motion, ever. It rocks with the cart, and in the air it only bobs and pitches as one rigid piece. Its "acting" is light: glints, light bands, the power-on sequence.
- Moving holds everywhere else on twos: `idleSway` on `tip`, hands and head, `breathe` on `sq`, cape ribbon, bunting, fountain, clouds.

## 4. Camera and composition
- **Wide where the place matters:** S1 shows the entire palace at zoom 0.62 (the sky plate is tall), S5 pulls back to 0.62 so the whole glide over the garden is in one frame, S9 and S15 make the rider tiny in a huge sky.
- **Pans where it is detailed:** S2 tracks across 1000 units of garden at zoom 1.0; S3 and S4 pan between crowd, horse and dais. Ground stays near panel y 900 to 990; no push-in that empties the lower picture.
- **Close-ups only at peaks:** four inserts of the controls (37.64, 69.08, 117.68, 130.86) drawn large with `handInsert`; no face close-ups.
- **Flight framing:** horse in the upper third, land or cloud deck in the lower part, so the height is always visible; climbing is read from clouds scrolling down and the nose-up pitch. One impact frame (71.65). One tape transition (107.9).
- Bubbles and labels sit in the sky band, tails to heads, never over a face; faces and action stay inside the panel (the bottom third of the film is the caption strip).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** the dove using the horse as a perch; the hand waved at the brass eye; the child bouncing off the tail; the late laugher; a guest chasing his blown turban; in the sky the hawk's unimpressed glance, twisting the pommel, knocking on the head.
- **Suspense:** the plant at 63.68 (the audience sees the second control, the hero does not); the held beat before the launch; the wrong-way knob; the hand stopping a finger-width from the lever.
- **Surprise:** the first lift off after a scene of laughter; the impact frame; HIGHER!
- **Emotion:** the frame widening on an unanswered call; cold breath; gold light on his face when he finds the lever; the king reaching up after his son.

## 9. Series bible (episodes 1 to 5 must match)
**Characters (accent colours never change with the lighting; skin keeps the theme colour):**
- **Prince Firuz** (`person` prince, s 0.95): cream tunic `#f6efe0` with gold trim `#f2c040`, teal trousers `#2a8f9a`, brown boots, black hair, small gold crown. **Accent crimson `#e8264f`**: sash and the ribbon cape `firuzCape`. Disguise in episode 4: a plain brown cloak over the same tunic, crimson sash still showing.
- **The inventor** (`person` vizier, age 0.7): violet robe `#5a3a8a`, lilac sleeves `#8a6ac8`, plum cape `#3a2458`, green sash, pointed grey beard. **Accent saffron `#f2a71b`** tall turban with a loose tail (the scrap found in episode 3).
- **The king, Firuz's father** (`person` caliph, age 0.6): **emerald `#1f9a6a`** robe, gold cape, white crown turban, grey beard. His banners are emerald with a gold sun.
- **The princess** (from episode 2; `person` lady): reserved **turquoise `#1fb8b0`** robe with gold trim, white veil, rose sash `#ff7aa8`; never crimson, saffron or emerald. The foreign king of episode 4: royal blue with silver.
- Crowds: bright varied colours, never the leads' accents.

**The ebony horse:** carved, not alive. Ebony `#3a3448` (shade `#241f30`, far legs `#2c2738`), brass `#e0b038`; straight carousel legs with brass joint discs and brass hooves; block neck and wedge head; pupil-less brass disc eye; mane of seven stepped blocks with a brass edge; carved S-curl tail; built-in saddle for two on a crimson and gold cloth; brass chest plate with a sun boss. It never moves a leg, blinks or breathes. **Controls:** the **ascent knob** on the near side at the base of the neck (brass disc r 14, engraved up arrow; a quarter turn is a gentle rise, more is faster); the **descent lever** hidden under the first mane block behind the ear (38 long, ball tip, engraved down arrow). When a control is used the brass lights in order (`powerOn`: neck, chest, joints, hooves).

**The king's palace:** cream walls `#f6ead0`, coral arch trim `#e8604a`, turquoise domes `#2fb8b0` with gold finials, two minarets, emerald banners; garden with rose hedge, two cypresses, fountain, star-tiled court, dais and striped canopy. The princess's palace (episode 2) must differ: rose stone, a blue dome and two towers by a river (as her script says).

**Sky and flight grammar:** the horse flies as one rigid piece inside `flyRig`: climbing = nose up 20 to 55 degrees and clouds scrolling down; descending = nose down 10 to 15 degrees and clouds scrolling up; cruising = level with a 6 px bob and clouds sliding opposite to travel; a turn = 4 drawings. Two thin gold wind ribbons trail from the rear hooves; speed lines only for bursts. Riders' capes, veils and beards stream opposite to travel. Horse in the upper third, land or cloud deck below, a shadow on any deck or ground within reach. Time of day is a repainted plate, never a tint: day azure, high cobalt with pale horizon, golden hour, sunset (blue-violet above, coral and gold below), and for episode 2 a light warm night (deep blue, large warm moon, lamp-lit roofs). Landings: slow level descent, dust ring, no squash of the horse (wood), riders bump and settle.
