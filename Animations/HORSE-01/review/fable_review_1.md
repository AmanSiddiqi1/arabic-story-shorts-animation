# HORSE-01 "The Flying Wooden Horse" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 1 (v1)

Film reviewed: `Animations/HORSE-01/renders/HORSE-01_v1.mp4` (138.17 s). Sources: all 24 contact strips in `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second), 48 full-size panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited below, four tight acting strips (52.2-55.8, 60-66, 103.4-107.9, 114-117.6) and a freeze scan of the panel (no fully frozen stretch of 0.7 s or more was found). Times are seconds on the film.

## Verdict: REVISE

No BLOCKER: the story is in order, every narrated event is on screen, subtitles are sharp and complete in both languages, the ending and TO BE CONTINUED are present, no broken frames.

The garden half is close to the owner's reference in look (wide, full, bright, varied), and the wooden horse reads as a machine throughout. Two things clearly hurt the story and the owner's points, and both are also the look the next four episodes inherit:

1. **The ground acting is a line-up.** From 18 to 71 s everyone stands in one row facing the camera, the inventor stands in front of the horse and in front of the prince, and the film's central plant (the inventor points at two controls, the prince does not look at the second) cannot be seen at all.
2. **The sky is one empty picture for 37 seconds.** From 97 to 134 s the horse sits in the middle of a blank cloud haze with nothing in the lower half, and the rider's cream arms and legs merge into a knot.

Notes 1 to 6 are the revision. Notes 7 to 12 are smaller and can ride along.

Not checked: sound was not listened to; stepped cel timing (twos and threes) cannot be judged from stills.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 55.8-71.6 s (S7): the warning and the missed second control cannot be seen
- **What is wrong.** The inventor steps to x 1080 and stands directly in front of the horse's chest and neck; his turban covers the knob. Firuz is behind him: from 59.2 to 65.2 only a crown and a sliver of tunic show (63.8, 64.9). The UP label's arrow lands on the inventor's turban, not on the knob. The DOWN arrow points into the balloon's black wedge. Firuz's gaze is not visible, so "he is not looking at the second point" does not exist on screen. It is not a two-shot: nine figures stand shoulder to shoulder across the frame.
- **Why it matters.** This is the plant the whole episode pays off at 111.18 and 117.68, and the reason for the moral. Without it the lever later is a lucky find rather than something he was told and ignored.
- **Fix (restage, same cues).**
  - Keep x 1030 to 1170 free of every figure from 58.9 to 71.6 so the neck, knob and head are in clear view. Guests: left group no further right than 780, right group no further left than 1340, the child with the left group.
  - Inventor stays right of the head at about x 1215, facing left (do not step to 1080). Firuz says LET ME TRY IT! at 57.16 from 1150, then at 58.94, when the inventor's palms go out, he walks round in front of the cart to x 975 beside the saddle (walk + footfalls, 59.2 to 61.3), cape arriving late. Result: prince left, horse's neck and head in the middle, inventor right.
  - 62.36 balloon above the inventor (see note 5 for the tail). 63.10 (الصعود): inventor leans (`tip` about -0.12) and points his near arm at the knob; the knob glints once; UP label with its arrow tip on the knob. 63.68 (والهبوط): his other arm goes up to the ear, the lever spot glints, DOWN label with its arrow tip at the ear. Hold both labels to 64.9.
  - Firuz during 63.10: gaze on the knob (head toward it, small nod). At 63.68 his head turns away and down to the stirrup with a blink, one hand already on the saddle, heels bouncing (`sq` 0.97 to 1.03 on twos). A short dotted gaze line or a small "!" over the stirrup is acceptable if the gaze alone is too small. He must be fully visible, nothing in front of him.
  - 64.44 (أولًا): king half rises (already planned). 65.12 anticipation crouch, 66.66 seated: the vault now needs no duck under an arm. Inventor's alarm at 70.04 from x 1215, both palms out.
  - Camera: zoom 1.25, centre about x 1095, ground line near panel y 950; drift 20 units toward the prince on 65.12.

### 2. 97.2-129.8 s (S11 to S14): one empty picture for half a minute
- **What is wrong.** The horse is in the middle of the panel over a flat cloud band; the lower 45 percent is blank haze with one grey oval. The same composition holds from 97.2 to 108 (blue) and again from 108.5 to 129.8 (gold), with only two short inserts and a 2 s tilt. Nothing travels: no lateral cloud drift is readable, so the horse looks parked in the air.
- **Why it matters.** The owner asked for full scenes with several layers and no empty lower picture; and the story here is rising fear (cold, lost, hours passing). A static, empty frame reads as calm.
- **Fix.**
  - **Dress the deck in three layers** (bake once per plate): far, a horizon of tall cumulus towers and two or three mountain tops breaking through; middle, the deck with holes that show the land far below (fields, a river bend, a tiny village); near, big soft foreground puffs crossing the bottom of the panel at about 2x the middle layer's speed. Add a flock of birds below the horse at 99.2 (تمر) and the hawk's far silhouette once more.
  - **Make it travel:** all three layers slide opposite to the horse's facing the whole time (series bible: cruising = clouds sliding opposite to travel), with the gold ribbons trailing.
  - **Change the framing on the words:** 97.18 wide as now; 99.76 (تحت) push to rig size about 1.5x with the hooves low in frame so a cloud passes right under the boots; 101.08 (وشعر) medium on the rider, breath puffs and frost glints; 103.76 (يبحث) stay medium for the search (note 6); 107.9 transition; 108.69 start wide in gold and push in slowly to the medium by 113.42 (الأذن).
  - Horse in the upper third in the wides, as the plan says; in the mediums it may fill the middle.
  - Shadow: drop the grey oval when the horse is more than about 250 px above the deck, or draw it as a soft horse-shaped shadow that slides over the deck's bumps; an oval on white fog reads as a smudge.

### 3. 132.1-138.2 s (S15, the ending wide): no sun in "as the sun set", and an empty frame for the longest hold
- **What is wrong.** The final 6 s show a very small rider in the centre of a pink field. There is no sun disc: rays fan from behind the rider and a faint glow sits beside the horse (136.0). The lower half is flat pink. It is the one near-monochrome picture in the film.
- **Why it matters.** The last narrated words are غروب الشمس, and this picture is the cliffhanger the next episode starts from.
- **Fix.** A large sun disc (diameter about 300 px) half sunk into the cloud horizon at panel y about 760, sinking another 60 px from 132.14 to 138.0. The rider, at about 1.6x the present size, in the upper third and overlapping the top of the disc, in his own colours with a warm rim. Sky bands as in the bible: blue-violet top, coral, gold at the horizon; deck with gold-lit tops and violet undersides; two or three cloud towers; a first star and a thin moon high up at 135; three birds crossing the disc. Lever glint at 133.08 as now. TO BE CONTINUED... stays in the top band.

### 4. 75.1-134 s: the mounted prince is a cream knot, and one pose serves most of the flight
- **What is wrong.** Cream sleeves over a cream tunic over cream thighs, both arms crossing the torso down to the pommel: at phone size the body is a pale bundle with a teal shin (87.5, 102.8, 110.5, 124.8). The same crouch is used for clinging, calling, thinking and deciding, so the planned arc (terror, loneliness, cold stubbornness, hope) is carried only by the face.
- **Why it matters.** Owner point "every limb readable"; and for 60 s he is the only actor on screen.
- **Fix.**
  - Readability: the whole leg in teal `#2a8f9a` (thigh included), crimson sash visible at the waist, gold cuffs on both wrists and a sleeve tone one step darker than the tunic (about `#e2d2b0`) so arms separate from chest. Series bible colours are kept.
  - Never both arms across the torso. Base riding pose: near hand on the pommel, far arm free.
  - One clear silhouette per beat: 75.12 flat along the neck, arms round it; 76.22 one eye, then head up; 80.44 torso twisted to camera-left and down, both hands cupped at the mouth, elbows out; 83.60 one hand behind the ear, leaning out over the side; 86.22 shoulders drop, arms hang, head down (`sq` 0.96); 90.22 jaw set, sits tall; 101.08 hugs himself, hands on opposite upper arms, shoulders up, 12 fps shiver; 108.69 head snaps up, finger raised; 122.82 leans out and looks straight down; 127.22 looks at his own open hand, then at the lever.
  - Cape always streaming opposite to travel; after each pose change let it arrive 3 to 4 drawings late.

### 5. 24.2-29.4, 32.4-34.0, 57.2-58.7, 62.4-64.6 s: balloon tails are black wedges over the horse
- **What is wrong.** When the balloon sits high above the speaker the tail becomes a long, wide black triangle. At 27.7-29.2 it covers the horse's head; at 62.5-64.5 it covers the neck and the knob at the very moment they are pointed at.
- **Why it matters.** The horse is the subject of every one of these lines, and the wedge is the darkest shape on screen.
- **Fix.** In `h1SayAt`: tail length at most 70 px and base width at most 26 px; if the balloon cannot sit that close, move the balloon, not the tail. Place balloons beside the speaker's head on the side away from the horse (inventor's lines to the right of him once he stands at x 1215; king's line as now). No balloon or tail may overlap the horse's head, neck or knob.

### 6. 18.1-55.8 s (S3 and S6): the inventor hides his own invention; the prince's curiosity and inspection are not staged
- **What is wrong.**
  - 18.1-29.7: the inventor stands at the horse's chest, Firuz and a maiden pressed against his shoulder. "Firuz alone is not laughing, he is studying the knob" does not read: he is a head behind a robe. The dove on the horse's head (21.6) is a few pixels.
  - 50.1-55.8: Firuz walks in and stops in the row beside the inventor, facing the camera. The planned inspection (hoof, chest, neck, eyes stop on the knob at 54.06) is two small arm lifts; his crown covers the knob.
  - 49-66: the maiden, the lilac courtier and the tray servant hold one arms-down pose; only eyes move.
- **Why it matters.** The comedy of S3 needs the horse in clear view, and S6 is where the audience learns that this boy acts before he listens.
- **Fix.**
  - S3: inventor at x 1215 or beyond for 18.1-29.7 (bow, raised finger, both bubbles, arm to the sky from there). Firuz one full step in front of the right group with clear space both sides, body turned to the horse, head tilted, not laughing, gaze on the knob; a single small glint on the knob at 21.6 ties his look to it. Dove 2x its present size, landing with a wing flutter; one guest points at it.
  - S6 (use the cleared zone from note 1): 50.10 Firuz walks to the hooves (x about 1000); 52.42 crouches to a hoof and raps it (`sq` 0.9, small TOK); 53.0 stands and steps to the chest plate, palm flat on it; 53.38 on his toes looking up the neck; 54.06 (بنفسه) head stops, eyes on the knob, finger hovering near it, knob glint, one blink. Inventor watching from x 1215 with half lids. Camera 1.25.
  - Guests: give each a second and third pose in 47-66 (whisper to a neighbour, hand to mouth, pointing, a step forward and back, clap), staggered so no two change together, all tracking the prince with `lookAt`.

### 7. 80.4-82.4 s: HELLOOO! is cut by the top of the panel
- **What is wrong.** The balloon's upper third is outside the tube (80.7-82.2).
- **Fix.** Clamp balloon top to panel y >= 80. Better: start with the rider lower (upper-middle of the panel), balloon beside his head toward the open sky, then the planned fading arcs travelling down toward the land, since nobody below hears him.

### 8. 37.6-39.4 and 69.0-69.9 s: the hidden lever is on show in both knob inserts
- **What is wrong.** In the two knob close-ups the brass lever and its slot are plainly visible on the upper neck (38.6, 69.2), 80 s before he "finds" it.
- **Why it matters.** "Hidden beneath the carved mane" and the A LEVER! reveal lose their surprise; an attentive viewer wonders why he did not see it.
- **Fix.** In inserts 1 and 2 draw the first mane block down over the slot (no brass bar, no slot). Optionally a 3-frame glint on the block's lower edge at 39.2 as a fair hint. In insert 3 (117.68) lift or slide the mane block as a solid cel over 6 drawings to uncover the lever; the present translucent grey square (118.2-120.2) reads as a rendering fault.

### 9. 103.7-107.8 s (S11): the search does not read as a search
- **What is wrong.** At the wide size the six planned checks are small arm changes on a seated figure (tight strip 103.4-107.9: reach to the neck, look sideways, slump).
- **Fix.** With the medium framing of note 2: 103.76 (يبحث) leans far over the near side, head below the saddle line; 104.54 (علامة) twists round to look back at the tail, cape wrapping across him for 3 drawings; 105.26 (تدله) twists the pommel, nothing happens, small "?"; 106.46 (طريقة) knocks on the horse's head, small TOK, shakes his hand; 107.16 (الهبوط) slump with a breath puff. Eyes lead every move by two drawings. Never looks at the ear here.

### 10. 109.4-113.4 s: the memory picture is too small
- **What is wrong.** The thought cloud holds a tiny cart, horse and inventor; the pointing arm is a few pixels and the arrow at the ear appears only at 112.7.
- **Fix.** Cloud about 1.5x (it may take the whole upper-left of the panel; move the rider down-right). Inside: only the horse's head and neck with the inventor's bust and pointing arm (a crop, not the whole cart), and at 111.18 (أشار) the DOWN label from S7 flashing at the ear exactly as it looked at 63.68, so the callback is word for word. At 113.42 the rider's eyes snap to the real ear with a blink and the cloud pops.

### 11. 130.7-132.1 s: the hand reaches the lever instead of stopping short
- **What is wrong.** By 132.0 the fingertips overlap the lever's ball tip and a glint fires on the contact point; it looks pressed.
- **Why it matters.** The cliffhanger is that he has not pulled it yet.
- **Fix.** Stop the index fingertip 14 to 18 px from the ball with clear sky colour in the gap; unsteady 2 px tremble on twos; move the glint to the lever tip itself, not the gap. Hold 131.4 to 132.1.

### 12. 122.8-125.6 s: the "strange country" is an abstract quilt
- **What is wrong.** The land in the tilt is a mosaic of yellow and teal tiles with a few dots; it shows "ground" but not "somewhere far from home".
- **Fix.** Give it three readable features different from the home kingdom's green fields: a broad winding river catching the gold light, a walled city with a blue dome and two towers on its bank (the princess's palace of episode 2, tiny), and a line of hills. Long evening shadows. Firuz's gaze goes land (124.92), then lever (125.40), with a blink between.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **`ebonyHorse` rigid-prop rule**: a character-sized prop that acts only through light (ordered glints, light bands). Worth a short entry in the Capabilities Guide as the pattern for statues, automata and idols.
- **Shrinking landmark plate** (`kingdomBelow` + `palaceToy` scaling under drifting cloud): a general "rising high" device.
- **Control insert** (`handInsert`: large prop detail, blurred set behind, hand entering from the edge, engraved arrow): reusable for keys, locks, lamps, rings.
- **Balloon tail limits** (max length and base width, never across the subject) belong in the style's `label` guidance once fixed here.
- After the revision: the three-layer dressed cloud deck and the half-sunk sun wide, for every flying or sea story.
