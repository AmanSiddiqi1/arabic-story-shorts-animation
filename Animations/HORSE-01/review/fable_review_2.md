# HORSE-01 "The Flying Wooden Horse" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 2 (v2)

Film reviewed: `Animations/HORSE-01/renders/HORSE-01_v2.mp4` (138.17 s). Sources: 22 of the 24 contact strips in `review/v2_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second; 12-18 s and the 0.2 s tail strip were not opened), 19 panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited below, and six full-resolution crops. Times are seconds on the film.

## Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

No BLOCKER. The story is in order, every narrated event is on screen, subtitles are sharp and complete in both languages, the cliffhanger and TO BE CONTINUED are present, no broken frames.

Both round 1 problems that hurt the story are solved: the warning is now a clear three-part picture (prince, horse's neck and head, inventor) with both controls labelled, and the sky is a dressed, travelling, re-framed place instead of one empty picture. The film is ready for the owner. The notes below are polish; notes 1 to 4 are the ones worth doing if another pass is made, and note 3 (hairline seams) is the only visible defect.

Not checked: sound was not listened to; stepped cel timing (twos and threes) cannot be judged from stills. An ffmpeg freeze scan found nothing, but the tape noise changes every frame, so that scan proves nothing; "no frozen holds" below is judged from the strips only.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. 63.68-65.1 s: the look-away is there but soft
- **What is wrong.** At 63.7-63.9 Firuz's eyes are shut and from 64.0 he grins with his pupils down, body still square to the camera, hand at his head. It reads as "pleased with himself" more than "looking at something else while the second control is shown". His pose at 63.2 (looking at the knob) and at 64.3 differ mainly in the pupils.
- **Why it matters.** This is the plant for 111.18 and 117.68. It now works for an attentive viewer; a stronger turn makes it work for everyone.
- **Fix.** At 63.68 (والهبوط) turn his whole body to face the saddle (flip facing away from the inventor), `tip` about 0.08 toward the saddle, near hand flat on the saddle cloth, far foot lifting toward the stirrup, gaze on the stirrup. Keep the blink on the turn. Hold to 65.1. Optional: one small glint on the stirrup at 63.9 so the eye sees what he is looking at instead.

### 2. 90.2-94.7 s: "turned the knob the other way" is too small to read
- **What is wrong.** The rider is at wide size; the reach to the knob and the pink curved arrow (92.5-94.0) are a few pixels. The same framing has held since 86.0, the longest single picture in the film.
- **Why it matters.** The line is a cause and effect joke: he tries the opposite, and it climbs more. Only the effect (HIGHER! at 94.7) reads.
- **Fix.** Push in to the medium size used at 101.2 (about 1.8) from 91.84 (إدارة) to 92.50 (المقبض), centred on the rider's hand and the knob. Curved arrow about 3x its present size, drawn the opposite way round to the arrow in the 69.0 insert. Hopeful look down at 94.12 (الآخر), then snap back out to the wide on 94.74 (فارتفع) with the kick up. Eyes lead the reach by two drawings.

### 3. 108.7-138.2 s: hairline vertical seams in the cloud layers
- **What is wrong.** Thin vertical lines stand in the gold and sunset cloud layers, for example panel-left at 136.5-137.5 (through the left cloud tower, about film x 95, y 390-690, and a second one near x 310, y 700-1100), at 116.5 (upper right, about x 930) and at 128.8 (about x 600, y 700-1200). They look like the joins between baked strips.
- **Why it matters.** Small, but one sits in the final 4 s hold, the most looked-at picture in the film.
- **Fix.** In `h01_deck.js`, draw the tiled strips at whole-pixel x with a 2 px overlap (or bake each layer 2 px wider and crop), and make sure the blur pass does not fade the strip's left and right edges to transparent. Check the blue deck (97-108) on a still as well.

### 4. 132.1-138.2 s: a mountain peak sits right behind the horse in the last picture
- **What is wrong.** A violet snow peak stands directly behind the horse's body and tail, the same value as the ebony, so the silhouette of the film's last image is muddled. The sun disc is buried to its top third, so it reads as a glowing dome more than a setting sun.
- **Why it matters.** Last picture, and the picture the next episode opens from.
- **Fix.** Move that peak at least 300 px left or right of the rider (or drop it from the sunset far layer). Start the disc about 50 px higher so a clear half circle shows at 132.2 and still shows an arc at 138.0. Horse and rider entirely against sky and disc.

### 5. 66.7-68.9 s: seated with arms folded while the narrator says he turned the knob
- **What is wrong.** After the vault he sits with both arms folded across his chest from 67.5 to 68.7. The reach to the knob happens only in the insert at 69.0.
- **Why it matters.** Folded arms is the one "both arms across the torso" pose left, and the cut to the hand has no lead-in.
- **Fix.** Keep the smug fold for 67.4-68.0 only. At 68.16 (وأدار) eyes drop to the knob, then the near arm reaches down to it (two drawings later), far hand on the pommel, so the insert at 69.08 continues a move already started.

### 6. 126.0-130.5 s: the thinnest picture left in the sky
- **What is wrong.** After the country tilt the frame returns to a small rider in the upper third over cream cloud; the lower half is soft cream with pink undersides and nothing else for 4.5 s.
- **Fix.** Either hold the rider at about 1.3x with the horse in the middle of the panel, or keep a ragged hole in the lower third through which the river and the blue-domed town stay in view and slide past, so "the earth and the lever" are both on screen while he speaks.

### 7. 75-134 s: cape lag and gold cuffs (carried over from round 1, note 4)
- **What is wrong.** The cape streams correctly but changes with the body on pose changes. The inserts show a gold cuff (69.2, 119.5, 131.5) that the rider does not have.
- **Fix.** Cape: add `lagged` on the cape's root angle (delay about 0.25 s, strength 0.5) so it arrives 3 drawings late after each pose change. Cuffs: fine to wait for the Library `pal.cuff` proposal; until then nobody will notice.

### 8. 118.0-118.3 s: grey slanted panes in the lever insert
- **What is wrong.** Two translucent grey parallelograms cross the picture as the block lifts (above the head at 118.0, by the muzzle at 118.2). They read as panes of glass.
- **Fix.** Make it one warm light band (cream-gold, additive, soft edges) sweeping once across the lever between 118.4 and 118.7, or drop it; the glint at 118.5 is enough.

### 9. 109.7-113.2 s: the memory picture's lower edge cuts the inventor
- **What is wrong.** The cloud's lower scallops cut across the inventor's bust and the cart, and his raised arm stops at his own turban instead of reaching toward the ear.
- **Fix.** Shift the picture inside the cloud up by about 40 px and let his arm extend toward the ear at 111.18 so arm, arrow and label make one line.

### 10. 62.4-65.0 s: the warning balloon touches the horse's muzzle
- **Fix.** Move LEARN UP AND DOWN FIRST! about 30 px right (`minX`), leaving sky between muzzle and balloon.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Three-layer travelling cloud deck** (`h01_deck.js`: far towers and peaks, deck with holes onto land, near puffs in front of the subject, three speeds, one bake per palette): the model for every flying or sea-of-cloud scene. Move it to `Library/` once the seams of note 3 are fixed.
- **Half-sunk sun wide** with banded sky and a subject overlapping the disc.
- **`h1Say` balloon** (narrow tail with length and width limits, top clamp, `minX` keep-clear): a candidate for the style's `label` guidance.
- **`cover` field pattern on a prop** (a solid block that hides a control until the reveal, then slides as one cel).
- **Three-zone "instruction" staging**: listener, object, teacher left to right with a kept-clear zone over the object, labels landing on the spoken words.
