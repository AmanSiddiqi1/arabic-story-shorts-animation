# KD-005 The Raven and the Snake: Fable review, round 1 (v1 cloud render)

Reviewed: `renders/KD-005_v1.mp4` through all 28 strips in `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 fps) plus 30 full-size frames pulled into `review/v1_pack/extra/` (`f_<t>.png`, montages `m_1..m_10.jpg`, `day.jpg`).

**Overall.** The story is told in order, is faithful and is easy to follow. Subtitles are verbatim, well timed and readable in both languages; the English reads correctly against the Arabic in every chunk. The ending is present (THE END at 159.7, held to 163.3). No broken frames. Strong parts that must be kept as they are: the crane up the trunk (1.5-3.0), the hatch and CHICKS counter (8.0-9.7), the worm falling from the open beak (18.5-19.5), the eyes-in-the-burrow insert (51.7-52.4), the plan board with its three chips (63.7-81.2), the brow wipe beside the frozen thief (96.2-96.9), the hand reaching while the snake rises (130.7-131.5), the cobweb and NO SNAKE! World anchors used below are the ones in the plan: jackal mark x 200, low branch tip (430, 690), root stone (395, 862), burrow (860, 890), ground y 900.

## Notes

### 1. 138.0-140.0 MAJOR: "then returned to their homes" is not shown; the three villagers pile into one stack under the tree
- **Wrong:** on "عادوا" (138.35) to "بيوتهم." (140.03) nobody leaves. At 138.0-139.2 the three stand at the trunk; at 139.5-139.7 they slide onto the same x and overlap into one body (the woman vanishes behind man1, man2 is a sliver; see `f_139.7.png`). Then the scene cuts. The raven's feet also rest on man1's cap from 136.0 to 139.2 (`f_137.2.png`, `f_138.7.png`).
- **Why it matters:** this is a narrated event and the release of the climax. The audience has to see the danger walk away so the raven's vigil in the next scene makes sense. The stack reads as a render fault.
- **Fix:** start the exit earlier and stagger it. Man1 shoulders the staff at 137.7 (just after "العقد،" ends at 137.61) and steps off left on "ثم" (138.03); the woman follows 0.25 s later, patting the necklace at her neck and looking back once at the burrow; man2 follows 0.5 s after her, still puffing. Use one `kfn` per walker into `walk()` and `footfalls()`, spacing at least 110 world units, all three walking in front of the trunk and fully off the left edge by 139.9. The raven turns his head to follow them out (`lookAt` the last walker), then looks down at the burrow on the cut. Lift the raven clear of the heads for all of S12 and S13: either perch him on the lookout twig side of the branch at least 90 world units above the tallest cap, or stop the group 120 units further right of the branch tip.

### 2. 38.8-43.8 MAJOR: the raven's peck plan does not read; PECK! fires with nothing pecking
- **Wrong:** the cloud holds only the sleeping snake until 42.2. PECK! appears at 41.4 outside the cloud, on the tree's foliage, while no beak touches anything (`f_41.6.png`). The mini raven enters at 42.2, after both cue words ("أنقر" 41.32, "عينيها" 41.92), and only hovers over the coil until 43.7. Two thirds of the cloud is blank paper. The real raven's own mime is one small lean.
- **Why it matters:** this is the raven's wrong plan, and the jackal's replay at 48.1 (the eye opens, SNAP!) is a punchline that only works if the first version showed a confident, successful peck. At the moment the replay has nothing to contradict.
- **Fix:** inside the cloud, mini raven tiptoes in from the left on "سأنتظر" (38.78), freezes with one foot up on "تنام،" (40.06) while the Z Z rises, then two clear jabs at the snake's head: beak down on 41.32 and again on 41.92 (bird mime: `headRot` forward, `drop` 6, `sq` 0.9, 3 frames down, 4 back). Put PECK! inside the cloud at the snake's head on the first jab and re-pop it on the second; remove the one on the foliage. On "بمنقاري!" (42.72) the mini raven puffs his chest (crest up, `tip` 0.08) and the real raven does the same below, in sync. Scale the cloud contents up about 1.4x and centre them so the coil and the mini raven fill the cloud. Keep the real raven's two jabs on the same two words, with larger `headRot`.

### 3. 38.7-51.2 MAJOR: the jackal is cropped to a head at the left edge for 12 seconds, including his own lines
- **Wrong:** when the camera widens for the cloud the jackal sits half out of frame: only his head and one foreleg show from 38.7, and at 45.0-51.0, while he says "لا تفعل! الحَيّة سريعة وخطيرة", his body is cut by the border (`f_46.9.png`, `f_49.0.png`). The stop-sign paw planned for 46.54 is invisible.
- **Why it matters:** this is the turn of the story and the speaker is off screen. The Capabilities Guide rule applies: keep both faces in a two-shot and never crop the character the line belongs to.
- **Fix:** key the camera with `twoShot(jackal, raven, {margin})` extended to include the cloud, or simpler: shift this camera about 130 world units left and move the cloud anchor left by the same amount so the frame holds jackal (whole body), raven and cloud. If all three will not fit, shrink the cloud to 0.85 rather than crop the jackal. Then stage the stop: on "لا" (46.54) the jackal's ears snap up one frame before the head, the near forepaw comes up flat toward the raven (`anticipate` 3 frames, then `settleTo`), eyes wide, mouth open; hold through "تفعل!" (47.55).

### 4. 46.6-48.0 MAJOR: NO! is stamped on the raven, not beside the jackal who says it
- **Wrong:** the red NO! sits on the raven's chest and feet (`f_46.9.png`), so it covers the listener and reads as the raven's word. It is placed at world (380, 660), which is the branch.
- **Why it matters:** wrong speaker, and lettering over a character at the exact moment his reaction (the deflating) should be seen.
- **Fix:** move it to the jackal: world about (250, 640), up and to the right of his muzzle, in clear sky, tilted toward the raven; size can stay 84. With it gone from the raven, show the raven's reaction on the same beat: beak snaps shut, crest drops, `sq` 0.9, a one-frame flinch back toward the trunk.

### 5. 60.0-63.2 MAJOR: in the whisper the jackal's muzzle passes through the raven's head
- **Wrong:** from 60.5 the jackal's snout overlaps the raven's face; by 61.8 his nose and upper jaw are inside the raven's head and the raven's beak crosses the jackal's eye (`f_61.8.png`). The whisper paw is not visible. The push-in at 62.7-63.0 enlarges the collision.
- **Why it matters:** "وأصغى إلى صديقه باهتمام" is the hinge into the plan. It should be a clean, warm, conspiratorial silhouette; instead the two faces merge.
- **Fix:** stop the raven's second hop about 70 world units further right (stone centre, not its left edge) and keep the jackal's nose tip at least 25 units short of the raven's head. Turn the raven three-quarters so his ear side faces the jackal: `headRot` tilted toward him on "وأصغى" (60.13), beak pointing down and right, away from the jackal's face. Raise the jackal's near paw beside his muzzle on "صديقه" (61.06) as the whisper screen. Raven's eye widens in two steps and he nods twice on "باهتمام." (61.88, 62.3). Push in on the gap between the two heads, not on the overlap.

### 6. 10.2-13.5 and 14.4-16.8 MAJOR: the villain's entrance is tiny and half the panel is bare ground
- **Wrong:** at 10.2-13.2 the ground band fills the lower 45 percent of the panel and the snake is a short dark stroke about 30 px tall at the burrow lip (`m_10.jpg` left, strip 12.0-13.2). THE SNAKE label is bigger than the snake. On the climb (14.5-16.7) she is a thin line on the trunk, and at 16.5 her head lands on the chicks as a dark arc that cannot be read as a head.
- **Why it matters:** this is the inciting beat and the only time we see the snake's power. If she is small and quick here, the raven's helplessness and the later scare at 130.9 lose weight.
- **Fix:** frame the burrow as a proper close shot: camera centre about (800, 800), zoom about 2.2, so the ground line sits near 80 percent of the panel height and the mound fills the lower middle. Eyes in the dark on "جاءت" (10.79), head slides out with a tongue flick on "حَيّة" (11.36), and hold the head large, cold and half-lidded through "سوداء" (11.86-12.76) before the body pours out and the camera starts to follow on "جحر قريب،". Thicken the body stroke by about 30 percent in these shots if the snake has a width option. For 16.36 "لتأكلهم.": bring the head up over the far rim, clearly above the chicks with a visible eye, hold 6 frames while the three beaks shut one by one, then whip to the sky. Do not show the mouth opening on them.

### 7. 145.2-148.8 MAJOR: the two day cycles strobe and both "days" are mostly a yellow dawn
- **Wrong:** sampled every 0.2 s (`day.jpg`): blue, orange, navy night, olive, then five samples of yellow dawn (DAY 1), then one sample of blue day and one of navy night back to back (about 147.0-147.4), then seven samples of yellow dawn (DAY 2). The full-panel jump blue to navy to yellow inside 0.4 s is a flash, and neither day ever looks like a day passing.
- **Why it matters:** "مرّ يوم، ثم يوم آخر" is the patient-wait beat. It should feel calm. A strobe is uncomfortable on a phone and reads as a fault.
- **Fix:** drive `skyCycle` with one smooth `u` per day and no holds: day 1 from 145.3 to 147.0 (sun arcs across a blue sky, short dusk, about 0.3 s of night with a cross-fade), DAY 1 counter pops on "يوم،" (146.29); day 2 from 147.0 to 148.7 the same, DAY 2 on "يوم" (147.55), ending in plain blue daylight for the push to the cobweb. Minimum 6 frames for any sky state and ease between presets so no two consecutive frames differ by a whole palette. The raven and the dashed eye-line hold still throughout, as now; add one slow blink per day.

### 8. 158.3-163.3 MAJOR: the closing image is weak: the jackal ends in front of the trunk, the salute is invisible, a quarter of the frame is bare ground
- **Wrong:** in the final wide the raven is about 60 px long, so the wing salute cannot be seen. The jackal keeps walking through the whole THE END hold and finishes overlapping the trunk, looking ahead rather than up (`f_163.0.png`). The lower 22 percent of the panel is empty tan.
- **Why it matters:** this is the last picture the viewer keeps, and it is the only thanks the jackal gets for the plan.
- **Fix:** stop the crane-out one step closer (zoom about 1.25 on the wide, camera centre raised so the ground line sits near 85 percent of panel height). Jackal trots in from the left during "تملأ الأغصان." (157.5-159.1), halts at x 200 with a `landSquash`-style settle, sits, and tilts his head up at the nest by 159.4. Raven answers at 159.6 with a big wing salute (`sag` -0.8 on the near wing, held 12 frames) and the jackal gives his small nod at 160.4. Both are still, apart from blinks and the chicks' beaks, from 161.0 to the end. Keep THE END where it is but nudge it left or up so it clears the raven's head by at least 40 px.

### 9. 106.5-120.0 MINOR: chase: the near-miss does not read, SWISH! covers the raven, the three runners clump
- **Wrong:** at 109.4-110.0 the staff sweeps flat at shoulder height, about 200 px below the bird, and SWISH! is printed over the raven and the necklace (`f_109.6.png`). At 113.2-114.0 the three villagers overlap into one mass at the foot of tree 1 (`f_113.5.png`). The raven's look back is too small to see at this scale. The lower quarter of the panel is empty for 14 s.
- **Why it matters:** this is the comic stretch and the proof of the plan ("he stayed close"). The joke is that he lets them nearly reach him.
- **Fix:** keep the swipe at 109.4 and, just before it, dip the raven's flight path down to within about 40 world units of the staff tip, swing the staff upward in an arc that passes just under his tail, and pop one loose feather. Move SWISH! to the staff's arc, below and behind the bird. Keep at least 90 world units between runners at every stop (use `stagger(i)` on the halt as well as the start), man2 always last and bent over. Zoom the tracking camera about 1.25 and raise its centre so the ground line sits near 82 percent of the panel. At each tree landing (113.28, 114.46) turn the raven's whole head back toward them (`turnFlip` or reversed `gaze` plus `headRot`) and give the necklace one deliberate shake.

### 10. 131.5-134.0 MINOR: the recoil is cluttered; man2 disappears behind the woman
- **Wrong:** after the snake rises, the woman and man2 share almost the same x; his head and raised arms sit behind her head and hands (`f_133.0.png`), so two reactions become one blob. Man1, who is nearest the snake, barely moves back.
- **Why it matters:** three distinct frights (bold, furious, puffing) is the planned character comedy and sells the scare.
- **Fix:** on the 3-frame recoil at 131.44 send them to three separate marks with `stagger`: man1 one step back with the staff snapping across his body, woman two steps back with hands to cheeks, man2 furthest left hiding behind the trunk with only his head and cap peeking out. No head overlaps another. Keep HISS! where it is.

### 11. 24.0-33.5 and 52.5-57.9 MINOR: the dialogue two-shots are wide and the jackal's gestures do not land

For "هذه الحَيّة" hold the wide for a full 1.6 s (35.2-36.8) with the raven's near wing thrown out toward the burrow (`sag` -0.8) and the jackal's head turning to follow it, then return. For 52.71-55.66: raven flexes a wing on "القوة" (52.71), jackal shakes his head on "ليست" (53.42), then lifts a forepaw to his temple on "الحل" (54.28) with the sparkle at the paw tip. ### 12. 17.5-18.0, 94.5-95.3, 140.0-145.0 MINOR: small polish

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- **Plan board in a thought cloud** (63.7-81.2): dimmed live scene behind, a strip map inside the cloud, numbered `S.box` chips popping on the instruction words, and the outcome withheld. Worth a `planBoard` helper and a Capabilities Guide entry.
- **Eyes-in-the-dark insert** (51.7-52.4): a 0.7 s cutaway to the burrow under "وقد تؤذيك" is cheap suspense; add as a `burrow {eyes}` example.
- **Brow wipe beside a frozen thief** (96.2-96.9): the victim's innocent action as the suspense beat; note under comedy and suspense beats.
- **Guide additions from this review:** in zoomed ground shots keep the ground line at about 80 percent of panel height; no sky state shorter than 6 frames in `dayCycle`; when a thought cloud forces a wider camera, re-run `twoShot` so the listener is not cropped.
