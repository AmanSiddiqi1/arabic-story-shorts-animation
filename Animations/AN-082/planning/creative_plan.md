# AN-082 Hatim of the Tribe of Tayy: creative plan

Film length 161.59 s of narration + 3.0 s THE END = 164.59 s. 18 scenes, 161 timed actions (all in `creative_plan.json`; every cue is a script word and its start time from `inputs/timeline.md`). This file gives the direction; the JSON gives the frame-level list.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A king jokes at the grave of the most generous man in Arabia that his guests are hungry. That night the dead host serves him his own camel, and next day the host's son arrives with a new one.

**Spine.** Setup: Hatim the host (0-5), his grave between two stone figures (5-13.8), the wailing valley (13.8-22.6). Inciting: King Zu al-Kuraa laughs at the grave (40.28) and mockingly asks for dinner (44-52). Suspense: he sleeps beside it (53-60). Turn: he wakes screaming for his she-camel (61.70); she collapses (70.62); the men roast and eat her (77-81). Reveal: the dream of Hatim with a sword and an empty bowl (87-99). Surprise: a stranger with another she-camel (113.24), who is Adi son of Hatim (124.10). Resolution: Hatim ordered his son in a dream to repay his guest (145.46). Moral: the king rides on thinking of a generosity death did not end (156.58).

**Conflict.** Not king against ghost: mockery against hospitality. The king treats Hatim's fame as a joke; Hatim answers the joke literally and then pays for it. The king's arc is smug (half lids, chin up) to frightened to ashamed (150.24) to grateful (151.14).

**Surprises.** (1) The joke is answered. (2) The meal is his own camel: he realises it mid belly-pat at 80.78. (3) The host pays him back by a messenger who could not have known.

**Ending.** The first frame's welcome pose returns: a faint Hatim with open arms at his grave, far behind the riding king. Moral in one picture, plus the counter.

## 3. Character acting

- **King.** Starts with `lid 0.4`, chin up, chest out (`sq 1.04`), and a showman's hands (`cupMouth`, `armsOut`, `pointDown`, belly pats, `palmsUp`). The `wink 'R'` at 43.56 makes the companions his audience. Fear at 60.78-63: `eyes 'wide'`, `noBlink`, `tremble(t, 3)`, `headHold`, one look to camera. After the meal his hands slow down. Shame at 150.24 (eyes break away, head drops, `lid 0.6`), then a small true smile and a head bow at 151.14. The crown is his barometer: glint (23.2), hop (124.9, 131.16), askew (104.78), straightened (119.9).
- **Hatim.** Three gestures only, each repeated: open arms, hand on chest with a small bow, the empty bowl. In the dream he is regretful, never angry: `brow -0.5`, `lid 0.3`, slow moves, no blink, glide instead of walk.
- **comp1 / comp2.** Never in unison (`stagger`). comp1 polite and steady; comp2 sees danger first (statues at 28.78, one eye open at 55.32, under the blanket at 57.62), wakes first, eats fastest, and gets the skewer gulp (100.30).
- **Adi.** Calm, upright, economical; his father's hand-on-chest gesture (122.58, 153.26). His stillness in the pause at 137.3-138.3 is the suspense before the answer.
- **Camels.** `walk()` pacing gait with head bob and tassels a beat late; riders lag 2-3 frames (`lurch` on stops). The king's camel: sway, buckle, held brink, `fallY`, `impactSquash`, `rebound`, thrash fading to `lid 0.7`. The gift camel: light step, long-lash blinks, sniffing the crown.
- Everyone has a gaze target at all times (`lookAt`, `gazePlan`; saccades with ease 0.07 at 37.92, 50.74, 116.54, 132.48) and blinks (`LIFE.t`).

## 4. Camera and composition

- graveValley is built on one diagonal: sleepers and camp lower left, grave and statues upper right. Sound arcs, the king's shout, the mist and Hatim's descent all travel on it.
- Establishing crane up the rock (8.2); pan reveal of the ledge over the riders (27.42); low angle for the mock invitation; tilts to the silent grave as punctuation (36.78, 52.22, 75.56). The tilt at 75.56 is also how the slaughter stays off screen.
- Push-ins on decisions and realisations (58.2 the mist, 85.4 the telling, 131-136.7 the astonishment). One smash cut (60.78), one whip pan (66.98), one panel-only shake (70.62).
- Dialogue in `twoShot` with both faces kept; S17 lowers the two-shot so the cloud owns the top half.
- Final wide: rider lower right, mountain upper left, title centred in the upper sky clear of both.
- All faces and key action stay inside the picture panel (world y < 1230; heads usually y 520-760).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the laugh nobody shares; the upturned bowl and crumb; comp2's "not me"; the king wedged behind his companion; Adi scanning for a king and skipping him; the crown.
- **Suspense:** stillness (pupils only) at 37.92; the pebble at 52.22; the dying fire and the mist; the thud in the pause at 60.3; Adi's silent smile at 137.8.
- **Surprise:** smash cut to open eyes; the picture balloon of Hatim's face on "ابن حاتم".
- **Emotion and meaning:** setup and payoff pairs (welcome pose 0.0 / 157.08; chest bow 3.82 / 153.26 / 160.54; empty bowl 50.2 / 93.18; scream 62.94 / 83.76; fall 70.62 / 97.74); the GUESTS FED counter (2, then 5, then 6...) states the moral without a word.
