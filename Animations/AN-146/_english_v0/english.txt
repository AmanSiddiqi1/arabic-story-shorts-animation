A former thief repented and became an honest cloth merchant. He ran his shop for some time and closed it each evening before going home.

One night another thief came dressed like the merchant, carrying keys. He asked the market watchman to light a candle, pretending to check his accounts.

While the watchman went to get a flame, the impostor opened the shop. The guard returned to find him bent over account books, apparently calculating figures.

At dawn the impostor asked for a camel-driver. He loaded four large cloth bales, departed with them, and tipped the watchman two dirhams.

The real merchant arrived to unusual friendliness from the guard, who thought he had been tipped. Inside, the merchant noticed candle wax and missing bales.

He questioned the watchman, found the camel-driver and learned where the goods had gone. The driver led him to a storeroom near the port.

The merchant found his four bales still bound as before, with the thief’s cloak thrown over them. He recovered the goods and took the cloak as well.

The thief followed, admitting that the merchant had recovered his goods and asking for his cloak back. The merchant laughed, returned it, and they parted.
