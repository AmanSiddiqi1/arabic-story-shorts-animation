All times are word starts on the final MP3 from `inputs/timeline.md`. ## 1. Narrative breakdown
**Logline.** A court musician rides a mystery basket up to a hidden salon, lies about who he is, and ends up carrying the caliph there in a second basket, where one careless word turns a secret visit into a royal wedding.

**Spine.** Leaving court at night (0.0), Isaac finds a basket on ropes (9.58), sits in it out of curiosity and drink (17.64) and is hauled up as "the expected guest" (21.22). A young lady receives him (33.38); he tells her about the basket but hides that he is a musician (43.46) and calls himself a merchant (47.52). He comes back on nights 2 and 3 and loses track of time (57.6), then slips out of court without leave (62.58); the caliph is furious (65.88). The confession turns anger into delight (79.18) and the caliph wants to come (82.56). They hide their names (86.32) and rise in two baskets (92.08). The lady sings (100.4); Isaac guards the secret (104.52) but the caliph blurts his real name and orders him to sing (106.6, 109.16). She understands who the plain guest is (117.76) and withdraws (119.6). The house is Hasan bin Sahl's and she is his daughter Khadijah (126.56, 130.52); the caliph asks for her hand with a great dowry (139.42, 142.38). Isaac is sworn to silence (150.18), keeps it until the caliph's death (155.44), and then tells how a strange basket led to a wedding in the palace (159.36, 167.2).

**Conflict.** Two secrets pull against each other: Isaac hides his trade from the lady and his visits from the caliph; then both men hide who they are. The comedy is that the man with the most to hide gives it away himself.

**Surprises.** The rope jerking tight (19.3); the angry caliph melting (79.18); the slip of the name (106.6); the ghost crown (117.76); the dowry chest (142.38).

**Emotional arc.** Curiosity, wonder, charm and small lies, fear before the throne, relief, shared mischief, panic, embarrassment, tenderness, loyalty, a quiet loss, fond memory, celebration.

**Ending and moral.** Curiosity can open an unexpected door, a secret is safest with a loyal friend, and the truth slips out in the end. The last picture is the wedding with Isaac playing the same strum as the first shot (a bookend), then THE END at 168.9 held to 172.39.

## 3. Character acting
- **Isaac** (person, costume merchant in green with a yellow turban, oud always with him): a charmer who is a bad liar. Signature moves: eyes shut when he plays; saccades (gazePlan ease 0.07) whenever he is hiding something; his turban slips when he is startled and he pushes it straight. The rope-hauling HEAVE mime (arm angles aL/aR alternating) is set up by the servants in S3, repeated by him in S5 and by his old self in S18. Fear before the throne: `tremble`, `wet`, shoulders up, gaze up then down. Old Isaac: `age` 0.95, slower blinks, the same smile.
- **Al-Ma'mun** (costume caliph, age 0.25): large, confident gestures. Anger = stand up from the throne with `anticipate`, `brow` 1, the planned `flush`, crown jumping a frame late. The melt at 79.18 is one continuous change of `brow` from 1 to -0.5 with a long blink and a lean in. In disguise he behaves like a happy tourist (roaming gaze, giggle into his hand, waving from the basket); the command at 109.16 is the first moment he stands like a king again (`sq` 1.05, chin up, `pointDown`), which is what gives him away.
- **Khadijah** (costume lady): composed and clever. She enters curious, not afraid; hides a laugh (`mouthCover`) at the basket story; one raised brow and a glance at the oud neck at 48.5 tells us she half sees through the "merchant". Realisation 111.52-118.58 is slow: gaze travels, `lid` opens, a small "!", then hands to cheeks and a fast bow; her veil ends lag 2 frames on every move. At the wedding: shy smile, eye contact with the caliph (`lookAt` both ways).
- **Hasan** (costume vizier, age 0.7): hurry, shock, bow; honest open palms for "not married"; `clasp` and `wet` eyes for joy.
- **Maids and servants**: never in unison (`stagger`); maids are the audience inside the scene (they exchange looks); servants strain on the rope, tongue out.
- **Everyone**: `LIFE.t = t` for blinks, a gaze target at all times, `breathe` on holds, `settleTo` on pose changes, `walk` + `footfalls` for every step, `hop` + `landSquash` into the baskets, `swing` and `lurch` on the ropes.

## 4. Camera and composition
- The story is vertical, so the camera is too: the crane up the rope (11.98-13.9), the lift (21.22-24.6) and the double lift (92.08-94.5) all travel about 1260 world units up the tall alley set. The basket rises in pulls and the camera rides with it.
- Court scenes: wide for arrivals and exits, push in on the caliph's anger, tight private two-shot for the confession with the thought cloud above the heads.
- Salon scenes: `twoShot` for the first meeting with a slow push; medium on the two guests for the slip, widening on the name so the lady's reaction is in frame; close on her for the realisation, then a pull out for the crown and her exit.
- Faces stay between y 560 and 720 of the world in most shots; balloons, clouds, name plates and portrait circles use the space above the arches. Nothing important below y 930.
- Transitions: cuts on the narration pauses; one card "NEXT NIGHT..." at 49.72; a dissolve from the empty throne to the storyteller; a zoom through the thought cloud into the wedding.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour:** the hiccup and the slipping turban; HEAVE! HO!; the oud sticking out on both sides of the man who says he is a merchant; the candle melting to a stub while he sits in the same pose; the caliph enjoying his basket ride; "ME TOO!"; the tag SECRET cracking in two.
- **Suspense:** the rope going taut; tiptoeing out behind the laughing caliph (dramatic irony); knees knocking before the throne; the frozen half second after "ISAAC!" where only pupils move.
- **Surprise:** anger to delight; the ghost crown settling on the plain turban; the chest of gold.
- **Emotion:** the hand on the heart at 139.42; Hasan's tears of joy; the hand on the shoulder and the sealed lips at dawn; the lamp going out over the empty throne; old Isaac's eyes closing over the memory.
- **Running signs:** the lock tag SECRET (appears 74.28, cracks 107.96, whole again 150.9, opens 157.2); the basket (dark, lit, doubled, in a cloud, garlanded at the wedding); music notes whenever art is in the air.
