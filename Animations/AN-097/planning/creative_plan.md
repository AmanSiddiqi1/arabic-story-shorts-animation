# AN-097 The Generous Dealing of Yahya the Barmecide with Mansur: creative plan

Film length 171.24 s (MP3 168.24 s + 3.0 s THE END). All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard (17 scenes, 155 word-cued actions, subtitle chunks) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction behind it.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A debtor must find a million dirhams before sunset. The generous Yahya raises it all, adds a famous jewel to win the caliph's mercy, and forgives the frightened man's ungrateful words.

**Conflict.** A clock (sunset) against an impossible sum. Then a second, quieter conflict: will kindness survive ingratitude?

**Beats (cue word, time).**
1. Setup: the caliph's order (أمر 0.00). Inciting: a million before sunset (مليون 9.00); the hourglass starts.
2. Threat (فسيعاقب 16.72), shown as a prison icon only.
3. Mansur has a sliver of the sum; fear freezes him (فاشتد 25.82).
4. Turn: the stern guard pities him (رق 29.86) and takes him to Yahya, revealed giving to the needy (يحيى 34.18).
5. Comic low point: the strongbox holds 5,000 and a moth (خمسة 52.26). Yahya acts without a pause (فبعث 54.20).
6. LATER (60.8). Climax of the money plot: the total passes a million (يكفي 68.78), and more.
7. Surprise: the jewel (جوهرة 73.64). Yahya's hope in a thought cloud (يأمل 92.66): a heart on the caliph.
8. Second turn: Mansur's fearful verses (أنشد 110.02); Salih's rebuke (استنكر 117.66).
9. Reveal and resolution: Harun knows the jewel (عرف 135.86), the hoped-for heart appears, he returns it (فأعادها 140.78).
10. Surprise: Yahya hears of the verses and is not angry (يغضب 149.46). Moral (كرم 165.56).

**Emotional arc.** Dread, pity, hope, comic dismay, relief and joy, a sour note of shame, then warmth. **Ending.** Dawn; Mansur walks free; Yahya is already giving to the next person and waves away thanks. **Moral.** True generosity does not wait for thanks, and it forgives what fear makes people say.

## 3. Character acting

- **Harun** (`person` costume `caliph`, age 0.35, seated `sit: 1, seat: 'bench'` on the throne). Authority through stillness: he moves one arm at a time; `brow` 0.6 to 0.9, narrowed `lid` 0.3, `tip` 0.10 when he threatens. His whole change is in S14: eyes wide, `brow` -0.7, then `wet`, a long blink, hand to heart (`PERSON_HANDS.chest`), smile. Suppress blinks during the stare at 12.3-16.7.
- **Salih** (planned `soldier` preset, spear). Stiff march (`walk`, `footfalls`, heel-click `landSquash`), clipped bows (`tip` 0.2, `lid` 1). The gulp at 18.14 (`pulse` drop, long blink) plants his humanity; at 29.86 the `brow` eases from 0.4 to -0.4 and he leans the spear away before touching Mansur. Indignation in S12: `brow` 0.9, wagging `pointFwd`, `tip` 0.08. He is the audience's eyes: give him a reaction to every surprise (the 5,000, the million, the jewel, Yahya's mercy at 158.02).
- **Mansur** (`merchant`, green palette, s 0.95). Always a little too big in his reactions: `PERSON_HANDS.cheeks` and `tremble` at 25.82, collapse onto the stool (`sit` blend + `landSquash`), saccades with `gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`, `wet` eyes, a hop of joy with `anticipate`/`hop`/`landSquash` at 70.02, a bow of `tip` 0.3 at 82.64. In S11 the fear returns from outside in: glances at the sun, slowing walk, shiver, then the recital with a raised hand and averted eyes. Shame pattern at 124.34 (look up, eyes break away, head drops). His turban tassel lags every head move by 2 frames.
- **Yahya** (`vizier`, age 0.8). Default face: `lid` 0.15, smile, `breathe`. He never hesitates: the decision at 43.28 and the order at 54.20 follow the bad news with no hold, which is the character. Gestures are small and open (`palmsUp`, `armsOut`, low `wave` to refuse thanks). The one long stillness is 146.24-149.4 (only pupils move, then eyes shut and a breath) so the audience fears anger before the smile.
- **Listeners.** Every scene sets both gazes with `lookAt`. Groups react with `stagger` (3-4 frames): the lean over the strongbox at 50.14, the two lids at 64.26, the two bows in S4.
- **Secondary motion.** Keys on the treasurer's sash (`swing`), spear tip trailing turns, sack squash on footfalls (`kick`), caps lagging on the runners, casket glints.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, floor y 900 (dais top y 860). Faces stay between y 560 and y 760; counters, hourglass and balloons live in the top 300 units, never over a face.
- Establishing wides open each new place (S1, S4 landing, S7, S11, S13). Dialogue is in two-shots built with `twoShot(a, b, {margin})`. Push-ins mark decisions and feelings: the threat (S2), the fear (S3), the jewel insert (S8, zoom 2.6), Harun's recognition (S14), Yahya's mercy (S16).
- Rhymes: S13 repeats S1's framing with the sun now on the horizon; S17 repeats S4's blocking. The door arch on the left of every hall is the film's clock face: `skyCycle` shows through it from u 0.30 to dusk, night and dawn.
- Travel is always left to right toward the caliph; the pair leave Yahya's hall by the left arch and the street tracks right. In S11 Mansur leads, in S12's exit Salih leads: the order of the walkers tells who holds the moral high ground.
- Transitions: cuts on words; one whip (33.42) into Yahya's hall; one LATER... card (60.8) in the 60.38-61.20 pause while the sons' money travels.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Suspense:** the hourglass and the sinking sun are in almost every shot until 128.0, where the last grain hangs as the money lands.
- **Humour:** the huge counter against six coins; the dragged "heavy" strongbox that holds ten coins and a moth; the treasurer's look to camera; the bar that overflows at "and more"; Salih swatting the verse balloon to scraps.
- **Surprise:** the jewel after the money already suffices; Yahya's smile after the stillness at 146-149.
- **Emotion:** Salih's softening; Mansur's weight-of-the-sack hug at 81.46; the heart Yahya imagines at 101.48 appearing for real at 139.14; Yahya mending the cracked heart with two fingers at 150.84.
- **Clarity of the moral:** at 153.8-157.6 FEAR and SORROW literally squeeze the verse out of a small Mansur, so the forgiveness is seen, not only told.
