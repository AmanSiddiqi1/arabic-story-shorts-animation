## Remaining notes (polish, optional; do not trigger v3 by themselves)

### 1. 154.2-156.9 (S16, والحزن 154.38 to يقول 157.06): SORROW label is cut off by the balloon edge
What is wrong: the SORROW label box extends past the right side of the idea balloon and is clipped by the balloon's outline, so it reads "SORROV" with the last letter half missing (`extra/z_moral.png`, both frames). FEAR on the left fits.
Why it matters: it is one of two words in the film's lesson picture and it is on screen for 2.7 s.
Fix: move the SORROW label left by about 45-50 units inside the balloon (its right edge at least 15 units inside the balloon outline at that height), or lower both labels by about 25 units to where the ellipse is wider; keep size 42. Alternatively draw the labels after the balloon clip is released so they may overhang the outline whole, as FEAR's box already does at the top-left. Check stills at 154.6 and 156.4.

### 2. 54.6-56.4 (S6, فبعث 54.63, يطلب 55.28): the two runners arrive stacked on Yahya
What is wrong: runners A and B enter together from the right and stand overlapping each other and Yahya's robe at x about 830-900, half against the panel edge (`extra/m7.png`); only their sprint-offs separate them.
Why it matters: the order "send to both sons" would be crisper if the audience could count two messengers before they leave.
Fix: move Yahya's standing position about 90 units left for 54.2-57.2 (or shift the camera right by the same amount), stop runner A at x about 800 and have runner B enter only after A has turned to run (B in at 55.6, stop at about 820, out by 57.15), so the two are never side by side.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library

- Hero casket (`a97CasketHero`): opaque lid hinged behind the box, jewel lifting with overshoot, thin tapered rays inside a fixed radius. This is the working form of the "glowing object never at face height" rule proposed in round 1.
- Thought cloud that grows to panel width for its story and shrinks back before the cut (93.5-103.4): a good general pattern for "what he hopes will happen", with content scale about 1.0 so figures inside stay above 110 px.
- Idea balloon as a diagram (person squeezed between two labelled forces, then the result pops out): reusable for any spoken moral. Guide rule to add with it: labels inside a clipped balloon must be placed where the ellipse is wide enough, or drawn outside the clip.
- Thought disc on a cream, outlined circle to separate a dark icon from a busy background (108.2-110.5).
