Verdict: REVISE

# AN-097 Fable review, round 1 (v1 cloud render, 171.24 s)

Reviewed: all 29 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` in order, plus full-size detail frames in `review/v1_pack/extra/` (m1..m6 montages, `full_96.8.png`), against `planning/creative_plan.md` and the word timings in `v1_pack/narration.txt`.

## Summary

The story is told in order, every narrated event is on screen, and nothing is broken: no bad frames, no missing ending, subtitles verbatim, readable and in sync (Arabic large, English blue below, both clear at full size). There are no BLOCKERs. Several things work well and should be kept exactly as they are: the 1,000,000 counter against six coins (S3), the strongbox with ten coins and a moth (50.7-53.7), the running total and ENOUGH! / AND MORE! (66-71.5), Salih swatting the verse balloon with POP! (118.7), the fake-out stillness of Yahya before the smile (147.7-149.4), the mended heart (150.5-152), and the dawn ending with Mansur walking free.

The verdict is REVISE because four of the film's key story beats are weakly staged or hard to read: the jewel reveal hides the giver's face, Yahya's hope plays as an 11 s near-static miniature whose payoff (the softened heart) cannot be seen, the moral picture (FEAR and SORROW press the verse out of Mansur) is tiny and sits on Salih's head, and the fear that drives the verses is barely acted. These are staging changes only; no narration, subtitle or timing change is needed.

## Notes

### 1. 73.6-79.6 (S8, جوهرة 73.64 to الخليفة 79.56) MAJOR: the jewel reveal covers Yahya's face
What is wrong: in the close shot the open casket, its lid and the fat ray sticks sit exactly over Yahya's face from 73.7 until the cut at 79.7 (see `extra/m1.png`, fourth frame). His face is only seen through the lid as a ghost. The jewel itself is small (about 45 px on a 1080 frame) and the rays read as a bundle of sticks, not light.
Why it matters: this is the surprise of the film and the object the whole second half depends on (Harun must "know it" at 135.86). The audience needs to see both the stone clearly and the giver's warm, slightly sly face.
Fix: hold the casket at belt/chest height, not face height: hand targets about 60-70 units lower so the casket top stays below his beard (casket centre near his sash), and hinge the lid away from camera (lid drawn behind the box, opening upward-back, opaque). Draw the jewel 1.8-2x larger and lift it 10-15 units out of the box on جوهرة (73.64) with a small `pop`/overshoot; replace the thick rays by 8-10 thin tapered lines at 40 % alpha that rotate slowly, kept inside a radius that does not reach his face. Yahya: `gaze` down to the jewel at 73.64, then up to camera with a smile and one slow `blink` at نفيسة (about 74.5). Keep the memory inset and HE WILL KNOW IT label, but move the label below the inset so it does not touch the turban (it overlaps the turban at 78.2-79.5).

### 2. 91.6-103.4 (S10, يأمل 92.66 to المديون 104.00) MAJOR: Yahya's hope is an 11 s static miniature and the heart payoff is not visible
What is wrong: from 93.0 to 103.4 the frame is one fixed wide shot; everything that changes happens inside the cloud at 40-70 px figure height (`extra/full_96.8.png`, `extra/m2.png` frames 2-3). The "!" at 94.2 is visible, but the planned softening (100.72), the heart (101.48) and the freed Mansur (102.18) cannot be made out even at full size at 101.9. Yahya's own face has brows pulled down and reads stern or worried, not hopeful. Salih in the cloud balances the casket on his helmet with both hands empty in the air.
Why it matters: this is the set-up for the pay-off at 139.14 (the plan's "heart Yahya imagines appearing for real"). If the imagined heart is not seen here, the rhyme is lost, and 11 s without a readable change breaks the "something every 1-2 s" standard.
Fix: (a) after the cloud has formed (about 93.5), push the camera into the cloud so it fills the panel width (zoom about 1.7-1.9 centred on the cloud, eased over 0.6 s) and stay there until 102.8, then pull back to Yahya for the fade at 103.36; figures inside must be at least 110 px on screen (Capabilities Guide, thought cloud rule). (b) Stage three clear steps inside: on الجوهرة (about 95.4) Harun leans to the casket with "!"; on فيدرك (96.57) a dotted line from the jewel to a Yahya portrait plus the chests icon (already there, keep, make the portrait 1.5x); on فيلين قلبه (100.72-101.48) a big red `heart` (at least 70 px on screen) pops on Harun's chest with a pulse, his `brow` goes to -0.6 and he smiles; on الرجل المديون (102.18) cloud-Mansur's arms go up and a small broken-bars icon drops away. (c) Yahya outside the cloud: `brow` -0.3, soft smile, `gaze` up-left into the cloud, slow `breathe`, one hopeful nod at 101.5. (d) Casket in the cloud held in Salih's two hands in front of his chest (see note 6).

### 3. 152.4-158.9 (S16, الخوف 153.84, والحزن 154.38, يقول 157.06) MAJOR: the moral picture is tiny and stands on Salih's head
What is wrong: at 153.7 the camera pulls back and a 60 px Mansur appears on a thin line directly above Salih's helmet and spear, with FEAR / SORROW labels and two small icons in the top-left corner (`extra/m4.png` frames 2-3). It looks as if Mansur is standing on Salih. The squeeze (155.6) and the verse scroll popping out (157.06) are a few pixels of motion. Yahya, the speaker, is pushed off-centre and his teaching finger is gone by 155.
Why it matters: the plan calls this the place where "the forgiveness is seen, not only told". It is the lesson of the film and currently the least readable picture in it.
Fix: put the diagram in a large speech/idea balloon belonging to Yahya: balloon about 560 x 380 units centred near (430, 300) with its tail to Yahya's mouth, appearing on قال (about 152.9). Inside, Mansur at 0.9-1.0 scale (at least 130 px on screen). FEAR (dark cloud with bars) drops in from the left on الخوف and SORROW (big tear) from the right on والحزن, each with its label at >= 28 px (1280x720 scale) and a short squash on contact; on يجعلان (155.6) both press and Mansur's `sq` goes to 0.8 with `tremble`; on يقول (157.06) the verse scroll shoots out of his mouth with a small POP and he looks at it in dismay (`eyes: 'wide'`, hands to cheeks). Move Salih to the left third (x about 200) so nothing overlaps him, and keep Yahya's raised finger and `talk` through 152.7-157.6. Salih's bowed head at 158.02 then reads as his answer; hold Yahya's hand on Salih's arm (already there at 158.0-158.7, good).

### 4. 104.0-110.0 (S11, منصور 104.5, غلبه 107.56, الخوف 108.14) MAJOR: "overcome by fear" is not acted
What is wrong: after the first step the two men stand almost still for five seconds (compare 105.0 and 109.7 in the strips; `extra/m5.png` frames 1-2). The prison cloud at 107.56 is drawn at 0.55 scale, grey on a busy grey-brown wall, half behind the lamp post, and reads as a stain. Mansur's face is close to neutral; the tremble (2.5 units) is invisible at this framing.
Why it matters: the whole moral rests on the claim that fear, not ingratitude, made him speak. If the fear is not seen growing here, Salih's rebuke looks simply right and Yahya's forgiveness looks unexplained.
Fix: keep both walking (use `walk` + `footfalls`, world scrolling) from 104.0 to 107.4, Mansur a step ahead; glance back at the sinking sun/hourglass at 104.3 and 105.1 with a visible head turn (`tip`, `dir` flick), each glance slowing his stride. On غلبه (107.56) he stops (`landSquash`), and the prison cloud pops in at scale 1.1-1.2 directly above his head on a cream thought-bubble disc with a black outline so it separates from the wall; pulse it on الخوف (108.14). Mansur: `eyes: 'wide'`, `brow` -0.9, `wet`, two sweat drops, `tremble` 5-6, sack sagging (lower hand target by 15), knees dipping (`sq` 0.93). Push the camera in at 107.5 instead of 110.0 (the current push at 110.0-110.6 is good, just start it earlier) so the face is large when the fear lands. Salih keeps walking one more step, then turns back to him at 108.6: that gives the recital at أنشد (110.02) a listener.

### 5. 135.86-140.78 (S14, عرف 135.86, وتأثر 138.38) MAJOR: Harun's recognition is cluttered and the feeling is thin
What is wrong: at 136.0-136.3 the red "!" burst sits on Harun's left eye and crown (`extra/m3.png` frame 2) and the ray sticks cross his robe; the memory inset first appears in the top-left corner, then jumps at 137.0 to a small disc beside his face with a YAHYA'S GIFT label that covers Salih's helmet and Harun's shoulder. In "was moved" (138.4-140.7) his eyes close and a heart appears on the casket, but there is no visible tear, no smile and no hand to heart until the cut (`extra/m6.png` frames 1-2); the heart sits on the box, not on him.
Why it matters: this is the climax and the pay-off of note 2. It should be the clearest face in the film.
Fix: place the "!" above the crown, clear of the face (about 70 units higher and to the right). Keep the casket low on his lap (hands at lap height) so chest and face are free. One inset only: a 200 px disc in the top-left that stays put from 136.32 to 138.2, showing Yahya holding the same jewel (reuse the 73.6 pose), with the label under it. On وتأثر (138.38): slow blink, `brow` -0.7, `wet` with one clearly drawn tear on the cheek at 138.9, right hand to `PERSON_HANDS.chest` by 139.0, and the `heart` (80 px or more) popping on his chest beside that hand at بكرم (139.14) with two pulses; mouth to a smile under the moustache (raise the mouth corners or use the open smile so it reads). Salih: his eyes go from the jewel to Harun's face and his brows lift at 139.2 (he is the audience's eyes).

### 6. 128.1-131.6 (S13, الخليفة 128.0 to والجوهرة 131.2) MAJOR: Salih balances the casket on his helmet; PAID! is unreadable
What is wrong: Salih kneels with both arms up and empty hands while the casket floats on top of his helmet (`extra/m5.png` frames 3-4, `extra/m3.png` frame 1). The PAID! stamp is drawn on top of the 1,000,000 burst in the top-left corner, so neither can be read (`extra/m3.png` frame 1); the hourglass "last grain" and its tick at 128.0-128.7 are about 25 px and pass unnoticed.
Why it matters: the deadline is the film's clock; the moment it is beaten should be a clear, satisfying beat, and the offering pose should look respectful, not comic.
Fix: casket at the midpoint of Salih's two hands, held out at chest height toward the throne (hand targets about [40, -105] and [70, -105] relative to him, `tip` 0.15 bow), lifting 20 units on الجوهرة. Meter: show the 1,000,000 burst centred at the top (x about 500, y 150) at 129.86, then stamp PAID! diagonally over the lower-right corner of the burst only, or directly below it (y about 250), never fully over the digits. Hourglass: on المال (about 129.0) scale it up 2x with a `pulse` and hold the last grain for 0.5 s, then the green tick at full size; let it shrink back by 130.4.

### 7. 54.2-57.2 (S6, فبعث 54.63, يطلب 55.28, من 56.60) MAJOR: the dispatch of the runners is a muddle at the right edge
What is wrong: after the cut to the wide at 54.2 the treasurer and two runners are bunched behind and to the right of Yahya, half outside the panel (`extra/m1.png` frame 3); the red-capped runner then crosses in front of the strongbox at 55.5-57.5 overlapping the treasurer and Mansur. It is not clear that Yahya is sending two messengers in two directions "without delay".
Why it matters: the plan's point is that Yahya never hesitates; the order should be one crisp readable action.

Yahya stands on فبعث (54.63) with a `pointFwd` arm; runner A (red cap) steps in from the right to x about 760, bows, turns and sprints out through the left arch between 55.3 and 56.4 with a dust puff and speed lines; runner B (blue cap) follows the same path 0.6 s later (out by 57.2). Move the treasurer to the far right, still. Mansur and Salih turn their heads to follow each runner (`gaze` sweep with `stagger`). The son insets at 57.30 and 58.22 are good; add a small arrow from each running figure's exit to its inset if space allows.

### 8. 61.0-71.5 (S7) MINOR: Mansur and Salih are cut by the left edge during the film's happiest beat

On يكفي (68.78) give Mansur a full `anticipate` + `hop` + `landSquash` with both arms up and Salih a small approving nod; a slight camera nudge left (or zoom 0.95) keeps Yahya, both chests and the pair in frame.

### 9. 83.0-84.2 (S9, شكر 83.99) MINOR: THANK YOU! comes from the wrong man
What is wrong: the balloon tail points at Salih's spear tip (`extra/m2.png` frame 1) while Mansur, the one who thanks, is bowing a figure-width to the right with his head buried in Yahya's side; the balloon also starts at 83.0, a second before the word شكر.

### 10. 131.6-135.8 and 140.8-142.8 (S13/S14) MINOR: Mansur is clipped at the left edge when he matters
What is wrong: during Salih's whisper and Harun's frown (131.6-135.8) Mansur is half off the left edge, so his shrinking (134.58) is lost; at 141.7 he is again a sliver at the edge and the planned "waved free" does not read.

Fix: for 131.6-135.8 use a three-figure framing (camera x about 470, zoom about 1.25) or move Mansur to x about 300; give him `sq` 0.9, `lid` 0.6, eyes to the floor on منصور (134.9). At 141.74 Harun's free hand flicks toward the door; Mansur straightens, bows quickly and takes two steps to the arch before the cut at 142.84.

### 11. 20.7-29.6 and 51.5-56.3 (meter) MINOR: FOUND line too small, counter over the shelf

Why it matters: the gap between 600 (or 5,000) and 1,000,000 is the joke; the small number must be readable.

### 12. 72.0-82.6 and 91.7-103.7 (continuity) MINOR: the hourglass disappears

Why it matters: small continuity slip in the film's clock; its return at 82.7 pops.
Fix: keep it on screen through 79.7-82.6 (the hand-over) at least; it may stay hidden in the two close inserts and inside-cloud push, but fade it in/out over 0.3 s instead of popping.

## Subtitles and English

Verbatim against the pack, well timed, readable. English reads naturally; no change requested. (The plan's noted P7 gap is resolved in the current English: 140.78-147.86 carries "and returned it to him. Yahya also heard what the man had said after he helped him.")

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library

- The target meter (burst counter + bar + small "found" figure) with ENOUGH!/AND MORE! overflow: a good generic "goal vs. have" prop once the found-figure size is fixed.
- The fake-out stillness before a kind reaction (147.7-149.4: eyes shut, brows down, only then the smile) as a named acting pattern in the Capabilities Guide.
- Icon balloon "swatted to scraps" (118.6-118.9) as a reusable rebuke gesture.
- Proposed rule for the guide: a held object with rays or glow must never be drawn at the holder's face height in a close shot; hold it at chest/lap and keep rays inside a radius that clears the face.

Notes 1-5 re-stage five key beats (jewel reveal, hope cloud, fear on the road, recognition, moral diagram) with new camera moves and larger balloon contents; these are exactly the places where overlaps, clipping and readability problems tend to appear, so v2 should be checked on those time ranges (73-80, 91-104, 104-110, 128-141, 152-159) before the final render.
