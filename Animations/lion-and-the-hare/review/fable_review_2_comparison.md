# The Lion and the Hare: creative director review 2 (v1 against the original)

Reviewed: `renders/The Lion and the Hare - v1.mp4` (172.2 s) against the original. All 29 v1 strips at 4 fps were read in order and compared with the original strips, plus 10 fps pulls of the leap and fall (141.0-143.7) and of the climb (145.4-148.1), sixteen larger stills (9.6, 47.5, 61.6, 62.3, 99.9, 106.5, 108.2, 111.8, 131.0, 133.5, 134.5, 135.0, 138.1, 139.8, 146.2, 168.6) and a crop of the puddle at 62.0. Audio, loudness and subtitle QC are taken from Opus's report; I did not re-measure them. Blinks, gaze tracking and turn eases cannot be fully judged at 4 fps: where I say so below, it is from sampled frames only.

## Verdict

**v1 is meaningfully better than the original, and it is not yet final.** The three scenes that carry the story (the den, the reflection, the fall) now play as acting instead of labels. Five corrections are worth making before release; the largest are that the reflected face is still too small and the puddle shows no reflection at all.

## 3. Are the expressions, movements and interactions convincing?

- **Den (72-109): yes.** The lion closes in step by step, the hare trembles, swallows and then performs; the bow flatters, the squint doubts, the insult lands in the mane, and the hare's smile behind his back completes the scene. This is the standard the rest should reach.
- **Well edge (116-129): yes.** The two-shot gives the hare a listener; the lion's hesitation and squint into the dark set up the cut well.
- **Reflection (129-141): the idea works, the picture undersells it.** The mirror behaviour is correct and readable, but the face occupies a small centre of a frame mostly filled by empty rings and a large flat mane.
- **Fall and trap (141-148): mostly.** The dive, the disappearance under water and the wet, blinking surfacing are convincing. The climb is a bob, not an attempt.
- **Forest scenes (0-44, 149-172): only slightly better than before.** Cleaner and in sync, but the group still behaves as one block, and the bargain is unchanged.

## 4. New defects in v1

1. **POV composition (129.09-133.88, 137.3-140.5).** Reflected face about 150 px wide. The foreground mane is a large flat red shape whose two ringed ears sit at its front edge like eyes, so it reads as a second creature looking at us.
2. **Puddle without a reflection (60.5-65.0).** The code comments describe one; none is visible in the render.
3. **Thought cloud collisions (89.7-102.7).** The enlarged cloud rests on the lion's mane from 96.0 and he rises into it at 99.7-102.5; "I AM KING!" is clipped by the cloud edge (reads ", AM KING!" at 99.0-102.5); the cloud's hare is cut by the left edge from 98.0.
4. **Characters drawn on top of each other.** Lion's muzzle on the grey hare 9.0-10.3; hero on the grey hare 45.0-57.5; hare on the lion's muzzle 111.2-112.3 and 115.4-116.0; lion's mane and tail on the hare 142.7-143.3; fox on the tan hare 168.0-172.2 (reads as the fox holding him in its mouth, under "NO MORE FEAR!" and "THE END").
5. **Reflection vanishes early in the fall.** It fades at 142.9-143.0, half a second before he reaches the water.
6. **Hidden too soon.** Everyone is out of sight at 11.7; the lion stands alone until 15.5.

No sync slips, popping props or cropping faults were seen beyond these.

## 5. Corrections for the final version (prioritized)

1. **Enlarge the reflection and fix the foreground mane (129.09-133.88, 137.3-140.5).** In `wellPOV`: water radius `wr` 190 to about 290; face scale `sc` 1.25 to about 2.0 (lean to 2.2); compress the shaft rings to about 360 / 335 / 312 so the water fills the hole. Foreground mane: lower it by about 80 units so it covers no more than the bottom fifth, draw the ears as plain body-yellow half discs tucked under the mane edge with no inner disc, and add a strip of yellow back behind the mane at the bottom edge so it reads as the back of his head. Keep the hare's ears at two o'clock.
2. **Show the hare's reflection in the puddle (60.5-65.0).** It must be visible: the hare's side view flipped vertically, clipped to the puddle, at about 70% opacity under a light blue tint, drawn after the puddle fill. Widen the puddle to about 260 units if needed so head and ears fit. Appears as he arrives (61.4); copies his lean (62.2) and ear waggle. Bulb stays on "فكرة".
3. **Remove the five overlaps.**
   - 9.0-10.3: stop the lion's advance 90 units earlier, or move the grey hare back with the others.
   - 45.0-57.5: place the hero 110 units to the right of the grey hare so each has clear outline; point the arrow at him.
   - 111.2-112.3 and 115.4-116.0: keep the hare at least 150 units ahead of the lion's nose.
   - 142.7-143.3: shift the dive path about 40 units left (or have the hare lean 40 units right with ears blown back) so mane and tail clear him.
   - 168.0-172.2: stop the fox 160 units short of the tan hare, facing the way it walks.
4. **Clear the thought cloud (89.7-102.7).** Raise the cloud about 70 units and shift it left so its base stays at least 30 units above the lion at full height (99.7 on); move "I AM KING!" fully inside or above the cloud; move the cloud's hare 60 units right so it is whole.
5. **Make the trap read (145.72-147.42).** Two attempts with the paws planted on the wall stones: 146.02 up about 60 units, hold 0.15 s, slide down with two short scratch marks, small splash 146.5; 146.72 up about 30, slide. Hold the close framing until 147.3 and only then pull back; "NO WAY OUT!" after the second slide.
6. **Finish the leap (141.2-143.55).** Deepen the crouch (haunches down about 25 units, held 141.3-141.7) and keep the reflection visible until the splash, growing slightly and wearing his wide-eyed face, so they meet at the surface.
7. **Hold the flight until the words (10.4-13.6).** Flight still starts at 10.4, but keep the animals weaving between trunks until about 13.0 and let the last one dive into the bush on "وتختبئ" (13.20); eyes from 13.6. This also removes the dead wait at 11.7-15.5.
8. **Give the bargain a minimum of life (26.2-38.0).** At 26.2 all flinch back about 30 units, staggered, then hold with a small tremble; the deer's head stays low with eyes flicking up and away during the offer; on "تتوقف عن مطاردتنا" (35.52-37.26) the lion looks away for 1 s with his tail tip flicking before the nod at 38.65.

Items 1-4 are needed before release. Items 5-8 are worthwhile if time allows. The mimed telling, the staggered joys and the bookend framing can be dropped without harm.
