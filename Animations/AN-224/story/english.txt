Hind, daughter of al-Numan, was famed for beauty and wit. She married al-Hajjaj with a promise of a large payment should they divorce, but she was unhappy.

One day al-Hajjaj overheard Hind mocking their marriage in verse while looking in a mirror. Hurt by her words, he decided to divorce her.

He sent Abdullah bin Tahir with the divorce notice and two hundred thousand dirhams. Hind welcomed the news and gave the messenger the entire payment as a reward.

Caliph Abd al-Malik heard of Hind and proposed marriage. She replied with an insulting comparison about her former husband, and the caliph answered cleverly.

Hind demanded that al-Hajjaj lead her camel to the caliph’s city barefoot. Abd al-Malik found the request amusing and ordered him to comply.

Hind rode in her litter as al-Hajjaj walked barefoot holding the camel’s lead. She and her attendants laughed, and she answered him with verse about escaping distress.

Near the city she dropped a dinar but asked him to find a dirham. When he corrected her, she joked that she had gained a dinar in place of a lesser coin.

Embarrassed, al-Hajjaj delivered her to the palace. In the tale Hind became one of Abd al-Malik’s favored wives, ending with a reversal against her former husband.
