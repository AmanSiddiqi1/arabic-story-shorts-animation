## Notes (most important first; all polish)

### 1. 74.0-74.7 s: the missed oar looks caught for a moment
- **What is wrong.** At 74.1 Sinbad's hand overlaps the oar blade, at 74.4 the splash sits on the blade, and only at 74.7 does the oar lift away. For half a second it reads as "he got it", which is the opposite of "لم يصل".
- **Fix.** Keep a visible strip of water between fingertips and blade at all times: stop the reaching hand about one hand's width (30 to 40 world units) short of the blade at "يصل" 74.02, put the splash under the closing hand, not on the oar, and start the skiff's lift and the oar's slide away 0.15 s earlier (from about 73.9). Hold the empty closed hand to 74.5, then let it drop; eyes follow the oar going.

### 2. 68.0-70.0 s: continuity jump into the leap
- **What is wrong.** From 68.0 to 69.9 Sinbad is already chest-deep in open water with the island gone and the tail slapping. At the cut at 70.0 he is standing dry on a small dark disc, higher in frame, under a different sky. He appears to have climbed back out, and the disc reads as a board rather than the whale's back.
- **Fix (either).** (a) In 67.0-69.9 keep him standing on the last patch with water rising only to his knees, so the cut at 70.0 matches; or (b) keep the cut but widen the hump at 70.0 to about 3 figure-widths with the grey-blue skin tone and a grass tuft, water at his ankles, and match the sky to the previous shot. Option (a) is the smaller change: raise his body by the difference and clip at the knees instead of the chest.

### 3. 144.7-149.8 s: give the tail a little longer
- **What is wrong.** The tail is up for about 0.7 s (144.7-145.3) and its fluke sits among the palms on the whale's back, so a first-time viewer can miss it. After 147.7 the sea is empty for 2 s.
- **Fix.** Start the tail lift at 144.5 and hold it up to 145.9 with one slow wave (two extra held drawings), placed at the left end of the hump clear of the palms. Let the hump finish sinking at 148.3 instead of 147.5, leaving rings and the gold light path to the end. Sinbad's answering hand stays up until the hump is gone.

### 4. 95.7-108.7 s: the shore plate (carried over from round 1, note 7)
- **What is wrong.** The beach meets the sea as a brown wedge with a straight seam between two tans, filling the lower left of the frame for 13 s. He is set down on it as a stiff horizontal figure.
- **Fix.** Repaint the edge as a low curved wet-sand band (darker, with a foam scallop line and two or three shells). At 96.6-97.5 one foam sheet runs up the sand with him and drains back; he rolls off the plank with a two-drawing settle before the sit-up at 98.2.

### 5. 109.0-112.0 s: the hall floor
- **What is wrong.** The yellow checker floor fills the bottom 35 to 40% of the panel with nothing on it; this is the one place the owner's "lower picture empty" point still applies.
- **Fix.** Start the shot at the zoom used from 112.2 (floor in the bottom quarter), or dress the floor: carpet runner to the throne, two cushions, a fruit tray, a cat that looks up when the king stands.

### 6. 33.0-39.2 and 45.0-50.0 s: the four men overlap
- **What is wrong.** At the camp the men sit as two overlapping pairs in front of palm trunks, and during the rising water they stand as one block, so individual reactions (plum pointing, the lifted hem) are hard to pick out.
- **Fix.** Spread them by about half a figure-width each and move the pairs clear of the trunks; in 45.0-48.5 keep a gap between the left pair and the right pair until the huddle at 48.7, so the huddle itself becomes an action.

### 7. 127.5-128.2 s: feet on the gangplank
- **What is wrong.** Sinbad crosses to the ship hanging slightly above the plank line.
- **Fix.** `walk` with `footfalls` along the plank's line, plank bending 4 to 6 units under each step.
