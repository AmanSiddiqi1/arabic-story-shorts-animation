# SIND-01 "The Island That Moved" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review, round 1 (v1)

Film reviewed: `Animations/SIND-01/renders/SIND-01_v1.mp4` (149.83 s). Sources: all 25 contact strips in `review/v1_pack/strips/` (4 frames per second), 24 full-resolution panel stills pulled from the MP4 at the moments cited, and a frame-difference scan of the story panel in half-second steps. Times are seconds on the film. Yardstick: the owner direction in `Library/styles/cel-anime-80s/look.md` first, then the plan and its series bible.

## Verdict: REVISE

One BLOCKER (broken frames, note 1) and three places where the film misses the owner's points or the story: the sea shots leave the lower third of the picture empty (note 2), the last picture hides its own payoff (note 3), and the two moments of danger at sea are too small or too mild to feel (notes 4 to 6). Everything is confined to single shots or the sea kit; the structure, the timing and the cast are sound. Because SIND-02 and SIND-06 copy this kit, notes 2 and 7 are worth fixing here once.

Story order is correct, every narrated event is on screen, subtitles are sharp and present in both languages throughout, THE END is present with a 4 s hold.

Not checked: sound was not listened to; twos and threes cannot be judged from stills; the crown hop (113.92) and the point-blank spyglass (127.96) fall between sampled frames and were not seen.

## Notes (most important first)

### 1. BLOCKER 82.7-83.4 s: a hard-edged blue block slides over the close-up
- **What is wrong.** In the plank close-up a translucent blue trapezoid with straight edges enters from the right, covers Sinbad's head and the plank at 83.0 and leaves at 83.3. It is a swell polygon drawn without its wave shape at this zoom. It reads as a rendering fault, on the film's most emotional close-up.
- **Also.** The plan calls this the grip close-up ("بكل قوته" 82.06-82.78), but no hand is on the plank: only the top of his head shows behind the wood.
- **Fix.** Remove the swell from the close-up, or draw it with the same `seaY` crest outline, crest strip and foam as the other layers so it is clearly a wave, passing in front and below his chin, never a flat block. On "بكل" 82.06 bring both forearms over the plank with the fingers curled on the near edge (hands larger than at the wide, 1.3x), knuckles tightening on "قوته" 82.78 (a 2 unit squeeze, shoulders up, lids from 0.5 to 0.2), one drop running off the turban tail. Hold with breathing until the cut at 83.5.

### 2. 15.0-31.5, 52.2-57.5, 70.0-95.5, 122.2-131.0, 136.0-136.6 s: the lower third of every sea shot is empty
- **What is wrong.** With the horizon at world y 600 and the vessel or swimmer on it, the bottom 30 to 40% of the panel is flat cobalt. That is about a third of the film's running time and it is the owner's first point ("do not ... until the lower picture is empty"), and the second ("several layers of things to look at").
- **Why it matters.** The film is mostly sea. The quay and shore scenes look rich, then every sea shot looks unfinished, and the swimmer shots lose Sinbad in blank blue.
- **Fix, in the sea kit so SIND-02 and SIND-06 inherit it.**
  - Framing: in sea wides put the subject's waterline at 62 to 68% of the panel height instead of 48 to 52% (camera y up by about 180 world units, or zoom 1.15 to 1.25 centred below the horizon). The freed sky takes the cumulus, the sun and gulls that are already painted.
  - Add three water layers of interest, all flat cels on the existing wave function: (a) a broken reflection of the hull, sail stripes or swimmer under each floating thing, stepping on threes; (b) a foreground crest band across the bottom 12% with a lighter lip and foam, sliding faster than the others for parallax; (c) life, one item per shot: flying fish arcs (15-22), a pair of dolphins or a fish school shadow crossing (52-57), driftwood, the pot and the lost plum turban floating past the swimmer (72-80), the turtle already planned (93), gulls low over the water near land (95, 136).
  - A wake behind the moving dhow and skiffs, and rings round the swimmer on every stroke.
  - Check: no sea frame with more than about 15% of the panel as unbroken flat blue.

### 3. 141.9-149.8 s: the last picture hides the whale and sidelines Sinbad
- **What is wrong.** On "البحر يخفي عجائب" the camera pans right to the moored dhow. Sinbad ends as a small figure against the left edge, the captain stands large and central with folded arms, and the far whale (145.0-145.4) is a tail a few pixels high directly behind the ship's sterncastle, with a green hump touching the captain's turban. It cannot be read at phone size, and it is gone before THE END. The hold 146-149.8 is the stillest stretch of the film.
- **Why it matters.** This is the payoff of the shadow in the first shot and the picture's own moral (the sea hides wonders, and dangers). As rendered the film ends on a parked boat.
- **Fix.**
  - On "وعرف" 141.94 Sinbad turns from the family to the water: eyes first, then head with one blink, then body; the plan's S1 pose with `lid` 0.25 and the smaller smile. Sister tugging the sash, still looking at him.
  - On "البحر" 143.32 pan right only far enough to put Sinbad at panel x 25 to 30% (full figure, feet on the quay) and open clear water and horizon across the right 60%. Move the dhow out of this frame (crop it to a bow at the far right edge at most) and take the captain out of the picture.
  - On "يخفي" 144.02 the hump rises on the horizon at panel x 65 to 70%, clear of everything, drawn about three times the present size (hump 150 world units wide with its two palms). On "عجائب" 144.62 the tail lifts once like a hand (up over 5 drawings, hold 3, down), a small spout, rings. Keep it visible, slowly sinking, until 147.5.
  - Sinbad: a slow breath, the turban tail and sash end lifting late in the breeze, a half raised hand answering the tail at 145.3, one blink at 146.5. Only the sister sees it too and claps (plan). THE END at 145.6 as now, placed top centre, not over the whale.
  - Gold light: a moving light band on the water under the whale.

### 4. 44.5-52.2 s: the water does not rise
- **What is wrong.** "وبدأت المياه تتصاعد حول أطراف الجزيرة على نحو مخيف" lasts 7 s, and from 45.0 to 52.0 the island looks almost the same: a green strip with dry sand, four men standing in a tight block. The camera drifts and the men huddle at 48.7, but the shoreline does not climb. The spout (51.2) is a white sliver cut by the right panel edge.
- **Why it matters.** This is the turn of the story. Without the water visibly closing in, the captain's shout comes from nowhere.
- **Fix.** One continuous wide at zoom 1.15 to 1.2 with the island filling 70% of the panel width.
  - "وبدأت" 44.56: a foam line appears at both ends of the island; the skiff on the right lifts and bumps.
  - "المياه" 45.70 to "الجزيرة" 48.28: the island sinks in three visible steps (about 12, 28, 45 units), each with a foam surge that runs inward and swallows a named thing: flowers, a bush, the base of the outer palms. The dry strip under the men shrinks to a third.
  - "تتصاعد" 46.28: plum points at the water; fat merchant lifts his robe hem. "حول" 47.24: the planned pan round the rim, eyes of all four following the foam, heads a beat apart.
  - "على نحو" 49.06-49.82: water over their shoes, feet lifting alternately (never mirrored), rings round each ankle; the fire's logs start to steam.
  - "مخيف" 50.34: the ground breathes once (island up 10, down 14); all four drop into a wide stance, arms out, staggered.
  - 51.2 (in the pause): the spout rises from the blowhole mound inside the frame, at least a palm high, with falling drops; everyone's eyes go to it.

### 5. 72.3-76.5 s: the missed oar is too small and at the frame edge
- **What is wrong.** Sinbad is a head about 6% of the panel wide in a full-width wide; the skiff is cut by the right edge; the oar tip and his hand meet near the edge at 74.2-74.7 with a faint spark. His arms barely show above the water, so the swimming reads as a bobbing head.
- **Why it matters.** "لم يصل إلى القارب" is the low point the plan built (the hand that closes on water a hand's width from the oar). It needs to be the biggest thing on screen for one second.
- **Fix.**
  - 72.3-73.4: zoom 1.5 to 1.6 tracking Sinbad left of centre, the skiff's stern and the sailor holding out the oar in the right third, fully in frame. Overarm strokes with the whole forearm clear of the water, alternating, stroke period slowing as planned; rings and spray on each entry; turban tail trailing on the surface.
  - "لم" 73.46: push to zoom 2.0 on the gap: his reaching hand on the left, the oar blade on the right, both inside the middle half of the panel. A swell lifts the skiff away as he reaches.
  - "يصل" 74.02: the fingers close on water one hand's width short (small splash, 2 frame white flash on the gap only), the blade slides out right. Hold the empty hand 0.4 s, then it sinks.
  - 75.0: back to the wide for the wave over his head (keep that as it is), and add the turban surfacing first, then the gasp at 76.2.

### 6. 83.6-88.3 s: the ship does not disappear behind tall waves
- **What is wrong.** The dhow is a speck touching the right panel edge from 83.7 to 87.2 and slides out of frame rather than behind water; the waves are the ordinary swell. From 86.7 Sinbad's pose barely changes.
- **Why it matters.** "وشاهد السفينة تبتعد وراء الأمواج العالية" is the moment he is truly alone; the plan's "arm that comes down after the second swell" needs the swells.
- **Fix.** Ship on the horizon at panel x 62 to 70%, about 90 units long at 84.66, shrinking to 45 by 87.0, never touching the edge. Sinbad at x 30%, back three-quarters to us, looking at it. On "وراء" 86.24 a mid-layer swell (crest 70 to 90 units above the horizon line, proper wave outline with foam lip, see note 1) rises and hides the ship; it reappears smaller; on "العالية" 87.58 a second, taller swell hides it and passes: empty horizon. He waves and shouts through the first swell (as now at 85.5-86.5), keeps the arm up through the second, then the arm comes down in three drawings at 88.0 and his head lowers onto the plank in the pause before 89.17.

### 7. 89.2-97.6 s: on the plank he is a toppled standing figure with one arm in the air
- **What is wrong.** For the whole night, the dawn and the drift ashore Sinbad is the standing puppet turned on its side on top of the plank, with one forearm pointing straight up for eight seconds. He rides onto the beach as the same rigid figure. It reads as a doll, not an exhausted man, and the raised arm fights "حتى هدأت الريح".
- **Why it matters.** This is the quiet heart of the film and the pose is reused by the series (the bible's "cling" pose).
- **Fix.** One cling pose for the series: chest and both forearms over the plank, cheek on the wood, legs trailing under the front water layer (clipped at the wave line), turban tail floating. Moving hold: slow breath, the plank pitching with `seaY`, a hand slipping and re-gripping once (90.7), eyes closing in steps to "هدأت" 92.74 when the waves flatten and his shoulders sink. Turtle cameo at 93.2 with his eyes following it for a second. On "حملته الأمواج" 95.66-96.62 one clear wave lifts plank and man together and sets them on the sand with a foam sheet running up and back; he rolls off the plank onto his back (a 2 drawing settle), then the sit up and knock as now.
- **Shore plate.** The beach meets the sea as a brown cone with a straight seam between two tans (95.7-97.2). Repaint as a low curved wet-sand band (darker, with a foam scallop line and two or three shells) so "شاطئ حقيقي" looks inviting.

### 8. 69.9-71.6 s: the leap
- **What is wrong.** Sinbad stands facing the camera on a small green disc that looks like a lily pad, rises straight up with his arms at his sides and comes down the same way; no crouch, no direction, feet together.
- **Fix.** Make the last patch the whale's grey-blue back awash, 3 figure-widths wide, with a strip of grass and water streaming off it. Turn him to face right, toward the skiffs (three-quarter view). 69.6 (in the pause): look at the boats, look down at the water at his knees. "قفز" 69.88: crouch with arms back (3 drawings), spring along an arc up and right, arms thrown forward, legs trailing and separated, turban tail and sash late; the hump drops away under him as he leaves. Entry at 71.6 with the existing SPLASH!, then his head surfacing through the rings.

### 9. 31.7-39.2 s: the camp is small and the gags do not read
- **What is wrong.** The island is a thin strip across the middle of a wide with the bottom third empty water; the four men sit in two overlapping pairs in front of the palm trunks; the fire and pot are small between them; the coconut is not readable. 33.5-35.5 is the stillest character stretch in the film. The plan's "truly pleasant place" is what makes the reveal a surprise.
- **Fix.** Open on the wide for one second, then zoom 1.35 to 1.45 with the island's grass line at 70% of the panel height and pan left to right across the camp (palms with coconuts and a parrot or crabs, flowers, the beached skiff, the mound that is the blowhole). Spread the four so no head overlaps another or a trunk: Sinbad leaning back on his elbows looking at the sky ("جلس" 31.72), fat merchant unpacking food, plum fanning himself, rust building the fire. Coconut drops on plum's turban at 34.46 "قرب الأشجار" (BONK!, squash and settle). "وأشعلوا" 35.86: rust strikes, spark, flame grows over 4 drawings by "نارًا" 37.18 with a warm light patch on the faces; pot on at 37.96. Keep the stop at 39.22 with only the pupils moving, as now.

### 10. 59.3-60.8 s: the whale wide
- **What is wrong.** After the impact frame the whole whale fits well, but his eye is a closed line a few pixels long and the lower half of the panel is empty teal. The plan asked for the eye 1.6x oversize here and a grumpy half-awake look.
- **Fix.** Enlarge the eye in this wide; on "ضخم" 59.34 the lid opens half way and the pupil rolls up toward the fire, brow down on "نائم" 60.08, one slow blink. Add a fish school scattering from under the belly at 59.0, light shafts moving, bubbles from the blowhole side, and a darker deep band at the bottom. Hold the shot to 60.8 with the body breathing (the island and the men rising and falling with it).

### 11. 24.9-27.6 s: going ashore
- **What is wrong.** Six men stand upright, packed into two tiny skiffs, and glide to the island without rowing; then they hop out as a block. "فنزل بعضهم ليستريح" is also the last time the ship and island are seen together before the trouble.
- **Fix.** Two seated in each skiff rowing (oars dipping on alternate beats, rings at each dip) and one standing at the bow; Sinbad at the first bow pointing. Skiffs 1.4x larger, wake behind. Landing at 26.62: the bow nudges the sand, the island gives a tiny dip that only the audience notices, they step out one at a time.

### 12. Polish
- **127.5-128.2:** Sinbad runs to the ship but hangs in front of the bow with his legs over the hull. Keep his feet on the gangplank line with `walk` and `footfalls`, plank bending.
- **109.0-114.5 (hall):** the yellow checker floor fills the bottom 40% with nothing on it. Frame at zoom 1.2 with the floor at the bottom quarter, or dress it: carpet runner to the throne, cushions, a fruit tray, a cat.
- **4.2-5.5:** the whale shadow under the quay is a faint dark patch in the bottom right corner. Make it larger and darker, with a tail shape, gliding left to right under the gangplank on "يجهل" 4.22 while Sinbad looks up at the gulls.
- **15.0-23.0:** seven people fill the small dhow shoulder to shoulder and the captain stands on the sterncastle roof. Seat two sailors, or draw the hull 15% longer in this tracking shot, so the deck reads.
- **146-149.8:** whatever the final staging, every visible figure needs a breath and one small action during the hold.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- **Crane below the waterline** (57.7-60.5): one group transform that lifts and scales the surface scene to reveal what is under it; good for any "what is really there" reveal.
- **DAY counter over cross-faded sky plates** (16.5-19.7): time passing without a transition card.
- **Spyglass inset** (30.0-30.6): a round inset tied to a character's prop for dramatic irony.
- **Story cloud that replays the film's own reveal** (112.2, 140.5): cheap, clear "he told the story" device.
- **Tail slap as exit** (67.2-69.7) and the **wave rolling over a swimmer** (75.2-76.2).
- Sea kit rule to record in the capabilities guide after this revision: subject waterline at 62 to 68% of panel height, reflection under every floating thing, a foreground crest band, one living thing per sea shot.
