A devout man and his wife made trays and fans all day and sold them in the evening. Their life was simple and austere.

One evening a wealthy woman invited the man into her palace, claiming she wanted to buy trays. She closed the door and tried to force him into a relationship he refused.

He resisted and asked to wash upstairs. From the roof he prayed, terrified and unable to find a safe way out.

In despair he jumped from the roof. The legend says an angel caught him unharmed; this dangerous action is a miracle motif, not an example to follow.

He returned without money and told his wife, but they had no food to break their fast. She lit the oven so the neighbors would not learn of their poverty.

A neighbor came for a coal and warned that bread was burning. To their amazement, the oven held fresh loaves they had not baked.

The couple prayed for relief and a glowing ruby fell from the roof. That night the wife dreamed her husband’s heavenly seat lacked the jewel.

She preferred contentment to losing spiritual reward. They prayed that the ruby return, and the tale says it vanished. They continued their humble work.
