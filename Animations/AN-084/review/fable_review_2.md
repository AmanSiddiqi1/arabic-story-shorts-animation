## New notes on v2 (all MINOR, most important first)

### 1. 84.2-86.2: "HA HA!" is yellow lettering on the yellow sun
What is wrong: moving the sun to the left third (round-1 note 4) put it exactly where the "HA HA!" of Ma'an's first laugh is drawn. At 84.6 the yellow letters sit on the yellow disc and its rays; only the black outline separates them.
Why it matters: this is the release of the whole desert act and the lettering is the only cue that he is laughing aloud.
Fix: place the lettering on open blue sky, to the right of the sun and left of the tag column, above the gap between the two riders (about panel x 470-760, y 150-230), tilted as now. Alternatively keep the position and use the red lettering style of "NOT ONE LESS!".

### 2. 149.2-150.4: courtier A catches the throne from the front and covers the prince
What is wrong: the late lunge works as timing, but courtier A is drawn in front of the tipped throne, so his head and both arms lie across the prince's chest and lap (149.6, 150.2). The prince's laughing face is still visible above him.
Why it matters: the lean is the one action the narration names; the silhouette of the tilted throne and the laughing man should be clean.
Fix: move courtier A about 70 units right so he stands beside the throne's high side and puts both hands on the edge of the throne back (hands at about the height of the prince's shoulder), or draw him behind the throne for 149.2-150.5. Keep the late `stagger` timing and courtier B covering his mouth.

### 3. 172.4-172.6 and 173.2-173.5: two brief clumps on the way out
What is wrong: the Bedouin and the guard cross on the same floor line, so for about 0.3 s the guard's body is behind the Bedouin's and the two heads touch (172.5). Then at 173.4 the donkey's head sits directly behind the Bedouin's head, so its ears and eye appear to grow out of his keffiyeh.
Why it matters: these are the first two frames of the exit that round 1 asked for; they pass quickly but they are the only muddled moments left in the ending.
Fix: start the guard's side step 0.25 s earlier (about 172.1) or put him on a lane 20 units farther back so the Bedouin passes clearly in front; hold the donkey 60 units farther left (or delay the Bedouin's arrival at the arch by 0.2 s) so the donkey's head is beside his shoulder, not behind his head, until it turns at 173.8.

### 4. 174.6-178.6: the saluting bag is tight against the guard
What is wrong: in the held end frame the raised bag hangs level with the guard's face with only a few units of gap (177.5), and the guard stands square to the lens rather than bowed aside.
Why it matters: the final picture should be Bedouin, gold and donkey framed in the arch, with the guard as a secondary figure.
Fix: move the guard 40 units farther right for the hold, or raise the salute with the door-side hand so the bag is seen against the blue of the arch. Optional: guard `tip 0.1` toward the Bedouin with his gaze on the bag.

### 5. 160.6-162.2: "ALL OF THEM!" is red on red hardware
What is wrong: the red lettering is drawn across the red hanging lamp and just under the red frieze (161.0). It is legible, but the first letters merge with the lamp shade.
Fix: shift it about 150 px right into the gap between the two lamps, or 60 px down so it sits directly above the tag row on the pale wall. Same timing.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Cause-then-effect cutting for a recognition beat**: close-up of the giver repeating an earlier gesture through the same pose function, hard cut to the receiver's jolt, cut back to the open reaction, then a round match-shot inset at the same face size with a paired action (130.2-135.7 here). Worth a worked example in the guide under suspense.
- **Panel-space overlay props** (the tag column and row): confirmed to survive pushes and cuts untouched; add the "struck but legible" state as the documented default.
- **Check lettering colour against whatever was moved behind it**: a guide rule that after relocating a sun, lamp or other saturated object, every piece of lettering in that range is rechecked for same-colour collisions (new notes 1 and 5 both come from this).
- **Catchers and helpers stand beside, not in front of, the figure they support** (new note 2), alongside the existing carry-height rule.
