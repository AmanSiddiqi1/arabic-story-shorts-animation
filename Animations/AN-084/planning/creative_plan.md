# AN-084 Ma'an Son of Zaidah and the Bedouin: creative plan

Film length 175.60 s of narration + 3.0 s THE END = 178.6 s. 12 scenes, 134 timed actions (all in `creative_plan.json`; this file is the direction behind them). All times are seconds on the final MP3 and come from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Spine.** A prince, alone after a hunt and dressed plainly, meets a poor Bedouin taking his first cucumbers after two years of drought to "Prince Ma'an", whose generosity he has only heard about. The prince hides who he is and teases the man's hoped-for reward down a ladder: 1000, 500, 300, 200, 100, 50, 30. At thirty the Bedouin stops: one dinar less and his donkey walks into the prince's house. Ma'an rides ahead, sits on his throne and plays the whole ladder again. The Bedouin recognises him, repeats the donkey threat to his face, the prince laughs until he tips backward, and then pays **every** sum on the ladder: 2,180 dinars.

**Conflict.** Gentle: pride and hope against a straight-faced tease. There is no villain. The suspense is "when will he realise?" and "what will the prince do?".

**Engine: dramatic irony.** The audience knows from 32.74 (the Bedouin names Ma'an to Ma'an). Everything after that is played for the viewer over the Bedouin's head: two asides to the lens, a wink, a blue robe that stays blue under the crown.

**Surprises.** (1) the gulp before "five hundred" (a real 1 s silence at 57.5); (2) the donkey threat at 80.42; (3) the recognition at 131.08; (4) the donkey already half inside the door at 141.92; (5) the reversal at 157.4 to 161.7: the struck prices are un-struck and all of them are paid.

**Emotional arc.** Hope (the harvest) → proud confidence (1000) → shrinking (the ladder) → stubborn dignity (30, the donkey) → awe (the throne) → the same squeeze again → shock, then laughter between equals → amazement and joy.

**Ending and moral.** The Bedouin leaves with a gold-laden donkey; the prince eats a cucumber. Real generosity gives more than was asked, and honest wit wins a noble heart.

## 3. Character acting

- **Ma'an.** A man enjoying a secret. Rule: the face stays straight, the leak is elsewhere (one corner of the mouth, cheeks puffing, shoulders with `tremble`, the horse's tail). Three lens looks only: 33.4, 42.64 (with `wink:'R'`), and none in the palace until the wink at 130.2 aimed at the Bedouin. His pressing-down hand is the haggling gesture; give each press a 0.1 s `anticipate` dip. Laugh 1 (84.12) is a release (`tip -0.12`, `mouth:'open'`); laugh 2 (147.12-149.8) is bigger: `lid 1`, crown slips, `tip -0.3` with the throne rotating and a `rebound`.
- **The Bedouin.** Proud posture is his meter: chest out and arms wide at 1000; one notch lower (`sq`, shoulder drop) per step; eyes shut and both hands gripping the strap before 30; then he snaps taller than he started for the refusal (`settleTo` overshoot). In the hall he begins small (short steps, saccades around the room, `sq 0.94` at the throne) and regrows through the second ladder. Recognition: stillness with moving pupils (128.78-131), then `hop` + `landSquash`, mouth 'o', keffiyeh tail a beat late. Final state: wet happy eyes, bouncy `walk`.
- **Donkey.** The Bedouin's second face. Ears are the instrument (`earL`/`earR`): flop alternately when trotting, snap up with his master's refusal, droop with the falling price, straight up at the gold. It grins at its own cue (80.42) and is the punchline at the door (141.92).
- **Horse.** Small reactions only: ears flat at "1000", looks back at its rider at 34.3, tail swishes during the ladder.
- **Guard, courtiers, treasurer.** A chorus that reacts late and never together (`stagger(i)`): the guard's deadpan at the donkey mime and forehead slap at 136.0; courtiers' tennis-match heads at 119-123; the treasurer's climbing eyebrows as the slashes peel off.
- **Everyone, always:** a gaze target (`lookAt`, `gazePlan`), blinks (`LIFE.t`), `breathe` on holds, follow-through on cloth and tails.

## 4. Camera and composition

- Faces and action stay in the top two-thirds panel; tags and counter own the top third of the picture, faces the middle, mounts and floor the bottom. Nothing is ever placed over a face.
- **Status by height.** Mounted, Ma'an's head is about 70 units above the Bedouin's; on the dais it is about 90 above. When they laugh together at 149 the pull-out puts them in one frame as equals in joy.
- **Mirror staging.** Desert: Bedouin right, tag column right. Hall: Bedouin left, tag column left. Same sizes, same order.
- Desert: one tracking establishing shot (S1), then two-shots built with `twoShot`, broken by an insert (13.5), two pushes to Ma'an (33.4, 42.6), a slow push through the silence (56.4) and tilts for the clouds.
- Hall: door medium (S7), pan with his eye line to reveal the throne (99.14-100.34), whip to the door and back for the donkey (141.0-143.56), final pull-out.
- The LATER card at 95.72 hides the jump from the door medium to the arrival (camera key pair at the card's midpoint).
- Panel-only 2-frame shake on tag strikes; caption panel never moves.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the Bedouin describing the prince to the prince under a faceless silhouette; the gulp; the staircase of shrinking tags; the donkey grin; the prince miming donkey ears to a guard; the fast silent replay with "AGAIN?!"; the donkey chewing the spear tassel with a hoof already on the royal carpet; the throne tipping.
- **Suspense:** the held silence 57.5-58.4; the squint at 107.2 that almost recognises him; pupils-only stillness 128.78-131.
- **Surprise:** the un-striking of the tags (158.94). The red slashes were losses; removing them one by one turns every humiliation into a payment. This is the image of the moral.
- **Emotion:** the drought cloud with the kneeling figure (23.68) makes the cucumbers precious before the comedy starts; the counter climbing on each spoken sum (164-171) is the payoff; the caught bouncing coin and the kiss (170.12).
- **Setup and payoff pairs** (must use identical poses): money rub 45.58/112.32, arms wide 49.04/114.40, brow wipe 68.88/122.12, one-eye check 72.48/125.06, palm stop 74.10/126.62, wink 42.64/130.2, cloud donkey in the hall 80.42 / real donkey in the hall 141.92.
