Film length 171.90 s narration + 3.0 s hold = 174.90 s. 15 scenes, 136 timed actions, 16 lettering items, 7 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue names the Arabic word and its start time from `inputs/timeline.md`. The JSON (`creative_plan.json`) is the authority for times and coordinates; this file explains the intent.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

- **Set-up (0 to 43 s).** A scholar finds a small school and a smartly dressed teacher. Three test questions, three exact answers (3 / 3). He returns many times and finds more knowledge each visit. Privately he recalls his old assumption: schoolteachers have the BOOK (learning) but not the LAMP (judgment). These two icons are planted at 40.98 and 42.40 and paid off at 160.90.
- **Inciting event (43 to 64 s).** LATER: the school is chained shut. Neighbours say the teacher is at home grieving for someone he lost. The scholar goes to console him.
- **The interview (64 to 108 s).** A sickroom mood played straight. Father? Brother? Relative? No, no, no. A woman he loves. "There are many women in the world" fails. Then the confession: he has never seen her. The scholar's recoil at 99.22 is the comic peak of the present-day story.
- **The told flashback (108 to 150 s, dream wash).** A singer under the window sings of UMM AMR. Leap 1: poets only praise great beauty, so he falls in love with a glowing figure whose face is a question mark. Two days later the same cheerful singer: she and her donkey left and did not return. Leap 2: she is dead. He locks the school and turns black with mourning. Insert: THE SONG: GONE versus HIS GUESS: DEAD?
- **Resolution and moral (150 to 175 s).** The scholar leaves, more astonished than ever. In his thought cloud: a tall book stack, and beside it a tower of love and grief standing on two tiny song notes. After the last word it topples. THE END.
- **Conflict:** knowledge versus judgment inside one man. **Surprises:** "a woman" (83.26), "never seen her" (97.38), the donkey (134.02), the foundation of two notes (165.40). **Arc:** respect, concern, sympathy, disbelief, amused pity. **Moral:** learning is not judgment; do not build big conclusions on hearsay.

## 3. Character acting

- **Scholar** (`person` costume `'physician'`, age 0.6): the audience's eyes. Calm walk, raised brows (`brow` -0.4 to -0.9), beard stroke (`PERSON_HANDS.chin`). Reactions grow in three steps: impressed lean back (17.72, `settleTo`), full recoil with `tip` -0.14 and `sq` 1.06 (99.22), then the quiet head shake (154.86): the smaller last reaction is the joke. One look to camera only (101.10, `gaze [0, 0.05]`).
- **Teacher** (`person` costume `'merchant'`, green and yellow, age 0.2): proud and upright in class (`tip` -0.03, confident `talk`). In mourning: `tip` 0.07, `lid` 0.55, `wet: true`, `teardrops`, slow `breathe`, `tremble` at the end of sighs. Head shakes grow impatient (74.02, 75.32, 76.76). Confession: guilty saccades (`gazePlan` 0.07), `PERSON_HANDS.clasp`, tiny shrug. In the flashback he copies the scholar's thinking pose (117.90) before each leap; in love: `PERSON_HANDS.cheeks`, `lid` 0.45, `sq` 0.96, hearts with `stagger`. Wail at 141.8: head back, mouth open, `tremble` 3.
- **Singer** (`person` costume `'villager'`, red): identical cheerful acting on both days (eyes shut, bounce, `talk` 0.9). He never looks up. This sameness tells the viewer the second song is not sad news.
- **Pupils** (`child: true`): never in unison (`stagger`), the smallest always a beat late; gaze ping-pong during the test; cheer at 144.0; play at the end.
- **Neighbours:** arrive 0.4 s apart; the carpenter talks and points, the old woman only shakes her head and dabs an eye.
- **Secondary motion:** swinging CLOSED board (`swing`), crooked turban after the recoil, the book that slips from the sill (`fallY`, `impactSquash`), donkey ears and tail in the balloon, the swaying tower.
- Everyone blinks (`LIFE.t = t`); every figure has a gaze target; listeners look at the speaker.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the three head shakes; the practical "many women" balloon; freeze, double blink, recoil; the glamorous cloud lady with a question mark for a face; the homely donkey; pupils delighted that school is shut; the nose blow at the window; the tower on two notes.
- **Suspense:** the door opening on darkness (63.14); stillness before "a woman" (82.24) and before the recoil (98.9).
- **Surprise:** the mystery face appears at 83.26 before it is explained at 97.38; the camera tilt inside the cloud that reveals the foundation (165.40).
- **Emotion:** the grief is acted sincerely (wet eyes, sighs), so the comedy stays affectionate. The scholar rescues the dropped book: respect for the learning, pity for the judgment.
- **Setup and payoff:** BOOK and LAMP (S4 to S15); the thinking pose (S4 scholar, S10 teacher); the fallen book (S10 to S14); the CLOSED board (S5 shown, S12 explained).
