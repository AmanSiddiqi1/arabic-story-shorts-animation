# AN-107 The Thief and the Shroff: creative plan

Film length 171.41 s (MP3 168.41 s + 3.0 s end hold). All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard with every action, cue word, lettering and effect time is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A cocky thief steals a money-changer's purse to win a dare, learns that an innocent maid is being punished for it, and pulls a second, cleverer trick that clears her while he still keeps the gold.

**Conflict.** First the thief against the dare (can he take the purse alone?), then the thief against the consequence of his own success (the maid pays for it).

**Spine (cue word, time).**
1. Setup: the shroff parades his purse through the market (كيسًا 2.64).
2. Inciting: the gang sees it (رآه 6.50); the boast and the laugh (وحده 13.84, ضحك 14.86).
3. Rising: purse on the high shelf (الكيس 29.70); the door left open (الباب 40.36).
4. First climax: the grab (الكيس 46.60), out unseen.
5. Comic deflation: he waits for praise (ينتظر 58.30); none comes.
6. Turn: the warning cloud, the raised stick (ويضربها 72.16); "an innocent pays" (بريء 79.40); his decision (اللص 82.28).
7. He finds it true (الخادمة 90.00), knocks (طرق 91.14), plays the messenger (رسول 94.52).
8. The tease: the shroff reaches, the purse is whipped away (ليأخذه 112.82); the receipt demand (ورقة 121.12, مختومة 124.68).
9. Second climax: the shroff writes, the thief runs with the same purse (ركض 138.22, نفسه 142.14) and vanishes.
10. Resolution: no gold, but the stick drops (توقف 152.04); the maid is cleared (تقصيرها 158.94).
11. Moral payoff: the second trick earns the applause (نجح 162.22); the maid free in the doorway (ونجت 164.54); THE END 168.4.

**Surprises.** The praise that never comes; the thief walking back to the house he robbed; the purse offered and snatched away; the "return" that is a second theft.

**Emotional arc of the thief.** Swagger, glee, wounded pride, a flinch of guilt (the only lens stare with a gulp, 82.3), then cool cheek, then a modest bow. The maid goes from busy, to terrified, to hopeful, to free. The shroff goes from smug, to furious, to greedy, to flattened and a little ashamed.

**Moral.** A clever deed is not finished while an innocent person pays for it.

## 3. Character acting

- **Thief (hero).** Brows and lids carry him: `lid 0.45` smug, `brow 0.8` when laughed at, `brow -0.6` guilt. Signature shapes with arm angles so they read at phone size: one finger up (13.84) echoed by two fingers (163.72); purse held high (51.76, 106.92). Anticipation before every dart (`anticipate`), overshoot after (`settleTo`). Sneak gait with a hold on each toe; freeze on one leg at 42.8 with only the pupils moving. Two lens stares only: 46.6 (the rocking jar) and 82.3 (the gulp). Mask on, mask off at 94.5: he winks at the camera while the shroff studies the turban.
- **Shroff.** Belly-first strut (`tip -0.04`, `lid 0.3`), turban lagging one frame. Greed: pendulum eyes, creeping hand, `lid 0.4`. Anger: planned `flush`, stamp (`kick`), stick shaken only at the top of its arc. Defeat: smile held a beat too long, turban askew, `sq 0.9`, hard sit with `impactSquash` and `rebound`, then the shame look (eyes up to the maid, break away, head drops).
- **Maid.** Small and quick: double nod, brisk walk. Fear: `kneel 1`, `PERSON_HANDS.shield`, `wet`, `tremble`, head turned away. Hope: hands lower halfway (103.6), hand to chest (110.2). Accomplice: `mouthCover` and a tiny nod at 134.4. Release: peeks through her fingers, long breath (`sq 0.96` to 1), a long blink at 165.5.
- **Companions.** Never in unison (`stagger` 3 to 6 frames). pal1 is dry: folded arms, raised brow, slow head shake. pal2 is the laugher: knee slap, shrug, then the one who weighs the purse and wags the finger. Their clap at 162.2 mirrors their silence at 58.3 to 61.8.
- **Secondary motion.** The purse swings a beat behind the hips and stops dead when fingers come near; jars rock; the curtain hem keeps swaying; the door leaf overshoots and settles; the fallen turban rocks to rest.
- Everyone has a gaze target in every shot (`lookAt`, `gazePlan`, saccades with ease 0.07) and blinks (`LIFE.t = t`).

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground and floor at y 900; faces and key props stay above panel y 900, lettering in the upper panel and never over a face.
- Market scenes are tracking shots on the 2400 strip (`camX`): left to right for the stroll and the shadowing, right to left for the escape, so the escape visibly runs back the way he came.
- The house is one room read left to right: door (danger and escape), shelf (the prize), desk (the receipt), curtain (the absent owner). Full shots establish it; push-ins pick out the shelf (29.7, 46.6) and the doorway (40.4).
- Dialogue scenes use `twoShot` and keep the listener's face in frame; S11 holds thief and shroff 230 units apart with the purse hanging in the gap.
- S12 is staged in depth as dramatic irony: the writer's back in the centre, the thief in the doorway at the left, the maid at the right who sees both.
- S15 repeats the S7 framing so the applause reads as the payoff. One panel-only shake (112.9). One `later` card at 82.75 to 83.55.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** the stool both men need to reach the "high shelf"; the shroff's hand groping out of the curtain for water after the house has been robbed; the one-eyed peek for applause; the turban on a masked head and the label MESSENGER?; the double tease with the purse.
- **Suspense:** the bulging curtain and the freeze (42.8); the rocking jar (46.6); the silent backward step behind the writer (135.7).
- **Surprise:** the thought cloud of praise popping (62.9); the whip-away (112.8); THE SAME PURSE! (141.6).
- **Emotion:** the warning cloud is the heart of the film; the stick is raised and held, the cloud rim flashes red, and we cut to the hero's flinch instead of a blow. The full water jug beside the kneeling maid at 90.0 says she did her job. The stick dropping at 152.0 is the release.
