# ALI-06 "Morgiana's Clever Plan" (80s Cel Anime): Fable review 1 (v1, 147.1 s)

## Verdict: REVISE (one short, targeted round; no blockers)

Reviewed from the v1 review pack (25 strips, 4 frames per second) plus 18 stills taken from `ALI-06_v1.mp4`. Motion on twos, blink timing and follow-through cannot be judged at 4 frames per second; where a note says "check", it is a request to confirm on a contact sheet of every 4th frame.

**What already works and must not change.** The owner direction is met almost everywhere: wide, full sets (lanterns, washing line, lemon tree, well, cat, domes), bright varied colour with no tint over the cast at night, dusk or in the cave, English balloons with tails, the Library cast with readable limbs, light presentation, a clear THE END (142.2 to 147.1) over a living crowd with the lamps lit. No weapon is used: spears stay upright, the capture is an open palm and raised empty hands. Subtitles are sharp and complete. The comic unravelling (blank name tag, HASAN, KARIM'S!, the not-equal sign, question marks one per beat, 41 to 54) is the best passage in the film. The glade, OPEN SESAME!, the market returns with check marks, the three futures cloud, the match cut into the shop and the refit all read at once.

**Why REVISE.** Four story beats named in the narration or the plan are on screen but cannot be read: the villain being led away, Morgiana's stopping palm, the spear tips behind the wall, and the emotional peak of the feast. Each is a staging or framing fix; none needs a new drawing.

## Notes (most important first)

1. **67.3 to 70.3, "They took him to the judge" (اقتادوه 67.28, القاضي 69.48): the guest cannot be seen.** From 68.5 he is hidden behind the captain (only the feather shows), and by 70.1 the frame holds Morgiana, the old neighbour and three guards in front of an empty dark arch. The neighbour, who is not in the story, is the most visible figure. The finale's villain leaves without being seen and the planned look back at Morgiana is lost.
   Fix: stage the procession in profile order along the wall with clear gaps, moving right to the street gate: guard, **guest**, captain, guard, about 110 px apart, the guest never overlapped. Guest: both hands low and together in front (empty, cuffs hanging), `tip` forward 0.04, feather on `droop`, `sq` 0.96. At 69.48 he stops one drawing, `gaze` back to Morgiana with a timed blink, she holds his look (no smile, `brow` level), then he walks on. Keep the neighbour at the back gate (x 1530) or behind the guards, not in front of the arch. Start the fade into the arch only after 70.0, so he is still whole on "القاضي". Put the TO THE JUDGE! balloon tail on the captain, clear of the guest's head.

2. **89.0 to 110.0, the feast is played entirely in one wide shot.** The figures are about 15% of the panel height, the lower 35% is empty paving and the upper 40% is sky. This is the emotional heart of the series (thanks, "Gold did not save us; your wisdom did", the reward), and the coin (a 12 px dot with a "tink" tag at 101.3), Ali Baba's open hand and Morgiana's wet eyes (plan: `wet` 0.45, slow blink at 102.32) are invisible on a phone.
   Fix: keep the wide for the arrival (89.0 to 92.0) and the rise (92.0 to 97.4). From 97.44 ("قال") cut or push to the planned two-shot of Ali Baba and Morgiana and take it further than the plan: zoom about 1.7 to 1.9 against the current framing, heads in the upper third, table edge at the bottom of the panel, lantern string still in view. At 101.12 ("الذهب") a 1.0 s insert or a held larger coin: Ali Baba holds it up at face height (coin at least 60 px on screen), lets it drop, it spins and lands flat on the table; his eyes do not follow it, they stay on her. At 102.32 ("حكمتك") the same hand opens toward her; Morgiana `wet` 0.45, slow blink, shoulders drop. Return to a medium (zoom about 1.3) for the casket at 103.16 and the three-futures cloud.

3. **23.6 to 28.6, Morgiana's stop (أشارت 23.60, يبقى بعيدًا 28.06 to 28.58) is hidden.** Ali Baba strides in and ends up overlapping her (24.2 to 27.2); she is half behind him, the flat palm is not visible, and the STAY BACK. tag floats over the household at the left with no tail. The audience cannot tell who stops whom.
   Fix: keep a gap of at least 90 px between them. Ali Baba starts from 250, strides to about 330 and freezes mid-step (`tiptoeFreeze`) with the rear foot still lifted; Morgiana at 440 keeps facing the guest and throws her near arm straight back toward him, palm flat and open in the gap, without turning her head (eyes flick to him one drawing later). Make STAY BACK. a balloon with its tail on Morgiana, placed above the gap. Ali Baba then settles back onto his heels at 28.06 with a small `sq` 0.96 rebound; his "?" tag can go.

4. **30.5 to 34.3, the suspense beat is covered by the balloon.** NOBODY LEAVES BEFORE THE GUARDS ARRIVE. is one line spanning the full panel width (clipped at the left edge at 31.5) and sits exactly on the wall top, over the helmet points and spear tips that the audience is meant to see before he does (قبل 32.36, وصول 33.04, الحراس 33.58).
   Fix: break the balloon into two lines ("NOBODY LEAVES" / "BEFORE THE GUARDS ARRIVE.") at the same type size, about 520 px wide, placed in the sky band over the house at the left, tail to Morgiana, fully inside the panel. From 32.36 tilt or pan the camera right and up a little so the wall top between the street gate and the palm is clear; three helmet points and spear tips bob along the coping left to right, one per word, each at least 40 px tall on screen, with a warm glint. The guest's `gaze` goes to the wall one drawing after the third tip, then back to her.

5. **3.5 to 7.6, ring insert: the proof is small in an empty frame.** The hand fills the upper half, the stone is about 45 px wide, the split is a hairline, and the rest is a flat lilac field with nothing in it for four seconds; only light sweeps and an arrow move.
   Fix: reframe so the ring finger and stone are the centre of the picture and the stone is 150 to 180 px wide, with the split a clear dark zigzag (two tones of blue either side). Replace the flat field with the blurred courtyard (lantern glow, lemon tree, Morgiana's turquoise sleeve and two fingers holding the folded cuff at the frame edge). Give the hand life: the fingers tighten on the gift box at 3.8 ("الخاتم"), a small tremor at 6.86 ("المشقوق") as the glint crosses the crack. Keep THE SPLIT STONE! but bring it in on 6.86 rather than 7.0.

6. **16.4 to 54.2, the key and padlock are too small to carry the trap.** The key hangs on Morgiana's sash at about 30 px and is never shown; LOCKED! arrives on "مغلقًا" (22.86) but the planned swing of the key on its ribbon does not read, so the audience does not learn that she locked the gate.
   Fix: at 22.86 Morgiana lifts the key on its ribbon to shoulder height and lets it swing (`swing(t, 22.9, amp)`), key at least 70 px long with a glint; the guest's eyes go padlock, key, her face. A small inset circle on the padlock (as the ring circle at 62.6) is an alternative if the pose is tight. Lower it back to the sash by 24.5.

7. **54.2 to 67.0, the "half ring" is a flat row, and the map label covers the guest.** The captain stands so close to the guest that the two overlap inside the lantern glow, and MAP TO THE CAVE (65.2 to 67.0) sits on the guest's turban and brow.
   Fix: move the captain about 60 px right so the raised open palm and the guest's two empty hands have clear air between them at 58.74 ("بلا قتال"); stagger the guards' y by 10 to 20 px (nearest lowest) so the line reads as an arc. Lift the map card and its label about 90 px so every face is clear; show the young guard holding the folded map up toward the card at 65.18 ("خريطة").

8. **80.6 to 85.0, the road shot is half empty sky and the towns do not read.** The cart, donkey and guards occupy a thin band; the three "neighbouring towns" (المدن القريبة 83.76 to 84.26) are a low purple skyline and a single glow at the end of an arrow.
   Fix: lower the horizon framing less (zoom about 1.25, ground line at about 70% of the panel height), add birds and two more clouds, and paint three distinct `a2FarTown` silhouettes on separate hills with different roofs (dome, tower, palms). Light them one per word at 83.76, 84.0, 84.26, each with a small tag or check mark; the captain's list arm points to each in turn.

9. **Moving holds: check three stretches for stillness.** On the strips these look like held drawings with nothing but light moving: the ring hand (4.0 to 6.9, see note 5); the household at the door K end of the yard during the lie (36.0 to 41.0), where Ali Baba, the nephew and Morgiana look identical frame to frame; the seated family at 97.5 to 99.5. Confirm on a contact sheet of every 4th frame and add `breathe` and `idleSway` with different seeds, one weight shift each, staggered blinks, the scarf tail on `lagged`, and a glance between the nephew and Ali Baba at 39.9 ("مسافر").

10. **142.2 to 147.1, the end tableau is cut by the right edge.** The pointing crowd and the family arriving on the right are stacked on top of each other and clipped by the panel edge; Ali Baba (vermilion) is buried in the pile, so the planned last picture of vermilion and turquoise side by side under her lamps is not there.
    Fix: shift the camera about 80 px right or bring the group 80 px left, spread the arrivals at least 70 px apart, and place Ali Baba at the front beside the counter, nearest Morgiana, with one settled gesture (hand on chest). Keep the staggered arrivals; they keep the hold alive.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Name tags with a not-equal glyph** (`nameTags`): two hanging tags and a glyph that pin a lie in the sky band while the scene plays under them. Useful for any contradiction or comparison.
- **Thought cloud that resolves to one choice**: three small pictures, two fade, the chosen one grows and becomes the match cut into the next scene (111.0 to 116.2).
- **Staggered question marks**, one per listener per beat, as the crowd reaction pattern.
- **Refit as a story beat**: awning colour swap plus a name board drop and swing (118.5 to 119.2).
- A rule for the guide: a balloon never sits on the line where a reveal is about to happen (wall tops, doorways); long lines break at 520 px.
