# ALI-06 Morgiana's Clever Plan: creative plan (80s Cel Anime)

Style `cel-anime-80s`, episode 6 of 6 of the Ali Baba series: the finale. Narration 142.13 s, last word ends 141.56 s, end hold 5.0 s, film 147.13 s. 21 scenes, 171 actions, 19 lettering items, 9 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. Every action is in `creative_plan.json`; this file gives the direction.

The reference film is ALI-01-ANIME v4. The owner direction at the top of `Library/styles/cel-anime-80s/look.md` decides every doubt: wide views and pans, full sets, varied bright colour with no tint over the cast (evening included), English speech balloons, readable limbs, moving holds.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Reveal (0-16.4):** the film opens on the last picture of episode 5, at night. The guest comes back from the gate for his forgotten gift; Morgiana puts it in his hand and in the same move folds back his long cuff (1.52). Ring insert with the split stone (3.5-7.6), her memory of the night of the jars as an inset (8.4-10.7), THIS PROVES WHO YOU ARE. (12.44) and one impact frame: a band of light shows the leader inside the fine clothes (14.26).
- **Trap (16.4-35.6):** he steps back, runs for the courtyard gate again and finds the bar padlocked; Morgiana lets the key swing (22.86). Ali Baba starts forward and her flat palm stops him. NOBODY LEAVES BEFORE THE GUARDS ARRIVE. (30.48). Spear tips bob along the wall top.
- **Comic unravelling (35.6-54.2):** the merchant's mask goes back on, but he has forgotten his false name (41.84); the nephew's innocent question gets a second name (48.46). Two tags, HASAN and KARIM, and a not-equal sign; everyone leans in.
- **Capture without a fight (54.2-71.1):** guards enter by the back gate; a half ring of upright spears, an open palm, and he raises empty hands (58.74). The ring names him, the map to the cave is found (65.18), and he is walked to the judge with one last look at Morgiana. Tape transition NEXT MORNING...
- **Justice (71.1-89.0):** Ali Baba opens the rock one last time for the guards (OPEN SESAME!, 74.98); the hoard is carried out with care and listed; the cart goes to three towns and three owners get their things back.
- **Thanks (89.0-110.0):** sunset feast in the same yard, lanterns just lit. The household rises for Morgiana (92.0). Ali Baba drops a gold coin on the table: GOLD DID NOT SAVE US. YOUR WISDOM DID. The casket, and three futures in a thought cloud.
- **Her choice (110.0-124.5):** the smile, I WILL OPEN A SHOP IN THE MARKET!, a match cut into the real market; the family refits the false merchant's empty shop (coral awning down, turquoise up); the scale comes level and a queue forms.
- **Coda (124.5-147.1):** Ali Baba sells firewood with the donkey of episode 1 and lets the cave go. A storyteller starts on the forty thieves and the whole street points: REMEMBER CLEVER MORGIANA! Her lamps light one by one. THE END.
- **Emotional arc:** cool nerve, then comedy at the liar's expense, relief, generosity, tenderness, pride. **Conflict:** one unarmed young woman against a cornered man, won by attention and patience. **Moral:** wisdom and honesty are worth more than gold.

## 3. Character acting
All characters on twos (`celStep(t, 12)`), blinks and lip flaps on threes (`celStep(t, 8)`).
- **Morgiana** is the stillest person in the yard. Eyes move first, the body one drawing later, the scarf tail two drawings after that (`lagged`, `swing`). Her weapons are small and exact: two fingers on a cuff, a straight pointing arm, a flat palm thrown sideways without looking (24.6), fists on hips, a key on a ribbon, one raised eyebrow. She never gloats: at the gateway she simply holds his look. At the feast she is thrown for the first time (looks behind her to see whom they are thanking), then `wet` 0.45 and a slow blink at 102.32, and the smile at 110.05 built in three steps (eyes, mouth, shoulders).
- **Guest / leader:** mask on, mask off (the ALI-05 switch-on smile). False smile (`lid` 0.3, `brow` 0.2) over heavy brows (`brow` 0.7 to 0.8) that return whenever he thinks nobody is looking. Falling effort at the gate (three heaves, each smaller). The long cuffs, cloak and feather are his follow-through; the feather droops at the surrender. The lie collapses in his hands: a finger circling for a name, a hand flying after a blurted word, both hands over his mouth, a shrink (`sq` 0.93, `tremble`). Surrender is a breath out and two slow empty hands.
- **Ali Baba:** protective stride stopped mid-step (`tiptoeFreeze`), then trust. Earnest at the feast: hand on chest, the coin held up, dropped, the same hand opened to her. In the market, contented work and the chest touch when he gives change.
- **Nephew:** honest and eager, the pupil with a raised finger; hands to his head when the guest he invited is unmasked; the admiring mime of the sleeve at the feast.
- **Wife and kid:** the kid is the echo, always one beat late and slightly wrong (peeks out, copies the lean, taps the wrong temple, hops highest).
- **Captain and guards:** tall thin, short stout, young; brisk and polite, spear butts grounded one, two, three; the young one asks permission before taking the map and saves a goblet on his foot.
- **Groups** (household, guards, queue, listeners) always react on `stagger(i)` delays with different poses. **Moving holds** (`idleSway`, `breathe`, scarf, cuffs, plume, lantern string, awning hem, lamp flames, cat tail) in every pause; `groundShadow {flat: true}` under everyone.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Suspense without weapons:** a key, a bar, a palm, and spear tips that the audience sees before he does.
- **The lie made visible:** two name tags and a not-equal glyph; the family's staggered lean, down to the kid and the cat.
- **Payoffs across the series:** the ring (episodes 3 to 5) seen large at last; the jars' night as a memory with one pair of eyes under a lid; OPEN SESAME! said for the guards; the firewood and donkey of episode 1; the false merchant's shop becomes the honest one, and the nephew carries boxes into it again; Morgiana's lamp and the one honest oil jar become her trade.
- **Gold made small:** one coin spins and falls flat on the table while the open hand goes to her; a coin put back in the chest; a coin pushed back across the counter; change given at the woodpile. Honesty is one repeated gesture.
- **Choice shown, not told:** three futures in a cloud, Ali Baba stepping back with open hands.
- **Colour as story:** light teal and violet-rose night with amber lantern pools for the stand-off, clear blue day for justice, apricot sunset for thanks, gold for the ending; vermilion and turquoise end side by side under her lamps.
