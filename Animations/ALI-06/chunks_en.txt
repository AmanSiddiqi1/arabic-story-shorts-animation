Morgiana exposed the man’s sleeve, | revealing the silver ring with a split blue stone she had seen on the night of the jars. | “This proves who you are,” she said.
The man stepped back and tried opening the courtyard gate, but found it locked. | Morgiana motioned for Ali Baba to stay back, then said, “Nobody leaves before the guards arrive.”
The man tried to persuade them that he was merely a traveling merchant, | but forgot the false name he had chosen. | Asked about his shop by the nephew, he gave a different name, increasing everyone’s suspicion.
Guards entered through the other door and stopped him without a fight. | Morgiana identified him by his ring; they also found a map to the cave. | They took him to the judge.
Ali Baba led the guards to the rock hiding the treasure. | They carefully collected the stolen goods and searched neighboring towns for their owners, | so that what had been taken could be returned.
That evening the household gathered and thanked Morgiana for her courage and clear thinking. | Ali Baba said, “Gold did not save us; your wisdom did.” | He gave her a reward to choose her own future.
Morgiana smiled and said she would open a shop in the market. | The family helped her build an independent business trusted for its honesty.
Ali Baba continued to work and trade honestly, never secretly returning to the cave. | Whenever anyone mentioned the forty thieves, townspeople would say, “Remember clever Morgiana!”
