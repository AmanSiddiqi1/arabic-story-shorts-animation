## Notes (optional polish, no v3 required)

### A. 142.3 to 147.5: the order to drink
What is wrong: the official's entrance passes right behind the man, so at 142.5 to 143.0 his head sits on top of the red turban (`m6.jpg` left). At 143.2 to 144.5 he then stands half in front of the king. The king keeps both hands on his hips through أمره 143.56, so the order has no gesture. On نفسه 146.70 the man's head goes forward and down with his arms spread on the chest (`m6.jpg` right); it reads as ducking toward the bowl, not as leaning away from it.
Why it matters: this is the second climax. The beat is "the king orders, the man recoils".
Fix: bring the official in along the back wall at least 120 px right of the man's head before he turns, or start him already at x about 400 at 142.3. Stop him at x 490 as now but keep the king at x 720 so their shoulders do not overlap. On أمره 143.56 the king lifts one hand, open, toward the bowl and holds it to 145.3. On نفسه 146.70 tip the man's head and chest back and to the left (away from the bowl, `tip` negative, body x minus 20), palms up toward the bowl.

### B. 162.2 to 164.6: the hand on the blanket
What is wrong: the hand rises to the boy's head and pillow at 162.5 to 163.0 (`m8.jpg` middle) and partly covers his forehead. The king's shoulder and the physician's shoulder overlap.
Why it matters: the plan's saddest picture is the hand resting on the blanket and sliding off.
Fix: aim the hand at the blanket over the chest (about 90 px left of and 40 px below the boy's chin), rest 0.5 s, then slide as now. Move the kneeling king 50 to 60 px left (x about 450) so there is a gap between the two men.

### C. 121.5 to 122.0: the step back leaves the frame
What is wrong: the man's step back on بعد 121.56 takes half his face out of the left edge (`m4.jpg` right).
Fix: widen that shot by about 50 px on the left (frame x 180 to 960), or step back only to x 275.

### D. 157.8 to 158.7: the king's walk after the second death
What is wrong: the king crosses to the bed foot with both hands on his hips (`m7.jpg` right). It reads as satisfied or jaunty, right before the film's saddest moment. The same pose appears at 142.7.
Fix: arms hanging, head low (`tip` 0.08), `lid` 0.4, slow steps. Keep the kneel as it is.

### E. 28.0 to 28.7: entrance stack
What is wrong: the official stands directly behind the physician, so their heads stack.
Fix: keep the official 120 px or more to the left of the physician until the physician has passed, or let him stay in the doorway.

### F. 153.3 to 154.1: the hopeful eye
What is wrong: both eyes look closed over the smile (`m7.jpg` left), so it reads as relief, not as one eye peeking.
Fix: one eye clearly open (`wink` on one side only, the other `lid` 0), pupil turned toward the bowl, held for the 0.7 s.

### G. 166.0 to 174.9: the turban in the last wide shot
What is wrong: the turban lies against the king's knees, red on red, between the chest and the king (`m9.jpg` left and middle). The FALSE CONFIDENCE arrow lands correctly, but the turban is not a separate shape. This is about the same as v1.
Fix: let it settle about 70 px further left and 30 px lower, on the brown floor in front of the carpet edge, with the arrow following.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- **Carrier exits the room.** Sending the bowl carrier out through the door after the hand-over (116 to 117.6) and bringing him back only when needed is what cleared the sickroom. Worth a line in the guide: in a room with five or more people, anyone without a job in the next beat leaves.
- **Face overlays with multiply blending and eye cut-outs** (flush, green): the acting stays readable at alpha 0.45. Ready to move into `Library/` as the default for `flush`.
- **Reaction shot before a point-of-view insert.** The cut to the physician leaning at 113.0 before the blur view makes the insert his. Use the same pattern for any `blurVision` or thought insert.
- **Crossing paths rule.** A walker should not pass within one head width of a standing figure's head (notes A and E). This could be part of the proposed `headsClear` check, run on walks as well as on held poses.
