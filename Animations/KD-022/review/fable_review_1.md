# KD-022 The Ignorant Physician: Fable review, round 1 (v1 cloud render)

Reviewed: `renders/KD-022_v1.mp4` through all 30 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` plus 27 full-size frames in `review/v1_pack/extra/` (montages `m1.jpg` to `m9.jpg`, subtitle crop `sub.jpg`).

## Summary

The film tells the story in order, the ending is complete (FALSE CONFIDENCE, counter to 2, THE END held 171.5 to 174.9) and there are no broken frames. Subtitles are correct, readable and well timed in both languages; the English matches the Arabic chunk by chunk. No blockers.

What works and should not be touched: all of S1 (the cure, the CURED counter, the day cycle with the beard turning white, the doubled-jar blur view and the hands laid flat), the ONE DAY card, the gossip balloon, the store reveal and pan, the question marks, the sneeze, the recipe strip with tick and crosses, POISON! with its arrow, the lamp going out, the dimmed room for the grief, the close shot of the man at 139 to 142, the poison-jar thought cloud at 148, the rolling turban and the final wide shot.

The reason for REVISE is the sickroom. From 39 s on, every key beat there is staged with people standing directly in front of each other: heads stack into totems, the person the sentence is about is hidden, and the king's anger overlay covers his own face. These are the story's climaxes (refusal, the prince's death, the sentence, the man's death), so they are worth a v2. Everything outside the sickroom needs only small fixes.

World reference used below (from the plan): bed x 520 to 940, prince's head near (845, 735), king behind the bed at x 700, door on the left, floor y 900.

## Notes

### 1. 142.1 to 149.8, MAJOR: the man is hidden while he is sentenced
What is wrong: in the wide shot (see `m8.jpg` middle, 146.5) the man is a red turban and half a face squeezed between the short official, the tall official's arms and the foreground chest. The official with the bowl stands in front of him, and the THE SAME MEDICINE label sits on the turbans. At 143 to 145 the king and the tall official overlap each other as well.
Why it matters: "the king ordered him to drink the same medicine" is the second climax. We must see the man receive the order and recoil (نفسه 146.70). At present the viewer sees a crowd.
Fix: clear a lane. Short official stays by the door (x about 150). The man stands alone at x about 330 with empty wall behind his head. The tall official brings the bowl from the right and stops at x about 470, arm extended left, so the bowl hangs in the gap between them. The king stands at x about 620, hand open toward the bowl on أمره 143.56. Slide the foreground chest out of this shot, or frame above it, so the man is visible to the knees. Put THE SAME MEDICINE in the wall band (y 150 to 330) with the arrow down to the bowl, covering no head. The lean-away on نفسه 146.70 then reads: `tip` back, `sq` 0.94, both palms up. Keep the thought cloud at 148 as it is, but frame it so the man's whole face is clear of the official's sleeve.

### 3. 130.5 to 142.1, MAJOR: the anger overlay hides the king's face
What is wrong: `flush` fills the whole face with dense red dots at alpha 0.85 drawn over the features (`m7.jpg`, `m8.jpg` left). The eyes and brows are hard to find, and it reads as a rash or a mask. At 131.2 to 131.7, as he stands, his head also passes behind the tall official's turban, so the rise is lost.
Why it matters: the king's fury, and then the colder calm at عندها 142.10, are carried by his eyes and brows. The cold look only works if we could read the hot one.
Fix: draw the flush under the eyes, brows, moustache and mouth, and cap it: alpha about 0.45, climbing only to the brow line, strongest on the cheeks. Then make the anger with the face: `brow` -1, `lid` 0.25, tight frown, `tip` 0.08 toward the man. At 142.1 the dots drain and the face goes still with `lid` 0.3 and a straight mouth, as planned. Before غضب 130.50, move the tall official left to x about 420 so the king's rise on الملك 131.30 and his walk round the bed are in clear view.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- **Head clearance rule for rooms with four or more people.** No two head centres closer than about 1.2 head widths unless one is deliberately hiding. A small helper that asserts this per shot (`headsClear(people, min)`) would have caught notes 1, 2, 4 and 5 at the contact-sheet stage.
- **Face overlays go under the features.** `flush`, green and pale overlays should be drawn before the eyes, brows and mouth, with a capped alpha, so emotion colours never mask the acting.
- **Props are examined beside the face, never in front of it** (jar, bowl, letter): hand outside the cheek, head tilted to the prop.
- **Carrier exits after the hand-over.** A servant who delivers a prop leaves the sight line within about 0.7 s.
- Worth keeping as they are: the doubled-drawing `blurVision` point of view, the recipe strip scored with a tick and crosses, and the lamp tilt for an off-screen death.

Second review advice: Yes, a short second Fable review of v2 is warranted. Notes 1 to 5 and 7 restage most of the sickroom (about 39 to 45 s and 107 to 165 s) with new positions, framings and hand-overs for up to six figures, and that kind of change often creates new overlaps or cropped heads. The second review can be limited to those ranges plus a check of the recipe balloon at 36 to 39 s; the street and store scenes do not need to be looked at again unless they change.
