# KD-022 The Ignorant Physician: creative plan

Film length 171.49 s of narration plus a 3.4 s THE END hold (ends 174.9 s). All times are seconds on the final MP3 and come from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard (10 scenes, 144 timed actions, subtitle chunks) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction behind it.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** An old physician is honest enough to refuse a job his failing eyes cannot do. A confident fraud takes the job, poisons the prince through ignorance, and is made to drink his own mixture.

**The idea that drives the pictures.** The story sets two men side by side. One has knowledge and no sight, and he knows his limit. The other has sight and no knowledge, and he hides it behind confidence. So the film gives each of them one repeated visual signature:

- The old physician: a **blurred point of view** (`blurVision`). We see it at 15.4, again at 42.4 and again at 113.5. He always works nose-close with a squint.
- The ignorant man: a **mask**. Chest out, smirk and half-closed lids while anyone is watching; the moment the store door closes (78.4) the chest deflates and the eyes dart. The mask comes back for the king and is stripped for good at "are you sure?" (135.3).

**Conflict.** Honest limits against false confidence, with a third fault in the middle: the king and his officials check nothing. Nobody asks the man a single question until it is too late.

**Emotional arc.** Warm and orderly (S1), worried (S2), sly and comic (S3 to S6), then a hard turn at 118.2. From there the film is quiet: stillness, eyes and small hands. No jokes after the prince drinks, apart from the man's one hopeful open eye at 153.4, which is suspense more than comedy.

**Ending.** A wide, dim room: the still prince, the kneeling king, the dark lamp, the old physician at the bed foot, and one red turban alone on the floor. FALSE CONFIDENCE, a counter that reaches 2, then THE END.

**Moral.** False confidence kills. Knowing the limit of what you can do is part of knowledge.

## 3. Character acting

- **Old physician (`oldDoc`).** Age 0 in S1 until 12.4: upright, open eyes, economical hands. Age 1 after: stoop (`lean` 0.12), `lid` never under 0.45, `brow` about -0.5, head pushed forward (`tip` 0.1 to 0.14) whenever he looks at something. He walks tap, step, pause with the stick (the Lark blind-grope pattern). His eyes open fully only once, at عرف 35.00: knowledge is the one thing still sharp. In the last scene: `wet`, one tear, a long blink, the hand that slides off the blanket with an ease-out and hangs.
- **The ignorant man (`quack`).** Idle: slouch against the wall, `lid` 0.6. Confident mask: `sq` 1.08, `tip` -0.06, `lid` 0.4, smirk, elbows out, turban bobbing a beat late on every step. Unmasked: `sq` 0.94 to 0.96, `brow` -0.7, 2-frame saccades with `gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`, sweat. His gestures at the door (chest taps, fancy stirring, palms up) come back empty at 138.9 to 141.4. One look to camera only, at 148.7. The peek with one eye (`wink`) at 153.4.
- **King.** A father first: hand on the forehead, clasped hands, held breath. Amazed and uncritical at 108 to 112 (`brow` -0.8, nods, a clap). Panic at 120.9 (`reachBoth`). Kneel, `wet`, slow `tremble`. Anger with `flush` at 130.5, then the colder and more frightening calm from 142.1 (`flush` to 0, `lid` 0.3, straight mouth, quiet hand).
- **Prince.** In bed throughout (`lie` 1, 0.7 to drink). Ill: `lid` 0.6, fast shallow `breathe`, `painPulse` size 1. Trusting look to his father as he drinks. Poisoned: eyes wide, `tremble` 2.5, pulse to 2.2. Death: a one-second ease to stillness, eyes closed, head to the side, hand sliding to the blanket.
- **Officials.** The tall one decides, the short one copies four frames later (`stagger`). They nod, bow, usher and carry, and never ask a question. After the death the short one quietly stands in front of the door.
- **Listeners.** Every character has a gaze target in every shot. During the hold at 117.7 to 118.2 only eyes move. In the last wide shot the gazes turn one after another, 0.15 s apart, never together.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Setup and payoff.** The wrong-jar reach in S1 explains the refusal in S2 without a word. The recipe balloon in S2 returns as the scoring strip in S6. The palms-up gesture of S4 returns empty in S8. The prince's `painPulse` returns on the man in S9.
- **Dramatic irony.** The blue jar glints under his fingers and is passed over (94.4). The audience reads POISON! while he admires the colour. The poison jar sits at the same place in every store shot from 71.4.
- **Suspense by stillness.** The held room after the prince drinks (117.7); the half-second where the man might stop at 98.9; the open eye at 153.4.
- **Humour, early only.** The mask dropping when the door shuts; the label read upside down; the sneeze. Nothing comic after 118.2.
- **Emotion by indirection.** A lamp going out, a hand sliding, a turban rolling: the two deaths are never shown head-on. The old physician's hand that rests on the blanket and falls is the film's saddest picture and needs time (162.2 to 164.6).
- **The quiet third fault.** The king's thought cloud with a gold star and the officials' easy nods show how the fraud was let in. No lettering is needed for it.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus

- Start from `Library/template/`; take word times from `timing.json` (`w(p, n)`), using the cue words in the JSON. Chunk the subtitles exactly as in `subtitles` (four chunks per paragraph, verbatim).
- Build the costume layer as one `costume(P)` hook on `person`, so the six people here and the people of other human stories share one body, face and gesture set.
- Set `LIFE.t = t` every frame. Characters with an explicit `lid` (old physician, ill prince) need hand blinks: `P.lid = Math.max(P.lid, blink(t, seed))`. Use `noBlink` for the hold at 117.7 and for the king at 152.2 to 155.6.
- Walks use `walk()` with `footfalls()`; the old physician uses the grope pattern with the stick as the tapper. Pose changes use `anticipate` and `settleTo`. Group reactions use `stagger(i)`.
- The bowl is one object from 103.8 to 155.0: level 1, then 0.5 after the prince drinks, then 0; the same murky purple with red specks throughout. Hand-overs need the two figures within about 150 px of each other.
- S9 draw order: room, king and officials, the man, then the foreground chest. He leaves the picture by passing behind it. Draw nothing of him afterwards.
- Effects only in the listed pauses: whoosh 20.8, pop 45.05, pop 86.87, pop 106.75, chime 113.85, pop 117.9, soft thud 125.9, low hum 129.9, thud 132.5, pop 153.7, soft thud 157.7, chime 171.7. `master: 0.6`. No effect on either death.
- Panel-only shake at 86.6 (4 px) and 132.5 (6 px). Call `finish` last on every frame.
- After approval move the new assets into `Library/`, add `registry.js` entries, run `node Library/tests/regression.mjs` ("all identical") and rebuild the catalogue.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- **Dependencies.** Most of the people, poses and two of the three sets rest on assets that other stories have planned but nobody has built. If KD-022 is animated first, that work lands here.
- **Crowding.** Six figures share the sickroom in S7 and S8. Check a contact sheet for overlapping heads and keep to the two-shots.
- **Tone.** Two deaths, one of them a young prince. The staging is indirect on purpose. The level of softness is the main thing for the owner to confirm.
- **Interpolated word.** هو at 156.74 has an estimated time; the turban cue shares the time of أيضًا, so it is safe.
- **Owner questions (seven, in the JSON):** the softness of each death; the old physician staying in the room and trying to see the bowl; the recipe strip and POISON! label; the added lettering and the sneeze; the two cured townspeople and the CURED counter; the FALSE CONFIDENCE lettering with its counter.
