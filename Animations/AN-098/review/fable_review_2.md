**Fix:** move the S15 camera about 60-70 world units left for 154.5-161.7 (for example `{x:445,y:740,zoom:1.5}`), or place the lettering to the right of his head at world x about 300 and inside the frame. Check the medallions at 159.0 still clear the top edge.

### 2. Envoy's bow is a rigid plank lean (98.4-100.4, words 98.40 رسالته, 99.28 حقًا)
**Wrong:** the envoy tilts as one stiff piece about 30 degrees from the feet, face still visible and staring (`f_98.8.png`). It reads as a man tipping over rather than bowing his head, which weakens "behind his bowed head".
**Fix:** bend from the waist: feet and legs upright, torso `tip` forward about 20-25 degrees, head dropped further with `lid` nearly closed and `gaze` down, so his eyes are clearly off Yahya while the wink happens. Same timing.
