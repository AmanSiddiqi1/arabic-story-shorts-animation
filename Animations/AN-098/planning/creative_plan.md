# AN-098 Generous Dealing of Yahya Son of Khalid: creative plan

Film length 171.41 s of narration + 4.5 s end hold (ends 175.9). 17 scenes, 147 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue names the Arabic word from `inputs/timeline.md`. The full action list with acting and Library notes is in `creative_plan.json`.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Conflict.** Two layers. On top: a small bluffer against a proud governor (will the forgery hold?). Underneath: an old quarrel between two great men, Abdullah and Yahya, which nobody expects to end.
- **Spine.** Poor man hears of the quarrel (0-18) > forges the letter (18-31) > long road, bold delivery (31-40) > doubt and the bargain, reward or punishment (40-61) > envoy to Baghdad, days of dread (61-83) > Yahya sees the forgery and chooses peace (83-104) > the gifts (105-120) > the name that split them now joins them (120-129) > confession and offer (130-150) > Yahya refuses, explains, doubles (151-167) > two fortunes (167-171).
- **Surprises.** (1) 99.28: Yahya says the forged letter is his. (2) 152.48: he refuses a share. (3) 162.6: he doubles the gifts. Each is prepared by a held beat of stillness just before it.
- **Emotional arc of the forger.** Hungry and sly > cocky > sweating behind a fixed smile > sick with dread > stunned > ashamed and grateful > overwhelmed. He must stay likeable: his lies are small and his fear is big.
- **The idea that carries the film.** The script says Yahya's *name* was the cause of the quarrel and became the cause of friendship. A name cannot be drawn, so each man's name is a **portrait medallion**, and the quarrel is a **red rift** between the two medallions, with cobwebs because it is old. The audience meets this picture at 12.64, sees it again in Abdullah's doubt (47.3), watches Yahya decide to close it (94.88), sees it close for Abdullah (124.62) and gets it as the moral in Yahya's palm (158.94).
- **Ending and moral.** The poor man rides out with two strings of horses; Yahya waves from the balcony. Generosity turned a fraud into a peace.

## 3. Character acting
- **Forger** (`person` villager, muted olive, patches, bare feet). Acts with his whole body. Signature: the **mask on / mask off** smile, wide while he is watched, gone the moment the governor's eyes return to the letter (44.9), back in 2 frames. Saccades when he plots (16.2) and when he fears (64.92). Gulp = `pulse()` on `drop` with `sq` 0.92 (59.6). Dread = `headHold`, `tremble`, `wet`, peeking through fingers (81.94). Gifts = escalating reactions, each bigger than the last: eyes, jaw, hop back, hands to cheeks, knee wobble (110-119). Confession = `kneel` with `reachBoth`, head down, `lid` 0.5 (137.92). Last pose: sits down hard on the chest and looks at us (166.14).
- **Abdullah** (`merchant` preset as governor, red robe, black beard). Big and readable: brows do the work. Proud beard stroke in the cloud (11.34); narrowed `lid` 0.5 over the scroll with three slow gaze shifts (43.26); rises with anticipation and a lagging cape to give the bargain (50.42); each hand opens on its word (54.32 reward, 57.06 punishment). Joy is a real jump: `anticipate`, `hop`, `landSquash`, arms out (105.04). Alone, he softens: a small head shake at his old self (123.76) and a nod toward the west (127.88).
- **Yahya** (`vizier`, navy, grey beard, age 0.6). The stillest figure in the film, so his small moves are big. Reading freeze with `noBlink` (86.22); one eyebrow up, mouth flat, not angry (89.32); the decision is **suspense by stillness**: hand to beard, eyes to the window, long blink (91.04), the first hint of a smirk at "chance" (93.26), a warm full smile when the rift shuts (94.88). The wink behind the envoy's bowed head (99.28). In S14 he waits, only pupils moving (150.0). Refusal: one open palm (`shield`), then he closes the chest lid himself and slides it back (153.76).
- **Envoy and servants.** The envoy is all duty: straight back, quick bows, never sees the wink. Servants are always offset by 4 to 6 frames (`stagger`) and differ in tunic colour.
- **Horses.** Nodding heads with separate phases in the door arch; the white one nuzzles the forger's turban (113.88); the bay snorts at his double-take (134.52). Manes and tails use `swing()`.
- **Everyone, always:** a gaze target, blinks (`LIFE.t`), anticipation before every big move and `settleTo` after it; listeners look at speakers.

## 4. Camera and composition
- Establishing wide of the hut, slow push to the man (S1). Cloud scenes are framed with the thinker in the lower third and the cloud above, away from faces (S2, S5, S9, S10).
- Dialogue is always a `twoShot()` that keeps both heads; the governor stands on the dais (feet y 860) above the forger (y 900) for the threat, and Yahya sits or stands on the same dais but **leans down** to lift the man (141.68): same geometry, opposite meaning.
- Push-ins on decisions only: the bargain landing on the forger (56.4-59.6), Yahya's decision (91.04-95.8, to zoom 2.0), the thanks (140.6-143.5).
- Pull-outs for abundance: the gifts (108.8), the doubling (161.7), the procession (167.2).
- Road scenes are side-tracking shots on the `desertRoad` strip with a fixed direction rule: to Armenia left to right, to Baghdad right to left.
- Two scroll inserts (21.56-29.0 and 88.0-90.9) are drawn in panel space over the dimmed scene, with the reader's eyes above the top edge so there is always a face.
- All faces, hands and key props stay above world y 900; labels and counters live in the upper band; the caption panel holds only subtitles. Panel-only shake twice (FORGED!, x2).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour.** The empty purse and pockets; tongue out while forging and the glances over each shoulder; the thumb at his own chest on "its bearer" (29.1); the too-wide smile; the horse tipping his turban; pinching himself on the road and the double-take (133.68-134.52); ME? with a look behind him (157.98); sitting down hard on the chest (166.14).
- **Suspense.** The governor's slow eyes over the scroll; the two boxes, coins and stick; the door arch sky turning three days while he paces; Yahya's long silence before "but" (91.04), the single most important hold in the film.
- **Surprise.** The wink at 99.28 tells the audience what Yahya is doing without a word. The chest sliding back (153.76) is staged before the narration explains it.
- **Emotion.** Wet eyes when the cloak is laid on his patched shoulders (116.56); Abdullah alone with the letter (119.68-129.0); the kneeling confession and the hand under the elbow (137.92-141.68); the wave to the balcony (170.48).
- **Setup and payoff.** Rift opens 12.64, closes 94.88 / 124.62, ghosts and fades 160.76. Stick box 58.94, crossed out 142.82. Red seal (false) against yellow seal (true). One heap of gifts at 110-119, mirrored into two at 162-166, then flags 1 and 2 at 169.06.
