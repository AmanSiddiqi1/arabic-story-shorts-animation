## Notes (polish only, none triggers v3)

### A. 148.2-151.5 s. MINOR. The owner's question has no arm gesture
From 146.2 to 157.5 the owner's near arm stays on the thief's collar and the other hand stays on his hip (`extra/m4.jpg` third frame at 148.9, `extra/m5.jpg` first frame at 150.4). The planned full-arm point on "لماذا" (148.22) and palm-up shrug on "الخلفي" (150.16) do not read; the question is carried by the curved arrow and his mouth.
Fix, if reopened: on 148.22 raise the hip arm to a full-arm point along the arrow (hand above the thief's shoulder, toward the yard), hold to 149.6; on 150.16 turn it palm up with a small head tilt and gaze thief / yard / thief. Keep the collar grip and the arrow.

### B. 48.0-53.7 s. MINOR. The pair are cropped by the right frame edge
The servant is half out of frame from 49.5 and the friend is a sliver (`extra/m2.jpg` first frame at 53.0), so the two-person flinch on "افتحوا" is mostly lost.
Fix: either move the camera about 90 px right so both heads are inside the panel, or 120 px left so neither is in frame. Do not leave them on the edge.

### C. 120.0-120.3 s. MINOR. The lifted pot covers the thief's face
The pot and its leaves are drawn across his face (`extra/m3.jpg` third frame). The idea reads, but his tilted look under the pot is hidden.
Fix: hold the pot about 45 px higher and 20 px to his right so the eyes look up under its base.

### D. 162.7-163.3 s. MINOR. Busy overlap on the exit
The owner and the thief pass through the two sacks while the servant crouches behind the right one (`extra/m6.jpg` second frame); for about 0.5 s five shapes stack up.
Fix: walk the pair on the near side of the carpet (drawn in front of both sacks), or start the servant's rise 0.3 s later, after they have cleared the sacks.

### E. 22.2-24.7 s. MINOR. Farewell is still tight
Servant, leaf, owner and wife share about 200 px; at 24.7 the servant is sandwiched between the owner and the wife.
Fix: put the servant 60 px further inside (right of the doorway, in front of the shelf) so the owners pass on his left without crossing him.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- Scene overlay for a hand in front of the face (`k17FacePalm`): the Library draws arms behind the head, so a palm-over-eye pose needs a front overlay. Worth a shared `handOverFace` option.
- Paper-white outer keyline for red lettering on dark backgrounds (`k17Keyline`): a good default for any red SFX word in a night scene.
- "Shove, slide with dust, calm walk back to the mark": a three-part gag that makes stubbornness physical.
- Reaction shot that keeps the cause in frame: knocker at the left edge, reacting pair at the right, with the flinch size keyed to each hit.
