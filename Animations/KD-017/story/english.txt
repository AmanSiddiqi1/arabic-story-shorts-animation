A servant worked in a wealthy man’s house and knew every room and every door. But he made a plan to steal with a friend. His friend came inside whenever the owners went out, and they sold what they took and divided the money.

One night, the family went out to visit relatives. The friend arrived, and the two began putting valuable things into bags. They thought they had plenty of time and that nobody would return soon.

Suddenly, they heard loud knocking at the front door. The owner shouted from outside, “Open the door!” Both men were terrified. The servant told his friend, “Quick! Escape through the back door by the well!” and pointed the way.

The thief ran to the back and found the open door. But he saw no well beside it. He hurried back to the servant and said, “I found the door, but where is the well you mentioned?”

“I mentioned the well only to help you find the door!” the servant said. “Once you find it, go out immediately!” The thief replied, “But you said the well was there. I looked and could not find it. How can I leave without seeing it?”

The knocking grew louder, and the owner became angry. The servant begged his friend, “Forget the well! The door is in front of you, and you can still escape!” But the thief kept asking about the missing well.

At last, the front door opened, and the owner came inside. He saw bags filled with his belongings and then the two men arguing. The thief tried to run, but it was too late, and the owner caught him.

“Why did you not escape through the back door?” the owner asked. The thief answered, confused, “I found the door, but I could not find the well!” The man could not believe what he heard. He took the thief to the governor, and the bags stayed in the house.
