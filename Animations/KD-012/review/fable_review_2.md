## The two questions asked

**Foot drop, 43.0 to 46.5 s: reads now. FIXED.** The grey leg column with toenails enters from the top edge on 44.0, drops in three clear steps (sole near the top at 44.1, mid sky at 44.9, tree height at 45.6, a hand above the mound at 46.3) and slams B2 on 46.5 with CRUNCH! beside the mound. The mother and kit leave room 2 by the back tunnel just before the slam (room empty at 46.3) and the room is crushed at 46.5. The shot is wider than the zoom 2.0 asked for (four rooms in frame, hares small, their upward look and the growing shadow are not really visible at phone size), but the foot itself carries the beat and the build to CRUNCH is there. Keep as is.

**King's bend, 122.0 to 125.6 s: PARTLY. He looks down; he does not visibly bend.** Frames 121.9, 122.7, 123.6, 125.0: the eye goes wide and locks on the reflection, the head tips forward a little, and the trunk later sags to the water (touch at 126.2, in frame, king's eye, trunk tip, reflection and hare all inside the panel). That is better than v1. But the head lowering is small (the eye stays well above the shoulder line), and the camera pushes in during exactly the same second, so on screen the king's head and crown move UP the panel (crown at about y 130 of the 1300 px panel at 121.8, about y 105 at 123.1). The net picture is "camera moves closer to a standing elephant". Compare frame 140.3, the bow, where the head goes down to knee height and nobody could miss it. Also from 122.7 to 124.7 the trunk curls up short against the cheek, which reads as pulling away from the water, the opposite of the intent.

## NEW problems in v2

1. **154.2 to 157.1 s, "The elephants left in the morning": the king does not leave, and he walks into the rock.** Caused by the restaging for note 8 (king at x 1045, right of the rock, facing left). Frames 154.1 to 157.1: cow and scout exit left at 154.1, then the king stands to the right of the rock with his trunk against it for the whole sentence (154.27 to 156.52), his salute at about 156.2 is a trunk laid on the rock face and cannot be read, and at 157.1 his head and body overlap the rock with the hare apparently standing on his crown, while the camera pans away to the returning hares. v1 had him clear of the rock and walking off left. This is the last story event before the happy ending and it currently shows the opposite of the words for 2.3 s. See fix 2.
2. **122.7 to 124.7 s: trunk curls up to the cheek during the bend** (described above; folded into fix 1).
3. Nothing else new. No lettering on faces, no subtitle or frame defects, no new holds longer than about 1.2 s (111.2 to 112.0 and 25.5 to 26.7 are the longest, both acceptable).

## Reusable techniques (unchanged from round 1, now proven on screen)
- `bigFoot` foreground leg prop on `countedBeats` (43 to 46.5): approve and move to `Library/` after the final render.
- Rule for the Capabilities Guide: **never start a camera push during a pose change that is meant to be read as a vertical move** (bend, bow, sit, duck). The push cancels the move on screen. Pose first, camera second.
- Rule for the Guide: after moving a character's mark to fix one shot, check every later shot in the same scene block for collisions with set pieces (the rock here).
