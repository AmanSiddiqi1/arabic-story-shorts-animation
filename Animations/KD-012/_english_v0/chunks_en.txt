In a distant forest, a herd of elephants lived near a wide plain.|A long time passed without rain, the small ponds dried up, and water became scarce.|The elephants grew very thirsty and began searching for a new place to drink.
After a long journey, one elephant found a beautiful lake.|Little hares lived all around it in homes beneath the ground.|The elephants hurried toward the water without noticing the hares’ burrows.
The elephants’ footsteps were heavy.|They damaged some burrows and frightened the hares.|The hares gathered far away and said,|“If the elephants return every day, we will have no safe homes left.”
A clever hare said, “I will speak to the king of the elephants,|but I will not go near his feet.”|That evening he climbed onto a high rock|and called to the elephant king in a voice everyone could hear.
“I am the messenger of the moon!” the hare declared.|“This lake belongs to the moon,|and he is angry because you have hurt the hares who live beside it.”|The elephant king stopped in surprise and asked, “Where can I see the moon?”
The hare led him to the lake’s edge on a clear night.|The moon was shining in the sky, and its reflection appeared on the surface of the water.|“Look at the moon here,” said the hare, “and do not disturb him.”
The elephant king bent down to look at the reflection and accidentally stirred the water with his trunk.|The moon’s image shook and disappeared for a moment.|“See?” said the hare. “You have angered him again!”|The king grew afraid and stepped back.
The elephant king gathered his herd and said,|“We will find water somewhere else, and we will not harm these hares.”|The elephants left in the morning.|The hares returned to their burrows, happy that their homes were safe once more.
