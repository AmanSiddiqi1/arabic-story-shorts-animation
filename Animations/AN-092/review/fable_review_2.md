## Notes (polish only; none requires v3)

### 1. 151.5-152.1, NEW, minor: Ja'far walks through the diwan and across the Caliph's face
What is wrong: coming out from behind the right end of the diwan to fetch the purse, Ja'far slides left behind the Caliph (151.7-151.85, heads merge) and at 152.0 is suddenly drawn in front of the Caliph and in front of the diwan, covering the Caliph's face for a few frames, before settling left of him at 152.1.
Why it matters: it is the only layering glitch in the film and it sits on the Caliph just as he turns to reward Ali.
Fix: keep Ja'far drawn behind the Caliph and the diwan for the whole sideways move, and let him pass along the back of the diwan to beyond its left end (clear of the Caliph's head by at least 40 units) before the draw order changes; only then step forward and down to Ali. Start the move at 150.96 (نهاية) instead of 151.5 so he is in place when the Caliph waves at 152.40. Alternative: bring him round the right end and across the front of the dais below the seat line, so his head never crosses the Caliph's.

### 2. 135.9-136.22, minor: THAT'S ALL! is a 0.3 s flash
What is wrong: the cut back to the three faces lasts about 0.3 s, and the lettering is still fading in for most of it (135.95 and 136.0 frames), so it cannot be read. The cut also takes the lemon away 0.45 s after it lands, before its roll toward the stranger finishes.
Fix: stay on the insert until 136.22 so the lemon completes its two bounces and roll with the counter on 4, and put THAT'S ALL! on the insert's open wall (between the two chins, under the counter) at full opacity from 135.7. Then cut straight to the toss framing at 136.22. If the three-face reaction is kept instead, the lettering must be fully opaque on the first frame of the cut.

### 3. 145.3-150.2, minor: Ja'far crowds the laughing Caliph
What is wrong: Ja'far stands shoulder to shoulder with the Caliph, so the Caliph's flung-out left arm lands on Ja'far's chest (146.0) and the two heads and hats read as one cluster at phone size.
Fix: move Ja'far 70-90 units further right (still behind the diwan end), or tip the Caliph's arm spread up and to the left. Keep the behind-the-hand laugh at 147.0.

### 4. 44.7-46.1, minor (present in v1 too): MINE! is red lettering on the red and white awning
Fix: move it to the blue sky above the awning, or to the plain wall left of the stranger's head.

### 5. 110.2-111.7, cosmetic: depth counter straddles the cloud outline
The "720 FT..." text sits across the left edge of the thought cloud. Move it about 40 units right so it is fully inside the cloud, still outside the bag outline.

### 6. 96.9-97.4, cosmetic: ENOUGH! starts over the witness band

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- Table-top insert for a punchline prop: own composition, people cut at the chin, container at one end, one item per spoken word into a row, counter badge on open wall.
- Pile-following camera for `claimPile`: side framing that tilts up with the pile, 0.6-0.7 s reaction cut-ins, symmetrical wide only as bookends.
- Laugh recipe: held puffed beat with the mood scribble, burst with `dustBurst`, head rock at 2.5 Hz, arms out then surface slaps, tear and a smaller second wave, lettering above the laugher.
- Guide rule to add: when a figure parked behind furniture has to come forward, route him past the end of the furniture before changing draw order; never switch layers while he overlaps a seated figure (note 1).
