# AN-092 Ali the Persian: creative plan

Film length 170.67 s (MP3 167.67 s + 3.0 s THE END). 15 scenes, 159 actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`. The script has paragraphs 1-4 and 6-8 (paragraph 5 was removed from the audio on purpose). The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the intent.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Shape.** A frame story. A sleepless Caliph wants to laugh (P1); Ali the Persian tells him a tale (P2-P7); the Caliph laughs and rewards him (P8). The tale is a courtroom farce built on one contrast that must be on screen the whole time: **a very small bag against very big claims**.

| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | 0-24 | The Caliph cannot sleep; Ja'far proposes Ali; Ali sits before him |
| Plant | 28.8 | One small leather bag with his lunch |
| Inciting | 39.56 | A stranger snatches it in the market and claims it |
| Rising | 52.9-72.9 | The judge asks what is inside; the list goes from sensible to absurd |
| Turn | 76.86 | Ali, stunned, decides to out-lie the liar |
| Escalation | 83.4-96.0 | House, school, garden, Baghdad, Basra, armies, thousands of witnesses |
| Climax | 97.06 | The judge's patience ends: a bottomless sea, or a hall for all mankind? |
| Suspense | 116.6-123 | "Open it." Everyone leans in |
| Reveal | 130.8-135.4 | Bread, a little cheese, olives, one lemon |
| Resolution | 136-145 | Ali throws the bag at his rival's feet and leaves; the judge faces the absurdity |
| Emotion | 144.92 | The Caliph explodes with laughter; the gift |
| Moral | 155-166 | Not a complicated ruling but a simple look inside exposed two unbelievable claims |

**Conflict:** two men, one bag, and a judge caught between two growing mountains of nonsense. **Surprises:** the first absurd item after three sensible ones (60.62); Ali joining in instead of protesting (76.86); the size of the truth (130.82). **Emotional arc:** the Caliph's worry (a black scribble over his head) shrinks as he listens and bursts when he laughs; in the last shot he is asleep. **Moral:** checking the facts beats arguing about them.

## 3. Character acting

- **Caliph:** restless and heavy at first (lid 0.35-0.4, brow negative, head drop); his interest is measured by posture: lean 0.06-0.08 forward as the tale starts. The laugh is prepared by a held, trembling, puffed-cheek beat (144.92) and released with a rock back (tip -0.2 at 2.5 Hz), with a smaller second wave (falling pattern). Yawn at 154.34, asleep at 166.6.
- **Ja'far:** quick, deferential. Skid stop with a lurch, beard-stroking with saccades, a hop on the idea, laughter hidden behind his hand and out of phase with the Caliph.
- **Ali:** open and easy on the road; a full-body jolt at the grab (36.98); stillness for the pause at 73.2 (only pupils, then one blink); the decision at 76.86 is a slow change (lids narrow, smile creeps, sleeves go up) before the step. After that he performs with big arm shapes (use `aL/aR` angles for the wide gestures so they read at phone size). Calm and unhurried while opening the bag; deadpan for the toss; chin up on the exit.
- **The stranger:** his eyes find the bag before anything else (35.56); sneak steps with darting glances; shameless confidence while listing (tiptoe stretch, arms up); smug rock on his heels (75.74) that cracks tile by tile during Ali's list (lids open at Baghdad, arms unfold at the army, jaw drops at the soldiers, droop at 96.0). A gulp and a sweat drop at 117.46; a last hope when the bag is shaken (134.58); the wavering smile at 142.66.
- **Judge:** the straight man. A running turban gag in four steps (tilt back 66.42, slip 89.52, straighten 98.62, roll off 144.24). His nod freezes on the first absurd tile; he sinks behind the desk at 93.46; anticipation arms-up from 96.4 before the slam. After the slam every gesture is a different hand shape (wag, cupEar, palmsUp, headHold, both arms out) so the long speech keeps changing. The film's single look to camera is his, at 143.36.
- **Onlookers:** never in unison (`stagger`); their eyes ping-pong between speakers; one covers her mouth at the lion; they lean in for the opening and their heads follow Ali out.
- **Secondary motion:** bag swing on its strap, cape and turban lag, pile sway after each tile, the lemon's two bounces and roll, the rolling turban.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour by scale:** every tile is measured against the bag by the bubble trail; the tilt at 70.96 and the label IN THIS SMALL BAG?! state the joke once, clearly.
- **Rule of three, then break it:** three small sensible tiles that fit the bag, then the tents.
- **Competition:** Ali's tiles are bigger, wider and finally alive (marching soldiers, hand-raising witnesses), so "a bigger exaggeration" is literal.
- **Suspense by stillness:** the held beat when the bag opens (121.0-121.8): only pupils move.
- **Anticlimax:** the food appears one item per word, small, on a plain cloth, with a counter that stops at 4; the last hope and the single lemon.
- **Dramatic irony:** the audience saw the loaf balloon at 31.02, so it knows roughly what is inside while both men swear to cities.
- **Emotion:** the worry scribble is the Caliph's mood meter; the closing sleep answers the opening sleeplessness without a word.
