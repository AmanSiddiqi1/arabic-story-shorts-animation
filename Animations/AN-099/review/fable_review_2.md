## Remaining notes (polish, none triggers v3)

### A. 126.0-131.5, 146.0-148.0, 152.5-154.6 s: the stranger still frowns where he should be calm or pleased
What is wrong: in the close two-shot his mouth is a down-turned curve with brows slightly raised while the caliph asks why (126 to 127.5), in the silence after his speech (147.2) and, most visibly, while the caliph praises him and the ticked balloons appear (153.0 to 154.5, he looks up at them with a sad mouth).
Why it matters: these are his moments of plain conviction and of being honoured. A sad face under the praise balloons contradicts the line "the caliph was more pleased with this answer".
Fix: mouth `closed` (flat) for 126.0 to 127.7 and 146.3 to 148.2; small `smile` with `lid` 0.2 from 152.5 to 154.7, gaze up to the balloons then to the caliph. Keep the frown only for the held breath 113.5 to 117.9.

### B. 109.7-154.7 s: two-shot could still sit lower in the panel
What is wrong: cloth and floor take roughly the bottom 30 % of the panel and there is empty wall above the crown; the top-band icons (133.5 to 138.5) and praise balloons (152.5 to 154.5) are pushed against the top edge as a result.
Fix: raise the camera centre a further 30 to 40 world units or reduce zoom slightly so the top band has room; see note G for the balloons.

### C. 122.0-122.4 s: the palm reaches the cup during the pull-out
What is wrong: at 122.2, as the camera cuts wider, his outstretched palm is right against the rim of the cup for a few frames, which briefly looks like pushing the cup or reaching for it.
Fix: start lowering the palm at 122.0 before the caliph withdraws the cup, or keep the palm at its 121.0 position (about 40 units short) until the cup has moved away.
