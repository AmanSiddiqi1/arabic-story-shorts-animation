# AN-099 Caliph al-Maamun and the Strange Scholar: creative plan

Film length 172.47 s (narration 169.47 s + 3.0 s THE END). One set, seven people, 16 scenes, 137 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; exact cues, poses and Library calls for every action are in `creative_plan.json`. This file gives the direction behind them.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Spine: the distance to the throne.** The hall is staged left to right as a ladder of honour: door, bare floor, the scholars' cushions, the foot of the dais, the cushion beside the throne. The stranger starts at the far left and each good answer moves him one seat to the right. The audience should be able to follow the story with the sound off just by where he is sitting.

**Conflict** is social, not physical: being overlooked. **Surprises:** the quiet hand at the back; the snob handing over his cushion; the refusal of a caliph's cup. **Emotional arc:** hopeful and slightly embarrassed (S2), snubbed but steady (S3), calm authority (S5 to S9), warmth (S10), one tense held breath (S11, S12), plain conviction (S13, S14), shared joy (S15), dignity (S16). **Moral:** knowledge and a clear mind raise a person, not clothes or money; protect what earned your place.

## 3. Character acting

- **The stranger** (`ascetic` preset, age 0.4, bare feet, s 0.72). Economy is his character: he is the only one who never hurries or waves. Posture rises through the film: head drop 6 at the entrance, level by S6, fully upright by S8. Signature poses, each used twice as setup and payoff: the **quiet raised hand** (43.62, 69.24; arm angles about [2.3, 2.6], not a head-height hand target), **hand on chest with a small bow** (thanks at 46.8, refusal at 117.94), and the **temple tap** (136.52, 166.28). His gaze is always on the person he addresses; during the cup offer only his pupils move and his blinks are suppressed.
- **The caliph** (`caliph` preset, age 0.35, s 0.8). Appetite for ideas: leans forward for questions (`tip` 0.06 to 0.07 with a lean-back anticipation), sits up with a `sq` 1.05 settle at each good answer, laughs with his head back at 148.0 while his crown jewel follows a beat late. He is surprised at the refusal (brow -0.9, mouth o, saccades cup to man) but never angry.
- **The proud scholar** (`vizier` preset, dark red). Chin up, lids half down, answers first and loudest. His arc is the film's second story: sideways look at the patches (26.4), folded arms (54.9), jaw drop (75.0), eyes lowered as he offers the cushion (82.68), frozen over the rim of his goblet (122.8), the lowest and longest bow at the end.
- **The old scholar** (`physician` preset, age 1). Half asleep (lid 0.7), forgets his own answer at 38.4, covers his ears during the quarrel, is fully awake by S8, catches his slipping turban at 90.56, declines wine and keeps his bread.
- **The young scholar** (`merchant` preset, green, age 0.05). Copies the proud one two to four frames late until S5, then becomes the first friendly face (makes room at 61.06, slides the bowl at 101.94).
- **Ensemble rule:** every group action uses `stagger(i)` with different delays and amounts; listeners always have a gaze target (`look`, `lookAt`); `LIFE.t = t` for blinks; `breathe()` on every hold longer than a second; every sit or stand uses `settleTo` or `landSquash`.

## 4. Camera and composition

- Panel world 1000 x 1230, floor y 900. Wide shots (zoom 1.0 to 1.25) are for geography and the four walks; because five seated figures are small in a wide, every beat that depends on a face gets a medium or close (zoom 1.6 to 2.7).
- Establishing: close on the caliph, pull out during the first pause (3.36 to 4.30). The pan to the door at 13.2 introduces the stranger small under the tall arch.
- The snub (S3) keeps the stranger at the left edge of the frame; never crop the victim.
- The raised hand starts in a wide so the eye has to find the one moving thing, then the camera pushes to him.
- Each move to a new seat is a tracking wide with the shrinking floor arrow.
- The cup: two-shot at zoom 2.1 to 2.4 with the goblet on the centre line between the faces; S13 is one unbroken held shot.
- S14 pulls out to give the thought cloud the top band while the real cup stays at the bottom of the frame.
- S16 mirrors S2: same door, same wide, but every eye line now points at him; final push to the doorway, the cup alone in the right foreground.
- Balloons, labels and the counter live in the top band (world y 280 to 560); nothing is placed over a face or in the caption panel.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Wordless questions.** The script never says what the questions were, so nothing is invented: Q1 is a knot that the stranger pulls straight, Q2 three pieces he joins, Q3 a balloon that grows wider than the room. The same knot returns inside the S14 thought cloud, curling up again, so the audience sees what the wine would undo.
- **Humour:** the scholars' answers making the knot worse; the old man forgetting his answer; balloons shoving each other; the turban slip; the proud scholar caught mid-sip.
- **Suspense:** two held stillnesses, the hand at 43.62 and the cup at 113.5. Both are followed by the smallest possible gesture.
- **Mirrors:** blank book (S3) against full book (S13); entrance unnoticed against exit honoured; the empty spot by the door shown again at 104.12 and in the cloud at 142.1.
- **Emotion:** the proud scholar's cushion; the caliph coming down from the throne to eat beside him; the last temple tap in the doorway.
- **Last image:** the untouched cup. It states the moral without a caption.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- **Crowding:** six figures in a 1000-wide hall. Review a contact sheet at phone size; if seat C hides the proud scholar's face, move C to x 610.
- **Front-facing figure:** there is no profile, so address is carried by gaze, small head shifts and `tip`. The cup offer relies on world hand targets.
- **Wine on screen** follows the narration; nobody is shown drunk in the real hall, only the mild imagined self in the thought cloud.
- **Translation:** the English is a condensed paraphrase of the Arabic (P1 drops "to debate difficult questions", P7 drops the address to the Commander of the Faithful and turns the last sentence into a question). Both are kept verbatim; P7's second English chunk is long (17 words) and may need a smaller size.
- **Owner questions (7, in the JSON):** wine shown; the tipsy imagined self; wordless questions; the added cushion and bows; the invented lettering list; three scholars standing for the assembly; the caliph's dark beard and his coming down to the floor for the meal.
