# AN-044 The Merchant and the Two Sharpers: creative plan

Film length 169.54 s of narration + 3.0 s end hold (to 172.5 s). 16 scenes, 155 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; the exact cue words and times are in `creative_plan.json` (authoritative for timing). The text below is the direction behind it.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Spine.** A rich, trusting merchant of Sindah loads his camels and sets out (P1). Two cheats attach themselves to him dressed as merchants (P2). At the first rest stop they plot to rob him (P3). Each secretly decides to get rid of the other and keep everything (P4). Each poisons a bowl for the other (P5). They eat, each too pleased with his own plan to suspect his own bowl, and both die (P6). Next morning the merchant finds them, reads what they left, and understands (P7). He travels on, safe, never knowing the whole story (P8).

**Conflict.** The audience expects cheats versus merchant. The real conflict is cheat versus cheat; the merchant never fights at all. That is the joke and the moral: he is saved by their greed.

**Surprises.** (1) 67.9 "each hid another idea": the masks drop the moment they stop facing each other. (2) 83.4: the two thought clouds are shown side by side and are mirror images. (3) 103.2: the tableau of two smiles, two bowls, two hidden vials. (4) 125.4: both point at each other and pull out their own vials in the same instant.

**Emotional arc.** Warm and sunny (S1-S4), conspiratorial dusk (S5-S6), night and comic menace (S7-S12), a sober quiet morning (S13-S14), relief and distance (S15-S16).

**Ending and moral.** A low shot of the empty mat: two bowls, two crossed arrows "MADE BY 1" and "MADE BY 2" forming an X, the caravan small and safe on the horizon. Whoever sets a trap for his partner falls into it.

## 3. Character acting

- **Merchant** (`person` costume `merchant`): open and warm. Brows raised, easy smile, big honest gestures (`armsOut`, `wave`, `chest`). Careful with his treasure (saccades left and right before shutting the chest at 15.6). At the discovery: recoil with `settleTo`, `cheeks`, then slow hands down, `wet` eyes for one beat, `kneel`, a sober `mouthCover` at فهم. No light bulb, no gloating. His last look back at 160.9 is short.
- **Sharper A, "1"** (tall, s 1.05, green merchant robe and yellow turban after 30.46): smooth. `brow -0.4`, `lid` 0.3-0.4, thin smile; practised hands. His mask is perfect while B can see him and drops the instant B looks away (66.4, 73.8). He wins every exchange except the last.
- **Sharper B, "2"** (short, s 0.88, red robe, oversized white turban): the copycat. Copies A half a beat late (`stagger`) and with the wrong hand; overdoes handshakes and nods; the turban slips over his eyes (36, 60.9). His poisoning repeats A's pose exactly (setup and payoff) plus a fumbled cork and far too much poison.
- **Mirror rule.** From 82.8 the two are staged as mirror images: back to back, same peek, same snap away, same smile. At 123.1 they move in unison for the only time (three gaze snaps: belly, own bowl, each other).
- **Eating (S11).** Each man's `lookAt` target is the other's mouth, never his own bowl. A's smile widens a notch on each of B's swallows; B wiggles on each of A's.
- **Deaths (S12).** `tremble`, `sq` 0.9, green dots rising up the face, pointing with `pointFwd`, one look to camera, one rigid frame, then `fallY` backward, `impactSquash`, a single `rebound` of the shoes, stillness. Turbans roll on `swing`.
- **Camels.** Never in unison. Bored half lids, chewing, loose lip. Camel2 is the silent witness: its eyes follow the cheats at 47.8, it looks at their tiny bundles at 39.9, and at 134.3 it turns its head to point the merchant toward the rock. Loads lag one beat when the camels rise or kneel.
- Everyone blinks (`LIFE.t = t`); shock holds use `noBlink`.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230 per view, ground y 900; faces kept between y 560 and 760 so nothing important is near the caption panel.
- Establishing wides open each location (S1 gate, S5 camp). The S5 wide must show the rock standing between the tent and the mat, so "alone" and later "a far place" both read.
- Dialogue and plotting in `twoShot` (S6, S11). Secrets in singles: push in to A (S7), whip to B (S8), then pull out to reveal both clouds.
- Inserts on the bowls for each poisoning (zoom 2.6, hands and bowl only), then back out to faces.
- S12 pulls out fast as they fall, and holds on the empty mat.
- S13 tracks the merchant left to right across the whole camp, ending with the rock at frame right; S14 reveals by rounding the rock.
- S16 starts low on the mat (no bodies in frame) and cranes up and out to the horizon road.
- One LATER card (127.28, NEXT MORNING...) with the camera cut hidden at its midpoint. One wipe (42.6) for day to dusk, one whip pan (78.62).
- Thought clouds sit above and to the outer side of the thinker; the two mirrored clouds are 330 x 220 at (1700, 380) and (2060, 380).

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** two tiny bundles beside six big loads (GOODS? 2 against GOODS 6); B's slipping turban; the costume change behind a palm; the halo that slides off A's turban; B pouring the whole vial; LOOK DOWN! that nobody obeys.
- **Suspense:** the green badge. Once a bowl is poisoned it carries a green dot on its rim for the rest of the film, so the audience always knows more than the eaters. Bowl A has a yellow rim, bowl B a red rim, so the swap is readable without words.
- **Surprise:** the identical clouds at 83.4 and the double accusation at 125.4.
- **Emotion:** the merchant's half-finished wave at two empty cushions (128.2), his wet eyes at 137.1, and the fact that he never learns the swap (the crossed arrows flash in his cloud at 154.5 and fade before he can read them).
- **Lettering diet:** about 17 sound and spoken words, plus short labels on key nouns. Nothing over a face.

- Camels: phase offset 0.5, chew offset 0.37 s; rising and kneeling are two-stage (`kneel` 0..0.5..1) with `settleTo`.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- The camel is effort L and appears in 9 scenes: approve it on a still sheet first.
- Poison and two deaths: staged without blood, faces or close bodies (green comic tint, fall behind a dune lip, shoes and turbans only). The owner may ask for it softer.
- No real silence for effects; expect to keep only 8-10 of the listed ones.
- B's missing beard depends on the `merchant` preset; fall back to a short beard via `pal.beard`.
- Seven owner questions are listed in the JSON (`owner_questions`): the cheats' distinct looks and tags, the softened deaths, the added mutual-discovery beat, the invented clues, the invented lettering and closing caption, the NEXT MORNING... card, and the costume change on "pretended".
