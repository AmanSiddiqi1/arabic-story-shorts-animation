An ascetic received a milking cow and led it home in the evening.|He imagined drinking its milk every day.|But a thief saw him on the road|and decided to follow him and steal the cow after he fell asleep.
At the same time a devil was also following the ascetic.|He did not want the cow; he intended to abduct the sleeping man.|The devil met the thief near the house,|and each asked the other why he was there.
“I will take the cow after her owner sleeps,” said the thief.|“And I will take the man himself,” said the devil.|Both thought the moment was right,|so they agreed to wait until the ascetic put out the lamp and rested.
The ascetic went inside, tied the cow in a corner,|ate his supper, and fell asleep.|The thief and the devil slipped in.|But before either moved, they began arguing over who should go first.
The devil said, “Let me take the man first.|If you take the cow before me, she may wake up and make a noise.|Then the ascetic will rise, and I cannot take him.”|But the thief refused to wait.
The thief replied, “No, let me steal the cow first!|If you try to abduct the man, he may wake up.|Then I cannot leave with the cow.”|Their voices grew louder, and neither would give way.
Suddenly the thief shouted, “Ascetic, wake up!|A devil wants to abduct you!”|The devil shouted too, “Wake up! This is a thief trying to steal your cow!”|The ascetic heard the yelling, opened his eyes, and jumped up.
The neighbors came when they heard the commotion,|and the thief and the devil fled empty-handed.|The ascetic stayed in his home with his cow beside him.|Both intruders had been close to success, but their quarrel saved the man.
