## Summary

v2 is a clear improvement. The blocker is gone (DAY 1..5 reads correctly), the yard act now shows both speakers and their mimes, the thief is visibly the one who shouts first, the two exits are readable (jump arc, legs in the window, POOF in open space), the road and yard wides no longer float over a deep ground band, and the epilogue gag can be seen. Subtitles are unchanged, verbatim and readable. No blocker.

One correction pass is still worth it, and it is narrow. The re-staging put the thief on top of the sleeper for the whole climax (125.9-139.5), and this created three new errors in the scene the fable exists for: the sleeper's Z letters are drawn over the thief's face while he speaks, he sits and stands on the man he is trying to wake, and in the devil's reply the devil's arm and the badge cross the thief's face. Separately, the cow was shrunk to fix the tangle and is now the size of a dog indoors (she is taller than a man's waist on the road), and in the double reach she is hidden behind the thief. The quarrel pair still stand shoulder to shoulder and frontal, but that beat is readable now and is not a reason for v3 by itself.

Nothing below changes narration, subtitle text or timing.

## Fixes for v3 (one pass, most important first)

### 1. 125.94-139.5 s. Z letters cover the thief's face through both accusations
- Wrong: the sleeper's Z stream rises up and to the right from his head, exactly where the thief now is, and is drawn after him. A Z sits on the thief's eyes or mouth at 126.2-127.2, 130.0-132.4 and again at 135.0-139.2 (`extra/f_130.7.png`, `f_131.9.png`, `f_136.9.png`, `f_138.7.png`).
- Why: these are the thief's own lines ("Ascetic, wake up! A devil wants to abduct you!") and then the moment he is accused. His face is the picture.
- Fix: from 125.94 until the Z's stop, send the Z stream up and to the LEFT of the sleeper's head (toward the empty wall between the door post and the window, x about 330-400), or draw the Z's before both intruders. Also thin them with the meter: three Z's at 10-7, two at 6-3, one small Z at 2, a single faint, shrinking Z at 1 (135.0), none from 139.5. No Z may overlap a head of the thief or the devil at any sampled frame from 125.9 to 139.5.

### 2. 126.0-139.5 s. The thief sits and stands on the sleeper, and the devil's arm crosses his face
- Wrong: (a) after the leap the thief lands on the sleeper's chest and stays there, kneeling then standing on the blanket, to 139.5 (`extra/f_129.2.png`). (b) After the devil's leap (133.64-134.5) the devil lands at about x 560, one body width from the thief; from 136.2 his extended arm and the torn badge lie across the thief's head, so under the THIEF! arrow there is a face made of Z, badge and hand (`extra/f_136.9.png`, `f_138.7.png`). (c) The sack mime at 131.70 cannot be read: both hands are at his head behind a Z.
- Why: a man sitting on the victim is not "calling him awake", it removes the last suspense before the waking; and the reply "this is a thief" needs a clear thief to point at. In v1 this second half was the clearer one; it should not end up worse.
- Fix:
  - Thief lands kneeling on the FLOOR beside the head, on the window side: feet at about x 560, on the floor line, behind the mat edge, torso leaning left over the head (`tip` about -0.12). Nothing of him overlaps the sleeper's body except a hand that shakes the shoulder on 128.12.
  - Keep cupped hands and the WAKE UP! burst at his mouth as now (128.80), the full-arm point at the devil on 129.88-131.6 as now.
  - 131.70 (`خطفك`): both arms up and round, then pulled down over his own head in one move (the sack over a head), 0.5 s, head visible between the arms at the start; then hands off.
  - Devil's leap 133.64: land on the cow side at about x 700, at least 140 units from the thief, facing left. Yellow WAKE UP! above the devil as now (134.96).
  - 136.84 (`لص`): the devil's pointing arm stops 30-40 units short of the thief, hand below the thief's chin line; the torn badge half goes to his other hand or down by his side. THIEF! label and arrow above the thief with clear air around his head. The thief does his own "who, me?"
