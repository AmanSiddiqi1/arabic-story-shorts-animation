## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A poet and a princess fall in love at a glance; an attendant who loves him herself brings them together; after three happy years the king's unexplained anger ends it in a hermitage in the desert.

**Shape.** Two minutes of warm romance, then a quiet, sudden tragedy. The film must earn the fall: the happier and lighter the first 140 s, the harder the closed door at 150 s lands.

## 3. Character acting

- **Adi (green).** Open and a little vain at first: chin up, sash straightened, speech as a ribbon. In love he becomes still: the unfinished wave at 42.68 that drifts down for a full second, hands over the heart, a long blink. Signature pose: the bow (`tip` 0.14, `PERSON_HANDS.chest`, `lid` 1 at the bottom) at 35.12, 99.32, 167.36 and 173.5. In S16 his smile drops in 4 frames, palms come up, shoulders drop (`sq` 0.96), `wet` eyes, one look back; no struggle.
- **Hind (blue and white, crown).** Composure: `lid` 0.2, folded hands, she moves her eyes before her head. She breaks composure three times, each larger: the laugh behind her hand (39.32), leaning out of the window with her chin on her arms (98-101.9), and the collapse at the door (154.02: `kneel`, `wet`, `tremble`, crown set down). At the hermitage she is almost motionless; only `age`, tears and the sky move.
- **Mariyah (red, yellow scarf).** Always moves first, in quick short steps; eyes in saccades (`gazePlan(…, 0.07)`). Comic business: the low secret wave, the silent clap, placing Adi on his mark, the "now" nod. Her mask: the bright smile snaps on in 6 frames after each private moment (70.78, 87.92). Hand over the sash where the small heart is hidden: 70.78, 86.04, 126.64.
- **King (red, crown, cape).** Slow and heavy. Beard stroking, narrowed eyes, a drumming finger (`countedBeats`). His agreement is held 0.4 s behind a stern face. In S16: `brow` ramp to 1, sharp stand with overshoot (`settleTo`), a rock-steady pointing arm (arm angles, not hand targets), two small cold claps, then his back.
- **Friend, vizier, guards.** Friend: wind-up (`anticipate`), dash with `dustBurst`, braking overshoot, panting (`breathe`). Vizier reacts 5 frames after the king (`stagger`) and freezes mid bite at the proposal. Guards walk in step, eyes front.
- **Everyone:** `LIFE.t = t` for blinks, a gaze target at all times, listeners look at speakers. Blinks suppressed in the three held looks (31.9, 98.1, 151.5).

## 4. Camera and composition

- Establishing wide of the yard, push to Adi, then room made for the inset (S1).
- S3 tracks Hind's walk across the gap and ends as a `twoShot` with Mariyah still visible at the edge: the matchmaker is always in the picture.
- S4: when the women leave the camera stays on Adi and pushes in (zoom 1.8 to 2.3). The empty right side of the frame is the image of the "deep mark".
- S9 is built on a diagonal eye line from street to balcony; S10 cuts close on Hind with Adi tiny below.
- Hall scenes put the king high and right on the dais and petitioners low on the carpet. S16 uses a straight line door, Adi, king; it goes wide and still for the arrest, then pushes slowly toward the closed door.
- S18 is the widest and emptiest frame of the film: one dark figure in the sand, the city small behind.
- S20 pulls out; day-coloured inset upper left against the night, cell lower right, heart between them.
- Keep heads above world y 860 in seated scenes and all key action above y 1000. Panel-only shake (2 units) for the door and the heart crack.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour (first two minutes only):** Mariyah's hidden wave and wink; the whisper balloon with stars; Adi's gulp as Hind approaches; the friend's sprint and panting return; the fizzling Z marks; Mariyah measuring Adi's mark in the street; the vizier frozen mid bite.
- **Suspense:** the stillness of the first look; the king's finger drumming at 107.4 and his held stern face before YES!; the lamps going out one by one at 140.4.
- **Surprise:** the question disc flipping to a crown; Mariyah's own heart; the king's anger arriving in a happy hall with no cause, staged as a happy Adi walking in mid verse.
- **Emotion:** the three hearts (the lovers' large one, Mariyah's small hidden one, the cracked one at the end); the limp verse ribbon as the only thing left of Adi; the crown set down on the floor; the bow remembered in old age.
- **Bookend:** the story starts at a domed prayer house full of light and ends at a small copy of it in the dark.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus

- `DUR = 180.0` exactly. THE END at 177.4.
- Cues: use `w(p, n)` and check against the times in the JSON. Anticipation starts up to 0.4 s before a cue; never later than the word.
- Walking: `walk(xf, t)` plus `footfalls` for every entrance and exit; the person is front-facing, so sideways travel is translation with leg phase. Sitting and kneeling are blends (`sit`, `kneel` 0..1) with `settleTo`.
- Pointing and other poses that must read at a distance use arm angles (`aL`/`aR`); hands on props or other figures use world targets (`handLW`/`handRW`).
- Write Adi's bow, Mariyah's hand-on-sash and the S3 glance as pose functions of a local clock; S10, S19 and S20 call them again (S20 at half speed inside the inset).

- The closed door in S16 and S17 is `townDoor` drawn over the hall's door arch (`open` 1 to 0 in 0.3 s, ease-in, dead stop).
- The king's turned back: flip with a squeeze as in `turnFlip`, then cover the face with a skin disc and draw the cape over the body.
- Sound (`SND`, `master: 0.6`): pop 25.45, chime 46.5, whoosh 68.0, chime 177.45. Nothing else: the narration has no other gap of 0.3 s.
- Lettering (11): ADI, HIND, KING AL-NUMAN, MARIYAH, WHO IS SHE?, HIND!, YES!, YEARS, WHY?, AL-HIRA, THE END, plus the card 3 DAYS LATER. No dashes.
