# KD-016 The Dove, the Fox, and the Crane: creative plan

Final MP3 161.73 s, last word ends 161.42 s, THE END held to 164.7 s (end_hold 3.0). All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard (11 scenes, 168 timed actions, subtitle chunks) is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the direction behind it.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A fox bluffs a dove into throwing down her chicks. A heron teaches her that the fox cannot climb. The fox then flatters the heron into hiding his own head under his wing, and eats him.

**Moral.** It is easy to give good advice to others and still fail to guard yourself.

**Conflict.** A bully who has only a bluff, a mother who believes it, and an adviser who is wise about everyone except himself.

**Structure in three movements**

| Movement | Time | What happens | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| The bluff works | 0-51.7 | Nest, eggs, chicks (CHICKS 3). The fox threatens; she throws one chick (2), days later another (1). | warmth, then fear and grief |
| The bluff is broken | 51.7-99.3 | A tear reaches the heron; he shows the truth and coaches her; she dares the fox; he slides down the trunk. | hope, comedy, triumph |
| The adviser falls | 99.3-164.7 | She names the heron; the fox flatters him, asks about the wind, gets him to hide his head, leaps. The river is quiet. | suspense, dry dark ending |

**Surprises.** (1) The fox, challenged, really cannot climb (94.2 THUD). (2) The dove's proud, helpful pointing wing at 102.84 is what dooms her friend. (3) The leap at 145.64 after two seconds of total stillness.

**Emotional arc of the dove.** Content (lid half shut) to terror (tremble, wet eyes) to grief (the wail) to resolve (the BRAVE pose) to pride (eyes closed, off guard) to helpless witness (flapping, unheard) to a quiet, sadder wisdom (wing over the last chick).

**Ending.** No gloating picture of the fox. The last image is the empty shallows, one grey feather on the current, and the dove holding her one chick. THE END at 162.0.

## 3. Character acting

- **Dove** (Library `bird`, new kind `dove`). Fear: `tremble`, `wet`, `sq` 0.9, saccades with `gazePlan(…, 0.07)`. Giving a chick: the same pose both times (wing nudge, `lid` 1, head turned away). Grief: the Lark wail pose (`headRot` -0.6, `beak` 1.7, eyes shut, `sag` -0.8). Resolve: tears stop at 70.2, one blink, brow set. BRAVE pose (new `puff`): chest out, head high. Pride at 99.44: `lid` 0.6 so she does not see the fox's face. Final: slow head drop, long blink, wing over the chick.
- **Fox** (Library `fox`, s 0.8). `walk` + `footfalls` + `dustBurst` on every entrance, never sliding. Three threats with falling effort: full bluff with `rear` against the trunk (S2), one lazy paw (S4), a yawn (S7). The failed climb uses `hop`, a scrabble with dust, `fallY`, `impactSquash`, `rebound`. From 97.02 he wears a mask: smile, ears forward and soft eyes while watched; `lid` 0.6, `brow` 1 and a tongue across the teeth when the other looks away. Before the leap: 1.9 s `anticipate` crouch with only the tail tip moving. One look to camera only (44.66).
- **Heron** (new). Tall, slow, exact. Listener acting in S6 (head tilt, crest sinking, a wince). Teacher gestures with the near wing (`wing`, `settleTo`). Vanity is planted at 77.86 (preens), grows at 110.58 (`puff`, `crest` up, `lid` 0.5) and is why he does not hear the dove. The `tuck` 0 to 1 over 138.68-142.44 is the key pose: by 142.44 no head or neck is visible. After the catch: eyes `wide`, crest flat, a 5 frame look to camera, then the shame pattern (eyes break away, head lowers) at 154.38.
- **Chicks** (Library nest `ck`). Never in unison (`stagger`). The last chick is a small character: presses against its mother (50.86), waves (76.24), copies the BRAVE pose (88.62), blows a raspberry (95.2).
- **Secondary motion.** Palm fronds sway; fox tail tip; heron crest plume trails head moves by 2-3 frames; reeds bend with the wind and settle with `swing`; ripples (`ripple`) at every wading step; feathers drift on a damped sway.

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1400 x 1230, ground y 900. The picture is built on one **vertical** (fox at the foot of the palm, nest 570 units above) and one **horizontal** (fox on the bank, heron in the shallows).
- Framings (x, y, zoom): FULL (700,615,1.0); WIDE_PALM (430,590,1.15); NEST_MED (390,360,1.9); NEST_CLOSE (390,300,2.6); FOX_LOW (260,760,1.9); RIVER_WIDE (900,660,1.25); RIVER_TWO from `twoShot()` about (930,640-720,1.75-1.9).
- The same WIDE_PALM framing for all three visits of the fox, so the repeat and the reversal read without words. In S7 the dove is framed high against the sky and the fox small below.
- Two linking moves: the tear at 51.1 carries the camera from the nest down to the river; the pointing arrow at 102.84 carries it from the nest across to the heron.
- Suspense at the river: a slow push (z 1.8 to 2.0) over 138.4-144.6 while almost nothing moves; then keys on take-off and landing, with a 6 px panel-only shake.
- Three small ground ticks between fox and heron make the closing distance readable (x 700, 760, 820).
- Faces and key action stay in the upper two thirds of the picture panel; the caption panel holds only subtitles.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour.** The dove's wobbly copy of the BRAVE pose; the fox sitting with his mouth open for delivery; the yawned third threat; two failed climbs and the THUD; the chick's raspberry; a tear landing on the heron's beak.
- **Suspense.** Her frozen wing over the last chick at 84.6 (will she give in again?); the shrinking distance ticks; the dove's unheard warning at 135.22; the long still crouch.
- **Surprise.** The cloud at 67.8 reverses the cloud at 34.2 (same layout, opposite outcome). The helpful point at 102.84 turns into the betrayal.
- **Emotion.** The chick looking back at its mother as it is pushed; the counter going 3, 2, 1; the single tear that crosses the whole set; the final wing over the last chick.
- **Lettering diet.** Eight sound or shout words in the whole film plus two labels and the card; none after GULP! until THE END.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- The heron's `tuck` pose carries the climax; test it on a still sheet before staging.
- In shots wide enough for fox and nest the dove is only about 125 px tall; her emotion must live in the close shots and in big silhouettes.
- `nestDraw` must draw 2 and 1 chicks cleanly with per-chick acting; check first.
- No narration gap exists at the leap (145.64) or the second chick drop, so there is no effect there.
- Owner questions (six, in the JSON `owner_questions`): heron versus the English title "Crane"; chicks never shown eaten; the heron's death hidden behind a dust cloud and reeds; the added failed climb and the snag; the CHICKS counter and the fox passing the firm dove at the end; the list of invented lettering.
