# AN-043 The Hedgehog and the Wood Pigeons: creative plan

Film length 178.778 s of narration + 4.5 s hold = 183.28 s. 17 scenes, 178 timed actions (all in `creative_plan.json`, each tied to a spoken word). All times are seconds on the final MP3.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Spine.** A hedgehog wants the dates at the top of a palm he cannot climb. He cannot go up, so he makes the owners of the tree bring everything down, first the fruit, then themselves.
- **Conflict.** Appetite dressed as holiness against trust. The pigeons are not stupid: they are polite, and he uses it.
- **Beats.** Home in the crown (0.0); the wish and the failed climb (15.56 to 21.3); the burrow and the act (22.56 to 29.7); thirty years (39.26); the advice (55.82 to 66.68); the harvest and the theft (68.6 to 80.0); the real plan (82.5 to 88.7); the wind excuse and the sermon (99.14 to 108.9); into the burrow (115.04); door blocked, teeth (123.8 to 127.9); TRAP (132.2); the protest and the boomerang (135.5 to 144.1); the tale of the two cheats (146.1 to 168.8); the listener at the door (170 to 178.6).
- **Surprises.** The wink to the lens at 27.78; the date that falls exactly on "what falls" (42.68); the dates stuck on his spines (76.88); the halo that is only the door (119.36); the hidden hoard spilling at the pigeons' feet (130.4).
- **Emotional arc.** Charm and comedy for 110 s, a cold turn at 120.66 when the mask comes off for good, fear, then the small courage of the male pigeon, and an ending that is held breath rather than relief.
- **Ending.** As scripted: unresolved. The pigeon is still telling, the hedgehog still listening, the door still blocked.
- **Moral.** Deceit returns upon the deceiver (143.04), said by the victim and illustrated by his tale; the hedgehog laughs at it without seeing himself.

## 2. Scene-by-scene storyboard
**S1 0.0-13.7 s (P1, palmGrove, establishing / crane).** The pigeon couple at home in the crown of the tall palm, eating dates. A spat date stone falls the whole height of the tree and lands at the hedgehogs' bush: the camera follows it down to meet them.
**S2 13.7-22.0 s (P1, palmGrove, medium / tilt).** The hedgehog stares up at the dates, dreams of them, then tries to climb the trunk twice and falls on his back. Lying there, the idea of a trick is born.
**S3 22.0-30.2 s (P2, palmGrove, medium / push-in).** He digs a burrow right beside the trunk, then sits in front of it and puts on a show of prayer and simple living, checking that the pigeons are watching.
**S4 30.2-44.6 s (P2, palmGrove, two-shot / crane).** The male pigeon notices, flies down to a low stub on the trunk and asks about his life. The hedgehog claims thirty years of this and that he eats only dates that fall; one falls on cue and he takes it with a humble show.
**S5 44.6-54.0 s (P3, palmGrove, two-shot / drift).** The pigeon is won over and asks how he and his wife can live as simply. She flies down and is less sure. The hedgehog hides his delight.
**S6 54.0-68.6 s (P3, palmGrove, medium / push-in).** The advice, shown as a four-picture plan above the hedgehog: shake all the dates down, heap a year of food, come and live by the ground, spend your time in worship. The pigeons copy his praying pose and fly up to start.
**S7 68.6-75.0 s (P4, palmGrove, wide / drift).** Long hours of work in one shot: the sky runs from midday to dusk while the pigeons tug the bunches bare and dates rain onto a growing heap. Below, the hedgehog "prays" with one eye open.
**S8 75.0-90.3 s (P4, palmGrove, medium / push-in).** At dusk the hedgehog carries the whole heap into his burrow, rolling on it so the dates stick to his spines, and hides it for himself. Then he waits at his door in the dark and imagines the hungry birds walking into his paws.
**S9 90.3-101.4 s (P5, palmGrove, wide / crane).** Morning. The pigeons come down and find nothing. They ask the hedgehog, who blames the wind, while not a single frond moves.
**S10 101.4-110.4 s (P5, palmGrove, two-shot / push-in).** The sermon on patience and trust, given by a hedgehog with a date stuck in his back spines and a full belly he has to hide.
**S11 110.4-116.5 s (P6, palmGrove, wide / push-in).** He talks until the sun is low. Worn down, the pigeons believe him and walk into his burrow.
**S12 116.5-120.3 s (P6, burrowChamber, wide / static).** Inside the burrow. The pigeons step down into a dim earth room, still sure he is a good friend.
**S13 120.3-132.4 s (P6, burrowChamber, medium / whip).** The trap closes. He runs to the door, fills it with raised spines and shows his teeth. The room goes dark, his spiky shadow reaches the birds, and when they back away the hidden dates spill out at their feet.
**S14 132.4-144.5 s (P7, burrowChamber, two-shot / drift).** The male pigeon steps in front of his mate and speaks up: yesterday a teacher, today a killer. Does he not fear his trick coming back on him? The hedgehog, who has been creeping closer, stops.
**S15 144.5-154.3 s (P7, burrowChamber, wide / pull-out).** The pigeon starts a story to make his point: two men who set out to cheat a merchant, each secretly planning to betray the other. A story cloud opens over the room and the hedgehog's foot comes down.
**S16 154.3-169.6 s (P8, burrowChamber, wide / push-in).** The hedgehog asks for the story. The pigeon tells it: each cheat poisons the other's food, both fall, and the merchant is left alive. The hedgehog laughs and does not see that the story is about him.
**S17 169.6-183.3 s (P8, burrowChamber, full / pull-out).** The held ending: the hedgehog sits in his own doorway, listening, while the pigeons stay shut inside and the storyteller talks on. Night comes in the door behind him. THE END.

Word-level timing of every action, the lettering and the effects are in the JSON; the animator should work from it scene by scene.

## 3. Character acting
- **Hedgehog: two faces.** Mask: eyes shut, brows raised (brow -0.5), paws clasped, slow sway (`breathe`). Real: open eyes, smirk, brow down, later `teeth`. Until 120.66 the real face shows only when no pigeon looks at him (peek 29.74, side-eye 44.14, paw rub 53.3, lip lick 64.04, grin 67.5, lens look 116.2). After 120.66 the mask never returns. Two looks to camera only: 27.78 (wink) and 116.2 (teeth).
- **His body.** Short waddle with spines lagging one frame; anticipation crouch before the run (17.76), failed climb with scrape marks (19.06 to 21.4), `fallY` + `impactSquash` onto his back; `curl` and roll to load dates (76.88); the skid, `turnFlip` and `spike` 0 to 1 with overshoot in the doorway (123.28 to 124.5). The teeth reveal (126.3 to 127.9) is the slowest move in the film, with no blink.
- **Male pigeon.** Curious head tilts, open beak of wonder at "thirty", bows, copies every pose at once. In the trap: gulp and tremble first, then chest out and a wing across his mate (132.68). As storyteller: big wing shapes, never a pause.
- **Female pigeon.** The sceptic. She looks at the hedgehog when her mate looks where the hedgehog points; nods 5 to 10 frames late; one eye open during the copied prayer (66.48); the last look at the sky before going in (115.78); the first to turn when he runs (122.3). She never speaks.
- **Listeners always react.** Pigeon's head goes back with each ten of the counter (39.26); birds sink with each YAK (113.4); the hedgehog's raised foot freezes at "do you not fear" (141.78), comes down as the tale starts (152.68), paws to cheeks at the deaths (166.3).
- **Secondary motion.** Stub dips under landings, bunches swing after each tug, spines sway after stops, fronds sway always except during the wind excuse (99.14), when everything is dead still.

## 4. Camera and composition
- The palm is the vertical axis of act one: the crown is the pigeons' world, the foot of the trunk the hedgehog's. The first move down is carried by a falling date stone (8.7 to 10.3), so the camera never tilts without a reason. His look up (14.08) takes us back up.
- Dialogue happens on a diagonal: bird on the low stub at (560, 640), hedgehog at (740, 900). Two-shots at zoom 1.65 to 1.9 keep both faces; the advice scene leaves the top of the panel for the picture strip.
- S7 is one wide held shot with the sky running: time passing as a picture. The LATER card (NEXT MORNING...) sits in the pause at 90.3.
- Inside the burrow the door arch is on the left and the hoard on the right, with the birds between: the geography is the trap. Push in to an extreme close mouth at 126.3, whip to the birds at 128.3, widen for TRAP!.
- S15 to S16: the story cloud takes the upper half and the three animals sit under it like an audience; push into the cloud for the poisoning, back out for the laugh.
- Last shot pulls out to the full room (zoom 1.0) with THE END in the upper earth band. Faces and key action always stay above the caption panel.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Humour.** The failed climb; the wink; the date that falls on cue and the trembling tiny nibble; the last date bouncing off his motionless head; dates stuck on spines; the wife's paw snatching back a stray date behind the pigeons' backs (100.98); the date on his back and the swallowed burp during the sermon on not caring for food; YAK YAK YAK.
- **Suspense.** The pat on the ground by his burrow with a lip lick (64.04); eyes shining in the dark doorway (88.68); the female's look back at the sky (115.78); three slow steps toward the birds during the protest; the foot frozen in the air.
- **Surprise.** The door "halo" at 119.36, then the room going dark and the spiked shadow reaching the birds (124.6); dates spilling from behind the roots (130.4).
- **Emotion and irony.** The male's shield wing; the shared look before the tale (158.76); the boomerang arrow flickering over the laughing hedgehog (168.84); spine shadows as cage bars at the end (176.64).

## 8. Warnings and open questions
- The hedgehog figure carries the film; if time is short, cut pose names (belly, ear, cheeks) before cutting sit/rear, clasp, spike, curl or teeth.
- Two long subtitle chunks (paragraph 8 chunk 2, paragraph 3 chunk 3) may need a smaller Arabic size.
- The script ends unresolved; no escape or punishment is added.
- 7 owner questions are listed in the JSON (`owner_questions`): the wife's extra appearances, the generic prayer pose, the softened eating and poisoning, THE END on an open ending, four added gags, and the invented lettering.
