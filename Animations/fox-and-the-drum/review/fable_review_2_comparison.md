# The Fox and the Drum: creative director review 2 (v1 compared with the original)

Reviewed: `renders/The Fox and the Drum - v1.mp4` (163.5 s). All 28 v1 strips at 4 fps were read in order against the original strips, plus 20 larger stills (3.7, 22.5, 39.5, 43.6, 61.4, 66.1, 77.8, 99.5, 108.6, 124.4, 124.6, 125.5, 126.05, 128.0, 130.55, 130.7, 136.5, 141.5, 155.3, 160.9). The audio was not listened to; sync, loudness and duration are taken from the coordinator's QC. Motion between strip frames (blinks, turn easing, 2-frame snaps) could not be checked at 4 fps and is marked so below.

Positions are world units as in review 1: hide-tree trunk x 325..435, drum rim left edge x 655, fox snout tip at x+157, tail to x-142.

---

## 1. Verdict

**v1 is a clear improvement and is close to release, but not releasable yet: one new defect breaks the payoff.** Between 124.5 and 126.2, and again at 130.5-130.7, the fox's body is not drawn at all. Only a full-colour head sits on the drum skin, and at 124.6 there is no fox in the frame. This must be fixed, together with two smaller staging faults (the paw resting on the drum after the boom, and the courage beat hidden behind the tree).

What is now better than the original:
- The story reads without labels. Lettering is down from about 45 pieces to 12.
- The branch is visibly the cause: gap at rest, lift, strike, ripple rings, rebound.
- "From a distance", the sneak and the second approach are three different things; the snout stays off the skin.
- The paw touch (96.5-98.4) is the clearest new beat in the film: the `reach` leg reads at phone size and the boom starts at the paw.
- The cross-section (126.2-130.4) makes "inside" and "empty" unmistakable, and the face changes from eager to crestfallen.
- No smile after the disappointment, no bubbles in the moral, no wipe card, and the fox is on screen for his last words.

What is still weak: the fox is very small and cut by the left border for long stretches (56-91, 102-109), and the moral (139-157) is nearly static at phone size.

## 4. New defects in v1

- **A. 124.5-126.16 and 130.46-130.7: the fox's body disappears.** At 124.4 the whole fox stands at the hole. At 124.6 there is no fox. From 124.7 a head in full colour is drawn on top of the drum skin with no neck, body or tail outside. The same picture returns for the first frames after the cross-section (130.55), then nothing (130.7), then the fox pops in at 131.0. It reads as a fox face printed on the drum. The clip appears to be inverted (the fox is kept inside the drum outline instead of being cut where it passes the tear).
- **B. 98.4-101.0: the paw rests on the drum after the boom.** Wide eyes and flat ears say fear while the paw stays on the thing that frightened him for 2.6 s.
- **C. 102.3-109.6: landing, noticing and courage are behind the trunk.** Only a head on the right and a tail on the left show. The breath, shake-off and chest of "جمع شجاعته" cannot be seen.
- **D. 56.4-91.0 and 102.4-107.0: the fox is on the left border.** Camera x 610 at zoom 1.4 shows x 253..967; his head is at 420-490 and his body is cut. The hero occupies under a tenth of the frame width for about 40 s.
- **E. 139.4-157.0 and 132.86-139.14: static talk.** One pose for 18 s in the moral; in the surprise line (134.92-139.14) the mouth is closed while he speaks.
- **F. 160.2-160.8:** he walks behind the trunk during "يبحث عن طعام".
- **G. 3.6-6.0:** rump and tail cut by the left border at the hunger stop.
- **H. 126.2-130.4:** in the cross-section his forelegs hang in mid-air inside the drum, and the head is about a fifth of the panel width (smaller than asked).

## 5. Corrections for the final

### Needed before release
1. **Head in the hole (defect A), 123.6-126.16 and 130.46-131.0.** Draw the whole fox as at 124.4. Hide only the part of the head that lies inside the hole polygon (centre (748, 769)): draw the fox, then redraw the hole's dark fill over it, clipped to the hole outline, so the muzzle and face vanish into the dark up to the ears. Hindquarters and tail stay outside: `tailUp` 0.7 with a fast wag from 123.6 to 126.1. On the cut back at 130.46 the same pose with the tail limp (`tailUp` -0.3), then he backs out by 131.0 with no pop. Camera about x 640, zoom 1.9, so the tail is in frame.
2. **Paw off the drum (defect B).** At 98.4 drop `reach` from 1 to about 0.55 within 3 frames and hold it there, trembling (`tremble` 2 px), until the crouch starts at 100.6. The paw must be 25-30 units clear of the rim.
3. **Fox in front of the hide tree after the jump (defects C and F).** Switch the draw order two frames after take-off (about 101.45), where the motion hides the change, and keep the fox in front of the trunk to the end of the film. Landing at x 380, the silence, the eyes on the bough, the breath, shake-off and stamp, the sit and the walk-off are then all in full view.

### Should fix
4. **Two-shot framing (defect D), 56.4-91.0 and 102.4-107.0.** Camera x 568, zoom 1.28 (shows about x 177..959; drum right edge 945). The fox's head then sits a fifth of the way in. Keep y so the branch root stays in frame.
5. **Moral and surprise line (defect E).** Make the looks head moves, not pupil moves:
   - 134.92-139.14: `talk` on; `headUp` -0.4 (nose up to the branch) over 135.9-137.1; back down to the hole with the head shake at 137.1-139.1.
   - 145.30 "خفت": `turnFlip` to the left for 0.9 s toward the hide tree, ears 0.6, then back.
   - 147.40 "الحقيقة": one nod (`drop` pulse 8).
   - 148.60-150.14 "الصوت العالي": `headUp` -0.35 toward the branch.
   - 153.48-155.2 "والشيء الكبير": the head travels up and down the drum (`headUp` -0.3 to 0.2).
   - 155.3: the paw pat with `reach` 0.8, the torn skin flaps; `lid` 0.3 on "مهمًّا".

### Nice to have
6. **Cross-section (defect H).** Show head and neck only (clip at the wall, no legs), and enlarge so the head is about a third of the panel width.
7. **Sit.** `sit` 0.55 with the hind legs folded under and the tail lying on the ground on the left.
8. **Opening (defect G).** Have him reach x 170 or more by 3.5 so the whole fox is in frame at the hunger stop.
9. **Hide, 38.5-40.5.** Lower and tighter (`drop` 14, `sq` 0.92, a `tremble` burst) while he faces away.
10. **Last soft hit at 162.0** (bough on the torn drum, quiet thud, after the last word), if it is not already there; I could not see it in the strips.

No further comparison review is needed if corrections 1-3 are checked with stills at 124.6, 125.5, 130.55, 99.5 and 108.6 before the final render.
