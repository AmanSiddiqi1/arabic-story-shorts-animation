# The Fox and the Drum: creative director review 1 (original film)

Reviewed: `Originals/The Fox and the Drum - Halftone Pop 9x16 - Noto Naskh Arabic.mp4` (163.5 s). All 28 strips at 4 fps were read in order, plus 16 larger stills (9.5, 27.0, 39.5, 46.0, 62.75, 64.4, 81.5, 96.9, 97.6, 101.6, 108.2, 116.0, 122.5, 127.0, 135.5, 142.0). Sync was judged against the word times in `narration.txt`; the audio was not listened to or re-measured. `scene_e.js`, `fox_sets.js` and `Library/cast/fox.js` were read only to give positions and field names.

All times are film times. Positions are world units of `scene_e.js` (1000 x 1230, ground y 900). Fox scale 0.95, feet at `x`: tail reaches about x-142, eye x+104, raised paw x+105, snout tip x+157, head height about y 780. Hide-tree trunk x 325..435. Drum centre (800, 775), outer radius 145 (left edge x 655, top y 630). Hole centre (748, 769). Branch tip at rest (812, 596), at full dip y 648. Drum hits (`HB`): 24.8, 25.9, 43.4, 45.6, 61.2, 62.8, 64.4, 66.0, 68.2, 69.8, 71.4, 77.6, 84.2, 104.2, 105.8, 133.6, 144.0, plus the touch at 98.4.

## 1. Diagnosis

### What works
- **One continuous world.** Walk, hide tree and drum are a single stage, so the geography is easy to follow.
- **Broad sync.** The stop is on "توقف" (26.1), the hide on "اختبأ" (37.0-38.3), the jump on "قفز" (101.4), the sit on "جلس" (139.4).
- **The alarm pose at 26.0-30.0** (tail up and puffed, ears back, wide eyes) is the best pose in the film.
- **The three thought clouds** (meat 14.2, monster 33.3, monster plus meat 80.5) show narrated thoughts and read at phone size.
- **The drum design** is bold, and the last image (holed drum under the branch, 160-163.5) is the right closing picture.

### Main weaknesses
1. **The fox reaches the drum 17 s too early, so "from a distance" and both approaches are lost.** At 72.5-74.5, on "نظر الثعلب إلى الطبل من بعيد", he strolls out of hiding to x 470, nose about 30 units from the rim, with no fear, and does not react to the hit at 77.6 or 84.2. "اقترب ببطء، خطوة بعد خطوة" (91.1-96.2) then has only 108 units to cover and ends with his muzzle drawn over the drum skin (94.0 on). The second approach (109.6-113.4) ends the same way (x 600, nose at x 757, 100 units inside the rim).
2. **The protagonist is off screen for the revelation.** From 55.5 to 72.0 only a sliver of muzzle shows at the left border while the film explains the drum. The drum itself dissolves in through fading bushes at 52.7-53.7, before "رفع رأسه" (53.78) and 2.2 s before "فرأى" (54.94). "Lifted his head" appears to be played nose down with an open mouth (53.0-55.7; check the sign of `headUp` at 52.78).
3. **The branch does not read as the cause.** Its leaf cluster rests on the drum top for the whole film (no gap), the strike is a 52-unit dip, and seven identical hits at a 1.6 s metronome (61.2-71.4) each get a "BOOM!". At 80.5-91.0 the thought cloud covers the branch.
4. **The payoff is told by labels.** The hole starts growing at 115.0, 5 s before "صنع ثقبًا" (120.14), half hidden by his head. "Tried with his teeth" is a mouth flapping on a sine (115.0-123.6) with four "CHOMP!". "Looked inside" (123.6-126.2) is a level stare; the head never goes in. Emptiness is a "0 FOOD INSIDE" counter (126.4), "EMPTY!" three times (130.5 lettering, 130.5 burst cropped to "EMP" at the right border, 137.1 bubble) and "WHAT?!" (132.9). From 134.0 to 139.1 the fox smiles, which is the wrong feeling for "قال بدهشة".
5. **Hiding does not hide.** At 38.3-52.8 his whole face sticks out on the danger side of the trunk and his rump on the other. "وانتظر قليلًا" is a 4 s freeze. "لم ير أي حيوان" and "أراد أن يهرب" are instant mirror flips (45.3, 47.0, 50.2) with sweat drops.
6. **The touch is invisible and its sound has no cause.** The raised paw (96.4-98.3) cannot reach past his own snout, so it is hidden behind head and rim; "TAP" says it instead. The "BOOM!" at 98.4 comes 2 s after the touch started with neither the paw nor the branch seen striking. He then stands frozen for 3 s (98.4-101.3). The jump has no crouch and no landing, and travels only 100 units.
7. **No eye lines, blinks or turns.** Pupils point forward for the whole film; no blinks; six instant flips (27.3, 28.7, 45.3-48.5, 50.2, 52.8, 157.7); fear is one constant 3 px sine shake (26.1-30.0, 43.0-52.8).
8. **First fright.** He keeps walking for 1.3 s after the first boom (24.8) and reacts at 26.1. The yellow burst "BOOM!" sits on the hide-tree trunk in mid frame, so the sound seems to come from that tree; the second "BOOM!" is cropped by the right border (24.7-26.7, 43.5-46.2).
9. **The moral is three bubbles on a pose that reads as rearing.** `sit` tips the body back with the front legs in the air (139.5-157.0). "I WAS SCARED!", a muted-speaker icon and a grey ball with a cross replace acting for 14 s. The "ON HE GOES..." wipe (156.9-158.5) covers the last word of the moral ("مهمًّا", to 157.29) and the getting up, although no time passes. The fox is out of frame by 160.2 with "يبحث عن طعام" (160.28-161.54) still to come.
10. **About 45 pieces of lettering, arrows, counters and icons in 163 s** (list in item 12), most naming what the fox should act. The frame shake on every hit also shakes the subtitle panel.
11. **Opening.** The fox's tail and back are cut by the left border until 7.5. Hunger is a "GRRR..." label on his belly (8.5-12.6) and "NOTHING!" (10.8-13.5).
