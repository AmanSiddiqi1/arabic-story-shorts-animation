# AN-085 The City of Labtayt: creative plan

Film length 174.36 s (MP3 171.36 s + 3.0 s end hold). 14 scenes, 139 timed actions. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json` (exact camera keys, actions, lettering, effects, subtitles).

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Spine.** A sealed tower (9.66) gathers one lock from each king until there are 24 (21.82). Nobody knows what is inside (25.24). A new king from another family (37.04) decides to open it (46.54), brushes off his elders (51.78 to 63.7) and their gold (69.82), and strips the locks (72.66 to 74.76). Inside: stone Arab riders (77.98) and a written warning (90.02) that an army like them will come and the land will fall (99.22). The door is already open (104.86). The same year the army of Tariq ibn Ziyad arrives (110.74), the crown falls (116.52), and the treasure hall is found (120.12): crowns, books, instruments, the mirror (139.86), the table (145.58). The treasure goes to the Caliph al-Walid (154.88). The tower is remembered for a warning heard too late (165.16 to 171.08).
- **Conflict.** Tradition and counsel against one man's impatience. The door is the antagonist: everything is staged around it.
- **Surprises.** (1) The room holds statues, not gold. (2) The statues are a picture of the future: the army later enters in the same formation and poses. (3) The gold was in the city all along and ends up leaving it.
- **Emotional arc.** Ritual calm, curiosity, stubborn pride, cold dread, awe, then rue. Light humour only from bystanders (guard, nobles, a child).
- **Ending and moral.** The former king sits bare-headed on the tower step under 24 empty slots, cups his ear too late; TOO LATE. then THE END over the dusk tower. Moral: a warning kept for generations deserves a hearing, and some doors do not close again.

## 3. Character acting
- **The new king** (person, costume `caliph` recoloured green, pointed crown, age 0.15). Fast walk, chin up, eyes always on the door. Decision at 46.54: `PERSON_HANDS.chin`, `lid 0.35`, `brow 0.5`, slow half smile. Refusals are done with shut eyes (`lid 1`) and a turned head: he literally will not look. First backward step at 84.06, tremble on the hands only at 90.02, look to lens and gulp at 104.86 (comedy beat structure: hold, realise, look, gulp). Ending uses the shame pattern: look up, eyes break away, head drops; `cupEar` at 166.62 with a falling arm.
- **Earlier kings** (same figure, five palettes and ages, one shared crown shape). The same lock-hanging pose is repeated so it reads as ritual. As memories they are pale (`dream wash`, alpha 0.55), never in step (`stagger`), and their signature is the look-away at the door line (`gazePlan`, `PERSON_HANDS.shield`), set up in S3 and paid off in S5 and S14.
- **Vizier and noble.** Distinct: the vizier argues (sleeve catch, `pointUp`, `talk`), the noble worries (`clasp`, head shake, gives up his turban pin). Both kneel (`kneel`), sag when refused (`sq 0.95`), hide their eyes as the locks fall (`cheeks`, `mouthCover`), and tremble three different ways at 100.86.
- **Guard.** The audience's stand-in: counts locks on his fingers and loses count, one look to camera at 21.82, bows aside, tries to shut the door at 101.72.
- **Riders.** As stone: total stillness, `noBlink`, no gaze change; only dust moves. Alive: horses with real leg phase (`walk`, `footfalls`, `dustBurst`), camels slower, spears bobbing, a `bumpChain` halt on Tariq's raised hand.
- **Tariq.** Upright, unhurried, a walking horse, one glance up at the tower as he enters. In the hall he is curious (eye through the flask, a small wave at the mirror city) but never clownish; hand on chest at the table.
- Everyone has a gaze target and blinks (`LIFE.t = t`), pose changes use `anticipate` and `settleTo`, listeners watch speakers.

## 4. Camera and composition
- World 1000 x 1230, ground y 900; faces and action stay above y 900. The lock grid (x 368..632, y 500..800) is never covered.
- Establishing crane in S1; static-to-push in the montage; `twoShot` for the argument with one widening to include the pale kings; push-ins on the decision (46.54), the reading (88.5) and the final ear gesture (166.2).
- S7 is shot from inside the tower so the reveal is made by a moving edge of light, followed by a slow pan and a rise along the weapons.
- S8 eases out as the thought cloud opens so the living riders in the cloud sit directly above their statues.
- S10 tracks with the riders; S11 is one long pan with six stops; S12 a push into the mirror and a whip to the table; S14 pulls out for the last wide.
- **Screen direction is meaning:** kings enter from the left; statues face left; the army rides right to left; the treasure leaves left to right.
- One `later` card (35.88, YEARS LATER...) hides the cast and prop change.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **The counter as a character:** 1 to 24 in S2, 24 to 0 in S6, and 24 empty slots at the end.
- **Suspense by stillness:** two glints in the tower slit (25.24), the hesitating hand (31.86), the unblinking statues, the one gleam of stone eyes (96.26).
- **The match:** one pose table for statues, prophecy cloud and the real army; the crown falls in the cloud (99.22) and then for real (116.52) with the same bounce.
- **Humour by acting:** the guard's lost count, the noble's turban pin, the lid dropping shut by itself, the sneeze in the books, waving at the mirror city.
- **Legend framing:** small tags (THE LEGEND SAYS..., SO THE TALE SAYS, IT IS SAID, GOLD?) match the script's own caution ("the legend says", "it was said").
- **Emotion:** the earlier kings return above the door, sad rather than angry; children pass the story on.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus
- Start from `Library/template`; build `timing.json` from `planning/inputs/timeline.json`; write `chunks_ar.txt` / `chunks_en.txt` from the JSON `subtitles` and verify by program.
- One `lockCount(t)` function (keys in the JSON notes) drives the door, the counter and the clicks. Falling locks: `fallY` + `rebound` from `lockSlot(i)` to heap slots.
- One `RIDERS` table (mount, x, y, s, weapon, arm angles, palette) used in S7, S8 (cloud at scale about 0.3) and S10.
- S7: draw statues, then the darkness layer with the wedge cut out; wedge far edge x 260 (74.76), 520 (78.0), 760 (79.4), 980 (80.1).
- Raised weapons use arm angles `aR` (about [2.45, 2.85]), not hand targets.
- Panel-only shake for lock snaps, the stamp and the crown bounce. `LIFE.t = t` for blinks; statues `noBlink`.
- Narration is nearly continuous: real pauses only at 6.6-6.9, 10.52-10.92, 22.36-22.64 and after 171.08. The other effects sit on word boundaries; keep each under 120 ms at gain 0.25 or less and remove any that masks speech. `SND` master 0.6.
- Lettering: headlines at least 44 px, labels at least 28 px (1280 x 720 scale), no en or em dashes, never over a face. Call `finish` last.
- After approval move the new assets into `Library/`, run `node Library/tests/regression.mjs` and rebuild the catalogue.
