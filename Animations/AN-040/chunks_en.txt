A cat and a crow were friends|who met each day beneath a large tree at the forest’s edge.|The crow flew off to find food|while the cat rested in the shade, then they sat together and talked.
One day, while they were beneath the tree,|a fierce leopard appeared among the trees.|It saw them and approached slowly, watching the cat.|The crow noticed the danger and quickly flew up to a high branch.
The cat could find no safe escape.|The leopard was closer to him than the tree trunk was,|and the cat could not climb before it arrived.|It looked up and called to its friend the crow for help.
The crow thought quickly.|In the distance it saw shepherds leading sheep,|and strong dogs guarding the flock.|It flew toward them, swooped close to the dogs, and flapped its wings before them.
The dogs tried to catch the crow,|but it flew a short distance away and returned.|Each time they approached, it flew ahead again.|In this way it drew the dogs away and made them follow.
The crow headed toward the tree where the cat waited.|It kept flying low so the dogs would not lose sight of it.|The dogs ran quickly after it,|unaware of its plan.
When the dogs reached the tree,|they saw the leopard standing near the cat.|They instantly forgot the crow and rushed at the leopard, barking fiercely.|Frightened by the pack, the leopard fled into the trees and vanished.
Once the danger had passed, the cat emerged|He looked at the crow and thanked him for not leaving him alone.|The friends returned to the tree,|while the dogs went back to the shepherds and the leopard stayed away.
