# AN-040 The Cat and the Crow: creative plan

Film length 164.10 s of narration + 3.0 s THE END = 167.1 s. 16 scenes, 173 timed actions (all in `creative_plan.json`, which is the authority for times). Every time below is seconds on the final MP3.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A leopard corners a cat under the tree where he meets his friend the crow every day. The crow cannot fight a leopard, so it fetches someone who can: it teases a pack of sheepdogs into chasing it all the way home.

**Conflict.** Strength against wit, and a test of friendship: at 36.82 the crow flies to safety and for twenty seconds it looks as if the cat has been abandoned.

**Surprises.** (1) The crow seems to desert the cat. (2) Its plan is never explained; the audience works it out at 126.84 a moment before the dogs do. (3) The fierce leopard's legs spin on the spot before it bolts.

**Emotional arc.** Cosy (0 to 24) > dread (24 to 59) > mischief and speed (60 to 122) > release and triumph (123 to 144) > tenderness and rest (144 to 167).

**Ending and moral.** The last picture repeats the first (same framing, same poses, later light). A true friend does not leave you alone in danger, and wit can do what strength cannot. No moral text on screen; the mirrored picture says it.

## 3. Character acting

- **Cat.** Soft and slow when safe: half lids, tail curl, breathing (`breathe`), yawns. In danger everything reverses: `arch 1`, `puff 1`, ears flat, eyes wide, `tremble`. Ears always react before the eyes (36.1 wake-up). The appeal at 54.92 to 58.54 is head up along the trunk (`headRot -0.6`, `lookAt` the crow), wet eyes, one raised paw. Coming out (144.66) is in stages with a hold after each. The thank-you copies the opening bump exactly but lasts twice as long.
- **Crow.** Quick, upright, cheeky. Thinking = saccades (`gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`) and foot taps; decision = a dead stop, then a lean (`tip 0.12`). Every take-off has `anticipate` and a stretch, every landing `landSquash`; the branch answers with `swing`. With the dogs it performs: chest out, tail shake, looks back to check they follow (`headRot`). One lens look only, at 121.22. After the thanks it is embarrassed: gaze away, preening.
- **Leopard.** Economy of movement. Eyes first (`peekEyes`), a silent snarl, a stalk with a hold on every paw (stepped `LX` keys into `walk`), tail tip twitching on the holds. It never hurries until 140.02. Fear at 136.74 flips every field: `ears -1`, `tailTuck 1`, `brow -0.6`, `sq 0.88`, `tremble`; then the run-in-place wind-up.
- **Dogs.** Three personalities in every shot: dog1 bold and first, dog2 copies 3 frames late, dog3 slow and puzzled (first question mark at 119.06, the head tilt at the crow at 158.76). Use `stagger(i)`; never three identical poses. Bark = jaw + a 6 unit forward jolt.
- **Listeners react.** Cat asleep during the stalk (only an ear turns); crow shifting foot to foot at the cat's call; sheep chewing at the empty guard post; the cat's eyes in the bush switching from leopard to dogs at 128.70.
- **Secondary motion.** Tails, ears and tongues lag 2 frames; branch dip; grass flattened under paws; feathers left behind; treetop shakes that announce the leopard (24.4) and see it off (141.56).

## 4. Camera and composition

- Open close on the pair, crane out to the tree (5.68 to 7.6): the same wide returns at 157.0 for the ending.
- S3 whip to the tree line in the pause; S4 holds the wide for the stalk so the distance closing is visible, then follows the crow up and **tilts down** to leave the cat small (39.16).
- S5 is a flat diagram shot: the ground line is the ruler. Bars go under the ground line, the cloud above the cat.
- S6 vertical two-shot on the eye line (`twoShot`, margin 120): cat bottom right, crow top left, the call travelling on the diagonal.
- S7 whip pan as the crow's point of view; the crow stays in a round inset (planned `inset circle`).
- S9/S10 medium tracking, then the **pull-out at 94.64** that shows the hop trail as one line: the plan made visible without words.
- S11/S12 side tracking on `chaseRoad`; S12 closer with a 2 unit panel-only shake.
- S13 starts wide with the right side empty (room for the arrival), pushes to dog1 and leopard for the look (126.84), pulls back for the charge.
- S15 slow push on two faces; S16 pull-out to the opening frame.
- Keep all faces and key action inside the picture panel (x 54..1026, y 54..1250); lettering never over a face.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Suspense:** dramatic irony (the audience sees the eyes at 24.74, the cat does not wake until 36.1); the stalk in real steps; the cross-cut inset at 105.14 so the chase has a clock.
- **Apparent betrayal:** the tilt down at 39.16 and the crow half opening its wings and folding them again at 57.04.
- **Humour:** SNAP! on air, the head bump of dog2 and dog3, the feather sneeze, the pile-up at 125.28, question marks, the crow bubbles popping over the dogs (130.40), the leopard's spinning legs.
- **Surprise:** the plan is kept secret; the reveal is three pairs of eyes sliding down from the branch to the leopard.
- **Emotion:** setup and payoff of the head bump (1.84 and 151.40); the memory cloud at 153.56 that answers "he did not leave him alone"; the closing picture equal to the opening.
- **Lettering diet:** sound words only on real actions; labels pop on their word and leave within 2 s.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus

- Start from `Library/template/`; word cues with `w(p, n)`; all positions from `kfn` functions fed to `walk()` and `footfalls()` (leopard `LX`, dogs `DX`, cat `CX`). Tables of positions over time are in `implementation_notes` in the JSON.
- forestEdge key points: forest x 0..480, occluder trunks x 330 and 470, big trunk x 760, perch (950, 392), cat spot (1080, 900), crow spot (1190, 900), bush x 1300, dogs stop at 1470/1540/1590. Design values: measure on the baked set.
- Draw order: set, shade, leopard, cat, bush, dogs, crow, foreground trunks, lettering, `finish`.
- `LIFE.t = t` for blinks; `noBlink` on the freezes (34.3, 62.7, 126.8 to 127.4).
- Chase: one distance function `D(t)` drives the parallax and every gait phase, so easing off at 108.76 slows everything together.
- The inset draws a second scene through its own camera inside a circular clip in panel space.
- Sound: only the twelve effect times listed in the JSON (all in pauses, `master 0.6`); barks and caws are lettering only.
- Subtitles: chunk marks are in the JSON `subtitles` (4 chunks per paragraph, verbatim).
