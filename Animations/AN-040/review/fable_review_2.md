## Remaining notes (polish, not required for release)

### 1. 149.2 to 154.3: the crow stands in front of two dark trees during the thank-you
**What.** The crow lands to the left of the cat exactly where two dark green round trees sit on the horizon (`extra/m4.jpg` second frame, `extra/m5.jpg` third and fourth). A black bird on dark green: its outline, the look away at 152.5 and the beak touch at 151.4 are muddy. The cat's white head is clean.
**Why.** This is the payoff of the opening gesture, and the crow is half of it.
**Fix (either).** Widen the `an40HillTrees` clearing on the left to about x 880 (it currently starts at 990), or land the crow about 90 units further right with the cat stopping 90 further right as well (cat x about 1225 at 147.9), so both heads are against sky. No timing change.

### 2. 158.2 to 158.9: the passing dogs cover the friends
**What.** Dogs 1 and 2 pass directly in front of the cat and crow; at 158.5 dog 1's head hides the crow completely and dog 2's back hides the cat's body (`extra/m4.jpg` third frame).
**Why.** The sentence is about both groups going their own way. For 0.7 s one group is erased.
**Fix.** Drop the dogs' ground line a further 45 to 60 units (feet at about y 1045) so their backs pass below the cat's chest, or keep the line and open a 0.25 s gap between dog 2 and dog 1 at the friends' x so the friends are seen between them. Dog 3's slow-down at 158.76 already works.

### 3. 135.4 to 136.8: the three barks pile into one word
**What.** The three `WOOF!` stamps overlap into `WOO..WOOF!` / `WOO.WOO.WOOF!` (`extra/m5.jpg` second frame).
**Fix.** Offset each by about 110 px in x and 40 px in y (a rising diagonal above the dogs' heads), or scale 0.75 with 0.12 s stagger, each one fully legible.

### 4. 124.7 to 140.2: the climax is safe but small
