## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A miller dreams of gold buried on his donkey's path and tells his wife. She betrays the secret to a neighbour, who kills her for the whole hoard and runs. At dawn the donkey refuses to walk past the open pit, and the miller, who cannot see why, kills it in a rage.

**Conflict.** Three wants that never meet: the miller wants his wife, the wife wants the neighbour, the neighbour wants nothing, until there is gold. Then greed against greed.

**Emotional arc.** Warm and lightly comic (0 to 54 s: hearts, a dream, a broken SHH!), then night and appetite (54 to 85 s), then two killings told in silence and shadow (85 to 145 s), then slow grief in morning light (145 s to the end).

**Surprises.** The neighbour who shut his door on her heart opens it for gold (51.38). His shadow acts before he does (94.12). The donkey is the only one who sees the truth (124.58).

**Ending and moral.** The miller sits between the pit and the donkey with one coin; the neighbour disappears down the road. A secret told to the wrong person costs everything, and anger punishes the innocent.

## 3. Character acting

- **Miller** (trusting, physical): gaze always on his wife in S2 and S4, with a follow-through look after she leaves (2.2). Joy uses `anticipate` + `hop` + `landSquash` (35.94). Secrecy is `gazePlan` saccades then `PERSON_HANDS.hush`. In S13 to S15 his gaze visits hooves, harness, stone, never forward. Rage: `flush`, `tremble` fists, `lid` 0.2 with no blink. Grief: slow gaze travel, `kneel` with `impactSquash`, `wet`, one tear, long blink, no big gestures.
- **Wife** (mask on / mask off): bored `lid` 0.35 toward her husband, `tip` 0.1 toward the neighbour. The one-sided smile at 42.36 exists only while the miller's back is turned. In S8 she is brisk and cold: no blink on 71.84 to 73.7 when she crosses out her husband.
- **Neighbour** (reserve, then appetite): leans away from her heart (`tip` -0.08, `PERSON_HANDS.shield`) at 18.08 and 50.46; the lean reverses with `coinEyes` at 51.38. In S9 the face he shows her (smile) and the face he shows us (flat mouth, `brow` changes) alternate. Before the killing the man stays almost still and the shadow acts. After it: looks away while lowering her, a swallow, then a 2-frame gaze switch to the gold.
- **Donkey** (the innocent witness): S1 loose ears swinging a beat late, soft blink. S13: ears turn forward first (120.86), then brace (`tip` -0.1), `earL/earR` -1, `eyes: 'wide'`, `tremble`, `snort`. It pleads with `headTurn` to the miller and back to the pit (133.60), `wet`. Refusal: `bray`, then `lie` 0.4. End: `lie` 1, `lid` 1, `sag` 1.
- **Secondary motion:** scarf tail and sack lag by 2 to 3 frames; lamp and door use `swing`; coins arc and roll with rotation from distance; groups react with `stagger` (the freeze at 59.74, the nod at 52.0).

## 4. Camera and composition

- The film opens and closes on the same wide of the mill so the ring is understood and the ending rhymes with the start.
- Dialogue and plotting in `twoShot` (S4, S8, S10, S14, S15); decisions get push-ins (the neighbour 76.94, the miller's face 139.66, the donkey's eye 124.58).
- The pit is always at the front right of the ring (world 650, 962). The miller is always staged left of the donkey and looking left or at the animal until 155.98. In S14 the pit sits just outside the right frame edge.
- Violence is framed away: tilt up to the wall for the shovel shadow (93.5), black panel (95.9 to 97.46), whip to the wall for the knife (142.32).
- Light is the storyteller in the last third: `dark` 0.55, then a shaft from the window sweeps left to right (154.34 to 154.74) and the camera pans with it.
- Faces and key action stay between y 300 and 980 of the world; balloons and clouds sit above heads; lettering never covers a face.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour (first third only):** the bouncing heart, the neighbour sweeping and backing into his own doorway, the SHH! that falls off her like a dropped sign.
- **Suspense:** her heel one step from the rim (87.54); the shadow lifting the shovel while the man is still (94.12); the half step toward the shovel that is never finished (105.30).
- **Dramatic irony:** the audience sees the pit, the mound and the coin glint in the gloom while the miller harnesses the donkey beside them (113 to 130 s).
- **Emotion:** the donkey's wet eye turned back to its master (133.60); the knife glint reflected in that eye (141.50); belief and truth side by side (146.30 and 148.38); three crossed-out icons (167.14).
- **Silence:** no effects under the killing, the beating or the stabbing. Eight quiet effects in the whole film, all in listed pauses.
