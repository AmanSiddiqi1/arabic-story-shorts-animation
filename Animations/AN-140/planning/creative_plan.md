# AN-140 The Sons of Yahya bin Khalid and Said bin Salim: creative plan

Film: 0 to 174.9 s (MP3 171.93 s, last word ends 171.62 s, THE END held 3.0 s). 17 scenes, 151 actions, 5 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3. The machine-readable storyboard is `creative_plan.json`; this file explains the intent.

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A man drowning in debt asks two great brothers for help, gets only a prayer and leaves crushed. The same day mules loaded with money arrive at his door: the brothers had gone quietly to the caliph.

**Conflict.** Said against his debts, and against his own fear that the powerful will humiliate him. The film makes that fear visible (thought cloud, S5), seems to confirm it (empty hands, S8), then overturns it.

**Spine (cue word times).**
- Setup 0.28: Said, a respectable man of Baghdad.
- Inciting 6.40: the bills pile up. 10.12: creditors at the door every day.
- 21.84 to 40.94: Abdullah ibn Malik's advice: only the Barmakids.
- Suspense 45.70: Said's fear of haughty words. 55.58: "bear what you hear". 62.48: he decides.
- 63.14: the visit. Emotion 73.70: the prayer. **Turn 80.08: no money.**
- Low point 99.92: he sits worrying.
- **Surprise 108.48:** the servant bursts in. 126.72: the trembling hand.
- Reveal 134.46 / 138.80 / 146.56 / 147.36: four amounts.
- Climax 155.06: 3,300,000 dirhams.
- Moral 165.04: they worked in secret. Resolution 170.28: a new life.

**Emotional arc.** Pride, burden, shame, fear, a flicker of hope, disappointment, gloom, shock, tears of joy, understanding, gratitude.

**Surprises.** (1) The prayer ends and the hands come down empty. (2) The door bangs open. (3) The amounts keep coming: after the caliph's two gifts, each brother adds a million.

**Ending.** Said waves from his open, lit doorway under a new morning; the door closes gently. No gloating over the creditors: they are paid and bow.

**Moral.** True generosity works in silence. Judge people by what they do, not by what they say to your face.

## 3. Character acting

- **Said** carries the film with his eyes. Pride (hands on hips, gaze at his house) becomes burden (sq 0.92, tip back under the bills), then hiding (pupils only in the shutter slit: suspense by stillness). With Abdullah he pleads with `palmsUp` and worried brow (-0.6). The hesitation at 41.70 is a body beat: `sit` 1 to 0.5 to 1. In S8 hope is a small smile and raised hands; the fall is three saccades (Fadl's hands, Jaafar's hands, his own palm) and fingers that curl shut. Joy at the end has `wet` eyes, hands to cheeks, then `anticipate` + `hop` + `landSquash`.
- **Abdullah** is the calm centre: slow gestures, half smiles, `moustache` hand hiding amusement (91.38, 121.54), one wink at 96.30. He never looks surprised by the good news.
- **Al-Fadl and Jaafar** are dignified and nearly still. Fadl leads, Jaafar follows 4 frames later (`stagger`). Their only tell in S8 is one 0.4 s glance at 72.76. Their first smiles appear inside the letter (138.80). The praying pose is one shared constant used in S8, mocked by Said in S10 and replayed in the S17 cloud (setup and payoff).
- **Creditors** differ: fat and angry (brow 0.8), sly (smirk), old and impatient (stick). Never in unison. At 123.96 all three gape at the chests; when paid, the angry brow flips.
- **Servant**: run, `lurch` skid, hands on knees with fast `breathe`, then mimes the news.
- **Mules**: ear flicks, head turns, tail swishes on different seeds; each sinks when loaded and rises when unloaded.
- Secondary motion: turban slip under the bills and at the heap, cap trailing on the servant, bills fluttering, door leaves shaking on knocks.

## 4. Camera and composition

- Establishing wides open each location (S1, S7); two-shots (`twoShot`) for the conversations; push-ins on decisions and blows (16.5 shutter, 62.3 decision, 78.0 empty palm, 125.1 trembling hands).
- Height tells status: in the Barmakid hall the brothers sit 60 units above Said; in his fear cloud they are 3 times his size; at the end the heap towers over him, but now height is a gift.
- Balloons and clouds sit in the top third; faces stay at y 600 to 760; nothing important below y 900.
- The letter flashback is framed by `letterFrame` rollers and entered and left by match cuts on the open letter in Said's hands.
- One `later` card (SOON...) at 105.3 over "لم يمر وقت طويل". Final pull-out and upward tilt at 166.56 reveals the heap bottom to top.
- Amount tags live at the left edge in S14 and S15 and become a panel-space column at top left in S16.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Humour:** bills stacking until only the eyes show; DAY 1, 2, 3 with the same knocking; the HMPH! giants; Said mocking the prayer; the turban that nearly falls as he looks up the heap.
- **Suspense:** the eyes in the shutter slit; the long stillness after the hands come down empty; the trembling seal.
- **Surprise:** BANG! of the door in the gloomiest moment; the news strip filling picture by picture; the third and fourth million.
- **Emotion:** the fading smile at 82.24; wet eyes over the letter; the quiet thanks at 170.28.
- **Dramatic irony on second viewing:** the glance at 72.76 and the brothers' heads following him out at 84.96.

## 8. Warnings and open questions

- The narration has only four gaps of 0.3 s or more, hence five effects.
- The English translation is a summary: it omits clauses in P4, P5, P6 (the whole last Arabic sentence) and P8 ("and to begin a new life"). Both languages are kept verbatim; see the `SCRIPT:` entries in `owner_questions`.
- Additions beyond the script for the owner to confirm (defaults in the JSON): creditors shown being paid; the brothers' glance and the finger to the lips; all invented English lettering; the caliph drawn as a figure.
- Many human characters share one front-facing figure: colour coding must stay constant, and door entrances rely on the door leaf and `dir` flips.
