# AN-140 The Sons of Yahya bin Khalid and Said bin Salim: Fable review, round 1

Reviewed: `renders/AN-140_v1.mp4` (174.9 s) through all 30 strips of `review/v1_pack/strips/` plus 32 full-size frames in `review/v1_pack/extra/` (montages `m1.jpg` to `m8.jpg`).

Summary: the story is told in order, complete and faithful, with no broken frames. Subtitles (Arabic and the re-translated English) are correct, in sync and readable throughout; THE END holds 2.7 s. Several things work well and must be kept: the bills piling up to Said's eyes (6.4 to 9.2), DAY 1/2/3 (12.7 to 14.2), Said's eyes in the window (17 to 21), Said mocking the prayer (90.3 to 91.4), the letter becoming the picture (128.2 to 128.7), the tags arriving one by one (134.5 to 147.5), and the door closing at the end. No BLOCKER.

It is REVISE because the two moments the whole film is built on are staged so that the audience cannot see them: the turn (the brothers' hands come down empty, note 1) happens off screen, and during the climax and moral Said is hidden behind his own letter for 17 seconds (note 2). In addition the adviser Abdullah acts behind a pile of coins for about 70 s of screen time (note 3). These are staging and camera fixes, not new drawings.

Do not change narration, subtitle text or timing. All times are seconds on the final MP3; word times from `planning/inputs/timeline.md`.

## Notes

### 1. 78.0 to 83.9, MAJOR: the turn is off screen
What is wrong: at 78.2 the camera pushes in on Said alone. The brothers leave the frame (only a sliver of al-Fadl's turban stays at the right edge), so their hands coming down empty on "لكنهما" (78.06) is never seen. Said keeps both fists raised until about 81, so nothing visibly changes for 3 s. The NO MONEY label (81.0) points its arrow at empty blue wall beside Said's elbow. The three planned looks (Fadl's hands, Jaafar's hands, his own palm) cannot read because there is nothing in frame to look at.
Why it matters: this is the film's turn and the set-up for the ending. If the viewer does not see two pairs of empty hands, the later surprise has nothing to overturn.
Fix:
- Keep the three-shot of 72 to 78 (Said left, brothers right on the dais) until 81.6. Do not push in at 78.2.
- 78.06 "لكنهما": the rays cut off; al-Fadl lowers his hands to his knees, Jaafar 4 frames later; palms open and empty, facing up for a beat, then resting. Said's hands stay up 0.4 s longer than theirs, so he is the last one still praying (small comic beat), then come down.
- 79.68 "لم": Said's `gaze` snaps to al-Fadl's hands; 80.08 "يسلماه": to Jaafar's hands; 80.98 "مالًا": down to his own open palm (`palmsUp` on one hand, held out toward the dais). NO MONEY pops at 80.98 with its arrow pointing at that empty palm (or use two short arrows at the brothers' hands). Keep the label clear of all faces.
- 81.64 "وهو": now the push-in to Said alone (the existing close framing is good). 82.24 "جالس": fingers curl shut, the smile fades, `lid` drops, slight `sq` sag. Hold still to 83.9.
- The brothers stay calm and kindly, not cold: a small nod is enough. Keep the 72.76 glance.

### 2. 149.6 to 166.5, MAJOR: Said is hidden behind the letter through the climax and the moral
What is wrong: after the letter sequence Said holds the open letter flat in front of his body. It covers him from chin to feet and, with its base on the ground, reads as a lectern or a stone pillar (frames 150.5, 153.7, 161.0, 164.5). Only his face shows for 17 s: no hands to cheeks, no visible `wet` eyes, no body reaction as the counter climbs to 3,300,000, and nothing but a face under a cloud when he "understands". From 159.7 the thought cloud also sits on his turban.
Why it matters: the plan's emotional payoff (tears of joy, understanding, gratitude) is carried by Said's body, and it is exactly here that he has none.
Fix:
- From 149.64 Said holds the letter low in ONE hand at his side or chest height at about half the current size (as he already does at 155.0 to 157.5, which reads well). Never let the sheet cover his torso again.
- 149.64 "صار": `wet` eyes, free hand to his cheek. 153.20 to 155.06 while the counter rolls: his gaze follows the burst, body rises on each step (1,000,000 / 2,000,000 / 3,000,000), and on "وثلاثمئة" 155.06 a small jump with `landSquash`, turban slip.
- 158.52 "فهم": he looks down at the letter, then slowly up to the left (toward the palace direction), hand to chest. Raise the cloud by about 60 px or shift it left so its lower edge clears his turban.
- 163.78 "بل": when the cloud brothers spring up, Said's brows lift and a smile opens; 165.04 "الخفاء": he puts his own finger to his lips, echoing 148.46 (pays off the S15 gesture).

### 3. 21.8 to 62.5, 86.6 to 105.3 and 107 to 122, MAJOR: Abdullah acts behind his coins
What is wrong: Abdullah sits behind the desk with a coin stack directly in front of his mouth and chest (frames 33.0, 48.7, 91.0, 96.3, 116.5). Planned gestures are invisible or absent: the "no one" finger at 33.70, the point at Said 39.30 and at the door 40.46, patting the stool 92.02, the "who knows" finger and wink 96.30, the moustache hand hiding a smile at 91.38 and 121.54. As a result 31.7 to 36.9 and 92.2 to 97.9 are both about 5.5 s in which nothing on screen changes. He only becomes a character when he walks round the desk at 52.5.
Why it matters: Abdullah is the one who knows how this ends. His calm amusement is the film's dramatic irony and half of its humour, and it is currently lost.
Fix:
- Move the tall coin stack to the far (right) end of the desk and keep it below his chin, or seat him at the near corner so head, chest and both arms are clear of the desk top.
- Make each planned gesture a real arm pose above desk height, on its word: 33.70 "أحدًا" raised index finger with a slow shake; 37.32 / 37.84 glance up at his balloon; 39.30 "وإن" point at Said; 40.46 "يطلب" arm swings to point at the door.
- 92.02 "دعاه": he leans out and pats the stool twice (or beckons, palm down). 94.72 "بقية اليوم": he points up at the window while the sun slides. 96.30 "لعل": raised finger, then one clear wink with a half smile, held 0.5 s. During 86.7 to 92 give him the moustache hand at 91.38.
- 121.54 "وجعفر": when the medallions appear he strokes his moustache and smiles, unsurprised, while Said and the servant gape.

### 4. 21.6 to 62.5 and 86.6 to 122.4, MAJOR: conversation scenes are framed too wide
What is wrong: in the Abdullah room the figures are about 170 px tall in a 1196 px panel, parked on the middle line, and the bottom 40 percent is empty chequered floor (frames 33.0, 56.2, 91.0, 96.3). On a phone the faces, which carry these scenes, are tiny. The slight pushes at 31.2 and 99 are not enough.
Why it matters: about 75 s of the film is two people talking in this room; at this size the acting from notes 3 and 9 will not read even once it is fixed.
Fix: keep one wide establishing shot on each entrance (21.8 to 24.5, 86.6 to 88.0, 107.2 to 109.5), then cut or ease to a `twoShot` at roughly 1.5x to 1.7x so the two heads sit at y 600 to 760 and the floor line is in the lower quarter. Use single close shots for 41.7 to 45.5 (Said hesitates), 62.3 (jaw set) and 99.9 to 101.5 (chin on fist). Balloons and clouds stay in the top third, so check they still fit. The Barmakid hall three-shot at 71.5 to 78 is the right size and can be the model.

### 5. 45.7 to 51.7, MAJOR: the fear cloud's tiny Said is unreadable
What is wrong: inside the cloud the two giant brothers read well, but the small Said between them is about 25 px tall at 1080 wide (frame 48.7), a green smudge at their belts. The brothers look straight out at the viewer, not down at him, and their folded arms at 49.96 hide him further.
Why it matters: this picture is the fear that the rest of the film answers. The joke and the dread both depend on seeing a very small man under two very large ones.
Fix: enlarge the cloud to fill the top 45 percent of the panel and place the small Said in front of and below the brothers, at least 110 px tall (the plan's own minimum), on clear floor, looking up with `palmsUp`. Brothers' `gaze` straight down at him, heads tipped (`tip`), lids half closed. HMPH! at 48.38 goes between their heads, not at the panel edge on top of the lamp. At 51.38 "شيئًا" the NOTHING label points at small Said's empty palm. Real Said below: `tremble` from 45.70 and a gulp.

### 6. 155.6 to 159.5, MAJOR: paying the creditors is a tangle
What is wrong: the three creditors cross in front of Said in quick succession. At 157.5 the fat creditor covers Said completely; at 158.0 to 158.5 two creditors are drawn on top of each other and tipped over at Said's feet (frame 158.2), which reads as a collision or a glitch, not a bow. No coin or purse is clearly seen changing hands.
Why it matters: this is the release of the pressure set up at 10 to 21. The plan wanted them paid and bowing, with the angry brow flipping; that small, kind beat is illegible.
Fix: one clear exchange per creditor, each standing to the LEFT of Said and never in front of him, spaced so no two bodies overlap: fat 156.28 "ألف", sly 156.9, old 157.5. Each: Said's hand out with a purse (`handOver`), the creditor takes it, his bill gets the struck state, brow flips from 0.8 to raised, one bow of the head (not a whole-body tip past about 25 degrees), then he walks off left. All three gone by 159.3 so the cloud at 159.66 opens over a calm street. If three do not fit, pay the fat one clearly and let the other two receive theirs in the background.

### 7. 153.0 to 157.2, MINOR: the total burst hides the fourth tag
What is wrong: the counter starburst sits on the tag column and covers the fourth tag (Jaafar's 1,000,000) completely and clips the third (frames 153.7, 156.2). The sum on screen shows three amounts adding to 3,300,000.
Why it matters: the four amounts are the reveal; the viewer should be able to check the addition.
Fix: place the burst below the column with about 20 px gap (or to the right of it at x about 560, clear of Said's house door and faces), and draw a short rule under the fourth tag so it reads as a sum. Keep WOW! off the digits (at 155.2 it covers the last three zeros for two frames; offset it up and right).

### 8. 149.7 to 173.5, MINOR: the messenger is frozen in a wave
What is wrong: the navy messenger by the door holds the same raised hand from 149.7 until he leaves at about 173.5, some 24 s (visible in every frame of strips 150 to 172).
Why it matters: a held wave next to the hero during the climax looks like a stuck pose and pulls the eye.
Fix: hand down by 150.5; give him small living reactions instead: watches the counter (gaze up-left 153.2 to 155), a nod at 155.06, hands clasped in front afterwards, a bow to Said at 168.3, then he exits. Same for the servant on the left: he stands with fists on hips from 157.5 to 169; let him pat the mule or look up the heap at 166.56.

### 9. 107.8 to 117.0, MINOR: Said under-reacts to the door
What is wrong: BANG! lands at 108.5 and the servant skids in beside him, but Said stays seated, facing front, chin near his fist until about 116.7 when he stands. The plan says he rises in stages.
Why it matters: the bang in the gloomiest moment is the film's surprise; the hero should be the first thing to jump.
Fix: 108.48 "جاء": startle (pop up off the stool about 15 px, `sq` stretch, turban slip, eyes wide, head snaps toward the door). 110.22 to 111.0: leaning toward the servant. 114.28 "بغالًا": half risen (`sit` 0.5), mouth open. 116.98 "بالأموال": standing, hands to head. 119.76 "رسالة": one step toward the door. Also thin the dust rings left on the floor at 109 to 110.7; they read as stray ellipses.

### 10. 113.0 to 122.0, MINOR: the news balloon is small and half empty
What is wrong: the strip balloon is drawn at its full width from 113.0 but its right half is blank for 7 s; the mule silhouettes are about 45 px tall and the loads and the sealed scroll are specks (frames 116.5, 121.5). The "x 4" burst sits below the balloon, detached from the mules.
Why it matters: this strip is how the audience hears the good news; it should be as readable as the tags later.
Fix: after note 4's tighter framing give the balloon the full panel width and about 1.4x its height; let its frame grow rightward as each item lands instead of showing empty paper; mules at least 80 px with coloured (not black) loads that glint at 116.98; the scroll with its red seal at least 40 px at 119.76; put "x 4" inside the balloon above the mules.

### 11. 169.5 to 170.3, MINOR: the night passes as a flicker
What is wrong: the sky goes dusk mauve, flat orange, navy with moon and stars, then yellow morning within about 0.7 s (frames 169.5, 169.7, 170.0, 170.2). At 170.0 the extra frame shows a yellow moon on an orange sky. It reads as a strobe, and the buildings change from red to blue roofs at the same time.
Why it matters: "a new life" should feel like a calm sunrise; three full-panel colour changes in under a second are harsh on a phone.
Fix: start the `skyCycle` sweep in the gap after "توقع" (about 168.9) and let it run to about 170.6, eased, without the orange step and without the moon (dusk straight through a short deep blue to dawn yellow). Said's arms-up thanks stays on "حياة" 170.28.

### 12. 122.5 to 128.2, MINOR: arrival and the trembling hand
What is wrong: (a) at 122.5 to 123.7 Said and the servant run through the mule line and are half hidden by mule bodies; the planned gaping creditors are not in the shot. (b) At 126.0 to 128.2 the three men stand in a straight row facing the viewer; the tremble on the letter is hard to see and the servant and messenger look at the camera, not at the letter.
Why it matters: (a) is the first sight of the treasure and (b) is the last held breath before the reveal.
Fix: (a) run Said in front of the mules (draw him after them), with one beat at 123.38 where he looks back over his shoulder at the chests; add the three creditors at the far left, mouths open, if they fit. (b) Servant and messenger turn their `gaze` to the letter and lean in; visible `tremble` on Said's hands and the sheet (amplitude about 4 px) from 126.72 "بيد" to 128.2, lower lip tight, eyes moving along the first line.
