## Notes (polish only, most useful first)

### 1. 51.38 to 51.8: NOTHING label is on for about 0.3 s, then hangs over a bare wall
What is wrong: NOTHING fades in at the cloud's right edge at 51.4, still half transparent at 51.45, and the cloud pops off by 51.7, leaving the label and its arrow pointing at empty wall for one or two frames (strip 51.7, `m1.jpg`).
Why it matters: it is the punchline of the fear picture ("and give him nothing") and nobody can read it.
Fix: pop the label at full opacity on "يعطوه" 49.96 (it fits: the arms fold on the same word) or at the latest on "شيئًا" 51.38, pointing at small Said's open palm, and keep cloud and label together until 52.3, fading out as one while Abdullah starts to rise at 52.02. Never let the label outlive the cloud.

### 2. 156.3 to 158.4: the three payments are still rushed
What is wrong: the exchanges themselves read (purse, struck bill), but each leaver walks across the next creditor: at 156.95 the fat creditor passes in front of the sly one and the servant; at 157.25 the DIRHAMS box sits on the sly creditor's forehead during the camera push; at 157.55 the sly creditor covers the old one, who is half faded behind him; at 158.15 the old creditor's exit lean with the stick across his body reads as a stumble (`m7.jpg`, `m8.jpg`).
Why it matters: it is the release of the pressure from 10 to 21 s; it should feel calm and kind, not like a scramble.
Fix (any one is enough): (a) pay the fat creditor in the foreground as now and let the other two receive theirs half a step back, standing still, then all three bow and leave together to the left in single file from 158.0; or (b) keep three exchanges but send each leaver off BEHIND the waiting ones (draw him before them, y about 20 px higher) and cap the walk lean at about 8 degrees. Fade DIRHAMS out by 157.1, before the push-in starts.

### 3. 165.04 to 166.3: the finger to the lips does not read
What is wrong: on "الخفاء" Said winks (good) but his hand stays at chest height and then goes back behind his head (165.65, 166.1 in `m13.jpg`). The echo of Jaafar's gesture at 148.46 is lost, while cloud Jaafar is doing it at the same moment.
Fix: at 165.04 raise the free hand so the index finger sits upright on his mouth (hand drawn over the beard), hold with the wink to 166.3, then lower to the hip for the pull-out. Drop the hand-behind-head pose after 164.0; it has already been used from 150.4 to 155.

### 4. 166.0 to 166.5: cloud collides with Said's turban during the pull-out

### 5. 169.7 to 170.1: muddy mid cross-fade at dawn

Fix: fade the stars out first (169.4 to 169.7), then wipe the dawn in from the horizon upward (a rising yellow band, 169.6 to 170.5) instead of a whole-frame alpha blend; switch the street palette at the midpoint under the wipe rather than blending it.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- "Last one still praying": a group gesture ends and the hero holds it 0.4 s longer; a cheap, readable way to show hope outlasting the room (78.1 to 79.3).
- Balloon strip that grows rightward item by item on the spoken words, never showing empty paper (113 to 122).
- Cross-fade rule from note 5: change time of day with a horizon wipe or by fading the stars first, not by alpha-blending two full palettes.
- Label rule from note 1: a label attached to a cloud or balloon shares its lifetime and never outlives it.
- Queue rule from note 2: in a serial hand-over, leavers exit behind the waiting line.
