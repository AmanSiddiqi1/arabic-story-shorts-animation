## Series changes
- `src/sb_props.js` `sbGem`: new optional `o.line` and `o.tintB` (darker outline, second hard facet colour). Defaults unchanged.
- `src/sb_valley.js` `diamondValley`:
  - The floor plate is now 2500 x 830 (to y 1640), with large gem clusters (`SB_GEMCL`, `SB_VGEM`), bones, a dead tree, thorns, near boulders and a cool shadow side.
  - New `sbGemGlints` (live glints and halation on the clusters).
  - `sbFloorBands` covers the deeper floor.
- `src/sb_roc.js`:
  - `sbRoc` wing: leading-edge and shoulder lobes. This changes the roc's look everywhere it is drawn.
  - `sbRocEgg`: new optional `o.seat` (contact shadow, buried base). Default unchanged.
- `src/sb_island.js` `sbNestLogs('front')`: straw and three shed feathers baked in.
- SIND-06 has its own copies of these files and is not affected. If SIND-06 should get the new roc wing, egg seat or valley floor, copy them there deliberately.
