## Notes (most important first; all polish)

### 1. 28.7-34.5 s: Sinbad has no head in the lookout tree
- **What is wrong.** From the moment he reaches the fork until he climbs down, a dark green foliage ball of the crown is drawn over his head (stills 29.2, 31.0, 33.6). For almost 6 s the hero is a torso, arms and legs. This was also so in v1 and I missed it in round 1.
- **Why it matters.** "فرأى قبة بيضاء هائلة" is his discovery; the eyes, the blink and the point at the dome carry it, and none of it can be seen.
- **Fix.** Draw the front foliage ball before Sinbad (or shift that ball 90 world units up and left) so the head and turban are clear of leaves from 28.7 to 34.5. Then play the beat the plan asks for: eyes go right at 29.2 ("فرأى"), blink, head follows at 29.6, brows up and mouth 'o' at 30.2 ("قبة"), the arm points at 31.6 ("هائلة") under the A WHITE DOME? tag, and he goes still with only pupils moving at 33.2 ("الجزيرة الصامتة").

### 2. 134.2-135.6 s: the diamond glow bleaches Sinbad and the young trader
- **What is wrong.** When he holds out the stone a white-cyan glow about 300 px wide is drawn over the figures: Sinbad's skin, sash and tunic and the young trader's face go nearly white (stills 134.5, 135.2), while the other two traders keep their colours. It also was in v1.
- **Why it matters.** It is the owner's "no tint over the characters" point, at the moment of the gift ("أعطاهم بعض الألماس"), and it hides Sinbad's smile.
- **Fix.** Draw the halation behind the cast (before the figures), radius about 140 px centred on the stone, and put only a star glint and a thin white rim (`castFinish` with `rim: '#ffffff'`, light from the stone) on the two nearest figures. Skin and the tangerine cloth keep their own colours. The four faces can still "light up" through acting: brows up and two white eye glints on threes, staggered left to right from 134.2.

### 3. 83.0-92.4 and 108.2-116.4 s: the valley wall is still bare
- **What is wrong.** In the two long static valley shots the floor fills the lower quarter of the panel and the upper 60% is plain coloured bands with two ledges. These are the items of round 1 note 6c that were not done.
- **Why it matters.** The owner's "full scenes" point is weakest where the story stays longest (about 17 s in these two shots).
- **Fix (either is enough).** (a) Frame: move the camera down about 140 world units in both shots so the floor line sits near 60% of the panel height; the large gem clusters and bones then fill the foreground, as they do in the pan at 77.2-79.0. (b) Dress the wall once in `diamondValley`: a slanting shaft of sunlight across the bands, two or three dark cave mouths (one with `peekEyes` that blink at 84.96 "حيات"), a dead root hanging from a ledge, and a strip of sky with a circling eagle at the top of the 0.62 wide.

### 4. 84.9-92.4 and 102-116.6 s: the snakes are big, not huge
- **What is wrong.** The green snake is about one and a half Sinbads long and thinner than his leg; the blue coil on the ledge is the size of his torso (still 85.3). The narration says "حيات كبيرة" and the plan says giant scale.
- **Why it matters.** The snakes are the reason he cannot simply wait on the floor; at this size the foot held in the air at 85.3 over-reacts.
- **Fix.** Scale both snakes about 1.7 times: body as thick as Sinbad's torso, head as large as his chest, the green one entering from beyond the left edge so its length is never seen whole. Keep the sleepy lids, the yawn and the slow wave; keep the SSSS... tag clear of the head.

### 5. 126.95-127.4 s: the last step onto the rim
- **What is wrong.** At 126.95 and 127.1 he is in the air above the top of the shelf with straight legs and no shadow, and at 127.3 he lies as a straight horizontal figure along the grass line. It now reads as a flop onto the rim, which is acceptable, but it is the stiffest half second in the film.
- **Fix.** Keep one foot on the shelf top at 126.95-127.1 (a reach with both hands to the grass, body tipped about 25 degrees), a `groundShadow` under him, then at 127.15-127.45 the belly pose bent at the hips (knees tucked, `tip` following the slope) instead of a straight line.

### 6. 78.2-78.8 s: the hand in the diamond insert
- **What is wrong.** The forearm and fingers are tan jointed cylinders, darker than his face in the same frame and with visible joint circles, so the hand reads as a wooden mannequin's.
- **Fix.** Fill the hand and forearm with the face's skin colour, hide the joint circles under one outline, and end the forearm in the white sleeve cuff of his tunic.

### 7. 89.6-90.95 s: the failed climb is small
- **What is wrong.** He hangs from a small knob with his feet about 40 px off the ground for a second and drops (stills 90.2, 90.6, 90.9). It reads as a hop more than as a climb that fails.
- **Fix.** Let him get higher before he slides: hands on the knob at 89.9, a second reach to the ledge above at 90.3 (lift about 150 world units, feet scrabbling in two drawings, small pebbles falling), fingers slip at 90.75 ("آمن"), slide down the wall with a dust trail, `landSquash` onto his seat as now.

### 8. 93.5-93.65 s and 142.7-143.3 s: two small finish items
- **What is wrong.** (a) The THUD! inverted flash stays on for about four frames (stills 93.5, 93.57, 93.63 are all dark blue-green); the style asks for two, and the plan keeps the impact frame for WHUMP!. (b) At 142.7-143.3 a flat lilac patch lies over the lower right of the sail and has no visible cause.
- **Fix.** (a) Cut the inverted flash to two frames at 93.58, or replace it with the dust burst and a 4 px panel shake that are already there. (b) Remove the lilac quad on the sail, or make it the far roc's small soft shadow moving left to right across the sail in step with the bird at 143.3-144.3.

## Reusable techniques for the Library
- **Cause before effect for a payoff prop**: the far silhouette crosses the sun, drops a fleck that grows into the prop and lands in the hero's hand during the end hold (142.2-146.5). Add to the capabilities guide under endings: "give the end hold one event".
- **Darkening without a tint**: a shadow shape sweeping across the set plus a cool second plate on the largest object, with the cast drawn after it in its own colours and a cyan rim (46.3-48.2).
- **Seated prop option** (`sbRocEgg` `seat`): cut the base below the ground line and cover it with a front ring; a general pattern for anything sitting in a nest, basket or pot.
- **Travelling glint run** (`sbGemGlints`): star glints with halation stepping across a field on threes; reusable for gold, water and snow.
- Rules learned for reviews and implcheck: when a character stands in a tree, check that no crown ball is drawn over the head (note 1); a glow is drawn behind the cast, never over it (note 2).
