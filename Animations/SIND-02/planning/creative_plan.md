# SIND-02 The Roc and the Diamond Valley: creative plan (80s Cel Anime)

Style `cel-anime-80s`, staged to the owner direction in `Library/styles/cel-anime-80s/look.md` (reference film ALI-01-ANIME v4) and to the series bible in `Animations/SIND-01/planning/creative_plan.md` section 6. Narration 143.82 s, last word 143.28 s, end hold 4.0 s, film 147.82 s. 22 scenes, 205 actions, 20 lettering items, 8 sound effects. All times are seconds on the final MP3; every cue is a word start from `inputs/timeline.md`. The full storyboard is in `creative_plan.json`; this file gives the direction.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (0-17.7):** Sinbad has been home too long (the cat takes his warm cushion on the quay bench) and sails again on the series dhow. A fine day, a wooded island, a shade tree, sleep. Behind him, small, the traders push off without looking at the tree.
- **Inciting (17.7-25.9):** after the one `LATER...` transition he wakes in afternoon light: footprints, a keel groove, the striped sail small on the horizon. In an inset a merchant counts heads and counts a sack as Sinbad.
- **Discovery (25.9-44.1):** he tightens his sash, walks inland, climbs a leaning tree and a white dome rises over the treetops of a silent island. Up close it is an egg in a nest of logs: THE ROC.
- **Reveal (44.1-55.0):** wind, a wing shaped shadow, the sun covered, and the roc comes home. The camera pulls out to 0.42 so the whole bird fits; its wings are the series clouds.
- **Turn (55.0-71.9):** the idea, the turban unwound, a soft bow on an ankle like a tree trunk, a pat for the bird. Take off, the flight over the mountains (wonder, not terror), the landing in a walled valley.
- **Treasure and trap (71.9-92.5):** the floor glitters with diamonds; the walls cannot be climbed; huge sleepy snakes live in the rocks. He takes a few stones and sits down to think.
- **The way out (92.5-128.1):** THUD, a joint of meat from the rim, diamonds stuck to it, an eagle carrying the next one up to its nest. AHA. He straps himself to the meat with the turban cloth, is lifted up the whole cliff, and tiptoes away past an eaglet that notices him.
- **Resolution (128.1-147.8):** the amazed traders on the rim, a diamond for each, a ship for Baghdad, the tale told on deck. A far roc crosses the sun and one giant feather falls into his hands. THE END.
- **Conflict:** one small man against scale (egg, bird, cliffs, snakes). **Moral:** a cool head and a gentle hand find the way out; wonders are for sharing.
- **Emotional arc:** restless, content, abandoned, resolved, awed, daring, delighted, trapped, clever, free, generous, proud.

## 3. Character acting
All pose channels on twos (`celStep(t, 12)`), lids, tongues and glints on threes (`celStep(t, 8)`). Nobody is frozen: `idleSway`, `breathe`, ribbons, per-member seeds.
- **Sinbad:** as the bible: chest and arms lead, eyes before head before body (`gazePlan`, a blink on every turn), ribbons late (`lagged`). The open-armed breath returns three times (6.94 at the bow, 133.2 as a ta-da, 141.0 as wings). His concentration tell is the tongue out (60.0, 71.92). Fear is played as stillness: a foot held in the air (85.3), a "rock" pose on one leg (126.0), `tremble` and moving pupils only. Low point at 25.18: arms come down after the shoulders, the turban tail hangs straight for the first time. Joy at 67.0: one eye (`wink`), both, a grin, knees tucked over a peak.
- **The roc:** weight and calm. Every move has a long anticipation (`anticipate` 0.45 s before take off), landings fold the legs (`impactSquash`), wing tips and tail streamers arrive last. It behaves like a hen: tilts its head at the egg, turns it with the side of the beak, wriggles down, sighs, sleeps (`lid` over 5 drawings). It never looks at Sinbad; the one half-open eye at 62.36 sees nothing.
- **Snakes:** slow travelling wave always on; heavy lids, a yawn, a chin on a warm rock. Curious, never hunting.
- **The eagle:** quick and sharp against the roc's slowness: hard head tilts (`headRot`), a crouch before each lift, the load swinging a beat late (`swing`). The eaglets bob out of time; one stares at "the meat that has eyes".
- **Ship merchants:** the SIND-01 trio with the same tempers (fat and comfortable, plum and nervous, rust and practical), always `stagger(i)`.
- **Valley traders:** eldest slow and formal (beard wags, claps Sinbad's shoulders), second watchful, the young one all reaction (double take, drops the joint on his foot). Three different responses to a diamond: bite it, hold it to the sun, hide it in a cap.
- **Cameos:** cat (one slow blink at the ship), monkey (freezes on the snore, then steals the fruit).

## 4. Camera and composition
Owner direction first: wide where the place matters, pans and tilts across detailed sets, the lower picture never empty, close-ups only at peaks.
- **Wides at zoom 1.0 to 1.15** for the quay, the sea, the glade, the egg, the rim, the port and the homeward deck.
- **Scale shots:** the egg fills the frame at zoom 1.0 with Sinbad under a quarter of its height; the pull-out to 0.42 at 47.96 for the whole roc; the flight at 0.36; S8 at 1.25 with only the roc's foot, shank and belly band in frame so the leg reads as a tree trunk.
- **Vertical moves are this film's signature** (SIND-01 went below the water; this one goes up and down a cliff): crane up to the dome (30.2), crane down into the valley (68.46), tilt up and down the walls (80.76), the quick tilt to the throwers (96.22), the crane with the eagle (105.22) and the long lift (119.42), each one group translate on `t`.
- **Pans as storytelling:** to the island (9.92); left to the empty sea (22.04); through the wood (26.6); across the glittering floor (77.16); along the shelf to the rim (125.46).
- **Close shots, three only:** the roc's eye cut-in (62.36, 0.6 s), the diamond in his fingers (78.3, 0.8 s), the stuck diamonds (99.94, 1.0 s).
- **Impact frame:** WHUMP! at 48.94 only.
- **Echo framings:** S1 repeats the SIND-01 opening; S22 repeats the SIND-01 homeward gold sea, with the far roc where the far whale was.
- Faces and key action stay between world y 250 and y 900 in panel terms; balloons sit in the sky and point at the speaker.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **Dramatic irony:** the traders leaving behind his back (16.46); the feather that falls past him unseen (33.9); the growing shadow of the meat (91.9); the throwers on the rim before the narration names them (96.74).
- **Suspense by stillness and silence:** the island going quiet (33.18); the half-open eye (62.36); the snake's head rising while he lies strapped (115.82); the eagle looking straight down at him (116.54); the eaglet that tells (126.0).
- **Surprise:** the thump from inside the egg (39.54); the sun covered by a wing (47.28); THUD! (93.58).
- **Humour:** the cat; the fat merchant and the skiff; the head count; arms that cannot reach round the ankle; pedalling legs before landing; no turban when he pats his head; the melon diamond put back; STICKY!; the rock pose; the joint on the foot; the crooked turban.
- **Emotion:** the arms coming down on the beach; the pat and the bow to the bird; the diamonds lighting four faces; the feather.
- **Setup and payoff:** the turban (bible: "a clear separate piece") becomes rope, sling, strap, scarf and turban again; the feather in S5, S6 and S22; the open arms; cloud wings against the series cumulus.
- **Colour as story:** fresh morning, gold afternoon of being left, bright day at the nest with a cool wing shadow, blue sky flight, rose and ochre valley with white sparkle, green rim, gold sea. Plates change; the cast never takes a tint.

## 8. Warnings and open questions
- The roc is the main risk: build and review it on stills at three scales before acting. Huge and kind, never a monster.
- The valley needs vertical camera travel, so it is its own module (Library strips only slide sideways).
- Sinbad is about 90 px high in the two widest shots; the tangerine cloth and one arrow tag must carry him.
- The strapped lie pose turning from flat to upright (118.2) needs a frame-by-frame check.
- Seven pauses only: 8 effects under 0.5 s; nothing under the landing or the THUD!, which fall on words.
- New modules exceed the usual four because the story visits four new places; three reuse planned names and all are reusable.
- Scripts match; no SCRIPT question. Owner questions: 9, in the JSON (the feather ending, the gentle roc, the sleepy snakes, the cartoon meat and early throwers, the turban as rope, the lettering, the comic additions, the two ships, the wing shadow).
