# AN-095 Ja'far the Barmecide and the Bean-Seller: creative plan

## 1. Narrative breakdown

**Logline.** A Bedouin poet who lost his generous patron is sent by a dream to a rich merchant in Basra with one strange password, a single bean, and learns how Ja'far once turned a shivering bean-seller's last bean into a fortune.

**Shape.** A frame story with a mystery. The frame (the Bedouin) carries loss and gratitude; the inner story (the bean-seller) is playful and escalating. The question "why a bean?" is planted at 50.12, asked aloud at 87.40 and answered from 132.50 to 149.66.

Emotion runs: cheerful ritual, shock, hushed grief, wonder, puzzlement, delight, gratitude. **Ending:** after the last word the Bedouin finally recites his poem for Ja'far. **Moral:** a kindness is never lost; the one who received it carries it on.

## 3. Character acting

- **The Bedouin** is open-faced and physical. Eager bounce and chin up in S1 and the first half of S2; after 19.68 he moves slowly, head low, long blinks. His recital gesture (free hand sweeping up, `talk` 0.8) appears three times: full size in S1 (6.32), half size and wet-eyed at the stone (33.16), full size again in the last shot (175.5). Curiosity in S7: `PERSON_HANDS.chin`, `tip` 0.08, saccades between purses and host (`gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)`). Keffiyeh tails lag head moves by 2 frames.
- **Ja'far** is calm, warm and a little mischievous. He always moves with anticipation and a settle (`anticipate`, `settleTo`), half-lidded pleased smile (`lid` 0.3 to 0.5), and looks directly at whoever he is helping (`lookAt`). At the window (108.80) his cup stops halfway: concern is shown by stillness and a lean (`tip` 0.12). In S11 and S12 he is playing a game: side glances, a wink to the lady. Dream version: slow float, alpha 0.85, same welcome pose as S1.
- **The merchant** begins as a polite busy man (one hand keeps counting, `lid` 0.35). On the name (62.28) only his pupils move for 0.6 s; then the face collapses (`wet`, `brow` -0.8, `tremble`, `PERSON_HANDS.cheeks`). Generous gestures are big arm angles that read at phone size (raised-hand promise at 76.52 uses `aR` about [2.45, 2.85]).
- **The young bean-seller** has the merchant's moustache and red headwear but a thin, hunched body. Hope then drop on each ignored offer (arm sinks with follow-through). Cold: `tremble` rising from 2 to 4, `PERSON_HANDS.folded`, breath puffs. In the hall he stays small and frightened (`sq` 0.95, darting eyes) and warms up slowly. His amazement escalates in four steps: jaw drop (122.16), knees fold (143.60), eyes over the gold with the film's one look to camera (149.66), the pinch (156.18).
- **The lady** glides, veil trailing; amused, quick, competitive with Ja'far. **Courtiers** react one after another (`stagger(i)`), each differently. **Townsfolk** in S2 use short fast steps, shoulders up, glance and look away, `PERSON_HANDS.hush`.
- Everyone blinks (`LIFE.t = t`), every listener looks at the speaker, and no blink during held shock (`noBlink`).

## 4. Camera and composition

- World 1000 x 1230, ground y 900; faces and key props stay in the panel's upper area; subtitles own the bottom third.
- Establishing wides open S1, S2, S9 and S10; S10 repeats the S1 framing on purpose.
- Two-shots (`twoShot`) for all dialogue in the merchant's house; push-ins on decisions and feelings: the Bedouin's shock (21.84), the merchant's tears (62.28 to 64.36), his eyes before the flashback (89.06).
- **Match-cuts:** old eyes to young eyes at 90.2 (same panel position and zoom 2.8), and hand-on-heart in Basra to hand-on-heart in the present at 165.0.
- The window diagonal in S9: Ja'far upper right looking down-left, the seller lower left looking up-right; one high shot from the window makes the seller small.
- Insert shots for the bowl (121.7) and the last bean (132.5); the pull-out and tilt in S12 follow the growing pile; the final pull-out at 172.2 reveals the treasure.
- The two journeys (S5, S14) are the same side-on tracking shot in the same direction.
- Transitions: wipes and dissolves between places; one `later` card at 70.84 ("3 DAYS LATER..."); the flashback enters on the match-cut with a "YEARS AGO..." label.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities

- **Suspense:** the merchant is only a silhouette in the dream; the bean glows with a question mark; the Bedouin's own "?" stays with him until it flips to "!" at 165.78.
- **Emotion through light:** Ja'far is the warm yellow window. It is lit in S1, black in S2, lit in the rain in S9, and returns in memory at 156.80 and 167.64.
- **Humour:** the purse that makes the Bedouin's knees dip three times; the three-day feast time-lapse; "ME?"; the picture sums (half a bean = a heap of gold); the seller disappearing behind his own fortune; the pinch; two purses beside a mountain of treasure ("A DROP!").
- **Surprise:** a rich man weeping at the word "bean"; gold instead of coppers; the curtain reveal.
- **Echoes that pay off:** the same glowing bean in the dream and in the basket; the same 1000 counter; the poem finally delivered under THE END.
- **Restraint:** the execution and the body are never shown (dark palace, plain stone with a turban cloth).

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus

- Start from `Library/template/`; cue everything with `w(p, n)` from `timing.json`; the times in the JSON are the same word starts.
- Build the costume layer first as `personC(ctx, P)` wrapping `person` in the story's `src`, check it on a contact sheet (all six presets, walking, kneeling, gestures), then stage. Robes hide the legs but the hem should sway with `walk()` phase; headwear tails lag by 2 frames.
- Walks: `walk(X, t)` + `footfalls` everywhere (S1, S2, S3, S5, S9, S13, S14) with `dustBurst` on dry ground and `ripple` splashes in rain. Sits and kneels land with `landSquash`. Jumps and heaves use `anticipate`, `hop`. Falling things (scroll, coins, tears) use `fallY` + `rebound`. Hanging signs use `swing`. Long holds use `breathe`.
- Gaze: `lookAt` for every eye line, `gazePlan` for held targets, `gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)` for saccades. One look to camera only (149.66).
- Coins and rain are seeded (`K.rng` with fixed seeds) so each frame is a pure function of `t`. Heaps grow by drawing the first n coins of a fixed layout.
- Gold in S10 to S12 is shown by heap size, a bar meter and x2 stamps; no figures (the script gives none). The 1000 and 1500 counters are from the script.
- Sound effects only at 7.4, 10.6, 15.4, 19.3, 51.55, 58.5, 164.9 and 175.6 (the real gaps), `master: 0.6`. CLINK!, SNAP! and similar are lettering only.
- Lettering never covers a face; headline at least 44 px, labels at least 28 px at 1280x720 scale; no en or em dashes. Call `finish` last every frame.
- Library stays unchanged until the new drawings are approved; then one function per drawing, registry entries, `node Library/tests/regression.mjs` must print "all identical".

## 8. Warnings and open questions

1. **Sensitive content.** The execution and "the place of his body" are softened to a dark chained palace and a plain stone with a turban cloth. Please confirm.
2. **Scope.** The costume system is the one large asset and six characters depend on it; approve it before staging.
3. **Basket and vessel.** The script uses both السلة and إناء; the plan uses a basket with the bean pot and a small tin bowl for the gold, with the gold in the basket from S13.
4. **Long single-set scenes.** S4 (17.4 s) and S7 (19.4 s) need a pace review first.
5. **Few pauses.** Only seven gaps of 0.3 s or more exist, so the film has very few sound effects.
6. **Interpolated word.** فضل (about 168.50) was not recognised; the memory cloud is cued to ينس (167.64) instead.
