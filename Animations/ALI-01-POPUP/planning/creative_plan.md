# ALI-01-POPUP: Open Sesame (Paper Pop-up Book) creative plan

Style `paper-popup`. Narration 138.94 s (last word 138.71), end hold 4.5 s, film 143.44 s. Six spreads, five page turns. Every time below is on the final MP3; the JSON carries the cue word for each action. This is one of two films of the same story in different styles: it must be native to the pop-up book (mechanisms, cards, page turns), not a re-skin.

## 1. Narrative breakdown
- **Setup (0-15.2):** Ali Baba, poor woodcutter, with wife, small son, hut and donkey; the daily walk to the forest.
- **Inciting (18.0):** hooves. **Suspense (22.4-33.4):** fear, the donkey hidden, the climb, the eyes in the leaves.
- **Rising (34.5-40):** forty men with heavy sacks stop at the rock, right under his tree.
- **Reveal (42.8-48.3):** OPEN SESAME, the rock cracks and slides apart on a pull-tab: the film's signature mechanism.
- **Wait (50.3-69.3):** they go in, the rock shuts, "much later" (dusk reprint), they leave, CLOSE SESAME.
- **Climax (78-87.6):** Ali Baba copies the leader's pose, small and scared; a beat of nothing; it opens before his eyes.
- **Surprise and character (89.5-104.4):** gold, silver, cloth; he takes one tiny sack and runs home.
- **Turn (105.6-122.6):** the secret told to his wife, then pulled out of him by Cassim.
- **Moral and hook (123.8-138.7):** Cassim thinks only of gold, grabs the map, half listens to the words, sets off with a cart of empty sacks. Will he remember how to get out?
- **Conflict:** need against greed, shown as one tiny sack against ten empty ones. **Arc:** cosy, afraid, amazed, modest, uneasy. **Ending:** open question; the shut rock is the last card standing.

## 3. Character acting
- **Ali Baba** (blue robe, red sash, white turban, bare feet, s 0.8): open, quick to smile. Fear = `eyes: 'wide'`, brow -0.6, `sq` 0.9, `tremble`. In the tree he acts with eyes only (gaze, `lid`, `wink`): squeeze shut when a thief looks up, one eye peeking. The descent is the opposite of the climb: slow, toes feeling. At the rock: chin, recall saccades, a half rehearsal, then the leader's exact arm angles on bent knees. In the cave: stretch 1.08, cheeks, then the "but" (head shake, hand on heart, a gulp at the thieves' sacks). With Cassim he leans away further on each question and finally sags (`sq` 0.93, `lid` 0.5). Turban always trails with `lagged`.
- **Donkey:** the comic chorus. Ears arrive before the head (`earL/earR`, then head `lagged` 0.12); hides with only ears showing, ears go flat when a horse sniffs; tiptoes behind him; trots home with the load bouncing.
- **Leader:** stillness and economy; chin high, brow 0.6; cape and headcloth follow through (`swing`, `lagged`). One sweep of the eyes up at the tree (53.3) is the film's tightest moment.
- **Thieves and horses:** never in sync: `stagger(i, 0.35)` entries, own seeds, four robe colours; rider 2 eager, rider 3 nervous (he is the one who looks up at 42.0 and whose sack snags). Heavy going in (short stride, `tip` 0.1), light coming out.
- **Wife:** `cheeks` at the coins, solemn nods through `flow`, lips locked; hides behind her husband and peeks; `headHold` when the secret is out.
- **Cassim** (green robe, big yellow turban, s 0.86): peeks turban first, saccades to the coin, bites it, leans in (`tip` 0.12, brow 0.8), circles with mirror flips; then round eyes, slow grin, wringing hands (`osc`), coin discs over the eyes; on the road chin first, not looking where he goes; at the end `headHold`, map upside down.
- Everyone: `LIFE.t` blinks, `breathe`, small `idleSway` per channel with own seeds; head turns carry a blink; hops have `groundShadow` and `landSquash`.

## 4. Camera and composition
- The desk and book never move; the camera only pushes in on the 1000-wide page (zoom 1.0 to 2.1) and cuts under page turns. No shake.
- Wide for mechanisms and groups (arrival, file into the door, ride-off); push-ins for feeling: the leaf gap (31.25, 48.5), the door (46.2), Ali Baba's face at 87.6, the tiny sack (99.5), Cassim's grin (121.4), the far rock (137.5).
- Tilt up with the climb so the tree feels tall. Two-shot at the hut table; whip to the door for Cassim; speaker and listeners on opposite sides.
- Depth rows on the page: back y 905..915, mid 950, front 985..990. Bubbles and tags sit on the board above heads, never on a face or the leaf gap. Pull-tabs live at the page bottom (y 1180), still inside the story panel.

## 5. Storytelling opportunities
- **The mechanism is the magic:** the same pull-tab that raised the sun opens the rock. The viewer learns the book's grammar in S1 and is paid in S3.
- **Suspense:** thieves pass directly under the tree; two looks upward (42.0, 53.3) answered by total stillness of the leaves; the beat of nothing after Ali Baba's own call (84.0-85.0).
- **Humour:** donkey ears over the bush; the moth from the empty pouch, echoed by the moth from Cassim's empty sack; tiny sack against a mountain of gold with a badge "1"; Cassim biting the coin; the mule's flat look.
- **Setup and payoff:** the leader's arms-up pose, copied by Ali Baba, mimed again to his wife. Cassim not listening to the whisper sets up the final question.
- **Emotion:** modesty in the cave is one clear gesture (hand on heart, then the smallest sack). The ending leaves one card standing: the rock.

## 7. Implementation notes for Opus
- Frame: `S.background`, `S.box(..., THEME.panel)`, board print, `bookFold(ctx, 0, WW, GR)`, cards, overlays, subtitles, `S.finish`. Brown ink (`C.black`), `dotPat` stipple, palette names.
- Characters and props through `castFinish`; set pieces through `popCard` with back-to-front delays and `out` before each turn. Group expensive figures (rider + horse, chain, cart + load) into single `castFinish` calls and bake settled cards.
- Page turns with `S.transition` at 15.9, 56.5 ("Much later...", the `later` transition), 88.9, 105.0, 131.8; the opening flyleaf is the second half of the same primitive. Swap spread and camera at the covered midpoint.
- Motion: `walk`/`footfalls` for all travel, `GAIT.horse`/`GAIT.donkey` strides; `hop`, `anticipate`, `landSquash`, `groundShadow` for dismounts, mounts and Ali Baba's jump back; `flow` for arm raises, nods and the climb height; `lagged` for turbans, capes, sacks, ears; `turnFlip` for every turn; `gazePlan(t, plan, 0.07)` for saccades; `lookAt` for the eyes in the gap.
- `ARMS_UP` shared by leader, Ali Baba and the hut mime. Typed bubbles keyed to the two words of each phrase.
- Lettering 20 items, effects 14, all effects inside the listed pauses; none at 0.0 or 131.8.
- Subtitles: chunks copied from `chunks_ar.txt` / `chunks_en.txt` (three per paragraph), shared with the other film.

## 8. Warnings and open questions
- Performance of `castFinish` in the crowded forest scenes (34-70 s) is the main risk; test early.
- No profile view of the person: the climb, riders and door entry depend on staging (trunk slot, `rideOn`, arch clip).
- `hutRoom` inside a `popCard` and Cassim's peek round the door need a check against the HUT anchors.
- Spreads 2 and 3 share one layout for 73 s; the dusk reprint and camera work must keep it fresh.
- Ten owner questions are listed in the JSON (forty shown as five plus a chain, family as wife and son, English bubbles, invented gags, the map, no weapons shown, flyleaf opening, dusk reprint for "much later", THE END rather than TO BE CONTINUED, no bubble when Ali Baba closes the door). No script mismatch was found between the Arabic and the English.
