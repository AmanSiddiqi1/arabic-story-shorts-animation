## Notes (most important first)

### 1. The heavy sacks stay on the horses after the men have carried them in (50.3-55.9)
**Wrong:** the walkers do carry a sack on the shoulder into the door (51.3-52.6), but all four horses keep their own cream sack on the saddle through the door closing and up to the page turn (clear at 53.5, 54.5-55.7). At dusk (57.2) the saddles are bare. So the sacks are doubled, and the viewer sees loaded horses left outside while the narrator says دخل الرجال الكهف بأكياسهم.
**Why:** full sacks in, flat sacks out is the film's picture of the thieves' wealth and sets up "one tiny sack".
**Fix:** tie each horse's `load` to its rider. When rider i dismounts (50.3 + his stagger), the horse's load drops to none over 4 frames as the sack appears on his shoulder (same sack drawing, a small `sq` 0.94 dip on the walker as he takes the weight, the horse's back rising 3 px with a `lagged` tail flick). From 53.9 on, every horse is bare. Nothing else changes.

### 2. A third of the page is empty in the hut, and nearly half on the road (105.5-131.0; 132.2-139.0)
**Wrong:** the room card's floor sits at about 62% of the panel height; below it is flat pink page with nothing on it for 26 s (see 108.0, 110.0, 115.4, 127.5, 130.3). On the road the sand below the figures is empty for the whole spread (133.5-137.5). The forest and cave spreads solved this with the groove, tab, stones and chest; these two did not.
**Why:** it is the largest remaining composition weakness, and it is where the anime version will look fuller. The story's turn is told by three faces that occupy a band 20% of the frame high.
**Fix (hut):** lower the camera target so the floor line sits at about 78-80% of the panel height in every hut key, and raise the base zoom from about 1.0 to 1.25 for the two-shot (105.6-112.8) and 1.2 for the three-shot (117.7-120.3, 127.2-131.0); the lantern and window may crop. On the strip of page that remains, put front-row cards that belong to the story: a striped rug card popping forward with the room at 105.3, Ali Baba's sandals by the door, and let the rolling coin (111.67) leave the room card and roll onto the page toward the door, so Cassim's find at 115.35 happens on the page in front. **Fix (road):** same camera correction (ground line at about 75%), and a printed road with two or three front-row stone or tuft cards larger than the back row.

### 3. "He heard horses approaching quickly" is under-acted (16.5-22.4)
**Wrong:** from 18.0 to 21.5 Ali Baba stands holding his sticks with both arms; there is no cupped ear, the sound arcs at 18.8-19.2 are faint marks by the right trunk, and the rider strip (20.5-21.2) is a few 20 px silhouettes half behind foliage. 21.7-22.4 is a held picture (lowest motion of the forest scenes). Only the donkey acts.
**Why:** this is the inciting beat; the fear at 22.41 needs a cause the viewer has seen.
**Fix:** 18.03 (سمع): he straightens with a 2-frame head snap toward the right, one arm leaves the bundle and cups the ear (the other keeps the sticks, so arms differ), a stick slips out and drops with a `tick`. 18.75 (أصوات): `soundArcs` three times larger, travelling from the right frame edge toward him. 19.41 (خيول): rider strip at 2x, on the pale ridge road in a clear gap between trunks (move the strip's slot to where no foliage card covers it, or lift it above the small trees), each rider bobbing; his gaze tracks it with `lookAt`. 21.01 (بسرعة): dust puffs trailing the strip. 21.7-22.4 (the pause): a take, not a hold: `anticipate` crouch, `sq` 0.9, eyes wide, sticks dropped, donkey's ears flat; then 22.41 (خاف) the tremble as now.

### 4. In the cave wide shot he is small and looks cross (91.5-95.2)
**Wrong:** for 3.7 s the frame is the whole spread; Ali Baba is about 11% of the panel height by the door, hands on hips, brows angled down (93.0). The saccades to gold, silver and cloth are too small to see, so the treasure pops but nobody reacts until the push-in at 95.4.
**Why:** amazement is the emotion of this spread; the pops need his face.
**Fix:** hold the wide only for the gold (90.8-91.5). From 91.5 frame tighter (zoom about 1.3, centred between him and the heap that is popping, drifting left as silver and cloth arrive, so the navy margins leave the frame). Brows up (`brow` -0.5), mouth open, no hands on hips: 91.56 (وفضة) he points with the near arm and takes one step toward it with `walk`; 92.56 (وأقمشة) the other hand goes to his turban, which trails with `lagged`; 93.84 (ثمينة) head tips back for the stars with a blink. Then the existing 95.40 hands-to-cheeks plays as the top of a build, not the first reaction.

### 5. Cassim's peek and the coin bite do not read (113.3-117.6)
**Wrong:** the peek (114.2-116.0) is a sliver of yellow turban behind the door leaf at the right edge, and the CASSIM arrow lands on top of it. At 116.9-117.5 (push-in) he stands with a hand at his turban and the coin near his hip; nothing goes to his mouth, and his face is glum.
**Why:** "noticed the new coins" is the hinge between the two brothers; the bite is the planned laugh.
**Fix:** 113.26 (لكن): door leaf swings a further 25 degrees so a clear gap opens on its room side; 114.55 (قاسم): turban, then both eyes, lean in through that gap at least 60 px clear of the leaf (`tip` 0.1); move the CASSIM tag above the door with the arrow ending beside his head, not on it. 115.35 (لاحظ): 2-frame saccade down to the coin, which gives one glint. 116.25: he steps in, bends (`sq` 0.9) and lifts the coin. 116.87 (الجديدة): in the push-in the coin is held at his mouth between finger and thumb, one eyebrow up, a `tick`, then he looks at it with a slow one-sided grin and pockets it.

### 6. The leaf gap shows a blue "eye" and then a red blob while he stands up (71.1-74.0)
**Wrong:** when the turban rises over the flap at 71.2, his robe and sash pass behind the eye gap, in the push-in. At 71.2-71.5 it reads as one large blue eye, and at 72.0-73.7 as a red mouth in the foliage.
**Why:** the gap has meant "his eyes" for 43 s; a strange shape there in close-up confuses the relief beat.
**Fix:** keep the eyes in the gap and bring the turban up only as far as the brow line above the flap (he cranes, he does not stand): eyes look right after the riders (70.48), blink, then the turban top rises 20 px with `lagged` at 71.14. If he must stand, close the gap as he rises (a leaf card swings across it at 71.1 with a small back()), so nothing shows through.

### 7. Paper tags, bubbles and badges dissolve out
**Wrong:** every label exit is a cross-fade to transparent: ALI BABA tag 3.0, title sign 4.2, THIEVES? 25.2, badge 40 at 39.0, OPEN SESAME! 44.7, CLOSE SESAME! 65.7, Ali Baba's bubble 84.7, GOLD/CLOTH tags 95.7, badge 1 at 101.0, CASSIM 116.2, the first "?" at 120.5.
**Why:** the style rule is that nothing just appears and paper cannot dissolve; these are the last un-paper frames in the film (the thieves' ghosts were the first).
**Fix:** exits in 5-6 frames as paper: bubbles and tags flip shut about their top edge (scaleY to 0 with a slight ease-in, a `paper` tick only where there is a narration gap), badges spin edge-on (scaleX to 0), the title sign is pulled up on its strings. If the fade lives in `Library/styles/paper-popup/style.js`, add it there as the style's default exit for this style only (no other style or finished film uses it) and run the regression test; otherwise wrap in the story.

### 8. Road gags: the "10" pile is half out of frame, the upside-down map is not visible (133.7-137.2)
**Wrong:** at 134.0-135.5 the sack pile and its badge sit against the right frame edge, partly cropped, while the left third of the frame is empty; the slipping sack is a small pale shape under the mule's belly. At 136.6 the map looks the same as before.
**Why:** "ten empty sacks against one tiny one" is the moral in one image, and the upside-down map is the answer to the final question.
**Fix:** shift the travelling camera about 120 px right for 133.07-135.6 so Cassim sits at the left third and the whole cart and pile are inside the frame; the slipping sack falls behind the cart onto the road in clear view (134.2), flat and floppy. 136.63 (سيتذكر): rotate the map card 180 degrees in his hands with a visible half-turn (the red X ends up at the bottom, the tree upside down), he tilts his head the other way to follow it, then the head scratch.

### 9. Cassim's "grin" reads as startled (121.4-123.0)
**Wrong:** in the push-in his eyes are fully round and the mouth is hidden under the moustache; with the wringing hands it reads as alarm.
**Fix:** `lid` 0.6, brows low inside and up outside (`brow` 0.7), a slow wide grin with teeth appearing below the moustache between 121.37 and 122.0 (`flow`), pupils sliding to the picture card and back to Ali Baba; keep the wringing hands and add a small shoulder rise.

## Reusable techniques worth adding to the Library
- **Prop hand-over rule:** a carried prop exists once. When a rider takes his load, the mount's `load` goes to none in the same frames; worth a small helper (`handOver(t, t0, from, to)`) returning the two visibilities and the weight dip.
- **`abDoorClip` "into a slot":** walkers drawn between two cards and clipped to the opening, opaque shade deeper in. Works for any door, cave or tent in this style.
- **One eased value for tab and mechanism (`abRockState`):** the pull-tab and the thing it drives share one number, with a jerk before the slide and a settle rock after. Move with `abPullTab`/`abSlot` to `Library/styles/paper-popup/`.
- **Two-point suspense frame:** a diagonal push that holds the threat low on one side and the hider high on the other (53.0-54.2), with the background sway stopped for the beat.
- **Paper exits:** flip-shut, edge-on spin and string pull-up as the style's standard label exits (note 7).
- **Hanging picture card** (rock picture, map, "?") sharing the title sign's swing; and the **reprint** of one layout in a dusk palette.
- **Motion scan in the review pack:** a per-second frame-difference row over the story panel finds still holds quickly; worth adding to `tools/review/make_pack.py`.
